## Preparation Strategy for PYQs

1. **Time-bound practice**: Solve papers with strict 3-hour time limits to simulate exam conditions
2. **Topic-wise analysis**: After each paper, categorize mistakes by subject area
3. **Formula revision**: Create quick formula sheets for problems you struggled with
4. **Difficulty mapping**: Note down questions above your comfort zone for future revision
5. **Trend analysis**: Track frequently appearing topics across years
