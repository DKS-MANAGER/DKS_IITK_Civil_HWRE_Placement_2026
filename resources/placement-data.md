## 1. Governance & Dataset Taxonomy

### 1.1 Provenance & Scope Definition
This document provides verified institutional hiring data derived directly from the IIT Kanpur 2025–2026 placement export. The full dataset encompasses 516 job profiles across 276 recruiting firms. This page isolates the **Civil Engineering & HWRE-relevant postings**, breaking down compensation structures, eligibility constraints, domain requirements, and targeted preparation tracks.

### 1.2 Definition of Relevance Tiers
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Tiers reflect Domain Relevance to Civil/HWRE, NOT Employer Prestige or Quality.**
> - **Tier 1 (High Relevance):** Job description specifically mandates or prioritizes Civil, Structural, Geotechnical, Environmental, or Water Resources Engineering graduates.
> - **Tier 2 (Medium Relevance):** Multidisciplinary engineering, analytics, EPC, or infrastructure roles where Civil/HWRE postgraduates are explicitly eligible.
> - **Tier 3 (Low Relevance / Niche Hook):** Broad industrial or technical postings with marginal civil touchpoints (e.g., plant maintenance, building automation, piping).
> - **PSU Track:** Public Sector Undertakings recruiting M.Tech Civil engineers via GATE scores or dedicated examinations.
