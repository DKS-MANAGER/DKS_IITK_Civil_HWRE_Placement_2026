# Civil Engineering Foundations

## Overview

Civil engineering preparation for placements centers on fluency in core derivations, scaling laws, code-level design checks, and awareness of model limitations. The foundation spans fluid mechanics, hydraulics, hydrology, structural mechanics, geotechnics, and environmental systems.

## Core Technical Domains

### Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulics
- Bernoulli principle, continuity equation, momentum and energy equations, dimensional analysis
- Viscous flow behavior, pipe friction characteristics, laminar versus turbulent regime identification, boundary layer development
- Pump and turbine performance, drag and lift on submerged bodies, pipe network analysis

### Open Channel Flow
- Gradually varied flow (GVF) and rapidly varied flow (RVF)
- Hydraulic jump behavior and unsteady flow propagation
- Stage-discharge relationships and control structures

### Hydrology & Water Resources
- Unit hydrograph theory and flood routing methods
- Hydrologic cycle processes, rainfall-runoff relationships, infiltration mechanics, catchment response characterization
- Reservoir and canal design principles

### Groundwater Systems
- Darcy's law for flow through porous media
- Theis solution for aquifer drawdown
- Aquifer property determination and well hydraulics

### Sediment Transport
- Sediment transport mechanics, scour processes, erosion mechanics, bed evolution modeling

### Structural Mechanics (breadth for core-design roles)
- Bending, shear, torsion, deflection, and energy methods in strength of materials
- Consolidation, permeability, bearing capacity in soil mechanics
- Slope stability, earth pressure theories, retaining wall design
- RCC and steel basics including IS 456 and IS 800 load combinations
