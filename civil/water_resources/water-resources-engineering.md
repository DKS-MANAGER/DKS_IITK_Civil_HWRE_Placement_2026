# Water Resources Engineering

## Scope

Water resources engineering encompasses the planning, development, distribution, and management of water for human and environmental needs. It integrates hydrology, hydraulics, groundwater, and irrigation into systems-level design.

## Surface Water Systems

### Reservoir Design
- Storage capacity determination using mass curves and sequency analysis
- Yield estimation: Firm yield, secondary yield, and design flood
- Sedimentation and dead storage considerations
- Reservoir operation rules: Conservation, flood control, and multi-purpose optimization

### Canal Distribution Systems
- Canal alignment and cross-section design
- Lining versus unlined canals: Seepage loss, maintenance cost, and structural trade-offs
- Distribution system hydraulics: Laterals, distributors, and watercourses
- Scheduling and rotation of water deliveries

### Stage-Discharge Relationships
- Rating curves: Stage-discharge relationships for natural and controlled channels
- Afflux calculations at bridges and culverts
- Backwater effects from downstream controls

## Groundwater Systems

### Aquifer Characterization
- Aquifer types: Confined, unconfined, leaky-confined
- Porosity, specific yield, and specific storage
- Heterogeneity and anisotropy in aquifer properties

### Well Hydraulics
- Steady-state radial flow (Thiem equation)
- Unsteady-state flow (Theis and Jacob solutions)
- Collector wells and radial collector wells
- Well losses: Linear and nonlinear components

### Groundwater Contamination
- Advection-dispersion equation for solute transport
- Natural attenuation and remediation strategies
- Monitoring network design

## Integrated Water Resources Management (IWRM)

### System Optimization
- Linear programming for reservoir operation
- Simulation-optimization frameworks
- Multi-objective trade-offs: Irrigation, hydropower, flood control, environmental flows

### Climate Adaptation
- Changing precipitation patterns and extreme event frequency
- Drought planning and water allocation during scarcity
- Demand management and water conservation

## Infrastructure Assessment

### Dam Safety
- Hazard potential classification
- Inflow design flood (IDF) selection
- Breach wave (dam break flood) analysis
- Emergency action planning

### River Basin Planning
- Water balance at basin scale
- Inter-basin transfer feasibility
- Environmental flow requirements (E-flow)
