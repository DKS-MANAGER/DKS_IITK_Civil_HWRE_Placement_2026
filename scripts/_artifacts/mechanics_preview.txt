L869: 6.1. Trusses
L870: A truss is structure composed of slender member joined together at end points by bolting/Rivelting/welding. Ends of the
L871: member are pointed to a common plate called Gusset plate
L872: 6.1.1. Assumption
L873: • Member joint together by smooth pin
L874: • All loading are applied to joint
L875: • Self Weight of member is negligible.
L876: 6.1.2. Zero Force Member
L877: They are provided to reduce effective length of other members.
L878: • If two non – collinear member exist at a truss joint and no external force or support reaction is applied at joint, the
L879: member must be zero force member.

L881: • If 3 member joint at a point and out of 2 are collinear and also no external load acts at joint, then the third member is
L882: zero force member.
