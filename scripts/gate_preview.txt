  106: 1.1. Fundamental Concepts of Mechanics
  107: • Mechanics is a branch of physical science which deals with effects of force on objects.
  108: • Application of law of mechanics to field problem is termed as Engineering mechanics.
  110: • Static only-In Gate Syllabus.
  111:  1.1.1. Basic Terminologies in Mechanics
  112: • Length: Used to locate the position of point of space and thereby describe the size of physical system.
  113: • Space: The geometrical region in which study of body is involved is called space.
  114: • Time: It is a measure of succession of event. As static is time independent, this quantity plays an important role in
  115: dynamics.
  116: • Mass: Measure of inertia of body, which is its resistance to a change in velocity.
  117: • Particle: It is a concentrated point mass of negligible dimension. Such body cannot exist theoretically.
  118: e.g., a bomber aeroplane is a particle for a gunner operating from ground.
  119: • Rigid body: A body is said to be rigid, if the relative position of any two points in it do not change under the action
  120: of forces.
