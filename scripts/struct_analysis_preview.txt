L4089: • This theorem is based on the principles of statics
L4090: • It utilizes two conditions that are Equilibrium and Yield
L4091: • It is a lower bound theorem which states that for any distribution of moments the frame should be safe and admissible
L4092: under the value of load W, where W is less than or equal to collapse load Wu
L4093: • Hence W ≤ Wu,  M ≤ Mp
L4094: Kinematic Theorem
L4095: • It is based on the concept of work done and energy absorbed
L4096: • It utilizes two conditions that are Equilibrium and Mechanism
L4097: • It is a upper bound theorem which states that for a given frame subjected to load W, the W is found to correspond to
L4098: any assumed mechanism, must be either greater or equal to the collapse load Wu
L4099: • Hence W ≥ Wu
