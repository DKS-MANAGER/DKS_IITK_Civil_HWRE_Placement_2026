## 3. General Interview Communication Protocol

1. **Structured Delivery**: Always state your top-level answer first, followed by supporting points (Pyramid Principle).
2. **Listen Actively**: Clarify scope before answering complex technical or case questions.
3. **Quantify Results**: Cite metrics, percentage gains, and error reductions from your actual projects.
4. **Admit Gaps Gracefully**: If you do not know a formula, outline the physical governing principle and your logical approach.
