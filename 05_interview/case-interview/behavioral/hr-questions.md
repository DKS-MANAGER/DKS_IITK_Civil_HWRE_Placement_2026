# Universal HR & Fit Question Bank

> 50+ universal behavioral, motivational, and situational questions with evaluation criteria and model structures for IIT Kanpur campus placements.

---

## 📑 Question Bank Index
- **Category 1**: Self-Introduction & Career Trajectory (Q1 - Q10)
- **Category 2**: Motivation, Company & Role Fit (Q11 - Q20)
- **Category 3**: Leadership & Teamwork (Q21 - Q30)
- **Category 4**: Conflict, Pressure & Resilience (Q31 - Q40)
- **Category 5**: Strengths, Weaknesses & Self-Awareness (Q41 - Q50)

---

## Category 1: Self-Introduction & Career Trajectory

### Q1: "Walk me through your resume in 90 seconds."
- **Model Structure**:
  1. *Foundation*: Strong quantitative and analytical grounding in Civil Engineering at IIT Kanpur.
  2. *Research & Analytics*: Developing predictive models, handling large datasets, and optimizing systems under uncertainty (BridgeRisk / Time-series).
  3. *Leadership & Impact*: Key positions of responsibility (PORs), cross-functional team leadership, or competition achievements.
  4. *Aspiration*: Why this specific company and role represents the natural next step to create tangible business impact.

### Q2: "Why did you choose IIT Kanpur, and how has your time here shaped your thinking?"
- **Key Focus**: Rigorous academic culture, comfort with difficult unstructured problems, resilience, and collaborative peer learning.

## Category 2: Motivation, Company & Role Fit

### Q11: "Why our firm specifically over competitors?"
- **Model Structure**: Cite 3 specific, non-generic pillars:
  1. Practice specialization / industry leadership.
  2. Training, apprenticeship culture, and mentorship programs.
  3. A specific recent initiative, thought leadership report, or corporate transformation project you researched.

### Q12: "Where do you see yourself in 3 to 5 years?"
- **Key Focus**: Mastering the core role in Years 1-2, leading complex problem workstreams in Years 3-4, and mentoring junior analysts while driving measurable client value.

---

## Category 3: Leadership & Teamwork

### Q21: "Tell me about a time you led a team with diverse perspectives."
- **Key Focus**: Active listening, objective evaluation criteria (decision matrices), clear modular ownership, and inclusive consensus building.

### Q22: "How do you handle a team member who is underperforming on a deadline?"
- **Key Focus**: Private empathetic check-in, identifying root bottleneck (skill gap vs personal issue), re-scoping workload, and supporting without compromising delivery standards.

---

## Category 4: Conflict, Pressure & Resilience

### Q31: "Tell me about a time you had a major disagreement with a senior or peer."
- **Key Focus**: Depersonalizing conflict, bringing objective data benchmarks and pilot tests rather than emotional opinions.

### Q32: "Describe how you prioritize when you have 3 critical deadlines due simultaneously."
- **Key Focus**: Eisenhower matrix (Urgent vs Important), stakeholder communication to set realistic expectations, ruthless 80/20 time blocking.

---

## Category 5: Strengths, Weaknesses & Self-Awareness

### Q41: "What is your single greatest professional weakness?"
- **Key Focus**: Authentic weakness (e.g. diving too deep into quantitative details before stepping back for top-down synthesis) + clear actionable system you implemented to manage it.

### Q50: "Do you have any questions for me?"
- **Key Focus**: Thoughtful questions about firm culture, project delivery challenges, or long-term industry evolution.
