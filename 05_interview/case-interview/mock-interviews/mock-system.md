# The Peer-to-Peer Mock Interview System

> Complete operational protocol for conducting high-yield 2-person mock interview sessions before campus recruitment.

---

---

## 2. Interviewer Protocol & Etiquette
1. **Never Give Away the Solution Early**: Let the candidate struggle for 60 seconds with math or structuring before offering a structured hint.
2. **Push on Edge Cases & Sanity Checks**: Ask *"Does that 45 Cr number make intuitive sense given the market size?"*
3. **Score Objectively**: Use the standardized 5-point rubric across Structure, Math, Communication, and Coachability.

---

## 3. Standardized Evaluation Scorecard

| Dimension | Weight | Poor (1-2) | Good (3) | Outstanding (4-5) |
|:---|:---:|:---|:---|:---|
| **Problem Structuring** | 30% | Generic, overlapping, missing MECE | Structured, standard issue tree | Custom, creative, prioritized hypothesis |
| **Quantitative Speed** | 25% | Frequent arithmetic errors, slow | Accurate with paper calculation | Mental speed, estimates bounds quickly |
| **Business Acumen** | 20% | Theoretical, misses commercial reality | Identifies obvious cost/rev drivers | 80/20 instinct, industry-aware insights |
| **Communication & Presence**| 15% | Bottom-up rambling, defensive | Clear, organized responses | Pyramid Principle, crisp synthesis |
| **Coachability** | 10% | Ignores hints, argumentative | Incorporates hints slowly | Seamlessly pivots on interviewer hints |

`[PREPARATION HEURISTIC]` Target Mock Passing Bar: Total Weighted Score $\ge 80\%$.
