# Resume Defense — Line-by-Line Technical Credential Defense

> **Focus**: Defending academic CPI, project bullets, internships, software claims, and handling grilling questions.

---

## 1. Resume Defense Master Rules

1. **Every Bullet is Fair Game**: Interviewers will pick the bullet you least expect. Know the numerical value, software version, formula, and business outcome for every single claim.
2. **The 3-Level Defense**:
   * **Level 1 (What)**: 15-second summary of what you did.
   * **Level 2 (How)**: 45-second explanation of mathematical/computational methods used.
   * **Level 3 (Why)**: Deep explanation of why you selected that specific approach over alternatives.
3. **Never Fake Tools**: If you wrote Python scripts with Pandas, do not claim expert C++ software architect mastery.

---

## 2. Common Grilling Questions & Model Answers

### Question 1: "Why is your B.Tech CPI lower/higher than your M.Tech CPI?"
* **Model Answer**:
  > *"During my undergraduate degree, I explored a very broad curriculum across multiple general disciplines while engaging in campus leadership. When I entered the M.Tech program in Hydraulics and Water Resources at IIT Kanpur, I was able to focus intensely on advanced computational fluid mechanics, hydrology, and scientific programming. My academic performance in M.Tech reflects my strong passion and dedication to my core technical specialization."*

### Question 2: "You have listed Python, SQL, HEC-RAS, and OpenFOAM. Which one are you strongest in, and how do you rate yourself out of 10?"
* **Model Answer**:
  > *"I am strongest in Python for scientific computing and data analysis, which I use daily for hydrological modeling, statistical distributions, and automation. I would rate myself a 7.5 or 8 out of 10 — I am proficient in object-oriented scripting, data processing libraries (NumPy, Pandas, SciPy), and machine learning frameworks, though I recognize there is always more to learn regarding large-scale distributed computing architecture."*
