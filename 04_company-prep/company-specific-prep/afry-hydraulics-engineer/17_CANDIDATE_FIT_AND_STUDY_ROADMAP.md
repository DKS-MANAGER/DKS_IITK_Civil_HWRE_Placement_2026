## 3. The 7-Day Rapid Revision Plan (Emergency Pre-Interview Sprint)

* **Day 1: Open-Channel Hydraulics & GVF**
  * Revise specific energy, critical depth, and Manning equation.
  * Derive differential equation of GVF and review M1/M2/S2 curves.
  * Derive Bélanger equation and energy loss $\Delta E$ for hydraulic jumps.
  * *Study*: [`03_OPEN_CHANNEL_HYDRAULICS.md`](03_OPEN_CHANNEL_HYDRAULICS.md).
* **Day 2: Pressure-Pipe Hydraulics & Losses**
  * Revise Darcy-Weisbach, Colebrook-White, and Swamee-Jain formulas.
  * Review minor loss coefficients (bellmouth, trash racks, bends).
  * Calculate pump head curve and system head curve intersection.
  * *Study*: [`05_PRESSURE_PIPE_HYDRAULICS.md`](05_PRESSURE_PIPE_HYDRAULICS.md).
* **Day 3: Water Hammer & Surge Tanks**
  * Derive Joukowsky equation ($\Delta H = a \Delta v / g$) and acoustic wave speed $a$.
  * Understand rapid vs. slow valve closure and negative pressure column separation.
  * Master surge tank mass oscillation period $T$ and Thoma stability criterion $F_{Th}$.
  * *Study*: [`06_WATER_HAMMER_AND_MASS_OSCILLATION.md`](06_WATER_HAMMER_AND_MASS_OSCILLATION.md).
* **Day 4: Spillways & Energy Dissipators**
  * Review ogee spillway USBR downstream coordinates and crest pressure behavior.
  * Compare USBR Type II vs. Type III stilling basins (baffle pier cavitation limits).
  * Calculate trajectory flip bucket throw distance and Veronese plunge pool scour.
  * *Study*: [`07_SPILLWAYS_AND_HYDRAULIC_STRUCTURES.md`](07_SPILLWAYS_AND_HYDRAULIC_STRUCTURES.md) and [`08_ENERGY_DISSIPATION_SYSTEMS.md`](08_ENERGY_DISSIPATION_SYSTEMS.md).
* **Day 5: HEC-RAS & Bentley HAMMER Core Workflows**
  * Review HEC-RAS 1D boundary conditions (subcritical downstream vs. mixed flow).
  * Understand bridge modeling low flow (energy/momentum) vs. high flow (pressure/weir).
  * Review Bentley HAMMER Method of Characteristics (MOC) grid ($\Delta x = a \Delta t$).
  * *Study*: [`10_HEC_RAS_PREPARATION.md`](10_HEC_RAS_PREPARATION.md) and [`11_BENTLEY_HAMMER_PREPARATION.md`](11_BENTLEY_HAMMER_PREPARATION.md).
* **Day 6: Excel VBA Automation & Aptitude Drill**
  * Review the 10 production VBA scripts (Manning solver, normal depth, jump, Joukowsky).
  * Solve 30 quantitative and hydraulics numerical problems under timed conditions.
  * *Study*: [`12_EXCEL_VBA_HYDRAULIC_AUTOMATION.md`](12_EXCEL_VBA_HYDRAULIC_AUTOMATION.md) and [`13_QUANTITATIVE_APTITUDE_AND_OA.md`](13_QUANTITATIVE_APTITUDE_AND_OA.md).
* **Day 7: Thesis Defense Rehearsal & Mock Interview**
  * Rehearse the 3-minute executive pivot pitch connecting OpenFOAM scour to consulting.
  * Practice answering challenging probes on commercial software gaps and AFRY values.
  * Complete pre-interview verification checklist in [`READINESS_SCORECARD.md`](READINESS_SCORECARD.md).
  * *Study*: [`14_TECHNICAL_INTERVIEW_QUESTION_BANK.md`](14_TECHNICAL_INTERVIEW_QUESTION_BANK.md) and [`16_CANDIDATE_PROJECT_DEFENCE.md`](16_CANDIDATE_PROJECT_DEFENCE.md).
