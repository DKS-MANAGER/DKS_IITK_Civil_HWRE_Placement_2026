## 3. Compensation Breakdown & Evidentiary Anomaly Analysis

The figures entered into the IIT Kanpur recruitment proforma are recorded below, followed by an immediate audit of mathematical and unit inconsistencies:

| Compensation Item | Recorded Proforma Value | Evidentiary Status & Verification Finding |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Cost to Company (CTC)** | **₹7,20,000 per annum (INR 7.20 LPA)** | `[TIER 1 / PROFORMA FACT]` — Confirmed baseline gross CTC. |
| **Gross Salary** | **₹6,76,800 per annum** | `[TIER 1 / PROFORMA FACT]` — Difference from CTC is ₹43,200 (exact employer EPF contribution: $12\% \times ₹3,60,000 = ₹43,200$). |
| **Fixed Take-Home Salary** | **₹52,800 per annum (Ambiguous)** | `[TIER 1 / UNIT INCONSISTENCY]` — Severe mathematical discrepancy flagged below. |
| **Base Salary** | **₹30,000 (Ambiguous)** | `[TIER 1 / UNIT INCONSISTENCY]` — Monthly vs. annual entry error flagged below. |

### Critical Compensation Audit & Inconsistency Analysis
1. **The Take-Home Inconsistency**:
   * The proforma states "Fixed take-home salary per annum: ₹52,800".
   * If ₹52,800 were annual, the monthly take-home would be an absurd $\frac{₹52,800}{12} = ₹4,400\text{/month}$, which is legally and practically impossible for an M.Tech engineering role with a ₹6.768 LPA gross pay.
   * **Inference**: In Indian campus placement forms, recruiters frequently enter **monthly figures into annual fields**. A monthly take-home of **₹52,800/month** yields an annual take-home of:
     $$12 \times ₹52,800 = ₹6,33,600\text{ per annum}$$
   * Comparing ₹6,33,600 annual take-home with ₹6,76,800 gross salary yields total annual deductions of:
     $$₹6,76,800 - ₹6,33,600 = ₹43,200$$
     This ₹43,200 corresponds exactly to the employee's mandatory provident fund (EPF) deduction ($12\%$ of ₹3,60,000 base).
2. **The Base Salary Inconsistency**:
   * The proforma lists "Base salary: ₹30,000".
   * This is clearly a **monthly base salary** (i.e., ₹30,000/month $\times 12 = ₹3,60,000$ annual basic pay), which constitutes exactly $50\%$ of the ₹7.20 LPA package—the standard statutory benchmark mandated under Indian Wage Code regulations.
3. **Actionable Directive**: The candidate must verify the final offer letter annexure with the IITK Placement Office, recognizing that ₹52,800 represents the intended monthly net credit, not the annual sum.
