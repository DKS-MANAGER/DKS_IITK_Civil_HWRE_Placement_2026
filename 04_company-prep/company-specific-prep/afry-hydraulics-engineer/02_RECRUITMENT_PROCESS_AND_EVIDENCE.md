## 3. Candidate Interview Debrief Analysis & Verified Feedback

An analysis of candidate feedback from premier Indian institutions and engineering consultancy interview debriefs reveals several clear recruiter priorities:

### 3.1 Primary Focus Areas Reported by Candidates
1. **Derivations Over Fact Recall**:
   * Recruiters do not ask for trivia. They ask candidates to draw a control volume and derive governing equations from first principles (e.g., *“Derive the momentum equation for a hydraulic jump in a horizontal rectangular channel and prove the Bélanger equation for conjugate depths”*).
2. **Physical Understanding of Fluid Behavior**:
   * Questions test the physical intuition behind numbers: *“What happens to the pressure on an ogee spillway crest if the actual discharging head exceeds the design head? Why does cavitation occur, and how do we prevent it?”*
3. **Software Fundamentals vs Black-Box Tooling**:
   * Interviewers frequently test whether candidates understand what happens *inside* software: *“In HEC-RAS, how does the engine calculate the water surface profile across a bridge? When does it switch from the energy method to the momentum or pressure-flow orifice equation?”*
   * *“In Bentley HAMMER, what is the Courant criterion for transient wave speed and pipeline segment length $\Delta x = a \Delta t$?”*
4. **Honesty on Software Exposure**:
   * If a candidate has not used Bentley HAMMER commercially, attempting to bluff will backfire immediately. Candidates who explain the underlying **Method of Characteristics (MOC)** and water hammer physics, while stating: *“I have mastered the governing equations, wave propagation physics, and transient boundary conditions during my M.Tech HWRE, and I have prepared through official Bentley documentation to build models immediately,”* receive top evaluation scores.

## 5. Candidate Evaluation Rubric

During technical interview rounds, the AFRY evaluation panel scores candidates across four distinct dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight | Scoring Benchmark ($\ge 80\%$ required for selection) |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulic Fundamentals** | **35%** | Flawless derivations of open-channel flow, Saint-Venant equations, pipe friction, hydraulic jump, and water hammer; deep grasp of governing assumptions. |
| **2. Hydraulic Structures & Transient Systems** | **25%** | Accurate sizing and design knowledge for spillways, stilling basins, flip buckets, surge tanks (Thoma criterion), and penstock pressure envelopes. |
| **3. Software Logic & Automation Acumen** | **20%** | Clear comprehension of HEC-RAS 1D algorithms, Bentley HAMMER MOC mechanics, and Excel VBA subroutine programming for engineering automation. |
| **4. Research Depth, Defense & Communication** | **20%** | Articulate defense of M.Tech thesis (CFD sediment transport and scour modeling in OpenFOAM), seamless pivot to consulting practice, and executive clarity aligned with AFRY values. |
