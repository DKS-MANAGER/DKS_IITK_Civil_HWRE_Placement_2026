## 1. Executive Summary & Mission

This preparation hub provides an authoritative, mathematically rigorous, and evidence-audited training curriculum for the **Hydraulics Engineer** campus recruitment drive by **AFRY India Private Limited** at IIT Kanpur (Placement 2026–27 Phase 1).

AFRY (formed by the historic merger of Sweden's **ÅF** and Finland's **Pöyry**) is one of the world's premier engineering, design, and advisory consultancies in **hydropower, dams, water infrastructure, tunneling, and hydraulic transient modeling**. Operating out of its major Indian delivery center in Noida, AFRY delivers feasibility studies, detailed engineering design, and Owner's Engineering services for flagship hydropower developments across India (NHPC, SJVNL, HPPCL) and globally.

This suite is strictly aligned with the supplied IIT Kanpur placement proforma, which explicitly mandates expertise in:
1. **1D water profile estimation and river rating curves**
2. **Steady and unsteady open-channel flow analysis (Saint-Venant equations)**
3. **Steady and unsteady pressure-pipe flow analysis (Darcy-Weisbach & Colebrook-White)**
4. **Head-loss calculations in pressure conduits and minor fittings**
5. **Mass oscillation and water hammer in pressurized water systems**
6. **Hydraulic energy dissipation systems (trajectory bucket, roller bucket, stilling basin)**
7. **Spillways (ogee spillway, sluice-type spillway, broad-crested weir)**
8. **Hydraulic model studies and similitude laws**
9. **Software Proficiency: HEC-RAS, Bentley HAMMER, and MS Excel VBA automation**
