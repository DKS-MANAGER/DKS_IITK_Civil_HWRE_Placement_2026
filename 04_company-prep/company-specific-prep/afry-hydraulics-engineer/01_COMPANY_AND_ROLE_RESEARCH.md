## 1. Corporate Heritage & Global Business Model

### 1.1 Foundation & The ÅF–Pöyry Merger
**AFRY AB** is a global Swedish-Finnish engineering, design, and advisory company headquartered in Stockholm, Sweden (listed on Nasdaq Stockholm under the ticker `AFRY`). The corporate entity in its current structure is the result of a landmark merger in **February 2019** between two European engineering powerhouses:

1. **ÅF (Aktiebolaget Stockholms Ångpanneförening)**: Founded in **1895** in Malmö/Stockholm, Sweden, originally established to verify the safety and efficiency of industrial steam boilers. Over 125 years, ÅF evolved into a northern European market leader in energy infrastructure, industrial engineering, and sustainable design.
2. **Pöyry PLC**: Founded in **1958** in Helsinki, Finland, by the visionary engineer Dr. Jaakko Pöyry. Pöyry achieved preeminent global fame as one of the world's most distinguished consulting engineering firms in **hydropower, dams, water resources, tunneling, and forestry/process industries**.

Upon completion of the merger in 2019, the unified multinational rebranded as **AFRY**, combining the letters "ÅF" and "Pöyry" to symbolize an integrated technological consultancy of ~19,000 specialists operating across more than 50 countries globally.

### 3.2 Strategic Importance of Pumped Storage Plants (PSPs)
India has embarked on an ambitious target of installing 500 GW of non-fossil electricity capacity by 2030. Because solar and wind power are inherently intermittent, **Pumped Storage Hydropower Plants (PSPs)** represent the only cost-effective, grid-scale bulk energy storage technology capable of rapid response. 

AFRY is a primary consultant for PSP feasibility, hydraulic transient safety, and headrace/tailrace waterway optimization in India. In PSP systems:
* The water conveyance system operates in **bi-directional flow** (turbining mode down to the lower reservoir, and pumping mode up to the upper reservoir).
* Water hammer surges, rapid load rejection, pump-trip transients, and mass oscillations in surge tanks become critical structural constraints.
* This explains why the proforma explicitly demands expertise in **mass oscillation, water hammer, and Bentley HAMMER modeling**.

## 6. Detailed Analysis of the Hydraulics Engineer Role

### 6.1 The Role Mandate at AFRY Noida
As a **Hydraulics Engineer** within the Noida team, the candidate will report to a Principal Hydraulic Engineer or Project Engineering Manager. The core mission is to transform conceptual fluid transport problems into verified, code-compliant, and constructible hydraulic designs.

### 6.2 Key Day-to-Day Engineering Responsibilities
1. **River Modeling & Rating Curve Generation**:
   * Developing 1D steady and unsteady hydrodynamic models in **HEC-RAS** for natural rivers, diversion channels, and tailrace outlets.
   * Extracting stage-discharge rating curves ($Q = C (h - h_0)^\beta$) to set turbine centerline elevations, tailwater levels, and flood inundation boundaries.
2. **Pressure Conduit & Waterway Sizing**:
   * Calculating friction losses using the Darcy-Weisbach and Colebrook-White equations across varied tunnel lining types (shotcrete, concrete, steel, GRP).
   * Calculating minor losses across bellmouth intakes, trash racks, bends, bifurcations, trifurcations, gate slots, and expansion transitions.
3. **Transient Hydraulic Analysis**:
   * Simulating emergency shutdown of hydraulic turbines, rapid valve closures, and pump trips using **Bentley HAMMER**.
   * Determining maximum transient pressure envelopes (to design penstock steel shell thickness) and minimum pressure envelopes (to prevent vapor cavity formation, sub-atmospheric buckling, and cavitation).
   * Calculating mass oscillation water levels in surge tanks and verifying Thoma dynamic stability.
4. **Spillway & Energy Dissipator Design**:
   * Generating mathematical profile coordinates for ogee spillways under design head ($H_d$) and checking for negative crest pressures under super-flood conditions.
   * Designing hydraulic jump stilling basins (USBR Type II/III/IV) or trajectory flip buckets with plunge pool scour estimation.
5. **Excel VBA Automation**:
   * Developing validated in-house engineering calculation workbooks utilizing Visual Basic for Applications (VBA) to standardize repetitive hydraulic workflows, perform iterative normal-depth solvers, and generate calculation reports for client submission.
6. **Physical Model Study Coordination**:
   * Formulating specifications for physical hydraulic scale model tests (typically 1:25 to 1:50 scale models executed at research institutes like CWPRS Pune or university laboratories) and correlating physical results with numerical simulations.
