## 2. Five Explicit Job Responsibilities (`[JD VERIFIED]`)

The official job description explicitly defines five core responsibilities for this role:

### 1. Diagnose Business Challenges & Design Enterprise Solutions
* **JD Statement**: *"Diagnose client business challenges and design enterprise architecture and digital solutions that deliver measurable outcomes."*
* **What You Actually Do**: Meet with client business unit heads, analyze current workflows and system bottlenecks, construct MECE issue trees, and design multi-tier target enterprise architectures that achieve quantified business KPIs.

### 2. Lead Selection & Design of Cutting-Edge Technologies
* **JD Statement**: *"Lead the selection and design of cutting-edge technology solutions — Cloud, AI/Data Analytics, Industry X (Manufacturing Digitalization), IoT, XR, Customer Experience, and more — tailored to each client's strategic priorities."*
* **What You Actually Do**: Objectively evaluate competing cloud platforms (AWS vs Azure vs GCP), AI architectures (RAG vs fine-tuned models), and industrial IoT sensor stacks. Create structured trade-off matrices considering cost, security, latency, and vendor lock-in.

### 3. Translate Technology Possibilities into Actionable Strategies
* **JD Statement**: *"Translate complex technology possibilities into actionable business strategies, guiding clients from vision through to execution."*
* **What You Actually Do**: Present technical concepts (e.g., event-driven microservices, vector search indexing, edge computing) in executive business terms that C-level decision-makers can understand and sponsor.

### 4. Define What Should Be Built — Not Just How to Build It
* **JD Statement**: *"Define what should be built — not just how to build it — leveraging AI and automation to enhance quality and accelerate delivery."*
* **What You Actually Do**: Lead upstream requirements engineering, write functional specifications, define user stories and acceptance criteria, and utilize GenAI/automation tooling to accelerate delivery timelines.

### 5. Drive the Full Consulting Lifecycle
* **JD Statement**: *"Drive the full consulting lifecycle: problem diagnosis, solution design, implementation oversight, and value realization."*
* **What You Actually Do**: Stay with the transformation program from initial diagnostic workshops through vendor procurement, agile sprint oversight, user acceptance testing (UAT), change management, and post-launch KPI measurement.

### 5.1 The 6 Architectural Evaluation Criteria
When recommending enterprise technologies, digital consultants evaluate competing options against six objective dimensions:
1. **Business Feasibility & Strategic Fit**: Does the technology directly move the target business KPI, or is it an over-engineered novelty?
2. **Security, Privacy & Compliance**: Does it satisfy data residency mandates (e.g. Japan Financial Services Agency regulations, GDPR, industrial IP protection)?
3. **Integration & Legacy Compatibility**: How does the new system communicate with 30-year-old on-premises mainframes, ERPs (SAP), or industrial PLCs?
4. **Maintainability & Organizational Skill**: Can the client's internal IT team operate and support the platform after Accenture rolls off, or does it create dangerous vendor dependency?
5. **Scalability & Elasticity**: Can the architecture handle 10x sensor data volume or holiday traffic surges without catastrophic latency spikes?
6. **Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) & Payback**: Does the 5-year CapEx + OpEx yield an internal rate of return (IRR) exceeding the client's corporate hurdle rate?

## 6. End-to-End Teaching Case: Manufacturing Downtime & Predictive Maintenance

To understand how digital consulting operates in practice, study this comprehensive, realistic engagement model:

### 1. The Business Context & Symptom
A Tier-1 Japanese automotive parts manufacturer operating 4 production facilities across Kanto and Tokai manufactures high-precision transmission gears. Over the past 24 months, **unplanned machining downtime surged by 22%**, resulting in:
* **JPY 480 Million** in annual lost gross margin (idle factory labor + lost output).
* **JPY 120 Million** in expedited airfreight penalties to avoid halting Toyota assembly lines.
* **Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)** depressed at **68%** (world-class benchmark is $\ge 85\%$).

### 2. Diagnostic Analysis & Root-Cause Triage
The consulting team conducts an on-site operational diagnostic:
* **Current Maintenance Model**: Purely *calendar-based preventive maintenance* (technicians replace spindle bearings every 1,000 operating hours regardless of condition).
* **Failure Pattern Breakdown**:
  * $65\%$ of breakdowns occur *prematurely* between hours 400 and 800 due to micro-vibrations and coolant degradation causing thermal expansion.
  * $35\%$ of replaced bearings were perfectly healthy when discarded at hour 1,000, wasting parts inventory.
* **MTBF & MTTR Reality**: Mean Time Between Failures ($\text{MTBF}$) is 320 operating hours; Mean Time to Repair ($\text{MTTR}$) is 14 hours because spare bearings must be fetched from a central warehouse.

### 3. Technology Evaluation Matrix (Trade-Off Analysis)
The consultant evaluates three potential architectural options:

| Option | Architecture Description | CapEx (Initial) | Annual OpEx | Downtime Reduction | 3-Year TCO | Implementation Risk | Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Option A: Status Quo + Increased Inspections** | Hire 12 additional maintenance technicians to inspect machines manually every 4 hours | JPY 5M | JPY 96M | 15% | JPY 293M | High labor shortage risk in Japan; human inspection cannot detect internal bearing micro-fractures | **Rejected** |
| **Option B: Full On-Premises Industrial SCADA** | Deploy proprietary on-premise industrial servers and hardwired vibration sensors across all plants | JPY 240M | JPY 30M | 60% | JPY 330M | Heavy initial CapEx; rigid architecture; lacks cloud machine learning capabilities | **Rejected** |
| **Option C: Hybrid Cloud-Edge IoT + Predictive Maintenance (Recommended)** | Retrofit wireless magnetic vibration/temperature sensors; ingest telemetry via factory Edge gateways; run anomaly detection on AWS IoT Core | **JPY 65M** | **JPY 28M** | **70%** | **JPY 149M** | Low CapEx; rapid 90-day pilot; elastic ML analytics; integrates directly with corporate SAP ERP | **Selected** |

### 5. Financial Business Case & Value Realization
* **Gross Annual Savings**:
  * $70\%$ reduction in unplanned breakdown losses: $\text{JPY } 480\text{M} \times 0.70 = \mathbf{\text{JPY } 336\text{M}}$.
  * $80\%$ reduction in emergency airfreight fines: $\text{JPY } 120\text{M} \times 0.80 = \mathbf{\text{JPY } 96\text{M}}$.
  * Total Gross Annual Benefit $= 336 + 96 = \mathbf{\text{JPY } 432\text{M/year}}$.
* **Investment & Operational Costs**:
  * Year 1 Implementation Cost (CapEx + Consulting Fees) $= \text{JPY } 110\text{M}$.
  * Annual Cloud & Sensor Maintenance OpEx $= \text{JPY } 28\text{M/year}$.
* **Financial Metrics**:
  * **Net Year 1 Financial Gain**: $\text{JPY } 432\text{M} - (\text{JPY } 110\text{M} + \text{JPY } 28\text{M}) = \mathbf{\text{JPY } 294\text{M}}$.
  * **Payback Period**: $\frac{\text{JPY } 110\text{M}}{\text{JPY } 432\text{M} - \text{JPY } 28\text{M}} \times 12\text{ months} \approx \mathbf{3.3\text{ Months}}$.
  * **3-Year Return on Investment (ROI)**:
    $$\text{3-Year Net Benefit} = (432 \times 3) - (110 + 28 \times 3) = 1,296 - 194 = \mathbf{\text{JPY } 1,102\text{M}} \quad (\mathbf{568\%\text{ ROI}})$$

### 6. Phased Implementation Roadmap & Governance
* **Phase 1: Proof of Value (Months 1–3)**:
  * Deploy on 10 critical CNC milling machines on Plant 1 Line A.
  * Validate telemetry streaming, calibrate ML vibration baselines, and verify zero false alerts during normal shift changeovers.
* **Phase 2: Plant 1 Full Rollout & SAP Integration (Months 4–7)**:
  * Scale to all 60 machines in Plant 1. Integrate automated maintenance ticketing with client's SAP PM (Plant Maintenance) module.
  * Train shop floor maintenance technicians on mobile diagnostic tablets.
* **Phase 3: Multi-Plant Expansion & Fleet Optimization (Months 8–14)**:
  * Deploy across remaining 3 manufacturing plants (180 additional machines).
  * Establish centralized enterprise telemetry monitoring dashboard in Tokyo headquarters.

### 7. Governance, Change Management & Common Pitfalls
* **Shop-Floor Skepticism**: Senior factory foremen may distrust automated AI alerts. **Mitigation**: Co-design tablet interfaces with senior technicians; display raw physical vibration spectrums alongside the AI confidence score.
* **Edge Network Outages**: Factory Wi-Fi occasionally drops during heavy welding operations. **Mitigation**: Deploy edge gateways with local flash memory caching up to 48 hours of telemetry, automatically reconciling with the cloud upon reconnection.
