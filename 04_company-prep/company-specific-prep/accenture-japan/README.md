## 5. Structured Study Plans (30-Day, 14-Day & 7-Day Tracks)

### Track A: 30-Day Comprehensive Mastery Track (Recommended)
* **Days 1–5 (Foundations & Role)**: Read [`ROLE.md`](ROLE.md), [`PLACEMENT_FACTS.md`](PLACEMENT_FACTS.md), and [`SYLLABUS_AND_COVERAGE.md`](SYLLABUS_AND_COVERAGE.md). Master the 5-way consulting taxonomy and upstream translation pipeline. Study the end-to-end CNC Spindle Downtime Case.
* **Days 6–10 (Quantitative & SQL)**: Solve all 20 Consulting Math problems in [`TECHNICAL_TEST.md#4-section-3-consulting-mathematics--unit-economics`](TECHNICAL_TEST.md). Implement Problems 1–15 in SQL (joins, aggregations, CTEs).
* **Days 11–15 (Advanced SQL & Python)**: Complete SQL Problems 16–30 (Window functions, streaks, deduplication). Study Python foundations and solve Problems 1–10 in [`TECHNICAL_TEST.md#3-section-2-python-coding--algorithmic-logic`](TECHNICAL_TEST.md) using the 12-component structure.
* **Days 16–20 (Python Algorithms & Cases 1–4)**: Complete Python Problems 11–20. Master Cases 1–4 in [`CONSULTING_CASES.md`](CONSULTING_CASES.md) (Smart Factory, GenAI RAG, Banking Cloud Migration, Omnichannel Retail).
* **Days 21–25 (Cloud, AI & Tech Questions)**: Drill Sections 1, 3, and 4 in [`QUESTION_BANK.md`](QUESTION_BANK.md) (Cloud shared responsibility, Strangler Fig, RAG vs Fine-Tuning, CAP Theorem).
* **Days 26–28 (Behavioral, Japan & Project Defense)**: Prepare 5 authentic STAR stories in [`BEHAVIOURAL_HR.md`](BEHAVIOURAL_HR.md). Drill project defense questions in [`PROJECT_STRATEGY.md`](PROJECT_STRATEGY.md). Review Japanese business etiquette (*Ho-Ren-So*) in [`JAPAN_RELOCATION_AND_LANGUAGE.md`](JAPAN_RELOCATION_AND_LANGUAGE.md).
* **Days 29–30 (Full Mock Assessment & Revision)**: Complete timed diagnostic evaluation in [`READINESS_SCORECARD.md`](READINESS_SCORECARD.md). Revise high-yield formulas and cheat sheets in [`RAPID_REVISION.md`](RAPID_REVISION.md).

### Track B: 14-Day Accelerated Core Track
* **Day 1–2**: [`ROLE.md`](ROLE.md) + [`PLACEMENT_FACTS.md`](PLACEMENT_FACTS.md).
* **Day 3–4**: [`TECHNICAL_TEST.md`](TECHNICAL_TEST.md) Section 3 (Consulting Math) + Section 1 (SQL Problems 1–15).
* **Day 5–6**: [`TECHNICAL_TEST.md`](TECHNICAL_TEST.md) Section 1 (SQL Problems 16–30) + Section 2 (Python Problems 1–8).
* **Day 7–8**: [`CONSULTING_CASES.md`](CONSULTING_CASES.md) Cases 1–4 + 5 MECE drills.
* **Day 9–10**: [`QUESTION_BANK.md`](QUESTION_BANK.md) Level 1–3 Tech questions + Cloud & AI sections.
* **Day 11–12**: [`BEHAVIOURAL_HR.md`](BEHAVIOURAL_HR.md) STAR stories + [`PROJECT_STRATEGY.md`](PROJECT_STRATEGY.md).
* **Day 13–14**: [`INTERVIEW.md`](INTERVIEW.md) Round 1/2 drills + [`RAPID_REVISION.md`](RAPID_REVISION.md).

### Track C: 7-Day High-Intensity Sprint
* **Day 1**: Role & Foundations: [`ROLE.md`](ROLE.md) + [`PLACEMENT_FACTS.md`](PLACEMENT_FACTS.md).
* **Day 2**: Consulting Math & SQL Core: [`TECHNICAL_TEST.md`](TECHNICAL_TEST.md) Math + Top 10 SQL queries.
* **Day 3**: Python Core: Sliding window, Hash maps, Stack, Two-pointer in [`TECHNICAL_TEST.md`](TECHNICAL_TEST.md).
* **Day 4**: Digital Cases: Case 1 (Smart Factory) and Case 2 (RAG Insurer) in [`CONSULTING_CASES.md`](CONSULTING_CASES.md).
* **Day 5**: Tech Q&A: Cloud migration, REST vs Kafka, RAG in [`QUESTION_BANK.md`](QUESTION_BANK.md).
* **Day 6**: Behavioral & Project Defense: Top 3 STAR stories + Engineering Thesis defense.
* **Day 7**: Emergency Revision: [`RAPID_REVISION.md`](RAPID_REVISION.md) 60-minute review guide.
