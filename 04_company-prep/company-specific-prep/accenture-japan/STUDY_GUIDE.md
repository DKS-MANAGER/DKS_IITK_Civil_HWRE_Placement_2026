## 2. Study Tracks by Time Available

### Track A: 30-Day Comprehensive Pathway (Recommended)
* **Week 1 (Days 1–7)**: Modules 01 (Quant Math) and 02 (Python DSA). Attempt first 10 code problems.
* **Week 2 (Days 8–14)**: Module 03 (SQL Analytics & Database Arch). Write all 20 queries from scratch.
* **Week 3 (Days 15–21)**: Module 04 (Digital Technologies: Cloud, AI/GenAI & Security).
* **Week 4 (Days 22–30)**: Module 05 (6 Consulting Cases & Japan Culture) + Module 06 (Full 90-min Mock Test).

### Track B: 14-Day Accelerated Sprint
* **Days 1–4**: Modules 01 (Commercial Math) and 02 (Python Algorithms).
* **Days 5–8**: Modules 03 (SQL Analytics) and 04 (Digital Tech & Cloud).
* **Days 9–11**: Module 05 (6 Consulting Cases, Hou-Ren-So & STAR stories).
* **Days 12–14**: Mock Assessment in Module 06 and Rapid Revision Formula Sheet.

### Track C: 72-Hour Rapid Cram
* Focus strictly on:
  1. [`06_MOCK_TESTS_QUESTION_BANK_AND_RAPID_REVISION.md`](06_MOCK_TESTS_QUESTION_BANK_AND_RAPID_REVISION.md) (Section C: Formula Sheet & Quick SQL).
  2. [`03_SQL_DATABASE_ARCH_AND_ENTERPRISE_ANALYTICS.md`](03_SQL_DATABASE_ARCH_AND_ENTERPRISE_ANALYTICS.md) (Section B: Top 10 Window Queries).
  3. [`02_PYTHON_ALGORITHMIC_LOGIC_AND_CODE_PUZZLES.md`](02_PYTHON_ALGORITHMIC_LOGIC_AND_CODE_PUZZLES.md) (Sliding Window, Binary Search, Merge Intervals).
  4. [`05_CONSULTING_CASES_JAPAN_CULTURE_AND_BEHAVIORAL.md`](05_CONSULTING_CASES_JAPAN_CULTURE_AND_BEHAVIORAL.md) (Cases 1, 2, 3 & Hou-Ren-So / Meishi Etiquette).
