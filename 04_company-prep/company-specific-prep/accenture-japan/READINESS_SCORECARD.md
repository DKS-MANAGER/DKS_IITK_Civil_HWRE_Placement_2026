  * *Completion Metric*: Construct a 3-level MECE tree for an ambiguous business problem in $< 90\text{ seconds}$.
* **Days 13–18: SQL & Enterprise Data Manipulation**
  * *Topics*: Relational joins, aggregations, CTEs, window functions (`ROW_NUMBER`, `DENSE_RANK`, `LAG`, `LEAD`).
  * *Tasks*: Code and verify all 30 SQL queries against the synthetic schema (`TECHNICAL_TEST.md`).
  * *Completion Metric*: Formulate complex CTE with window functions in $< 5\text{ minutes}$.
* **Days 19–24: Python Algorithmic Logic & Cloud/AI Architectures**
  * *Topics*: Sliding window, two pointers, prefix sums, hash maps; Cloud IaaS/PaaS/SaaS, RAG pipelines.
  * *Tasks*: Solve all 20 Python coding problems (`TECHNICAL_TEST.md`); review 20 Cloud and 20 AI questions (`QUESTION_BANK.md`).
  * *Completion Metric*: Write bug-free $O(N)$ Python code for sliding-window problem in $< 8\text{ minutes}$.
* **Days 25–28: Project Defense, Behavioral STAR Stories & Japan Readiness**
  * *Topics*: Real IITK research defense (`PROJECT_STRATEGY.md`), 5 STAR stories (`BEHAVIOURAL_HR.md`), Japanese language plan.
  * *Tasks*: Rehearse 10 project-defense question sets; rehearse 18-month Japanese language roadmap.
  * *Completion Metric*: Defend thesis methodology and transition smoothly to digital consulting in $< 2\text{ minutes}$.
* **Days 29–30: Full Timed Mocks & Readiness Scorecard Benchmarking**
  * *Tasks*: Conduct two 30-minute timed mock interviews (Manager Round & Director Round); complete self-scorecard.
  * *Completion Metric*: Achieve composite score $\ge 85/100$ on the readiness scorecard.
