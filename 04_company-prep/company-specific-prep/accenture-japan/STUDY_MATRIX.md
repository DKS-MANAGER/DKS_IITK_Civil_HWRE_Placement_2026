## 1. Prioritization & Depth Taxonomy

* **Priority Levels**:
  * `[P1] — Essential Core`: Non-negotiable capabilities tested across both Manager and Director interview rounds.
  * `[P2] — Important Supporting`: Technical and analytical capabilities evaluated in online tests and architecture discussions.
  * `[P3] — Specializations`: Domain-specific topics (Industry X, IoT, advanced cloud) that distinguish top candidates.
* **Mastery Levels**:
  * **L1 (Awareness)**: Can define the concept and state its business purpose.
  * **L2 (Working)**: Understands mechanics, standard architectures, and can solve structured problems.
  * **L3 (Interview Ready)**: Can defend choices, articulate trade-offs, and solve live cases/queries under time limits.
  * **L4 (Strong Candidate)**: Synthesizes ambiguous business-technology challenges, handles partner pushback, and leads executive recommendations.
