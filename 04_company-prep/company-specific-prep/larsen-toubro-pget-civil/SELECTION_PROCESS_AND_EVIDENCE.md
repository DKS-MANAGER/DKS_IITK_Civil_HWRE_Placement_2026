## 2. Stage 2 Online Assessment (OA) Deep-Dive

### 2.1 Test Architecture & Sectional Breakdown

The preliminary assessment is typically delivered via enterprise proctoring platforms (Aon CoCubes, Mercer Mettl, or Wheebox):

* **Total Duration:** 120 Minutes
* **Total Questions:** 120 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
* **Marking Scheme:** Typically +1 mark for correct answers; 0 or -0.25 negative marking depending on test vendor (always read instructions).
* **Sectional Time Limits:** Test is usually partitioned into two 60-minute blocks.

## 3. Stages 3 & 4: Interview Intelligence

### 3.1 Technical Interview Panel Dynamics

The technical panel typically comprises 2 to 3 senior corporate executives:
1. **Chief Structural / Design Engineer (EDC):** Probes theoretical fundamentals, IS 456 stress blocks, moment distribution, shear center, dynamic loading, and STAAD modeling.
2. **Senior Project Director / Construction Manager:** Probes practical execution, concrete batching plant operations, slump testing, honeycombing remediation, cold joints, formwork striking times, pile load test interpretation, and construction safety.

**Key Technical Focus Areas for M.Tech Candidates:**
* *M.Tech Research Thesis:* You must explain your research problem statement in 2 minutes, detail the analytical/numerical/experimental methodology, and explicitly connect its value to L&T's infrastructure projects.
* *Bar Bending Schedule (BBS):* Calculating cutting lengths of bent-up bars, crank allowance ($0.42 d$), lap lengths ($L_d = 0.87 f_y \phi / (4 \tau_{bd})$), and stirrup hook lengths ($10d$ or $24d$).
* *Concrete Quality & Mix Design:* IS 10262:2019 steps, target mean strength ($f'_{ck} = f_{ck} + 1.65 s$), water-cement ratio curve, mineral admixtures (fly ash, GGBS, silica fume), and non-destructive testing (Rebound hammer per IS 13311 Part 2, UPV per IS 13311 Part 1).

### 3.2 HR Interview & Fitment Verification

The HR interview assesses psychological readiness for heavy civil construction environments:
* **The "Site Reality" Test:** L&T HR intentionally tests candidates on whether they can handle remote project sites (e.g., desert solar parks, Northeast tunnel portals, or offshore bridges) with 6-day workweeks and 10–12 hour shifts during critical pours.
* **Mobility Integrity:** Testing if the candidate will accept posting in any state across India without family objections.
* **Ethics & Safety Stance:** Will the candidate halt a critical concrete pour if safety scaffolding or rebar cover is compromised, even if the client or subcontractor resists?
