## 1. Company Identity & Construction Business Verticals

Larsen & Toubro Limited (NSE: LT, BSE: 500510) is an Indian multinational conglomerate headquartered in L&T House, Ballard Estate, Mumbai, Maharashtra. With an annual consolidated order book exceeding **₹4.75 Lakh Crores ($57+ Billion USD)** and consolidated revenues exceeding **₹2.2 Lakh Crores**, L&T is ranked consistently among the world's top 15 contractors by *Engineering News-Record (ENR)*.

   * *Specialization:* Mega-scale underground and elevated metro rail networks, long-span marine bridges, tunnels (NATM & TBM), hydroelectric dams, nuclear power reactor containment structures, and defense infrastructure.

2. **Buildings & Factories IC (B&F IC):**
   * *Specialization:* Iconic towers, modern commercial complexes, hyperscale data centers, institutional campuses, superspecialty hospitals, airport terminals, and precast factories.

5. **Minerals & Metals IC (M&M IC):**
   * *Specialization:* EPC execution of iron & steel manufacturing plants, aluminum smelters, copper processing complexes, industrial cement plants, and bulk material handling systems.
6. **Power Transmission & Distribution IC (PT&D IC):**
   * *Specialization:* Extra-high-voltage (EHV) substations (765kV/400kV GIS & AIS), long-distance transmission line grids, utility-scale solar photovoltaic farms, and battery energy storage systems (BESS).

## 2. PGET Role Architecture & Career Trajectory

### 2.1 Role Purpose: What Does an L&T PGET Actually Do?

At L&T, candidates inducted with a Master of Technology (M.Tech) degree in Civil Engineering are designated as **Post Graduate Engineer Trainees (PGET)**. PGETs occupy an accelerated technical track compared to Graduate Engineer Trainees (GETs with B.Tech):

* **Design Engineering & Technical Coordination:** Checking structural analysis models (STAAD.Pro, ETABS, SAP2000), reviewing consultant Good-for-Construction (GFC) drawings, validating temporary works engineering (formwork, falsework, staging, cofferdams), and resolving Requests for Information (RFIs).
* **EPC Site Execution & Quality Management:** Supervising large-scale concreting pours (self-compacting concrete, mass concrete thermistor monitoring), bar bending schedules (BBS), pre-stressing and post-tensioning operations, deep foundation piling, and enforcing rigorous ISO 9001 QA/QC inspection testing plans (ITP).
* **Project Planning & Controls:** Utilizing Primavera P6 and MS Project for critical path method (CPM) scheduling, resource allocation, monthly progress billing, joint measurement certification with client representatives, and subcontractor management.
* **Health, Safety & Environment (HSE):** Implementing zero-harm policies, hazard identification and risk assessment (HIRA), Permit-to-Work (PTW) protocols, and environmental impact controls.


## 4. Academic Eligibility Criteria & Mobility Rules

`[OFFICIAL COMPANY POLICY — STRICT RECRUITMENT BENCHMARK]`

L&T enforces strict, non-negotiable eligibility criteria during campus shortlisting:

1. **Academic Performance:**
   * **10th Standard (SSC):** Minimum **65.0%** aggregate.
   * **12th Standard (HSC) / Diploma:** Minimum **65.0%** aggregate.
   * **Graduation (B.Tech / B.E. Civil):** Minimum **65.0%** aggregate or **6.50 / 10.0 CGPA** (without rounding off).
   * **Post-Graduation (M.Tech Civil):** Minimum **6.50 / 10.0 CGPA** across completed semesters.
2. **Backlog Rules:**
   * **Zero Standing / Active Backlogs:** Candidate must not have any pending backlogs at the time of appearing for the online test or joining.
   * **Past Cleared Backlogs:** Must have cleared all past backlogs within the regular course duration.
3. **Medical Fitness:**
   * Candidate must pass the pre-employment medical test conducted by L&T-authorized medical practitioners (color vision, blood parameters, physical fitness for site conditions).
4. **Mobility & Relocation Commitment:**
   * **PAN-India Mobility:** Strict willingness to work anywhere across India (remote project sites, coastal zones, mountain tunnels, or urban metro projects) is an absolute mandatory condition of employment. Candidates refusing site postings are disqualified.
5. **Service Agreement:**
   * L&T recruits under a standard **2-year service agreement** (1-year training + 1-year service) with a training indemnity cost recovery clause (typically ₹1,50,000 – ₹2,00,000) if the candidate leaves voluntarily prior to 24 months.
