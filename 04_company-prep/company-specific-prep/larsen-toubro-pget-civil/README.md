## 1. Executive Snapshot & Role Context

Larsen & Toubro Limited (L&T) is India's premier technology, engineering, construction, manufacturing, and financial services conglomerate, and the largest civil infrastructure contractor in South Asia. 

The **Post Graduate Engineer Trainee (PGET)** recruitment track at IIT Kanpur is designed to induct high-caliber Master of Technology engineers into L&T Construction's six major Independent Companies (ICs):
1. **Heavy Civil Infrastructure IC:** Metro underground & elevated corridors, marine ports, river bridges, tunnels, nuclear power containment, and defense facilities.
2. **Buildings & Factories (B&F) IC:** High-rise residential/commercial towers, tech parks, hospitals, data centers, airports, and precast manufacturing.
3. **Transportation Infrastructure (TI) IC:** Dedicated Freight Corridors (DFC), high-speed rail (MAHSR), expressways, and intelligent highway networks.
4. **Water & Effluent Treatment (WET) IC:** Megascale lift irrigation, municipal water supply, desalination, industrial effluent treatment, and river rejuvenation.
5. **Minerals & Metals (M&M) IC:** Heavy industrial plants, cement/steel factories, and bulk material handling systems.
6. **Power Transmission & Distribution (PT&D) IC:** Grid substations, transmission towers, and solar utility structures.

Unlike conventional junior site engineer roles, **PGETs are fast-tracked into engineering coordination, technical design review, EPC project execution, contract billing, quality control (QA/QC), and digital construction engineering (BIM/CDE).**

## 3. Evidentiary Standards & Tagging Convention

Every factual statement, question, and framework in this package is strictly classified according to the repository evidentiary taxonomy:
* `[OFFICIAL — L&T]`: Sourced directly from L&T annual reports, campus placement brochures, or official job circulars.
* `[IITK PLACEMENT SOURCE]`: Derived from the official IIT Kanpur placement export and historical SPO records (`placement_data.csv`).
* `[STANDARD CODES & MANUALS]`: Extracted from national and international building codes (IS 456, IS 800, IS 1893, IS 10262, IRC 37, CPWD Specifications).
* `[CANDIDATE-REPORTED — HISTORICAL]`: Insights compiled from past IIT/NIT placement candidate debriefs across previous L&T recruitment cycles.
* `[PREPARATION INFERENCE]`: Pedagogical practice questions, numerical models, and exam simulations developed specifically for candidate mastery.
* `[UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED]`: Clear demarcations of parameters not yet officially published (e.g., exact 2026–27 OA platform vendor).
