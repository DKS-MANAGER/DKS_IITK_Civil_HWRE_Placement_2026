## Mock Assessment 1: Core Civil Engineering Technical (60 Questions, 60 Mins)

### Question 1
**[Technical Q1] In limit state design as per IS 456:2000, what is the maximum strain in concrete at the outermost compression fiber in bending?**
- A) 0.002
- B) 0.0035
- C) 0.0045
- D) 0.0015
*Answer: B* | **Solution/Explanation:** As per Clause 38.1(b) of IS 456:2000, the maximum strain in concrete at the outermost compression fiber in bending is taken as 0.0035.

### Question 2
**[Technical Q2] For Fe 415 steel, what is the design yield strength in limit state of collapse (flexure)?**
- A) 415 N/mm²
- B) 360.9 N/mm²
- C) 280.0 N/mm²
- D) 250.0 N/mm²
*Answer: B* | **Solution/Explanation:** Design yield strength f_yd = f_y / gamma_ms = 415 / 1.15 = 360.87 N/mm² ≈ 360.9 N/mm².

### Question 3
**[Technical Q3] A singly reinforced rectangular beam has breadth 250 mm and effective depth 450 mm. If M25 concrete and Fe 500 steel are used, what is the limiting moment of resistance Mu,lim?**
- A) 135.2 kNm
- B) 173.8 kNm
- C) 153.9 kNm
- D) 188.5 kNm
*Answer: C* | **Solution/Explanation:** For Fe 500, Mu,lim = 0.1338 * fck * b * d² = 0.1338 * 25 * 250 * 450² * 10^-6 = 153.94 kNm.

### Question 4
**[Technical Q4] What is the minimum percentage of longitudinal reinforcement required in a RCC column according to IS 456:2000?**
- A) 0.6%
- B) 0.8%
- C) 1.0%
- D) 0.15%
*Answer: B* | **Solution/Explanation:** Clause 26.5.3.1 states minimum longitudinal reinforcement in columns is 0.8% of the gross cross-sectional area (to prevent buckling during unexpected eccentricities).

### Question 5
**[Technical Q5] The maximum spacing of shear stirrups in a beam of effective depth d=500 mm as per IS 456 is:**
- A) 375 mm
- B) 300 mm
- C) 450 mm
- D) 500 mm
*Answer: B* | **Solution/Explanation:** Clause 26.5.1.5 specifies maximum spacing shall not exceed 0.75d (0.75*500 = 375 mm) or 300 mm, whichever is less. Hence 300 mm.

### Question 6
**[Technical Q6] As per IS 800:2007, what is the maximum permissible slenderness ratio (KL/r) for a member carrying compressive loads resulting from dead loads and superimposed loads?**
- A) 180
- B) 250
- C) 300
- D) 350
*Answer: A* | **Solution/Explanation:** Table 3 of IS 800:2007: Member carrying compressive loads resulting from dead loads and imposed loads = 180.

### Question 7
**[Technical Q7] In limit state design of steel connections, the partial safety factor for material gamma_m0 for resistance governed by yielding is:**
- A) 1.10
- B) 1.25
- C) 1.50
- D) 1.00
*Answer: A* | **Solution/Explanation:** As per Table 5 of IS 800:2007, gamma_m0 = 1.10 for resistance to yielding.

### Question 8
**[Technical Q8] A standard proctor compaction test yielded maximum dry density of 1.85 g/cc at OMC of 15%. If specific gravity G=2.67, what is the degree of saturation at OMC?**
- A) 78.4%
- B) 84.6%
- C) 90.4%
- D) 95.2%
*Answer: C* | **Solution/Explanation:** gamma_d = G*gamma_w / (1+e) => 1.85 = 2.67*1.0 / (1+e) => 1+e = 1.4432 => e = 0.4432. S*e = w*G => S = w*G/e = (0.15 * 2.67) / 0.4432 = 0.9036 = 90.4%.

### Question 9
**[Technical Q9] For a normally consolidated clay, the effective friction angle phi' is 30°. What is the coefficient of active earth pressure Ka?**
- A) 0.50
- B) 0.333
- C) 0.25
- D) 0.577
*Answer: B* | **Solution/Explanation:** Ka = (1 - sin 30°) / (1 + sin 30°) = (1 - 0.5) / (1 + 0.5) = 0.5 / 1.5 = 1/3 = 0.333.

### Question 10
**[Technical Q10] A square footing of size 2m x 2m is placed at a depth of 1.5m in purely cohesive soil with unconfined compressive strength qu = 100 kPa. Using Terzaghi's theory (Nc=5.7), what is the ultimate bearing capacity?**
- A) 285 kPa
- B) 370.5 kPa
- C) 399 kPa
- D) 420 kPa
*Answer: C* | **Solution/Explanation:** For clay, cu = qu/2 = 50 kPa. For square footing: q_ult = 1.3*c*Nc + gamma*Df*Nq. 1.3*50*5.7 + gamma*1.5 (~28 kPa) = 370.5 + 28.5 = 399 kPa.

### Question 11
**[Technical Q11] The time factor Tv for 50% consolidation under two-way drainage is approximately:**
- A) 0.197
- B) 0.405
- C) 0.848
- D) 0.125
*Answer: A* | **Solution/Explanation:** For U <= 60%, Tv = (pi/4)*(U/100)^2 = (3.14159/4)*(0.5)^2 = 0.1963 ≈ 0.197.

### Question 12
**[Technical Q12] In a horizontal rectangular channel, if initial Froude number F1 = 4, the ratio of sequent depths y2/y1 is:**
- A) 4.25
- B) 5.20
- C) 5.92
- D) 3.50
*Answer: B* | **Solution/Explanation:** y2/y1 = 0.5 * (sqrt(1 + 8*F1^2) - 1) = 0.5 * (sqrt(1 + 8*16) - 1) = 0.5 * (sqrt(129) - 1) = 0.5 * (11.357 - 1) = 5.18 ≈ 5.20.

### Question 13
**[Technical Q13] A 4-hour unit hydrograph has a peak discharge of 40 m³/s. The peak discharge of a storm runoff from a 4-hour storm with effective rainfall of 3.5 cm will be:**
- A) 140 m³/s
- B) 120 m³/s
- C) 160 m³/s
- D) 100 m³/s
*Answer: A* | **Solution/Explanation:** By linearity of unit hydrograph: Q_peak = Pe * Q_unit = 3.5 * 40 = 140 m³/s.

### Question 14
**[Technical Q14] A two-peg test in leveling is carried out to eliminate or detect:**
- A) Curvature error
- B) Collimation error
- C) Refraction error
- D) Gradation error
*Answer: B* | **Solution/Explanation:** The two-peg test checks whether the line of collimation is truly parallel to the bubble tube axis (collimation error).

### Question 15
**[Technical Q15] Simpson's one-third rule for volume computation assumes the boundary between ordinates is:**
- A) Straight line
- B) Circular arc
- C) Parabolic arc
- D) Elliptical
*Answer: C* | **Solution/Explanation:** Simpson's rule assumes the boundary between three consecutive ordinates is a second-degree polynomial (parabola).

### Question 16
**[Technical Q16] On a two-way two-lane highway, design speed V = 80 km/h, acceleration of overtaking vehicle a = 2.5 km/h/sec. The initial spacing 's' between vehicles is:**
- A) 16 m
- B) 18.8 m
- C) 22 m
- D) 25 m
*Answer: B* | **Solution/Explanation:** As per IRC: s = 0.2*Vb + 6 = 0.2*(80 - 16) + 6 = 0.2*64 + 6 = 12.8 + 6 = 18.8 m.

### Question 17
**[Technical Q17] If 5-day BOD at 20°C is 200 mg/L with base-e deoxygenation constant k = 0.23 /day, ultimate BOD L0 is:**
- A) 240 mg/L
- B) 293 mg/L
- C) 310 mg/L
- D) 350 mg/L
*Answer: B* | **Solution/Explanation:** BOD5 = L0 * (1 - e^(-0.23*5)) = L0 * (1 - e^-1.15) = L0 * (1 - 0.3166) = 0.6834*L0 => L0 = 200 / 0.6834 = 292.6 mg/L ≈ 293 mg/L.

### Question 18
**[Technical Q18] For an activity with Early Start ES=4, Early Finish EF=10, Late Start LS=7, Late Finish LF=13, the Total Float is:**
- A) 0
- B) 3
- C) 6
- D) 4
*Answer: B* | **Solution/Explanation:** Total Float TF = LS - ES = LF - EF = 7 - 4 = 13 - 10 = 3 days.

### Question 19

### Site Scenario 1: Concrete Slump Failure at Pour (Case 1)
**Scenario Context:** A transit mixer arrives on site with M35 concrete specified for an elevated metro pier. Site slump test measures 60 mm against the specified 150±25 mm. The pouring crew suggests adding 30 liters of water directly into the drum to regain workability.

**Engineering Protocol & Action:** Strictly forbid addition of uncontrolled water. Adding water increases water-cement ratio, drastically reducing compressive strength and increasing shrinkage cracks. Action: If permissible by approved mix design and within travel time, dose an approved compatible superplasticizer under QA/QC supervisor control, rotate drum at high speed for 3 minutes, and re-test slump. If it still fails, reject the entire batch.

### Site Scenario 2: Deep Excavation Strut Deforming Under Heavy Rainfall (Case 2)
**Scenario Context:** During 12m deep basement excavation in clay, heavy monsoon rains cause the top level steel waler and strut system to show noticeable lateral inward deflection (18 mm against allowable 5 mm). Water is ponding behind the diaphragm wall.

**Engineering Protocol & Action:** Immediately evacuate workers from the excavation pit. Close perimeter heavy crane/dump truck movement within a zone of 2x depth (24m). Install emergency dewatering pumps behind the wall to relieve hydrostatic pressure. Install temporary inclined raker props or additional intermediate strut tieback anchors. Monitor inclinometers continuously.

### Site Scenario 3: Thermal Cracking in Mass Raft Foundation Pour (Case 3)
**Scenario Context:** During a 3.0 m thick mass raft foundation pour (4,000 m³), thermocouple data indicates the core temperature has reached 72°C while surface temperature is 46°C, yielding a temperature differential of 26°C (allowable limit is 20°C).

**Engineering Protocol & Action:** Apply thermal insulation (tarpaulins, curing blankets, or polystyrene boards) to the top concrete surface immediately to reduce heat loss and lower the differential below 20°C. Increase chilled water circulation through pre-embedded cooling pipe coils inside the raft core. Do not spray cold water on the hot surface, as thermal shock will cause severe cracking.

**Engineering Protocol & Action:** Immediately evacuate workers from the excavation pit. Close perimeter heavy crane/dump truck movement within a zone of 2x depth (24m). Install emergency dewatering pumps behind the wall to relieve hydrostatic pressure. Install temporary inclined raker props or additional intermediate strut tieback anchors. Monitor inclinometers continuously.

### Site Scenario 18: Thermal Cracking in Mass Raft Foundation Pour (Case 18)
**Scenario Context:** During a 3.0 m thick mass raft foundation pour (4,000 m³), thermocouple data indicates the core temperature has reached 72°C while surface temperature is 46°C, yielding a temperature differential of 26°C (allowable limit is 20°C).

**Engineering Protocol & Action:** Apply thermal insulation (tarpaulins, curing blankets, or polystyrene boards) to the top concrete surface immediately to reduce heat loss and lower the differential below 20°C. Increase chilled water circulation through pre-embedded cooling pipe coils inside the raft core. Do not spray cold water on the hot surface, as thermal shock will cause severe cracking.

### Site Scenario 19: Pile Integrity Test Detects Necking at 8m Depth (Case 19)

**Engineering Protocol & Action:** Mark the pile as defective. Do not proceed with pile cap construction over this pile. Perform confirmatory high-strain dynamic pile testing or cross-hole sonic logging (CSL) if sonic tubes exist. ### Site Scenario 21: Concrete Slump Failure at Pour (Case 21)
**Scenario Context:** A transit mixer arrives on site with M35 concrete specified for an elevated metro pier. Site slump test measures 60 mm against the specified 150±25 mm. The pouring crew suggests adding 30 liters of water directly into the drum to regain workability.

**Engineering Protocol & Action:** Strictly forbid addition of uncontrolled water. Adding water increases water-cement ratio, drastically reducing compressive strength and increasing shrinkage cracks. Action: If permissible by approved mix design and within travel time, dose an approved compatible superplasticizer under QA/QC supervisor control, rotate drum at high speed for 3 minutes, and re-test slump. If it still fails, reject the entire batch.
