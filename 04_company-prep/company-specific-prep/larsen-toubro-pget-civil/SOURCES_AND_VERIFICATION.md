## 1. Statutory Codes & Standards

All technical questions, numerical derivation parameters, and permissible limits in this preparation suite are directly linked to official regulatory engineering codes:

1. **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS):**
   * **IS 456:2000 (Reaffirmed 2021):** *Plain and Reinforced Concrete — Code of Practice.* (Clauses 26, 38, 39, 40, Annex B, Annex E, Annex G).
   * **IS 800:2007 (Reaffirmed 2017):** *General Construction in Steel — Code of Practice.* (Sections 5, 7, 8, 10, Table 3, Table 5, Table 7, Table 11).
   * **IS 1893 (Part 1): 2016:** *Criteria for Earthquake Resistant Design of Structures — General Provisions and Buildings.*
   * **IS 13920:2016:** *Ductile Design and Detailing of Reinforced Concrete Structures Subjected to Seismic Forces.*
   * **IS 10262:2019:** *Concrete Mix Proportioning — Guidelines (Second Revision).*
   * **IS 2911 (Part 1 to 4): 2010:** *Design and Construction of Pile Foundations — Code of Practice.*
   * **IS 6403:1981:** *Code of Practice for Determination of Bearing Capacity of Shallow Foundations.*
   * **IS 13311 (Part 1 & 2): 1992:** *Non-Destructive Testing of Concrete (UPV and Rebound Hammer).*
2. **Indian Roads Congress (IRC):**
   * **IRC 37:2018:** *Guidelines for the Design of Flexible Pavements.*
   * **IRC 58:2015:** *Guidelines for the Design of Plain Jointed Rigid Pavements for Highways.*
   * **IRC 73:1980:** *Geometric Design Standards for Rural Highways.*
3. **American & European Standards (for comparison & international EPC):**
   * **ACI 318-19:** *Building Code Requirements for Structural Concrete and Commentary.*
   * **AISC 360-16:** *Specification for Structural Steel Buildings.*
   * **Eurocode 2 (EN 1992-1-1) & Eurocode 3 (EN 1993-1-1):** *Design of Concrete and Steel Structures.*
