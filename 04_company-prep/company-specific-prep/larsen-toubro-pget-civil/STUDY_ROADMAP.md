## 3. Track B: 3-Day Accelerated Technical Sprint

*Target: Intensive preparation over 72 hours.*

* **Day 1: Structural, RCC & Steel Core**
  * Morning: [`02_STRUCTURAL_ENGINEERING_AND_RCC.md`](02_STRUCTURAL_ENGINEERING_AND_RCC.md) — Solve all 30 problems. Master limit state design, flexure, shear, torsion, compression members, IS 800 connections.
  * Afternoon: [`03_GEOTECHNICAL_AND_FOUNDATION_ENGINEERING.md`](03_GEOTECHNICAL_AND_FOUNDATION_ENGINEERING.md) — Soil classification, Terzaghi bearing capacity, pile group settlement, earth pressure.
  * Evening: Attempt **Mock 1 (Core Civil 60 Qs)** in [`11_MOCK_TESTS_AND_SOLUTIONS.md`](11_MOCK_TESTS_AND_SOLUTIONS.md).
* **Day 2: Water Resources, Materials & Surveying**
  * Morning: [`04_FLUID_MECHANICS_HYDRAULICS_AND_WATER_RESOURCES.md`](04_FLUID_MECHANICS_HYDRAULICS_AND_WATER_RESOURCES.md) — Pipe networks, Hardy Cross, open channel flow, hydraulic jumps, pumps.
  * Afternoon: [`06_CONSTRUCTION_MATERIALS_SURVEYING_AND_QS.md`](06_CONSTRUCTION_MATERIALS_SURVEYING_AND_QS.md) — Concrete mix design, workability, NDT testing, surveying, leveling, rate analysis.
  * Evening: Attempt **Mock 2 (Aptitude & Professional 60 Qs)** in [`11_MOCK_TESTS_AND_SOLUTIONS.md`](11_MOCK_TESTS_AND_SOLUTIONS.md).
* **Day 3: Construction Management, Aptitude & Full Simulation**
  * Morning: [`07_CONSTRUCTION_PLANNING_SAFETY_AND_QUALITY.md`](07_CONSTRUCTION_PLANNING_SAFETY_AND_QUALITY.md) — CPM/PERT, floats, crashing, HIRA safety, QA/QC.
  * Afternoon: [`01_APTITUDE_AND_REASONING.md`](01_APTITUDE_AND_REASONING.md) — Speed math, permutations, syllogisms.
  * Evening: **Full Simulation: Mock 3 (120 Qs | 120 Mins)** in [`11_MOCK_TESTS_AND_SOLUTIONS.md`](11_MOCK_TESTS_AND_SOLUTIONS.md).

*Target: Ideal preparation schedule for top-percentile test score and confident interview performance.*

* **Day 2:** Structural Mechanics & RCC — IS 456, IS 13920, and all problems in [`02_STRUCTURAL_ENGINEERING_AND_RCC.md`](02_STRUCTURAL_ENGINEERING_AND_RCC.md).
* **Day 3:** Geotechnical & Foundations — Deep foundations, pile testing, settlement in [`03_GEOTECHNICAL_AND_FOUNDATION_ENGINEERING.md`](03_GEOTECHNICAL_AND_FOUNDATION_ENGINEERING.md).
* **Day 4:** Hydraulics, Environmental & Transportation — Pipe flow, wastewater treatment, IRC highway geometry in Modules 04 & 05.

* **Day 6:** EPC Project Controls & Safety — CPM/PERT, crashing, HIRA, ISO standards in [`07_CONSTRUCTION_PLANNING_SAFETY_AND_QUALITY.md`](07_CONSTRUCTION_PLANNING_SAFETY_AND_QUALITY.md).
