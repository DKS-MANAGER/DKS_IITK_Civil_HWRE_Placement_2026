## 2. AECOM's Landmark Projects in India (Technical Deep-Dive)

Be prepared to discuss AECOM's flagship Indian projects with engineering depth:

---

### Project 1: The Chenab River Railway Bridge (Jammu & Kashmir)
- **Overview:** The world's highest railway bridge ($359\text{ m}$ above the riverbed, $1.315\text{ km}$ total length), a critical link on the Udhampur-Srinagar-Baramulla Rail Link (USBRL).
- **AECOM's Role:** Proof consultant and independent design check consultant for the monumental steel arch span ($467\text{ m}$) and foundation stability.
- **Key Technical Challenges:**
  - Extreme wind forces: Designed to withstand wind speeds up to $266\text{ km/h}$ using boundary layer wind tunnel testing and computational aerodynamic modeling.
  - Seismic vulnerability: High-seismic Zone V design incorporating special blast-resistant and temperature-expansion bearings.
  - Deep gorge slope stability: High-altitude slope reinforcement with micro-piles and prestressed rock anchors anchored deep into fractured limestone.

---

### Project 2: Delhi-Meerut Regional Rapid Transit System (RRTS)
- **Overview:** India's first semi-high-speed regional commuter rail network ($82.15\text{ km}$ corridor, operating speed $160\text{ km/h}$).
- **AECOM's Role:** Detailed Design Consultant (DDC) and Project Management Consultant (PMC) for key elevated viaduct sections, station architectural-structural design, and multi-modal integration hubs.
- **Key Technical Challenges:**
  - Viaduct Pier Hydraulics & Drainage: Sizing stormwater collection and cross-drainage networks along busy highway medians without interrupting traffic.
  - Multi-modal TOD Integration: Master planning Transit-Oriented Development with zero-runoff stormwater attenuation basins and managed groundwater recharge shafts.

---

### Project 3: Mumbai Metro Line 3 (Aqua Line — Colaba-Bandra-SEEPZ)
- **Overview:** Mumbai's first fully underground metro corridor ($33.5\text{ km}$ length, 27 underground stations) threading through high-density urban fabric and coastal geological formations.
- **AECOM's Role:** General Consultant (GC) joint venture leading project management, civil design verification, tunnel boring machine (TBM) oversight, and station construction management.
- **Key Technical Challenges:**
  - High Groundwater & Coastal Dewatering: Managing aggressive coastal tidal water tables with secant pile cut-off walls and pressurized dewatering systems.
  - Geotechnical Settlement: Micro-monitoring surface settlement using real-time automated total stations to protect century-old heritage masonry structures directly above TBM alignments.

---

### Project 4: Dholera Special Investment Region (SIR) Smart City (Gujarat)
- **Overview:** India's first greenfield industrial smart city ($920\text{ km}^2$), planned as a global manufacturing hub along the Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC).
- **AECOM's Role:** Master Planning Consultant and Program Manager for the 22.5 $\text{km}^2$ Activation Area trunk infrastructure.
- **Key Technical Challenges:**
  - Low-lying Coastal Flood Mitigation: Dholera lies in a low-lying tidal coastal plain susceptible to monsoon storm surges. AECOM designed an integrated canal bund and flood-control bund network with automated tidal flap sluice gates.
  - Recycled Water & ZLD: 100% wastewater collection with tertiary membrane treatment, supplying industrial cooling loops to achieve Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD).

---

### Project 5: Namami Gange / National Mission for Clean Ganga (NMCG)
- **Overview:** The Government of India's flagship river basin rejuvenation program to eliminate untreated sewage discharge and restore the ecological flow of the River Ganga.
- **AECOM's Role:** Project Management and Technical Advisory for sewage treatment plants (STPs), interceptor trunk sewer networks, and riverfront development across Uttar Pradesh and Bihar.
- **Key Technical Challenges:**
  - Interception & Diversion (I&D): Rerouting open municipal nullahs into mechanized treatment plants before river outfalls.
  - Advanced Biological Nutrient Removal: Sizing modern SBR (Sequencing Batch Reactor) and MBBR plants to meet strict effluent standards ($BOD \le 10\text{ mg/L}, TSS \le 10\text{ mg/L}, N \le 10\text{ mg/L}$).

## 4. AECOM's Sustainable Legacies ESG Strategy

AECOM's overarching strategic vision is built on four pillars:
1. **Embed Sustainable Development and Resilient Design:**
   - Committing to Science-Based Net-Zero greenhouse gas emissions.
   - Designing infrastructure to withstand 100-year climate change projections (extreme rainfall intensification, sea-level rise, heatwaves).
2. **Improve Social Outcomes:**
   - Designing inclusive infrastructure that benefits underprivileged communities, prioritizing water accessibility, clean sanitation, and pedestrian safety.
3. **Achieve Net-Zero Carbon:**
   - Reducing **embodied carbon** in infrastructure by optimizing concrete mix designs (fly ash/GGBS slag replacement) and minimizing structural steel over-design.
4. **Enhance Governance:**
   - Uncompromising business ethics, anti-corruption, and total transparency in procurement and construction supervision.
