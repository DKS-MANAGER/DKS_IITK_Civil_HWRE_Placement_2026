## 2. Day-to-Day Responsibilities & Technical Deliverables

As an ECP in the Civil / Water Resources / Infrastructure track, the engineer reports to a Senior Engineer or Project Lead and contributes to:

### 2.1 Engineering Calculations & Hydrologic/Hydraulic Sizing
- **Open-Channel & Pipe Flow Calculations:** Sizing stormwater culverts, open drainage channels, gravity sewer lines, and pressurized pumping mains using Manning’s equation, Darcy-Weisbach, and Colebrook-White friction formulations.
- **Hydrological Assessment:** Deriving design rainfall hyetographs, calculating peak discharge via the Rational Method and SCS-CN unit hydrograph methods, and sizing detention basins.
- **Hydraulic Structure Modeling:** Running 1D/2D backwater profiles, water surface profile computations ($M_1, M_2, S_1, S_2$ curves), bridge waterway opening calculations, and scour depth estimations.

### 2.2 Numerical Simulation & Digital Modeling
- **Hydrodynamic Modeling:** Building, calibrating, and executing 1D/2D flood risk simulations in **HEC-RAS**, urban drainage networks in **SWMM**, or water distribution models in **WaterGEMS / EPA-NET**.
- **CFD & Complex Hydraulics (Specialist Track):** Simulating 3D multiphase flow, free-surface turbulence, aeration, and hydrodynamic scour beneath structures using tools like **OpenFOAM** or ANSYS Fluent.
- **Spatial Analytics:** Delineating catchments, preparing digital elevation models (DEMs), land-use rasters, and flood hazard maps using **QGIS / ArcGIS**.

### 2.3 Technical Documentation & Drawing Verification
- Drafting **Design Basis Memoranda (DBM)**, hydraulic calculation sheets (in Excel/Mathcad/Python), and technical evaluation reports.
- Interfacing with CAD/BIM modelers to ensure that engineering calculations are translated into **Civil 3D / Revit** plan, profile, and cross-section drawings.
- Performing QA/QC checks against applicable design codes (Indian Standards / IRC for India domestic projects; Eurocodes / CIRIA / AASHTO for global delivery projects).

## 3. Location Dynamics: Bengaluru vs Gurugram

While both offices operate under the unified AECOM India umbrella, the technical focus exhibits distinct operational centers of gravity:

| Dimension | Gurugram Hub (Delhi-NCR) | Bengaluru Hub (Karnataka) |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Project Mix** | Blend of **Major Domestic Indian Infrastructure** (Metro, RRTS, Smart Cities, NHAI Highways) and Global GDC delivery. | Primarily **Global Delivery Center (GDC) / Enterprise Capabilities**, delivering international megaprojects (UK, Middle East, US, Australia). |
| **Governing Design Codes** | Heavy emphasis on **Indian Standards (IS Codes), IRC, MoRTH, and CPHEEO** manuals, alongside Eurocodes/AASHTO. | Heavy emphasis on **Eurocodes, British Standards, CIRIA manuals, US FHWA/AASHTO**, and Australian standards. |
| **Core Technical Pods** | Transportation & Highways, Rail/Transit, Bridge Structures, Urban Water, PMC site coordination. | Water Resources & Coastal, Advanced Hydrodynamics/CFD, Global Bridge Design, 3D/4D BIM automation, Digital Twins. |

## 4. Mentorship & Professional Development (ADVANCE Program)

AECOM supports early-career engineers through structured global talent programs:
- **Global Graduate Development Program (GGDP):** A 2-to-3-year structured development roadmap offering technical masterclasses, project management training, and cross-discipline rotations.
- **Professional Chartership Pathways:** Mentorship and financial support to attain chartered engineer status through international institutions such as the **Institution of Civil Engineers (ICE)**, **Chartered Institution of Water and Environmental Management (CIWEM)**, or **American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE)**.
- **Tredence / AECOM University:** Access to thousands of technical learning modules on Bentley ProjectWise, Civil 3D, Python automation, and Sustainable Legacies ESG design principles.
