## 2. Stage-by-Stage Breakdown & Rubrics

### 2.1 Stage 1: Application & Resume Shortlisting
- **Screening Parameters:** High CGPA in B.Tech and M.Tech, evidence of computational and analytical engineering rigor, relevant thesis in hydraulics/CFD/structures.
- **Shortlist Pitfalls:** Generic resumes that fail to highlight numerical modeling, CAD/GIS proficiency, or engineering design coursework.

### 2.2 Stage 2: Online Assessment (OA) `[PREPARATION INFERENCE]`
- **Structure:** 60 to 90 minutes.
  1. **Quantitative & Reasoning Aptitude (~20–25 Qs):** Arithmetic, percentages, work-rate, speed-distance, Venn diagrams, logical deductions, data interpretation.
  2. **Verbal Ability (~10–15 Qs):** Reading comprehension, error detection, technical sentence completion.
  3. **Core Technical Civil/Fluids (~25–30 Qs):** Fluid properties, Bernoulli's equation, pipe friction, open-channel flow regimes, Manning's equation, Mohr's circle, bending moments, soil compaction.

### 2.3 Stage 3: Technical Interview Round `[PREPARATION INFERENCE]`
- **Panel:** Typically 1 to 2 Senior Technical Leaders / Associate Directors from the Civil Infrastructure or Water Resources practice groups.
- **Core Areas Evaluated:**
  - **M.Tech Thesis Defense:** Detailed questioning on OpenFOAM governing equations, Reynolds-Averaged Navier-Stokes (RANS) closure models ($k-\epsilon$ vs $k-\omega$ SST), mesh resolution ($y^+$), and scour physics.
  - **Hydraulics & Fluid Mechanics:** Derivations of momentum equation for hydraulic jumps, specific energy curves, Moody diagram friction factors, water hammer transients.
  - **Consultancy Problem Solving:** *"How would you size a stormwater drainage culvert crossing a 4-lane highway given a 50-year design storm?"*
  - **Software Fluency:** Pragmatic discussion on AutoCAD, Civil 3D, HEC-RAS, GIS, and Python engineering calculation scripts.

### 2.4 Stage 4: HR & Behavioral Round `[PREPARATION INFERENCE]`
- **Panel:** Human Resources Partner / Talent Acquisition Lead.
- **Key Themes:**
  - **Safety Culture:** How the candidate prioritizes safety in design, field investigations, and lab environments.
  - **Global Delivery & Teamwork:** Ability to communicate technical findings to cross-border teams across the UK, Middle East, and US.
  - **Location Flexibility:** Confirming willingness to work in either **Bengaluru** or **Gurugram** based on pod allocation.
  - **Professional Growth:** Ambition to pursue professional chartership (ICE / CIWEM / PE) under AECOM's GGDP mentorship program.
