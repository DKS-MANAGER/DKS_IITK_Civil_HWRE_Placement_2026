## 2. Landmark Infrastructure Projects in India

Understanding AECOM's Indian portfolio is critical for interview conversations:

### 2.1 Water & Environmental Engineering
1. **Namami Gange National Mission for Clean Ganga:**
   - Detailed project reports (DPR), condition assessments, and construction management for massive Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs) and interceptor sewer networks across Kanpur, Varanasi, Haridwar, and Prayagraj.
2. **Dholera Special Investment Region (SIR) Smart City:**
   - Master planning and detailed design of smart water supply, trunk storm drainage networks, recycled water conveyance, and flood defense bunds for India's greenfield smart city in Gujarat.
3. **Mega Desalination & Industrial Water Reuse:**
   - Engineering consultancy for coastal seawater reverse osmosis (SWRO) facilities and zero-liquid-discharge (ZLD) effluent treatment systems for industrial corridors.

### 2.2 Bridges & Transportation Engineering
1. **Chenab River Railway Bridge (Jammu & Kashmir):**
   - The world's highest railway arch bridge (359 meters above the riverbed). AECOM served as proof consultant, conducting independent structural, aerodynamic wind tunnel, and hydrodynamic foundation verification.
2. **Regional Rapid Transit System (Delhi-Meerut RRTS):**
   - India's first semi-high-speed rail corridor (180 km/h). AECOM served as detailed design consultant for viaduct structures, stations, and drainage.
3. **Mumbai Metro Line 3 (Aqua Line) & Delhi Metro Phase 4:**
   - Comprehensive engineering design, underground station box structural engineering, geotechnical baseline modeling, and tunnel ventilation design.
4. **Western Dedicated Freight Corridor (WDFC):**
   - Detailed design, bridge hydraulics, culvert drainage design, and construction supervision for heavy-haul freight rail networks.

## 4. Technical Collaboration & Workshare Workflow

In the Global Delivery Center (GDC) model:
1. **Scope Definition:** The regional office (e.g., AECOM UK, US, or Middle East) defines design criteria, local regulatory codes (Eurocodes, AASHTO, British Standards), and environmental constraints.
2. **Engineering Execution:** India engineers (ECPs, Design Engineers, Lead Engineers) perform numerical modeling, calculations, hydraulic simulations, structural design, and drafting.
3. **Quality Audit & Verification:** Strict adherence to AECOM's Global Quality Management System (QMS), peer design reviews, and checking procedures before client delivery.
4. **Tool Standardization:** Extensive use of ProjectWise, Civil 3D, Revit, HEC-RAS, MicroStation, and Python automation to maintain synchronized global models.
