## 3. Boundary Conditions & Non-Fabrication Rules

1. **Compensation Transparency:** The base salary, variable bonus, and benefits breakdown are unstated on the proforma and marked strictly as `[UNVERIFIED]`. No deductions or splits are fabricated.
2. **Eligibility & Cutoffs:** CGPA cutoffs and branch exclusions are not stated on the proforma and are left as unverified until the official Pre-Placement Talk (PPT).
3. **Selection Stages:** Only the "Application" stage is confirmed on the proforma. All subsequent rounds (Aptitude, Tech Interview, HR) are derived from historical patterns and marked as `[PREPARATION INFERENCE]`.
