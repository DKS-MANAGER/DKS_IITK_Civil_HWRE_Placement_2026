## 2. Core Business Lines & Practice Areas

AECOM operates as a pure-play infrastructure design, engineering, and advisory consultancy, having divested its capital-intensive construction contracting divisions to focus strictly on high-margin professional services.

### 2.1 Water & Hydraulic Infrastructure
- **Flood Risk Management & Coastal Engineering:** Coastal surge defense, river training works, 1D/2D hydrodynamic flood mapping, sea-level rise adaptation.
- **Dams & Hydropower:** Dam safety evaluations, spillway hydraulic modeling, CFD aeration and cavitation simulations, reservoir sedimentation routing.
- **Water & Wastewater Treatment:** Desalination, advanced municipal sewage treatment plants (STPs), effluent treatment plants (ETPs), sludge digesters, water reuse.
- **Conveyance & Transmission:** Large-diameter raw water pipelines, transient surge analysis, pumping stations, smart urban water distribution grids.

### 2.2 Transportation Infrastructure
- **Bridges & Civil Structures:** Long-span suspension, cable-stayed, and arch bridges; hydrodynamic scour countermeasures; seismic retrofitting.
- **Transit & Rail:** High-speed rail corridors, underground metro rail tunneling, station box designs, freight corridors.
- **Highways & Drainage:** Geometric highway design, culvert hydraulics, highway stormwater runoff collection networks.

### 2.3 Environmental & Climate Resilience
- **Sustainable Legacies Strategy:** AECOM's corporate commitment to achieve science-based net-zero carbon emissions, embed social equity, and enhance biodiversity across all client projects.
- Environmental Impact Assessments (EIA), hydrological basin-level risk modeling, and groundwater contamination remediation.

## 3. AECOM's Global Delivery Model & Enterprise Capabilities

AECOM executes large-scale global projects through a collaborative delivery network:
1. **Frontline Regional Teams:** Client-facing teams in North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific who interface directly with project owners and regulatory authorities.
2. **Global Delivery Centers (GDC) / Enterprise Capabilities (EC):** Centralized engineering design hubs in India (Bengaluru and Gurugram) and other global centers that perform detailed engineering design, 3D/4D BIM modeling, computational fluid dynamics (CFD), structural analysis, and finite element modeling (FEM).
3. **Seamless Digital Engineering:** Leveraging cloud platforms like Bentley ProjectWise, Autodesk Construction Cloud (ACC), Civil 3D, Revit, and GIS to collaborate across time zones in a 24-hour design cycle.

AECOM's operational culture is governed by its **Core Values**:

- **Collaborate:** Foster multidisciplinary global teamwork across geographical boundaries.
