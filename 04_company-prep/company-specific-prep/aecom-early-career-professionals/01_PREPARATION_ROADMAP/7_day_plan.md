## Detailed Daily Timetable

### Day 1: Fluid Mechanics & Governing Equations (6.0 Hours)
- **Morning (3.0 hrs):**
  - Reynolds Transport Theorem: mass, momentum, and energy conservation.
  - Navier-Stokes equations derivation in Cartesian and cylindrical coordinates.
  - Continuity equation, Euler's equation, and Bernoulli's equation with energy grade line (EGL) and hydraulic grade line (HGL).
- **Evening (3.0 hrs):**
  - Boundary layer theory: laminar vs turbulent boundary layers, displacement and momentum thicknesses.
  - Boundary layer separation, adverse pressure gradients, drag and lift over immersed bodies (cylinders and bridge decks).
  - Solve Questions 1–10 in [`02_TECHNICAL_PREPARATION/technical_question_bank.md`](../02_TECHNICAL_PREPARATION/technical_question_bank.md).

### Day 2: Open-Channel Flow & Hydraulic Structures (7.0 Hours)
- **Morning (3.5 hrs):**
  - Specific Energy diagram ($E-y$ curve), critical depth $y_c$, Froude number ($Fr$), and choking in transitions.
  - Hydraulic jump mechanics: Belanger momentum equation, conjugate depths, jump length, and energy loss derivation.
  - Stilling basins, baffle blocks, and USBR energy dissipator standards.
- **Evening (3.5 hrs):**
  - Gradually Varied Flow (GVF) equation derivation: $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{S_0 - S_f}{1 - Fr^2}$.
  - Classification and sketching of all 12 water surface profiles ($M_1, M_2, M_3, S_1, S_2, S_3, C, H, A$).
  - Solve Questions 11–20 in Technical Question Bank.

### Day 3: Pipe Flow, Head Loss & Pump Systems (6.0 Hours)
- **Morning (3.0 hrs):**
  - Darcy-Weisbach equation and friction factor ($f$); laminar vs turbulent regimes; Moody chart analysis.
  - Minor head losses: sudden expansion, contraction, bends, valves, equivalent length method.
  - Complex pipe networks: pipes in series, parallel, and the Hardy Cross iterative loop method.
- **Evening (3.0 hrs):**
  - Centrifugal pumps: Euler turbine equation, manometric head, pump operating point (system curve intersection).
  - Affinity laws for pumps; Net Positive Suction Head (NPSH), cavitation phenomena, and Thoma’s cavitation parameter.
  - Water hammer transients: Joukowsky pressure surge equation $\Delta P = \rho c \Delta V$ and surge tank mitigation.
  - Solve Questions 21–30 in Technical Question Bank.

### Day 4: Hydrology, Runoff & Storm Drainage (7.0 Hours)
- **Morning (3.5 hrs):**
  - Precipitation analysis: Thiessen polygon, isohyetal methods, Intensity-Duration-Frequency (IDF) curves.
  - Infiltration indices ($\Phi$-index) and Horton’s equation; evaporation via FAO-56 Penman-Monteith method.
  - Unit Hydrograph (UH) theory: assumptions, derivation from storm hydrographs, S-curve, and Synthetic Unit Hydrograph (Snyder/SCS).
- **Evening (3.5 hrs):**
  - Flood frequency analysis: Gumbel Extreme Value distribution, plotting positions (Weibull), return periods.
  - Hydrologic and hydraulic flood routing: Muskingum channel routing and level-pool reservoir routing.
  - Urban drainage design: Rational Method $Q = \frac{1}{360} C I A$, stormwater retention ponds, and culvert hydraulics.
  - Solve Questions 31–40 in Technical Question Bank.

### Day 5: Technical Projects & Applied Case Studies Deep-Dive (7.0 Hours)
- **Morning (3.5 hrs):**
  - **Technical Project Defense Preparation:** *Computational Fluid Dynamics & Hydrodynamic Scour Modeling*.
  - Governing equations, volume-of-fluid (VOF) free surface modeling, $k-\omega$ SST turbulence closure.
  - Near-wall treatment ($y^+$ resolution), mesh convergence, bed shear stress, and Shields parameter.
  - Master all questions in [`04_PROJECT_AND_RESUME_PREPARATION/thesis_deep_dive.md`](../04_PROJECT_AND_RESUME_PREPARATION/thesis_deep_dive.md).
- **Evening (3.5 hrs):**
  - **Applied Projects Defense:** Machine Learning Risk Framework (XGBoost/SHAP risk modeling), Streamflow Time-Series (SARIMAX), IDF Curve Derivation.
  - Rehearse the 60-second elevator pitch and 2-minute project walkthroughs.
  - Complete [`04_PROJECT_AND_RESUME_PREPARATION/project_explanations.md`](../04_PROJECT_AND_RESUME_PREPARATION/project_explanations.md).

### Day 6: Civil Core & Quantitative Aptitude (6.0 Hours)
- **Morning (3.0 hrs):**
  - Soil Mechanics & Foundation: Effective stress, Terzaghi bearing capacity, Rankine active/passive lateral earth pressure, pier scour depth calculations (Lacey's formula $R = 1.35 (q^2/f)^{1/3}$).
  - Structural Fundamentals: SFD/BMD for simply supported/cantilever beams, basic RCC limit state design (IS 456).
- **Evening (3.0 hrs):**
  - Quantitative Aptitude & Reasoning Sprint: Work-rate, speed-distance, percentages, probability, seating arrangements.
  - Complete 30 solved aptitude questions in [`03_ASSESSMENT_PREPARATION/aptitude/`](../03_ASSESSMENT_PREPARATION/aptitude/).
