## 2. Pillar 1: First-Principles Physical Mechanics
- **Why it matters:** In technical interviews, AECOM Directors frequently ask candidates to step up to the whiteboard and derive fundamental conservation laws (Continuity, Navier-Stokes, Reynolds Transport Theorem, Energy, Momentum).
- **Core Focus:**
  - Derivation of the hydraulic jump momentum equation and conjugate depths:
    $$\frac{y_2}{y_1} = \frac{1}{2}\left(\sqrt{1 + 8Fr_1^2} - 1\right)$$
  - Specific energy curves ($E = y + \frac{V^2}{2g}$) and critical depth relationships ($y_c = \sqrt[3]{\frac{q^2}{g}}$).
  - Pipe network friction losses (Darcy-Weisbach $h_f = \frac{f L V^2}{2 g D}$) and Moody diagram regimes.
  - Soil shear strength (Mohr-Coulomb criterion $\tau = c' + \sigma' \tan\phi'$) and Rankine earth pressure theory.

---

## 3. Pillar 2: Digital Tools & Computational Simulation
- **Why it matters:** As a Global Delivery Center (GDC) / Enterprise Capabilities hub, AECOM India relies heavily on digital engineering and multi-region BIM/CADD delivery workflows.
- **Core Focus:**
  - **M.Tech Thesis Defense:** Master every equation, boundary condition, and turbulence model ($k-\epsilon$, $k-\omega$ SST) in OpenFOAM used for simulating scour beneath submerged bridge decks.
  - **Hydrological / Hydraulic Software:** Articulate the underlying numerical algorithms of **HEC-RAS** (Standard Step method for 1D; St. Venant shallow water equations for 2D) and **SWMM** (dynamic wave routing).
  - **Engineering Automation:** Demonstrate how Python, MATLAB, and Excel spreadsheets accelerate repetitive hydraulic calculations and pipe network sizing.

---

## 4. Pillar 3: Consultancy Problem-Solving & Communication
- **Why it matters:** An engineering consultant must balance mathematical precision with practical constructability, budget limits, and safety standards.
- **Core Focus:**
  - **Practical Scenarios:** Formulate structured approaches to open-ended infrastructure questions (e.g., culvert sizing, flood inundation mitigation, pier scour remediation).
  - **Code Awareness:** Familiarity with key Indian Standards (IS 456, IS 3370, IRC 5, IRC 78, IRC SP 13) and international benchmarks (Eurocodes, CIRIA C753 SuDS manual, AASHTO).
  - **Communication:** Practice concise, structured verbal explanations using the CAR method (Context $\to$ Action $\to$ Result).
