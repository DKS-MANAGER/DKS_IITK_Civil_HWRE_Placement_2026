## 2. Topic Preparation Action Plan

### P0 Domains (Non-Negotiable Core)
- Be prepared to derive the **Belanger hydraulic jump equation** and draw specific energy diagrams on a blank whiteboard.
- Explain the physical mechanism of **horseshoe and wake vortex formation** inducing pier and submerged deck scour.
- Solve rapid pipe friction and pump operating point problems without a calculator.
- Master 30+ Quantitative and Logical Reasoning question archetypes for the OA.

### P1 Domains (High-Yield Technical Differentiators)
- Articulate the mathematical difference between 1D Saint-Venant equations and 2D shallow water hydrodynamic modeling in HEC-RAS.
- Explain how machine learning risk modeling combines structural inspection data with gradient boosting (XGBoost) and explainability (TreeSHAP).
- Review Terzaghi’s bearing capacity equation and factor of safety against overturning and sliding for retaining walls and gravity dams.

### P2 Domains (General Breadth)
- Review IS 456 basic concrete limit states (limit state of collapse vs serviceability) and basic surveying leveling checks.
