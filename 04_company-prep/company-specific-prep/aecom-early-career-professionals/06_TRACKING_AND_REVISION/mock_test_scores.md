## 4. Test Strategy Rules of Engagement

1. **Time Management Budget:**
   - Quantitative Aptitude: Allocate maximum **1.5 minutes per question**. If stuck past 90 seconds, flag and move forward.
   - Logical & Verbal: Allocate **1.0 minute per question**.
   - Technical & Hydrology: Allocate **1.5 minutes per calculation**; **30 seconds per code/theory question**.
2. **Negative Marking Mitigation:**
   - Guessing penalty is $-0.25$.
   - Never blind-guess among 4 unknown options (expected value $= 0.25(+1) + 0.75(-0.25) = +0.0625$, high variance).
   - Only take an educated guess if you can confidently eliminate at least **two incorrect options** (expected value $= 0.5(+1) + 0.5(-0.25) = +0.375$).
