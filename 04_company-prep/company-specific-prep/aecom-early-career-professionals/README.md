## 3. Candidate Alignment & Value Proposition

**Target Candidate Background:**
- **M.Tech:** Civil Engineering (Specialization: Hydraulics and Water Resources Engineering — HWRE), **Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur (IIT Kanpur)**.
- **B.Tech:** Civil Engineering / Infrastructure Engineering from a premier engineering institution.

### Candidate Value Proposition for AECOM ECP:
1. **Computational Fluid Dynamics & Hydraulic Structures:** Postgraduate specialization in *Computational Fluid Dynamics & Hydrodynamic Scour Modeling* using **OpenFOAM and ParaView** aligns directly with AECOM's complex bridge hydraulics, hydrodynamic modeling, and scour countermeasure design.
2. **Machine Learning & Risk Analytics:** Experience developing predictive risk frameworks (XGBoost + TreeSHAP explainability across multi-variate infrastructure inspection records), bridging computational data science with structural and hydraulic risk management.
3. **Hydrological & Statistical Modeling:** Hands-on experience in *Streamflow Time-Series Modeling (SARIMAX)*, *IDF Curve Derivation*, and *FAO-56 Penman-Monteith Evapotranspiration*, directly applicable to AECOM's urban stormwater drainage, flood risk assessment, and water master planning projects.
4. **Engineering Consultancy Readiness:** Strong grounding in civil engineering fundamentals (fluid mechanics, open-channel flow, soil mechanics, concrete design, surveying) paired with programming proficiency in Python, MATLAB, C++, and SQL.
