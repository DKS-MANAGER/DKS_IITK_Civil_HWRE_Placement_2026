## SECTION A: Quantitative Aptitude (Q1 – Q7)

### Q1.
Two pumps P and Q together can empty an underground stormwater sump in 6 hours. If Pump P operates at $150\%$ of the pumping speed of Pump Q, how long would Pump P take to empty the sump alone?
- A) 8 hours
- B) 10 hours
- C) 12 hours
- D) 15 hours

### Q2.
A survey drone flies along an 80 km pipeline alignment at 40 km/h with a tailwind, and returns against the same wind at 24 km/h. What is the average round-trip speed of the drone?
- A) 28.0 km/h
- B) 30.0 km/h
- C) 32.0 km/h
- D) 33.3 km/h

### Q3.
An infrastructure fund allocates ₹20 Crores across two highway projects. Project Alpha yields 12% annual return, while Project Beta yields 7% annual return. If the total annual return is ₹1.90 Crores, how much was invested in Project Alpha?
- A) ₹8 Crores
- B) ₹10 Crores
- C) ₹12 Crores
- D) ₹14 Crores

### Q4.
A batch of concrete aggregate has a moisture content of 5% by dry mass. If 840 kg of moist aggregate is dried completely in a site oven, what is the dry mass of the aggregate?
- A) 798 kg
- B) 800 kg
- C) 805 kg
- D) 812 kg

### Q5.

- A) $129\pi\text{ m}^2$
- B) $120\pi\text{ m}^2$
- C) $141\pi\text{ m}^2$
- D) $150\pi\text{ m}^2$

### Q6.
In an inspection team of 8 engineers, in how many ways can a lead engineer and an assistant engineer be selected such that order matters?
- A) 28
- B) 56
- C) 64
- D) 72

### Q7.
The probability of a flood exceeding the 50-year design capacity in any single year is $p = 0.02$. What is the probability that the capacity will be exceeded at least once during a 30-year design lifespan?
- A) $1 - (0.98)^{30} \approx 0.454$
- B) $(0.02)^{30}$
- C) $0.600$
- D) $1 - 30(0.02) = 0.400$

## SECTION C: Civil & Fluid Mechanics Core (Q13 – Q19)

### Q13.
What is the kinematic viscosity $\nu$ of water if its dynamic viscosity is $\mu = 1.0 \times 10^{-3}\text{ Pa}\cdot\text{s}$ and density is $\rho = 1000\text{ kg/m}^3$?
- A) $1.0 \times 10^{-6}\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$

- C) $1.0 \times 10^{-3}\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$
- D) $1.0\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$

### Q14.
In a pipeline of diameter $D = 200\text{ mm}$ carrying oil ($S = 0.85$), flow velocity is $V = 1.5\text{ m/s}$. What is the mass flow rate?
- A) $35.2\text{ kg/s}$
- B) $40.1\text{ kg/s}$
- C) $47.1\text{ kg/s}$
- D) $52.3\text{ kg/s}$

### Q15.

- A) $0.52\text{ m}$
- B) $0.74\text{ m}$
- C) $0.98\text{ m}$

### Q16.
