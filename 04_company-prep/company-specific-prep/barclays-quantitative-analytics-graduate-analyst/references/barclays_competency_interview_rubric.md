## 2. Comprehensive Competency Breakdown & Behavioral Rubrics

### Competency 1: Leadership Skills
> *"Leads by example, builds strong relationships, motivates and inspires people. Takes accountability for own development and supports others through sharing knowledge and honest two-way feedback."*

#### Capabilities & Expected Behaviors (BA4 Level):
1. **Developing Others**:
   - Provides regular, constructive, and objective feedback to colleagues to support their development.
   - Supports colleagues with their development by offering help and guidance.
   - Openly shares knowledge, experience, and expertise to support others' learning.
   - Invests time in developing own skills and capabilities to meet future business needs.
2. **Enabling High Performance**:
   - Agrees personal objectives that deliver a clear contribution to the team.
   - Embraces autonomy and uses initiative within agreed parameters to deliver own objectives, overcoming obstacles where necessary.
   - Balances quality of output with pace of delivery, prioritizing effectively.
   - Seeks opportunities to challenge oneself, actively seeking feedback to improve performance.

#### Interview Questions & Evaluator Rubric:
- **Core Question**: *"Tell me about a time you mentored a junior peer or teammate who was struggling with a complex technical topic."*
- **Positive Evidence**: Candidate breaks down complex concepts, patiently coaches peer, follows up, and learns from the teaching experience.
- **Negative Evidence**: Impatience, doing the work for the peer rather than coaching, blaming others for lack of progress.

---

### Competency 2: Business Skills
> *"Understands business goals, thinks commercially, and identifies opportunities to improve processes, services, and client outcomes."*

#### Capabilities & Expected Behaviors (BA4 Level):
1. **Driving Continuous Improvement and Innovation**:
   - Actively reviews existing processes and tasks, identifying areas for improvement or simplification.
   - Champions and implements continuous improvement ideas, encouraging colleagues to do the same.
   - Learns from mistakes, sharing lessons learned with others.
2. **Defining and Executing Change**:
   - Demonstrates a positive and flexible approach to change, actively supporting others through change.
   - Understands the reasons for change and communicates them effectively to others.
   - Manages own time effectively during periods of transition and change.
3. **Commercial Decision Making**:
   - Understands how own role contributes to team and business performance.
   - Considers commercial impact and customer outcomes when making decisions or recommendations.
   - Understands and applies cost management principles within own area of responsibility.

#### Interview Questions & Evaluator Rubric:
- **Core Question**: *"Describe a project where you recognized that an existing manual workflow was inefficient and redesigned it."*
- **Positive Evidence**: Quantifiable efficiency gains, user adoption focus, understanding impact on cost/time.

---

### Competency 3: Interpersonal Skills
> *"Communicates effectively, builds strong relationships, and collaborates openly across diverse teams to deliver shared outcomes."*

#### Capabilities & Expected Behaviors (BA4 Level):
1. **Building Self-Insight**:
   - Recognizes own strengths and development areas, actively seeking feedback from others.
   - Reflects on own behavior and impact on others, adapting style where appropriate.
   - Demonstrates resilience and composure under pressure.
2. **Building Relationships**:
   - Builds open, honest, and trusting relationships with colleagues and stakeholders.
   - Communicates clearly, concisely, and effectively, adapting communication style to the audience.
   - Actively listens to others, demonstrating empathy and understanding diverse perspectives.
3. **Working Collaboratively**:
   - Works effectively with colleagues across different teams, functions, and locations.
   - Encourages teamwork and collaboration, supporting colleagues to achieve shared goals.
   - Manages conflict constructively, seeking win-win outcomes.

#### Interview Questions & Evaluator Rubric:
- **Core Question**: *"Tell me about a disagreement you had with a colleague regarding technical methodology. How did you resolve it?"*
- **Positive Evidence**: Objective focus on evidence/data, active listening, respectful alignment, no defensive ego.

---

### Competency 4: Control Environment
> *"Takes personal responsibility for managing risk, maintaining governance, and upholding Barclays' commitment to conduct, compliance, and regulatory integrity."*

#### Capabilities & Expected Behaviors (BA4 Level):
1. **Building an Effective Control Environment**:
   - Understands and complies with relevant policies, procedures, and regulatory requirements (SR 11-7, PRA SS1/23).
   - Identifies, manages, and escalates potential risks, errors, or breaches in a timely and transparent manner.
   - Follows through on risk remediation actions and supports a strong risk management culture.
   - Takes personal responsibility for data integrity, audit trails, and security of sensitive banking information.

#### Interview Questions & Evaluator Rubric:
- **Core Question**: *"Describe a situation where you discovered an error in code or data that could have gone unnoticed. What did you do?"*
- **Positive Evidence**: Immediate transparent escalation, thorough root-cause audit, implementation of permanent preventive control.
- **Negative Evidence**: Concealing error, fixing silently without documentation, rationalizing that the error was insignificant.

---

### Competency 5: Technical Skills & Planning/Organising
> *"Applies deep technical expertise, quantitative rigor, and structured organization to deliver high-quality analytical deliverables on schedule."*

#### Capabilities & Expected Behaviors (BA4 Level):
1. **Technical Capability (Risk QA Focus)**:
   - Command of credit risk concepts (PD, LGD, EAD, IFRS 9 ECL, Basel III/IV IRB).
   - Proven mathematical fluency in discrimination (AUC/Gini/KS), calibration (Hosmer-Lemeshow), and stability (PSI).
   - Production proficiency in Python data pipelines, SQL window querying, and Excel governance models.
2. **Planning and Organising**:
   - Plans, organizes, and prioritizes daily workload to meet agreed deadlines.
   - Anticipates potential roadblocks and implements contingency plans.
   - Structures complex quantitative analyses into clear, logical steps.
