### 3.1 What QA Model Monitoring Does (And What It Does NOT Do)
- **What It DOES**:
  - Monitors the ongoing performance, accuracy, stability, and calibration of **Credit Risk Models** across Retail (Mortgages, Cards, Personal Loans, Auto, SME) and Wholesale (Corporate Lending, Leveraged Finance) banking portfolios.
  - Monitors **IFRS 9 Impairment & Expected Credit Loss (ECL)** models and **Basel Regulatory Capital (IRB)** models.
  - Performs statistical back-testing, calculates discrimination metrics (ROC-AUC, Gini, KS), calibration tests (Hosmer-Lemeshow, Brier score), and population stability indices (PSI, CSI).
  - Produces regular **Model Monitoring Packs** presented to Senior Management, Model Governance Committees, and Portfolio Risk Teams.
  - Automates monitoring pipelines using Python, SQL, Excel, SAS, and R.
- **What It DOES NOT DO**:
  - It is **NOT** a Front-Office Quant Developer role building algorithmic high-frequency trading (HFT) strategies or derivatives pricing engines (Black-Scholes / Local Volatility).
  - It is **NOT** an independent Model Validation role (which sits in the 2nd Line of Defense under Independent Model Review / Model Risk Management); QA Model Monitoring sits directly within Quantitative Analytics to track model health post-deployment and initiate model remediation.

## 4. Key Accountabilities & Working Environment

From the official job specification:

1. **Model Construction Mastery**: Thoroughly understand the mathematical, statistical, and empirical construction of credit risk scorecards, PD rating systems, LGD workout engines, and EAD conversion factor models.
2. **Production of Monitoring Packs**: Produce timely, auditable, and mathematically accurate periodic monitoring packs across wholesale and retail portfolios.
3. **KPI & Trend Interpretation**: Interpret model performance KPIs, identify performance degradation, detect population drift, and isolate root causes (e.g., macroeconomic shifts vs. poor underwriting).
4. **Portfolio Interaction**: Discuss monitoring findings with business portfolio teams (e.g., UK Mortgages, Barclaycard, Corporate Lending) to determine whether model deterioration stems from credit policy changes or data pipeline breaks.
5. **Committee Presentation**: Present findings and recommended actions (e.g., recalibration, post-model adjustments [PMA], or model rebuild) to Model Governance Committees.
6. **Automation & Standardization**: Drive automation of data extraction (SQL), metric calculation (Python/R/SAS), and executive dashboard generation (Excel).
7. **Governance Documentation**: Record minutes of oversight meetings, maintain model issue logs, track remediation action items, and ensure compliance with internal Enterprise-Wide Risk Management Framework (EWRMF) policies.
