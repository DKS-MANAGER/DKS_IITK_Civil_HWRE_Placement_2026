### 1.1 The Barclays Quantitative Analytics (QA) Organization
**Barclays** is a transatlantic consumer, corporate, and investment bank founded in London in 1690, operating across 40+ countries. 

Inside Barclays, the **Quantitative Analytics (QA)** organization represents the bank’s global quantitative center of excellence. Historically famous for its pioneering Front-Office derivative pricing models (Barclays Capital), QA today encompasses a vast, critical **Risk Quantitative Analytics (Risk QA)** division.

The **QA Model Monitoring** department in **Noida, India** operates as the bank's frontline quantitative sentinel. Once credit risk models are developed and approved by regulatory bodies, they do not remain static. Economic conditions change, borrower credit behavior evolves, and regulatory policies shift. **QA Model Monitoring tracks, back-tests, audits, and evaluates every live decision, impairment, and regulatory capital model across Barclays' global banking portfolios**.
