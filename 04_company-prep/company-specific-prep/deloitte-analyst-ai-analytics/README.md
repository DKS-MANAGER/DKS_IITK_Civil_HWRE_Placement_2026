Depending on your preparation timeline before the campus drive:

### Pathway A: 30-Day Complete Mastery (Comprehensive)
* **Days 1–7**: Modules `01_` (Aptitude/Logic) & `02_` (Python DSA) + Questions Q001–Q060.
* **Days 8–14**: Modules `03_` (SQL) & `04_` (Statistics) + Questions Q061–Q120. Attempt **Mock 01 & Mock 02**.
* **Days 15–21**: Modules `05_` (Data Eng), `06_` (ML), `07_` (GenAI/RAG), `08_` (Agents/Graph) + Questions Q121–Q180. Attempt **Mock 03**.
* **Days 22–27**: Modules `09_` (ERP), `10_` (Business Cases), `11_` (Behavioral STAR) + Questions Q181–Q200.
* **Days 28–30**: Attempt **Mock 04** under strict 90-minute timed conditions. Review [`RAPID_REVISION.md`](RAPID_REVISION.md).

### Pathway B: 14-Day Accelerated Pathway (High-Yield)
* Focus strictly on **Priority 1 (P1)** topics identified in [`STUDY_MATRIX.md`](STUDY_MATRIX.md).
* Complete even-numbered questions in [`QUESTION_BANK_WITH_SOLUTIONS.md`](QUESTION_BANK_WITH_SOLUTIONS.md) (100 questions).
* Complete **Mock 02** (Technical) and **Mock 04** (Comprehensive).

### Pathway C: 72-Hour Emergency Revision (Final Sprint)
1. Read [`RAPID_REVISION.md`](RAPID_REVISION.md) end-to-end (Formulas, Window SQL, Python idioms, ML metrics).
2. Review the 7 Enterprise Business Cases in [`10_BUSINESS_CASES_RESEARCH_POC_AND_CLIENT_COMMUNICATION.md`](10_BUSINESS_CASES_RESEARCH_POC_AND_CLIENT_COMMUNICATION.md).

4. Review Questions Q081–Q120 (SQL/Stats) and Q161–Q180 (GenAI/RAG) in [`QUESTION_BANK_WITH_SOLUTIONS.md`](QUESTION_BANK_WITH_SOLUTIONS.md).
