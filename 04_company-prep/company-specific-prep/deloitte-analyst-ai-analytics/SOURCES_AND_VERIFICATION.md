## 3. Public Candidate Reports & Historical Pattern Analysis

The following patterns reflect aggregated campus recruitment debriefs from premier engineering institutions across 2024–2026 `[PUBLIC CANDIDATE REPORT — UNVERIFIED]`:

1. **Online Assessment Format**:
   * Platform: AMCAT / CoCubes / HirePro / Modern Hire.
   * Typical Sectional Structure: Quantitative Aptitude (20–25 questions), Logical Reasoning (20–25 questions), Verbal Ability (20–25 questions), and Computer Programming/SQL/Tech MCQs (20–30 questions).
   * In select tech tracks, 1 or 2 live coding questions in Python/C++/Java are included.
2. **Technical Interview Dynamics**:
   * Duration: 30 to 45 minutes.
   * Emphasis on SQL queries (joins, window functions, aggregation), Python fundamentals, basic OOP, data wrangling, and relational database design.
   * Machine Learning & AI discussions focus on project architecture, feature engineering, and model validation rather than pure theoretical proofs.
3. **Managerial & Partner Interview**:
   * Focus on business communication, client scenario handling, resolving stakeholder conflict, shift flexibility, and learning agility.
   * Case studies and guesstimates are frequently used to test structured thinking.
