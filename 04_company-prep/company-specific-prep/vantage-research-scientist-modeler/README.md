## 1. Executive Summary & Mission

This preparation hub provides an authoritative, mathematically rigorous, and evidence-based training curriculum for the **Scientist – Modeler** campus recruitment drive by **Vantage Research** at IIT Kanpur (Placement 2026–27 Phase 1).

Vantage Research is a premier Contract Research Organization (CRO) specializing in **Model-Informed Drug Development (MIDD)** and **Quantitative Systems Pharmacology (QSP)**. The firm recruits candidates across engineering, mathematics, and physics disciplines who possess deep mathematical maturity, ordinary/partial differential equation (ODE/PDE) modeling acumen, parameter estimation skills, and scientific coding fluency.

### Critical Placement Insight: The Dual-Evaluation Mechanism
* Candidates submit a single application for the **Scientist – Modeler** role.
* Candidates are concurrently evaluated across two professional tiers:
  1. **Scientist 1 (Sc1)**: Demonstrates strong fundamentals, execution discipline, and rapid learning potential under guidance.
  2. **Scientist 2 (Sc2)**: Demonstrates technical maturity, independent model formulation, deep scientific reasoning, advanced sensitivity/optimization expertise, and clear client-facing communication.
* **Target Objective**: This package trains the candidate primarily to the **Scientist 2 standard**, while securing the foundational core as an Sc1 benchmark.
