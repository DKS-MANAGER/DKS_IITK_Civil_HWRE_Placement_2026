## 6. Official Qualifications & Skill Requirements (Verified from JD)

### Stated Technical Competencies (`[OFFICIAL VR PORTAL]`)
1. ODE/PDE model simulation using MATLAB, R, Python, or other programming languages.
2. Ability to develop ODE models for physical or engineering systems.
3. Parameter estimation and optimization, including least-squares minimization and global optimization.
4. Local and global sensitivity analysis.
5. Mathematical visualization and interpretation.
6. Stochastic simulation approaches.
7. Statistics and uncertainty analysis.

### Stated Research & Communication Aptitude (`[OFFICIAL VR PORTAL]`)
1. Drawing inferences from simulated and experimental data.
2. Asking meaningful "what-if" questions to guide model exploration.
3. Surveying scientific literature and extracting biological mechanisms.
4. Communicating effectively with interdisciplinary client research teams.
5. Summarizing research workflows and contributing to pharmaceutical decision goals.
