## 2. Corporate Profile: Merilytics & The 2023 Strategic Acquisition

Founded in 2011 in Hyderabad, India by **Paavan Choudary**, **Merilytics** emerged as an elite analytics and data solutions provider delivering advanced decision sciences, business intelligence, machine learning, and data management solutions to global clients across financial services, private equity, healthcare, and retail.

### 2.1 The Strategic Rationale of the May 2023 Acquisition
In May 2023, Accordion officially acquired Merilytics to form the operational cornerstone of its **Global Data & Analytics Practice**:
1. **The Private Equity Paradigm Shift**: Historically, private equity firms generated target returns (20–25% IRR) through financial leverage (cheap debt) and multiple expansion (buying at 8× EBITDA, selling at 12× EBITDA). In today's higher interest-rate macroeconomic environment, multiple expansion has evaporated. Value creation must be driven by **operational performance improvement, organic revenue acceleration, pricing power, and EBITDA margin expansion**.
2. **In-House Data Execution vs. Third-Party Vendors**: Rather than outsourcing complex data engineering and reporting to fragmented third parties, the acquisition allows Accordion to provide an integrated delivery model: US-based CFO advisors paired with Hyderabad-based elite data engineers, AI specialists, and financial modelers.
3. **The Hyderabad Global Hub**: Following the acquisition, Accordion expanded its presence in Hyderabad, creating a state-of-the-art analytics center housing hundreds of data professionals collaborating directly with North American and European portfolio company leadership.

### 3.1 What Does "The Office of the CFO" Actually Mean?
In private equity, the CFO is no longer just a backward-looking chief bookkeeper; the CFO is the **strategic co-pilot to the CEO and the PE Board of Directors**. The Office of the CFO requires:
- **Instant Financial Visibility**: Knowing daily cash burn, weekly booking rates, and monthly gross margins without waiting 20 days for month-end accounting close.
- **Granular Profitability Attribution**: Knowing which product SKUs, customer cohorts, and sales territories generate genuine positive contribution margins versus hidden operational losses.
- **Data-Backed Scenario Modeling**: Stress-testing what happens to debt service coverage ratios (DSCR) if customer churn increases by 3% or raw material inflation jumps by 5%.

## 6. The Data & AI Analyst 2 Daily Workflow

An Analyst at Accordion (Merilytics) operates across three distinct modes:
1. **The Ingestion & Modeling Mode (40% of time)**:
   - Extracting messy transactional tables from client ERPs (NetSuite, SAP, Microsoft Dynamics, QuickBooks) and CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot).
   - Writing production SQL queries and Python cleaning scripts to harmonize disparate schemas.
   - Building dynamic Excel models with multi-sheet architectures, PowerPivot relationships, and DAX measures.
2. **The Synthesis & Verification Mode (35% of time)**:
   - Performing reconciliation tie-outs: Verifying that the analytical model reconciles 100.0% to the general ledger and audited P&L.
   - Decomposing variances: Identifying whether an EBITDA drop was driven by volume decline, price discounting, product mix shift, or cost inflation.
   - Formulating hypotheses: *"Why did West Coast gross margins compress by 420 bps in Q3?"*
3. **The Client Communication Mode (25% of time)**:
   - Translating technical query findings into executive-ready PowerPoint exhibits and interactive Power BI dashboards.
   - Presenting directly to US engagement leads, portfolio company CFOs, and private equity operating partners.
   - Answering the strategic **"So What?"**: What operational changes should management enact based on this analysis?
