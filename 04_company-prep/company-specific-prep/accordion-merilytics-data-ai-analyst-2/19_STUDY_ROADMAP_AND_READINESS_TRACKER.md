## 4. Pre-Interview Execution Checklist (Day of Interview)

### 4.1 Technical Readiness & Benchmarks
- [ ] Memorized standard PE KPIs: LTV:CAC (>3x), NRR (>110%), DSO (<45d), Rule of 40 ($\ge 40\%$).
- [ ] Rehearsed live SQL screen-share setup (clean editor font, fast indentation).
- [ ] Memorized the 10-step logical query processing order.
- [ ] Reviewed the 30-second and 3-minute candidate project defense pitches.

### 4.2 Consulting Delivery Etiquette
- [ ] **Lead with the Answer**: State the executive recommendation first (Pyramid Principle), then state the supporting calculations.
- [ ] **Structure Aloud**: *"I'd like to analyze this margin compression across three lenses: First, Revenue & Price Realization; Second, COGS & Direct Materials; and Third, Operating Overhead."*
- [ ] **Always State the "So-What"**: Never finish an analysis by stating a bare number; explain what operational action management should take based on the findings.
