## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Mission

This preparation suite provides an authoritative, evidence-audited, and technically exhaustive placement training package for the **Data & AI Analyst 2** recruitment drive by **Accordion (Merilytics)** at IIT Kanpur.

### 1.1 Corporate Context: The Accordion–Merilytics Integration
- **Accordion Partners**: Founded in 2009 by Nick Leopard and headquartered in New York City, Accordion is the premier financial consulting firm exclusively dedicated to the **Office of the CFO in Private Equity (PE)**. Accordion works alongside sponsor sponsors (e.g., Carlyle, KKR, Bain Capital, Francisco Partners, Thomas H. Lee) and their middle-market portfolio companies (portcos) to drive operational improvements, financial planning & analysis (FP&A), EBITDA expansion, and transaction exit readiness.
- **Merilytics**: Founded in 2011 by Paavan Choudary and headquartered in Hyderabad, India, Merilytics emerged as a world-class analytics and data solutions provider specializing in business intelligence, machine learning, and decision sciences for global private equity and corporate clients.
- **The May 2023 Acquisition**: In May 2023, Accordion acquired Merilytics to form the operational foundation of its new in-house **Global Data & Analytics Practice**. The acquisition merged Accordion’s deep CFO advisory and financial transformation heritage with Merilytics' world-class data engineering, AI, and analytics execution engine.
- **The Hyderabad Delivery Engine**: The Hyderabad delivery center serves as the core global analytics engine, scaling to hundreds of data professionals who collaborate directly with US onshore consulting teams and C-suite portfolio company executives.
