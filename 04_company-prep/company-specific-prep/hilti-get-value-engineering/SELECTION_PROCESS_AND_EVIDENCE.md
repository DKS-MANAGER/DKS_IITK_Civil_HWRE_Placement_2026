## 3. Preparation Strategy Recommendations (`[PREPARATION INFERENCE]`)

1. **Master the Whiteboard Free-Body Diagram:** Technical interviewers at Hilti frequently test your intuition by drawing an irregular bracket or support frame on a whiteboard and asking you to sketch the load path, shear force diagram, and bending moment diagram in under 2 minutes.
2. **Understand Anchorage Failure Modes Inside-Out:** Do not walk into a Hilti interview thinking anchors are just "bolts in holes". You must be able to clearly derive and explain the Concrete Capacity Design (CCD) method, concrete cone breakout angles (typically $35^\circ$ idealization yielding a $1.5 h_{ef}$ projected edge distance), pullout vs concrete breakout, and pryout failure under shear.
3. **Internalize Value Engineering Philosophy:** Value engineering is NOT "making things as cheap as possible". It is **optimizing total installation efficiency, safety, and material consumption without ever compromising structural design margins**. Be prepared to defend this distinction with absolute clarity.
