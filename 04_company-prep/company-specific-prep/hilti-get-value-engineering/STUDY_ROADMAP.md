## 2. Dedicated Study Tracks

### Track A: 1-Day Emergency Revision Track (Exam-Eve / Assessment Tomorrow)
*Target: 6 to 8 hours of high-yield focus directly prior to assessment.*
* **Hour 1–2: Master Formulas & Checklists**  
  - Review [`RAPID_REVISION.md`](RAPID_REVISION.md) thoroughly (Anchor breakout equations, CCD projected area $A_{Nc}$, beam deflections, interaction equations).
  - Skim the "5-Minute Pre-Assessment Mental Checklist".
* **Hour 3–4: Anchors & Support System Essentials**  
  - Read [`03_ANCHORS_SUPPORT_SYSTEMS_AND_FAILURE_MODES.md`](03_ANCHORS_SUPPORT_SYSTEMS_AND_FAILURE_MODES.md) (All 8 failure modes, cracked concrete effects, borehole cleaning traps).
* **Hour 5–6: Value Engineering Principles & Cases**  
  - Review the 9-step Value Engineering methodology and Cases 1 through 5 in [`04_VALUE_ENGINEERING_AND_DESIGN_OPTIMISATION.md`](04_VALUE_ENGINEERING_AND_DESIGN_OPTIMISATION.md).
* **Hour 7–8: Timed Mock Simulation**  
  - Attempt Mock Test 1 (Structural Fundamentals) and Mock Test 3 (Hilti Connections & VE) in [`09_MOCK_TESTS_AND_SOLUTIONS.md`](09_MOCK_TESTS_AND_SOLUTIONS.md). Verify all mistakes against the separated answer keys.

### Track B: 3-Day Focused Technical Sprint
*Target: 4 to 5 hours daily for candidates with 72 hours until testing/interview.*
* **Day 1: Structural Mechanics & Connections**  
  - Complete [`01_STRUCTURAL_ENGINEERING_FUNDAMENTALS.md`](01_STRUCTURAL_ENGINEERING_FUNDAMENTALS.md) and [`02_STEEL_RCC_AND_CONNECTION_DESIGN.md`](02_STEEL_RCC_AND_CONNECTION_DESIGN.md).
  - Solve worked numericals on base plates and weld throats.
  - Attempt Mock Test 1 (30 Questions, 45 Mins).
* **Day 2: Anchors, Failure Modes & Value Engineering**  
  - Master [`03_ANCHORS_SUPPORT_SYSTEMS_AND_FAILURE_MODES.md`](03_ANCHORS_SUPPORT_SYSTEMS_AND_FAILURE_MODES.md) and [`04_VALUE_ENGINEERING_AND_DESIGN_OPTIMISATION.md`](04_VALUE_ENGINEERING_AND_DESIGN_OPTIMISATION.md).
  - Review modular support systems (Hilti MT vs hot-rolled channel) in [`06_HILTI_PRODUCTS_APPLICATIONS_AND_INDUSTRY_AWARENESS.md`](06_HILTI_PRODUCTS_APPLICATIONS_AND_INDUSTRY_AWARENESS.md).
  - Attempt Mock Test 2 and Mock Test 3 (60 Questions total).
* **Day 3: Calculations, Interview Defense & Final Simulation**  
  - Practice derivations in [`05_ENGINEERING_CALCULATIONS_AND_NUMERICALS.md`](05_ENGINEERING_CALCULATIONS_AND_NUMERICALS.md).
  - Rehearse technical interview questions and STAR stories in [`07_TECHNICAL_INTERVIEW_AND_CASE_STUDIES.md`](07_TECHNICAL_INTERVIEW_AND_CASE_STUDIES.md).
  - Attempt Mock Test 4 (Full 40-Question Simulation) under strict exam conditions.

### Track C: 7-Day Complete Mastery Roadmap
*Target: Comprehensive mastery for students preparing thoroughly.*
* **Day 1:** Structural Mechanics, Equilibrium, SFD/BMD, Curvature & Deflection (Module 01).
* **Day 2:** Structural Steel (Tension, Compression, Bending) & Welded/Bolted Connections (Module 02).
* **Day 3:** Concrete Design, Development Length, and Base Plate Design (Module 02).
* **Day 4:** Post-Installed Anchors, CCD Method, and Failure Modes (Module 03).
* **Day 5:** Value Engineering Methodology, 20 Solved Case Studies & Modular Systems (Modules 04 & 06).
* **Day 6:** Numerical Problem Bank (Module 05) & Quantitative Aptitude / Logic Drill (Module 08).
* **Day 7:** Solve All 4 Mock Assessments in Module 09, review Readiness Scorecard, and conduct mock interview rehearsal.
