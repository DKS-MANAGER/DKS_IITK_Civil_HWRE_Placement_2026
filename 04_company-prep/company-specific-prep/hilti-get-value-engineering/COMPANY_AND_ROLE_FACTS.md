### Core Business Unit: Energy & Industry (E&I)
The Energy & Industry division addresses severe, mission-critical engineering environments:
1. **Oil, Gas & Chemical Plants:** Heavy pipe routing, explosive zone firestopping, dynamic vibration resistance, and marine/coastal atmospheric corrosion.
2. **Power Generation & Nuclear:** Seismic-qualified anchorages, heavy equipment base plates, and radiation-resistant penetrations.
3. **Data Centers & High-Tech Facilities:** Rapid-installation modular overhead trapeze supports for immense cable density, seismic bracing, and zero-downtime retrofits.
4. **Heavy Infrastructure & Tunnels:** Pre-cast tunnel segment fastening, trackbed utility fastening, and structural post-installed rebar extensions.
