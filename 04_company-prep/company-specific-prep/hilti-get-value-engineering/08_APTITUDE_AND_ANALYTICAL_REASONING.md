## 2. 30 Solved Aptitude & Reasoning Practice Questions

### Problem 8.1: Work Efficiency and Multi-Crew Installation
**Question:** Crew A takes 12 hours to assemble a modular pipe rack. Crew B takes 18 hours for the same rack. If both crews work together for 4 hours, what fraction of the assembly remains to be completed?
**Solution:**
- Rate of Crew A: $1/12$ per hour.
- Rate of Crew B: $1/18$ per hour.
- Combined rate: $\frac{1}{12} + \frac{1}{18} = \frac{3 + 2}{36} = \frac{5}{36}$ per hour.
- Work done in 4 hours: $4 \times \frac{5}{36} = \frac{20}{36} = \frac{5}{9}$.
- Fraction remaining: $1 - \frac{5}{9} = \frac{4}{9}$.

### Problem 8.2: Ratio & Mixture Optimization
**Question:** A chemical resin mixture contains Polymer A and Hardener B in the ratio $5:2$. If $14\text{ liters}$ of Hardener B are added, the ratio becomes $5:4$. Find the original volume of Polymer A.
**Solution:**
- Let original volumes be $5x$ and $2x$.
- After adding $14\text{ liters}$ of B: $\frac{5x}{2x + 14} = \frac{5}{4}$.
- Cross-multiplying: $4(5x) = 5(2x + 14) \implies 20x = 10x + 70 \implies 10x = 70 \implies x = 7$.
- Original volume of Polymer A: $5 \times 7 = 35\text{ liters}$.

### Problem 8.3: Time, Speed, and Material Transport
**Question:** A transport truck carries modular steel components from a fabrication yard to a site $180\text{ km}$ away at an average speed of $45\text{ km/h}$. On the return trip empty, its speed increases to $60\text{ km/h}$. Find the average speed for the entire round trip.
**Solution:**
- Average speed for equal distance round trip:
  $$V_{avg} = \frac{2 v_1 v_2}{v_1 + v_2} = \frac{2 \times 45 \times 60}{45 + 60} = \frac{5400}{105} = 51.43\text{ km/h}$$

### Problem 8.4: Percentage Cost Escalation
**Question:** The price of raw structural steel increases by $25\%$. By what percentage must a fabricator reduce steel consumption so that the total expenditure remains unchanged?
**Solution:**
- Let initial price $= P$, consumption $= C$, expenditure $= P \cdot C$.
- New price $= 1.25 P$.
- New consumption $C' = \frac{P \cdot C}{1.25 P} = \frac{C}{1.25} = 0.80 C$.
- Reduction: $(1 - 0.80) \times 100\% = 20.0\%$.

### Problem 8.5: Gear Ratio and Rotational Velocity
**Question:** Gear A with 36 teeth meshes with Gear B with 12 teeth. Gear B is keyed to the same shaft as Gear C with 48 teeth. Gear C meshes with Gear D with 16 teeth. If Gear A rotates at $100\text{ rpm}$ clockwise, find the rotational speed and direction of Gear D.
**Solution:**
- Ratio A to B: $\omega_B = \omega_A \times (N_A / N_B) = 100 \times (36 / 12) = 300\text{ rpm}$ (counter-clockwise).
- Shaft speed: $\omega_C = \omega_B = 300\text{ rpm}$ (counter-clockwise).
- Ratio C to D: $\omega_D = \omega_C \times (N_C / N_D) = 300 \times (48 / 16) = 300 \times 3 = 900\text{ rpm}$ (clockwise).
- **Result:** $900\text{ rpm}$, clockwise.

### Problem 8.6: Pulley System Mechanical Advantage
**Question:** A crane rigging system uses a 4-pulley tackle system (4 rope segments supporting the movable load block). Neglecting friction, what effort force is required to hoist a $24\text{ kN}$ modular steel assembly?
**Solution:**
- Mechanical Advantage $\text{MA} = 4$.
- Required Effort: $E = \frac{\text{Load}}{\text{MA}} = \frac{24\text{ kN}}{4} = 6.0\text{ kN}$.

### Problem 8.7: Probability in Fastener Quality Inspection
**Question:** A batch of 1,000 anchor bolts contains $5\%$ defective bolts. If 3 bolts are drawn at random without replacement, what is the probability that all 3 bolts are non-defective?
**Solution:**
- Non-defective bolts: $950$; Defective: $50$.
- Probability:
  $$P = \frac{950}{1000} \times \frac{949}{999} \times \frac{948}{998} \approx 0.950 \times 0.9499 \times 0.9499 \approx 0.857 \quad (85.7\%)$$

### Problem 8.8: Combinations in Anchor Hole Grouping
**Question:** A base plate features a grid of 8 pre-drilled holes. In how many distinct ways can an engineer select 4 holes to install anchor bolts?
**Solution:**
$$C(8, 4) = \frac{8 \times 7 \times 6 \times 5}{4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1} = \frac{1680}{24} = 70\text{ ways}$$

### Problem 8.9: Profit Margin & Value Pricing
**Question:** A modular bracket costs $\text{INR } 1,200$ to manufacture. It is marked up by $40\%$ above cost. During a contract negotiation, a commercial discount of $15\%$ is offered. Find the final selling price and net profit percentage.
**Solution:**
- Marked Price: $1200 \times 1.40 = \text{INR } 1,680$.
- Selling Price after $15\%$ discount: $1680 \times 0.85 = \text{INR } 1,428$.
- Net Profit: $1428 - 1200 = \text{INR } 228$.
- Profit Margin: $(228 / 1200) \times 100\% = 19.0\%$.

### Problem 8.10: Linear Sequence & Series in Modular Grid Spacing
**Question:** Modular support frames are placed along an industrial pipe corridor. The distance from the origin to frame $n$ follows the arithmetic progression: $d_n = 4.5 n + 2.0\text{ meters}$. Find the total distance covered by 20 consecutive frames, and the spacing between the 15th and 16th frames.
**Solution:**
- Spacing between consecutive frames is the common difference: $d = 4.5\text{ meters}$.
- Distance of 20th frame from origin: $d_{20} = 4.5(20) + 2.0 = 92.0\text{ meters}$.
- Spacing between 15th and 16th frame: $4.5\text{ meters}$.

### Problems 8.11 to 8.30: Core Aptitude & Analytical Practice Set

#### Problem 8.11: Syllogism
*Statements:* All structural members are load-bearing. Some load-bearing elements are ductile. All ductile elements absorb seismic energy.
*Conclusion:* Can we definitely conclude that "Some structural members absorb seismic energy"?
*Solution:* No. While all structural members are load-bearing, only *some* load-bearing elements are ductile. Those ductile elements might not overlap with structural members. Hence, conclusion does not follow with certainty.

#### Problem 8.12: Direction Sense Test
*Problem:* A surveyor starts at Point A, walks $40\text{ m}$ North, turns right and walks $30\text{ m}$, turns right again and walks $70\text{ m}$, then turns left and walks $10\text{ m}$ to reach Point B. Find the straight-line displacement from A to B.
*Solution:* North-South: $+40 - 70 = -30\text{ m}$ (South). East-West: $+30 + 10 = +40\text{ m}$ (East). Displacement: $\sqrt{(-30)^2 + 40^2} = \sqrt{900 + 1600} = \sqrt{2500} = 50\text{ m}$ South-East.

#### Problem 8.13: Pipe Drainage Rate
*Problem:* Pipe A fills a hydraulic testing reservoir in 6 hours. Pipe B empties it in 9 hours. If both are opened simultaneously, how long will it take to fill the reservoir?
*Solution:* Net rate $= 1/6 - 1/9 = (3 - 2)/18 = 1/18$ per hour. Time $= 18\text{ hours}$.

#### Problem 8.14: Seating Arrangement Deduction
*Problem:* Six engineers (P, Q, R, S, T, U) sit around a circular table facing the center. P is opposite S. Q is to the immediate right of P. R is between P and T. Who is sitting opposite Q?
*Solution:* Tracing clockwise positions: If P is at 12 o'clock, S is at 6 o'clock. Q is at 11 o'clock (immediate right when facing center). R is at 1 o'clock, T is at 2 o'clock. U must be at 7 o'clock or 5 o'clock. Opposite Q (11 o'clock) is the position at 5 o'clock, which must be U.

#### Problem 8.15: Coding & Decoding
*Problem:* In a certain code, "BEAM" is coded as "YVZN". How is "STRUT" coded using the same substitution cipher?
*Solution:* The cipher replaces each letter with its opposite in the alphabet ($A \leftrightarrow Z, B \leftrightarrow Y, \dots$).
$S \leftrightarrow H, T \leftrightarrow G, R \leftrightarrow I, U \leftrightarrow F, T \leftrightarrow G$.
"STRUT" is coded as "HGIFG".

#### Problem 8.16: Blood Relations
*Problem:* Pointing to an engineer, Ananya says: "His mother is the only daughter of my grandfather." How is Ananya related to the engineer (assuming Ananya is female)?
*Solution:* "Only daughter of my grandfather" is Ananya's mother. The engineer's mother is Ananya's mother. Therefore, Ananya is the engineer's sister.

#### Problem 8.17: Venn Diagram Overlap
*Problem:* In an engineering team of 60 professionals, 35 know AutoCAD, 28 know Revit, and 15 know both. How many engineers know neither software?
*Solution:* Total knowing at least one $= 35 + 28 - 15 = 48$. Neither $= 60 - 48 = 12$.

#### Problem 8.18: Clocks & Angles
*Problem:* What is the exact angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock at 3:30?
*Solution:* At 3:30, minute hand is at $180^\circ$ (pointing at 6). Hour hand position $= 3 \times 30^\circ + 30 \times 0.5^\circ = 90^\circ + 15^\circ = 105^\circ$. Angle $= 180^\circ - 105^\circ = 75^\circ$.

#### Problem 8.19: Calendar Reasoning
*Problem:* If October 26, 2026 is a Monday, what day of the week was October 26, 2025?
*Solution:* 2026 is a normal year (not a leap year, 365 days). $365 \pmod 7 = 1$ odd day. Going back one year: Monday minus 1 day = Sunday.

#### Problem 8.20: Simple vs Compound Interest in Equipment Financing
*Problem:* An EPC contractor borrows $\text{INR } 10\text{ Lakhs}$ for 2 years at $10\%$ per annum compound interest (compounded annually). How much more interest is paid compared to simple interest?
*Solution:* Simple Interest $= 1,000,000 \times 0.10 \times 2 = \text{INR } 200,000$. Compound Interest $= 1,000,000 \times (1.10^2 - 1) = 1,000,000 \times 0.21 = \text{INR } 210,000$. Difference $= \text{INR } 10,000$.

#### Problem 8.21: Data Sufficiency — Beam Deflection
*Problem:* Can we determine the maximum deflection of a beam?
Statement 1: The beam is a simple span of length $4\text{ m}$ carrying a center load of $10\text{ kN}$.
Statement 2: The flexural rigidity $EI$ is $2 \times 10^4\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}^2$.
*Solution:* Both statements together are sufficient ($\Delta = P L^3 / (48 E I)$), but neither alone is sufficient.

#### Problem 8.22: Spatial Cube Unfolding
*Problem:* A cube has faces numbered 1 through 6. Face 1 is opposite 6, 2 opposite 5, 3 opposite 4. In an unfolded cross net, if face 1 is in the center, which faces can border face 1?
*Solution:* Faces 2, 3, 4, and 5 can border face 1. Face 6 cannot touch face 1 as it is opposite.

#### Problem 8.23: Number Analogy
*Problem:* 8 : 64 :: 12 : ?
*Solution:* Pattern: $x : x^2 \implies 12 : 144$ (or $x : x^3/8 \implies 12 : 216$). In standard tests, 144 is primary.

#### Problem 8.24: Speed, Stream, and Fluid Velocity
*Problem:* A boat moves at $12\text{ km/h}$ in still water. If the river flows at $3\text{ km/h}$, how long will it take to travel $45\text{ km}$ downstream?
*Solution:* Downstream speed $= 12 + 3 = 15\text{ km/h}$. Time $= 45 / 15 = 3\text{ hours}$.

#### Problem 8.25: Critical Path Analysis (Project Duration)
*Problem:* Task A takes 3 days. Task B depends on A and takes 5 days. Task C depends on A and takes 2 days. Task D depends on both B and C and takes 4 days. What is the minimum project duration?
*Solution:* Path 1: A $\to$ B $\to$ D $= 3 + 5 + 4 = 12$ days. Path 2: A $\to$ C $\to$ D $= 3 + 2 + 4 = 9$ days. Critical path is 12 days.

#### Problem 8.26: Probability of Sum on Two Dice
*Problem:* What is the probability of rolling a sum $\ge 10$ with two standard fair 6-sided dice?
*Solution:* Favorable pairs for sum 10: (4,6), (5,5), (6,4) [3]. For sum 11: (5,6), (6,5) [2]. For sum 12: (6,6) [1]. Total favorable $= 6$. Total outcomes $= 36$. Probability $= 6/36 = 1/6 \approx 16.67\%$.

#### Problem 8.27: Geometric Progression in Material Attenuation
*Problem:* An acoustic sound barrier reduces industrial noise intensity by $50\%$ for every $50\text{ mm}$ of panel thickness. What thickness is required to reduce noise to $\le 6.25\%$ of its original level?
*Solution:* $(1/2)^n \le 0.0625 = 1/16 = (1/2)^4 \implies n = 4$ layers. Total thickness $= 4 \times 50\text{ mm} = 200\text{ mm}$.

#### Problem 8.28: Matrix Logic Puzzle
*Problem:* In a $3 \times 3$ grid, rows follow: Row 1: [2, 4, 16], Row 2: [3, 9, 81], Row 3: [4, 16, ?]. What is the missing number?
*Solution:* The third column is the square of the second column: $4^2 = 16$, $9^2 = 81$, $16^2 = 256$. Missing number is 256.

#### Problem 8.29: Worker Allocation
*Problem:* 15 workers can assemble 60 pipe brackets in 4 hours. How many workers are needed to assemble 150 pipe brackets in 5 hours?
*Solution:* Rate per worker-hour $= \frac{60}{15 \times 4} = \frac{60}{60} = 1$ bracket per worker-hour.
To assemble 150 brackets in 5 hours: Required total worker-hours $= 150$. Number of workers $= 150 / 5 = 30$ workers.

#### Problem 8.30: Percentile vs Percentage
*Problem:* In a campus assessment of 500 candidates, 425 candidates scored lower than Rahul. What is Rahul's percentile rank?
*Solution:* $\text{Percentile} = \left(\frac{425}{500}\right) \times 100 = 85.0\text{th percentile}$.
