## 3. European Pricing & Reimbursement Austerity Mechanisms

Unlike the US free-pricing model, European health authorities enforce rigid pricing controls:
1. **International Reference Pricing (IRP)**: A country benchmarks its maximum drug price against a statutory basket of peer nations (e.g., Greece, Portugal, Poland). If a manufacturer launches at a discount in Italy, that lower price automatically triggers forced price reductions across France and Spain.
2. **Mandatory Statutory Clawbacks**: In Germany (AMNOG) and the UK (VPAG), if national pharmaceutical expenditures exceed statutory government budget caps, manufacturers must repay the excess revenue directly to the government.

## 4. Demographic & Epidemiological Megatrends

1. **The "Silver Tsunami" (Global Aging)**: By 2030, 1 in 6 people worldwide will be aged 60 or older. This demographic shift drives exponential demand in oncology, neurodegenerative diseases (Alzheimer's, Parkinson's), cardiometabolic disorders, and ophthalmology.
2. **The Chronic Disease Epidemiological Transition in Emerging Markets**: Developing nations (India, China, Brazil, Southeast Asia) are rapidly shifting from acute infectious disease mortality to chronic non-communicable diseases (NCDs) such as hypertension, type-2 diabetes, and ischemic heart disease, creating massive volume markets for branded generics and affordable biologics.
