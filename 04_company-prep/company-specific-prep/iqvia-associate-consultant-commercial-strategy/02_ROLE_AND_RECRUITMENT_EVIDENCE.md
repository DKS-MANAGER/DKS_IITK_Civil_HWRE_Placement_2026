### 3.1 What Interviewers Specifically Probe in Consulting Cases
1. **Structuring Without Rigid Frameworks**:
   * *Common Mistake*: Memorizing the generic "4Ps" or "Porter's 5 Forces" and forcing them onto a clinical launch problem.
   * *Winning Behavior*: Constructing a bespoke issue tree addressing the client's specific clinical, competitive, and reimbursement hurdles.
2. **Mental Math & Sanity Checking**:
   * Calculating addressable patient populations, annual treatment costs, and market revenues under time pressure without calculator errors.
3. **The Deck-Building & Slide Test**:
   * Translating raw data tables into clean, executive-ready slide layouts featuring **insight-driven action titles** and MECE exhibit structures.

## 4. HR & Cultural Fit Interview: Global Consulting Delivery

The final stage evaluates alignment with IQVIA's consulting culture:

* **Why Consulting After Engineering?**: Interviewers want to see that the pivot is deliberate and evidence-based—driven by a passion for structured problem-solving, commercial strategy, and executive impact.
* **Why Healthcare Strategy?**: Demonstrating genuine intellectual curiosity for the biopharmaceutical industry without pretending to have clinical credentials.
* **Turnaround Under Pressure**: Client launch deliverables operate under intense time constraints. The candidate must prove work ethic, independent ownership, and graceful handling of critical feedback.
