## 1. What is Commercial Due Diligence (CDD)?

## 3. Auditing the Management "Hockey-Stick" Forecast

In private equity and M&A transactions, target management almost always presents an aggressive "hockey-stick" revenue forecast:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    MANAGEMENT CASE vs IQVIA SENSITIZED CASE                 │
│                                                                             │
│ Revenue ($M)                                                                │
│  $300 ▲                                             /  Management Case      │
│       │                                            /   (Peak Share: 45%)    │
│  $200 │                                           /                         │
│       │                                      .───'                          │
│  $100 │                               .─────'                               │
│       │                     .────────'───────════════  IQVIA Sensitized Case│
│       │               .────'                           (Peak Share: 22%)    │
│    $0 └──────────────┴───────────────┴───────────────► Time                 │
│                   Launch           Year +3         Year +6                  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 Common Management Fallacies Uncovered in CDD
1. **Unrealistic Peak Market Share**: Management assumes their drug will capture $40\text{–}50\%$ of the market without acknowledging that 2 well-funded competitors are launching within 12 months.
2. **Gross vs Net Price Neglect**: Assuming steady net realization without modeling expanding PBM rebate demands ($45\%$ GTN expanding to $60\%$ GTN).
3. **Frictionless Physician Adoption**: Modeling instant trial without accounting for hospital formulary review delays (typically 6 to 9 months post-FDA approval).

## 4. End-to-End CDD Case Study: Private Equity Acquisition of an Anti-VEGF Biosimilar

### 4.1 Transaction Context
A global healthcare private equity fund is evaluating the \$450M buyout of **OcuBio**, a European biosimilar manufacturer marketing a biosimilar to ranibizumab (Lucentis) for Wet Age-Related Macular Degeneration (AMD). Target management projects revenue to expand from \$45M to \$180M within 4 years ($41\%$ CAGR). IQVIA Consulting is engaged to validate the revenue plan.

### 4.2 Key Findings from IQVIA Secondary & Primary Research
* **Market Sizing Finding**: Total European anti-VEGF market is \$2.8B, expanding at $4.5\%$ CAGR driven by an aging demographic.
* **Competitive Threat (Red Flag 1)**: Next-generation long-acting therapies (e.g., Vabysmo / faricimab), requiring eye injections every 16 weeks instead of monthly, are capturing $30\%$ of new patient starts. The market is shifting away from monthly ranibizumab molecules entirely.
* **Payer & Pricing Pressure (Red Flag 2)**: Four rival ranibizumab biosimilars launched simultaneously in Europe, triggering a vicious tender price war. Price erosion reached $-65\%$ vs reference brand, compared to management's assumed $-35\%$.
* **Prescriber Sentiment (Red Flag 3)**: Blind KOL interviews with 30 leading retinal specialists revealed that $80\%$ prefer transitioning stable patients to long-acting faricimab rather than switching between short-acting biosimilars.

```

│                       REVENUE AUDIT RECONCILIATION                          │

├────────────────────┼───────────┼───────────┼───────────┼────────────────────┤
│ Management Case    │ $65.0M    │ $95.0M    │ $135.0M   │ $180.0M            │
```

* **Final Strategic Verdict to Private Equity Sponsor**:
  > **DO NOT PROCEED AT THE PROPOSED \$450M VALUATION.**  
  > *Target management's \$180M terminal revenue forecast is fundamentally unachievable due to severe class displacement by long-acting anti-VEGF agents and aggressive European tender price erosion. True peak revenue is capped at \$82M. Recommend re-negotiating purchase price down to $\le \$210\text{M}$ or walking away from the transaction.*
