## 2. The Clinical "Leakage Funnel" Analysis

In commercial consulting, patient journeys are analyzed through **quantitative attrition funnels**. At each step, a percentage of eligible patients leaks out of the system, drastically diminishing the final commercial brand volume:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       THE CLINICAL ATTRITION LEAKAGE FUNNEL                 │
│                                                                             │
│ 100,000 Total Symptomatic Patients                                          │
│   │                                                                         │
│   ├── [ LEAK 1: Under-diagnosis (40% never visit doctor) ] ──► 40,000 lost  │
│   ▼                                                                         │
│ 60,000 Clinically Diagnosed Patients                                        │
│   │                                                                         │
│   ├── [ LEAK 2: Specialist Referral Delay (30% untreated) ] ──► 18,000 lost │
│   ▼                                                                         │
│ 42,000 Referred to Specialist & Prescribed Brand                            │
│   │                                                                         │
│   ├── [ LEAK 3: Prior Authorization Rejection (25% denied) ] ► 10,500 lost │
│   ▼                                                                         │
│ 31,500 Prescription Dispensed & Initiated                                   │
│   │                                                                         │
│   ├── [ LEAK 4: Discontinuation at 6 Months (35% drop-off) ] ─► 11,025 lost │
```

## 3. Commercial Interventions to Plug Patient Funnel Leaks

Strategy consultants advise brand teams on targeted commercial solutions to address specific leakage points:

| Funnel Leakage Point | Clinical & Behavioral Root Cause | Commercial Strategy Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Leak 1: Under-Diagnosis** | Mild or non-specific early symptoms; primary care doctors lack awareness. | Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) educational awareness; digital symptom checkers. |
| **Leak 2: Referral Delay** | Community PCPs do not refer patients to specialized tertiary centers. | Continuing Medical Education (CME) programs for PCPs; diagnostic toolkits. |
| **Leak 3: Biomarker Testing** | High cost or long turnaround times for genetic/pathology testing. | Manufacturer-subsidized **free genetic testing kits** sent to community clinics. |
| **Leak 4: Payer PA Rejection** | Complex paperwork and prior authorization hurdles from insurers. | Dedicated **Field Reimbursement Managers (FRMs)** and electronic PA software. |
| **Leak 5: Early Discontinuation** | Manageable side effects (e.g., mild nausea) cause patients to stop refills. | Dedicated **Patient Support Programs (PSPs)** with 24/7 nurse call navigators. |

## 4. End-to-End Case Study: Plugging Funnel Leaks in Multiple Sclerosis (MS)

### 4.1 Client Challenge
A biopharma client's novel once-daily oral MS therapy, **NeuroClear**, generated \$85M in Year 2 sales, falling $40\%$ below executive expectations (\$140M plan). The brand team assumed sales reps were failing to detail neurologists. IQVIA was hired to map the patient journey using longitudinal claims data (APLD).

### 4.2 Analytical Findings & Funnel Diagnosis
* **The Prescribing Finding**: Neurologists were actively prescribing NeuroClear (high trial rate, $+25\%$ written scripts YoY).
* **The Hidden Leak (Prior Authorization Failure)**: $38\%$ of written prescriptions were never filled at specialty pharmacies because health plans required patients to fail two cheaper injectable drugs first (**step-therapy failure**). Neurologists were not appealing rejected claims due to administrative burden.
* **The Adherence Leak**: Among patients who received the drug, $42\%$ discontinued therapy within 90 days due to transient gastrointestinal discomfort that typically resolves by Week 4.

### 4.3 Strategic Recommendations to Brand Leadership
1. **Deploy Digital Co-Pay & PA Hub (\$3.5M investment)**: Integrate an automated electronic prior authorization (ePA) workflow with specialty pharmacies, reducing claim rejection from $38\%$ to $14\%$.
2. **Launch "NeuroCare" Nurse Navigator Program (\$2.0M investment)**: Provide patients with proactive phone calls at Day 7, 14, and 30 to coach them through transient gastrointestinal symptoms.
3. **Projected Commercial Outcome**:
   * Plugging the access and adherence leaks is projected to rescue 6,200 patients annually.
   * Total Incremental Revenue: $6,200 \times \$60,000\text{ net price} = \mathbf{+\$372\text{ Million}}$ over 3 years, delivering a $67\times$ return on program investment.
