## 1. Corporate Lineage, Global Scale & Business Model

### 1.1 The Genesis: Merging Market Data Authority with Clinical Power
IQVIA was established in October 2016 through the \$17.6 billion merger of equals between **Quintiles Transnational Holdings Inc.** (founded in 1982 by Dr. Dennis Gillings) and **IMS Health Holdings, Inc.** (founded in 1954 by Bill Frohlich and David Dubow). Rebranded as **IQVIA** in November 2017, the organization unites **IQ** (Information and Intelligence) with **VIA** (the strategic pathway to healthcare advancement).

* **IMS Health Heritage**: The global pioneer in pharmaceutical market intelligence, establishing the industry's official sales tracking currencies: the National Prescription Audit (NPA), Xponent® (physician-level prescription auditing), and MIDAS (cross-country volume and pricing audits).
* **Quintiles Heritage**: The world's largest Contract Research Organization (CRO), providing clinical development, trial execution, and biostatistical filings across Phases I–IV.

### 3.1 Daily Responsibilities of the Associate Consultant
* **Secondary Desk Research**: Mining public and proprietary sources (SEC 10-K filings, clinical trial registries, medical journals, investor day transcripts, IQVIA audit summaries) to synthesize industry intelligence.
* **Market Sizing & Patient Flow Modeling**: Building Excel models that filter total population down to diagnosed, treated, and brand-eligible patient pools.
* **Executive Storytelling in PowerPoint**: Structuring narrative slide decks using the Pyramid Principle, writing action-oriented slide headlines, and visually presenting strategic insights.
* **Critical Evaluation of the "So-What"**: Moving beyond descriptive data to articulate concrete commercial implications (e.g., *"Competitor X's Phase III trial showed superior progression-free survival; therefore, our client must reposition Brand Y around superior safety and convenient oral administration"*).
* **Project Communication & Workstream Ownership**: Proactively highlighting data gaps, managing task timelines, and drafting succinct meeting minutes for global stakeholder calls.

## 5. Major Biopharmaceutical Megatrends Shaping Current Engagements

1. **The 2025–2030 Patent Cliff**: Over \$200 billion in annual pharmaceutical revenue faces patent expiration (e.g., Keytruda, Eliquis, Opdivo). Clients hire IQVIA to formulate Loss-of-Exclusivity (LOE) defense strategies and evaluate in-licensing targets to replace lost revenues.
2. **US Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) Impact**: Medicare drug price negotiation introduces unprecedented price pressure on mature blockbusters. Small-molecule therapies face price negotiation 9 years post-launch, compared to 13 years for biologics, shifting commercial R&D portfolio allocation.
3. **The GLP-1 Metabolic Revolution**: Rapid expansion of GLP-1/GIP receptor agonists across diabetes, obesity, cardiovascular, and kidney disease represents one of the largest market expansions in pharmaceutical history, demanding complex patient journey and supply-chain forecasting.
4. **Cell & Gene Therapies (CGT)**: One-time curative treatments (CAR-T, CRISPR gene-editing) carry million-dollar price tags, forcing clients to develop innovative outcome-based and annuity reimbursement models with health payers.
