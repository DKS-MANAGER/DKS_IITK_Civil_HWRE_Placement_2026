## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Mission

This preparation hub provides an authoritative, structured, and evidence-audited training curriculum for the **Associate Consultant (Commercial Strategy Consulting)** campus recruitment drive by **IQVIA India** at IIT Kanpur.

IQVIA Consulting Services (CS) is the management consulting division of IQVIA (NYSE: `IQV`), a Fortune 500 healthcare intelligence and technology multinational. Unlike generalist strategy firms (McKinsey, BCG, Bain) or technology implementation consultancies, IQVIA Commercial Strategy Consulting operates with a singular, high-value focus: **advising global biopharmaceutical, medical device, and healthcare private equity leadership on high-stakes commercial decisions**.

The **Associate Consultant** role inside IQVIA's Indian consulting delivery centers (Gurugram/Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Mumbai) works directly with onshore engagement managers and global biopharma clients in the US and Europe to deliver:
1. **Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy**: Formulating product launch sequencing, competitive differentiation, value propositions, and sales-force alignment for novel therapeutics.
2. **Commercial Due Diligence (CDD)**: Evaluating market size, competitive defensibility, pipeline sustainability, and revenue trajectory for private equity acquisitions and pharma licensing deals.
3. **Portfolio & Market Assessment**: Prioritizing therapeutic franchises, evaluating clinical unmet needs, and assessing capital allocation across pipeline assets.
4. **Patient Journey & Clinical Flow**: Mapping diagnosis bottlenecks, physician referral patterns, treatment initiation barriers, and compliance drop-offs.
5. **Forecasting & Analogue Analysis**: Synthesizing historical launch analogues and epidemiological funnels into defensible long-range revenue models.
6. **Market Access & Pricing**: Formulating payer value dossiers, evaluating Health Technology Assessment (HTA) hurdles, and establishing formulary tier access.
7. **Executive Storytelling**: Synthesizing complex market data into C-suite ready **Microsoft PowerPoint presentations** and **Excel commercial models**.
