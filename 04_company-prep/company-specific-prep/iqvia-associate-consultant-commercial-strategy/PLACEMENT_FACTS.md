## 3. Compensation Breakdown & Evidentiary Anomaly Analysis

The figures entered into the IIT Kanpur recruitment proforma are recorded below, followed by an immediate audit of mathematical and unit inconsistencies:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       IQVIA PROFORMA COMPENSATION AUDIT                     │
├──────────────────────────┬──────────────────────┬───────────────────────────┤
│ Compensation Element     │ Stated Value (INR)   │ Evidentiary Evaluation    │
├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────┼───────────────────────────┤
│ Basic Salary             │ *Blank on Proforma*  │ Implied: ₹4,80,000 / year │
│ House Rent Allowance     │ ₹4,80,000 / year     │ ₹40,000 / month           │
│ Other Allowances         │ ₹2,40,000 / year     │ ₹20,000 / month           │
├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────┼───────────────────────────┤
│ SUM OF ENTERED ALLOWANCES│ ₹7,20,000 / year     │ HRA + Others              │
├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────┼───────────────────────────┤
│ Stated Gross on Proforma │ ₹12,00,000 / year    │ Implies Basic = ₹4,80,000 │
│ Stated Fixed Take-Home   │ ₹12,57,600 / year    │ ⚠️ ANOMALY: Exceeds Gross │
│ Total Cost to Company    │ ₹13,77,600 / year    │ CTC exceeds Gross by ₹1.77L│
└──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────┴───────────────────────────┘
```

### Critical Compensation Inconsistency Analysis
1. **The Missing Basic Salary Entry**:
   * On the recruitment proforma, HRA is listed as ₹4,80,000 and Other Allowances as ₹2,40,000, totaling ₹7,20,000. The Basic salary field is left blank.
   * However, the stated Gross Salary is explicitly recorded as **₹12,00,000**.
   * By statutory accounting: $\text{Basic} = \text{Gross} - (\text{HRA} + \text{Others}) = ₹12,00,000 - ₹7,20,000 = \mathbf{₹4,80,000}$ per annum (₹40,000/month), which conforms to standard Indian compensation structuring ($40\%$ Basic, $40\%$ HRA, $20\%$ Special Allowance).
2. **The Take-Home Inconsistency**:
   * The proforma records a "Fixed take-home salary per annum" of **₹12,57,600**, while recording a "Gross Salary" of **₹12,00,000**.
   * In payroll mechanics, take-home pay must be less than gross salary due to employee deductions (Employee EPF $12\%$ of Basic, Professional Tax $\approx ₹2,400\text{/yr}$, and Income Tax/TDS).
   * **Root-Cause Analysis**: The difference between stated take-home and gross is $₹12,57,600 - ₹12,00,000 = \mathbf{₹57,600}$. Notice that exactly $12\%$ of the ₹4,80,000 Basic salary equals $0.12 \times 4,80,000 = \mathbf{₹57,600}$ (the employer's statutory Provident Fund contribution). The HR representative erroneously added the employer PF contribution to the Gross salary ($₹12,00,000 + ₹57,600 = ₹12,57,600$) instead of subtracting employee deductions.
   * **CTC Delta**: Total CTC is ₹13,77,600. The difference between CTC and Gross is $₹13,77,600 - ₹12,00,000 = ₹1,77,600$. This ₹1,77,600 encompasses Employer EPF (₹57,600), Gratuity ($4.81\%$ of basic $\approx ₹23,088$), group medical/term life insurance, and potentially an annual performance bonus / variable pay component ($\approx ₹80,000 - ₹96,000$).
   * **Actionable Directive**: The candidate must treat ₹12.00 LPA Gross as the confirmed payroll baseline and verify the final offer annexure with SPO. Realistic in-hand monthly credit after EPF and income tax under the New Tax Regime is approximately **₹88,000 – ₹92,000/month**.
