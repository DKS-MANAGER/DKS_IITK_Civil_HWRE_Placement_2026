## 1. The Pyramid Principle & Executive Storylining

In commercial strategy consulting at IQVIA, PowerPoint is the primary medium of executive communication. Consulting slides are not academic lecture notes or decorative graphic design; they are structured, data-driven decision instruments designed for biopharma C-suite leaders (Chief Commercial Officers, VP Market Access, Global Brand Leads).

### 1.1 The SCQA Framework for Executive Introductions
Every engagement deck, pitch, or written case presentation must open with the **SCQA** narrative structure:
- **Situation**: Uncontroversial status quo agreed upon by all stakeholders (e.g., *"Client asset X demonstrated superior 14-month progression-free survival in Phase III TNBC clinical trials."*).
- **Complication**: The strategic tension, disruption, or constraint threatening success (e.g., *"However, entrant Y recently secured preferred Tier 2 formulary access with Express Scripts via aggressive 45% discounting, while US hospital networks face significant 340B margin pressures."*).
- **Question**: The core business question client leadership must answer (e.g., *"How can Client launch Asset X to achieve $500M peak revenue by Year 4 without triggering an unsustainable price war?"*).
- **Answer (Governing Thought)**: The overarching strategic recommendation (e.g., *"Client should pursue a value-based contracting strategy targeting high-prescribing academic medical centers while positioning Asset X as the premium first-line option for high-risk biomarker-positive subgroups."*).

### 2.1 The Rules of Action Titles
- **Bad Title (Topic Only)**: *"US Market Access Landscape"* (Tells the reader what the slide is about, but reveals nothing about the conclusion).
- **Mediocre Title (Factual)**: *"Payer Formularies Restrict TNBC Products"* (Descriptive, but lacks quantification and strategic consequence).
- **World-Class Action Title**: *"Restrictive Tier 3 Payer Formularies Threaten 40% of Eligible Volume Unless Offset by Targeted Copay Assistance and Outcomes-Based Rebates."*

## 3. The 30–45 Minute Written Deck-Building Interview

In Round 2 of IQVIA's Associate Consultant selection, candidates are provided a 5–10 page raw case packet (data tables, physician interview quotes, competitor launch curves) and given 30 to 45 minutes to draft 3 to 5 executive slides.

### 3.1 Strict Time-Budgeting Protocol

```

│             Phase              │ Time Alloc │                     Exact Action                       │
```

## 4. Complete 8-Slide Simulated Client Deliverable Blueprint

**Engagement**: US Commercial Launch Strategy for *OncoVance* (Novel Oral Targeted Kinase Inhibitor in Second-Line HER2-Mutant Metastatic Colorectal Cancer).

---

### Slide 1: Executive Summary & Strategic Roadmap
- **Action Title**: *OncoVance Can Achieve $420M Peak US Sales by Year 4 by Concentrating Commercial Resources on High-Volume Academic Centers and Securing Rapid NCCN Compendia Inclusion.*
- **Visual Architecture**: 3-Box Executive Summary Layout (Market Opportunity | Commercial Obstacles | Strategic Imperatives).
- **Core Exhibit**:
  - Box 1: \$1.2B addressable market; 14,000 eligible 2L patients; current standard of care shows poor 4.2-month PFS.
  - Box 2: Low biomarker testing rate (only 42% tested for HER2 amplification in community settings); aggressive incumbent pricing.
  - Box 3: 3-Pronged strategy: Diagnostic NGS partnership, Tier 2 commercial formulary parity, 60-person specialized field force.
- **Bottom Banner**: *Decision Requested: Executive Committee approval for $45M Year 1 commercial launch budget.*

---

### Slide 2: Epidemiological Funnel & Target Patient Sizing
- **Action Title**: *Of 150,000 Annual US Colorectal Cancer Cases, 14,000 2L HER2-Amplified Patients Form OncoVance’s Addressable Target Pool.*
- **Visual Architecture**: Horizontal Waterfall / Step-Down Funnel Chart.
- **Data Cascade**:
  - US Incident CRC: 150,000
  - Stage IV Metastatic at Presentation / Recurrence: 45% = 67,500
  - Progressing Past First-Line Standard of Care: 60% = 40,500
  - HER2 Genomic Amplification Rate: 5% = 2,025 annual incident candidates
  - Cumulative 2L/3L Prevalent Flow Pool: 14,000 patients across addressable lines
- **Callout Box**: *Critical Growth Driver: Increasing community NGS testing from 42% to 75% expands eligible patient volume by 78%.*

---

### Slide 3: Clinical Differentiation & Target Product Profile (TPP)
- **Action Title**: *Superior Median Progression-Free Survival (8.6 vs. 4.2 Months) and Oral Route of Administration Provide a Decisive Competitive Advantage Over IV Chemotherapy.*
- **Visual Architecture**: Head-to-Head Comparison Table (OncoVance vs. Standard-of-Care Trifluridine/Tipiracil vs. Regorafenib).
- **Comparison Dimensions**: Primary Endpoint (mPFS), Objective Response Rate (ORR), Grade 3/4 Adverse Events (Neutropenia, Diarrhea), Dosing Route, Quality of Life index.
- **Bottom Banner**: *Physician Perception: 74% of surveyed oncologists rank mPFS improvement > 3 months as "highly compelling" for 1L/2L switching.*

---

### Slide 4: Stakeholder Journey & Diagnostic Leakage Funnel
- **Action Title**: *Diagnostic Attrition in Community Practices Is the Primary Commercial Bottleneck, Resulting in a 58% Loss of Eligible Candidates Before Prescription.*
- **Visual Architecture**: Horizontal Process Chevron with Attrition Drop-Off Percentages.
- **Stages**:
  1. Disease Progression Confirmed (100% | 14,000 pts)
  2. NGS Biopsy Ordered (48% | 6,720 pts $\rightarrow$ **52% Leakage due to tissue exhaustion/cost**)
  3. HER2 Mutation Detected & Communicated (42% | 5,880 pts)
  4. Specialized Treatment Initiated (35% | 4,900 pts)
- **Callout Banner**: *Mitigation: Establish sponsored "reflex testing" protocols with Labcorp/Quest Diagnostics to automate HER2 screening at initial biopsy.*

---

### Slide 5: Payer Landscape & Gross-to-Net (GTN) Strategy
- **Action Title**: *A 28% Net Price Concession ($11,200/Month Net) Secures Tier 2 Preferred Status Across 80% of Commercial and Medicare Part D Lives.*
- **Visual Architecture**: Split Layout (Left: Payer Tier Distribution Pie Chart | Right: GTN Waterfall Bar Chart).
- **GTN Waterfall Components**:
  - Wholesale Acquisition Cost (WAC): \$15,500 / month
  - Statutory Medicaid / 340B Mandated Discounts: -\$1,860
  - Commercial PBM Form Rebates: -\$1,705
  - Co-Pay Assistance & Patient Foundation Grants: -\$735
  - **Net Realized Price: $11,200 / month (72.3% Net Realization)**.

---

### Slide 6: Prescriber Segmentation & Sales Force Sizing
- **Action Title**: *Targeting Deciles 8–10 Oncologists Captures 72% of High-Volume Prescribers with a Lean 50-Representative Sales Force.*
- **Visual Architecture**: 80/20 Prescriber Lorenz Curve & Territory Allocation Map.
- **Data Table**:
  - Decile 8–10 (High Prescribers): 1,200 Oncologists $\rightarrow$ 18 calls/year $\rightarrow$ 21,600 calls
  - Decile 5–7 (Medium Prescribers): 2,400 Oncologists $\rightarrow$ 8 calls/year $\rightarrow$ 19,200 calls
  - Total Annual Calls: 40,800 calls.
  - Rep Capacity: 850 calls/rep/year $\rightarrow$ 48 reps $\approx$ **50 Headcount Required**.
- **Bottom Banner**: *Sales Force Efficiency: Focus 70% of detailing time on 250 National Comprehensive Cancer Network (NCCN) institutional centers.*

---

### Slide 7: 5-Year Revenue Forecast & Scenario Analysis
- **Action Title**: *Peak Net Sales Reach $420M in Year 4 Under Base Case Assumptions, with Downside Risk Heavily Insulated by NCCN Category 1 Guidelines.*
- **Visual Architecture**: 10-Year Revenue Line Graph with Bear/Base/Bull Forecast Bands and Sensitivity Tornado.
- **Scenario Outputs**:
  - Bear Case (Low testing uptake, Tier 3 access): Peak \$210M.
  - **Base Case (Target GTM Execution, Tier 2 access): Peak $420M**.
  - Bull Case (1L label expansion, EU approval): Peak \$680M.
- **Key Sensitivities**: Tornado chart confirms **Duration of Therapy (DoT)** has 2.4× higher impact on revenue than list price changes.

### Slide 8: 180-Day Pre-Launch Execution Gantt & Risk Mitigation
- **Action Title**: *A Structured 6-Month Readiness Plan Prioritizes Regulatory Compendia Listing, Payer Dossier Submissions, and Key Account Contracting.*
- **Visual Architecture**: Clean Quarterly Gantt Roadmap (Months T-6 to T+3) across 4 Workstreams:
  1. *Regulatory & Medical*: NCCN compendia dossier submission, Phase III publication in JCO.
  2. *Market Access & Pricing*: Finalize PBM contracts, publish AMCP dossier, launch copay portal.
  3. *Commercial & Field*: Complete sales force hire, compliance training, CRM territory mapping.
  4. *Diagnostic Alliances*: Finalize commercial reflex-testing agreement with national reference labs.
- **Risk Box**: *Identified Risk: Delayed PBM formulary review cycle. Mitigation: Pre-launch bridging free-drug supply program (30-day starter supply).*
