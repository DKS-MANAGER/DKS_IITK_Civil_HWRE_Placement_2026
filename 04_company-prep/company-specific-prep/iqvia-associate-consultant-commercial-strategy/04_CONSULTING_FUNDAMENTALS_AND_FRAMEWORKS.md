## 2. Hypothesis-Driven Problem-Solving & The MECE Principle

### 2.1 The Hypothesis-Led Approach
Novice analysts approach problems inductively: they collect mountains of data, hoping an answer will emerge. Strategy consultants operate **deductively and hypothesis-first**:
1. Formulate an initial hypothesis on Day 1 based on business intuition and preliminary data.
2. Structure an issue tree to test the hypothesis.
3. Prioritize high-impact analyses that prove or disprove the hypothesis.
4. Pivot immediately if data disproves the initial hypothesis.

### 2.2 The MECE Principle (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive)
Every strategic breakdown must satisfy two conditions:
* **Mutually Exclusive (ME)**: Sub-categories do not overlap. Each data point belongs to exactly one branch.
* **Collectively Exhaustive (CE)**: Together, all branches cover 100% of the problem space. No blind spots exist.

## 4. The "So-What" Engine: From Raw Data to C-Suite Directive

The single most common criticism junior consultants receive is: *"You presented the data, but what is the 'So What?'"*

```

│                                                                             │
```

## 5. Workstream Ownership, Timeline Management & Risk Escalation

In global strategy delivery, Associate Consultants own distinct analytical workstreams:
1. **Independent Task Scoping**: When assigned a task (e.g., *"Assess the competitive pipeline for KRAS inhibitors"*), define the deliverable format, data sources, and timeline upfront before spending hours searching.
2. **Early Risk Escalation**: If critical secondary data is inaccessible or contradictory, do not wait until the final deadline. Escalate immediately with a structured framing:
   * *Problem*: *"The SEC 10-K filings do not break out US vs European revenue for Competitor A."*
   * *Impact*: *"This introduces uncertainty into our European market sizing model."*
   * *Proposed Solution*: *"I propose estimating European volume using IQVIA MIDAS cross-country audit ratios as a proxy."*
3. **Meeting Minutes & Executive Alignment**: At the close of every client or internal team call, draft a 5-bullet summary outlining: Key Decisions Made, Analytical Deliverables Assigned, Responsible Owners, and Deadlines.
