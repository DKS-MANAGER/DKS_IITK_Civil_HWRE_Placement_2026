## 3. Pre-Interview Execution Checklist (Day of Interview)

### 3.1 Mental Readiness & Anchor Benchmarks
- [ ] Memorized demographic anchors: US Population (335M), EU-5 (325M), India (1,400M).
- [ ] Memorized Medicare Part B vs. Part D reimbursement models.
- [ ] Committed mental math shortcuts: Factoring, percentages, $1 \text{ Billion} / 1 \text{ Million} = 1,000$.
- [ ] Reviewed the 30-second and 3-minute project defense pitches.

### 3.2 Consulting Communication Etiquette
- [ ] **Lead with the Answer**: Always give the executive recommendation or number first (Pyramid Principle), then state the supporting pillars.
- [ ] **Structure Aloud**: *"I'd like to analyze this problem through three distinct lenses: First, Market Feasibility; Second, Competitive Dynamics; and Third, Execution & Payer Access."*
- [ ] **State Assumptions Explicitly**: In sizing questions, never pluck a number from thin air without justifying its business logic.
- [ ] **End with the "So-What"**: Never stop at a bare dollar calculation; connect every number to client strategy and execution risk.
