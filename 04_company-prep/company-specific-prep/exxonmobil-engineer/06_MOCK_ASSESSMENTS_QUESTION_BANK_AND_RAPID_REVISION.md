#### Step-by-Step Worked Solutions

* **M1-Q01: (B)** Maximum bending moment $M = \frac{P L}{4} = \frac{18.0 \times 4.0}{4} = 18.0\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m} = 1.8 \times 10^7\text{ N}\cdot\text{mm}$. Section modulus $Z = \frac{b d^2}{6} = \frac{150 \times (300)^2}{6} = 2.25 \times 10^6\text{ mm}^3$. Flexural stress $\sigma = \frac{M}{Z} = \frac{1.8 \times 10^7}{2.25 \times 10^6} = \mathbf{8.0\text{ MPa}}$.
* **M1-Q02: (C)** Maximum in-plane shear stress $\tau_{\max} = \sqrt{\left(\frac{\sigma_x - \sigma_y}{2}\right)^2 + \tau_{xy}^2} = \sqrt{\left(\frac{90 - (-30)}{2}\right)^2 + 25^2} = \sqrt{60^2 + 25^2} = \sqrt{3600 + 625} = \sqrt{4225} = \mathbf{65.0\text{ MPa}}$.
* **M1-Q03: (B)** Torsional stress $\tau = \frac{16 T}{\pi D^3} = \frac{16 \times 4000\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}}{\pi \times (0.080\text{ m})^3} = \frac{64,000}{\pi \times 0.000512} = \frac{64,000}{0.0016085} = 39,788,735\text{ Pa} \approx \mathbf{39.8\text{ MPa}}$.
* **M1-Q04: (B)** By double integration of moment equation $M(x) = -\frac{w(L-x)^2}{2}$, tip deflection $\delta = \frac{w L^4}{8 E I}$.
* **M1-Q05: (B)** Hoop stress $\sigma_h = \frac{P D}{2 t} = \frac{(2.0\text{ MPa})(1200\text{ mm})}{2(12\text{ mm})} = \frac{2400}{24} = \mathbf{100\text{ MPa}}$.
* **M1-Q06: (B)** For a continuous beam on 4 simple supports without horizontal loads, vertical reactions $R = 4$. Equations of static equilibrium $E = 2$ ($\sum F_y = 0, \sum M = 0$). Degree of indeterminacy $D_s = R - E = 4 - 2 = \mathbf{2}$.
* **M1-Q07: (C)** Resolving forces perpendicular to the collinear members at the unloaded joint yields $F_3 \sin \theta = 0 \implies F_3 = \mathbf{0}$.
* **M1-Q08: (B)** By standard moment distribution or slope-deflection tables, fixed end moment for central point load is $M_F = \frac{W L}{8}$.
* **M1-Q09: (B)** Maximum ILD ordinate for bending moment at section $C$ (distance $a = 3\text{ m}$, $b = 7\text{ m}$, $L = 10\text{ m}$) is $\frac{a b}{L} = \frac{3.0 \times 7.0}{10.0} = \mathbf{2.10\text{ m}}$.
* **M1-Q10: (B)** Per IS 456 Clause 38.1, $x_{u,\max} / d = \frac{700}{1100 + 0.87 f_y}$. For Fe 415: $\frac{700}{1100 + 361.05} = \frac{700}{1461.05} = \mathbf{0.48 d}$.
* **M1-Q11: (C)** Limiting moment capacity $M_{u,\lim} = 0.36 \frac{x_{u,\max}}{d} \left(1 - 0.42 \frac{x_{u,\max}}{d}\right) f_{ck} b d^2 = 0.36(0.48)(1 - 0.42 \times 0.48) f_{ck} b d^2 = 0.138 f_{ck} b d^2 = 0.138(20)(250)(450)^2 = 0.138 \times 20 \times 250 \times 202,500 = 139,725,000\text{ N}\cdot\text{mm} = \mathbf{139.7\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}}$.
* **M1-Q12: (C)** When nominal shear stress $\tau_v > \tau_{c,\max}$, IS 456 Clause 40.2.3 strictly dictates that the cross-sectional dimensions of the beam must be increased to prevent diagonal compression failure of the web concrete.
* **M1-Q13: (C)** Loss due to elastic shortening of concrete $= m \cdot f_c = 6 \times 10\text{ MPa} = \mathbf{60\text{ MPa}}$.
* **M1-Q14: (B)** Effective throat thickness $t_e = K \cdot s = 0.707 \times 8\text{ mm} \approx \mathbf{5.6\text{ mm}}$ (Clause 10.5.3.1 of IS 800:2007).
* **M1-Q15: (C)** For a solid rectangular beam, plastic section modulus $Z_p = \frac{b d^2}{4}$, elastic modulus $Z_e = \frac{b d^2}{6}$. Shape factor $S = \frac{Z_p}{Z_e} = \frac{b d^2 / 4}{b d^2 / 6} = \frac{6}{4} = \mathbf{1.50}$.
* **M1-Q16: (C)** Slenderness ratio $\lambda = \frac{L_{\text{eff}}}{r_{\min}} = \frac{3600\text{ mm}}{30\text{ mm}} = \mathbf{120}$.
* **M1-Q17: (B)** Design shear strength $V_{dsb} = \frac{f_u A_{nb}}{\sqrt{3} \gamma_{mb}} = \frac{400\text{ MPa} \times 245\text{ mm}^2}{\sqrt{3} \times 1.25} = \frac{98,000}{2.165} = 45,264\text{ N} \approx \mathbf{45.3\text{ kN}}$.
* **M1-Q18: (B)** Fundamental soil identity $S_r e = w G_s \implies w = \frac{S_r e}{G_s} = \frac{1.0 \times 0.60}{2.65} = 0.2264 = \mathbf{22.6\%}$.
* **M1-Q19: (B)** Submerged unit weight $\gamma' = \gamma_{\text{sat}} - \gamma_w = 19.81 - 9.81 = 10.0\text{ kN/m}^3$. Effective vertical stress $\sigma' = \gamma' z = (10.0\text{ kN/m}^3)(4.0\text{ m}) = \mathbf{40.0\text{ kPa}}$.
* **M1-Q20: (B)** Terzaghi ultimate bearing capacity for strip footing on undrained clay: $q_u = c_u N_c + \gamma D_f N_q = (40 \times 5.7) + (18 \times 1.5 \times 1.0) = 228 + 27 = \mathbf{255\text{ kPa}}$.
* **M1-Q21: (C)** Time factor $T_v = \frac{c_v t}{d^2}$. For two-way drainage, $d_1 = H / 2 = 2.0\text{ m}$. For one-way drainage, $d_2 = H = 4.0\text{ m}$. Since $T_v and $c_v$ are identical for $50\%$ consolidation: $\frac{t_2}{t_1} = \left(\frac{d_2}{d_1}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{4.0}{2.0}\right)^2 = 4$. Thus, $t_2 = 4 \times 200\text{ days} = \mathbf{800\text{ days}}$.
* **M1-Q22: (A)** $\text{True Bearing} = \text{Magnetic Bearing} - \text{West Declination} = 48^\circ 30' - 3^\circ 15' = \mathbf{N 45^\circ 15' E}$.
* **M1-Q23: (A)** Taking sights from both sides of the river and averaging readings exactly cancels line-of-collimation error, earth curvature, and mean atmospheric refraction.
* **M1-Q24: (B)** Tangent length $T = R \tan(\Delta / 2) = 500 \tan(30^\circ) = 500 \times 0.57735 = \mathbf{288.7\text{ m}}$.
* **M1-Q25: (B)** $\text{Lag distance} = v t = 20 \times 2.5 = 50\text{ m}$. $\text{Braking distance} = \frac{v^2}{2 g f} = \frac{20^2}{2 \times 9.81 \times 0.35} = \frac{400}{6.867} = 58.25\text{ m}$. $SSD = 50 + 58.25 = 108.25\text{ m} \approx \mathbf{108\text{ m}}$.
* **M1-Q26: (C)** Equilibrium super-elevation $e = \frac{V^2}{127 R} = \frac{80^2}{127 \times 250} = \frac{6400}{31,750} = \mathbf{0.2016} \approx \mathbf{0.202}$.
* **M1-Q27: (B)** Standard penetration load for high quality crushed stone base at $2.5\text{ mm}$ penetration is standardly specified as $\mathbf{1370\text{ kg}}$ ($13.44\text{ kN}$) and $2055\text{ kg}$ at $5.0\text{ mm}$.
* **M1-Q28: (B)** $\mathrm{BOD}_5 = L_0 (1 - e^{-5 k}) \implies 180 = L_0 (1 - e^{-5 \times 0.23}) = L_0 (1 - e^{-1.15}) = L_0 (1 - 0.3166) = 0.6834 L_0$. Therefore, $L_0 = \frac{180}{0.6834} = \mathbf{263.4\text{ mg/L}} \approx \mathbf{263\text{ mg/L}}$.
* **M1-Q29: (B)** Surface area $A_s = B \times L = 8.0 \times 25.0 = 200\text{ m}^2$. $\text{Surface Overflow Rate} = \frac{Q}{A_s} = \frac{4800\text{ m}^3/\text{day}}{200\text{ m}^2} = \mathbf{24.0\text{ m}^3/(\text{m}^2\cdot\text{day})}$.

#### Solution 01: (C)
* **Governing Principle:** Bernoulli's equation is derived by integrating Euler's equation along a streamline. It requires steady flow ($\partial \mathbf{V}/\partial t = 0$), incompressible fluid ($\rho = \text{const}$), and inviscid fluid ($\mu = 0$, frictionless). If the flow is rotational, the constant $H$ varies across different streamlines. #### Solution 02: (B)
* Flow velocity: $V = 2.0\text{ m/s}$.

#### Solution 03: (A)
* **Diagnosis:** The symptoms (gravel rattling noise, high-frequency vibration, suction level drop) are classic indicators of **cavitation** caused by $\mathrm{NPSH}_A < \mathrm{NPSH}_R$.
* **Engineering Remedy:** Throttling the downstream discharge valve introduces friction, shifting the pump operating point to a **lower discharge $Q$**:
  1. Suction line velocity and frictional head loss drop with $Q^2$, thereby increasing $\mathrm{NPSH}_A$ at the pump suction nozzle.
  2. The manufacturer's required $\mathrm{NPSH}_R$ decreases substantially at lower capacities.
  3. This restores $\mathrm{NPSH}_A > \mathrm{NPSH}_R$, suppressing vapor bubble formation and collapse.
  - *Operational Boundary:* Throttling is strictly conditional on keeping flow above the pump's **Minimum Continuous Stable Flow (MCSF)** to avoid internal flow recirculation cavitation and rapid fluid temperature rise.
  - *(Options B and C worsen cavitation by increasing impeller blade tip velocity or dropping suction pressure further).*

### Thermodynamics & Energy Conversion
* **Steady-Flow Compressor Power (Compressible Gas, Ideal Gas):**
  $$\dot{W}_{\text{comp}} = \dot{m}(h_2 - h_1) = \dot{m} c_p (T_2 - T_1) = \frac{\dot{m} c_p T_1}{\eta_{\text{isen}}} \left[\left(\frac{P_2}{P_1}\right)^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma} - 1\right]$$
* **Liquid Pump Shaft Power (Incompressible Fluid):**
  $$\dot{W}_{\text{pump}} = \frac{Q \Delta P}{\eta_{\text{pump}}} = \frac{\rho g Q H}{\eta_{\text{pump}}}$$
* **Carnot Maximum Cycle Efficiency:**
  $$\eta_{\text{Carnot}} = 1 - \frac{T_L}{T_H} \quad (\text{Strictly in Kelvin!})$$
* **Heat Exchanger LMTD:**
  $$\Delta T_{lm} = \frac{\Delta T_1 - \Delta T_2}{\ln(\Delta T_1 / \Delta T_2)}$$
  - True Counter-flow: $\Delta T_1 = T_{h,\text{in}} - T_{c,\text{out}}$, $\Delta T_2 = T_{h,\text{out}} - T_{c,\text{in}}$.

# 3. 5-Minute Pre-Test Mental Checklist

Before clicking start on your assessment or entering the interview room, review these 10 rapid principles:

1. **Mass Conservation First:** In any branched pipe network or open channel junction, verify that $\sum Q_{\text{in}} = \sum Q_{\text{out}}$ before calculating head losses.
2. **Parallel Lines Share Head Loss:** If two pipes branch and rejoin between nodes A and B, $h_{L,1} = h_{L,2}$, but their discharges divide inversely proportional to $\sqrt{K_{\text{pipe}}}$.
3. **Critical Flow Minimum Energy:** At critical depth ($Fr = 1$), specific energy is at its absolute minimum for a given discharge. For rectangular channels, $E_{\min} = 1.5 y_c$.
4. **Hydraulic Jump Destroys Energy:** Head loss across a jump is $\Delta E_L = \frac{(y_2 - y_1)^3}{4 y_1 y_2}$. It occurs only from supercritical ($\mathrm{Fr}_1 > 1$) to subcritical ($\mathrm{Fr}_2 < 1$).
5. **Cavitation Inception:** Cavitation occurs when static pressure drops below vapor pressure ($P \le P_v$). Prevention requires $\mathrm{NPSH}_A > \mathrm{NPSH}_R$.
6. **Water Hammer Pressure:** Instantaneous closure causes surge $\Delta P = \rho c \Delta V$. A valve is fast if $T_v \le 2L/c$.
7. **Pipe Wall Thin-Wall Limit:** $D/t \ge 20$. Hoop stress is twice longitudinal stress ($\sigma_h = 2\sigma_L$). Longitudinal seam fails first.
8. **Exner Sediment Conservation:** If sediment transport accelerates downstream ($\frac{\partial q_s}{\partial x} > 0$), bed scours ($\frac{\partial z_b}{\partial t} < 0$).
9. **Hierarchy of Safety Controls:** Elimination $\to$ Substitution $\to$ Engineering Controls $\to$ Administrative Controls $\to$ PPE.
10. **Stop-Work Authority:** Any individual on site has the absolute obligation to immediately stop any operation perceived as unsafe.
