### Step-by-Step Numerical Derivations & Diagnostic Explanations

#### Solution Q01: (C)
* **Governing Principle:** Bernoulli's equation is derived by integrating Euler's equation along a streamline. For rotational flows, the total head constant $H$ changes across streamlines. Only when the flow field is irrotational ($\nabla \times \mathbf{V} = 0$) does the constant apply uniformly across the entire domain.

#### Solution Q02: (B)
* **Calculation:**
  $$h_f = f \frac{L}{D} \frac{V^2}{2g} = (0.020) \times \left(\frac{500\text{ m}}{0.20\text{ m}}\right) \times \frac{(2.0\text{ m/s})^2}{2(9.81\text{ m/s}^2)} = (0.020)(2500) \times \frac{4.0}{19.62} = 50.0 \times 0.20387 = \mathbf{10.19\text{ m}}$$
  $$\Delta P_f = \rho g h_f = (1000\text{ kg/m}^3)(9.81\text{ m/s}^2)(10.19\text{ m}) = 99,964\text{ Pa} \approx \mathbf{100.0\text{ kPa}}$$

#### Solution Q03: (A)
* **Diagnosis:** Symptoms indicate cavitation due to insufficient $\mathrm{NPSH}_A$. Throttling the discharge valve increases backpressure and moves the pump leftward on its curve to a **lower discharge $Q$**. At lower $Q$, required $\mathrm{NPSH}_R$ decreases while suction velocity head losses drop, restoring $\mathrm{NPSH}_A > \mathrm{NPSH}_R$.

#### Solution Q04: (C)
* **Governing Law:** From the momentum balance on a fluid cylinder of radius $r$ in fully developed pipe flow: $\tau(r) = \frac{r}{2} \left(-\frac{dP}{dx}\right)$. Thus, shear stress varies **linearly** from $\tau = 0$ at the centerline ($r = 0$) to maximum $\tau_w = \frac{R}{2} \left(-\frac{dP}{dx}\right)$ at the pipe wall ($r = R$).

#### Solution Q05: (B)
* **Continuity Balance:**
  $$Q_1 = A_1 V_1 = \frac{\pi}{4}(0.30)^2(2.0) = 0.14137\text{ m}^3/\text{s}$$
  Discharge in each symmetrical branch: $Q_2 = Q_1 / 2 = 0.070686\text{ m}^3/\text{s}$.
  $$V_2 = \frac{Q_2}{A_2} = \frac{0.070686}{\frac{\pi}{4}(0.15)^2} = \frac{0.070686}{0.017671} = \mathbf{4.0\text{ m/s}}$$

#### Solution Q07: (B)
* **Borda-Carnot Expansion Loss:**
  $$V_2 = V_1 \left(\frac{A_1}{A_2}\right) = 6.0 \times \left(\frac{0.05}{0.15}\right) = 2.0\text{ m/s}$$
  $$h_L = \frac{(V_1 - V_2)^2}{2g} = \frac{(6.0 - 2.0)^2}{2(9.81)} = \frac{16.0}{19.62} = \mathbf{0.8155\text{ m}} \approx \mathbf{0.82\text{ m}}$$
