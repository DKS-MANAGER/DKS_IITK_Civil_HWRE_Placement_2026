## 1. Evidence Classification Taxonomy

In compliance with repository evidence standards, every factual claim, syllabus topic, and test structure is tagged with one of the following canonical labels:

* `[OFFICIAL-CURRENT]`: Directly verified from live, authoritative ExxonMobil official corporate communications or current campus recruitment notices.
* `[OFFICIAL-HISTORICAL]`: Verified from older archived official notices, official university placement records, or institutional placement brochures.

## 3. Disciplinary Scope of the Generic "Engineer" Title

When ExxonMobil recruits under the broad job title **"Engineer"** at premier engineering campuses:
1. **Multidisciplinary Eligibility:** The job opening typically invites candidates across Civil, Chemical, Mechanical, Electrical, and Computer Science disciplines.
2. **Evaluation Strategy:**
   - Online written tests typically have a shared aptitude component (Quantitative, Verbal, Logic, DI) and either branch-specific technical modules or general engineering mechanics/fluids.
   - Technical interview panels **strictly evaluate the candidate based on their registered discipline and thesis research**. A Civil/HWRE candidate is grilled on fluid dynamics, open-channel flow, pipe networks, sediment transport, computational fluid dynamics (CFD), hydrology, structural mechanics, and foundation engineering.
   - Cross-disciplinary questions are largely confined to universal engineering physics: thermodynamics of fluid flow, heat exchangers, corrosion, and process safety.
3. **Eventual Placement:** At EMSTPL Bengaluru, Civil/HWRE postgraduates are typically routed into:
   - **Offshore & Marine Facilities / Subsea Engineering:** Scour protection, pipeline stability, wave-current hydrodynamic loading, jacket platform analysis.
   - **Flow Assurance & Pipeline Hydraulics:** Water hammer, transient surges, multiphase gathering, hydraulic network modeling.
   - **Site Infrastructure & Environmental Engineering:** Coastal terminals, flood protection, industrial drainage, bund containment, wastewater processing.
   - **Computational Engineering (CFD/R&D):** Multiphase hydrodynamic simulation, digital twins, turbomachinery.
