## 3. Authoritative Educational & Standards References

All technical equations, derivations, and code provisions across this repository are cross-referenced with established academic textbooks and international engineering codes:

### A. Academic Textbooks & Open Educational Resources (NPTEL)
1. **Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulics:**
   - White, F. M., *Fluid Mechanics*, 8th/9th Eds., McGraw-Hill.
   - Subramanya, K., *Flow in Open Channels*, 4th/5th Eds., Tata McGraw-Hill.
   - Chaudhry, M. H., *Open-Channel Flow*, 2nd Ed., Springer.
   - NPTEL Course: *Advanced Hydrology & Hydraulics*, IIT Kharagpur / IIT Kanpur.
2. **Structural Mechanics & Materials:**
   - Gere, J. M., and Goodno, B. J., *Mechanics of Materials*, Cengage Learning.
   - Hibbeler, R. C., *Structural Analysis*, Pearson.
   - Punmia, B. C., *Strength of Materials and Theory of Structures*, Laxmi Publications.
3. **Geotechnical Engineering:**
   - Das, B. M., and Sobhan, K., *Principles of Geotechnical Engineering*, Cengage Learning.
   - Terzaghi, K., Peck, R. B., and Mesri, G., *Soil Mechanics in Engineering Practice*, Wiley.
4. **Thermodynamics & Heat Transfer:**
   - Cengel, Y. A., and Boles, M. A., *Thermodynamics: An Engineering Approach*, McGraw-Hill.
   - Incropera, F. P., and DeWitt, D. P., *Fundamentals of Heat and Mass Transfer*, Wiley.
5. **Process Safety & Risk:**
   - Crowl, D. A., and Louvar, J. F., *Chemical Process Safety: Fundamentals with Applications*, 3rd/4th Eds., Prentice Hall.

### B. Engineering Codes and Consensus Standards
* **Bureau of Indian Standards:**
  - `IS 456:2000` (Plain and Reinforced Concrete — Code of Practice)
  - `IS 800:2007` (General Construction in Steel — Code of Practice)
  - `IS 1893:2016` (Criteria for Earthquake Resistant Design of Structures)
* **American Standards (Energy & Infrastructure):**
  - `ASME B31.4` (Pipeline Transportation Systems for Liquids and Slurries)
  - `ASME B31.3` (Process Piping)
  - `API 5L` (Specification for Line Pipe)
  - `API 650` (Welded Tanks for Oil Storage)
  - `FHWA HEC-18` (Evaluating Scour at Bridges, 5th Edition, 2012)
  - `NFPA 30` (Flammable and Combustible Liquids Code)
  - `OSHA 29 CFR 1910.119` (Process Safety Management of Highly Hazardous Chemicals)
