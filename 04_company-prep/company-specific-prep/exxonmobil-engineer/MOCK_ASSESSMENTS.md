### Step-by-Step Derivations

* **M1-Q01: (D)** Laminar Hagen-Poiseuille pipe flow: $u(r) = u_{\max}(1 - r^2/R^2)$. Average velocity $V = \frac{1}{2} u_{\max} \implies \frac{u_{\max}}{V} = \mathbf{2.00}$.
