## 2. Study Pathways by Time Available

* **Block 1 (45 mins)**: Fluid Mechanics & Pipeline Hydraulics in [`03_FLUID_MECHANICS_AND_HWRE_HYDRAULIC_SYSTEMS.md`](03_FLUID_MECHANICS_AND_HWRE_HYDRAULIC_SYSTEMS.md).
* **Block 2 (60 mins)**: Broad Civil Core Fundamentals in [`02_CIVIL_CORE_ENGINEERING_AND_WORKBOOK.md`](02_CIVIL_CORE_ENGINEERING_AND_WORKBOOK.md).
* **Block 3 (45 mins)**: Psychometric & Situational Judgment (SJT) in [`01_COMMON_ENGINEERING_APTITUDE_AND_PSYCHOMETRIC.md`](01_COMMON_ENGINEERING_APTITUDE_AND_PSYCHOMETRIC.md).
* **Block 4 (30 mins)**: Energy Facilities & OIMS Safety in [`04_ENERGY_FACILITIES_FLOW_ASSURANCE_AND_SAFETY_OIMS.md`](04_ENERGY_FACILITIES_FLOW_ASSURANCE_AND_SAFETY_OIMS.md).
* **Block 5 (40 mins)**: Thesis & STAR Rehearsal in [`05_BEHAVIORAL_INTERVIEW_AND_HWRE_THESIS_DEFENSE.md`](05_BEHAVIORAL_INTERVIEW_AND_HWRE_THESIS_DEFENSE.md).

### Track B: 7-Day Comprehensive Mastery Pathway
* **Days 1–2**: Civil Core Engineering Fundamentals & 56 Practice Problems (Module 02).
* **Days 3–4**: Fluid Mechanics, Hydraulic Systems & 12 Production HWRE Problems (Module 03).
* **Day 5**: Psychometric Assessment, SJT Scenarios & Common Engineering (Module 01).
* **Day 6**: Flow Assurance, Industrial Facilities & OIMS Safety (Module 04).
* **Day 7**: Timed Mock Assessments in Module 06 & Thesis Defense in Module 05.

### Track C: 15-Minute Pre-Test Rapid Checklist
* Review Section C in [`06_MOCK_ASSESSMENTS_QUESTION_BANK_AND_RAPID_REVISION.md`](06_MOCK_ASSESSMENTS_QUESTION_BANK_AND_RAPID_REVISION.md):
  - Darcy vs Fanning friction factor ($f = 4 c_f$).
  - Mohr's circle center and radius.
  - IS 456 limiting neutral axis depth ($0.53, 0.48, 0.46$).
  - Terzaghi bearing capacity equation.
  - Critical depth in rectangular channel ($y_c = (q^2/g)^{1/3}$).
  - Stop-Work Authority golden rule: Safety overrides schedule 100% of the time.
