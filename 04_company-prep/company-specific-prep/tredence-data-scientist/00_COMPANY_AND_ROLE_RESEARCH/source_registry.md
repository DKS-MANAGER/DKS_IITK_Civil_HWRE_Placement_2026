## 1. 5-Tier Evidentiary Standard & Hierarchy

To maintain the highest level of factual integrity, every statement, data point, and claim in this preparation hub is classified according to the following strict evidentiary hierarchy:

| Evidence Tier | Taxonomy Level | Criteria & Authority Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`[TIER 1]`** | **Verified Institutional / Proforma Fact** | Directly recorded in the signed IIT Kanpur Placement Proforma, SPO circulars, or official JD document (`drive.google.com/file/d/1_hXsqMi-vh1TtJg64Z-k3EpDElDAS3mR/view`). Legally binding campus information. |
| **`[TIER 2]`** | **Official Corporate Primary Source** | Published on the official Tredence website (`tredence.com`), corporate press releases, SEC filings, official YouTube tech talks, or leadership whitepapers. |
| **`[TIER 3]`** | **Partner & Industry Verification** | Official partner announcements from hyperscalers (Databricks Partner Awards, Snowflake Elite Services, Microsoft, Google Cloud) and credible analyst benchmarks (Gartner, Everest, AIM). |
| **`[TIER 4]`** | **Verified Candidate Experience** | Documented, consistent candidate interview debriefs and assessment transcripts from premier Indian campuses (IITs, NITs, BITS) across public platforms (LeetCode Discuss, GeeksforGeeks). |
| **`[TIER 5]`** | **Pedagogical Preparation Inference** | Internally formulated mock questions, synthetic practice datasets, and curriculum frameworks designed to prepare the candidate for expected evaluation domains. |
