## 1. Skill Classification Standard

To avoid unfocused preparation, this matrix rigorously categorizes all competencies into three operational classes:
- **`[EXPLICIT JD REQUIREMENT]`**: Directly named in the primary Job Description or official campus proforma. Mandatory for passing technical screening and interviews.
- **`[RECOMMENDED TREDENCE SKILL]`**: Frequently evaluated in Tredence data science project delivery (decision intelligence, last-mile adoption, cloud hyperscalers). Highly differentiated in Round 1/2 interviews.
- **`[ADVANCED BREADTH TOPIC]`**: Listed in the extended JD as advanced capabilities (e.g., Graph Networks, Reinforcement Learning, Computer Vision). Evaluated at conceptual intuition level for campus hires.
