## 2. Structured Analysis of the Discrepancy

Based on empirical campus recruitment records from premier Indian engineering institutes (IITs, NITs, BITS) and standard corporate compensation structures, four distinct hypotheses account for this discrepancy:

### Hypothesis A: Role Tiering & Degree Differential (B.Tech vs. M.Tech / Dual Degree)
- **Mechanism**: In several enterprise analytics campus drives, companies quote an aggregate CTC bracket.
- **Precedent**: ₹12.5 LPA frequently represents the baseline undergraduate (B.Tech) entry offer, whereas ₹14.5 LPA represents the postgraduate (M.Tech / Dual Degree) tier, or a differentiated "Data Scientist" track versus an "Associate Data Scientist" / "Analytics Consultant" track.
- **Proforma Alignment**: The IITK proforma explicitly titles the role **Data Scientist** at **₹14.5 LPA**, while the extended JD references **Associate Data Scientist**.

### Hypothesis B: Fixed Base Salary vs. Total Cost to Company (CTC)
- **Mechanism**: The slide quoting ₹12.5 LPA may represent the **Fixed Annual Base Component**, whereas the headline figure of ₹14.5 LPA represents the **Total Cost to Company (CTC)** inclusive of annual performance bonuses, medical insurance coverage, and employer statutory contributions (EPF/Gratuity).
- **Industry Norm**: A ~15% differential between total CTC (₹14.5L) and fixed base (₹12.5L) is a standard corporate compensation ratio across mid-market analytics consulting firms in India.

### Hypothesis C: Multi-Year Retention / Variable Staging
- **Mechanism**: Some corporate presentations report first-year cash earnings (e.g., ₹12.5 LPA) separately from the total package valuation (e.g., ₹14.5 LPA which includes milestone or end-of-year retention pay).
- **Status**: Cannot be confirmed as a definitive fact without an explicit formal offer letter or annexure.

### Hypothesis D: Presentation Version / Cross-Campus Deck Reuse
- **Mechanism**: Corporate recruitment decks are frequently reused across Tier-1 and Tier-2 engineering colleges. Tredence may offer ₹12.5 LPA at general NIT/state engineering drives while offering ₹14.5 LPA at IITs. A legacy slide from a non-IIT drive may have inadvertently remained in the appendix of the presentation deck.
- **Status**: Supported by the fact that the **official IIT Kanpur Proforma signed with the Students' Placement Office (SPO)** explicitly binds the offer at **₹14.5 LPA**.
