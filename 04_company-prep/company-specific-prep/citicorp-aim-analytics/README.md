1. **Online Assessment (OA)**:
   * **Quant & Speed Math** (15–20 Qs): Ratios, percentages, mixtures, work-rate $\rightarrow$ Use [`01_common/aptitude/quantitative/`](../../../01_common/aptitude/quantitative/).
   * **Logical Reasoning & Puzzles** (15–20 Qs): Arrangements, sequences, data sufficiency $\rightarrow$ Use [`01_common/aptitude/logical-reasoning/`](../../../01_common/aptitude/logical-reasoning/).

2. **Technical Interview Round 1**:
   * Whiteboard / live SQL writing.
   * Probability brainteasers and statistical distribution grilling.
   * Walkthrough of your resume's technical and quantitative projects.
3. **Business Case Round 2**:
   * Banking case diagnostic (e.g., credit card customer churn, loan default cut-off).
   * Machine learning metric trade-offs (Precision vs Recall, KS statistic, AUC).
   * Guesstimate / market sizing.
4. **HR & Fitment Round**:
   * "Why Citigroup analytics from M.Tech Civil / HWRE?"
   * STAR behavioural examples on teamwork, leadership, and ethical risk decisions.
