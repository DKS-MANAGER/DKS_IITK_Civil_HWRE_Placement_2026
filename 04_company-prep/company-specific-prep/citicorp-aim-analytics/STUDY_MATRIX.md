# Citi AIM Analytics — Prioritized Study Matrix

> **Target Role**: Spec Analytics Analyst — Business Analytics (SBS), Citi AIM  
> **Framework**: Aligned with the official IITK Phase 1 Proforma responsibilities and requirements.

---

## 1. Prioritization Framework

* **P1 (Essential)**: Direct core requirements named in the proforma ("Basic Statistics and Probability", "Logical/Analytical/Quantitative/Reasoning", "Data Manipulation", "SQL", "Hypothesis testing", "Predictive modeling", "Segmentation").
* **P2 (Important Supporting Skills)**: Named operational competencies ("Presentation & Visualization", "Stakeholder Management", "Teamwork", "Compliance & Risk Control", complex window functions, A/B testing, cohort analysis).
* **P3 (Supplementary Industry Context)**: Advanced credit scoring metrics (KS statistic, PSI), machine learning ensembles, and regulatory finance terminology.
