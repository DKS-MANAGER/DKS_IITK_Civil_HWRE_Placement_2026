## 2. Study Tracks by Time Available

### Track A: 30-Day Comprehensive Pathway (Recommended)
* **Week 1 (Days 1–7)**:
  - Complete [`01_STATISTICS_PROBABILITY_AND_BANKING_RISK.md`](01_STATISTICS_PROBABILITY_AND_BANKING_RISK.md).
  - Attempt Diagnostic Baseline Mock (Diag-01) in Module 06.
* **Week 2 (Days 8–14)**:
  - Complete [`02_SQL_DATABASE_ARCH_AND_BANKING_DATA_MANIPULATION.md`](02_SQL_DATABASE_ARCH_AND_BANKING_DATA_MANIPULATION.md).
  - Attempt SQL Sectional Mock (Sec-SQL-01) in Module 06.
* **Week 3 (Days 15–21)**:
  - Complete [`03_PREDICTIVE_MODELING_ML_AND_CREDIT_RISK.md`](03_PREDICTIVE_MODELING_ML_AND_CREDIT_RISK.md) and [`04_BANKING_CASES_VISUALIZATION_AND_EXECUTIVE_STORYTELLING.md`](04_BANKING_CASES_VISUALIZATION_AND_EXECUTIVE_STORYTELLING.md).
  - Attempt ML & Cases Sectional Mock in Module 06.
* **Week 4 (Days 22–30)**:
  - Complete [`05_BANKING_COMPLIANCE_BEHAVIORAL_AND_HWRE_DEFENSE.md`](05_BANKING_COMPLIANCE_BEHAVIORAL_AND_HWRE_DEFENSE.md).
  - Solve Level 2 & 3 questions in Module 06 Question Bank.
  - Attempt Full-Length 75-Minute Placement Mock (Full-01) under strict timed conditions.
  - Final review of Module 06 Section C (Rapid Revision Cheat Sheet).

### Track B: 14-Day Accelerated Sprint
* **Days 1–4**: Modules 01 (Statistics & Probability) and 02 (SQL & Banking Data).
* **Days 5–8**: Modules 03 (Predictive Modeling/WoE) and 04 (Banking Cases).
* **Days 9–11**: Module 05 (Compliance, Behavioral STAR & Engineering Defense).
* **Days 12–14**: Full-01 Mock and high-yield questions from Module 06.

### Track C: 72-Hour Rapid Cram
* Focus strictly on:
  1. [`06_MOCK_TESTS_QUESTION_BANK_AND_RAPID_REVISION.md`](06_MOCK_TESTS_QUESTION_BANK_AND_RAPID_REVISION.md) (Section C: Formula Sheet & Top SQL).
  2. [`02_SQL_DATABASE_ARCH_AND_BANKING_DATA_MANIPULATION.md`](02_SQL_DATABASE_ARCH_AND_BANKING_DATA_MANIPULATION.md) (Window functions & Joins).
  3. [`03_PREDICTIVE_MODELING_ML_AND_CREDIT_RISK.md`](03_PREDICTIVE_MODELING_ML_AND_CREDIT_RISK.md) (WoE, IV, KS, PSI).
  4. [`05_BANKING_COMPLIANCE_BEHAVIORAL_AND_HWRE_DEFENSE.md`](05_BANKING_COMPLIANCE_BEHAVIORAL_AND_HWRE_DEFENSE.md) (Engineering-to-Banking Defense).
