## 1. Syllabus Classification Hierarchy

To ensure students prioritize high-yield preparation while mastering necessary technical depth, topics are organized into three clear priority tiers:

1. **Tier 1: Core Proforma Requirements (`[CORE — PROFORMA]`)**:
   - Explicitly listed in the IIT Kanpur Placement Season Phase 1 Recruitment Proforma.
   - Includes: Basic statistics & probability, quantitative & analytical reasoning, SQL & Access data manipulation, hypothesis testing, customer segmentation, predictive modeling, data visualization, stakeholder communication, and compliance/risk control.
2. **Tier 2: Supporting Analytics Pillars (`[SUPPORTING]`)**:
   - Technical concepts essential for performing modern analytics and passing technical interview rounds.
   - Includes: Advanced SQL window functions, CTEs, join fan-out debugging, A/B test sample sizing, confusion matrix threshold optimization, ROC-AUC, and financial services case frameworks.
3. **Tier 3: Advanced Banking & Risk Extensions (`[ADVANCED / SUPPLEMENTARY]`)**:
   - Banking-specific risk analytics and model governance methods that give candidates a decisive competitive advantage in managerial and technical rounds.
   - Includes: Credit risk decile analysis, Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS) statistic, Population Stability Index (PSI) model drift monitoring, Fair Lending regulations, and SHAP explainability.
