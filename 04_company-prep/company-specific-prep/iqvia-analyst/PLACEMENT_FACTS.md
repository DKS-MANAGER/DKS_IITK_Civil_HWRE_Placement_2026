## 3. Compensation Breakdown & Evidentiary Anomaly Analysis

The figures entered into the IIT Kanpur recruitment proforma are recorded below, followed by an immediate audit of mathematical and unit inconsistencies:

```

│ Basic Salary             │ ₹4,80,000 / year     │ ₹40,000 / month           │

├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────┼───────────────────────────┤
│ CALCULATED GROSS SALARY  │ ₹12,00,000 / year    │ Sum = Basic + HRA + Others│
├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────┼───────────────────────────┤
│ Stated Gross on Proforma │ ₹12,00,000 / year    │ Perfectly matches sum     │
│ Stated Fixed Take-Home   │ ₹12,57,600 / year    │ ⚠️ ANOMALY: Exceeds Gross │

└──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────┴───────────────────────────┘
```

### Critical Compensation Inconsistency Analysis
1. **The Take-Home Inconsistency**:
   * The proforma records a "Fixed take-home salary per annum" of **₹12,57,600**, while recording a "Gross Salary" of **₹12,00,000**.
   * By statutory definition in Indian payroll (Payment of Gratuity Act, Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act), take-home pay must equal Gross Salary minus employee statutory deductions (EPF $12\%$ of Basic, Professional Tax $\approx ₹2,400\text{/yr}$, and Tax Deducted at Source / TDS).
   * Here, the stated take-home exceeds the stated gross by **₹57,600 per annum** ($₹12,57,600 - ₹12,00,000 = ₹57,600$).
   * **Root-Cause Hypothesis**: In campus proforma entries, recruiter HR representatives frequently conflate figures. ₹57,600 represents exactly $12\%$ of the ₹4,80,000 Basic pay ($0.12 \times 4,80,000 = 57,600$), which is the employer's statutory Provident Fund contribution. If the HR representative erroneously added the employer PF contribution to the Gross salary ($₹12,00,000 + ₹57,600 = ₹12,57,600$) instead of subtracting employee deductions, this exact anomaly is generated.
   * **CTC Delta**: Total CTC is ₹13,77,600. The difference between CTC and Gross is $₹13,77,600 - ₹12,00,000 = ₹1,77,600$. This ₹1,77,600 encompasses Employer EPF (₹57,600), Gratuity ($4.81\%$ of basic $\approx ₹23,088$), group medical/term life insurance, and potentially an annual performance bonus / variable pay component ($\approx ₹80,000 - ₹96,000$).
   * **Actionable Directive**: The candidate must treat ₹12.00 LPA Gross as the confirmed payroll baseline and verify the final offer annexure with SPO. Realistic in-hand monthly credit after EPF and income tax under the New Tax Regime is approximately **₹88,000 – ₹92,000/month**.
