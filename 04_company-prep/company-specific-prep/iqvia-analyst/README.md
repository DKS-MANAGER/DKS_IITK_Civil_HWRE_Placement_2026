## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Mission

This preparation hub provides an authoritative, mathematically rigorous, and evidence-audited training package for the **Analyst** recruitment drive by **IQVIA India** at IIT Kanpur.

IQVIA (NYSE: IQV) is a Fortune 500 global leader in healthcare intelligence, clinical research, real-world evidence (RWE), and commercial solutions, operating across 100+ countries with 87,000+ employees. Formed by the historic 2016 merger of **Quintiles** (the premier clinical research organization) and **IMS Health** (the global standard in healthcare market data since 1954), IQVIA possesses the world's largest proprietary healthcare data ecosystem—encompassing 1.2+ billion non-identified patient records, 100+ billion healthcare records processed annually, and exhaustive global pharmaceutical sales data (NPA, Xponent®, SMART).

The **Analyst** role inside IQVIA's Commercial Delivery and Analytics Hub in India (Bengaluru, Kochi, Gurugram) delivers high-impact commercial decision support to premier European and US pharmaceutical and biotechnology clients. The role requires bridging advanced quantitative modeling with commercial strategy:
1. **Marketing Mix Modelling (MMM)**: Quantifying sales drivers, promotional carryover (adstock decay), and channel saturation to maximize brand revenue.
2. **Physician & Customer Analytics**: Segmenting Healthcare Professionals (HCPs) and profiling patient journeys to direct digital and sales-rep promotional spend.
3. **Forecasting & Market Sizing**: Forecasting prescription volume (TRx/NRx) across multi-year patent and loss-of-exclusivity (LOE) horizons.
4. **Promotional ROI & Optimization**: Solving linear and non-linear resource allocation problems to determine optimal field-force sizing and multi-channel marketing budgets.
5. **Multi-Tool Fluency**: Executing analytical pipelines across **Python, SQL, R, SAS, MS Excel**, and presenting actionable recommendations via **PowerPoint**.
