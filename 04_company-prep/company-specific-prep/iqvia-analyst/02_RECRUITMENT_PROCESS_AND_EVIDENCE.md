## 4. Technical Interview Rounds: Panel Architecture & Evaluation Pillars

During live interviews, the IQVIA evaluation panel grades candidates across four distinct dimensions:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

├──────────────────────────────────────┼───────┼──────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Commercial Case & Business Sense  │  20%  │ Structured problem solving,  │
│                                      │       │ translating metrics to ROI   │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼───────┼──────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. Communication & Research Defense  │  20%  │ Articulate defense of resume │
│                                      │       │ projects, executive presence │
└──────────────────────────────────────┴───────┴──────────────────────────────┘
```

### 4.1 What Interviewers Probe in Technical Rounds
1. **Resume Project Deep-Dive**: The panel will select the candidate's strongest computational or machine-learning project (e.g., infrastructure risk classification with XGBoost and SHAP) and probe every design decision:
   * *"Why did you choose XGBoost over Random Forest or Logistic Regression?"*
   * *"How did you handle class imbalance, and why did you look at PR-AUC instead of ROC-AUC?"*
   * *"How do SHAP values work mathematically, and how did you explain feature importance to non-technical stakeholders?"*
2. **Live SQL Coding**: Candidates are frequently asked to share their screen or type into a collaborative text editor (Notepad, Google Docs, or CoderPad):
   * Writing multi-table `JOIN` statements (e.g., linking a `prescriptions` table with an `hcp_details` table).
   * Aggregating volume by physician specialty and filtering with `HAVING SUM(trx) > 500`.
   * Finding the top 3 prescribers per territory using window functions (`DENSE_RANK() OVER (PARTITION BY territory_id ORDER BY trx DESC)`).
3. **Applied Healthcare Business Scenarios**:
   * *"A brand manager notices that despite a 20% increase in sales-rep visits to cardiologists in Q2, overall new prescription volume (NRx) remained completely flat. How would you systematically investigate the root cause using data?"*

## 5. HR & Cultural Fit Interview: Global Delivery & Quality Rigor

The final stage evaluates alignment with IQVIA's corporate values and global delivery model:

* **Global Stakeholder Management**: IQVIA India analytics teams deliver directly to brand managers and commercial directors in the US and Europe. Interviewers check whether the candidate can communicate clearly in professional English, manage time-zone handoffs, and structure presentation slides logically.
* **Turnaround Under Pressure**: Client commercial launches and brand planning cycles have non-negotiable quarterly deadlines. Candidates must demonstrate high personal resilience, task prioritization, and structured problem ownership.
* **Uncompromising Quality Control (QA/QC)**: In commercial pharma, an erroneous calculation in a marketing mix model or an inaccurate physician incentive payout can trigger severe financial and regulatory penalties. The panel will look for candidates who exhibit meticulous data verification habits.
