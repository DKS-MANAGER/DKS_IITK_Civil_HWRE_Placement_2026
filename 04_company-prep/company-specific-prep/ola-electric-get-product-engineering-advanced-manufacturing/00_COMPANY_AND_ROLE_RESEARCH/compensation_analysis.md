## 3. Analysis of the Stated "Fixed Take-Home" (₹20,90,909)

The placement proforma specifically quotes an annual take-home figure of **₹20,90,909**. Let us examine the mathematical delta:

$$\Delta = \text{Fixed Gross (₹23,00,000)} - \text{Stated Take-Home (₹20,90,909)} = \mathbf{₹2,09,091}$$

### Deductions Explaining the ₹2,09,091 Delta:
1. **Statutory Employee Provident Fund (EPF):**  
   $12\%$ of Basic Salary ($12\% \times ₹9,20,000 = ₹1,10,400\text{ annually}$).
2. **Professional Tax (Karnataka / Tamil Nadu):**  
   Standard slab of ₹200/month ($₹2,400\text{ annually}$).
3. **Statutory Gratuity & Group Medical Insurance:**  
   Gratuity provision ($\approx ₹44,244$) + Group Mediclaim deduction ($\approx ₹15,000 - ₹20,000$).
4. **Sum of Deductions:**
   $$₹1,10,400\text{ (EPF)} + ₹2,400\text{ (PT)} + ₹44,244\text{ (Gratuity)} + ₹18,000\text{ (Insurance)} \approx \mathbf{₹1,75,044}$$
   The stated take-home represents the **gross pre-tax disposable salary before personal income tax (TDS) is deducted**.

## 4. Net Monthly In-Hand Salary (Post-Income Tax Estimates)

Under the Indian Income Tax Act (FY 2024–25 / AY 2025–26), personal income tax (TDS) must be accounted for to determine actual cash credited to the candidate's bank account each month:

### Tax Computation under the New Tax Regime (Default):
- Gross Taxable Income: $\approx ₹23,00,000 - ₹75,000\text{ (Standard Deduction)} = ₹22,25,000$.
- Tax Slab Calculations:
  - Up to ₹3,00,000: Nil ($₹0$)
  - ₹3,00,001 to ₹7,00,000 ($5\%$ of ₹4L): ₹20,000
  - ₹7,00,001 to ₹10,00,000 ($10\%$ of ₹3L): ₹30,000
  - ₹10,00,001 to ₹12,00,000 ($15\%$ of ₹2L): ₹30,000
  - ₹12,00,001 to ₹15,00,000 ($20\%$ of ₹3L): ₹60,000
  - Above ₹15,00,000 ($30\%$ of ₹7,25,000): ₹2,17,500
- Total Basic Tax: $₹20,000 + ₹30,000 + ₹30,000 + ₹60,000 + ₹2,17,500 = ₹3,57,500$.
- Add $4\%$ Health & Education Cess: $₹3,57,500 \times 1.04 = \mathbf{₹3,71,800\text{ per annum}}$.
- Monthly TDS: $\approx ₹30,983\text{ per month}$.

### Projected Realistic Monthly Net Take-Home:
$$\text{Monthly Pre-Tax Disposable} \approx \frac{₹20,90,909}{12} \approx ₹1,74,242$$
$$\text{Estimated Monthly Net Cash Credit} = ₹1,74,242 - ₹30,983\text{ (TDS)} \approx \mathbf{₹1,43,000 - ₹1,48,000\text{ per month}}$$

*(In the first or second month, the ₹2,00,000 joining bonus will be credited post-tax, adding approximately ₹1,38,000 net cash).*

## 5. Joining Bonus Terms & Clawback Provisions

The ₹2,00,000 joining bonus is governed by standard automotive industry retention clauses:
1. **Disbursement Timing:** Paid within the first 30 to 60 days following formal joining.
2. **Clawback Period:** Typically subject to a **12-month or 24-month retention clause**. If the candidate voluntarily resigns or is terminated for cause before completing the tenure, the gross amount (₹2,00,000) must be repaid in full to the company.
3. **Tax Implications:** The bonus is fully taxable in the month of payout as per the candidate's marginal tax bracket ($30\% + \text{cess} \implies 31.2\%$).
