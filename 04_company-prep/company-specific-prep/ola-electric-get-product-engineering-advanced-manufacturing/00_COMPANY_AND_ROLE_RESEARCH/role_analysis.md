## 1. Role Purpose & Strategic Positioning

The **Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET)** in **Product Engineering & Advanced Manufacturing** is a premier campus technical track designed to bridge the gap between **virtual product R&D** and **high-volume gigawatt-scale production**. 

Unlike conventional automotive roles that isolate design engineers in corporate offices and manufacturing engineers on the shop floor, Ola Electric operates an agile, integrated engineering model. GETs are trained to understand how CAD/FEA design decisions directly dictate tooling wear, robotic cycle times, weld joint fatigue, and production scrap rates in a factory producing thousands of vehicles per day.

## 2. Core Functional Domains & Deliverables

### Domain A: Product Engineering (Vehicle Systems & CAE)
GETs assigned to Product Engineering operate within the Vehicle Systems, Chassis & Suspension, Powertrain, or Battery Pack packaging pods:
1. **Structural & Mechanical Design:**
   - 3D CAD modeling of chassis tubular frames, swingarms, suspension linkages, motor brackets, and battery enclosures.
   - Applying Design for Manufacturing and Assembly (DFM/DFA) principles to eliminate fasteners, reduce part counts, and optimize draft angles for die-cast parts.
2. **Finite Element Analysis (FEA) & Simulation:**
   - Conducting linear static, non-linear dynamic, and modal vibration simulations on chassis and enclosure sub-assemblies.
   - Performing mesh convergence studies, stress concentration audits, and multi-axial fatigue life predictions under cyclic road loads (pot-holes, curb bumps).
3. **Thermal Management & Fluid Systems:**
   - Modeling heat rejection from lithium-ion cells and power electronic inverters.
   - Designing passive phase-change thermal barriers, heat sinks, and thermal interface materials (TIM) to prevent localized cell hot-spots.
4. **Physical Validation & Homologation Testing:**
   - Instrumenting prototypes with strain gauges, accelerometers, and thermocouples during track durability and four-poster shaker rig testing.
   - Executing regulatory abuse testing compliant with **AIS-156 Amendment 3** (drop tests, penetration, thermal propagation).

---

### Domain B: Advanced Manufacturing & Process Engineering
GETs deployed to the Futurefactory or Gigafactory lead production line efficiency, automation, and quality yield:
1. **Automated Assembly & Robotic Process Optimization:**
   - Programming, optimizing, and balancing automated assembly stations utilizing ABB and KUKA 6-axis industrial robots.
   - Monitoring robotic welding parameters (arc voltage, wire feed rate, shielding gas flow, torch angle) to eliminate porosity, spatter, and burn-through.
2. **Precision Tooling & Manufacturing Processes:**
   - Overseeing high-pressure aluminum die-casting (HPDC) parameters (die temperature, injection shot velocity, vacuum venting) to eliminate cold shuts and shrinkage porosity in swingarms.
   - Supervising precision stamping lines, die clearances, and springback compensation on high-strength steel chassis tubes.
3. **Cleanroom Battery Cell Packaging:**
   - Managing ultra-dry room operations ($RH < 2\%$) for 4680 cell manufacturing and pack assembly.
   - Supervising automated micro-laser welding of tabless current collectors and busbars, ensuring electrical contact resistance is minimized.
4. **Quality Engineering, SPC & Six Sigma:**
   - Implementing Statistical Process Control (SPC) across critical-to-quality (CTQ) dimensions: tracking $\bar{X}-R$ control charts and calculating process capability ($C_p, C_{pk} \ge 1.33$).
   - Leading Root Cause Analysis (RCA) using 8D methodology, Fishbone diagrams, and 5-Whys when defect spikes occur.
   - Driving Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) improvements, bottleneck analysis, and takt time reduction to boost daily line throughput.

## 4. Key Cross-Functional Interfaces

On any given day, an Ola Electric GET coordinates across multiple stakeholder groups:
- **Design Studio & Styling:** Translating clay/digital concept styling surfaces into manufacturable CAD parts with proper tooling clearances.
- **CAE & Simulation Pod:** Cross-verifying virtual stress and thermal simulation predictions with physical strain-gauge and thermocouple data.
- **Tooling & Die Makers:** Sourcing and validating high-pressure die-casting dies, stamping dies, and plastic injection molds.
- **Plant Operations & Assembly Shift Supervisors:** Resolving line stoppages, takt time bottlenecks, and ergonomics issues in real time.
- **Quality Assurance & Metrology:** Utilizing Coordinate Measuring Machines (CMM) and 3D optical scanning to perform First Article Inspections (FAI).
- **Homologation & Regulatory Affairs:** Ensuring all components meet ARAI / ICAT certification standards under CMVR (Central Motor Vehicles Rules).
