## 3. Strict Rules of Non-Fabrication & Evidence Boundaries

1. **No Speculation on Blank Proforma Fields:**  
   The blank programme eligibility matrix on the campus proforma is recorded explicitly as `[UNVERIFIED]`. We never pretend that Civil Engineering is confirmed eligible or confirmed banned without official SPO written proof.
2. **Synthetic Data Labeling:**  
   All four practical engineering projects in Module 05 utilize explicitly labeled **synthetic production datasets** and simplified analytical geometries. They are never represented as proprietary Ola Electric CAD files or confidential plant telematics.
3. **Mock Assessment Authenticity:**  
   Technical and aptitude mock tests in Module 03 are pedagogical preparation tools matching industry test patterns; they are strictly never presented as leaked or stolen actual company assessment papers.
