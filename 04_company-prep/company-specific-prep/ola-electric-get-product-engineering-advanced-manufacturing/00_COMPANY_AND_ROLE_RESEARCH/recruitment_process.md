## 2. Granular Stage-by-Stage Analysis

### Stage 1: Application Submission & Resume Shortlisting
- **Mechanism:** Students' Placement Office (SPO) 1-Stop portal registration.
- **Criteria Evaluated:** Academic performance (CPI / CGPA), foundational engineering projects, computational proficiency (CAD/FEA/CFD/Python), and demonstrated technical leadership.
- **Candidate Focus:** Resume must feature clear **Google X-Y-Z bullet points** linking continuum mechanics, numerical simulation, and machine learning to vehicle and thermal systems.

### Stage 2: Pre-Placement Talk (PPT)
- **Format:** Virtual or in-person presentation by senior Ola Electric engineering leadership (Vice Presidents / Chief Engineers from Vehicle Engineering, Futurefactory Plant Operations, or Gigafactory).
- **Core Content:** Vision for the Futurefactory, Gigafactory 4680 cell roadmap, Roadster motorcycle rollout, MoveOS innovation, and manufacturing culture.
- **Strategic Imperative:** Take notes on specific buzzwords, active plant metrics (e.g., current GWh run rates, automation percentages, defect reduction initiatives) to weave into GD and interview answers.

### Stage 3: Group Discussion (GD) — The High-Stakes Screen
- **Format:** 8 to 10 candidates seated in a group; 3 minutes individual thinking time followed by 12 to 15 minutes of open discussion. Moderated by senior technical recruiters or plant leads.
- **Explicit Listing:** The IIT Kanpur proforma **explicitly lists Group Discussion** as a core selection stage.
- **Typical Topic Types:**
  - Technological debates (e.g., *Battery Swapping vs Fast Charging in Indian Metros*; *LFP vs NMC Chemistry for Tropical Climates*).
  - Industrial and economic policy (e.g., *Make in India vs Immediate Global Supply Chain Sourcing*; *Can India Become an EV Export Hub by 2030?*).
  - Manufacturing strategy (e.g., *Robotic Automation vs Labor Employment in High-Volume Assembly*).
- **Evaluation Rubric:**
  - Structured articulation: Opening with a clear thesis statement.
  - Evidence-based claims: Citing actual government policies (FAME-II, PM E-DRIVE, PLI-ACC) and technical metrics.
  - Collaborative leadership: Inviting silent peers into the discussion and synthesizing diverging viewpoints without aggression.

### Stage 4: Online Technical & Aptitude Assessment
- **Platform:** Wheebox, Mettl, or HackerEarth.
- **Duration:** 90 to 120 minutes.
- **Structure:**
  - Section 1: Quantitative Aptitude & Analytical Reasoning (25–30 questions).
  - Section 2: Core Engineering Mechanics & Strength of Materials (25–30 questions).
  - Section 3: Manufacturing Processes, SPC, Quality & GD&T (25–30 questions).
  - Section 4: Materials, Metallurgy & Battery Fundamentals (20–25 questions).
- **Marking Scheme:** Typically $+1.0$ for correct answer; $-0.25$ penalty for incorrect answer.

### Stage 5: Technical Interview (Round 1 / Round 2)
- **Panel:** Lead Vehicle Architecture Engineer, Senior CAE/FEA Lead, or Futurefactory Advanced Manufacturing Head.
- **Duration:** 45 to 60 minutes.
- **Format:** Whiteboard derivations, free-body diagram sketch tests, CAD/FEA interpretation, plant troubleshooting scenarios, and project grilling.
- **Key Probe Areas:**
  - First-principles mechanics (Mohr's circle, stress concentrations, failure theories).
  - Manufacturing defect diagnosis (die-casting porosity, weld fatigue, injection molding sink marks).
  - Practical scenarios (e.g., *A battery enclosure fails vibration testing at 45 Hz. Walk me through your diagnostic and redesign steps.*).
  - Academic thesis defense: Testing candidate honesty and depth on OpenFOAM CFD and machine learning.

### Stage 6: HR & Culture-Fit Interview
- **Panel:** Senior Talent Acquisition Business Partner / Human Resources Director.
- **Duration:** 20 to 30 minutes.
- **Format:** Behavioral questions based on the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
- **Key Verification Points:**
  - "Why Ola Electric?" and understanding the fast-paced, high-pressure manufacturing startup culture.
  - Justification for the transition from Civil/HWRE to EV engineering.
  - Willingness to deploy to either the **Bengaluru R&D hub** or the **Krishnagiri/Hosur manufacturing complex** (shift work, plant floor presence).
  - Alignment with compensation terms (₹23L Fixed Gross + ₹2L Joining Bonus).
