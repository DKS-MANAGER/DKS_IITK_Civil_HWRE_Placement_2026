## 2. Priority Tier Definitions & Study Focus

### Priority Tier P0: Non-Negotiable Core (Must Master — 65% of Prep Time)
- **Topics:** Mohr's circle, principal stresses, Von Mises yield criterion, beam bending and torsion, $C_p$ and $C_{pk}$ derivation, 3-sigma control limits, high-pressure die-casting defect troubleshooting, lithium-ion battery thermal runaway mechanisms, and AIS-156 Amendment 3 regulations.
- **Why P0?** If you fail an interview question on Mohr's circle, process capability, or thermal runaway, you will be eliminated immediately regardless of your resume.

### Priority Tier P1: High-Yield Engineering Operations (25% of Prep Time)
- **Topics:** Robotic MIG welding parameters and HAZ crack mitigation, plastic injection molding defects (sink marks, warpage, weld lines), FEA mesh independence and element selection, Lean production metrics (takt time, cycle time, line balancing, OEE), metallurgy of aluminum-silicon casting alloys, and government EV policies (PM E-DRIVE, PLI-ACC).
- **Why P1?** These topics bridge theoretical mechanics to the actual day-to-day operations of the Ola Futurefactory and Gigafactory.

### Priority Tier P2: Supplementary & Contextual Depth (10% of Prep Time)
- **Topics:** NVH resonant frequency isolation, battery enclosure polymer flame retardancy (UL-94 standards), cathodic electrodeposition (CED) paint processes, and battery swapping economics.
- **Why P2?** Differentiates a good candidate from a top-percentile candidate during senior plant manager interviews.
