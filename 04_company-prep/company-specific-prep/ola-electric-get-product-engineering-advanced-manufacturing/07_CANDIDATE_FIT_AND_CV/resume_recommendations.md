# Consulting-Grade Resume Bullet Recommendations (Google X-Y-Z Format)

## 1. The Google X-Y-Z Formula
Every high-impact technical resume bullet must follow the structure:
$$\text{"Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]"}$$
- **X (Outcome):** Clear technical or operational result achieved.
- **Y (Metric):** Measurable quantitative benchmark (%, time saved, accuracy, stress reduction, marks).
- **Z (Action):** The rigorous engineering methodology, computational tool, or experimental technique utilized.
