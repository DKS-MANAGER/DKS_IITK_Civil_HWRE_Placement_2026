## 3. The 2-Minute Comprehensive Executive Pitch (Deep-Dive Opening)
> *"Good morning. I am [Your Name], currently pursuing my M.Tech at IIT Kanpur, following my undergraduate engineering degree from [Undergraduate Institute].
>

This experience taught me how to model complex fluid and thermal phenomena without relying on idealized assumptions.

I learned how to handle extreme class imbalance, extract signal from noisy sensor data, and provide transparent, interpretable engineering insights.
> 
> I chose to apply to Ola Electric because you are not merely assembling imported parts; you are leading India's clean energy transition through radical vertical integration—designing in-house motor powertrains, scaling over 5,000 industrial robots at Futurefactory, and pioneering 4680 cell manufacturing at the Gigafactory. 
> 
> Whether it is solving structural fatigue and battery pack thermal dissipation in Product Engineering, or driving Six Sigma process capability ($C_p, C_{pk}$) and line balancing in Advanced Manufacturing, I bring the mathematical depth, computational toolset, and shop-floor ownership to deliver immediate impact.
