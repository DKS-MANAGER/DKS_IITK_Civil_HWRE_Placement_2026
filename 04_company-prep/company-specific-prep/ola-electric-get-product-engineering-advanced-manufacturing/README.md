## 1. Executive Summary & Preparation Mandate

This preparation suite is engineered specifically for the **Ola Electric Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET)** recruitment drive in **Product Engineering & Advanced Manufacturing** at IIT Kanpur (2026–27 Phase 1).

Ola Electric is India’s largest pure-play electric two-wheeler (E2W) OEM, vertically integrating vehicle design, motor powertrain assembly, battery pack packaging, automated robotic manufacturing (Futurefactory in Krishnagiri, Tamil Nadu), and indigenous lithium-ion battery cell development (Gigafactory producing 4680 cylindrical Bharat Cells).

### The Dual Challenge for the Candidate
1. **Critical Discipline Transition (Engineering/Fluids to EV Product Engineering):**  
   While candidates from non-mechanical branches may possess deep expertise in numerical fluid-thermal simulation (OpenFOAM, CFD, Navier-Stokes RANS, high-performance computing), machine learning (XGBoost/SHAP), and mathematical modeling, this role demands rigorous mastery of **mechanical engineering mechanics, CAD/FEA, manufacturing processes (die-casting, robotic welding, stamping, injection molding), metallurgy, statistical process control (SPC / Six Sigma), and lithium-ion battery pack safety**.
2. **Eligibility Ambiguity:**  
   The official IIT Kanpur placement proforma leaves the **programme eligibility matrix completely blank**. As established in our research, the candidate must treat eligibility as `[UNVERIFIED]` and proactively clarify with the Students' Placement Office (SPO), while preparing a bulletproof, first-principles interdisciplinary narrative.
