# GD Evidence, Data & Policy Reference Repository

## 1. Regulatory Policies & Government Schemes

### PM E-DRIVE (PM Electric Drive Revolution in Innovative Vehicle Enhancement)
- **Approved Outlay:** ₹10,900 Crore (approved September 2024 for 2-year implementation).
- **Target Fleet:** Supports 24.79 lakh electric two-wheelers (e-2W), 3.16 lakh electric three-wheelers (e-3W), and 14,028 electric buses.
- **Subsidy Structure for e-2W:**
  - *FY25:* ₹5,000 per kWh (capped at ₹10,000 per vehicle).
  - *FY26:* ₹2,500 per kWh (capped at ₹5,000 per vehicle).
  - Explicit design: Gradual taper to prepare the industry for unsubsidized market parity by 2026.
- **Fast-Charging Allocation:** ₹2,000 Crore earmarked to install 22,100 fast chargers for e-4Ws, 1,800 for e-buses, and 48,400 for e-2W/3Ws.

### PLI-ACC (Production Linked Incentive for Advanced Chemistry Cell Battery Storage)
- **Approved Outlay:** ₹18,100 Crore for 50 GWh cumulative capacity.
- **Selected Beneficiaries:** Ola Electric (awarded 20 GWh), Rajesh Exports (ACC Energy Storage), Reliance New Energy (awarded re-bid capacity).
- **Mandate:** Minimum $60\%$ domestic value addition (DVA) within 5 years and minimum committed investment of ₹225 Crore/GWh.

### Automotive & Auto Components PLI
- **Approved Outlay:** ₹25,938 Crore.
- **Objective:** Incentivize domestic production of Advanced Automotive Technology (AAT) products, including electric powertrains, electronic control units (ECUs), and vehicle telemetry systems.

### AIS-156 (Amendment 3) — Safety Regulatory Mandates
- **Effective Dates:** Phase 1 (December 2022), Phase 2 (March/April 2023).
- **Critical Requirements:**
  - *Thermal Runaway Early Warning:* Audio-visual warning to driver at least **5 minutes** prior to external smoke/fire breakout.
  - *Cell Spacing & Thermal Barrier:* Physical spacing and high-temperature thermal barriers between individual cells.
  - *Ingress Protection:* Mandatory **IP67** rating for pack enclosures.
  - *Testing Mandates:* Nail penetration, overcharge, over-discharge, mechanical crush, thermal shock cycling ($-40^\circ\text{C}$ to $+60^\circ\text{C}$), and drop testing.

## 3. Battery Chemistry & Cost Economics

### Global Lithium-Ion Battery Price Trajectory (BNEF Data)
- **2010:** $\$1,200/\text{kWh}$ (pack-level average).
- **2020:** $\$140/\text{kWh}$.
- **2022 (Commodity Spike):** Spiked to $\$151/\text{kWh}$ due to lithium carbonate and nickel inflation.
- **Late 2024:** Plummeted to **$\$80\text{--}\$90/\text{kWh}$** for LFP packs; projected to breach $\$70/\text{kWh}$ by 2026.
- **Tipping Point:** $\$100/\text{kWh}$ is widely recognized by the automotive industry as the threshold for upfront sticker-price parity between EVs and ICE vehicles.

## 4. Infrastructure & Grid Impact Data

### Power Demand Projections (Central Electricity Authority - CEA)
- By 2030, even if India achieves 80 million EVs across all segments, EV charging will consume **$< 3.5\%$ of total national electricity generation**.
- The primary challenge is not total generation capacity, but **distribution transformer (DT) loading during local evening peak hours** ($7\text{ PM}\text{ to }11\text{ PM}$).
- *Mitigation:* Time-of-Day (ToD) metering tariffs (charging $30\%$ lower electricity rates during off-peak night hours: $11\text{ PM}\text{ to }6\text{ AM}$).

### Life Cycle Emissions (Well-to-Wheel Analysis in India)
- **Current Grid Emission Factor (India):** $\approx 0.71\text{ kg } CO_2/\text{kWh}$ (grid is $\approx 72\%$ thermal coal).
- **Well-to-Wheel Emissions (ICE 2W):** $\approx 45\text{--}55\text{ g } CO_2/\text{km}$.
- **Well-to-Wheel Emissions (EV 2W on Coal Grid):** $\approx 25\text{--}30\text{ g } CO_2/\text{km}$ (even on a coal grid, EVs are **$35\text{--}45\%$ cleaner** due to central thermal plant thermodynamic efficiency $\sim 38\%$ vs small ICE engine efficiency $\sim 20\%$).
- **By 2030 (with 500 GW Non-Fossil Grid Target):** EV emissions drop to $< 12\text{ g } CO_2/\text{km}$.
