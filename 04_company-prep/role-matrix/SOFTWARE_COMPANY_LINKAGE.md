## 1. Evidentiary Provenance Standard

To maintain strict truth-in-advertising and prevent guesswork:
- **`[VERIFIED]`**: Explicitly stated in SPO company job descriptions, test briefs, or verified placement interview debriefs.
- **`[SOURCE-DERIVED]`**: Directly derived from standard industrial role specifications or engineering consultancy requirements.
- **`[INFERRED]`**: Inferred from underlying engineering workflow requirements (e.g., structural drafting implies AutoCAD; geotech slope stability implies SLOPE/W).

## 5. Strategic Rule of Minimum Viable Stacks

> `[PREPARATION HEURISTIC]`
> - **Core Civil (Structural & EPC)**: Master AutoCAD + one analysis tool (ETABS or STAAD.Pro) + Excel. Add Primavera P6 if targeting Project Management / Site Execution.
> - **Core HWRE & Water Resources**: Master HEC-RAS + QGIS + Python + Excel. Add HEC-HMS or EPANET for catchment/network roles.
> - **CFD & Fluid Mechanics R&D**: Master OpenFOAM + ParaView + Linux shell + Python.
> - **Analytics & Non-Core**: Master Excel (advanced models) + SQL (joins/window functions) + Python (Pandas/NumPy).
