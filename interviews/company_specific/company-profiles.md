## Interview Experience Template

Use this format to log your interview experience:

| Field | Required Content |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Role** | Company, discipline, location, date |
| **Rounds** | Shortlisting, written, technical, HR, GD |
| **Technical asks** | Exact questions, follow-up depth, what you missed |
| **HR asks** | Motivation, relocation, teamwork, leadership, gaps |
| **Takeaway** | 3 weak areas + 3 improvement actions |
| **Attachments** | Resume version, prep sheet, score sheet, interviewer cues |

## Submission Rules for Interview Logs

1. Use the template in `/templates/interview_template.md`
2. Keep names anonymized unless explicitly approved
3. Include exact rounds, question themes, your answers, and postmortem
4. Tag files by company, year, and role for searchability
5. Prefer short factual logs over long narratives
6. Submit within 48 hours of interview
