# Hiremi

## Interview Process
Hiremi's selection process consists of multiple rounds:

1. **Group Discussion (GD)** — Evaluates confidence in speaking and unique presentation of ideas
2. **Coding Round** — Challenging if not adequately practiced; requires confidence and clarity in explaining code

## Key Notes
- Self-presentation plays a significant role
- Coding round requires extensive practice
- GD assesses communication and logical thinking

## Preparation Tips
- Practice coding problems extensively
- Prepare for GD on general topics
- Work on clear and confident self-presentation
