# Johnson Controls

## Interview Process
Johnson Controls' interview process consists of four rounds:

1. **Online Test** — Aptitude and core subject questions
2. **L1 Technical Interview** — Basic core questions
3. **L2 Technical Interview** — Area of interest from resume
4. **HR Round** — General HR questions and fit assessment

## Key Notes
- Technical rounds focus on core engineering fundamentals
- L2 round is tailored to your resume and stated interests
- HR round assesses cultural fit and communication

## Preparation Tips
- Review core civil/HWRE concepts
- Be prepared to discuss projects listed on your resume in depth
- Practice basic aptitude for the online test
