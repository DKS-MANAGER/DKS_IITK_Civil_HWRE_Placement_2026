# Tech Mahindra

## Process Overview

Tech Mahindra's on-campus recruitment follows a **5-round** structure:

| Round | Focus | Duration / Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Aptitude | Basic quantitative and logical aptitude | Elimination filter |
| 2. English Comprehension | Writing, listening, fill-in-the-blanks | Essay (300–400 words), audio transcription |
| 3. Coding | 2–3 basic programming problems | Array sum, prime numbers, etc. |
| 4. Technical HR | Programming theory and coding | 30–40 minutes |
| 5. General HR | Personal details, location preference | Fit and logistics |

## Round-by-Round Preparation

### Round 1: Aptitude
- Practice speed math, percentages, ratios, time-work, and data interpretation.
- Use RS Aggarwal or equivalent for foundational speed.

### Round 2: English Comprehension
- **Writing:** Structure essays with an introduction, body, and conclusion. Keep language simple and clear.
- **Listening:** Practice dictation exercises; watch English news or TED talks and transcribe key sentences.
- **Fill in the blanks:** Focus on prepositions, tenses, and common collocations.

### Round 3: Coding
- Questions are basic; focus on correct logic and clean code.
- Practice array manipulation, string operations, and number theory basics.
- Be ready to explain your approach verbally after coding.

### Round 4: Technical HR
- Expect both theoretical questions (OOP, DBMS, OS basics) and live coding.
- Review your resume projects; you may be asked to walk through one.
- Prepare to write code on paper or a shared editor.

### Round 5: General HR
- Know your resume thoroughly.
- Be clear about location preferences and willingness to relocate.
- Prepare a short answer for "Why Tech Mahindra?"

## Common HR Questions
- Tell me about yourself.
- Why do you want to join Tech Mahindra?
- What are your strengths and weaknesses?
- Where do you see yourself in five years?
- Are you willing to work in shifts or relocate?

## Key Takeaways
- Aptitude and English are elimination rounds; do not skip them.
- Coding is straightforward but requires clarity of thought.
- Technical HR is conversational; confidence matters as much as correctness.
