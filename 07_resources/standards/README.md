# Engineering Codes, Guidelines & Standards Reference

> **Domain**: Indian Standards (IS), Indian Roads Congress (IRC), CPHEEO, and US Federal Standards (FHWA).

---

## 1. Water Resources & Hydraulic Engineering Standards
* **IS 4410**: Glossary of terms relating to river valley projects.
* **IS 6966**: Guidelines for hydraulic design of barrages and weirs.
* **IS 10646**: Guidelines for hydraulic design of canal drops/falls.
* **FHWA HEC-18**: Evaluating Scour at Bridges (US Federal Highway Administration) — *CSU equation, pier scour, contraction scour.*
* **CPHEEO Manual on Water Supply & Treatment**: Central Public Health & Environmental Engineering Organisation guidelines for municipal water distribution.

---

## 2. Structural & Construction Standards
* **IS 456:2000**: Plain and Reinforced Concrete — Code of Practice.
* **IS 800:2007**: General Construction in Steel — Code of Practice.
* **IS 1893:2016 (Part 1)**: Criteria for Earthquake Resistant Design of Structures.
* **IS 875 (Parts 1–5)**: Design Loads (Dead, Imposed, Wind, Snow, Special) for Buildings and Structures.
* **IS 10262:2019**: Concrete Mix Proportioning — Guidelines.

---

## 3. Highway & Transportation Standards
* **IRC 37:2018**: Guidelines for the Design of Flexible Pavements.
* **IRC 58:2015**: Guidelines for the Design of Plain Jointed Rigid Pavements for Highways.
* **IRC 6:2017**: Standard Specifications and Code of Practice for Road Bridges — Section II (Loads & Stresses).
