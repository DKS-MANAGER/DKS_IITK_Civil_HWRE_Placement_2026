## Day-by-Day Detailed Directives

* **Day 1 (Aptitude)**: Solve 30 questions on Speed-Time-Distance, Percentages, Work-Time, and Syllogisms. Memorize formula cheat-sheet.
* **Day 2 (Core Civil & HWRE)**: Review shear force/bending moment diagrams, under-reinforced beam rules, open channel flow critical depth, and unit hydrograph assumptions.
* **Day 3 (Fluids & CFD)**: Revisit Navier-Stokes equation terms, $k$-$\epsilon$ vs $k$-$\omega$ SST models, boundary layer $y^+$ definitions.
* **Day 4 (Target Role Specific)**:
  * For Consulting: Review MECE issue trees, profitability equations, 3 guesstimates.
  * For Software/Data: Practice Python array sliding window and SQL multi-table joins.
* **Day 5 (Project Defense)**: Practice explaining your thesis and coursework projects out loud using the 4-step framework. Anticipate grilling questions.
* **Day 6 (Behavioral & HR)**: Polish your 90-second self-introduction. Prepare 4 STAR stories (Conflict, Pressure, Failure, Leadership).
* **Day 7 (Interview Tomorrow)**: Light review of target company profile, test setup (camera/mic/internet), sleep 8 hours.
