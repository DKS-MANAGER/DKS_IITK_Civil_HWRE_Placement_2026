## 1. 60-Minute Deep Revision: Core Frameworks

### 1. Profitability Tree
$$\Delta \text{Profit} = [\Delta \text{Price} \times Q + P \times \Delta Q] - [\Delta \text{Fixed} + \Delta \text{Variable} \times Q]$$

### 2. Market Entry (4-Pillar Model)
1. **Market Attractiveness**: TAM, CAGR, margins, competition.
2. **Company Fit & Capabilities**: Capital, distribution, tech IP.
3. **Financial Feasibility**: Capex, break-even years, ROI.
4. **Entry Mode**: Build (Greenfield) vs Buy (M&A) vs Partner (JV).

## 2. 30-Minute Core Revision: Formulas & Equations

| Metric / Concept | Formula / Shortcut | Use Case |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Rule of 72** | $t \approx \frac{72}{g\%}$ | Doubling time calculation |
| **CAGR Approximation** | $\text{CAGR} \approx \frac{\text{Total Growth } \%}{n} \times 0.9$ | Multi-year growth rate estimation |
| **Contribution Margin**| $\frac{P - VC}{P}$ | Unit profit contribution |
| **Break-Even Volume** | $\frac{\text{Fixed Costs}}{P - VC}$ | Output required for zero loss |
| **ROI / Payback** | $\frac{\text{Net Annual Benefit}}{\text{Initial Investment}}$ | Investment return evaluation |

## 3. 10-Minute Rapid Revision: Common Traps & High-Yield Q&A

### ⚠️ Common Interview Traps
- Jumping into frameworks before scoping the goal and timeline.
- Making arithmetic errors by not writing down zeros or bounding numbers.
- Rambling bottom-up instead of leading with Pyramid Principle synthesis.

### ⚡ Rapid Practice Questions & Answers
1. **Q**: *An airline's profit fell 40% with flat revenue. Why?* -> **A**: Fuel/ATF cost spike, older fleet inefficiency, or route mix shift to low-margin Tier-2 cities.
2. **Q**: *How to price a new toll tunnel saving 45 mins?* -> **A**: Value-based pricing: Value of time saved + fuel saved; capture ~30% in toll.
