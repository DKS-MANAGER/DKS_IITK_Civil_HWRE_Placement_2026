## Key Principles

1. **Never skip error analysis** — it's where the real improvement happens.
2. **Reattempt wrong questions** at 1/3/7 days (see [ERROR_LOG.md](ERROR_LOG.md)).
3. **Question selection** — in the test, skip questions you can't solve in 90 sec.
4. **Accuracy first** — on easy questions, never rush.
5. **Use [RAPID_REVISION.md](RAPID_REVISION.md)** in the final 1–3 days.
