# Problems on Ages

> **Priority:** P1 · **Role relevance:** Important (all roles)
> **Difficulty range:** Easy → Hard · **Time per question:** 30 sec – 2 min

---

## 1. What You Need to Know

1. Age difference is constant over time
2. Ratio of ages changes over time
3. Setting up linear equations
4. Past, present, future ages

---

## 2. Key Principle

**The age difference between two people never changes.**

If A is 5 years older than B now, A was 5 years older than B 10 years ago and will be 5 years older in 10 years.

---

## 3. Worked Examples

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** A is twice as old as B. In 5 years, A will be 1.5 times B. Present ages?
**Solution:** A = 2B. A+5 = 1.5(B+5). 2B+5 = 1.5B+7.5. 0.5B = 2.5. B = 5, A = 10.
**Answer:** A=10, B=5.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** The ratio of ages of A and B is 3:4. After 5 years, ratio is 4:5. Present ages?
**Solution:** (3x+5)/(4x+5) = 4/5. 15x+25 = 16x+20. x = 5. A=15, B=20.
**Answer:** A=15, B=20.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** A father is 3 times as old as his son. In 12 years, he will be twice as old. Present ages?
**Solution:** F = 3S. F+12 = 2(S+12). 3S+12 = 2S+24. S = 12, F = 36.
**Answer:** Father=36, Son=12.

## 5. Placement Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** A is twice as old as B. In 5 years, A will be 1.5 times B. Present age of B?
- A) 5  B) 10  C) 8  D) 12
**Answer:** A) 5. 2B+5 = 1.5(B+5). 0.5B = 2.5. B = 5.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** The ratio of ages of A and B is 3:4. After 5 years, ratio is 4:5. Present age of B?
- A) 15  B) 20  C) 25  D) 16
**Answer:** B) 20. (3x+5)/(4x+5) = 4/5. x=5. B = 20.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** A father is 3 times as old as his son. In 12 years, he will be twice as old. Present age of son?
- A) 10  B) 12  C) 15  D) 8
**Answer:** B) 12. 3S+12 = 2(S+12). S = 12.
