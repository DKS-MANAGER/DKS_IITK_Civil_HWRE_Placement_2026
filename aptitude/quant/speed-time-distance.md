# Speed, Time & Distance

> **Priority:** P0 · **Role relevance:** Critical (all roles)
> **Difficulty range:** Easy → Very Hard · **Time per question:** 30 sec – 3 min

---

## 1. What You Need to Know

1. Speed = Distance / Time
2. Unit conversion (km/h ↔ m/s)
3. Average speed
4. Relative speed (trains, boats)
5. Train crossing problems

---

## 2. Core Concepts

- **Speed** = distance covered per unit time.
- **Average speed** — NOT the arithmetic mean of speeds (unless equal times).
- **Relative speed** — add for opposite directions, subtract for same direction.
- **Train crossing** — distance = train length (+ platform length).

---

## 3. Formula Sheet

| Concept | Formula |
|:--------|:--------|
| Speed | `Distance/Time` |
| km/h → m/s | `× 5/18` |
| m/s → km/h | `× 18/5` |
| Average speed (equal distances) | `2xy/(x+y)` |
| Relative speed (opposite) | `V₁ + V₂` |
| Relative speed (same) | `V₁ - V₂` |
| Train crossing | `(L₁+L₂)/(V₁±V₂)` |

---

## 4. Standard Methods

1. **Convert units** to be consistent (km/h or m/s).
2. **Identify** whether it's same/opposite direction.
3. **Apply** the appropriate speed formula.
4. **For trains** — include train length in distance.

---

## 5. Fast Methods / Shortcuts

### Shortcut 1: Unit Conversion
**When useful:** Converting between km/h and m/s.
**Why it works:** 1 km/h = 1000m/3600s = 5/18 m/s.
**Example:** 72 km/h = `72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s`.
**When NOT to use:** When units are already consistent.

### Shortcut 2: Average Speed (Equal Distances)
**When useful:** Same distance at two different speeds.
**Why it works:** Harmonic mean of the two speeds.
**Example:** 30 km/h up, 60 km/h down. Avg = `2×30×60/90 = 40 km/h`.
**When NOT to use:** When times are equal (use arithmetic mean).

---

## 6. Worked Examples

### Example 1 (Easy)
**Q:** A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. Speed?
**Method:** Speed = D/T. **Calculation:** `120/2 = 60 km/h`. **Answer:** 60 km/h.

### Example 2 (Medium)
**Q:** A train 100 m long crosses a pole in 10 s. Speed in km/h?
**Method:** Speed = 100/10 = 10 m/s. **Calculation:** `10 × 18/5 = 36 km/h`. **Answer:** 36 km/h.

### Example 3 (Medium)
**Q:** A man travels 30 km at 30 km/h and 30 km at 60 km/h. Average speed?
**Method:** Equal distances. **Calculation:** `2×30×60/90 = 40 km/h`. **Answer:** 40 km/h.

### Example 4 (Hard)
**Q:** Two trains 100 m and 120 m long move towards each other at 40 and 50 km/h. Time to cross?
**Method:** Relative speed = 90 km/h = 25 m/s. **Calculation:** `(100+120)/25 = 8.8 s`. **Answer:** 8.8 s.

### Example 5 (Very Hard)
**Q:** A boat travels 24 km upstream in 6 h and 24 km downstream in 4 h. Speed of stream?
**Method:** Upstream = 4 km/h, downstream = 6 km/h. **Calculation:** Stream = `(6-4)/2 = 1 km/h`. **Answer:** 1 km/h.

---

## 7. Common Traps

| Trap | Why it's wrong | Correct approach |
|:-----|:---------------|:-----------------|
| Arithmetic mean of speeds | Wrong for equal distances | Use harmonic mean |
| Forgetting train length | Distance is wrong | Include train length |
| Wrong relative speed direction | Add vs subtract | Opposite=add, same=subtract |
| Unit mismatch | Wrong answer | Convert to consistent units |

## 11. Placement Questions

### Q1 (Easy, 30 sec)
**Q:** A car travels 180 km in 3 hours. Its speed in m/s is?
- A) 60  B) 16.67  C) 50  D) 20
- **Answer:** B) 16.67
- **Explanation:** 180/3 = 60 km/h = `60 × 5/18 = 16.67 m/s`.
- **Fast method:** km/h × 5/18.
- **Concept:** Unit conversion. **Difficulty:** Easy. **Time:** 30 sec. **Trap:** Choosing 60 (km/h not m/s).

### Q2 (Medium, 90 sec)
**Q:** A train 100 m long crosses a pole in 10 seconds. Its speed in km/h is?
- A) 36  B) 40  C) 30  D) 45
- **Answer:** A) 36
- **Explanation:** Speed = 100/10 = 10 m/s = `10 × 18/5 = 36 km/h`.
- **Fast method:** m/s × 18/5.
- **Concept:** Train crossing. **Difficulty:** Medium. **Time:** 90 sec. **Trap:** Forgetting conversion.

### Q3 (Hard, 2 min)
**Q:** A man travels half the distance at 40 km/h and the other half at 60 km/h. Average speed?
- A) 50  B) 48  C) 45  D) 52
- **Answer:** B) 48
- **Explanation:** `2×40×60/(40+60) = 4800/100 = 48 km/h`.
- **Fast method:** Harmonic mean.
- **Concept:** Average speed. **Difficulty:** Hard. **Time:** 2 min. **Trap:** Choosing 50 (arithmetic mean).
