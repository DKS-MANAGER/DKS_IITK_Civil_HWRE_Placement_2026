## 1. What You Need to Know

1. Profit sharing in ratio of Capital × Time
2. Simple partnership (equal time)
3. Compound partnership (different time)
4. Sleeping vs active partners

---

## 2. Formula Sheet

| Concept | Formula |
|:--------|:--------|
| Profit share | `(Capital × Time)` ratio |
| Simple partnership | Profit split in capital ratio |
| Compound partnership | Profit split in Capital×Time ratio |

---

## 3. Worked Examples

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** A invests 5000, B invests 3000 for equal time. Profit 8000. A's share?
**Solution:** Ratio 5:3. A = 5/8 × 8000 = 5000. **Answer:** Rs. 5000.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** A invests 50000 for 12 months, B invests 75000 for 8 months. Profit 45000. B's share?
**Solution:** A = 50000×12 = 600000. B = 75000×8 = 600000. Ratio 1:1. B = 22500. **Answer:** Rs. 22,500.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** A invests 20000 for 6 months, B invests 30000 for 4 months, C invests 40000 for 3 months. Profit 36000. C's share?
**Solution:** A = 120000, B = 120000, C = 120000. Ratio 1:1:1. C = 12000. **Answer:** Rs. 12,000.

## 5. Placement Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** A invests Rs. 5000 and B invests Rs. 3000 for equal time. Profit is Rs. 8000. A's share?
- A) 5000  B) 3000  C) 4000  D) 6000
**Answer:** A) 5000. Ratio 5:3. A = 5/8 × 8000 = 5000.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** A invests Rs. 50,000 for 12 months and B invests Rs. 75,000 for 8 months. Profit is Rs. 45,000. B's share?
- A) 22,500  B) 20,000  C) 25,000  D) 18,000
**Answer:** A) 22,500. Both Capital×Time = 600000. Ratio 1:1.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** A invests Rs. 20,000 for 6 months, B Rs. 30,000 for 4 months, C Rs. 40,000 for 3 months. Profit Rs. 36,000. C's share?
- A) 12,000  B) 10,000  C) 14,000  D) 8,000
**Answer:** A) 12,000. All Capital×Time = 120000. Ratio 1:1:1.
