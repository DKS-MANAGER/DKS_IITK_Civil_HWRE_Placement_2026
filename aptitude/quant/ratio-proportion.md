## 2. Core Concepts

- **Ratio** `a:b` compares two quantities of the same unit.
- **Proportion** `a:b = c:d` means `ad = bc`.
- **Direct proportion:** `y = kx` (as x increases, y increases).
- **Inverse proportion:** `y = k/x` (as x increases, y decreases).
- **Partnership:** profit split in ratio of `Capital × Time`.

---

## 3. Formula Sheet

| Concept | Formula |
|:--------|:--------|
| Proportion | `a:b = c:d ⟹ ad = bc` |
| Mean proportional of a,b | `√(ab)` |
| Third proportional of a,b | `b²/a` |
| Componendo | `(a+b)/b = (c+d)/d` |
| Dividendo | `(a-b)/b = (c-d)/d` |
| Partnership share | `Capital × Time` ratio |

---

## 4. Standard Methods

1. **Simplify** the ratio to lowest terms.
2. **Find the unit value** — divide total by sum of ratio parts.
3. **Multiply** each part by the unit value.
4. **For partnership** — compute `Capital × Time` for each partner.

---

## 5. Fast Methods / Shortcuts

### Shortcut 1: Dividing in a Ratio
**When useful:** Divide a quantity in ratio `a:b:c`.
**Why it works:** Each share = `(part/sum of parts) × total`.
**Example:** Divide 600 in ratio 2:3. Sum = 5. Shares = `2/5×600=240`, `3/5×600=360`.
**When NOT to use:** When the ratio is not in simplest form — simplify first.

### Shortcut 2: Partnership
**When useful:** Multiple investors with different capital and time.
**Why it works:** Profit is proportional to `Capital × Time`.
**Example:** A invests 50000×12, B invests 75000×8. Both = 600000. Profit split equally.
**When NOT to use:** When partners have different roles (active vs sleeping).

---

## 6. Worked Examples

### Example 1 (Easy)
**Q:** Divide 500 in ratio 2:3.
**Method:** Sum = 5. **Calculation:** `2/5×500=200`, `3/5×500=300`. **Answer:** 200, 300.

### Example 2 (Medium)
**Q:** A:B = 2:3, B:C = 4:5. Find A:B:C.
**Method:** Make B common. **Calculation:** A:B = 2:3 = 8:12; B:C = 4:5 = 12:15. **Answer:** 8:12:15.

### Example 3 (Medium)
**Q:** A invests 50000 for 12 months, B invests 75000 for 8 months. Profit 45000. B's share?
**Method:** Capital×Time. **Calculation:** A = 600000, B = 600000. Ratio 1:1. B = 22500. **Answer:** Rs. 22,500.

### Example 4 (Hard)
**Q:** The ratio of ages of A and B is 3:4. After 5 years, ratio is 4:5. Present ages?
**Method:** Let ages 3x, 4x. **Calculation:** `(3x+5)/(4x+5) = 4/5 ⟹ 15x+25 = 16x+20 ⟹ x=5`. **Answer:** 15, 20.

### Example 5 (Very Hard)
**Q:** A mixture contains milk and water in ratio 4:1. How much mixture must be replaced by water to make ratio 1:1?
**Method:** Let total 5 units. **Calculation:** After replacement, milk = 2.5. Milk removed = 4-2.5 = 1.5. Fraction = 1.5/4 = 3/8 of mixture. **Answer:** 3/8.

## 7. Common Traps

| Trap | Why it's wrong | Correct approach |
|:-----|:---------------|:-----------------|
| Not simplifying ratio first | Wrong unit value | Simplify to lowest terms |
| Wrong partnership base | Profit ∝ Capital×Time | Always multiply capital by time |
| Confusing direct/inverse | Wrong formula | Check: does y increase with x? |
| Adding ratios incorrectly | Need common term | Make middle term common |

## 11. Placement Questions

### Q1 (Easy, 30 sec)
**Q:** Divide Rs. 900 in ratio 2:3:4. What is the largest share?
- A) 200  B) 300  C) 400  D) 450
- **Answer:** C) 400
- **Explanation:** Sum = 9. Largest = `4/9 × 900 = 400`.
- **Fast method:** Largest part = `4/9 × 900`.
- **Concept:** Ratio division. **Difficulty:** Easy. **Time:** 30 sec. **Trap:** None.

### Q2 (Medium, 90 sec)
**Q:** A and B invest Rs. 20,000 and Rs. 30,000 for 8 and 6 months respectively. Profit is Rs. 34,000. B's share?
- A) 18,000  B) 16,000  C) 17,000  D) 15,000
- **Answer:** A) 18,000
- **Explanation:** A = 20000×8 = 160000; B = 30000×6 = 180000. Ratio 8:9. B = `9/17 × 34000 = 18000`.
- **Fast method:** Capital×Time ratio.
- **Concept:** Partnership. **Difficulty:** Medium. **Time:** 90 sec. **Trap:** Using capital only, ignoring time.

### Q3 (Hard, 2 min)
**Q:** The ratio of ages of A and B is 4:5. After 6 years, ratio is 5:6. Present age of B?
- A) 24  B) 30  C) 36  D) 25
- **Answer:** B) 30
- **Explanation:** `(4x+6)/(5x+6) = 5/6 ⟹ 24x+36 = 25x+30 ⟹ x=6`. B = 5×6 = 30.
- **Fast method:** Set up proportion, solve for x.
- **Concept:** Ages + ratio. **Difficulty:** Hard. **Time:** 2 min. **Trap:** Solving for A instead of B.
