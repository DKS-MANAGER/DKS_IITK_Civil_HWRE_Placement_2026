## 2. Formula Sheet

### BODMAS
**B**rackets → **O**rder (powers) → **D**ivision → **M**ultiplication → **A**ddition → **S**ubtraction

### Algebraic Identities
- `(a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²`
- `(a-b)² = a² - 2ab + b²`
- `a² - b² = (a+b)(a-b)`
- `(a+b)³ = a³ + 3a²b + 3ab² + b³`
- `a³ + b³ = (a+b)(a² - ab + b²)`

### Indices
- `a^m × a^n = a^(m+n)`
- `a^m / a^n = a^(m-n)`
- `(a^m)^n = a^(mn)`
- `a^0 = 1`

## 3. Worked Examples

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** Simplify: 12 + 8 ÷ 4 × 2
**Solution:** Division first: 8÷4=2. Then 2×2=4. Then 12+4=16. **Answer:** 16.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** Simplify: (3½ × 2⅔) / (1¼ + 1½)
**Solution:** Numerator = 7/2 × 8/3 = 28/3. Denominator = 5/4 + 3/2 = 11/4. Result = 28/3 ÷ 11/4 = 28/3 × 4/11 = 112/33. **Answer:** 112/33.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** Simplify: √(144) + √(81) - √(49)
**Solution:** 12 + 9 - 7 = 14. **Answer:** 14.

## 5. Placement Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** 12 + 8 ÷ 4 × 2 = ?
- A) 10  B) 16  C) 20  D) 8
**Answer:** B) 16. 8÷4=2, 2×2=4, 12+4=16.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** 2³ + 3² = ?
- A) 13  B) 17  C) 15  D) 11
**Answer:** B) 17. 8+9 = 17.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** Simplify: (a+b)² - (a-b)²
- A) 2ab  B) 4ab  C) a²+b²  D) 2a²
**Answer:** B) 4ab. (a²+2ab+b²) - (a²-2ab+b²) = 4ab.
