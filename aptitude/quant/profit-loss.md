# Profit, Loss & Discount

> **Priority:** P0 · **Role relevance:** Critical (all roles)
> **Difficulty range:** Easy → Very Hard · **Time per question:** 30 sec – 3 min

---

## 1. What You Need to Know

1. Cost Price (CP), Selling Price (SP), Marked Price (MP)
2. Profit & loss percentage
3. Successive discounts
4. Dishonest dealer problems
5. Marked price / discount relationships

---

## 2. Core Concepts

- **CP**: The price at which an item is bought.
- **SP**: The price at which an item is sold.
- **MP**: The listed price before discount.
- **Profit** = SP - CP (when SP > CP).
- **Loss** = CP - SP (when CP > SP).
- **Discount** is always on MP.

---

## 3. Formula Sheet

| Concept | Formula |
|:--------|:--------|
| Profit% | `(SP-CP)/CP × 100` |
| Loss% | `(CP-SP)/CP × 100` |
| SP (profit) | `CP × (1 + P%/100)` |
| SP (loss) | `CP × (1 - L%/100)` |
| Successive discounts d₁,d₂ | `d₁ + d₂ - (d₁d₂/100)` |
| MP with discount D | `SP = MP × (1 - D/100)` |

---

## 4. Standard Methods

1. **Identify CP, SP, MP** from the problem.
2. **Compute profit/loss** = SP - CP.
3. **Apply percentage** relative to CP.
4. **For discounts** — apply on MP, not CP.

---

## 5. Fast Methods / Shortcuts

### Shortcut 1: Successive Discounts
**When useful:** Two or more discounts applied in sequence.
**Why it works:** Same as successive percentage change — discounts compound.
**Example:** 20% then 10% discount = `20+10-(20×10/100) = 28%` total.
**When NOT to use:** When discounts are on different bases.

### Shortcut 2: Dishonest Dealer
**When useful:** A dealer uses false weights.
**Why it works:** Profit comes from the difference between claimed and actual weight.
**Example:** Claims 1 kg but gives 800 g. Profit = `200/800 × 100 = 25%`.
**When NOT to use:** When the dealer also marks up price.

---

## 6. Worked Examples

### Example 1 (Easy)
**Q:** CP = 100, SP = 125. Profit%?
**Method:** `(SP-CP)/CP × 100`. **Calculation:** `25/100 × 100 = 25%`. **Answer:** 25%.

### Example 2 (Medium)
**Q:** A shopkeeper marks up 20% and gives 10% discount. Net profit%?
**Method:** Successive. **Calculation:** `20-10-(20×10/100) = 8%`. **Answer:** 8%.

### Example 3 (Medium)
**Q:** CP = 500, sold at 20% loss. SP?
**Method:** `CP × (1 - L/100)`. **Calculation:** `500 × 0.8 = 400`. **Answer:** Rs. 400.

### Example 4 (Hard)
**Q:** A dishonest dealer sells at CP but uses 900 g instead of 1 kg. Profit%?
**Method:** Profit on actual weight. **Calculation:** `100/900 × 100 = 11.11%`. **Answer:** 11.11%.

### Example 5 (Very Hard)
**Q:** A man sells two items at Rs. 990 each, one at 10% profit, other at 10% loss. Net?
**Method:** Equal SP, equal % profit/loss → always loss. **Calculation:** Loss = `2×990 - (990/1.1 + 990/0.9) = 1980 - (900+1100) = -20`. **Answer:** Rs. 20 loss.

---

## 7. Common Traps

| Trap | Why it's wrong | Correct approach |
|:-----|:---------------|:-----------------|
| Discount on CP | Discount is on MP | Always apply discount to MP |
| Equal SP profit/loss = no loss | Actually a loss | Loss = `2×SP - (SP/1.1 + SP/0.9)` |
| Profit% on SP | Profit% is on CP | Always on CP |
| Adding discounts | Discounts compound | Use successive formula |

## 11. Placement Questions

### Q1 (Easy, 30 sec)
**Q:** A shopkeeper buys an item for Rs. 500 and sells it for Rs. 600. Profit%?
- A) 20%  B) 25%  C) 15%  D) 10%
- **Answer:** A) 20%
- **Explanation:** `(600-500)/500 × 100 = 20%`.
- **Fast method:** Profit 100 on 500 = 20%.
- **Concept:** Basic profit. **Difficulty:** Easy. **Time:** 30 sec. **Trap:** None.

### Q2 (Medium, 60 sec)
**Q:** A trader marks up an item by 25% and gives a 10% discount. Net profit%?
- A) 15%  B) 12.5%  C) 10%  D) 20%
- **Answer:** B) 12.5%
- **Explanation:** `25-10-(25×10/100) = 15-2.5 = 12.5%`.
- **Fast method:** Successive change formula.
- **Concept:** Markup + discount. **Difficulty:** Medium. **Time:** 60 sec. **Trap:** Adding 25-10 = 15%.

### Q3 (Hard, 2 min)
**Q:** A man sells two articles at Rs. 990 each. On one he gains 10%, on the other he loses 10%. His net gain/loss is?
- A) Rs. 20 gain  B) Rs. 20 loss  C) No gain/loss  D) Rs. 10 loss
- **Answer:** B) Rs. 20 loss
- **Explanation:** CP₁ = 990/1.1 = 900; CP₂ = 990/0.9 = 1100. Total CP = 2000, total SP = 1980. Loss = 20.
- **Fast method:** Equal SP with equal % profit/loss → always loss of `2×SP - sum of CPs`.
- **Concept:** Equal SP profit/loss. **Difficulty:** Hard. **Time:** 2 min. **Trap:** Assuming no gain/loss.

---

## 12. Rapid Revision

- **Must-know:** Profit% = `(SP-CP)/CP × 100`; discount on MP
- **Shortcut:** Successive discounts `d₁+d₂-d₁d₂/100`
- **Trap:** Equal SP profit/loss = loss
- **Common Q:** Markup/discount, dishonest dealer, equal SP
