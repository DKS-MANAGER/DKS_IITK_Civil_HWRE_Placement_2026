## 4. Universal Quantitative Shortcuts & Formula Sheet

### 4.1 Percentage & Price Multipliers
- **Base Restoration Formula:** If $A$ is $x\%$ greater than $B$, then $B$ is less than $A$ by:
  $$\text{Deficit \%} = \left(\frac{x}{100 + x}\right) \times 100\%$$
- **Commercial Pricing Master Identity:**
  $$\frac{\text{Marked Price}}{\text{Cost Price}} = \frac{100 + \text{Profit \%}}{100 - \text{Discount \%}}$$
- **Equal SP Theorem:** When two items sell at equal $\text{SP}$, one at $+x\%$ and other at $-x\%$:
  $$\text{Overall Net Loss \%} = \left(\frac{x}{10}\right)^2\% = \frac{x^2}{100}\%$$

### 4.2 Interest & Capital Valuation
- **2-Year CI - SI Difference:** $\Delta_2 = P \left(\frac{R}{100}\right)^2$.
- **3-Year to 2-Year Difference Ratio:** $\frac{\Delta_3}{\Delta_2} = 3 + \frac{R}{100} \implies R = \left(\frac{\Delta_3}{\Delta_2} - 3\right) \times 100\%$.
- **Equal Annual Installments (CI, 2 Years):** $X = P \cdot \frac{(1+r)^2}{2+r}$.
- **Growing Perpetuity Present Value:** $\text{PV} = \frac{C_1}{r - g}$.

### 4.3 Variation, Ratio & Alligation
- **Alligation Ratio:** $\frac{\text{Cheaper Quantity}}{\text{Dearer Quantity}} = \frac{\text{Dearer Rate} - \text{Mean Rate}}{\text{Mean Rate} - \text{Cheaper Rate}}$.
- **Repeated Dilution Law:** $\text{Residual Volume} = V \left(1 - \frac{x}{V}\right)^n$.
- **Broken Gemstone Value Loss:** $\Delta V = 2k \sum_{i<j} w_i w_j$.
- **Active Partner Payout:** $\text{Total} = \text{Salary} + \left(\frac{C_A T_A}{\sum C_i T_i}\right) (\text{Gross Profit} - \text{Salary})$.

## 5. Strategic 60-Second Placement Heuristics

1. **Denominators are Bases:** Whenever calculating percentages, profits, or alligations, ask: *What physical entity is in the denominator?* (CP for profit, SP for margin, Time for speed, Dispensed weight for cheating merchant).
2. **Never Add Successive Percentages:** Always multiply decimal multipliers ($+20\%$ then $+10\% \implies 1.20 \times 1.10 = 1.38 \implies +38\%$, never $30\%$).
3. **Cancel Factors Before Multiplying:** In joint variation and multi-partner time integrals, factor out common GCDs immediately to avoid multi-digit arithmetic.
4. **Use Extreme Value Sanity Checks:** If mixing a $20\%$ and $40\%$ solution, the blend concentration MUST lie strictly in $(20\%, 40\%)$. Any option outside this interval is an instant elimination.
