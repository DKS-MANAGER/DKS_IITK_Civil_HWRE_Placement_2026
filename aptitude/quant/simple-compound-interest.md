# Simple & Compound Interest

> **Priority:** P1 · **Role relevance:** Important (all roles), Critical (Finance)
> **Difficulty range:** Easy → Very Hard · **Time per question:** 30 sec – 3 min

---

## 1. What You Need to Know

1. Simple Interest (SI) formula
2. Compound Interest (CI) formula
3. Difference between CI and SI
4. Compounding periods (half-yearly, quarterly)
5. Effective rate

---

## 2. Formula Sheet

| Concept | Formula |
|:--------|:--------|
| SI | `P × R × T / 100` |
| Amount (SI) | `P + SI` |
| CI Amount | `P(1 + r/100)^t` |
| CI = Amount - P | |
| CI - SI (2 years) | `P(r/100)²` |
| CI - SI (3 years) | `P(r/100)²(3 + r/100)` |
| Effective annual rate | `(1 + r/n)^n - 1` × 100 |

---

## 3. Standard Methods

1. **For SI** — use direct formula.
2. **For CI** — compute year by year or use the formula.
3. **For CI-SI difference** — use the shortcut for 2 or 3 years.
4. **For fractional years** — compute first full year, then partial.

---

## 4. Fast Methods / Shortcuts

### Shortcut 1: CI - SI for 2 years
**When useful:** Find difference between CI and SI for 2 years.
**Why it works:** CI = P(1+r/100)² - P = Pr/100 + Pr²/10000. SI = Pr/100. Difference = Pr²/10000 = P(r/100)².
**Example:** P=5000, r=10%. CI-SI = 5000(0.1)² = 5000×0.01 = 50.
**When NOT to use:** For periods other than 2 years.

### Shortcut 2: CI - SI for 3 years
**When useful:** Find difference for 3 years.
**Why it works:** CI - SI = P(r/100)²(3 + r/100).
**Example:** P=5000, r=10%. CI-SI = 5000(0.01)(3.1) = 50×3.1 = 155.
**When NOT to use:** For periods other than 3 years.

## 5. Worked Examples

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** SI on Rs. 5000 at 10% for 3 years?
**Solution:** SI = 5000×10×3/100 = Rs. 1500. **Answer:** Rs. 1500.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** CI on Rs. 5000 at 10% for 2 years?
**Solution:** Amount = 5000(1.1)² = 5000×1.21 = 6050. CI = 6050-5000 = Rs. 1050.
**Answer:** Rs. 1050.

### Q3 (Medium)
**Q:** CI - SI for 2 years on Rs. 8000 at 5%?
**Solution:** P(r/100)² = 8000(0.05)² = 8000×0.0025 = 20.
**Answer:** Rs. 20.

### Q4 (Hard)
**Q:** At what rate does Rs. 5000 become Rs. 6050 in 2 years (CI)?
**Solution:** 6050 = 5000(1+r/100)². (1+r/100)² = 1.21. 1+r/100 = 1.1. r = 10%.
**Answer:** 10%.

### Q5 (Very Hard)
**Q:** A sum triples in 2 years at CI. Find the rate.
**Solution:** 3P = P(1+r/100)². (1+r/100)² = 3. 1+r/100 = √3 = 1.732. r = 73.2%.
**Answer:** 73.2%.

## 7. Placement Questions

### Q1 (Easy, 30 sec)
**Q:** SI on Rs. 10000 at 8% for 3 years?
- A) 2400  B) 2200  C) 2600  D) 2800
**Answer:** A) 2400. `10000×8×3/100 = 2400`.

### Q2 (Medium, 90 sec)
**Q:** CI on Rs. 5000 at 10% for 2 years is:
- A) 1000  B) 1050  C) 1100  D) 900
**Answer:** B) 1050. `5000(1.1²-1) = 5000×0.21 = 1050`.

### Q3 (Hard, 2 min)
**Q:** The compound interest on a sum for 2 years is Rs. 1050 and simple interest is Rs. 1000. Find the rate.
- A) 10%  B) 5%  C) 8%  D) 12%
**Answer:** A) 10%. CI-SI = 50 = P(r/100)². SI = PrT/100 = 1000. P = 1000×100/(r×2). Substitute: 50 = (1000×100/(r×2))×(r²/10000). 50 = 1000r/200 = 5r. r = 10%.

## 8. Rapid Revision

- **Must-know:** SI = PRT/100; CI = P(1+r/100)^t
- **Shortcut:** CI-SI (2yr) = P(r/100)²
- **Trap:** CI-SI difference grows with time
- **Common Q:** Find rate, find principal, CI vs SI comparison, depreciation
