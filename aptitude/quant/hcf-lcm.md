## 2. Formula Sheet

| Concept | Formula |
|:--------|:--------|
| HCF × LCM | `= a × b` (for two numbers) |
| HCF of fractions | `HCF of numerators / LCM of denominators` |
| LCM of fractions | `LCM of numerators / HCF of denominators` |

### Divisibility Rules
| Divisor | Rule |
|:--------|:-----|
| 2 | Last digit even |
| 3 | Sum of digits divisible by 3 |
| 4 | Last 2 digits divisible by 4 |
| 5 | Last digit 0 or 5 |
| 6 | Divisible by 2 and 3 |
| 8 | Last 3 digits divisible by 8 |
| 9 | Sum of digits divisible by 9 |
| 11 | Alternating sum = 0 or ±11 |

## 3. Worked Examples

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** HCF of 12 and 18?
**Solution:** Factors of 12: 1,2,3,4,6,12. Factors of 18: 1,2,3,6,9,18. Common: 1,2,3,6. HCF = 6. **Answer:** 6.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** LCM of 12 and 18?
**Solution:** 12 = 2²×3. 18 = 2×3². LCM = 2²×3² = 36. **Answer:** 36.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** HCF of two numbers is 12 and LCM is 720. If one number is 60, find the other.
**Solution:** 12 × 720 = 60 × x. x = 8640/60 = 144. **Answer:** 144.

## 5. Placement Questions

- A) 6  B) 3  C) 9  D) 12
**Answer:** A) 6.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** LCM of 12 and 18?
- A) 36  B) 24  C) 54  D) 72
**Answer:** A) 36.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** HCF of two numbers is 12 and LCM is 720. If one is 60, the other is:
- A) 144  B) 120  C) 132  D) 156
**Answer:** A) 144. 12×720/60 = 144.
