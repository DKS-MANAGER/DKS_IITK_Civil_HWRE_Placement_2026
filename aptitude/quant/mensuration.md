## 3. Worked Examples

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** Area of a square with side 5 cm?
**Solution:** 5² = 25 cm². **Answer:** 25 cm².

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** Volume of a cylinder with r=3, h=5?
**Solution:** π×9×5 = 45π. **Answer:** 45π.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** A cube has surface area 216 cm². Find its volume.
**Solution:** 6s² = 216. s² = 36. s = 6. Volume = 6³ = 216 cm³. **Answer:** 216 cm³.
