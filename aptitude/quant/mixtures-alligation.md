## 2. Formula Sheet

| Concept | Formula |
|:--------|:--------|
| Alligation | `(Cheaper qty)/(Dearer qty) = (Mean - Cheaper)/(Dearer - Mean)` |
| Repeated dilution | `Final = Initial(1 - x/V)^n` |
| Mean price | `(C₁Q₁ + C₂Q₂)/(Q₁+Q₂)` |

## 3. Worked Examples

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** Mix 20% and 40% solutions to get 30%. Ratio?
**Solution:** Alligation: (40-30):(30-20) = 10:10 = 1:1. **Answer:** 1:1.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** A vessel has 40 L milk. 4 L removed and replaced with water, repeated 3 times. Final milk?
**Solution:** 40(1 - 4/40)³ = 40(0.9)³ = 40×0.729 = 29.16 L. **Answer:** 29.16 L.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** A mixture contains milk and water in ratio 4:1. How much must be replaced by water to make 1:1?
**Solution:** Let total 5 units, milk 4. After replacement milk = 2.5. Milk removed = 1.5. Fraction = 1.5/4 = 3/8. **Answer:** 3/8.

## 5. Placement Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** Mix 20% and 40% solutions to get 30%. The ratio is:
- A) 1:1  B) 2:1  C) 1:2  D) 3:1
**Answer:** A) 1:1. (40-30):(30-20) = 10:10 = 1:1.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** A vessel has 40 L milk. 4 L removed and replaced with water, repeated 3 times. Final milk?
- A) 29.16 L  B) 30 L  C) 28 L  D) 32 L
**Answer:** A) 29.16 L. 40(0.9)³ = 29.16.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** A mixture has milk:water = 4:1. How much must be replaced by water to make 1:1?
- A) 1/4  B) 3/8  C) 1/2  D) 2/5
**Answer:** B) 3/8.

## 6. Rapid Revision

- **Alligation:** (Mean-Cheaper):(Dearer-Mean)
- **Repeated dilution:** Initial(1 - x/V)^n
- **Trap:** Replacement removes mixture, not just milk
