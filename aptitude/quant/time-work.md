# Time & Work

> **Priority:** P0 · **Role relevance:** Critical (all roles)
> **Difficulty range:** Easy → Very Hard · **Time per question:** 30 sec – 3 min

---

## 1. What You Need to Know

1. Work = Rate × Time
2. Efficiency (rate of work)
3. Combined work (A + B)
4. Man-day-hour chain rule
5. Pipes & cisterns (inlet/outlet)

---

## 2. Core Concepts

- **Work** is represented as 1 unit (or 100%).
- **Efficiency** = work done per unit time = `1/Time`.
- **Combined rate** = sum of individual rates.
- **Inlet pipe** = positive work; **outlet pipe** = negative work.

---

## 3. Formula Sheet

| Concept | Formula |
|:--------|:--------|
| Efficiency | `1/Time` |
| A(x) + B(y) together | `xy/(x+y)` |
| A,B,C together | `xyz/(xy+yz+zx)` |
| Man-day-hour | `M₁D₁H₁/W₁ = M₂D₂H₂/W₂` |
| Pipes net rate | `1/x - 1/y` (inlet - outlet) |

---

## 4. Standard Methods

1. **LCM method** — set total work = LCM of all times.
2. **Find efficiencies** = total work / time.
3. **Sum efficiencies** for combined work.
4. **Solve** for time = total work / combined efficiency.

---

## 5. Fast Methods / Shortcuts

### Shortcut 1: LCM Method
**When useful:** Multiple workers with different times.
**Why it works:** Using LCM avoids fractions — work in integer units.
**Example:** A=12 days, B=18 days. LCM=36. A=3/day, B=2/day. Together = 36/5 = 7.2 days.
**When NOT to use:** When work is not a whole unit (e.g., wages).

### Shortcut 2: Efficiency Ratio
**When useful:** Comparing workers' speeds.
**Why it works:** Efficiency is inversely proportional to time.
**Example:** A is 3× as efficient as B → A takes 1/3 the time.
**When NOT to use:** When efficiency changes over time.

---

## 6. Worked Examples

### Example 1 (Easy)
**Q:** A can do work in 12 days, B in 18 days. Together?
**Method:** LCM = 36. **Calculation:** A=3, B=2, total 5. Time = 36/5 = 7.2. **Answer:** 7.2 days.

### Example 2 (Medium)
**Q:** A is thrice as efficient as B and finishes 60 days earlier. Together?
**Method:** Let B take 3x, A take x. **Calculation:** `3x-x=60 ⟹ x=30`. A=30, B=90. Together = `(30×90)/120 = 22.5`. **Answer:** 22.5 days.

### Example 3 (Medium)
**Q:** Pipe A fills in 10 h, B in 15 h. Leak makes it take 2 h more. Leak alone empties in?
**Method:** LCM=30. **Calculation:** A=3, B=2, total 5. Without leak 6h, with leak 8h. Net = 30/8 = 3.75. Leak = 5-3.75 = 1.25. Time = 30/1.25 = 24h. **Answer:** 24 h.

### Example 4 (Hard)
**Q:** A and B together in 12 days, B and C in 15, C and A in 20. All together?
**Method:** Add pairs. **Calculation:** `2(A+B+C) = 1/12+1/15+1/20 = 1/5`. A+B+C = 1/10. Time = 10 days. **Answer:** 10 days.

### Example 5 (Very Hard)
**Q:** 12 men or 18 women reap a field in 14 days. 8 men and 16 women in how many days?
**Method:** 1 man = 1.5 women. **Calculation:** 8 men = 12 women. Total = 28 women. 18 women take 14 days, 28 women take `18×14/28 = 9` days. **Answer:** 9 days.

---

## 7. Common Traps

| Trap | Why it's wrong | Correct approach |
|:-----|:---------------|:-----------------|
| Adding times instead of rates | Rates add, not times | Use 1/time |
| Outlet pipe as positive | Outlet empties | Subtract outlet rate |
| Ignoring "A leaves early" | Work distribution changes | Track who works when |
| Man/woman equivalence | Different efficiencies | Convert to common unit |

## 11. Placement Questions

### Q1 (Easy, 60 sec)
**Q:** A can complete a work in 12 days and B in 18 days. Working together, in how many days?
- A) 7.2  B) 7.5  C) 8  D) 6.5
- **Answer:** A) 7.2
- **Explanation:** `(12×18)/(12+18) = 216/30 = 7.2`.
- **Fast method:** `xy/(x+y)`.
- **Concept:** Combined work. **Difficulty:** Easy. **Time:** 60 sec. **Trap:** Adding 12+18 = 30.

### Q2 (Medium, 90 sec)
**Q:** Pipe A fills a tank in 10 hours and B in 15 hours. A leak makes it take 2 hours more. The leak alone empties the full tank in?
- A) 20 h  B) 24 h  C) 30 h  D) 18 h
- **Answer:** B) 24 h
- **Explanation:** LCM=30. A=3, B=2, total 5. Without leak 6h, with leak 8h. Net=3.75. Leak=1.25. Time=30/1.25=24h.
- **Fast method:** LCM method.
- **Concept:** Pipes & cisterns. **Difficulty:** Medium. **Time:** 90 sec. **Trap:** Forgetting to subtract leak.

### Q3 (Hard, 2 min)
**Q:** A and B together can do a work in 12 days, B and C in 15 days, C and A in 20 days. In how many days can A, B, C together do it?
- A) 10  B) 12  C) 8  D) 15
- **Answer:** A) 10
- **Explanation:** `2(A+B+C) = 1/12+1/15+1/20 = 1/5`. A+B+C = 1/10. Time = 10.
- **Fast method:** Add pair rates, divide by 2.
- **Concept:** Combined rates. **Difficulty:** Hard. **Time:** 2 min. **Trap:** Forgetting to divide by 2.
