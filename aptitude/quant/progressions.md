## 2. Formula Sheet

### Arithmetic Progression (AP)
- nth term: `Tₙ = a + (n-1)d`
- Sum of n terms: `Sₙ = n/2[2a + (n-1)d] = n/2(a + l)`
- Common difference: `d = T₂ - T₁`

### Geometric Progression (GP)
- nth term: `Tₙ = a·r^(n-1)`
- Sum of n terms: `Sₙ = a(rⁿ-1)/(r-1)` (r>1)
- Sum of infinite: `S∞ = a/(1-r)` (|r|<1)

### Harmonic Progression (HP)
- Reciprocals form an AP

## 3. Worked Examples

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** Find the 10th term of AP: 2, 5, 8, ...
**Solution:** a=2, d=3. T₁₀ = 2 + 9×3 = 29. **Answer:** 29.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** Sum of first 20 terms of AP: 5, 8, 11, ...
**Solution:** a=5, d=3. S₂₀ = 20/2[2×5 + 19×3] = 10[10+57] = 670. **Answer:** 670.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** Sum of infinite GP: 1 + 1/3 + 1/9 + ...
**Solution:** a=1, r=1/3. S∞ = 1/(1-1/3) = 1/(2/3) = 3/2. **Answer:** 3/2.
