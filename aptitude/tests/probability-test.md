## Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
A fair die is rolled. P(even number)?
- A) 1/3  B) 1/2  C) 2/3  D) 1/6

### Q2 (Easy)

### Q3 (Easy)

- A) 5/8  B) 3/8  C) 1/2  D) 3/5

### Q4 (Easy)

### Q6 (Medium)

### Q7 (Medium)

- A) 0.2  B) 0.9  C) 0.1  D) 0.5

### Q8 (Medium)

- A) 1/13  B) 4/13  C) 1/4  D) 3/13

### Q9 (Medium)
3 balls are drawn without replacement from 5 red and 3 blue. P(all red)?
- A) 5/14  B) 10/56  C) 5/28  D) 1/8

### Q10 (Medium)

### Q11 (Hard)
Two fair dice are rolled. P(sum ≥ 9)?
- A) 5/18  B) 1/6  C) 1/3  D) 1/9

### Q12 (Hard)
P(A) = 0.6, P(B) = 0.5, P(A ∩ B) = 0.3. P(A | B)?
- A) 0.5  B) 0.3  C) 0.6  D) 0.75

### Q13 (Hard)
A bag has 4 red, 3 green and 2 blue balls. 2 balls are drawn together. P(at least 1 red)?
- A) 5/6  B) 13/18  C) 3/4  D) 4/9

### Q14 (Hard)
P(A) = 0.3, P(B | A) = 0.5. P(A ∩ B)?
- A) 0.6  B) 0.15  C) 0.8  D) 0.2

### Q15 (Hard)
5 people sit in a row at random. P(2 particular people sit together)?
- A) 2/5  B) 1/5  C) 3/5  D) 1/2

### Explanations
1. Even outcomes {2,4,6}: 3/6 = **1/2**. → B
2. One favourable of two equally likely outcomes: **1/2**. → D
3. 5 red of 8: **5/8**. → A
4. 1 − 0.3 = **0.7**. → B
5. Favourable: (5,6),(6,5) → 2/36 = **1/18**. → C
6. {HT, TH} of 4 outcomes: 2/4 = **1/2**. → D
7. Independent: 0.5 × 0.4 = **0.2**. → A
8. Face cards = J,Q,K × 4 suits = 12; 12/52 = **3/13**. → D
9. (5/8)(4/7)(3/6) = 60/336 = **5/28**. → C
10. P(A∩B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A∪B) = 0.4 + 0.5 − 0.7 = **0.2**. → B
11. Sums ≥ 9: 4+3+2+1 = 10 outcomes; 10/36 = **5/18**. → A
12. P(A|B) = P(A∩B)/P(B) = 0.3/0.5 = **0.6**. → C
13. P(no red) = (5/9)(4/8) = 20/72 = 5/18; P(≥1 red) = 1 − 5/18 = **13/18**. → B
14. P(A∩B) = P(A)·P(B|A) = 0.3 × 0.5 = **0.15**. → B
15. Glue the pair: 4!·2!/5! = 48/120 = **2/5**. → A
