# Topic Test: Ratio & Proportion

> **Time:** 15 minutes · **Questions:** 15 · **Difficulty:** Mixed
> **Scoring:** +1 correct, 0 wrong · **Target:** 12+/15 = Strong

---

## Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
Divide 500 in the ratio 2 : 3. The larger share is:
- A) 200  B) 300  C) 250  D) 350

### Q2 (Easy)
If a : b = 3 : 4, find a when b = 20.
- A) 12  B) 16  C) 15  D) 18

### Q3 (Easy)
Simplify 24 : 36.
- A) 2 : 3  B) 3 : 4  C) 4 : 6  D) 6 : 9

### Q4 (Easy)
Mean proportional of 4 and 9?
- A) 6.5  B) 5  C) 7  D) 6

### Q5 (Easy)
If 5 pens cost Rs. 75, the cost of 8 pens?
- A) 120  B) 100  C) 110  D) 130

### Q6 (Medium)
A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5. Find A : B : C.
- A) 2 : 3 : 5  B) 8 : 15 : 12  C) 8 : 12 : 15  D) 4 : 6 : 5

### Q7 (Medium)
A invests Rs. 40000 for 6 months, B invests Rs. 60000 for 4 months. Profit Rs. 36000. A's share?
- A) 18000  B) 20000  C) 16000  D) 15000

### Q8 (Medium)
Two numbers are in ratio 3 : 5. Adding 9 to each makes the ratio 3 : 4. The smaller number?
- A) 6  B) 9  C) 15  D) 12

### Q9 (Medium)
A mixture has milk : water = 5 : 2. In 21 L of mixture, how much milk?
- A) 12 L  B) 10 L  C) 18 L  D) 15 L

### Q10 (Medium)
If x : y = 4 : 7, find (3x + 2y) : (5x − y).
- A) 26 : 13  B) 13 : 26  C) 26 : 9  D) 9 : 26

### Q11 (Hard)
A : B : C = 2 : 3 : 4 and A + B + C = 180. Find B.
- A) 40  B) 50  C) 60  D) 80

### Q12 (Hard)
Incomes of A and B are 5 : 4, expenditures 3 : 2. Each saves Rs. 200. A's income?
- A) 400  B) 500  C) 600  D) 450

### Q13 (Hard)
A 40 L mixture has milk : water = 3 : 1. Water to add to make it 1 : 1?
- A) 10 L  B) 15 L  C) 25 L  D) 20 L

### Q14 (Hard)
Two numbers are in ratio 4 : 5. Subtracting 4 from each makes the ratio 3 : 4. The numbers?
- A) 16, 20  B) 20, 25  C) 12, 15  D) 24, 30

### Q15 (Hard)
A invests Rs. 3000 for 4 months, B Rs. 4000 for 6 months, C Rs. 6000 for 4 months. Profit Rs. 10000. C's share?
- A) 5000  B) 4000  C) 6000  D) 2000

## Answer Key

| Q | Ans | Q | Ans |
|:-:|:---:|:-:|:---:|
| 1 | B | 9 | D |
| 2 | C | 10 | A |
| 3 | A | 11 | C |
| 4 | D | 12 | B |
| 5 | A | 13 | D |
| 6 | C | 14 | A |
| 7 | A | 15 | B |
| 8 | B | | |

### Explanations
1. 3/5 × 500 = **300**. → B
2. a = 3/4 × 20 = **15**. → C
3. 24 : 36 = **2 : 3**. → A
4. √(4 × 9) = **6**. → D
5. 75/5 × 8 = **120**. → A
6. A : B = 8 : 12, B : C = 12 : 15 ⇒ **8 : 12 : 15**. → C
7. Capital × time: 240000 : 240000 = 1 : 1 ⇒ A = 36000/2 = **18000**. → A
8. (3x + 9)/(5x + 9) = 3/4 ⇒ 12x + 36 = 15x + 27 ⇒ x = 3; smaller = 3x = **9**. → B
9. 5/7 × 21 = **15 L**. → D
10. (12 + 14) : (20 − 7) = **26 : 13**. → A
11. B = 3/9 × 180 = **60**. → C
12. 5x − 3y = 200 and 4x − 2y = 200 ⇒ x = 100; A = 5x = **500**. → B
13. Milk 30, water 10; 30/(10 + w) = 1 ⇒ w = **20 L**. → D
14. (4x − 4)/(5x − 4) = 3/4 ⇒ 16x − 16 = 15x − 12 ⇒ x = 4; numbers **16, 20**. → A
15. Capital × time = 12000 : 24000 : 24000 = 1 : 2 : 2 (total 5); C = 2/5 × 10000 = **4000**. → B
