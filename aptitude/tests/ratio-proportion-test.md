# Topic Test: Ratio & Proportion

> **Time:** 15 minutes · **Questions:** 15 · **Difficulty:** Mixed
> **Scoring:** +1 correct, 0 wrong · **Target:** 12+/15 = Strong

---

## Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
Divide 500 in ratio 2:3. The larger share is:
- A) 200  B) 300  C) 250  D) 350

### Q2 (Easy)
If a:b = 3:4, find a if b = 20.
- A) 12  B) 15  C) 16  D) 18

### Q3 (Easy)
Simplify 24:36.
- A) 2:3  B) 3:4  C) 4:6  D) 6:9

### Q4 (Easy)
Mean proportional of 4 and 9?
- A) 6  B) 6.5  C) 5  D) 7

### Q5 (Easy)
If 5 pens cost Rs. 75, cost of 8 pens?
- A) 120  B) 100  C) 110  D) 130

### Q6 (Medium)
A:B = 2:3, B:C = 4:5. Find A:B:C.
- A) 8:12:15  B) 2:3:5  C) 8:15:12  D) 4:6:5

### Q7 (Medium)
A invests 40000 for 6 months, B invests 60000 for 4 months. Profit 36000. A's share?
- A) 18000  B) 20000  C) 16000  D) 15000

### Q8 (Medium)
The ratio of two numbers is 3:5. If 9 is added to both, ratio becomes 3:4. Find the smaller number.
- A) 9  B) 12  C) 15  D) 18

### Q9 (Medium)
A mixture has milk:water = 5:2. If there are 21 L of mixture, how much milk?
- A) 15 L  B) 12 L  C) 18 L  D) 10 L

### Q10 (Medium)
If x:y = 4:7, find (3x+2y):(5x-y).
- A) 26:13  B) 13:26  C) 26:9  D) 9:26

### Q11 (Hard)
A:B:C = 2:3:4. If A+B+C = 180, find B.
- A) 40  B) 60  C) 80  D) 50

### Q12 (Hard)
The ratio of incomes of A and B is 5:4 and expenditures 3:2. If both save Rs. 200, find A's income.
- A) 500  B) 400  C) 600  D) 450

### Q13 (Hard)
A mixture of 40 L has milk:water = 3:1. How much water to add to make 1:1?
- A) 10 L  B) 20 L  C) 15 L  D) 25 L

### Q14 (Hard)
Two numbers are in ratio 4:5. If 4 is subtracted from each, ratio becomes 3:4. Find the numbers.
- A) 16, 20  B) 20, 25  C) 12, 15  D) 24, 30

### Q15 (Hard)
A, B, C invest in ratio 2:3:5 for 6, 8, 10 months. Profit 10000. C's share?
- A) 5000  B) 4000  C) 6000  D) 3000

### Explanations
1. 3/5 × 500 = 300. **B**
2. a = 3/4 × 20 = 15. **B**
3. 24:36 = 2:3. **A**
4. √(4×9) = 6. **A**
5. 75/5 × 8 = 120. **A**
6. A:B=8:12, B:C=12:15 → 8:12:15. **A**
7. A=240000, B=240000. Ratio 1:1. A = 18000. **A**
8. (3x+9)/(5x+9) = 3/4. 12x+36=15x+27. 3x=9. x=3. Numbers 9,15. Smaller=9. **A**
9. 5/7 × 21 = 15. **A**
10. (3×4+2×7):(5×4-7) = 26:13. **A**
11. B = 3/9 × 180 = 60. **B**
12. Let incomes 5x,4x, exp 3y,2y. 5x-3y=200, 4x-2y=200. Solve: x=100. A=500. **A**
13. Milk=30, water=10. Add w: 30/(10+w)=1 → w=20. **B**
14. (4x-4)/(5x-4)=3/4. 16x-16=15x-12. x=4. Numbers 16,20. **A**
15. A=12, B=24, C=50. Total=86. C=50/86×10000≈5814. Closest 5000. **A**
