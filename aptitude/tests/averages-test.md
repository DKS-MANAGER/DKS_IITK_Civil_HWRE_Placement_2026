# Topic Test: Averages & Mixtures

> **Time:** 15 minutes · **Questions:** 15 · **Difficulty:** Mixed
> **Scoring:** +1 correct, 0 wrong · **Target:** 12+/15 = Strong

## Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
Average of 10, 20, 30, 40, 50?
- A) 25  B) 35  C) 30  D) 40

### Q2 (Easy)
Average of the first 10 natural numbers?
- A) 5.5  B) 5  C) 6  D) 4.5

### Q3 (Easy)
Sum of 5 numbers is 100. Average?
- A) 25  B) 20  C) 15  D) 30

### Q4 (Easy)
Average of 3, 5, 7, 9?
- A) 6  B) 5  C) 7  D) 8

### Q5 (Easy)
Average of 4 numbers is 12. Their sum?
- A) 40  B) 44  C) 52  D) 48

### Q6 (Medium)
Average of 8 numbers is 20. The number 12 is removed. New average?
- A) 20  B) 21.14  C) 22  D) 19

### Q7 (Medium)
A batsman averages 40 over 10 innings. Runs in the 11th to raise the average to 42?
- A) 60  B) 58  C) 62  D) 64

### Q8 (Medium)
Average of 5 consecutive even numbers is 20. The largest?
- A) 24  B) 22  C) 26  D) 20

### Q9 (Medium)
30 students average 60; 20 students average 50. Overall average?
- A) 55  B) 57  C) 54  D) 56

### Q10 (Medium)
Average age of 10 people is 25. A 30-year-old leaves. New average?
- A) 25  B) 24.44  C) 24  D) 23

### Q11 (Hard)
Average of 15 numbers is 18. Average of the first 8 is 16 and of the last 8 is 20. The 8th number?
- A) 18  B) 20  C) 16  D) 22

### Q12 (Hard)
A 60 L mixture has milk : water = 2 : 1. Water to add to make it 1 : 1?
- A) 15 L  B) 25 L  C) 20 L  D) 30 L

### Q13 (Hard)
Average weight of 20 students is 50 kg. A 70 kg student joins. New average?
- A) 50.95  B) 51  C) 50  D) 52

### Q14 (Hard)
Boys average 20, girls average 16, class average 18. Ratio of boys to girls?
- A) 2:1  B) 1:2  C) 3:1  D) 1:1

### Q15 (Hard)
A vessel holds 30 L milk. 3 L is removed and replaced with water — done twice. Final milk?
- A) 25 L  B) 24.3 L  C) 24 L  D) 23 L

## Answer Key

| Q | Ans | Q | Ans |
|:-:|:---:|:-:|:---:|
| 1 | C | 9 | D |
| 2 | A | 10 | B |
| 3 | B | 11 | A |
| 4 | A | 12 | C |
| 5 | D | 13 | A |
| 6 | B | 14 | D |
| 7 | C | 15 | B |
| 8 | A | | |

### Explanations
1. 150 / 5 = **30**. → C
2. (1 + 10)/2 = **5.5**. → A
3. 100 / 5 = **20**. → B
4. 24 / 4 = **6**. → A
5. 12 × 4 = **48**. → D
6. (160 − 12) / 7 = **21.14**. → B
7. 11 × 42 − 10 × 40 = 462 − 400 = **62**. → C
8. Numbers 16, 18, 20, 22, 24 ⇒ largest **24**. → A
9. (30·60 + 20·50) / 50 = 2800 / 50 = **56**. → D
10. (250 − 30) / 9 = **24.44**. → B
11. 8·16 + 8·20 − 15·18 = 128 + 160 − 270 = **18**. → A
12. Milk 40, water 20; 40 / (20 + w) = 1 ⇒ w = **20 L**. → C
13. (1000 + 70) / 21 = **50.95**. → A
14. Equidistant averages (20 and 16 about 18) ⇒ **1 : 1**. → D
15. 30 × (1 − 3/30)² = 30 × 0.81 = **24.3 L**. → B
