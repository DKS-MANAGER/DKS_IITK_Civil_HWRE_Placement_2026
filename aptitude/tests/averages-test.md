## Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
Average of 10, 20, 30, 40, 50?
- A) 30  B) 25  C) 35  D) 40

### Q2 (Easy)
Average of first 10 natural numbers?
- A) 5  B) 5.5  C) 6  D) 4.5

### Q3 (Easy)
Sum of 5 numbers is 100. Average?
- A) 20  B) 25  C) 15  D) 30

### Q4 (Easy)
Average of 3, 5, 7, 9?
- A) 6  B) 5  C) 7  D) 8

### Q5 (Easy)
If average of 4 numbers is 12, find the sum.
- A) 48  B) 40  C) 44  D) 52

### Q6 (Medium)
Average of 8 numbers is 20. One number 12 removed. New average?
- A) 21.14  B) 20  C) 22  D) 19

### Q7 (Medium)
A batsman averages 40 in 10 innings. How many runs in 11th to average 42?
- A) 62  B) 60  C) 58  D) 64

### Q8 (Medium)
Average of 5 consecutive even numbers is 20. Find the largest.
- A) 24  B) 22  C) 26  D) 20

### Q9 (Medium)
30 students avg 60, 20 students avg 50. Overall average?
- A) 56  B) 55  C) 57  D) 54

### Q10 (Medium)
Average age of 10 people is 25. A 30-year-old leaves. New average?
- A) 24.44  B) 25  C) 24  D) 23

### Q11 (Hard)
Average of 15 numbers is 18. Average of first 8 is 16, last 8 is 20. 8th number?
- A) 18  B) 20  C) 16  D) 22

### Q12 (Hard)
A mixture of 60 L has milk:water = 2:1. How much water to add to make 1:1?
- A) 20 L  B) 15 L  C) 25 L  D) 30 L

### Q13 (Hard)
Average weight of 20 students is 50. A student of 70 joins. New average?
- A) 50.95  B) 51  C) 50  D) 52

### Q14 (Hard)
In a class, average of boys is 20, girls is 16, overall 18. Ratio of boys to girls?
- A) 1:1  B) 2:1  C) 1:2  D) 3:1

### Q15 (Hard)
A vessel has 30 L milk. 3 L removed and replaced with water, twice. Final milk?
- A) 24.3 L  B) 25 L  C) 24 L  D) 23 L

### Explanations
1. (10+20+30+40+50)/5 = 30. **A**
2. (1+10)/2 = 5.5. **B**
3. 100/5 = 20. **A**
4. (3+5+7+9)/4 = 6. **A**
5. 12×4 = 48. **A**
6. (160-12)/7 = 21.14. **A**
7. 11×42 - 10×40 = 462-400 = 62. **A**
8. Numbers 16,18,20,22,24. Largest 24. **A**
9. (30×60+20×50)/50 = (1800+1000)/50 = 56. **A**
10. (250-30)/9 = 24.44. **A**
11. 8×16+8×20-15×18 = 128+160-270 = 18. **A**
12. Milk=40, water=20. Add w: 40/(20+w)=1 → w=20. **A**
13. (1000+70)/21 = 50.95. **A**
14. 20b+16g = 18(b+g). 2b=2g. b:g=1:1. **A**
15. 30(0.9)² = 24.3. **A**
