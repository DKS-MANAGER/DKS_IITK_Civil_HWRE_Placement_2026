# Topic Test: Verbal Ability

> **Time:** 15 minutes · **Questions:** 15 · **Difficulty:** Mixed
> **Scoring:** +1 correct, 0 wrong · **Target:** 12+/15 = Strong

---

## Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
Choose the correct sentence:
- A) Neither the teacher nor the students was present
- B) Neither the teacher nor the students were present
- C) Neither the teacher nor the students is present
- D) Neither the teacher nor the student were present

### Q2 (Easy)
The word "MITIGATE" is closest in meaning to:
- A) Exacerbate  B) Alleviate  C) Intensify  D) Aggravate

### Q3 (Easy)
Choose the opposite of "AMPLE":
- A) Sufficient  B) Abundant  C) Meager  D) Bountiful

### Q4 (Easy)
Identify the error: The team of engineers **are** working on the project.
- A) The team of engineers  B) are  C) working on  D) the project

### Q5 (Easy)
"Despite his ______ appearance, he is actually very kind."
- A) gentle  B) rugged  C) friendly  D) warm

### Q6 (Medium)
Identify the error: She is **more smarter** than her brother.
- A) She is  B) more smarter  C) than her  D) brother

### Q7 (Medium)
The word "ARDUOUS" is closest in meaning to:
- A) Easy  B) Trivial  C) Strenuous  D) Simple

### Q8 (Medium)
Arrange: 1. The bridge collapsed. 2. Engineers had warned. 3. Warnings were ignored. 4. Investigation ordered.
- A) 1-2-3-4  B) 2-3-1-4  C) 2-1-3-4  D) 3-2-1-4

### Q9 (Medium)
Choose the opposite of "DEFUNCT":
- A) Dead  B) Active  C) Extinct  D) Dormant

### Q10 (Medium)
Identify the error: The number of students **have** increased this year.
- A) The number of students  B) have  C) increased  D) this year

### Q11 (Hard)
The word "PERSPICACIOUS" is closest in meaning to:
- A) Foolish  B) Insightful  C) Lazy  D) Average

### Q12 (Hard)
Identify the error: **Between** you and I, this plan **will not work**.
- A) Between  B) you and I  C) this plan  D) will not work

### Q13 (Hard)
"His ______ refusal to compromise ______ the negotiations."
- A) stubborn / stalled  B) willing / advanced  C) flexible / helped  D) eager / concluded

### Q14 (Hard)
Choose the opposite of "METICULOUS":
- A) Thorough  B) Careful  C) Sloppy  D) Precise

### Q15 (Hard)
Identify the error: Each of the students **were** given a certificate.
- A) Each of the students  B) were  C) given a  D) certificate

## Answer Key

| Q | Ans | Q | Ans |
|:-:|:---:|:-:|:---:|
| 1 | B | 9 | B |
| 2 | B | 10 | B |
| 3 | C | 11 | B |
| 4 | B | 12 | B |
| 5 | B | 13 | A |
| 6 | B | 14 | C |
| 7 | C | 15 | B |
| 8 | B | | |

### Explanations
1. Neither/nor: verb agrees with nearer subject (students=plural). **B**
2. Mitigate = alleviate. **B**
3. Ample = sufficient; opposite = meager. **C**
4. Team is singular → "is". **B**
5. "Despite" signals contrast with kind → rugged. **B**
6. "Smarter" already comparative. **B**
7. Arduous = strenuous. **C**
8. Chronological: warnings(2)→ignored(3)→collapse(1)→investigation(4). **B**
9. Defunct = extinct; opposite = active. **B**
10. "The number" is singular → "has". **B**
11. Perspicacious = insightful. **B**
12. Object of preposition → "me". **B**
13. Stubborn refusal → stalled. **A**
14. Meticulous = careful; opposite = sloppy. **C**
15. "Each" is singular → "was". **B**
