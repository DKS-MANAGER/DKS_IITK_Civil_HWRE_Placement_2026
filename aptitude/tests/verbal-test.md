### Questions 11–15: Reading Comprehension (Quantum Computing & Cryptography)

**Passage:**
Modern asymmetric cryptography—exemplified by RSA and Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC)—relies on the computational intractability of mathematical problems such as prime factorization and the discrete logarithm problem. Classical supercomputers require exponential time to factor large composite integers, ensuring robust security for global telecommunications and financial infrastructure.

However, the advent of fault-tolerant quantum computation threatens to upend this cryptographic paradigm. Shor's algorithm, executing on a sufficiently coherent quantum system, solves discrete logarithms and prime factorization in polynomial time by leveraging quantum superposition and quantum Fourier transforms. Consequently, an adversary equipped with a cryptanalytically relevant quantum computer (CRQC) could retroactively decrypt historical intercepted ciphertexts—a strategy termed "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later."

In response, cryptographers and standard-setting bodies have accelerated the development and standardization of Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC). Unlike quantum key distribution (QKD), which requires specialized physical optical infrastructure, PQC algorithms are deployed on classical hardware and rely on lattice-based cryptography, multivariate polynomials, code-based cryptography, and hash-based signatures, which remain computationally intractable for both classical and quantum computing architectures.

## Step-by-Step Solutions & Distractor Post-Mortem

#### Q1 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(B)**
- **Grammar Rule:**
  - With correlative conjunctions *neither... nor*, the verb must agree in number with the subject closer to it.
  - "Portfolio managers" is plural, requiring the plural auxiliary verb **`were`**.
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - Option (A) uses singular "was".
  - Option (C) incorrectly uses "or" instead of "nor".

#### Q2 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(C) -- `Assertive`**
- **Vocabulary Analysis:**
  - *Obsequious* means obedient or attentive to an excessive or submissive degree.
  - *Assertive* (confident, direct, self-assured) is the direct antonym.
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - Options (A), (B), (D) are synonyms of obsequious.

#### Q3 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(A) -- `Between you and I`**
- **Grammar Rule:**
  - The preposition *between* governs objective pronouns (*you and me*, not *you and I*).
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - "The committee's decision" and "was entirely unwarranted" are grammatically correct.

#### Q4 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(B) -- `sanguine ... glaring`**
- **Contextual Semantics:**
  - "Despite" sets up a stark contrast between positive public assurances (*sanguine* = optimistic) and severe internal failures (*glaring* = obvious/flagrant discrepancies).
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - Option (A) *tentative... negligible* provides no meaningful contrast.

#### Q5 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(B)**
- **Grammar Rule:**
  - Mandative subjunctive clauses following verbs of recommendation, demand, or insistence (*recommend, insist, demand*) use the bare base form of the verb without inflection (*step*, not *steps* or *will step*).
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - Option (A) *steps down* is indicative, violating the mandative subjunctive rule.

#### Q6 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(A) -- `Astute`**
- **Vocabulary Analysis:**
  - *Perspicacious* means having a ready insight into and understanding of things; discerning; *astute*.
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - *Pugnacious* means eager or quick to argue/fight.

#### Q7 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(A) -- `2-4-1-3`**
- **Logical Flow:**
  - (2) Introduces transformer architectures in LLMs.
  - (4) Contrasts transformers with predecessor RNNs.
  - (1) Explains how self-attention layers add historical context ("Furthermore...").
  - (3) Concludes the capability of modeling long-range dependencies ("Consequently...").
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - Option (B) breaks the comparative contrast between transformers and RNNs.

#### Q8 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(A)**
- **Critical Reasoning Analysis:**
  - The argument assumes that lower self-reported stress directly causes higher enterprise productivity.
  - Option (A) attacks this exact assumption by demonstrating that subjective stress reduction does not translate to objective output.
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - Option (C) mentions competitors, which is irrelevant to the internal causal mechanism.

#### Q9 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(B) -- `Verbose`**
- **Vocabulary Analysis:**
  - *Laconic* means using very few words; concise; terse.
  - *Verbose* means using or expressed in more words than are needed (direct antonym).
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - *Terse* and *taciturn* are synonyms.

#### Q10 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(A)**
- **Grammar Rule:**
  - In Option (A), "Having completed the statistical regression" is a participial modifier whose logical subject must immediately follow the comma. The subject following is "the research paper", implying the paper itself completed the regression (dangling participle).
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - Option (B) correctly places "the analyst" immediately after the introductory phrase.

#### Q11 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(B)**
- **Textual Evidence:**
  - Paragraph 2 explicitly states: "Shor's algorithm... solves discrete logarithms and prime factorization in polynomial time".
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - Option (A) confuses Shor's algorithm with physical optical attacks.

#### Q12 Solution
