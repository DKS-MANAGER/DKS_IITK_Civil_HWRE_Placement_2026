### Explanations

2. 10×9×8/6 = 120. **A**
3. 9P4 = 3024. **A**
4. 7! = 5040. **A**
5. 6×5/2 = 15. **A**
6. 8×7×6/6 = 56. **A**
7. 26³ = 17576. **A**
8. (5-1)! = 24. **A**
9. 10P3 = 720. **A**
10. 8! = 40320. **A**
11. Vowels AEI together as unit: 8!×3!/2! = 40320×3 = 120960. **A**
12. Men 4! = 24. Women in 5 gaps: 5P3 = 60. Total = 1440. **A**
13. 10P3 = 720. **A**
14. Last digit 5 (1 way), arrange 4: 4! = 24. **A**
15. C(8+3-1, 3-1) = C(10,2) = 45. **A**
