# Topic Test: Time & Work

> **Time:** 15 minutes · **Questions:** 15 · **Difficulty:** Mixed
> **Scoring:** +1 correct, 0 wrong · **Target:** 12+/15 = Strong

---

## Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
A can do a work in 10 days. A's one-day work (efficiency)?
- A) 10  B) 5  C) 1/10  D) 1/5

### Q2 (Easy)
A = 10 days, B = 15 days. Together?
- A) 6  B) 6.5  C) 7  D) 7.5

### Q3 (Easy)
A pipe fills a tank in 6 h. Its one-hour rate?
- A) 6  B) 1/6  C) 1/3  D) 3

### Q4 (Easy)
5 men do a work in 10 days. 1 man alone?
- A) 10  B) 2  C) 5  D) 50

### Q5 (Easy)
A = 20 days, B = 30 days. Together?
- A) 10  B) 12  C) 15  D) 18

### Q6 (Medium)
A = 12 days, B = 18 days. A leaves 3 days before completion. Total days?
- A) 7  B) 8  C) 9  D) 10

### Q7 (Medium)
Pipe A fills in 8 h, pipe B empties in 12 h. Both open — fill time?
- A) 24 h  B) 20 h  C) 16 h  D) 12 h

### Q8 (Medium)
A is twice as efficient as B. A alone finishes in 20 days. Together?
- A) 15  B) 10  C) 12  D) 13.33

### Q9 (Medium)
10 men do a work in 8 days. Men needed to finish in 5 days?
- A) 16  B) 12  C) 14  D) 18

### Q10 (Medium)
A and B together in 10 days; A alone in 15 days. B alone?
- A) 25  B) 20  C) 30  D) 35

### Q11 (Hard)
A = 10, B = 12, C = 15 days. A leaves after 2 days; B leaves 3 days before the end. Total days?
- A) 8  B) 7  C) 9  D) 10

### Q12 (Hard)
12 men **or** 18 women can reap a field in 14 days. 8 men + 16 women take?
- A) 9  B) 10  C) 8  D) 12

### Q13 (Hard)
Two pipes fill a tank in 24 and 32 min. Pipe B is closed after how many minutes so the tank is full in 18 min?
- A) 6 min  B) 10 min  C) 12 min  D) 8 min

### Q14 (Hard)
A can do a work in 15 days. A works 5 days, then B finishes the rest in 20 days. B alone?
- A) 25  B) 30  C) 35  D) 40

### Q15 (Hard)
Efficiencies of A, B, C are in ratio 2:3:4. Together they finish in 12 days. A alone?
- A) 36  B) 45  C) 54  D) 27

## Answer Key

| Q | Ans | Q | Ans |
|:-:|:---:|:-:|:---:|
| 1 | C | 9 | A |
| 2 | A | 10 | C |
| 3 | B | 11 | B |
| 4 | D | 12 | A |
| 5 | B | 13 | D |
| 6 | C | 14 | B |
| 7 | A | 15 | C |
| 8 | D | | |

### Explanations
1. 1/Time = **1/10**. → C
2. 10·15 / 25 = **6**. → A
3. **1/6** of the tank per hour. → B
4. 5 × 10 = **50 days**. → D
5. 20·30 / 50 = **12**. → B
6. LCM = 36; A = 3/day, B = 2/day. 3(x−3) + 2x = 36 ⇒ 5x = 45 ⇒ x = **9**. → C
7. Net rate = 1/8 − 1/12 = 1/24 ⇒ **24 h**. → A
8. A = 20, B = 40; together = 20·40/60 = **13.33**. → D
9. 10 × 8 / 5 = **16 men**. → A
10. 1/B = 1/10 − 1/15 = 1/30 ⇒ B = **30**. → C
11. LCM = 60; A = 6, B = 5, C = 4. 6·2 + 5(D−3) + 4D = 60 ⇒ 9D = 63 ⇒ D = **7**. → B
12. 1 man = 1.5 women ⇒ 8 men = 12 women; total 28 women; 18·14/28 = **9**. → A
13. LCM = 96; A = 4, B = 3. 4·18 + 3X = 96 ⇒ 3X = 24 ⇒ X = **8 min**. → D
14. A does 5/15 = 1/3; B does 2/3 in 20 days ⇒ B alone = **30**. → B
15. Total work = (2 + 3 + 4)·12 = 108; A alone = 108/2 = **54**. → C
