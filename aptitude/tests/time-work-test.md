## Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
A can do work in 10 days. Efficiency?
- A) 1/10  B) 10  C) 1/5  D) 5

### Q2 (Easy)
A=10 days, B=15 days. Together?
- A) 6  B) 6.5  C) 7  D) 7.5

### Q3 (Easy)
A fills tank in 6 h. Rate?
- A) 1/6  B) 6  C) 1/3  D) 3

### Q4 (Easy)
5 men do work in 10 days. 1 man in how many days?
- A) 50  B) 10  C) 2  D) 5

### Q5 (Easy)
A=20 days, B=30 days. Together?
- A) 12  B) 10  C) 15  D) 18

### Q6 (Medium)
A=12, B=18. A leaves 3 days before completion. Total days?
- A) 8  B) 9  C) 10  D) 7

### Q7 (Medium)
Pipe A fills in 8 h, B empties in 12 h. Both open, fill time?
- A) 24 h  B) 20 h  C) 16 h  D) 12 h

### Q8 (Medium)
A is twice as efficient as B. A finishes in 20 days. Together?
- A) 13.33  B) 15  C) 10  D) 12

### Q9 (Medium)
10 men do work in 8 days. How many men for 5 days?
- A) 16  B) 12  C) 14  D) 18

### Q10 (Medium)
A and B together in 10 days, A alone in 15. B alone?
- A) 30  B) 25  C) 20  D) 35

### Q11 (Hard)
A=10, B=12, C=15. A leaves after 2 days, B leaves 3 days before end. Total days?
- A) 7  B) 8  C) 9  D) 10

### Q12 (Hard)
12 men or 18 women in 14 days. 8 men + 16 women in?
- A) 9  B) 10  C) 8  D) 12

### Q13 (Hard)
Two pipes fill in 24 and 32 min. B closed after how long so tank full in 18 min?
- A) 8 min  B) 6 min  C) 10 min  D) 12 min

### Q14 (Hard)
A can do work in 15 days. Works 5 days, B finishes in 20 days. B alone?
- A) 30  B) 25  C) 35  D) 40

### Q15 (Hard)
A, B, C efficiencies in ratio 2:3:4. Together in 12 days. A alone?
- A) 54  B) 36  C) 27  D) 45

### Explanations
1. Efficiency = 1/10. **A**
2. 10×15/25 = 6. **A**
3. 1/6. **A**
4. 5×10 = 50. **A**
5. 20×30/50 = 12. **A**
6. LCM=36. A=3, B=2. 3(x-3)+2x=36. 5x=45. x=9. **B**
7. Net = 1/8 - 1/12 = 1/24. Time = 24. **A**
8. A=20, B=40. Together = 20×40/60 = 13.33. **A**
9. 10×8/5 = 16. **A**
10. 1/B = 1/10 - 1/15 = 1/30. B=30. **A**
11. LCM=60. A=6,B=5,C=4. 6×2+5(D-3)+4D=60. 12+5D-15+4D=60. 9D=63. D=7. **A**
12. 1 man=1.5 women. 8 men=12 women. Total=28. 18×14/28=9. **A**
13. LCM=96. A=4,B=3. 18×4+3X=96. 3X=24. X=8. **A**
14. A does 1/3. B does 2/3 in 20 days. B full = 30. **A**
15. Total efficiency=9. Work=9×12=108. A alone=108/2=54. **A**
