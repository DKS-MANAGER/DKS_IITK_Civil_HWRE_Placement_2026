## Questions

### Q1 (Easy, 30 sec)
What is 40% of 250?
- A) 100  B) 110  C) 90  D) 120

### Q2 (Easy, 30 sec)
Convert 3/8 to a percentage.
- A) 37.5%  B) 33.3%  C) 40%  D) 45%

### Q3 (Easy, 30 sec)
A number is increased by 20%. The new value is 120. Find the original.
- A) 96  B) 100  C) 105  D) 110

### Q4 (Easy, 30 sec)
What percentage of 200 is 50?
- A) 20%  B) 25%  C) 30%  D) 15%

### Q5 (Easy, 30 sec)
25% of a number is 40. Find the number.
- A) 150  B) 160  C) 170  D) 180

### Q6 (Medium, 60 sec)
A number is increased by 10% and then decreased by 10%. Net change?
- A) 0%  B) 1% increase  C) 1% decrease  D) 2% decrease

### Q7 (Medium, 60 sec)
If the price of rice increases by 25%, by what percentage must consumption decrease to keep expenditure constant?
- A) 20%  B) 25%  C) 15%  D) 30%

### Q8 (Medium, 60 sec)
A's salary is 40% less than B's. By what percentage is B's salary more than A's?
- A) 40%  B) 50%  C) 66.67%  D) 60%

### Q9 (Medium, 60 sec)
In an election, 5% of votes were invalid. The winner got 60% of valid votes and won by 1800 votes. Total votes?
- A) 8000  B) 10000  C) 6000  D) 5000

### Q10 (Medium, 60 sec)
The population of a town increases at 10% per annum. Present population is 20,000. Population after 2 years?
- A) 24,200  B) 24,000  C) 22,000  D) 25,000

### Q11 (Hard, 2 min)
A man spends 75% of his income. His income rises by 20% and expenditure rises by 15%. By what percentage do his savings increase?
- A) 30%  B) 35%  C) 25%  D) 40%

### Q12 (Hard, 2 min)
Due to a 20% reduction in price, a person buys 5 kg more sugar for Rs. 600. Original price per kg?
- A) Rs. 35  B) Rs. 30  C) Rs. 25  D) Rs. 40

### Q13 (Hard, 2 min)
In an exam, 35% failed English, 40% failed Math, 15% failed both. What percentage passed both?
- A) 30%  B) 35%  C) 40%  D) 45%

### Q14 (Hard, 2 min)
A student scores 30% and fails by 15 marks. Another scores 40% and gets 25 marks more than passing. Maximum marks?
- A) 350  B) 400  C) 450  D) 500

### Q15 (Hard, 2 min)
Fresh fruit has 68% water and dry fruit has 20% water. How much dry fruit can be obtained from 100 kg of fresh fruit?
- A) 40 kg  B) 32 kg  C) 36 kg  D) 30 kg

## Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Explanation |
|:--|:-------|:------------|
| 1 | A | 40% of 250 = 0.4×250 = 100 |
| 2 | A | 3/8 = 0.375 = 37.5% |
| 3 | B | Original = 120/1.2 = 100 |
| 4 | B | 50/200 × 100 = 25% |
| 5 | B | 40/0.25 = 160 |
| 6 | C | Net = 10-10+(10×-10)/100 = -1% |
| 7 | A | 25/125 × 100 = 20% |
| 8 | C | 40/(100-40) × 100 = 66.67% |
| 9 | B | 95% valid. Winner 60%, loser 40%. 20% = 1800. Total = 10000 |
| 10 | A | 20000 × (1.1)² = 24,200 |
| 11 | B | Initial: I=100, E=75, S=25. New: I=120, E=86.25, S=33.75. Increase = 8.75/25 = 35% |
| 12 | B | 600/0.8P - 600/P = 5 → P=30 |
| 13 | C | P(E∪M) = 35+40-15 = 60%. Passed = 100-60 = 40% |
| 14 | B | 0.3M+15 = 0.4M-25 → 0.1M=40 → M=400 |
| 15 | A | Pulp = 32% of 100 = 32 kg. Dry fruit pulp = 80%. 32/0.8 = 40 kg |
