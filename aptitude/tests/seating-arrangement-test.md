# Topic Test: Seating Arrangement

> **Time:** 15 minutes · **Questions:** 10 · **Difficulty:** Mixed
> **Scoring:** +1 correct, 0 wrong · **Target:** 8+/10 = Strong

---

## Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
A, B, C, D, E sit in a row. A is at the extreme left. B is second from the left. C is immediately to the right of B. E is at the extreme right. Who is in the middle (3rd position)?
- A) B  B) C  C) D  D) E

### Q2 (Easy)
P, Q, R, S sit around a circular table facing the centre. P is opposite R. Q is immediately to the left of P. Who is immediately to the right of P?
- A) Q  B) R  C) S  D) Cannot be determined

### Q3 (Easy)
Six people sit in a row. A is at the left end and B is at the right end. C is second from the left. D is immediately to the right of C. Who is third from the left?
- A) C  B) D  C) E  D) Cannot be determined

### Q4 (Medium)
Eight people sit in a row. A is 3rd from the left and B is 4th from the right. How many people sit between A and B?
- A) 0  B) 1  C) 2  D) 3

### Q5 (Medium)
Six people sit around a circular table facing the centre. C is opposite D. A is immediately to the right of C. B is immediately to the left of C. E is opposite A. Who is opposite B?
- A) C  B) D  C) E  D) F

### Q6 (Medium)
Five people P, Q, R, S, T sit in a row. T is left of P, P is left of Q, Q is left of R, and S is to the right of R. What is the order from left to right?
- A) T, P, Q, R, S  B) P, T, Q, R, S  C) T, P, R, Q, S  D) Cannot be determined

### Q7 (Hard)
Seven people A–G sit in a row facing north. D is exactly in the middle (4th). A is 2nd from the left. B is immediately to the right of A. C is at the far right end. F is immediately to the right of D. G is at the left end. Who is 6th from the left?
- A) C  B) E  C) F  D) G

### Q8 (Hard)
Eight people sit around a circular table facing the centre, evenly spaced. A is opposite C. B is 2nd to the right of A. D is 2nd to the left of A. Who is opposite B?
- A) A  B) C  C) D  D) E

### Q9 (Hard)
Six people sit in two rows of three, facing each other. Row 1 (left to right, from their own view): P, Q, R. Row 2 sits opposite Row 1. Q sits opposite Y. P sits opposite Z. Who sits opposite R?
- A) X  B) Y  C) Z  D) Cannot be determined

### Q10 (Hard)
Eight people A–H sit around a circular table facing the centre. A is opposite D. B is immediately to the left of A. C is immediately to the right of A. G is immediately to the left of D. H is immediately to the right of D. Who is opposite B?
- A) C  B) G  C) H  D) E

## Answer Key

| Q | Ans |
|:-:|:---:|
| 1 | B |
| 2 | C |
| 3 | B |
| 4 | B |
| 5 | D |
| 6 | A |
| 7 | B |
| 8 | C |
| 9 | A |
| 10 | C |

### Explanations
1. A = 1, B = 2, C = 3, E = 5 ⇒ D = 4. Middle (3rd) = **C**. → B
2. In a circle of 4, P and R are opposite; Q is on one side of P, so the fourth person **S** takes the other side. → C
3. A = 1 (left end), C = 2, D = 3 ⇒ 3rd from left = **D**. → B
4. B's position from the left = 8 + 1 − 4 = 5; between positions 3 and 5 there is **1** person (position 4). → B
5. Around C: B (left), C, A (right) are consecutive. D is opposite C, E is opposite A. The remaining person **F** is opposite B. → D
6. T < P < Q < R and S is right of R ⇒ single order **T, P, Q, R, S**. → A
7. G = 1, A = 2, B = 3, D = 4, F = 5, C = 7 ⇒ position 6 is the only one left, taken by **E**. → B
8. Opposite is +4 seats. B is at A + 2, so opposite B is at A + 6 = A − 2 = **D**'s seat. → C
9. The rows face each other, so the left–right order is mirrored: P ↔ Z and Q ↔ Y force R ↔ **X**. → A
10. B is immediately left of A; the seat opposite it is immediately right of D, i.e. **H**. → C
