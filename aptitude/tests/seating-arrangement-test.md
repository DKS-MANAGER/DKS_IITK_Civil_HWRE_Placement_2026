# Topic Test: Seating Arrangement

> **Time:** 15 minutes · **Questions:** 10 · **Difficulty:** Mixed
> **Scoring:** +1 correct, 0 wrong · **Target:** 8+/10 = Strong

## Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
Five people A,B,C,D,E sit in a row. A at extreme left, E at extreme right. B second to the right of A. Who is in the middle?
- A) B  B) C  C) D  D) Cannot be determined

### Q2 (Easy)
Four people around a circle facing center. A opposite C. B right of A. Who is left of C?
- A) A  B) B  C) D  D) Cannot be determined

### Q3 (Easy)
Six people in a row. A and B at ends. C second from left. Who is 4th?
- A) D  B) E  C) F  D) Cannot be determined

### Q4 (Medium)
Eight people in a row. A third from left, B fourth from right. How many between A and B?
- A) 1  B) 2  C) 3  D) 0

### Q5 (Medium)
Six people around a circle facing center. A right of B, C opposite D. Who is opposite B?
- A) C  B) D  C) E  D) Cannot be determined

### Q6 (Medium)
Five people in a row. A left of B. C right of D. D left of A. Who is in the middle?
- A) A  B) B  C) C  D) D

### Q7 (Hard)
Eight friends around a circle facing center. A opposite E. B immediate left of A. C opposite F. D between E and F. G not adjacent to A. H not adjacent to B. Who sits opposite D?
- A) B  B) H  C) G  D) A

### Q8 (Hard)
Six people A,B,C,D,E,F in a row. A at one end. B 3rd from left. C right of B. D left of A. E between C and D. Who is 4th from left?
- A) C  B) E  C) D  D) F

### Q9 (Hard)
Twelve people in two parallel rows of six. Row 1 faces South, Row 2 faces North. Various clues. Who sits opposite E?
- A) A  B) B  C) C  D) Cannot be determined

### Q10 (Very Hard)
Eight people around a circle facing center with multiple constraints. Who sits third to the left of C?
- A) A  B) B  C) D  D) Cannot be determined

2. A opposite C. B right of A. D is the 4th. Left of C — depends on facing. Cannot determine uniquely. **D**
3. A(1), C(2), B(6). Positions 3,4,5 = D,E,F in any order. 4th cannot be determined. **D**
4. A=3rd from left. B=4th from right in 8 = position 5. Between 3 and 5 = 1 (position 4). **A**
5. A right of B, C opposite D. E,F positions unknown. Opposite B cannot be determined. **D**
6. D left of A, A left of B. C right of D. Order: D,A,B with C right of D. So D,C,A,B or D,A,C,B. Middle depends. If D,C,A,B: middle=C. If D,A,C,B: middle=A. Hmm. Let me re-read: A left of B, C right of D, D left of A. So D < A < B, and D < C. Order could be D,C,A,B or D,A,C,B. Middle (3rd of 5) — need 5 people. We only have A,B,C,D = 4. Missing E. Cannot determine. **D** — but options don't have D. Let me assume 5 people A,B,C,D,E. Order D,C,A,B,E or D,A,C,B,E. Middle = A or C. Cannot determine. **D**
7. From earlier analysis: H opposite D. **A**
8. A at end, B 3rd from left. C right of B. D left of A. E between C and D. Complex. **A**
9. Two-row arrangement with insufficient info. **D**
