  $$2(-4) (A^{-1})^2 = -8 (A^{-1})^2$$

  $$\det(c M) = c^n \det(M) \implies \det(-8 (A^{-1})^2) = (-8)^3 \det(A^{-1})^2$$

  $$\det(2 A^{-1} \text{adj}(A)) = 8 \times \left(-\frac{1}{4}\right) \times 16 = 8 \times (-4) = -32$$

### Q15 — Solution: (C) $\kappa(A_\epsilon)$ asymptotically diverges as $\mathcal{O}(1/\epsilon)$
- **Mathematical Derivation:**  
  Determinant of $A_\epsilon$:
  $$\det(A_\epsilon) = 1(1 + \epsilon) - 1(1) = \epsilon$$
  Inverse matrix:
  $$A_\epsilon^{-1} = \frac{1}{\epsilon} \begin{pmatrix} 1 + \epsilon & -1 \\ -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$$
  For small $\epsilon$, $\|A_\epsilon\|_2 \approx 2$, while $\|A_\epsilon^{-1}\|_2 \approx \frac{2}{\epsilon}$.  
  Therefore, the condition number $\kappa(A_\epsilon) = \|A_\epsilon\|_2 \|A_\epsilon^{-1}\|_2 \approx \frac{4}{\epsilon} \to \infty$ as $\epsilon \to 0$.  
  The system becomes severely ill-conditioned, magnifying roundoff errors by a factor of $1/\epsilon$.
- **Correct Choice:** (C)
