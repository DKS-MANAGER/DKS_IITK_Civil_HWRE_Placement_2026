## Step-by-Step Solutions & Distractor Post-Mortem

#### Q1 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(C) -- `100`**
- **Mathematical Proof:**
  - Differences between terms:
    - $10 - 7 = 3$.
    - $16 - 10 = 6 = 3 \times 2$.
    - $28 - 16 = 12 = 6 \times 2$.
    - $52 - 28 = 24 = 12 \times 2$.
    - Next difference $= 24 \times 2 = 48$.
  - Next term $= 52 + 48 = 100$.
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - Option (B) $96$ calculates $52 + 44$.

#### Q2 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(C) -- `217`**

  - $1^3 + 1 = 2$.
  - $2^3 + 1 = 9$.
  - $3^3 + 1 = 28$.
  - $4^3 + 1 = 65$.
  - $5^3 + 1 = 126$.

  - Option (B) $215$ is $6^3 - 1$.

  - 2nd Letter: $F (6) \xrightarrow{+3} I (9) \xrightarrow{+3} L (12) \xrightarrow{+3} O (15) \xrightarrow{+3} R (18)$.
  - Next term is **`O15R`**.

  - Incorrect term is $72$.

  - Option (B) $35$ is correct in the sequence.
