## DI SET: Sales Data (Table)

**Study the following table and answer Q1–Q5.**

Sales (in ₹ lakhs) of five products across four quarters:

| Product | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 |
|:--------|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 120 | 150 | 180 | 200 |
| B | 80 | 90 | 110 | 130 |
| C | 200 | 180 | 160 | 150 |
| D | 60 | 75 | 90 | 110 |
| E | 140 | 130 | 120 | 100 |

### Q1 (Easy)
Which product had the highest total sales across all quarters?
- A) A  B) B  C) C  D) D

### Q2 (Medium)
What is the percentage increase in Product A sales from Q1 to Q4?
- A) 50%  B) 66.67%  C) 75%  D) 80%

### Q3 (Medium)
In which quarter did total sales across all products reach the highest?
- A) Q1  B) Q2  C) Q3  D) Q4

### Q4 (Hard)
What is the ratio of Product A's Q4 sales to Product B's Q4 sales?
- A) 15:13  B) 3:2  C) 20:13  D) 2:1

### Q5 (Hard)
Which product showed a consistent decline across all quarters?
- A) A  B) B  C) C  D) E

## DI SET: Water Distribution (Pie)

**A city's daily water supply of 50 ML is distributed as:**

| Category | % |
|:---------|--:|
| Domestic | 45% |
| Industrial | 20% |
| Agriculture | 15% |
| Commercial | 12% |
| Public Services | 8% |

### Q6 (Easy)
How much water is used for industrial purposes?
- A) 8 ML  B) 10 ML  C) 12 ML  D) 15 ML

### Q7 (Medium)
The angle for Agriculture in a pie chart would be:
- A) 45°  B) 54°  C) 60°  D) 48°

### Q8 (Medium)
What is the ratio of Domestic to Industrial usage?
- A) 9:4  B) 3:1  C) 5:2  D) 2:1

### Q9 (Hard)
If domestic usage is reduced by 20% and redistributed equally to Agriculture and Commercial, new Agriculture percentage?
- A) 18.5%  B) 19.5%  C) 20%  D) 18%

### Q10 (Hard)
If total supply increases to 60 ML and domestic stays at 45%, what is the increase in domestic volume?
- A) 2.5 ML  B) 4.5 ML  C) 5 ML  D) 3 ML

## Answer Key

| Q | Ans | Q | Ans |
|:-:|:---:|:-:|:---:|
| 1 | C | 6 | B |
| 2 | B | 7 | B |
| 3 | D | 8 | A |
| 4 | C | 9 | B |
| 5 | C | 10 | B |

### Explanations
1. C total = 690, highest. **C**
2. (200-120)/120 = 66.67%. **B**
3. Q4 total = 690, highest. **D**
4. 200:130 = 20:13. **C**
5. C declines consistently. **C**
6. 20% of 50 = 10. **B**
7. 15% of 360 = 54°. **B**
8. 45:20 = 9:4. **A**
9. 20% of 45 = 9. Each gets 4.5. New = 15+4.5 = 19.5%. **B**
10. New = 45% of 60 = 27. Old = 22.5. Increase = 4.5. **B**
