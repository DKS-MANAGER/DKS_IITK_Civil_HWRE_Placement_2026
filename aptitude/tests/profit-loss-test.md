## Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
CP = 200, SP = 250. Profit%?
- A) 25%  B) 20%  C) 15%  D) 30%

### Q2 (Easy)
CP = 150, SP = 120. Loss%?
- A) 20%  B) 25%  C) 15%  D) 30%

### Q3 (Easy)
CP = 80, profit 25%. SP?
- A) 100  B) 90  C) 110  D) 95

### Q4 (Easy)
SP = 90, loss 10%. CP?
- A) 100  B) 95  C) 110  D) 105

### Q5 (Easy)
MP = 200, discount 20%. SP?
- A) 160  B) 150  C) 170  D) 180

### Q6 (Medium)
Markup 25%, discount 10%. Net profit%?
- A) 12.5%  B) 15%  C) 10%  D) 20%

### Q7 (Medium)
CP = 400, sold at 15% profit. SP?
- A) 460  B) 450  C) 470  D) 440

### Q8 (Medium)
A dealer sells at CP but uses 950 g. Profit%?
- A) 5.26%  B) 5%  C) 6%  D) 4.5%

### Q9 (Medium)
Two successive discounts 20% and 10%. Single equivalent discount?
- A) 28%  B) 30%  C) 25%  D) 32%

### Q10 (Medium)
SP = 120, profit 20%. CP?
- A) 100  B) 96  C) 110  D) 90

### Q11 (Hard)
A man sells two items at Rs. 800 each, one at 20% profit, other at 20% loss. Net?
- A) Rs. 66.67 loss  B) Rs. 66.67 gain  C) No gain/loss  D) Rs. 50 loss

### Q12 (Hard)
A shopkeeper marks up 30% and gives 10% discount. Profit%?
- A) 17%  B) 20%  C) 15%  D) 18%

### Q13 (Hard)
A dishonest dealer uses 800 g and sells at 10% profit. Actual profit%?
- A) 37.5%  B) 35%  C) 40%  D) 33.3%

### Q14 (Hard)
By selling an item for Rs. 720, a man loses 10%. At what price to gain 10%?
- A) 880  B) 800  C) 860  D) 900

### Q15 (Hard)
A trader marks up 40% and gives two successive discounts of 10% each. Net profit%?
- A) 13.4%  B) 15%  C) 12%  D) 14%

### Explanations
1. 50/200 × 100 = 25%. **A**
2. 30/150 × 100 = 20%. **A**
3. 80×1.25 = 100. **A**
4. 90/0.9 = 100. **A**
5. 200×0.8 = 160. **A**
6. 25-10-(25×10/100) = 12.5%. **A**
7. 400×1.15 = 460. **A**
8. 50/950 × 100 = 5.26%. **A**
9. 20+10-2 = 28%. **A**
10. 120/1.2 = 100. **A**
11. CP₁=800/1.2=666.67, CP₂=800/0.8=1000. Total CP=1666.67, SP=1600. Loss=66.67. **A**
12. 30-10-3 = 17%. **A**
13. Sells 1000g for 800g price. Cost=800, SP=1000×1.1=1100. Profit=300/800=37.5%. **A**
14. CP=720/0.9=800. SP=800×1.1=880. **A**
15. 40-10-4=26%. Then 26-10-2.6=13.4%. **A**
