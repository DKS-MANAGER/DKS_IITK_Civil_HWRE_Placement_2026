# Topic Test: Profit, Loss & Discount

> **Time:** 15 minutes · **Questions:** 15 · **Difficulty:** Mixed
> **Scoring:** +1 correct, 0 wrong · **Target:** 12+/15 = Strong

---

## Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
CP = 200, SP = 250. Profit%?
- A) 20%  B) 25%  C) 15%  D) 30%

### Q2 (Easy)
CP = 150, SP = 120. Loss%?
- A) 20%  B) 25%  C) 15%  D) 30%

### Q3 (Easy)
CP = 80, profit 25%. SP?
- A) 90  B) 110  C) 100  D) 95

### Q4 (Easy)
SP = 90, loss 10%. CP?
- A) 95  B) 110  C) 105  D) 100

### Q5 (Easy)
MP = 200, discount 20%. SP?
- A) 160  B) 150  C) 170  D) 180

### Q6 (Medium)
Markup 25%, then discount 10%. Net profit%?
- A) 10%  B) 12.5%  C) 15%  D) 20%

### Q7 (Medium)
CP = 400, sold at 15% profit. SP?
- A) 450  B) 470  C) 460  D) 440

### Q8 (Medium)
A dealer sells at CP but uses a 950 g weight for 1 kg. Profit%?
- A) 5%  B) 6%  C) 4.5%  D) 5.26%

### Q9 (Medium)
Two successive discounts of 20% and 10%. Single equivalent discount?
- A) 28%  B) 30%  C) 25%  D) 32%

### Q10 (Medium)
SP = 120, profit 20%. CP?
- A) 96  B) 100  C) 110  D) 90

### Q11 (Hard)
A man sells two items at Rs. 800 each — one at 20% profit, the other at 20% loss. Net result?
- A) Rs. 66.67 gain  B) No gain / no loss  C) Rs. 66.67 loss  D) Rs. 50 loss

### Q12 (Hard)
A shopkeeper marks up 30% and gives a 10% discount. Profit%?
- A) 20%  B) 15%  C) 18%  D) 17%

### Q13 (Hard)
A dishonest dealer uses an 800 g weight for 1 kg and also sells at 10% profit on his stated cost. Actual profit%?
- A) 37.5%  B) 35%  C) 40%  D) 33.3%

### Q14 (Hard)
Selling an item for Rs. 720 gives a 10% loss. Selling price for a 10% gain?
- A) 800  B) 880  C) 860  D) 900

### Q15 (Hard)
A trader marks up 40% and gives two successive discounts of 10% each. Net profit%?
- A) 15%  B) 12%  C) 13.4%  D) 14%

## Answer Key

| Q | Ans | Q | Ans |
|:-:|:---:|:-:|:---:|
| 1 | B | 9 | A |
| 2 | A | 10 | B |
| 3 | C | 11 | C |
| 4 | D | 12 | D |
| 5 | A | 13 | A |
| 6 | B | 14 | B |
| 7 | C | 15 | C |
| 8 | D | | |

### Explanations
1. (250 − 200)/200 × 100 = **25%**. → B
2. (150 − 120)/150 × 100 = **20%**. → A
3. 80 × 1.25 = **100**. → C
4. 90 / 0.9 = **100**. → D
5. 200 × 0.8 = **160**. → A
6. 1.25 × 0.9 = 1.125 ⇒ **12.5%**. → B
7. 400 × 1.15 = **460**. → C
8. Sells 1000 g worth for 950 g cost: 50/950 × 100 = **5.26%**. → D
9. 20 + 10 − (20 × 10 / 100) = **28%**. → A
10. 120 / 1.2 = **100**. → B
11. Equal SP, equal ±20%: always a loss = (20²/100)% of... CP₁ = 800/1.2 = 666.67, CP₂ = 800/0.8 = 1000; total CP 1666.67, total SP 1600 ⇒ **Rs. 66.67 loss**. → C
12. 1.30 × 0.90 = 1.17 ⇒ **17%**. → D
13. (1000/800) × 1.10 − 1 = 1.375 − 1 = **37.5%**. → A
14. CP = 720 / 0.9 = 800; SP for +10% = 800 × 1.1 = **880**. → B
15. 1.40 × 0.90 × 0.90 = 1.134 ⇒ **13.4%**. → C
