# Problems on Ages

## Concept Definitions

- **Present Age**: Current age, typically the base variable $x$.
- **Past Age ($n$ years ago)**: $x - n$.
- **Future Age ($n$ years hence)**: $x + n$.
- **Age Difference Invariance**: The difference between two people's ages remains constant throughout life.

---

## Key Formulas & Shortcuts

### Linear Formulation
If present age is $x$:
- $n$ years ago: $x - n$
- $n$ years from now: $x + n$

### Ratio Applications
If present ages of $A$ and $B$ are in ratio $a : b$:
- Let present age of $A = ax$
- Let present age of $B = bx$

### Quick Cross-Multiplication
If present ratio is $a : b$ and future/past ratio is $c : d$ after $T$ years:
$$A\text{'s present age} = \frac{a \times T \times (d - c)}{ad - bc}$$
$$B\text{'s present age} = \frac{b \times T \times (d - c)}{ad - bc}$$

## Practice Problems

### Problem 1
Ten years ago, the ratio of a father's age to his son's age was $3 : 1$. Ten years hence, it will be $2 : 1$. Find their present ages.

### Problem 2
The sum of present ages of a mother and daughter is 50. Five years ago, the mother's age was seven times the daughter's age. Find their present ages.

### Problem 3
A man is 24 years older than his son. In two years, his age will be twice his son's age. Find the son's present age.

### Problem 4
The ratio of present ages of two brothers is $1 : 2$. Five years ago it was $1 : 3$. What will be the ratio after 5 years?

### Problem 5
A father is 38 years old. He was as old as his son is now at the time of the son's birth. What was the son's age five years ago?

---

## Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. Let ages 10 years ago be $3x$ and $x$.
2. Present ages: father $= 3x + 10$, son $= x + 10$.
3. Ages 10 years hence: father $= 3x + 20$, son $= x + 20$.
4. Ratio $2 : 1$:
   $$\frac{3x + 20}{x + 20} = \frac{2}{1} \implies 3x + 20 = 2x + 40 \implies x = 20$$
5. Father's present age $= 3(20) + 10 = 70$. Son's present age $= 1(20) + 10 = 30$.
6. **Answer**: Father **70 years**, Son **30 years**.

### Solution 2
1. Let daughter's present age $= d$. Mother's present age $= 50 - d$.
2. Five years ago: mother $= 45 - d$, daughter $= d - 5$.
3. Equation:
   $$45 - d = 7(d - 5) \implies 45 - d = 7d - 35 \implies 8d = 80 \implies d = 10$$
4. Mother $= 50 - 10 = 40$.
5. **Answer**: Mother **40 years**, Daughter **10 years**.

### Solution 3
1. Let son's present age $= s$. Father's present age $= s + 24$.
2. In 2 years: son $= s + 2$, father $= s + 26$.
3. Equation:
   $$s + 26 = 2(s + 2) \implies s + 26 = 2s + 4 \implies s = 22$$
4. **Answer**: Son is **22 years** old.

### Solution 4
1. Let present ages be $x$ and $2x$.
2. Five years ago:
   $$\frac{x - 5}{2x - 5} = \frac{1}{3} \implies 3x - 15 = 2x - 5 \implies x = 10$$
3. Present ages: 10 and 20.
4. After 5 years: 15 and 25. Ratio $= 15 : 25 = 3 : 5$.
5. **Answer**: **$3 : 5$**.

### Solution 5
1. Let son's present age $= x$. At son's birth ($x$ years ago), father's age was $x$.
2. Father's present age $= x + x = 2x$.
3. Given $2x = 38 \implies x = 19$.
4. Son's age 5 years ago $= 19 - 5 = 14$.
5. **Answer**: **14 years**.
