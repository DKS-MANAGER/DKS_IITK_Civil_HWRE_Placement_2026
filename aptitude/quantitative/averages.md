# Averages

## Concept Definitions

- **Average (Arithmetic Mean)**: Sum of values divided by the number of values.
- **Weighted Average**: Average where each value carries a different weight.
- **Alligation**: A rule to find the ratio in which two or more ingredients at given strengths must be mixed to produce a mixture of a desired strength.

---

## Key Formulas & Shortcuts

### Averages
$$\text{Average} = \frac{\text{Sum of Observations}}{\text{Number of Observations}}$$

### Weighted Average
$$\text{Weighted Average} = \frac{w_1 x_1 + w_2 x_2 + \dots + w_k x_k}{w_1 + w_2 + \dots + w_k}$$

### Consecutive Numbers
- The average of an AP is the middle term (odd number of terms) or the average of the two middle terms (even number of terms).
- Also: $\text{Average} = \frac{\text{First Term} + \text{Last Term}}{2}$.

### Rule of Alligation
Let cheaper CP $= C$, dearer CP $= D$, mean CP $= M$:
$$\frac{\text{Quantity of Cheaper}}{\text{Quantity of Dearer}} = \frac{D - M}{M - C}$$

### Repeated Dilution
If a vessel contains $x$ units of pure liquid, $y$ units are drawn off and replaced by water, repeated $n$ times:
$$\text{Pure Liquid remaining} = x \left(1 - \frac{y}{x}\right)^n$$

## Practice Problems

### Problem 1
The average age of 30 students is 15 years. Including the teacher, the average increases by 1 year. Find the teacher's age.

### Problem 2
In what ratio must tea costing Rs. 180/kg be mixed with tea costing Rs. 200/kg to produce a mixture worth Rs. 188/kg?

### Problem 3
A container has 80 liters of pure milk. 8 liters are taken out and replaced with water. This is repeated two more times. How much pure milk remains?

### Problem 4
The average of 11 numbers is 60. The average of the first six is 58 and the last six is 63. Find the sixth number.

### Problem 5
A batsman has an average of $x$ for 11 innings. In the 12th inning he scores 90 and his average rises by 5. Find the new average.

---

## Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. Total age of 30 students $= 30 \times 15 = 450$.
2. Total people $= 31$. New average $= 16$. Total age $= 31 \times 16 = 496$.
3. Teacher's age $= 496 - 450 = 46$.
4. **Answer**: **46 years**.

### Solution 2
1. Cheaper CP $C = 180$, dearer CP $D = 200$, mean CP $M = 188$.
2. Apply alligation:
   $$\frac{\text{Cheaper}}{\text{Dearer}} = \frac{200 - 188}{188 - 180} = \frac{12}{8} = \frac{3}{2}$$
3. **Answer**: **$3 : 2$**.

### Solution 3
1. $x = 80$, $y = 8$, $n = 3$.
2. Apply dilution formula:
   $$\text{Remaining Milk} = 80 \left(1 - \frac{8}{80}\right)^3 = 80 \times (0.9)^3 = 80 \times 0.729 = 58.32$$
3. **Answer**: **58.32 liters**.

### Solution 4
1. Sum of 11 numbers $= 11 \times 60 = 660$.
2. Sum of first 6 $= 6 \times 58 = 348$. Sum of last 6 $= 6 \times 63 = 378$.
3. The sixth number is counted twice in $348 + 378$.
   $$\text{Sixth number} = 726 - 660 = 66$$
4. **Answer**: **66**.

### Solution 5
1. Let average for 11 innings be $x$. Total runs $= 11x$.
2. After 12th inning: total runs $= 11x + 90$. New average $= x + 5$.
   $$11x + 90 = 12(x + 5) \implies 11x + 90 = 12x + 60 \implies x = 30$$
3. New average $= 30 + 5 = 35$.
4. **Answer**: **35**.
