# Averages & Mixtures

## Concept Definitions

- **Average (Arithmetic Mean)**: Sum of values divided by the number of values.
- **Weighted Average**: Average where each value carries a different weight.
- **Alligation**: A rule to find the ratio in which two or more ingredients at given strengths must be mixed to produce a mixture of a desired strength.
- **Mixtures**: Combining two or more substances; alligation applies to cost, concentration, or any measurable property.

---

## Key Formulas & Shortcuts

### Averages
$$\text{Average} = \frac{\text{Sum of Observations}}{\text{Number of Observations}}$$

### Weighted Average
$$\text{Weighted Average} = \frac{w_1 x_1 + w_2 x_2 + \dots + w_k x_k}{w_1 + w_2 + \dots + w_k}$$

### Consecutive Numbers (AP)
- Average of an AP = middle term (odd terms) or average of two middle terms (even terms)
- $\text{Average} = \frac{\text{First Term} + \text{Last Term}}{2}$

### Rule of Alligation
$$\frac{\text{Quantity of Cheaper}}{\text{Quantity of Dearer}} = \frac{D - M}{M - C}$$
Where $C$ = cheaper price, $D$ = dearer price, $M$ = mean price

### Repeated Dilution
$$\text{Pure Liquid remaining} = x \left(1 - \frac{y}{x}\right)^n$$
Where $x$ = initial volume, $y$ = volume drawn each time, $n$ = number of operations

### Shortcut: Average of First n Natural Numbers
$$\text{Average} = \frac{n+1}{2}$$

### Shortcut: Average of Squares/Cubes
- Average of first $n$ squares: $\frac{(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}$
- Average of first $n$ cubes: $\frac{n(n+1)^2}{4}$

## Practice Problems (10 Problems)

### Problem 1
The average age of 30 students is 15 years. Including the teacher, the average increases by 1 year. Find the teacher's age.

### Problem 2
In what ratio must tea costing Rs. 180/kg be mixed with tea costing Rs. 200/kg to produce a mixture worth Rs. 188/kg?

### Problem 3
A container has 80 liters of pure milk. 8 liters are taken out and replaced with water. This is repeated two more times. How much pure milk remains?

### Problem 4
The average of 11 numbers is 60. The average of the first six is 58 and the last six is 63. Find the sixth number.

### Problem 5
A batsman has an average of $x$ for 11 innings. In the 12th inning he scores 90 and his average rises by 5. Find the new average.

### Problem 6
The average weight of 8 persons increases by 2.5 kg when a new person replaces one weighing 65 kg. Find the weight of the new person.

### Problem 7
A mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 7:3. If 10 liters of water is added, the ratio becomes 7:5. Find the initial quantity of milk.

### Problem 8
The average of 5 consecutive even numbers is 42. Find the largest number.

### Problem 9
A vessel contains 40 liters of a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 3:1. How much water must be added to make the ratio 1:1?

### Problem 10
The average marks of 120 students is 35. If the average of passed students is 39 and failed students is 15, find the number of students who passed.

## Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. Total age of 30 students $= 30 \times 15 = 450$.
2. Total people $= 31$. New average $= 16$. Total age $= 31 \times 16 = 496$.
3. Teacher's age $= 496 - 450 = 46$.
4. **Answer**: **46 years**.

### Solution 2
1. Cheaper CP $C = 180$, dearer CP $D = 200$, mean CP $M = 188$.
2. Apply alligation:
   $$\frac{\text{Cheaper}}{\text{Dearer}} = \frac{200 - 188}{188 - 180} = \frac{12}{8} = \frac{3}{2}$$
3. **Answer**: **$3 : 2$**.

### Solution 3
1. $x = 80$, $y = 8$, $n = 3$.
2. Apply dilution formula:
   $$\text{Remaining Milk} = 80 \left(1 - \frac{8}{80}\right)^3 = 80 \times (0.9)^3 = 80 \times 0.729 = 58.32$$
3. **Answer**: **58.32 liters**.

### Solution 4
1. Sum of 11 numbers $= 11 \times 60 = 660$.
2. Sum of first 6 $= 6 \times 58 = 348$. Sum of last 6 $= 6 \times 63 = 378$.
3. The sixth number is counted twice in $348 + 378$.
   $$\text{Sixth number} = 726 - 660 = 66$$
4. **Answer**: **66**.

### Solution 5
1. Let average for 11 innings be $x$. Total runs $= 11x$.
2. After 12th inning: total runs $= 11x + 90$. New average $= x + 5$.
   $$11x + 90 = 12(x + 5) \implies 11x + 90 = 12x + 60 \implies x = 30$$
3. New average $= 30 + 5 = 35$.
4. **Answer**: **35**.

### Solution 6
1. Total weight increase $= 8 \times 2.5 = 20$ kg.
2. New person's weight $= 65 + 20 = 85$ kg.
3. **Answer**: **85 kg**.

### Solution 7
1. Let initial milk $= 7x$, water $= 3x$.
2. After adding 10L water: $\frac{7x}{3x+10} = \frac{7}{5}$
3. $35x = 21x + 70 \implies 14x = 70 \implies x = 5$
4. Initial milk $= 7 \times 5 = 35$ liters.
5. **Answer**: **35 liters**.

### Solution 8
1. 5 consecutive even numbers: $x-4, x-2, x, x+2, x+4$
2. Average $= x = 42$ (middle term)
3. Largest $= x+4 = 46$
4. **Answer**: **46**.

### Solution 9
1. Milk $= 30$ L, Water $= 10$ L (ratio 3:1 in 40L)
2. For 1:1 ratio, water needed $= 30$ L
3. Additional water $= 30 - 10 = 20$ L
4. **Answer**: **20 liters**.

### Solution 10
1. Total marks $= 120 \times 35 = 4200$.
2. Let passed $= p$, failed $= 120-p$.
3. $39p + 15(120-p) = 4200$
4. $39p + 1800 - 15p = 4200 \implies 24p = 2400 \implies p = 100$
5. **Answer**: **100 students passed**.
