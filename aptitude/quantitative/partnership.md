# Partnership

## Concept Definitions

- **Partnership**: When two or more people invest capital in a business.
- **Profit Sharing Ratio**: Profits are divided in the ratio of the product of each partner's capital and the time period for which it was invested.
- **Sleeping Partner**: Invests capital but does not take part in management.
- **Working Partner**: Invests capital and manages the business; may receive a salary or commission in addition to profit share.

---

## Key Formulas & Shortcuts

### Profit Distribution
If partners $A, B, C$ invest capitals $C_A, C_B, C_C$ for time periods $T_A, T_B, T_C$:
$$P_A : P_B : P_C = (C_A \times T_A) : (C_B \times T_B) : (C_C \times T_C)$$

### Same Capital, Different Time
If capitals are equal, profits are divided in the ratio of time periods.

### Same Time, Different Capital
If time periods are equal, profits are divided in the ratio of capitals.

### Withdrawals During the Year
When capitals change during the year, compute profit ratios using equivalent capitals for the respective time periods.

## Practice Problems

### Problem 1
A and B start a business. A invests Rs. 12,000 and B invests Rs. 8,000. At the end of one year, the total profit is Rs. 20,000. Find each person's share.

### Problem 2
P and Q buy a hotel. P invests 6 times what Q invests. Total profit after one year is Rs. 84,000. Find P's share.

### Problem 3
P, Q, and R start a hotel. Q and R invest equally, but P invests only $\frac{1}{5}$ of Q's investment. Total profit is Rs. 22,000. Find Q's share.

### Problem 4
A, B, and C invest capitals in the ratio $5 : 6 : 8$. At the end of the year, their profits are in the ratio $5 : 3 : 12$. Find the ratio of their time periods.

### Problem 5
A and B start a business with Rs. 20,000 and Rs. 30,000 respectively. After 6 months, C joins with Rs. 40,000. At the end of one year, the total profit is Rs. 27,000. Find B's share.

---

## Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. Investment ratio $= 12{,}000 : 8{,}000 = 3 : 2$.
2. Both invested for 1 year, so profit ratio is the same.
3. A's share $= \frac{3}{5} \times 20{,}000 = 12{,}000$.
4. B's share $= \frac{2}{5} \times 20{,}000 = 8{,}000$.
5. **Answer**: A gets **Rs. 12,000**, B gets **Rs. 8,000**.

### Solution 2
1. Let Q's investment $= x$. P's investment $= 6x$.
2. Ratio $= 6 : 1$. Total parts $= 7$.
3. P's share $= \frac{6}{7} \times 84{,}000 = 72{,}000$.
4. **Answer**: **Rs. 72,000**.

### Solution 3
1. Let Q's investment $= x$. R's $= x$. P's $= \frac{x}{5}$.
2. Ratio $= \frac{x}{5} : x : x = 1 : 5 : 5$.
3. Total parts $= 11$. Q's share $= \frac{5}{11} \times 22{,}000 = 10{,}000$.
4. **Answer**: **Rs. 10,000**.

### Solution 4
1. Profit is proportional to Capital $\times$ Time. So $T \propto \frac{P}{C}$.
2. $T_A : T_B : T_C = \frac{5}{5} : \frac{3}{6} : \frac{12}{8} = 1 : \frac{1}{2} : \frac{3}{2}$.
3. Multiply by 2: $2 : 1 : 3$.
4. **Answer**: **$2 : 1 : 3$**.

### Solution 5
1. Equivalent capitals:
   - A: $20{,}000 \times 12$
   - B: $30{,}000 \times 12$
   - C: $40{,}000 \times 6$
2. Ratio $= 240{,}000 : 360{,}000 : 240{,}000 = 2 : 3 : 2$.
3. Total parts $= 7$. B's share $= \frac{3}{7} \times 27{,}000 = 11{,}571.43$.
4. **Answer**: **Rs. 11,571.43**.
