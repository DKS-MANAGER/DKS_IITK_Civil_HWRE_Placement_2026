# Aptitude Shortcuts

This file compiles the most useful time-saving shortcuts across quantitative aptitude topics. Each shortcut preserves technical accuracy while reducing computation steps.

---

## Percentages

1. **Product Stability Rule**: If price increases by $x\%$, consumption must decrease by $\frac{x}{100+x} \times 100\%$ to keep expenditure constant.
2. **Successive Change**: Net change for $a\%$ and $b\%$ is $a + b + \frac{ab}{100}\%$.
3. **Population/Depreciation**: Apply $P(1 \pm \frac{R}{100})^n$ for compound growth/decay.
4. **Fraction Equivalents**: Memorize common conversions:
   - $1\% = \frac{1}{100}$, $10\% = \frac{1}{10}$, $20\% = \frac{1}{5}$, $25\% = \frac{1}{4}$,
   - $33.33\% = \frac{1}{3}$, $50\% = \frac{1}{2}$, $66.66\% = \frac{2}{3}$, $75\% = \frac{3}{4}$.

---

## Ratio & Proportion

1. **Combining Ratios**: To merge $A:B$ and $B:C$, make $B$ common using LCM.
2. **Componendo/Dividendo**: If $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}$, then $\frac{a+b}{a-b} = \frac{c+d}{c-d}$.
3. **Partnership**: Profit ratio $= (\text{Capital} \times \text{Time})$ for each partner.

---

## Profit, Loss & Discount

1. **MP/CP Relation**:
   $$\frac{MP}{CP} = \frac{100 + \text{Profit}\%}{100 - \text{Discount}\%}$$
2. **Successive Discounts**: Equivalent single discount $= d_1 + d_2 - \frac{d_1 d_2}{100}\%$.
3. **Same Price, Equal % Loss/Gain**: Always results in a net loss of $\left(\frac{x}{10}\right)^2\%$.
4. **Dishonest Dealer**:
   $$\text{Gain\%} = \frac{\text{Error}}{\text{True Value} - \text{Error}} \times 100$$

---

## Time & Work

1. **Two-Person Formula**:
   $$\text{Time together} = \frac{xy}{x+y}$$
2. **Three-Person Formula**:
   $$\text{Time together} = \frac{xyz}{xy + yz + zx}$$
3. **LCM Method**: Assign total work as the LCM of individual times to avoid fractions.
4. **Efficiency vs. Time**: If efficiency doubles, time halves.
5. **Pipes**: Net rate $= \frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{y}$; time to fill $= \frac{xy}{y-x}$.

---

## Speed, Time & Distance

1. **Speed Conversion**: km/h $\times \frac{5}{18} =$ m/s.
2. **Average Speed (equal distances)**:
   $$\frac{2xy}{x+y}$$
3. **Relative Speed**: Add for opposite directions, subtract for same direction.
4. **Train Crossing Pole**: $\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Train Length}}{\text{Speed}}$.
5. **Train Crossing Platform**: $\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Train Length} + \text{Platform Length}}{\text{Speed}}$.
6. **Boat Speed**: $u = \frac{d + u_p}{2}$, $v = \frac{d - u_p}{2}$.

---

## Averages & Alligation

1. **Weighted Average Shortcut**:
   $$\text{Weighted Average} = \frac{w_1 x_1 + w_2 x_2}{w_1 + w_2}$$
2. **Alligation Rule**:
   $$\frac{\text{Cheaper Quantity}}{\text{Dearer Quantity}} = \frac{D - M}{M - C}$$
3. **Dilution Formula**:
   $$x \left(1 - \frac{y}{x}\right)^n$$

---

## Number System

1. **Sum of first $n$ numbers**: $\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$.
2. **Sum of squares**: $\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}$.
3. **Sum of cubes**: $\left[\frac{n(n+1)}{2}\right]^2$.
4. **Sum of first $n$ odd numbers**: $n^2$.
5. **Sum of first $n$ even numbers**: $n(n+1)$.
6. **AP Average**: $\frac{\text{First Term} + \text{Last Term}}{2}$.
7. **Total Factors**: If $N = p^a q^b r^c$, total factors $= (a+1)(b+1)(c+1)$.

---

## Permutations & Combinations

1. **Permutation**: $nPr = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!}$ (order matters).
2. **Combination**: $nCr = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$ (order does not matter).
3. **Circular Permutation**: $(n-1)!$ (distinct clockwise/counterclockwise).
4. **Key Properties**: $nC_0 = nC_n = 1$, $nC_1 = n$, $nCr = nC_{n-r}$.

---

## Probability

1. **Basic Probability**: $P(E) = \frac{n(E)}{n(S)}$.
2. **Complement**: $P(E') = 1 - P(E)$.
3. **Addition Theorem**: $P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)$.
4. **Independent Events**: $P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B)$.
5. **Card Reference**: 52 cards, 4 suits, 12 face cards, 4 aces.

---

## Problems on Ages

1. **Present Age**: $x$. $n$ years ago: $x-n$. $n$ years hence: $x+n$.
2. **Age Difference**: Constant throughout life.
3. **Ratio Method**: If present ratio is $a:b$ and future ratio is $c:d$ after $T$ years:
   $$A = \frac{aT(d-c)}{ad-bc}, \quad B = \frac{bT(d-c)}{ad-bc}$$
