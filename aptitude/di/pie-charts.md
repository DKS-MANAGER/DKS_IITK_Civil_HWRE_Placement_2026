# Data Interpretation: Pie Charts

> **Priority:** P0 · **Role relevance:** Critical (all roles)
> **Difficulty range:** Easy → Very Hard · **Time per set:** 5 – 10 min

---

## 1. What You Need to Know

Pie charts show parts of a whole as slices. Key formula: **Value = (Angle/360) × Total** and **Angle = (Value/Total) × 360**.

---

## 2. Key Skills

1. Read percentages or angles from the pie
2. Convert between angle and value
3. Compare slices (ratios, differences)
4. Combine with absolute data when given

---

## DI SET 1: Project Budget Allocation

**A company has a total project budget of ₹600 crore. The pie chart shows the allocation across departments.**

| Department | % Allocation | Amount (₹ crore) | Angle |
|:-----------|:-----------:|------------------:|------:|
| Engineering | 35% | 210 | 126° |
| Procurement | 25% | 150 | 90° |
| HR & Admin | 15% | 90 | 54° |
| Quality Control | 10% | 60 | 36° |
| Research & Development | 10% | 60 | 36° |
| Contingency | 5% | 30 | 18° |

---

### Q1 (Easy, 60 sec)
**How much is allocated to Engineering?**
- A) ₹180 crore  B) ₹210 crore  C) ₹240 crore  D) ₹150 crore

**Answer:** B) ₹210 crore
**Explanation:** 35% of 600 = 0.35 × 600 = 210.

---

### Q2 (Medium, 90 sec)
**The central angle for Procurement is:**
- A) 72°  B) 80°  C) 90°  D) 108°

**Answer:** C) 90°
**Explanation:** 25% of 360 = 0.25 × 360 = 90°.

---

### Q3 (Medium, 90 sec)
**The ratio of Engineering allocation to Contingency is:**
- A) 5:1  B) 6:1  C) 7:1  D) 8:1

**Answer:** C) 7:1
**Explanation:** 210:30 = 7:1.

---

### Q4 (Hard, 2 min)
**If the Engineering budget is increased by 10% and Contingency reduced to compensate, what is the new Contingency percentage?**
- A) 1.5%  B) 2%  C) 2.5%  D) 3%

**Answer:** A) 1.5%
**Explanation:** New Eng = 210 + 21 = 231. Reduction from Contingency = 21. New Contingency = 30 - 21 = 9. % = 9/600 × 100 = 1.5%.

---

### Q5 (Hard, 2 min)
**If the total budget increases to ₹800 crore and department percentages remain the same, what is the new R&D allocation?**
- A) ₹60 crore  B) ₹70 crore  C) ₹80 crore  D) ₹75 crore

**Answer:** C) ₹80 crore
**Explanation:** 10% of 800 = 80.

## DI SET 2: Water Distribution (Pie + Table)

**A city's daily water supply of 50 million liters is distributed as follows:**

| Usage Category | % | Volume (ML) | Angle |
|:---------------|:--|------------:|------:|
| Domestic | 45% | 22.5 | 162° |
| Industrial | 20% | 10.0 | 72° |
| Agriculture | 15% | 7.5 | 54° |
| Commercial | 12% | 6.0 | 43.2° |
| Public Services | 8% | 4.0 | 28.8° |

---

### Q6 (Easy, 60 sec)
**How much water is used for industrial purposes?**
- A) 8 ML  B) 10 ML  C) 12 ML  D) 15 ML

**Answer:** B) 10 ML
**Explanation:** 20% of 50 = 10.

---

### Q7 (Medium, 90 sec)
**The angle for Commercial usage is closest to:**
- A) 40°  B) 43°  C) 45°  D) 48°

**Answer:** B) 43°
**Explanation:** 12% of 360 = 43.2°.

---

### Q8 (Medium, 2 min)
**If domestic usage is reduced by 20% and redistributed equally to Agriculture and Commercial, what is the new Agriculture percentage?**
- A) 18.5%  B) 19.5%  C) 20%  D) 18%

**Answer:** B) 19.5%
**Explanation:** Domestic reduction = 20% of 45% = 9%. Each gets 4.5%. New Agriculture = 15 + 4.5 = 19.5%.

---

### Q9 (Hard, 2 min)
**The ratio of Domestic to Industrial water usage is approximately:**
- A) 9:4  B) 2:1  C) 5:2  D) 3:1

**Answer:** A) 9:4
**Explanation:** 45:20 = 9:4. (Divide both by 5.)

---

### Q10 (Hard, 2 min)
**If the city's total water supply increases to 60 ML and domestic percentage stays at 45%, what is the increase in domestic volume?**
- A) 2.5 ML  B) 4.5 ML  C) 5 ML  D) 3 ML

**Answer:** B) 4.5 ML
**Explanation:** New domestic = 45% of 60 = 27. Old = 22.5. Increase = 27 - 22.5 = 4.5.

---

## 3. Common Traps

| Trap | Correct approach |
|:-----|:-----------------|
| Confusing angle with percentage | Angle = % × 3.6 |
| Using wrong total | Check if total changed |
| Equal angles ≠ equal values | Always compute actual values |
| Ignoring "of what" | Always verify the base |
