# Data Interpretation: Mixed Graphs

> **Priority:** P1 · **Role relevance:** Important (all roles), Critical (Consulting, Analytics, Finance)
> **Difficulty range:** Medium → Very Hard · **Time per set:** 5 – 10 min

---

## 1. What You Need to Know

Mixed graphs combine two or more chart types (e.g., bar + line, table + pie). You must read data from multiple sources and cross-reference.

---

## 2. Key Skills

1. Read multiple chart types simultaneously
2. Cross-reference data between charts
3. Combine absolute and percentage data
4. Handle complex calculations

---

## DI SET 1: Revenue & Profit (Bar + Line)

**The bar graph shows annual revenue (₹ crore) and the line graph shows profit margin (%) for a company over 4 years.**

| Year | Revenue (₹ crore) | Profit Margin (%) |
|:-----|------------------:|------------------:|
| 2021 | 100 | 10% |
| 2022 | 120 | 12% |
| 2023 | 150 | 15% |
| 2024 | 180 | 18% |

---

### Q1 (Easy)
**What was the profit in 2022?**
- A) ₹12 crore  B) ₹14.4 crore  C) ₹10 crore  D) ₹15 crore
**Answer:** B) ₹14.4 crore. Profit = 12% of 120 = 14.4.

### Q2 (Medium)
**What was the percentage increase in revenue from 2021 to 2024?**
- A) 70%  B) 80%  C) 90%  D) 60%
**Answer:** B) 80%. (180-100)/100 = 80%.

### Q3 (Medium)
**In which year did profit grow the most in absolute terms?**
- A) 2022  B) 2023  C) 2024  D) Cannot determine
**Answer:** C) 2024. Profit: 2021=10, 2022=14.4, 2023=22.5, 2024=32.4. Growth: 4.4, 8.1, 9.9. 2024 highest.

### Q4 (Hard)
**If profit margin in 2025 is 20% and revenue grows 10% from 2024, what is the 2025 profit?**
- A) ₹39.6 crore  B) ₹36 crore  C) ₹40 crore  D) ₹43.2 crore
**Answer:** A) ₹39.6 crore. Revenue 2025 = 180×1.1 = 198. Profit = 20% of 198 = 39.6.

### Q5 (Hard)
**The ratio of profit in 2023 to profit in 2021 is:**
- A) 2.25:1  B) 2:1  C) 2.5:1  D) 1.5:1
**Answer:** A) 2.25:1. 22.5:10 = 2.25:1.

## DI SET 2: Sales by Region (Pie + Table)

**A company's total sales of ₹500 crore are distributed by region (pie) and product (table).**

| Region | % |
|:-------|--:|
| North | 30% |
| South | 25% |
| East | 20% |
| West | 15% |
| Central | 10% |

| Product | % of total sales |
|:--------|:----------------|
| A | 40% |
| B | 30% |
| C | 20% |
| D | 10% |

---

### Q6 (Easy)
**What is the sales value in the North region?**
- A) ₹150 crore  B) ₹125 crore  C) ₹100 crore  D) ₹175 crore
**Answer:** A) ₹150 crore. 30% of 500 = 150.

### Q7 (Medium)
**What is the sales value of Product A?**
- A) ₹200 crore  B) ₹150 crore  C) ₹100 crore  D) ₹250 crore
**Answer:** A) ₹200 crore. 40% of 500 = 200.

### Q8 (Medium)
**The angle for the South region in a pie chart is:**
- A) 90°  B) 100°  C) 108°  D) 72°
**Answer:** A) 90°. 25% of 360 = 90.

### Q9 (Hard)
**If Product A is sold only in the North and South regions in the ratio 3:2, what is Product A's sales in the South?**
- A) ₹80 crore  B) ₹120 crore  C) ₹100 crore  D) ₹60 crore
**Answer:** A) ₹80 crore. Product A = 200. Split 3:2 → South = 2/5 × 200 = 80.

### Q10 (Hard)
**If the Central region's sales increase by 20% and total sales stay the same, what is the new Central percentage?**
- A) 12%  B) 11%  C) 10%  D) 13%
**Answer:** A) 12%. Central = 10% of 500 = 50. Increase 20% = 60. New % = 60/500 = 12%.

## 3. Common Traps

| Trap | Correct approach |
|:-----|:-----------------|
| Reading only one chart | Cross-reference both |
| Confusing absolute and percentage | Convert carefully |
| Using wrong base | Check which total applies |
