## 1. Mathematical & Theoretical Framework

### 1.1 The Epistemology of High-Density Advanced Caselets
Advanced DI Caselets represent the pinnacle of quantitative aptitude testing. Common in McKinsey Problem Solving Game (PSG), Bain, BCG, and Tier-1 Quant/PO assessments, these problems present **interconnected multi-dataset environments** (15–25 discrete data points across 3–4 linked schedules).

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                  THE 4-PILLAR MULTI-DATASET LINKAGE ARCHITECTURE                  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Schedule A: Operational Capacity | Production volumes, asset footprints, hours    |
| Schedule B: Unit Rate Economics  | Tariffs, price realizations, hourly wages      |
| Schedule C: Operating Cost Pool  | Raw materials, energy burn, depreciation       |
| Schedule D: Fiscal & Debt Levies | Taxes, interest coupons, cross-currency shifts |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

### 1.2 Quantitative Modeling Formulations

#### 1. End-to-End Value Chain Landed Margin
$$\text{Landed Cost per Unit} = \text{Fabrication Cost} + \text{Packaging} + \text{Freight} \times (1 + \text{Tariff Rate})$$
$$\text{EBITDA} = \text{Volume} \times \left(\text{Selling Price} - \text{Landed Cost}\right) - \text{Fixed Corporate Overhead}$$

#### 2. Capital Asset Pricing & Cash Waterfall
$$\text{Net Operating Income (NOI)} = \text{Gross Revenue} \times (1 - \text{Vacancy}) - \text{Opex}$$
$$\text{Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)} = \frac{\text{NOI}}{\text{Principal Repayment} + \text{Interest Expense}}$$
