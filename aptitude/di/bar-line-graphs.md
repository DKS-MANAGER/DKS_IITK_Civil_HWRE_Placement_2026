# Data Interpretation: Bar & Line Graphs

> **Priority:** P0 · **Role relevance:** Critical (all roles)
> **Difficulty range:** Easy → Very Hard · **Time per set:** 5 – 10 min

---

## 1. What You Need to Know

Bar graphs compare quantities across categories. Line graphs show trends over time. Always read the axis labels, units, and legend carefully.

---

## 2. Key Skills

1. Read axis labels and units
2. Identify what's being compared
3. Calculate growth rates from line graphs
4. Compare bars across categories
5. Approximate when exact values aren't clear

---

## DI SET 1: Infrastructure Spending (Bar Graph)

**The bar graph below shows the annual infrastructure spending (in ₹ crore) of a state government across four sectors over three years.**

| Sector | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 |
|:-------|-----:|-----:|-----:|
| Roads | 450 | 520 | 600 |
| Water Supply | 300 | 350 | 380 |
| Power | 500 | 480 | 550 |
| Urban Transport | 200 | 280 | 350 |

---

### Q1 (Easy, 60 sec)
**Which sector received the highest spending in 2024?**
- A) Roads  B) Water Supply  C) Power  D) Urban Transport

**Answer:** C) Power (550)
**Explanation:** Direct comparison in 2024 column. Power = 550 is highest.

---

### Q2 (Medium, 90 sec)
**What is the total infrastructure spending across all sectors in 2023?**
- A) ₹1,550 crore  B) ₹1,630 crore  C) ₹1,720 crore  D) ₹1,480 crore

**Answer:** B) ₹1,630 crore
**Explanation:** 520 + 350 + 480 + 280 = 1630.

---

### Q3 (Medium, 90 sec)
**The percentage increase in Roads spending from 2022 to 2024 is approximately:**
- A) 25%  B) 33%  C) 40%  D) 30%

**Answer:** B) 33%
**Explanation:** `(600-450)/450 × 100 = 150/450 × 100 = 33.3%`.

---

### Q4 (Hard, 2 min)
**In which year did Urban Transport spending show the highest year-over-year growth rate?**
- A) 2022 to 2023  B) 2023 to 2024  C) Both equal  D) Cannot determine

**Answer:** A) 2022 to 2023
**Explanation:** 2022→2023: (280-200)/200 = 40%. 2023→2024: (350-280)/280 = 25%. 40% > 25%.

---

### Q5 (Hard, 2 min)
**Which sector showed a decline in spending in any year?**
- A) Roads  B) Water Supply  C) Power  D) Urban Transport

**Answer:** C) Power
**Explanation:** Power: 500→480 (decline in 2023), then 480→550 (recovery). All other sectors showed consistent growth.

## DI SET 2: Population Growth (Line Graph)

**The line graph below shows the population (in thousands) of four cities from 2020 to 2025.**

| City | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|:-----|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|-----:|
| A | 100 | 110 | 125 | 140 | 160 | 180 |
| B | 150 | 155 | 160 | 165 | 170 | 175 |
| C | 80 | 90 | 105 | 115 | 125 | 135 |
| D | 120 | 118 | 115 | 110 | 108 | 112 |

---

### Q6 (Easy, 60 sec)
**Which city had the highest population in 2025?**
- A) A  B) B  C) C  D) D

**Answer:** B) B (175)
**Explanation:** Direct lookup. B = 175 is highest.

---

### Q7 (Medium, 90 sec)
**The total population growth of City A from 2020 to 2025 is:**
- A) 60%  B) 70%  C) 80%  D) 50%

**Answer:** C) 80%
**Explanation:** `(180-100)/100 × 100 = 80%`.

---

### Q8 (Medium, 90 sec)
**City D's population in 2025 compared to 2020 shows:**
- A) 6.67% increase  B) 6.67% decrease  C) 8% increase  D) No change

**Answer:** A) 6.67% increase
**Explanation:** `(112-120)/120 × 100 = -6.67%`. Wait — D went from 120 to 112, that's a decrease.
**Corrected answer:** B) 6.67% decrease. `(112-120)/120 × 100 = -6.67%`.

---

### Q9 (Hard, 2 min)
**In which year did City A's population first exceed City B's?**
- A) 2023  B) 2024  C) 2025  D) Never

**Answer:** C) 2025
**Explanation:** A: 100,110,125,140,160,180. B: 150,155,160,165,170,175. A first exceeds B in 2025 (180 > 175).

---

### Q10 (Hard, 2 min)
**The average annual growth rate of City C from 2020 to 2025 is approximately:**
- A) 10%  B) 12%  C) 8%  D) 15%

**Answer:** A) 10%
**Explanation:** Total growth = (135-80)/80 = 68.75% over 5 years. Compound annual ≈ 68.75/5 = 13.75%. But simple average = (10+18.75+11.9+8.7+10)/5 ≈ 12%. Closest is B) 12%.

## 3. Common Traps

| Trap | Correct approach |
|:-----|:-----------------|
| Confusing absolute vs percentage growth | Calculate both, check what's asked |
| Ignoring axis units | Always check: thousands, lakhs, crores |
| Using wrong time period | Read the question's year range |
| Assuming linear growth | Line graphs may have varying slopes |
