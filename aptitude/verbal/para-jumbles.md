# Para Jumbles

> **Priority:** P1 · **Role relevance:** Important (all roles)
> **Difficulty range:** Easy → Hard · **Time per question:** 1 – 2 min

---

## 1. Strategy

1. **Find the opening sentence** — introduces topic, no pronouns referring to previous
2. **Identify links** — pronouns (he, she, it, this, that), conjunctions (however, therefore, moreover)
3. **Look for logical flow** — general to specific, cause to effect, chronological
4. **Check mandatory pairs** — sentences that MUST go together
5. **Verify the sequence** — read through to ensure coherence

---

## 2. Key Linking Devices

| Device | Function |
|:-------|:---------|
| **Pronouns** (he, she, it, they, this, that) | Refer to noun in previous sentence |
| **Conjunctions** (however, therefore, moreover, furthermore) | Show logical relationship |
| **Articles** (a/an vs the) | "A" introduces new; "the" refers to known |
| **Chronological markers** (first, then, finally, initially) | Time sequence |
| **Cause-effect** (because, since, as a result, consequently) | Logical flow |

## 3. Practice Questions (15 Questions)

### Q1 (Easy)
**Arrange in logical order:**
A. The sun rises in the east.
B. It sets in the west.
C. The day begins with sunrise.
D. The cycle repeats daily.

**Answer:** C-A-B-D
**Explanation:** C introduces topic (day begins). A gives fact (sun rises east). B follows (sets west). D concludes (cycle repeats).

---

### Q2 (Easy)
**Arrange in logical order:**
A. Water boils at 100°C.
B. When heated, water turns to steam.
C. This is a physical change.
D. The process is reversible.

**Answer:** A-B-C-D
**Explanation:** A states fact. B explains what happens when heated. C classifies it. D adds property.

---

### Q3 (Medium)
**Arrange in logical order:**
A. However, the results were inconclusive.
B. The experiment was conducted carefully.
C. The hypothesis predicted a clear outcome.
D. Therefore, further research is needed.

**Answer:** C-B-A-D
**Explanation:** C introduces hypothesis. B describes experiment. A contrasts (however). D concludes (therefore).

---

### Q4 (Medium)
**Arrange in logical order:**
A. The company launched a new product.
B. Initially, sales were disappointing.
C. Then they changed their marketing strategy.
D. Subsequently, sales improved significantly.

**Answer:** A-B-C-D
**Explanation:** Chronological: launch → initial sales → strategy change → improved sales.

---

### Q5 (Medium)
**Arrange in logical order:**
A. This phenomenon is called photosynthesis.
B. Plants absorb sunlight using chlorophyll.
C. They convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose.
D. Oxygen is released as a byproduct.

**Answer:** B-C-D-A
**Explanation:** B starts process. C continues. D adds byproduct. A names the process.

---

### Q6 (Hard)
**Arrange in logical order:**
A. The policy was implemented in 2020.
B. It aimed to reduce carbon emissions.
C. However, industries resisted the change.
D. As a result, the targets were missed.
E. The government introduced stricter penalties.
F. Compliance improved thereafter.

**Answer:** A-B-C-D-E-F
**Explanation:** Chronological + cause-effect: implementation → aim → resistance → missed targets → penalties → compliance.

---

### Q7 (Hard)
**Arrange in logical order:**
A. The bridge collapsed during the earthquake.
B. Engineers had warned about structural weaknesses.
C. The warnings were ignored due to budget constraints.
D. An investigation was ordered immediately.
E. The report confirmed the engineers' concerns.
F. New safety regulations were enacted.

**Answer:** B-C-A-D-E-F
**Explanation:** B-C are linked (warnings ignored). A is the event. D-E-F are consequences.

---

### Q8 (Hard)
**Arrange in logical order:**
A. Renewable energy sources are gaining popularity.
B. Solar and wind power are leading this transition.
C. However, storage remains a challenge.
D. Battery technology is improving rapidly.
E. This will eventually solve the intermittency problem.
F. A fully renewable grid is becoming feasible.

**Answer:** A-B-C-D-E-F
**Explanation:** A introduces topic. B gives examples. C introduces problem (however). D-E-F are solution progression.

---

### Q9 (Very Hard)
**Arrange in logical order:**
A. The concept of smart cities integrates technology with urban planning.
B. Sensors collect data on traffic, energy, and waste.
C. This data is analyzed to optimize city services.
D. However, privacy concerns have emerged.
E. Citizens worry about constant surveillance.
F. Policymakers must balance efficiency with rights.

**Answer:** A-B-C-D-E-F
**Explanation:** A defines concept. B-C explain mechanism. D-E introduce concern. F concludes with policy need.

---

### Q10 (Very Hard)
**Arrange in logical order:**
A. The researcher formulated a hypothesis.
B. She designed an experiment to test it.
C. The data collected contradicted the hypothesis.
D. She revised the hypothesis accordingly.
E. A new experiment confirmed the revised hypothesis.
F. The findings were published in a journal.

**Answer:** A-B-C-D-E-F
**Explanation:** Scientific method sequence: hypothesis → experiment → contradiction → revision → confirmation → publication.

## 4. Practice with Options (Multiple Choice)

### Q11 (Medium)
**Sentences:**
1. The architect designed the building.
2. It was completed in 2023.
3. The building won an award.
4. The design was innovative.

**Options:**
A) 1-4-2-3  B) 1-2-4-3  C) 4-1-2-3  D) 1-4-3-2

**Answer:** A) 1-4-2-3
**Explanation:** 1 introduces architect. 4 describes design (innovative). 2 gives completion date. 3 is result (award).

---

### Q12 (Hard)
**Sentences:**
1. Climate change affects agriculture globally.
2. Rising temperatures reduce crop yields.
3. Farmers are adapting with new techniques.
4. Drought-resistant crops are being developed.
5. However, small farmers lack resources.
6. Government support is crucial.

**Options:**
A) 1-2-3-4-5-6  B) 1-2-4-3-5-6  C) 2-1-3-4-5-6  D) 1-3-2-4-5-6

**Answer:** A) 1-2-3-4-5-6
**Explanation:** 1 general statement. 2 specific effect. 3-4 adaptation responses. 5 contrast (however). 6 policy conclusion.

---

## 5. Rapid Revision

- **Opening sentence:** No pronouns, introduces topic
- **Pronouns** → refer to previous noun
- **However/Therefore** → contrast/conclusion
- **Chronological** → time markers
- **Mandatory pairs** → must stay together
- **Verify** by reading full sequence
