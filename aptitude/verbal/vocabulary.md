# Vocabulary

> **Priority:** P1 · **Role relevance:** Important (all roles)
> **Difficulty range:** Easy → Hard · **Time per question:** 30 – 60 sec

---

## 1. High-Value Word Groups

### Group 1: Words for "More/Increase"
| Word | Meaning | Example |
|:-----|:--------|:--------|
| **Ameliorate** | To make better | "The new policy ameliorated working conditions." |
| **Augment** | To increase/supplement | "She augmented her income with freelance work." |
| **Proliferate** | To increase rapidly | "Tech startups proliferated in the region." |
| **Surge** | Sudden increase | "There was a surge in demand." |
| **Escalate** | To increase rapidly | "Costs escalated beyond the budget." |
| **Exacerbate** | To make worse (negative increase) | "The drought exacerbated food shortages." |

### Group 2: Words for "Decrease/Reduce"
| Word | Meaning | Example |
|:-----|:--------|:--------|
| **Alleviate** | To relieve/reduce | "Medicine alleviated her pain." |
| **Mitigate** | To lessen severity | "Trees mitigate the heat island effect." |
| **Diminish** | To reduce/become smaller | "His influence diminished over time." |
| **Dwindle** | To gradually decrease | "Their savings dwindled to nothing." |
| **Subside** | To become less intense | "The floodwaters finally subsided." |
| **Abate** | To reduce in intensity | "The storm abated after midnight." |

### Group 3: Words for "Change/Transform"
| Word | Meaning | Example |
|:-----|:--------|:--------|
| **Metamorphosis** | Complete transformation | "The caterpillar underwent metamorphosis." |
| **Transmute** | To change form/nature | "Alchemy aimed to transmute lead to gold." |
| **Morph** | To gradually change | "The company morphed into a tech firm." |
| **Evolve** | To develop gradually | "The strategy evolved over decades." |
| **Permute** | To change order/arrangement | "The team permuted the lineup." |

### Group 4: Words for "Enough/Sufficient"
| Word | Meaning | Example |
|:-----|:--------|:--------|
| **Ample** | More than enough | "There was ample food for everyone." |
| **Sufficient** | Enough for a purpose | "Evidence was sufficient to convict." |
| **Bountiful** | Abundant, generous | "The harvest was bountiful." |
| **Meager** | Inadequate, insufficient | "They survived on meager rations." |
| **Paltry** | Insignificant, small | "He offered a paltry sum." |
| **Scanty** | Barely sufficient | "Scanty rainfall caused drought." |

### Group 5: Words for "Clever/Intelligent"
| Word | Meaning | Example |
|:-----|:--------|:--------|
| **Astute** | Sharply perceptive | "An astute business decision." |
| **Acumen** | Ability to judge well | "Financial acumen is essential." |
| **Sagacious** | Wise, prudent | "A sagacious leader." |
| **Perspicacious** | Having keen insight | "Her perspicacious analysis was praised." |
| **Shrewd** | Clever in a practical way | "A shrewd negotiator." |

### Group 6: Words for "Old/Outdated"
| Word | Meaning | Example |
|:-----|:--------|:--------|
| **Archaic** | Very old, outdated | "The law was archaic and needed reform." |
| **Antiquated** | Old-fashioned | "Antiquated technology slowed production." |
| **Obsolescent** | Becoming obsolete | "Obsolescent equipment was replaced." |
| **Defunct** | No longer existing | "The company became defunct." |
| **Redundant** | No longer needed | "Automation made his role redundant." |

### Group 7: Words for "Difficult/Hard"
| Word | Meaning | Example |
|:-----|:--------|:--------|
| **Arduous** | Extremely difficult | "The arduous journey took 3 months." |
| **Grueling** | Exhausting, punishing | "A grueling 12-hour exam." |
| **Perilous** | Full of danger | "The perillous crossing of the river." |
| **Formidable** | Inspiring fear/respect | "A formidable opponent." |
| **Daunting** | Intimidating | "The daunting task of reform." |

### Group 8: Words for "Easy/Simple"
| Word | Meaning | Example |
|:-----|:--------|:--------|
| **Trivial** | Of little importance | "A trivial mistake, easily fixed." |
| **Negligible** | So small as to not matter | "The difference was negligible." |
| **Paltry** | Small, insignificant | "A paltry effort." |
| **Effortless** | Requiring no effort | "An effortless solution." |

## 2. Synonym-Antonym Quick Reference

| Word | Synonym | Antonym |
|:-----|:--------|:--------|
| Ameliorate | Improve | Worsen |
| Augment | Increase | Diminish |
| Alleviate | Relieve | Exacerbate |
| Mitigate | Lessen | Intensify |
| Astute | Shrewd | Naive |
| Arduous | Strenuous | Effortless |
| Ample | Sufficient | Meager |
| Archaic | Ancient | Modern |
| Defunct | Extinct | Active |
| Perspicacious | Insightful | Obtuse |

## 3. Practice Questions (20 Questions)

### Q1
Choose the word closest in meaning to "MITIGATE":
- A) Exacerbate  B) Alleviate  C) Aggravate  D) Intensify
**Answer:** B) Alleviate

### Q2
Choose the opposite of "PROLIFERATE":
- A) Increase  B) Multiply  C) Diminish  D) Expand
**Answer:** C) Diminish

### Q3
Choose the word closest in meaning to "SAGACIOUS":
- A) Foolish  B) Wise  C) Lazy  D) Average
**Answer:** B) Wise

### Q4
Choose the opposite of "AMPLE":
- A) Sufficient  B) Abundant  C) Meager  D) Bountiful
**Answer:** C) Meager

### Q5
Choose the word closest in meaning to "ARDUOUS":
- A) Easy  B) Trivial  C) Strenuous  D) Simple
**Answer:** C) Strenuous

### Q6
"The bridge was in such ______ condition that it was closed for repairs."
- A) immaculate  B) precarious  C) pristine  D) excellent
**Answer:** B) precarious (dangerous, unstable)

### Q7
Choose the opposite of "ARCHAIC":
- A) Ancient  B) Modern  C) Outdated  D) Primitive
**Answer:** B) Modern

### Q8
"The engineer's ______ analysis identified the flaw in the design."
- A) cursory  B) superficial  C) perspicacious  D) hasty
**Answer:** C) perspicacious (keen insight)

### Q9
Choose the word closest in meaning to "DWINDLE":
- A) Surge  B) Decrease  C) Stagnate  D) Oscillate
**Answer:** B) Decrease

### Q10
"The team ______ after the heavy defeat."
- A) thrived  B) prospered  C) languished  D) flourished
**Answer:** C) languished (weakened, became listless)

### Q11
Choose the opposite of "DEFUNCT":
- A) Dead  B) Active  C) Extinct  D) Dormant
**Answer:** B) Active

### Q12
"Her ______ approach to the project ensured no detail was overlooked."
- A) negligent  B) meticulous  C) careless  D) haphazard
**Answer:** B) meticulous (extremely careful)

### Q13
Choose the word closest in meaning to "EXACERBATE":
- A) Improve  B) Worsen  C) Maintain  D) Reduce
**Answer:** B) Worsen

### Q14
"The politician's ______ promises failed to convince the voters."
- A) genuine  B) hollow  C) sincere  D) profound
**Answer:** B) hollow (empty, insincere)

### Q15
Choose the opposite of "METICULOUS":
- A) Thorough  B) Careful  C) Sloppy  D) Precise
**Answer:** C) Sloppy

### Q16
"The company's ______ growth surprised industry analysts."
- A) negligible  B) stagnant  C) robust  D) tepid
**Answer:** C) robust (strong, vigorous)

### Q17
Choose the word closest in meaning to "REDUNDANT":
- A) Essential  B) Obsolete  C) Valuable  D) Critical
**Answer:** B) Obsolete (no longer needed)

### Q18
"The floodwaters ______ after three days of heavy rain."
- A) escalated  B) intensified  C) subsided  D) surged
**Answer:** C) subsided (became less)

### Q19
Choose the opposite of "ASTUTE":
- A) Shrewd  B) Naive  C) Clever  D) Sharp
**Answer:** B) Naive

### Q20
"The project was ______ due to lack of funding."
- A) accelerated  B) abandoned  C) completed  D) enhanced
**Answer:** B) abandoned

## 4. Rapid Revision — Must-Know Words

**Top 10 for placement:** Mitigate, Augment, Ameliorate, Exacerbate, Astute, Arduous, Ample, Meticulous, Hollow, Subsided

**Don't confuse:**
- Mitigate ≠ Migrate
- Affect ≠ Effect
- Compliment ≠ Complement
- Principal ≠ Principle
- Elicit ≠ Illicit
