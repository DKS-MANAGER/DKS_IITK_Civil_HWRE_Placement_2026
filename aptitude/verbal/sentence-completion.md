# Sentence Completion

> **Priority:** P1 · **Role relevance:** Important (all roles)
> **Difficulty range:** Easy → Hard · **Time per question:** 30 – 90 sec

---

## 1. Strategy

1. **Read the whole sentence** — understand the logic/flow
2. **Identify the clue** — words like "although," "because," "however," "similarly"
3. **Predict the answer** — before looking at options
4. **Check both blanks** (for double-blank questions)
5. **Eliminate** options that don't fit the logic

---

## 2. Common Logical Relationships

| Relationship | Signal Words | Example |
|:-------------|:-------------|:--------|
| Contrast | although, but, however, despite, yet | "Although tired, he **continued** working." |
| Cause/Effect | because, since, therefore, thus, hence | "Because it rained, the match **was cancelled**." |
| Similarity | similarly, likewise, just as | "Just as he is kind, she is **compassionate**." |
| Definition/Explanation | that is, namely, in other words | "He is a philanthropist, **one who donates to charity**." |
| Time/Sequence | before, after, then, subsequently | "He finished the work, **then** he left." |

## 3. Practice Questions (20 Questions)

### Single Blank

### Q1 (Easy)
The manager's decision was ______; it pleased everyone.
- A) controversial  B) arbitrary  C) judicious  D) biased
**Answer:** C) judicious (wise, fair — positive context)

### Q2 (Easy)
Despite his ______ appearance, he is actually very kind.
- A) gentle  B) rugged  C) friendly  D) warm
**Answer:** B) rugged (contrast with "kind")

### Q3 (Medium)
The scientist's theory was ______ by the new evidence.
- A) refuted  B) supported  C) ignored  D) created
**Answer:** A) refuted (evidence typically tests/refutes theories)

### Q4 (Medium)
His ______ nature made him popular among colleagues.
- A) aloof  B) affable  C) hostile  D) indifferent
**Answer:** B) affable (friendly, easy to talk to)

### Q5 (Medium)
The ______ of the ancient manuscript surprised the archaeologists.
- A) discovery  B) loss  C) destruction  D) creation
**Answer:** A) discovery (positive surprise context)

### Q6 (Hard)
The politician's speech was full of ______ promises that were never fulfilled.
- A) genuine  B) hollow  C) sincere  D) concrete
**Answer:** B) hollow (empty, not fulfilled)

### Q7 (Hard)
The ______ of the evidence led to the suspect's acquittal.
- A) abundance  B) clarity  C) insufficiency  D) strength
**Answer:** C) insufficiency (not enough evidence → acquittal)

### Q8 (Hard)
Her ______ attitude toward the crisis inspired confidence in the team.
- A) pessimistic  B) nonchalant  C) composed  D) frantic
**Answer:** C) composed (calm, confident)

---

### Double Blank

### Q9 (Medium)
Although the project was ______, the team ______ to complete it on time.
- A) difficult / managed  B) easy / failed  C) simple / struggled  D) complex / refused
**Answer:** A) difficult / managed (contrast: although difficult, managed)

### Q10 (Medium)
The ______ of the evidence was ______ by the defense attorney.
- A) validity / challenged  B) truth / accepted  C) falsity / proven  D) clarity / ignored
**Answer:** A) validity / challenged (defense challenges validity)

### Q11 (Hard)
The ______ leader ______ the team's morale with his inspiring words.
- A) charismatic / bolstered  B) timid / destroyed  C) arrogant / ignored  D) indifferent / raised
**Answer:** A) charismatic / bolstered (positive leader → positive effect)

### Q12 (Hard)
The ______ of the ancient city was ______ by modern construction.
- A) preservation / enhanced  B) ruins / destroyed  C) beauty / marred  D) history / forgotten
**Answer:** C) beauty / marred (negative effect of modern construction)

### Q13 (Hard)
His ______ refusal to compromise ______ the negotiations.
- A) stubborn / stalled  B) willing / advanced  C) flexible / helped  D) eager / concluded
**Answer:** A) stubborn / stalled (stubborn refusal → negative outcome)

### Q14 (Very Hard)
The ______ of the novel lies in its ______ portrayal of human nature.
- A) weakness / superficial  B) strength / nuanced  C) flaw / biased  D) failure / inaccurate
**Answer:** B) strength / nuanced (positive evaluation)

### Q15 (Very Hard)
______ by the criticism, the artist ______ to create more meaningful work.
- A) Discouraged / refused  B) Inspired / resolved  C) Unaffected / continued  D) Angered / decided
**Answer:** B) Inspired / resolved (positive response to criticism)

## 4. Vocabulary for Sentence Completion

### Positive Traits
- **Judicious** — wise, showing good judgment
- **Affable** — friendly, easy to talk to
- **Composed** — calm, self-possessed
- **Charismatic** — compelling charm
- **Bolster** — support, strengthen
- **Nuanced** — subtle, sophisticated
- **Resolute** — determined

### Negative Traits
- **Hollow** — empty, insincere
- **Insufficiency** — not enough
- **Stubborn** — unreasonably unyielding
- **Marred** — spoiled, damaged
- **Stalled** — stopped progress
- **Superficial** — shallow
- **Arbitrary** — random, without reason

---

## 5. Rapid Revision

- **Read whole sentence first**
- **Find the logical clue** (although, because, etc.)
- **Predict before looking at options**
- **Check both blanks** for double-blank
- **Eliminate** wrong logic
