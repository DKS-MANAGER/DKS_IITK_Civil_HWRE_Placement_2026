# Grammar & Error Spotting

> **Priority:** P1 · **Role relevance:** Important (all roles)
> **Difficulty range:** Easy → Hard · **Time per question:** 30 – 90 sec

---

## 1. Core Grammar Rules Tested

### 1.1 Subject-Verb Agreement
- Singular subject → singular verb; plural subject → plural verb
- **Trap:** Intervening phrases don't change the subject's number
- **Example:** "The box of chocolates **is** open." (not "are")

### 1.2 Tense Consistency
- Maintain consistent tense within a sentence/paragraph
- **Trap:** Mixing past and present without reason

### 1.3 Pronoun Reference
- Pronoun must clearly refer to one antecedent
- **Trap:** Ambiguous "it," "they," "this"

### 1.4 Parallelism
- Items in a list must have the same grammatical form
- **Trap:** "She likes reading, writing, and to paint" → "to paint" breaks parallelism

### 1.5 Modifiers
- Modifiers must be placed next to what they modify
- **Trap:** "Running down the street, the bus hit him" → bus wasn't running

### 1.6 Prepositions
- Correct preposition for the verb/adjective
- **Trap:** "interested on" → "interested in"

### 1.7 Articles
- "a/an" for indefinite, "the" for definite
- **Trap:** Missing or extra articles

## 2. Practice Questions (20 Questions)

### Q1 (Easy)
**Identify the error:** The team of engineers **are** working on the project.
- A) The team of engineers  B) are  C) working on  D) the project
**Answer:** B) are → should be "is" (team is singular)

### Q2 (Easy)
**Identify the error:** Neither the manager nor the employees **was** present.
- A) Neither the manager  B) nor the employees  C) was  D) present
**Answer:** C) was → should be "were" (neither/nor: verb agrees with nearer subject)

### Q3 (Medium)
**Identify the error:** She is **more smarter** than her brother.
- A) She is  B) more smarter  C) than her  D) brother
**Answer:** B) more smarter → "smarter" already comparative, don't use "more"

### Q4 (Medium)
**Identify the error:** The report **which** I submitted yesterday **were** approved.
- A) The report  B) which I submitted yesterday  C) were  D) approved
**Answer:** C) were → should be "was" (report is singular)

### Q5 (Medium)
**Identify the error:** **Having finished** the work, **the TV was turned on** by him.
- A) Having finished  B) the work  C) the TV was turned on  D) by him
**Answer:** C) the TV was turned on → dangling modifier. Should be "he turned on the TV"

### Q6 (Medium)
**Identify the error:** He is **interested on** learning new languages.
- A) He is  B) interested on  C) learning new  D) languages
**Answer:** B) interested on → should be "interested in"

### Q7 (Hard)
**Identify the error:** **Not only** he is intelligent **but also** hardworking.
- A) Not only  B) he is intelligent  C) but also  D) hardworking
**Answer:** A) Not only → should be "He is not only intelligent but also hardworking" (parallel structure)

### Q8 (Hard)
**Identify the error:** The number of students **have** increased this year.
- A) The number of students  B) have  C) increased  D) this year
**Answer:** B) have → should be "has" ("The number" is singular; "A number" would be plural)

### Q9 (Hard)
**Identify the error:** **Between** you and I, this plan **will not work**.
- A) Between  B) you and I  C) this plan  D) will not work
**Answer:** B) you and I → should be "you and me" (object of preposition)

### Q10 (Hard)
**Identify the error:** Each of the students **were** given a certificate.
- A) Each of the students  B) were  C) given a  D) certificate
**Answer:** B) were → should be "was" (Each is singular)

### Q11 (Medium)
**Identify the error:** The committee **has** decided to **postpone** the meeting **until** next week.
- A) The committee  B) has  C) postpone  D) until
**Answer:** No error (D) — "until" is correct for future time

### Q12 (Medium)
**Identify the error:** **Scarcely** had he arrived **than** the meeting started.
- A) Scarcely  B) had he arrived  C) than  D) the meeting started
**Answer:** C) than → should be "when" (Scarcely...when)

### Q13 (Hard)
**Identify the error:** The data **suggests** that the hypothesis **is** correct.
- A) The data  B) suggests  C) that the hypothesis  D) is
**Answer:** No error (D) — "data" can be singular or plural; "suggests" is acceptable

### Q14 (Hard)
**Identify the error:** **Having been** warned about the danger, **the risk was ignored** by them.
- A) Having been  B) warned about the danger  C) the risk was ignored  D) by them
**Answer:** C) the risk was ignored → dangling modifier. Should be "they ignored the risk"

### Q15 (Hard)
**Identify the error:** I would **rather** you **did** the work yourself.
- A) I would  B) rather  C) did  D) the work yourself
**Answer:** No error (D) — "would rather + subject + past tense" is correct

### Q16 (Medium)
**Identify the error:** The teacher **along with** the students **are** going on a trip.
- A) The teacher  B) along with  C) are  D) going on a trip
**Answer:** C) are → should be "is" (along with doesn't change subject number)

### Q17 (Medium)
**Identify the error:** He **prevented** me **from** entering the room.
- A) He  B) prevented  C) from  D) entering the room
**Answer:** No error (D) — "prevent from" is correct

### Q18 (Hard)
**Identify the error:** **No sooner** did he leave **when** it started raining.
- A) No sooner  B) did he leave  C) when  D) it started raining
**Answer:** C) when → should be "than" (No sooner...than)

### Q19 (Hard)
**Identify the error:** The quality of the products **have** deteriorated.
- A) The quality  B) of the products  C) have  D) deteriorated
**Answer:** C) have → should be "has" (quality is singular)

### Q20 (Very Hard)
**Identify the error:** **Not only** the students **but also** the teacher **were** surprised.
- A) Not only  B) the students  C) but also the teacher  D) were
**Answer:** D) were → should be "was" (verb agrees with nearer subject: teacher)

## 3. Rapid Revision

- **Subject-verb:** Singular subject = singular verb
- **Neither/nor, either/or:** Verb agrees with nearer subject
- **Each, every, neither, either:** Singular
- **The number:** Singular; **A number:** Plural
- **Dangling modifiers:** Modifier must modify the subject
- **Parallelism:** Same grammatical form in lists
- **Prepositions:** interested in, prevent from, no sooner...than, scarcely...when
