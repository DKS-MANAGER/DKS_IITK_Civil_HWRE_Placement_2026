# Critical Reasoning

> **Priority:** P1 · **Role relevance:** Important (all roles), Critical (Consulting)
> **Difficulty range:** Medium → Very Hard · **Time per question:** 1 – 2 min

---

## 1. What You Need to Know

Critical Reasoning tests your ability to evaluate arguments. Key question types:
1. **Strengthen** — what supports the argument?
2. **Weaken** — what undermines the argument?
3. **Assumption** — what must be true for the argument to hold?
4. **Conclusion** — what follows from the premises?
5. **Inference** — what is logically implied?

---

## 2. Argument Structure

An **argument** has:
- **Premises** — the evidence/reasons
- **Conclusion** — the claim being argued for
- **Assumptions** — unstated premises

---

## 3. Strategy

1. **Identify the conclusion** — what's being argued?
2. **Identify the premises** — what supports it?
3. **Find the gap** — what's missing between premises and conclusion?
4. **Evaluate options** — which strengthens/weakens/fills the gap?

---

## 4. Worked Examples

### Q1 (Medium) — Weaken
**Argument:** "Our city should build more roads to reduce traffic congestion."
**Which weakens?**
- A) More roads attract more cars, worsening congestion
- B) Roads are expensive to build
- C) Public transport is underused
- D) Traffic is worst during peak hours

**Answer:** A) More roads attract more cars. This directly undermines the claim that roads reduce congestion.

### Q2 (Hard) — Assumption
**Argument:** "The new software will increase productivity because it automates data entry."
**Assumption:**
- A) Data entry is a significant part of employees' work
- B) The software is expensive
- C) Employees like the software
- D) The company has computers

**Answer:** A) Data entry is a significant part of employees' work. If data entry isn't significant, automating it won't increase productivity much.

### Q3 (Very Hard) — Strengthen
**Argument:** "Increasing the minimum wage will reduce poverty."
**Which strengthens?**
- A) Minimum wage workers spend most of their income on essentials
- B) Some businesses may lay off workers
- C) Minimum wage is already high
- D) Inflation will offset wage gains

**Answer:** A) Minimum wage workers spend most of their income on essentials. This supports the claim that higher wages reduce poverty.

## 5. Practice (15 Questions)

### Medium
1. Weaken: "Cars cause pollution, so we should ban cars." *(Medium)*
2. Strengthen: "Exercise improves health." *(Medium)*
3. Assumption: "The new policy will boost the economy." *(Medium)*
4. Conclusion: "All students who study hard pass. John studies hard." *(Medium)*
5. Weaken: "Online learning is as effective as classroom learning." *(Medium)*

### Hard
6. Assumption: "Hiring more engineers will speed up the project." *(Hard)*
7. Strengthen: "Renewable energy will replace fossil fuels." *(Hard)*
8. Weaken: "The drug is safe because it passed clinical trials." *(Hard)*
9. Conclusion: "If it rains, the match is cancelled. It rained." *(Hard)*
10. Assumption: "Reducing taxes will increase investment." *(Hard)*

### Very Hard
11. Strengthen: "Remote work increases productivity." *(Very Hard)*
12. Weaken: "The survey shows 80% prefer product A." *(Very Hard)*
13. Assumption: "The merger will create value for shareholders." *(Very Hard)*
14. Conclusion: "All mammals are warm-blooded. Whales are mammals." *(Very Hard)*
15. Strengthen: "Investing in education reduces crime." *(Very Hard)*

## 7. Rapid Revision

- **Identify conclusion + premises**
- **Find the gap** → assumption
- **Strengthen:** supports the conclusion
- **Weaken:** undermines the conclusion
- **Trap:** Options that are true but irrelevant
