## Arithmetic

### Percentages
- Fraction ↔ %: multiply/divide by 100
- % change = `(Change / Original) × 100`
- Successive change (a% then b%): `a + b + (ab/100)`
- Product stability: if A↑x%, B must ↓ `(x/(100+x))×100`%
- Population growth/decay: `P(1 ± r/100)^n`

### Ratio & Proportion
- `a:b = c:d ⟹ ad = bc`
- Partnership profit split: ratio of `Capital × Time`
- Mean proportional of a,b: `√(ab)`

### Averages
- Average = `Sum / Count`
- Weighted average = `Σ(wᵢxᵢ) / Σwᵢ`
- Alligation: `(Cheaper qty)/(Dearer qty) = (Mean - Cheaper)/(Dearer - Mean)`

### Profit, Loss & Discount
- Profit% = `((SP - CP)/CP) × 100`
- Loss% = `((CP - SP)/CP) × 100`
- SP = `CP × (1 ± P%/100)`
- Successive discounts d₁,d₂: `d₁ + d₂ - (d₁d₂/100)`
- Marked price: `MP × (1 - D/100) = SP`

### Simple & Compound Interest
- SI = `(P × R × T)/100`
- Amount (SI) = `P + SI`
- CI Amount = `P(1 + r/100)^t`
- CI - SI (2 years) = `P(r/100)²`
- CI - SI (3 years) = `P(r/100)²(3 + r/100)`

### Time & Work
- Work = `Rate × Time`; Rate = `1/Time`
- A(x) + B(y) together = `xy/(x+y)`
- Man-day-hour: `M₁D₁H₁/W₁ = M₂D₂H₂/W₂`
- Pipes: net rate = `1/x - 1/y` (inlet - outlet)

### Speed, Time & Distance
- Speed = `Distance/Time`
- km/h → m/s: `× 5/18`; m/s → km/h: `× 18/5`
- Average speed (equal distances) = `2xy/(x+y)`
- Relative speed: opposite `+`, same `-`
- Train crossing: distance = train length (+ platform length)

### Mixtures & Alligation
- Alligation rule (see Averages)
- Repeated dilution: `Final = Initial(1 - x/V)^n`

### Number System
- Sum of n naturals: `n(n+1)/2`
- Sum of squares: `n(n+1)(2n+1)/6`
- Sum of cubes: `[n(n+1)/2]²`
- Number of factors of `p^a q^b`: `(a+1)(b+1)`
- Unit digit cyclicity: 2,3,7,8 → cycle 4; 4,9 → cycle 2

## Algebra

- `(a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²`
- `(a-b)² = a² - 2ab + b²`
- `a² - b² = (a+b)(a-b)`
- `(a+b)³ = a³ + 3a²b + 3ab² + b³`
- `a³ + b³ = (a+b)(a² - ab + b²)`
- Quadratic: `x = [-b ± √(b²-4ac)]/2a`

## Probability

- P(E) = `n(E)/n(S)`
- P(A∪B) = `P(A) + P(B) - P(A∩B)`
- Independent: `P(A∩B) = P(A)×P(B)`
- Conditional: `P(A|B) = P(A∩B)/P(B)`
- Complement: `P(not E) = 1 - P(E)`

---

## Permutations & Combinations

- nPr = `n!/(n-r)!` (order matters)
- nCr = `n!/[r!(n-r)!]` (order doesn't matter)
- nCr = nC(n-r)
- Circular permutations: `(n-1)!`
- Arrangements with repetition: `n^r`

## Data Interpretation

- Percentage = `(Part/Total) × 100`
- Growth rate = `((New - Old)/Old) × 100`
- Ratio comparison: cross-multiply
- Approximation: round to nearest convenient number
- Pie chart: value = `(Angle/360) × Total`

## Reasoning

### Series
- Arithmetic: add/subtract constant
- Geometric: multiply/divide constant
- Alternating: two interleaved series
- Square/cube series: `n²`, `n³`

### Coding-Decoding
- Letter position: A=1 ... Z=26
- Reverse alphabet: A↔Z, B↔Y
- Shift by fixed number

### Direction Sense
- North→East→South→West (clockwise)
- Pythagoras for shortest distance: `√(a²+b²)`

### Clocks
- Angle between hands = `|30H - 5.5M|`
- Hands coincide: `60H/11` minutes past H

### Calendars
- Odd days: normal year = 1, leap year = 2
- Century: 100 yr = 5 odd days, 400 yr = 0 odd days
