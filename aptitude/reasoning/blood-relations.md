# Blood Relations

> **Priority:** P1 · **Role relevance:** Important (all roles)
> **Difficulty range:** Easy → Hard · **Time per question:** 30 sec – 2 min

---

## 1. Family Tree Symbols

| Symbol | Meaning |
|:-------|:--------|
| □ or + | Male |
| ○ or - | Female |
| = | Married couple |
| — | Siblings |
| \| | Generation gap (older on top) |

### Key Vocabulary
- **Maternal:** Mother's side (Maternal Uncle = mother's brother)
- **Paternal:** Father's side (Paternal Aunt = father's sister)
- **Sibling:** Brother or sister
- **Spouse:** Husband or wife
- **Niece/Nephew:** Child of brother/sister
- **Cousin:** Child of uncle/aunt

---

## 2. Types

### Type 1: Pointing / Narrative
A person points to a photograph or describes a relation.
- **Strategy:** Start from the last relation mentioned (usually "my" or "of") and work backwards.

### Type 2: Coded Relations
Symbols represent relations, e.g., A+B means "A is mother of B".
- **Strategy:** Break down term-by-term, draw mini family tree.

## 3. Worked Examples

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** Pointing to a boy, Suresh said, "He is the son of the only son of my mother." How is Suresh related to the boy?
**Solution:** Only son of my mother = Suresh himself. Son of Suresh = the boy. **Answer:** Father.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** If P×Q means "P is brother of Q", P+Q means "P is father of Q", P-Q means "P is sister of Q". Which means "A is uncle of C"?
- A) A+B-C  B) A×B+C  C) A-B×C  D) A×B-C
**Solution:** A×B means A is brother of B. B+C means B is father of C. So A is brother of father of C = uncle. **Answer:** B) A×B+C.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** A is B's brother, B is C's sister, C is D's mother, D is E's father. How is A related to E?
**Solution:** A-B siblings. C is D's mother. D is E's father. So C is E's grandmother. A is C's brother. So A is E's grand-uncle. **Answer:** Grand-uncle.

## 4. Practice (15 Questions)

### Basic
1. "He is the son of my father." Who is he? *(Easy)*
2. "She is the daughter of my mother." Who is she? *(Easy)*
3. A is B's father, B is C's mother. How is A related to C? *(Easy)*
4. "He is the brother of my sister." Who is he? *(Easy)*
5. A is B's son, B is C's daughter. How is A related to C? *(Easy)*

### Intermediate
6. Pointing to a photograph, a man says, "She is the daughter of the only son of my father." How is the man related to the girl? *(Medium)*
7. If P+Q means "P is mother of Q", P-Q means "P is father of Q", P×Q means "P is sister of Q". Which means "A is aunt of B"? *(Medium)*
8. A is B's brother, B is C's sister, C is D's mother. How is A related to D? *(Medium)*
9. "He is the son of the only daughter of my father." How is he related to me? *(Medium)*
10. If A×B means "A is brother of B", A+B means "A is sister of B", A-B means "A is father of B". Which means "A is uncle of B"? *(Medium)*

### Advanced
11. A is B's brother, B is C's sister, C is D's mother, D is E's father, E is F's brother. How is A related to F? *(Hard)*
12. Pointing to a woman, a man says, "She is the wife of the only son of my father." How is the woman related to the man? *(Hard)*
13. If P+Q means "P is wife of Q", P-Q means "P is husband of Q", P×Q means "P is daughter of Q". Which means "A is daughter-in-law of B"? *(Hard)*
14. A family has 3 generations. A is B's father, B is C's mother, C is D's brother, D is E's sister. How is A related to E? *(Hard)*
15. "He is the son of the only son of my grandfather." How is he related to me? *(Hard)*

## 5. Placement Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** Pointing to a photograph, a man says, "He is the son of the only son of my father." How is the man related to the boy?
- A) Father  B) Brother  C) Uncle  D) Son
**Answer:** A) Father. Only son of my father = me. Son of me = the boy.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** If P×Q means "P is brother of Q", P+Q means "P is father of Q", P-Q means "P is sister of Q". Which means "A is uncle of C"?
- A) A+B-C  B) A×B+C  C) A-B×C  D) A×B-C
**Answer:** B) A×B+C. A is brother of B, B is father of C → A is uncle of C.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** A is B's brother, B is C's sister, C is D's mother, D is E's father. How is A related to E?
- A) Uncle  B) Grand-uncle  C) Father  D) Brother
**Answer:** B) Grand-uncle. A is C's brother, C is E's grandmother.

## 6. Rapid Revision

- **Pointing problems:** Start from last relation, work backwards
- **Coded relations:** Draw mini family tree term-by-term
- **Maternal vs paternal:** Check which side
- **Trap:** "Only son/daughter" — unique identifier
