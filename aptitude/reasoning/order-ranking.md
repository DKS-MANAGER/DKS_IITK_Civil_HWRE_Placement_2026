# Order & Ranking

> **Priority:** P1 · **Role relevance:** Important (all roles)
> **Difficulty range:** Easy → Hard · **Time per question:** 30 sec – 2 min

---

## 1. Key Formulas

| Situation | Formula |
|:----------|:--------|
| Position from the other side | `Total + 1 − Position from this side` |
| Total, when one person's ranks from both sides are given (**overlapping**) | `Total = Left rank + Right rank − 1` |
| Total, when two different people's ranks are given with *k* people between them (**non-overlapping**) | `Total = Left rank of one + Right rank of other + k` |
| Number of people between two known positions | `|P₁ − P₂| − 1` |

**Overlapping vs non-overlapping:** if the two ranks describe the *same* person, subtract 1. If they describe *different* people who don't cross, add the people between them.

---

## 2. Worked Examples

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** In a row of 10 students, A is 4th from the left. Position from the right?
**Solution:** 10 + 1 − 4 = **7th from the right.**

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** In a row, A is 5th from the left and 8th from the right. How many students?
**Solution:** Same person, both sides ⇒ 5 + 8 − 1 = **12 students.**

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** In a row of 25 students, A is 10th from the left and B is 15th from the right. How many students are between A and B?
**Solution:** B's position from the left = 25 + 1 − 15 = 11. A is at 10. People between = |11 − 10| − 1 = **0 — A and B are adjacent.**

### Q4 (Very Hard)
**Q:** In a class of 50, A is 15th from the top and B is 20th from the bottom. How many students are between A and B?
**Solution:** B's position from the top = 50 + 1 − 20 = 31. Between A (15) and B (31): 31 − 15 − 1 = **15 students.**

## 4. Placement Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** In a row of 20 students, A is 8th from the left. Position from the right?
- A) 12th  B) 13th  C) 11th  D) 14th
**Answer:** B) 13th. 20 + 1 − 8 = 13.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** In a row, A is 10th from the left and 15th from the right. Total students?
- A) 23  B) 24  C) 25  D) 26
**Answer:** B) 24. 10 + 15 − 1 = 24.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** In a row of 30, A is 10th from the left and B is 12th from the right. How many students are between A and B?
- A) 7  B) 8  C) 9  D) 10
**Answer:** B) 8. B's position from the left = 30 + 1 − 12 = 19; 19 − 10 − 1 = 8.

---

## 5. Rapid Revision

- **Other side:** Total + 1 − this-side position
- **Total (same person, both sides):** Left + Right − 1
- **Total (two people, k between):** Left + Right + k
- **Between two positions:** |P₁ − P₂| − 1
- **Trap:** confusing the overlapping (−1) and non-overlapping (+k) cases
