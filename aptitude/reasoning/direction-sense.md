# Direction Sense

> **Priority:** P1 · **Role relevance:** Important (all roles)
> **Difficulty range:** Easy → Hard · **Time per question:** 30 sec – 2 min

---

## 1. Key Rules

### Cardinal Directions
```
        North (N)
           ↑
West (W) ← → East (E)
           ↓
        South (S)
```

### Facing Direction Rules
- **Facing North:** Left = West, Right = East
- **Facing South:** Left = East, Right = West
- **Facing East:** Left = North, Right = South
- **Facing West:** Left = South, Right = North

### Pythagoras Theorem
For shortest distance: **Distance = √(horizontal² + vertical²)**

---

## 2. Worked Examples

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** A walks 5 km east, then 3 km north. How far is he from start?
**Solution:** √(5² + 3²) = √(25+9) = √34 ≈ 5.83 km.
**Answer:** √34 km ≈ 5.83 km.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** A walks 3 km north, turns right and walks 4 km. He is now:
**Solution:** Right of North = East. 3 km N, 4 km E. Distance = √(3²+4²) = 5 km. Direction: North-East.
**Answer:** 5 km from start, in NE direction.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** A walks 5 km east, turns left and walks 5 km, turns left and walks 5 km. Where is he relative to start?
**Solution:** 5 km E, 5 km N, 5 km W. Final position: 5 km north of start.
**Answer:** 5 km directly north.

### Q4 (Very Hard)
**Q:** A walks 6 km east, turns right and walks 4 km, turns right and walks 3 km, turns right and walks 5 km. How far is he from start?
**Solution:** 6 km E, 4 km S, 3 km W, 5 km N. Net: E(6-3)=3 km, S(4-5)=-1 km = 1 km N. Distance = √(3²+1²) = √10 ≈ 3.16 km.
**Answer:** √10 km ≈ 3.16 km, North-East.

## 4. Placement Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** A walks 3 km east, then 4 km north. Shortest distance from start is:
- A) 7 km  B) 5 km  C) 6 km  D) 4 km
**Answer:** B) 5 km. √(3²+4²) = 5.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** A walks 5 km north, turns right and walks 3 km, turns right and walks 5 km. Where is he relative to start?
- A) 3 km east  B) 3 km west  C) 5 km north  D) 3 km south
**Answer:** A) 3 km east. Net: 5N-5S=0, 3E.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** A walks 6 km east, turns right and walks 4 km, turns right and walks 3 km, turns right and walks 5 km. How far is he from start?
- A) √10 km  B) 5 km  C) √13 km  D) 3 km
**Answer:** A) √10 km. Net: E(6-3)=3, N(5-4)=1. √(9+1)=√10.

## 5. Rapid Revision

- **Pythagoras:** √(x²+y²) for shortest distance
- **Turns:** Right of North = East, Right of East = South
- **Net displacement:** Sum horizontal and vertical separately
- **Trap:** "Left/Right" depends on facing direction
