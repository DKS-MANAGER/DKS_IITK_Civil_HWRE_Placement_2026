## 2. Formula Sheet

### Clocks
- Angle between hands = `|30H - 5.5M|` (H=hours, M=minutes)
- Hands coincide: `60H/11` minutes past H
- Hands opposite: `60H/11 + 30` minutes past H
- Hands at right angle: `60H/11 ± 15` minutes past H

### Calendars
- Odd days: normal year = 1, leap year = 2
- 100 years = 5 odd days
- 200 years = 3 odd days
- 300 years = 1 odd day
- 400 years = 0 odd days
- Leap year: divisible by 4 (except centuries not divisible by 400)

## 3. Worked Examples

### Q1 (Medium)
**Q:** Angle between hands at 3:00?
**Solution:** |30×3 - 5.5×0| = 90°. **Answer:** 90°.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** Angle between hands at 4:30?
**Solution:** |30×4 - 5.5×30| = |120 - 165| = 45°. **Answer:** 45°.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** What day was 15 August 1947?
**Solution:** Count odd days from 1900 to 1947. 47 years = 47 + 11 leap = 58 odd days. 58 mod 7 = 2. From 1 Jan to 15 Aug: Jan(31)+Feb(28)+Mar(31)+Apr(30)+May(31)+Jun(30)+Jul(31)+15 = 227 days. 227 mod 7 = 3. Total odd days = 2+3 = 5. 1900 was a Friday (reference). 5 odd days from Friday = Wednesday. **Answer:** Friday.
