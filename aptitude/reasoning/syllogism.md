# Syllogism

> **Priority:** P1 · **Role relevance:** Important (Consulting, Analytics, Product)
> **Difficulty range:** Easy → Hard · **Time per question:** 30 sec – 2 min

---

## 1. Proposition Types

| Type | Statement | Venn Diagram |
|:-----|:----------|:-------------|
| A (Universal Affirmative) | All A are B | Circle A inside Circle B |
| E (Universal Negative) | No A is B | Circles A and B separated |
| I (Particular Affirmative) | Some A are B | Circles A and B intersect |
| O (Particular Negative) | Some A are not B | Part of A outside B |

---

## 2. Rules for Valid Deductions

### Definite Conclusions
A conclusion is **definitely true** only if it holds in **all possible Venn diagrams**.

### Possible Conclusions
A conclusion with "is a possibility" is true if it holds in **at least one valid diagram**.

### Either-Or Case
Two conclusions form "Either-Or" if:
1. Both are individually doubtful
2. Subject and predicate are identical
3. They form a complementary pair: Some+No or All+Some Not

## 3. Worked Examples

### Q1 (Easy)
**Statements:** All pens are pencils. Some pencils are erasers.
**Conclusions:** 1. Some pens are erasers. 2. No pen is an eraser.
**Solution:** Pens inside pencils. Some pencils overlap erasers. But pens may or may not overlap erasers. Neither conclusion is definite. However, they form an either-or pair (Some+No). **Answer:** Either 1 or 2 follows.

### Q2 (Medium)
**Statements:** No cat is a dog. All dogs are animals.
**Conclusions:** 1. No cat is an animal. 2. Some animals are not cats.
**Solution:** Cats and dogs separated. Dogs inside animals. Cats may or may not overlap animals. Conclusion 1 is not definite. Conclusion 2: Some animals (dogs) are not cats — definite. **Answer:** Only 2 follows.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Statements:** All A are B. Some B are C. No C is D.
**Conclusions:** 1. Some A are C. 2. No A is D. 3. Some B are not D.
**Solution:** A inside B. Some B overlaps C. C and D separated. Conclusion 1: A may or may not overlap C — not definite. Conclusion 2: A may or may not overlap D — not definite. Conclusion 3: Some B (those that are C) are not D — definite. **Answer:** Only 3 follows.

## 5. Placement Questions

### Q1 (Easy)
**Q:** Statements: All pens are pencils. Some pencils are erasers.
Conclusions: 1. Some pens are erasers. 2. No pen is an eraser.
- A) Only 1  B) Only 2  C) Either 1 or 2  D) Neither
**Answer:** C) Either 1 or 2. They form a complementary pair (Some+No).

### Q2 (Medium)
**Q:** Statements: No cat is a dog. All dogs are animals.
Conclusions: 1. No cat is an animal. 2. Some animals are not cats.
- A) Only 1  B) Only 2  C) Both  D) Neither
**Answer:** B) Only 2. Some animals (dogs) are not cats.

### Q3 (Hard)
**Q:** Statements: All A are B. Some B are C. No C is D.
Conclusions: 1. Some A are C. 2. No A is D. 3. Some B are not D.
- A) Only 1  B) Only 2  C) Only 3  D) All
**Answer:** C) Only 3. Some B (those that are C) are not D.

---

## 6. Rapid Revision

- **Definite conclusion:** True in ALL diagrams
- **Possible conclusion:** True in at least one diagram
- **Either-Or:** Some+No or All+Some Not (same subject/predicate)
- **Trap:** "Some" doesn't mean "not all" — it means "at least one"
