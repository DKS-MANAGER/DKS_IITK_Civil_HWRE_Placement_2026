# Input-Output

> **Priority:** P2 · **Role relevance:** Useful (all roles)
> **Difficulty range:** Medium → Hard · **Time per question:** 1 – 3 min

---

## 1. What You Need to Know

Input-Output problems present a sequence of steps that transform an input into an output. You must identify the pattern and predict the next step.

---

## 2. Common Patterns

1. **Sorting** — numbers/words rearranged in order
2. **Shifting** — elements moved step by step
3. **Reversing** — order reversed
4. **Combining** — numbers and words processed separately
5. **Position-based** — elements placed by position

---

## 3. Worked Examples

### Q1 (Medium)
**Input:** 42 17 85 23 56 91
**Step 1:** 17 42 85 23 56 91
**Step 2:** 17 23 85 42 56 91
**Step 3:** 17 23 42 85 56 91
**Step 4:** 17 23 42 56 85 91
**Step 5:** 17 23 42 56 85 91 (sorted ascending)

**Q:** What is Step 3?
**Answer:** 17 23 42 85 56 91.

### Q2 (Hard)
**Input:** apple mango banana cherry
**Step 1:** apple banana mango cherry
**Step 2:** apple banana cherry mango
**Step 3:** apple banana cherry mango (sorted alphabetically)

**Q:** How many steps to sort alphabetically?
**Answer:** 2 steps.

## 5. Placement Questions

### Q1 (Medium)
**Q:** Input: 42 17 85 23. Sort ascending. What is Step 2?
- A) 17 23 85 42  B) 17 42 85 23  C) 17 23 42 85  D) 42 17 23 85
**Answer:** B) 17 42 85 23. Step 1: 17 42 85 23. Step 2: 17 23 85 42.

### Q2 (Hard)
**Q:** Input: apple 12 mango 5 banana 8. Sort numbers ascending and words alphabetically. What is Step 3?
- A) apple banana 5 8 mango 12  B) apple 5 banana 8 mango 12  C) 5 apple 8 banana 12 mango  D) apple banana mango 5 8 12
**Answer:** B) apple 5 banana 8 mango 12.

---

## 6. Rapid Revision

- **Identify the pattern** (sort, shift, reverse)
- **Track each step** carefully
- **Trap:** Confusing ascending vs descending
