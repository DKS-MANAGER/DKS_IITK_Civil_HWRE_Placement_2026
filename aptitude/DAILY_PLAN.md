# ☀️ Daily Aptitude Plan

> A practical, repeatable daily session (~90 minutes) that builds all four aptitude areas plus timed practice and error analysis.

---

## The Daily Session (90 min)

| Block | Duration | Activity |
|:------|:--------:|:---------|
| 1 | 20 min | **Quant** — solve a topic set (15 Q) |
| 2 | 20 min | **Reasoning** — solve a topic set (15 Q) |
| 3 | 15 min | **Verbal** — RC passage + grammar (10 Q) |
| 4 | 15 min | **DI** — one data set (5–8 Q) |
| 5 | 20 min | **Timed mixed set** — 15 Q under time pressure |
| 6 | 10 min | **Error analysis** — log mistakes in [ERROR_LOG.md](ERROR_LOG.md) |

**Total: 100 min** (adjust to your schedule).

---

## Daily Rotation (Weekly Cycle)

Rotate topics so all P0 areas are covered each week.

| Day | Quant Focus | Reasoning Focus | Verbal Focus | DI Focus |
|:----|:------------|:----------------|:-------------|:---------|
| Mon | Percentages | Series | RC | Tables |
| Tue | Ratio & Proportion | Coding-Decoding | Grammar | Bar/Line |
| Wed | Averages & Mixtures | Seating Arrangement | Sentence Completion | Pie |
| Thu | Profit/Loss | Puzzles | Para Jumbles | Caselets |
| Fri | Time & Work | Syllogism | Vocabulary | Mixed |
| Sat | TSD | Blood Relations | Critical Reasoning | Mixed |
| Sun | **Full Mock + Error Analysis** | — | — | — |

---

## Timed Mixed Set (Block 5) — Format

- **15 questions** — 5 Quant, 5 Reasoning, 3 DI, 2 Verbal
- **Time:** 20 minutes (~80 sec/question)
- **Mix difficulties:** 6 easy, 6 medium, 3 hard
- **Goal:** practice question selection + time allocation

---

## Error Analysis (Block 6) — Method

For each wrong question, log:
1. **Error type** — Conceptual / Calculation / Misread / Formula / Time pressure / Guessing / Question selection
2. **Cause** — why did you get it wrong?
3. **Correct method** — the right approach
4. **Reattempt date** — 1/3/7 days later

---

## Weekly Targets

| Metric | Target |
|:-------|:-------|
| Questions solved | 100+ |
| Timed sets | 6 |
| Full mocks | 1 |
| Errors logged | All |
| Reattempts | All logged errors |
