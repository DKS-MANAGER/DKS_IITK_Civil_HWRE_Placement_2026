# Problems on Train

## Concept Definitions

Train problems are applications of the fundamental relationship between distance, speed, and time. The key insight is determining the correct total distance covered during a crossing event.

- **Crossing a pole/man**: The train covers a distance equal to its own length.
- **Crossing a platform/bridge/tunnel**: The train covers the sum of its own length and the platform length.
- **Two trains crossing each other**: The combined length of both trains is covered at the relative speed.

---

## Key Formulas & Shortcuts

### Speed Conversion
- **km/h to m/s**: Multiply by $\frac{5}{18}$.
- **m/s to km/h**: Multiply by $\frac{18}{5}$.

### Crossing a Stationary Object
$$\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Length of Train}}{\text{Speed of Train}}$$

### Crossing a Platform
$$\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Length of Train} + \text{Length of Platform}}{\text{Speed of Train}}$$

### Two Trains Crossing
$$\text{Time} = \frac{L_1 + L_2}{\text{Relative Speed}}$$

## Practice Problems

### Problem 1
A train runs at 54 km/h and passes a pole in 12 seconds. Find the length of the train.

### Problem 2
A train 200 m long runs at 60 km/h. Find the time to cross a bridge 300 m long.

### Problem 3
A train 180 m long passes a pole in 12 seconds, then crosses a bridge in 30 seconds. Find the bridge length.

### Problem 4
Two trains of lengths 120 m and 140 m run in opposite directions at 48 km/h and 30 km/h. Find the crossing time.

### Problem 5
A train running at 60 km/h crosses a 200 m platform in 27 seconds. What is the train's length?

---

## Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. Convert speed:
   $$54 \times \frac{5}{18} = 15 \text{ m/s}$$
2. Distance covered $= \text{Length of train}$.
   $$\text{Length} = 15 \times 12 = 180 \text{ m}$$
3. **Answer**: **180 meters**.

### Solution 2
1. Convert speed:
   $$60 \times \frac{5}{18} = \frac{50}{3} \text{ m/s}$$
2. Total distance $= 200 + 300 = 500$ m.
3. Time:
   $$\text{Time} = \frac{500}{50/3} = 500 \times \frac{3}{50} = 30 \text{ seconds}$$
4. **Answer**: **30 seconds**.

### Solution 3
1. Speed from pole crossing:
   $$\text{Speed} = \frac{180}{12} = 15 \text{ m/s}$$
2. Distance covered on bridge:
   $$15 \times 30 = 450 \text{ m}$$
3. Bridge length $= 450 - 180 = 270$ m.
4. **Answer**: **270 meters**.

### Solution 4
1. Total length $= 120 + 140 = 260$ m.
2. Relative speed (opposite directions):
   $$48 + 30 = 78 \text{ km/h} = 78 \times \frac{5}{18} = \frac{65}{3} \text{ m/s}$$
3. Crossing time:
   $$\text{Time} = \frac{260}{65/3} = 260 \times \frac{3}{65} = 12 \text{ seconds}$$
4. **Answer**: **12 seconds**.

### Solution 5
1. Convert speed:
   $$60 \times \frac{5}{18} = \frac{50}{3} \text{ m/s}$$
2. Total distance covered:
   $$\frac{50}{3} \times 27 = 50 \times 9 = 450 \text{ m}$$
3. Train length $= 450 - 200 = 250$ m.
4. **Answer**: **250 meters**.
