# Percentages

## Concept Definitions

- **Percentage**: A fraction with denominator 100, denoted by `%`.
- **Base Value**: The original number before any increase or decrease. It is always the denominator in a percentage change formula.
- **Percentage Change**: The relative change of a value expressed as a fraction of the original value.
- **Product Stability Rule**: When two quantities are multiplied to give a constant product, an increase in one corresponds to a proportional decrease in the other.

| 1/2 | 50% | 1/9 | 11.11% |
| 1/3 | 33.33% | 1/10 | 10% |
| 1/4 | 25% | 1/11 | 9.09% |

| 1/6 | 16.67% | 1/15 | 6.67% |
| 1/7 | 14.28% | 1/20 | 5% |
| 1/8 | 12.5% | 1/25 | 4% |

### Percentage Increase / Decrease
$$\text{Percentage Increase} = \frac{\text{Increase}}{\text{Original Value}} \times 100$$
$$\text{Percentage Decrease} = \frac{\text{Decrease}}{\text{Original Value}} \times 100$$

### Product Stability Rule
- If $A$ is $x\%$ more than $B$, then $B$ is less than $A$ by: $\frac{x}{100 + x} \times 100\%$
- If $A$ is $x\%$ less than $B$, then $B$ is more than $A$ by: $\frac{x}{100 - x} \times 100\%$

### Successive Percentage Change
$$\text{Net Change} = \left( a + b + \frac{a \times b}{100} \right)\%$$
Use positive for increases, negative for decreases.

### Population / Depreciation
$$P_n = P \left( 1 \pm \frac{R}{100} \right)^n$$
Use $+$ for growth, $-$ for decay.

### Shortcut: Percentage to Fraction Quick Reference
- $37.5\% = 3/8$, $62.5\% = 5/8$, $87.5\% = 7/8$
- $66.67\% = 2/3$, $14.28\% = 1/7$, $12.5\% = 1/8$

## Practice Problems (10 Problems)

### Problem 1
If the price of petrol increases by 25%, by what percentage must consumption be reduced to keep expenditure unchanged?

### Problem 2
A man spends 75% of his income. His income rises by 20% and his expenditure rises by 15%. By what percentage do his savings increase?

### Problem 3
The length of a rectangle increases by 20% and its breadth decreases by 10%. Find the net percentage change in area.

### Problem 4
The population of a town grows at 10% per annum. If the present population is 20,000, find the population 2 years hence.

### Problem 5
Due to a 20% reduction in the price of sugar, a person buys 5 kg more sugar for Rs. 600. Find the original price per kg.

### Problem 6
A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. What is the net change?

### Problem 7
In an election, a candidate gets 60% of the votes and wins by 1200 votes. Find the total number of votes polled.

### Problem 8
A's salary is 25% more than B's. By what percentage is B's salary less than A's?

### Problem 9
The price of a commodity is increased by 30%. By what percentage should consumption be reduced so that expenditure increases by only 10%?

### Problem 10
A student scores 30% and fails by 15 marks. Another scores 40% and gets 25 marks more than passing marks. Find the maximum marks.

## Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. Price increases by $x = 25\%$.
2. Product stability rule: $\text{Reduction} = \frac{25}{100 + 25} \times 100 = \frac{25}{125} \times 100 = 20\%$
3. **Answer**: **20% reduction**.

### Solution 2
1. Assume initial income $= 100$. Expenditure $= 75$. Savings $= 25$.
2. New income $= 120$. New expenditure $= 75 + 15\% \text{ of } 75 = 86.25$.
3. New savings $= 120 - 86.25 = 33.75$. Increase $= 8.75$.
4. Percentage increase $= \frac{8.75}{25} \times 100 = 35\%$
5. **Answer**: **35%**.

### Solution 3
1. Length change $a = +20\%$, breadth change $b = -10\%$.
2. Net change $= 20 - 10 + \frac{20 \times (-10)}{100} = 10 - 2 = 8\%$
3. **Answer**: **8% increase**.

### Solution 4
1. $P = 20,000$, $R = 10\%$, $n = 2$.
2. $P_2 = 20,000 \times (1.1)^2 = 20,000 \times 1.21 = 24,200$
3. **Answer**: **24,200**.

### Solution 5
1. Let original price $= X$ per kg.
2. Original quantity $= 600/X$. New price $= 0.8X$. New quantity $= 750/X$.
3. Difference: $750/X - 600/X = 5 \implies 150/X = 5 \implies X = 30$
4. **Answer**: **Rs. 30 per kg**.

### Solution 6
1. Net change $= 20 - 20 + \frac{20 \times (-20)}{100} = -4\%$
2. **Answer**: **4% decrease**.

### Solution 7
1. Winner gets 60%, loser gets 40%. Difference = 20% = 1200 votes.
2. Total votes $= 1200 / 0.20 = 6000$
3. **Answer**: **6000 votes**.

### Solution 8
1. $A = 1.25B$. B is less than A by $\frac{25}{125} \times 100 = 20\%$
2. **Answer**: **20%**.

### Solution 9
1. Let original consumption $= C$, original price $= P$. Expenditure $= PC$.
2. New price $= 1.3P$, new expenditure $= 1.1PC$.
3. New consumption $= 1.1PC / 1.3P = 0.846C$.
4. Reduction $= 1 - 0.846 = 0.154 = 15.4\%$
5. **Answer**: **15.4% reduction**.

### Solution 10
1. Let max marks $= M$. Passing marks $= 0.3M + 15 = 0.4M - 25$
2. $0.1M = 40 \implies M = 400$
3. **Answer**: **400 marks**.
