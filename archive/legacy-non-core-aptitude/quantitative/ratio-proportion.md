# Ratio & Proportion

## Concept Definitions

- **Ratio**: Comparison of two quantities of the same kind. $a:b$ or $\frac{a}{b}$.
- **Proportion**: Equality of two ratios. $a:b = c:d$ or $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}$.
- **Direct Proportion**: $x \propto y$ means $x = ky$.
- **Inverse Proportion**: $x \propto \frac{1}{y}$ means $xy = k$.

---

## Key Formulas & Shortcuts

### Basic Properties
| Property | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| Cross multiplication | $a:b = c:d \implies ad = bc$ |
| Componendo | $\frac{a+b}{b} = \frac{c+d}{d}$ |
| Dividendo | $\frac{a-b}{b} = \frac{c-d}{d}$ |
| Componendo-Dividendo | $\frac{a+b}{a-b} = \frac{c+d}{c-d}$ |
| Invertendo | $b:a = d:c$ |
| Alternendo | $a:c = b:d$ |

### Mean Proportional
If $a:x = x:b$, then $x = \sqrt{ab}$ (geometric mean)

### Third Proportional
If $a:b = b:c$, then $c = \frac{b^2}{a}$

### Fourth Proportional
If $a:b = c:d$, then $d = \frac{bc}{a}$

### Partnership
$$\text{Profit share} \propto \text{Capital} \times \text{Time}$$
$$\frac{P_1}{P_2} = \frac{C_1 \times T_1}{C_2 \times T_2}$$

### Alligation (Mixture)
$$\frac{\text{Qty of Cheaper}}{\text{Qty of Dearer}} = \frac{D - M}{M - C}$$

## Practice Problems (10 Problems)

If $x:y = 2:3$ and $y:z = 4:5$, find $x:z$.

### Problem 7
A mixture contains alcohol and water in the ratio 4:3. If 5 liters of water is added, the ratio becomes 4:5. Find the quantity of alcohol.

### Problem 9

Two numbers are in the ratio 3:4. If 8 is subtracted from each, the new ratio becomes 1:2. Find the numbers.

## Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. $a:b = 3:4 = 15:20$ (multiply by 5)
2. $b:c = 5:6 = 20:24$ (multiply by 4)
3. $a:b:c = 15:20:24$
4. **Answer**: **15:20:24**

### Solution 2
1. Total parts $= 2+3+5 = 10$
2. First share $= 1200 \times 2/10 = 240$
3. Second share $= 1200 \times 3/10 = 360$
4. Third share $= 1200 \times 5/10 = 600$
5. **Answer**: **Rs. 240, Rs. 360, Rs. 600**

### Solution 3
1. A: $50000 \times 12 = 600000$
2. B: $75000 \times 12 = 900000$
3. C: $100000 \times 6 = 600000$
4. Ratio $= 600000:900000:600000 = 2:3:2$
5. **Answer**: **2:3:2**

### Solution 4
1. Water cost $= 0$, Milk cost $= 40$, Mean $= 32$
2. Alligation: $\frac{\text{Water}}{\text{Milk}} = \frac{40-32}{32-0} = \frac{8}{32} = \frac{1}{4}$
6. **Answer**: **1:4**

### Solution 5
1. Let boys $= 3x$, girls $= 5x$.
2. After 10 girls: $\frac{3x}{5x+10} = \frac{1}{2}$
3. $6x = 5x + 10 \implies x = 10$
4. Boys $= 3 \times 10 = 30$
5. **Answer**: **30 boys**

### Solution 6
1. $x:y = 2:3$, $y:z = 4:5$
2. Make $y$ common: $x:y = 8:12$, $y:z = 12:15$
3. $x:z = 8:15$
4. **Answer**: **8:15**

### Solution 7
1. Let alcohol $= 4x$, water $= 3x$.
2. After 5L water: $\frac{4x}{3x+5} = \frac{4}{5}$
3. $20x = 12x + 20 \implies 8x = 20 \implies x = 2.5$
4. Alcohol $= 4 \times 2.5 = 10$ liters
5. **Answer**: **10 liters**

### Solution 8
1. Old salaries: $2x, 3x, 5x$
2. New: $2x \times 1.15 = 2.3x$, $3x \times 1.10 = 3.3x$, $5x \times 1.20 = 6x$
3. Ratio $= 2.3:3.3:6 = 23:33:60$
4. **Answer**: **23:33:60**

### Solution 9
1. Let total profit $= P$. Charity $= 0.05P$. Remaining $= 0.95P$.
2. A's share $= \frac{3}{5} \times 0.95P = 855$
3. $0.57P = 855 \implies P = 1500$
4. **Answer**: **Rs. 1500**

### Solution 10
1. Let numbers $= 3x, 4x$.
2. $\frac{3x-8}{4x-8} = \frac{1}{2}$
3. $6x - 16 = 4x - 8 \implies 2x = 8 \implies x = 4$
5. Numbers $= 12, 16$
6. **Answer**: **12 and 16**
