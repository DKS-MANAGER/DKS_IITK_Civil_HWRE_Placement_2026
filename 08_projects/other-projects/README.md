# Project: GIS Catchment Modeling, EPANET Water Networks & Civil Lab Optimization

> **Project Code**: `other-projects`  
> **Domain**: Hydroinformatics, Water Supply Engineering, Geospatial Analysis & Materials

---

## 1. Sub-Project 1: Automated Watershed Delineation & Morphometric Analysis
* **Stack**: QGIS, Python (GDAL / PyQGIS), SRTM 30m DEM
* **Summary**: Automated digital elevation model (DEM) pit-filling, D8 flow direction calculation, stream order extraction (Strahler method), and morphometric parameter computation (bifurcation ratio, drainage density, relief ratio) across a $1,200\text{ km}^2$ river basin in Uttar Pradesh.
* **Key Outcome**: Reduced manual GIS pre-processing time from 6 hours to 12 minutes using an end-to-end Python pipeline.

---

## 2. Sub-Project 2: Municipal Water Distribution Network Optimization
* **Stack**: EPANET 2.2, Python (WNTR library)
* **Summary**: Hydraulic head loss and pressure optimization for a 45-node municipal piped water supply network under peak demand diurnal curve variations. Evaluated Hazen-Williams friction coefficients and valve throttling positions to eliminate negative pressure cavitation nodes.
* **Key Outcome**: Maintained residual pressure $>12\text{ m}$ at all consumer nodes while reducing pumping power consumption by $15\%$.

---

## 3. Sub-Project 3: Sustainable Concrete Mix Optimization with Supplementary Cementitious Materials (SCM)
* **Stack**: IS 10262 Mix Design, Excel, Python Regression
* **Summary**: Laboratory experimental evaluation of compressive and split-tensile strength of M30 concrete with partial cement replacement using fly-ash ($25\%$) and silica fume ($8\%$).
* **Key Outcome**: Achieved target 28-day strength ($38.5\text{ MPa}$) with $24\%$ lower embodied carbon footprint and $14\%$ material cost reduction per cubic meter.
