## How to Test Effectively

1. **Diagnose** — take a topic test to find weak areas.
2. **Study** — review the weak topic in your track → [TRACKS.md](TRACKS.md).
3. **Retest** — take the same topic test again.
4. **Escalate** — move up the ladder as you improve.
5. **Simulate** — do full mocks under timed conditions.
