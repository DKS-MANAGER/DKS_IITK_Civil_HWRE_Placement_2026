# Behavioural & HR Guide

> **Preparation for behavioural and HR interview rounds.** Focus on structured answers, self-awareness, and company fit.

---

## Why Behavioural Rounds Matter

Behavioural rounds assess **how you think, communicate, and fit** — not just what you know. They are often the deciding factor between equally technical candidates.

---

## The STAR Method

Structure every behavioural answer:

| Letter | Meaning | Example |
|:-------|:--------|:--------|
| **S** | Situation | Set the context |
| **T** | Task | What was your responsibility? |
| **A** | Action | What did you actually do? |
| **R** | Result | What was the outcome? |

---

## Common Behavioural Questions

- Tell me about yourself.
- Describe a time you led a team.
- Describe a conflict you resolved.
- Tell me about a failure and what you learned.
- Why do you want this role / company?
- Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

Full question bank → [`prep/behavioral/`](../prep/behavioral/)

## HR Round Preparation

- Know your resume inside out.
- Prepare a 60-second and 2-minute self-introduction.
- Prepare answers for salary / CTC expectations.
- Prepare questions to ask the interviewer.

## Practice Loop

1. Pick a question from the bank.
2. Write a STAR answer.
3. Practice aloud (record yourself).
4. Get feedback.
5. Refine and repeat.
