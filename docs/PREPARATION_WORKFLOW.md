# Preparation Workflow

> **The full end-to-end workflow** of the placement preparation system. Visual overview of how all pieces fit together.

---

## The Six-Stage Workflow

```
 01                02              03                04               05              06
UNDERSTAND    →   LEARN       →   PRACTICE     →   STRATEGIZE   →   INTERVIEW   →   PLACEMENT
                 │                │                │                │                │
Who am I?        Core subjects    Mock interviews  Company-wise    Execute          Postmortem
What roles?      & non-core       & question banks  prep & CTC     & log results    & iterate
```

| Stage | What | Where |
|:------|:-----|:------|
| **01 Understand** | Identify your career track | [GETTING_STARTED.md](GETTING_STARTED.md) · [TRACKS.md](TRACKS.md) · [ROLES.md](ROLES.md) |
| **02 Learn** | Core subjects, software, non-core | [`core/`](../core/) · [`software-and-tech/`](../software-and-tech/) · [`non-core/`](../non-core/) |
| **03 Practice** | Questions, mocks, aptitude | [TESTING_GUIDE.md](TESTING_GUIDE.md) · [`questions/README.md`](../questions/README.md) |
| **04 Strategize** | Company-wise prep | [COMPANIES.md](COMPANIES.md) |
| **05 Interview** | Execution & survival | [INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md](INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md) · [BEHAVIOURAL_HR_GUIDE.md](BEHAVIOURAL_HR_GUIDE.md) |
| **06 Placement** | Log, review, iterate | [`placement-control-panel.md`](placement-control-panel.md) |

## Detailed Workflow

### Stage 01 — Understand

1. Read [GETTING_STARTED.md](GETTING_STARTED.md).
2. Choose a track → [TRACKS.md](TRACKS.md).
3. Choose a role → [ROLES.md](ROLES.md).

### Stage 02 — Learn

1. Study your track's core subjects.
2. Learn relevant software → [`software-and-tech/README.md`](../software-and-tech/README.md).
3. Follow the [roadmap.md](roadmap.md) timeline.

### Stage 03 — Practice

1. Take topic tests to diagnose weak areas → [TESTING_GUIDE.md](TESTING_GUIDE.md).
2. Practice question banks → [`questions/README.md`](../questions/README.md).
3. Do aptitude → [`non-core/aptitude/`](../non-core/aptitude/).

### Stage 04 — Strategize

1. Review company profiles → [COMPANIES.md](COMPANIES.md).
2. Map companies to roles → [ROLES.md](ROLES.md).
3. Prepare company-specific answers.

### Stage 05 — Interview

1. Execute with the interview system → [INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md](INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md).
2. Handle behavioural rounds → [BEHAVIOURAL_HR_GUIDE.md](BEHAVIOURAL_HR_GUIDE.md).
3. Use the interview day survival guide → [`prep/interview/interview-day-survival.md`](../prep/interview/interview-day-survival.md).

### Stage 06 — Placement

1. Log interview experiences → [COMPANIES.md](COMPANIES.md).
2. Review and iterate → [`placement-control-panel.md`](placement-control-panel.md).

## Time-Based Variants

| Time | Workflow |
|:-----|:---------|
| 90 days | Full cycle (all 6 stages) |
| 30 days | Compressed (stages 02–05) |
| 7 days | Sprint (stages 03–05) |
| 1 day | Crash (stage 05 only) |
