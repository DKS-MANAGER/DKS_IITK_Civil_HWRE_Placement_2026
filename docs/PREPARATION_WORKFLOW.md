## Detailed Workflow

### Stage 01 — Understand
1. Read [GETTING_STARTED.md](GETTING_STARTED.md) for initial orientation.
2. Review target corporate sectors in [IITK_PLACEMENT_MAP.md](IITK_PLACEMENT_MAP.md).
3. Map target roles to required competencies → [ROLES.md](ROLES.md) and [TRACKS.md](TRACKS.md).

### Stage 02 — Learn
1. Master core engineering and water resources curriculum in [`core/`](../core/) and [`core/hwre/`](../core/hwre/).
2. Acquire essential computational tools (Python, OpenFOAM, GIS) → [`software-and-tech/README.md`](../software-and-tech/README.md).
3. Build business and unit economics literacy → [`non-core/common/business-fundamentals.md`](../non-core/common/business-fundamentals.md).

### Stage 03 — Practice (Assessment Ladder)
1. **Level 1 (Diagnose):** Take 15-question topic tests to isolate weak modules → [`aptitude/tests/`](../aptitude/tests/README.md).
2. **Level 2 (Speed Pacing):** Attempt 30–40 minute pure domain sectional tests → [`aptitude/tests/section/`](../aptitude/tests/section/README.md).
3. **Level 3 & 4 (Simulation):** Take 60-minute Full Placement Mocks (M01–M05, Hard 01, Expert 01) → [`aptitude/mocks/`](../aptitude/mocks/README.md).
4. **Remediate:** Drill underlying question modules across 720 Quant questions, 10 DI chapters, and 9 Verbal modules in [`aptitude/`](../aptitude/).

### Stage 04 — Strategize
1. Review 2025–26 IITK placement data, compensation tiers, and eligibility criteria in [`resources/placement-data.md`](../resources/placement-data.md).
2. Deep-dive into company-specific dossiers and test patterns in [`prep/company-profiles/`](../prep/company-profiles/).
3. Align resume talking points and claim defenses in [`prep/RESUME/RESUME_DEFENSE.md`](../prep/RESUME/RESUME_DEFENSE.md).

### Stage 05 — Interview
1. Master the 10 branching technical interview question trees in [`prep/interview/technical/technical-interview-bank.md`](../prep/interview/technical/technical-interview-bank.md).
2. Conduct interactive case simulations with exhibits and pushbacks → [`non-core/case-interviews/case-simulation-suite.md`](../non-core/case-interviews/case-simulation-suite.md).
3. Prepare the 10 STAR-L behavioral competency archetypes → [BEHAVIOURAL_HR_GUIDE.md](BEHAVIOURAL_HR_GUIDE.md).
4. Execute day-of-interview triage protocols → [`prep/interview/interview-day-survival.md`](../prep/interview/interview-day-survival.md).

### Stage 06 — Placement & Retesting Loop
1. Track objective performance velocity across mocks and interviews in [`prep/READINESS_SCORECARD.md`](../prep/READINESS_SCORECARD.md).
2. Classify test errors into Type C (Concept), Type A (Algebra), Type T (Timing), or Type M (Misread).
3. Execute the closed-loop Retest Protocol: Re-solve Level 4–6 caselets and retest with unseen diagnostic tests.
