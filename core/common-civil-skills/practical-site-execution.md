# Practical Site Execution

> Track: Core Civil Professional Skills  
> Domain: Field Engineering & Construction Execution  
> Level: Advanced Placement / Professional Execution  

---

## 1. Overview & Fundamentals

Practical site execution is the art of translating design drawings into physical structures safely, accurately, and economically. It involves managing labor, materials, equipment, and subcontractors while adhering to quality standards and project schedules.

---

## 2. Key Execution Domains

### A. Formwork & Scaffolding
- **Formwork:** Must be rigid, watertight, and easily removable. Release agents (shuttering oil) are essential for smooth finish and easy stripping.
- **Stripping Time (IS 456):**
  - Vertical formwork (walls, columns, beam sides): 16–24 hours.
  - Soffit formwork (slabs): 3 days.
  - Soffit formwork (beams): 7 days.
  - Props (slabs up to 4.5m span): 7 days.
  - Props (beams/arches up to 6m span): 14 days.

### B. Concreting Operations
- **Batching:** Weigh-batching is mandatory for structural concrete.
- **Transporting:** Avoid segregation. Use transit mixers for long distances.
- **Placing:** Place concrete as close to its final position as possible.
- **Compaction:** Use mechanical vibrators (needle/immersion vibrators). Do not over-vibrate (causes segregation) or under-vibrate (causes honeycombing).
- **Curing:** Minimum 7–10 days for OPC, 10–14 days for concrete with mineral admixtures (fly ash/GGBS).

### C. Surveying & Layout
- **Total Station:** Primary tool for high-precision layout, leveling, and coordinate mapping.
- **Auto Level:** Used for transferring levels and checking floor heights.
- **Reference Points:** Establish permanent Benchmarks (BM) and Grid Lines (X-Y axes) before starting any excavation.

---

## 3. Site Management Best Practices

- **Daily Progress Report (DPR):** Record labor count, material consumption, equipment hours, and work completed daily.
- **Material Reconciliation:** Track theoretical consumption vs. actual consumption to control wastage.
- **Subcontractor Management:** Clear scope of work, timely work permits, and regular progress reviews.
