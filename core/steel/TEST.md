## Section A — Materials & Limit State Design (Q1–Q5)

**Q1.** The partial safety factor for steel yielding ($\gamma_{m0}$) in IS 800:2007 is:
- (a) 1.10
- (b) 1.15
- (c) 1.25
- (d) 1.50

**Q2.** The modulus of elasticity of steel is:
- (a) $1 \times 10^5$ N/mm²
- (b) $2 \times 10^5$ N/mm²
- (c) $2.5 \times 10^5$ N/mm²
- (d) $5 \times 10^5$ N/mm²

**Q3.** The minimum pitch of bolts is:
- (a) 1.5d
- (b) 2.0d
- (c) 2.5d
- (d) 3.0d

**Q4.** The maximum slenderness ratio for a compression member (DL + IL loads) is:
- (a) 180
- (b) 250
- (c) 300
- (d) 400

**Q5.** Which of the following is a serviceability limit state in steel design?
- (a) Yielding
- (b) Buckling
- (c) Deflection
- (d) Plastic collapse

## Section B — Bolted & Welded Connections (Q6–Q10)

**Q6.** The design shear strength of a bolt is:
- (a) $V_{dsb} = \frac{f_{ub}}{\sqrt{3} \gamma_{mb}} (n_n A_{nb} + n_s A_{sb})$
- (b) $V_{dsb} = \frac{f_{ub}}{\gamma_{mb}} (n_n A_{nb})$
- (c) $V_{dsb} = \frac{f_{ub}}{\sqrt{3}} (A_{sb})$
- (d) $V_{dsb} = \frac{f_{ub} A_{sb}}{\sqrt{3} \gamma_{m0}}$

**Q7.** HSFG bolts transfer load by:
- (a) Bearing against the hole
- (b) Friction between the plates
- (c) Shear of the shank
- (d) Tension in the bolt

**Q8.** The effective throat thickness of a fillet weld is:
- (a) $t_t = K \times s$
- (b) $t_t = s$
- (c) $t_t = 0.5 \times s$
- (d) $t_t = 2 \times s$

**Q9.** The effective length of a fillet weld is:
- (a) Overall length
- (b) Overall length − 2s
- (c) Overall length − s
- (d) Overall length + 2s

**Q10.** The partial safety factor for shop welds ($\gamma_{mw}$) is:
- (a) 1.10
- (b) 1.25
- (c) 1.50
- (d) 1.15

## Section C — Tension & Compression Members (Q11–Q13)

**Q11.** The design strength of a tension member for gross section yielding is:
- (a) $T_{dg} = A_g f_y/\gamma_{m0}$
- (b) $T_{dg} = A_n f_y/\gamma_{m0}$
- (c) $T_{dg} = 0.9 A_n f_u/\gamma_{m1}$
- (d) $T_{dg} = A_g f_u/\gamma_{m1}$

**Q12.** The design compressive strength of a column is:
- (a) $P_d = A_e f_{cd}$
- (b) $P_d = A_g f_y$
- (c) $P_d = A_e f_y/\gamma_{m1}$
- (d) $P_d = A_n f_{cd}$

**Q13.** The effective length of a column with both ends fixed is:
- (a) 0.65L
- (b) 0.8L
- (c) 1.0L
- (d) 2.0L

**Q15.** The deflection limit for a cantilever beam (elastic cladding) is:

## 📋 Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Explanation |
|:-:|:------:|-------------|
| 1 | (a) | $\gamma_{m0} = 1.10$ for yielding/buckling |
| 2 | (b) | $E = 2 \times 10^5$ N/mm² |
| 3 | (c) | Min pitch = 2.5d |
| 4 | (a) | Compression (DL+IL): 180 |
| 5 | (c) | Deflection is a serviceability limit state |
| 6 | (a) | $V_{dsb} = f_{ub}(n_n A_{nb} + n_s A_{sb})/(\sqrt{3}\gamma_{mb})$ |
| 7 | (b) | HSFG transfers by friction (pre-tensioned clamping) |
| 8 | (a) | $t_t = K \times s$ (K = 0.70 for 60–90°) |
| 9 | (b) | $L_w$ = overall − 2s |
| 10 | (b) | Shop weld: 1.25, field weld: 1.50 |
| 11 | (a) | Gross yielding: $T_{dg} = A_g f_y/\gamma_{m0}$ |
| 12 | (a) | $P_d = A_e f_{cd}$ |
| 13 | (a) | Both ends fixed: 0.65L |
| 14 | (b) | LTB: lateral buckling of compression flange + twist |
| 15 | (c) | Cantilever: Span/240 |
