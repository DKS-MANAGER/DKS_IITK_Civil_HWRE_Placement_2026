## Section A — Soil Mechanics (Q1–Q5)

**Q1.** The relationship $Se = wG_s$ is called:
- (a) Darcy's law
- (b) Terzaghi's consolidation
- (c) Phase relationship
- (d) Mohr-Coulomb criterion

**Q2.** In a falling head permeability test, the formula involves:
- (a) Natural logarithm of head ratio
- (b) Common logarithm of head ratio
- (c) Linear head difference
- (d) Square root of head ratio

**Q3.** 50% consolidation corresponds to a time factor $T_v$ of:
- (a) 0.197
- (b) 0.2
- (c) 0.5
- (d) 0.848

**Q4.** The Mohr-Coulomb shear strength envelope for undrained saturated clay ($\phi_u = 0$ condition) is:
- (a) $\tau_f = \sigma \tan\phi'$
- (b) $\tau_f = c_u$
- (c) $\tau_f = c' + \sigma'\tan\phi'$
- (d) $\tau_f = \gamma H$

**Q5.** For double drainage, the drainage path is:
- (a) $H$
- (b) $H/2$
- (c) $2H$
- (d) $H/4$

## Section B — Foundations & Earth Pressure (Q6–Q10)

**Q6.** The Terzaghi bearing capacity equation for a strip footing is:
- (a) $q_u = cN_c + qN_q + 0.5\gamma BN_\gamma$
- (b) $q_u = cN_c + 0.5\gamma BN_\gamma$
- (c) $q_u = qN_q + 0.5\gamma BN_\gamma$
- (d) $q_u = cN_c + qN_q$

**Q7.** Rankine active earth pressure coefficient $K_a$ for $\phi = 30°$ is:
- (a) 0.333
- (b) 0.500
- (c) 0.750
- (d) 3.000

**Q8.** The safe bearing pressure is defined as:
- (a) $q_u$
- (b) $q_{nu}/F$
- (c) $q_{nu}/F + \gamma D_f$
- (d) $q_{nu} + \gamma D_f$

**Q9.** In pile design, the factor of safety for static formula is typically:
- (a) 1.5
- (b) 2.0
- (c) 2.5
- (d) 3.5

**Q10.** Overconsolidated clay has:
- (a) Current stress = max past stress
- (b) Current stress < max past stress
- (c) Current stress > max past stress
- (d) Zero past stress

## 📋 Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Explanation |
|:-:|:------:|-------------|
| 1 | (c) | $Se = wG_s$ is a phase relationship |
| 2 | (a) | $k = (aL/At)\ln(h_1/h_2)$ — natural logarithm |
| 3 | (b) | $T_v = 0.2$ for 50% consolidation |
| 4 | (b) | For saturated clay, $\tau_f = c_u$ (undrained, $\phi_u = 0$) |
| 5 | (b) | Double drainage: $H_{dr} = H/2$ |
| 6 | (a) | Terzaghi: $q_u = cN_c + qN_q + 0.5\gamma BN_\gamma$ |
| 7 | (a) | $K_a = \tan^2(45°-15°) = 0.333$ |
| 8 | (c) | $q_s = q_{nu}/F + \gamma D_f$ |
| 9 | (c) | Static formula: FOS = 2.5 |
| 10 | (b) | OC: current stress < max past stress |
