# GATE Civil — 30 / 60 / 90-Day Plan

> Realistic preparation plans. Assumes **4–5 study hours/day** (not 10–12). Adjust based on your daily availability.

## 90-Day Plan (Full Preparation)

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Days 1–30)

| Days | Subject | Daily Target | PYQ Target |
| ---- | ------- | ------------ | ---------- |
| 1–7 | Engineering Mathematics | 1 topic/day + 10 problems | 5 PYQs/day |
| 8–12 | Engineering Mechanics | 1 topic/day + 8 problems | 5 PYQs/day |
| 13–18 | Strength of Materials | 1 topic/day + 8 problems | 5 PYQs/day |
| 19–24 | Structural Analysis | 1 topic/day + 8 problems | 5 PYQs/day |
| 25–30 | Fluid Mechanics | 1 topic/day + 8 problems | 5 PYQs/day |

**Daily structure (4.5 hr):**
- 1.5 hr: Learn (notes + concepts)
- 1 hr: Formulas (memorize + apply)
- 1.5 hr: Practice (10–15 questions)
- 0.5 hr: Error log + revision

### Phase 2 — Building (Days 31–60)

| Days | Subject | Daily Target | PYQ Target |
| ---- | ------- | ------------ | ---------- |
| 31–36 | RCC | 1 topic/day + 8 problems | 5 PYQs/day |
| 37–42 | Steel | 1 topic/day + 8 problems | 5 PYQs/day |
| 43–48 | Geotechnical | 1 topic/day + 8 problems | 5 PYQs/day |
| 49–54 | Hydraulics + Hydrology | 1 topic/day + 8 problems | 5 PYQs/day |
| 55–60 | Environmental | 1 topic/day + 8 problems | 5 PYQs/day |

**Add:** 1 full mock test per week (Sunday), 2 hr analysis after each.

## Realistic Expectations

- 4–5 hr/day is sustainable for 90 days.
- If you have only 2–3 hr/day, use the 90-day plan but extend Phase 1 by 10 days.
- Never skip error analysis — it is the highest-ROI activity.
