## SECTION A: General Aptitude (10 Q)

**Q1.** A price increased by 20% then decreased by 20%. Net change:
(A) 4% increase (B) 4% decrease (C) No change (D) 2% decrease

**Q2.** Two trains 100 m and 120 m long run at 60 km/h and 40 km/h in opposite directions. Time to cross:
(A) 7.9 s (B) 8.5 s (C) 9.2 s (D) 10.1 s

**Q3.** A and B together can do a work in 12 days, B alone in 20 days. A alone:
(A) 25 days (B) 30 days (C) 32 days (D) 40 days

**Q4.** If 12 men can do a work in 8 days, 16 men take:
(A) 5 days (B) 6 days (C) 7 days (D) 10.7 days

**Q5.** A shopkeeper sells at 10% profit. If cost is ₹500, selling price:
(A) ₹550 (B) ₹545 (C) ₹555 (D) ₹560

**Q6.** Average of 5 numbers is 20. If one number 30 is removed, new average:
(A) 15 (B) 16 (C) 17.5 (D) 18

**Q7.** A number when divided by 7 leaves remainder 3. When divided by 7 again after adding 4, remainder:
(A) 0 (B) 1 (C) 2 (D) 3

**Q8.** Probability of getting at least one head in 3 coin tosses:
(A) 1/8 (B) 3/8 (C) 5/8 (D) 7/8

**Q9.** If x:y = 3:4 and y:z = 5:6, then x:z:
(A) 5:8 (B) 15:24 (C) 3:6 (D) 4:5

**Q10.** A train 150 m long crosses a pole in 9 s. Speed:
(A) 60 km/h (B) 65 km/h (C) 54 km/h (D) 72 km/h

### Section A

**Q1.** Net = 20 + (−20) + (20×−20)/100 = −4%. **(B)**

**Q2.** Relative speed = 60+40 = 100 km/h = 27.78 m/s. Time = (100+120)/27.78 = 7.92 s. **(A)**

**Q3.** A+B = 1/12, B = 1/20. A = 1/12 − 1/20 = 2/60 = 1/30 → 30 days. **(B)**

**Q4.** 12×8 = 16×t → t = 6 days. **(B)**

**Q5.** SP = 500 × 1.10 = ₹550. **(A)**
