## Section A — Concrete Properties & Design Philosophy (Q1–Q5)

**Q1.** The characteristic strength of concrete ($f_{ck}$) is defined as the strength below which not more than ___ of test cubes fall:
- (a) 1%
- (b) 5%
- (c) 10%
- (d) 15%

**Q2.** The short-term modulus of elasticity of concrete is:
- (a) $E_c = 5000\sqrt{f_{ck}}$
- (b) $E_c = 500\sqrt{f_{ck}}$
- (c) $E_c = 5000 f_{ck}$
- (d) $E_c = 5700\sqrt{f_{ck}}$

**Q3.** The partial safety factor for concrete ($\gamma_m$) in limit state design is:
- (a) 1.10
- (b) 1.15
- (c) 1.25
- (d) 1.50

**Q4.** The maximum strain in concrete at the outermost compression fibre in flexure is:
- (a) 0.002
- (b) 0.0035
- (c) 0.005
- (d) 0.01

**Q5.** Which of the following is NOT a limit state of collapse?
- (a) Flexure
- (b) Compression
- (c) Deflection
- (d) Torsion

## Section B — Flexural Design (Q6–Q10)

**Q6.** The limiting depth of neutral axis ratio ($x_{u,max}/d$) for Fe415 steel is:
- (a) 0.46
- (b) 0.48
- (c) 0.53
- (d) 0.60

**Q7.** The moment of resistance of a balanced Fe415 section is:
- (a) $M_{u,lim} = 0.138 f_{ck} b d^2$
- (b) $M_{u,lim} = 0.148 f_{ck} b d^2$
- (c) $M_{u,lim} = 0.36 f_{ck} b d^2$
- (d) $M_{u,lim} = 0.87 f_{ck} b d^2$

**Q8.** An over-reinforced section fails by:
- (a) Yielding of steel first (ductile)
- (b) Crushing of concrete first (brittle)
- (c) Simultaneous failure of steel and concrete
- (d) Shear failure

**Q9.** The minimum tension steel in a beam is:
- (a) $A_{st,min} = 0.85bd/f_y$
- (b) $A_{st,min} = 0.12\%$ of $bD$
- (c) $A_{st,min} = 0.8\%$ of $bD$
- (d) $A_{st,min} = 4\%$ of $bD$

**Q10.** Doubly reinforced sections are used when:
- (a) The section is under-reinforced
- (b) Depth and breadth are restricted
- (c) Shear is critical
- (d) The span is short

## Section C — Shear, Bond & Columns (Q11–Q13)

**Q11.** The nominal shear stress in a beam is:
- (a) $\tau_v = V_u/bd$
- (b) $\tau_v = V_u/bD$
- (c) $\tau_v = M_u/bd$
- (d) $\tau_v = V_u/d^2$

**Q12.** The development length of a bar is:
- (a) $L_d = 0.87 f_y \phi / 4\tau_{bd}$
- (b) $L_d = 0.87 f_y \phi / \tau_{bd}$
- (c) $L_d = f_y \phi / 4\tau_{bd}$
- (d) $L_d = 0.87 f_y / 4\tau_{bd}$

**Q13.** A column is classified as short if:
- (a) $l_{eff}/D < 12$
- (b) $l_{eff}/D \ge 12$
- (c) $l_{eff}/D < 6$
- (d) $l_{eff}/D > 20$

## Section D — Slabs, Footings & Prestress (Q14–Q15)

**Q14.** A slab is two-way if the aspect ratio $l_y/l_x$ is:
- (a) $> 2$
- (b) $\le 2$
- (c) $= 3$
- (d) $> 3$

**Q15.** The total prestress loss is approximately:
- (a) Pre-tensioning 18%, post-tensioning 15%
- (b) Pre-tensioning 15%, post-tensioning 18%
- (c) Both 10%
- (d) Both 25%

## 📋 Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Explanation |
|:-:|:------:|-------------|
| 1 | (b) | $f_{ck}$ = 5% of test cubes fall below this value |
| 2 | (a) | $E_c = 5000\sqrt{f_{ck}}$ N/mm² (short-term) |
| 3 | (d) | $\gamma_m = 1.5$ for concrete |
| 4 | (b) | Max strain in concrete (flexure) = 0.0035 |
| 5 | (c) | Deflection is a serviceability limit state, not collapse |
| 6 | (b) | Fe415: $x_{u,max}/d = 0.48$ |
| 7 | (a) | $M_{u,lim} = 0.138 f_{ck} b d^2$ for Fe415 |
| 8 | (b) | Over-reinforced: concrete crushes first (brittle) |
| 9 | (a) | $A_{st,min} = 0.85bd/f_y$ |
| 10 | (b) | Doubly reinforced when depth/breadth restricted |
| 11 | (a) | $\tau_v = V_u/bd$ |
| 12 | (a) | $L_d = 0.87 f_y \phi / 4\tau_{bd}$ |
| 13 | (a) | Short column: $l_{eff}/D < 12$ |
| 14 | (b) | Two-way: $l_y/l_x \le 2$ |
| 15 | (a) | Pre-tensioning ~18%, post-tensioning ~15% |
