## Primary Research — CFD & Hydraulics

### [2D Pipeline Scour — SedFOAM (Reference Case)](https://github.com/DKS-MANAGER/2DPipelineScour)
> OpenFOAM 2412 · `sedFoam_rbgh` · Eulerian Two-Phase · k-ω SST · Mao (1986) Validation

Full two-phase Eulerian CFD simulation of scour beneath a submarine pipeline. Resolves bed evolution, granular rheology (Boyer μ(I)), and tunnel erosion under steady current. Validated against Mao (1986) and Larsen et al. (2016).

`sedFoam` · `Eulerian Two-Phase` · `Pipeline Scour` · `snappyHexMesh` · `IIT Kanpur HPC`

---

### [2D Pipeline Scour — sedExnerFoam Extended (EXN)](https://github.com/DKS-MANAGER/2DPipelineScourEXN)
> OpenFOAM 2412 · `sedExnerFoam` · Exner Equation · ALE Mesh Motion · FAM

Transitions from full two-phase to efficient Exner+ALE moving-mesh formulation. Includes critical C++ source patches for OpenFOAM 2412 compatibility (8 solver-level fixes documented). Parallelized with `singleProcessorFaceSets` constraint.

`sedExnerFoam` · `ALE` · `Finite Area Method` · `Exner Morphodynamics`

---

### [Pipeline Scour — Calibrated sedExnerFoam](https://github.com/DKS-MANAGER/PipelineScour_Calibrated)
> OpenFOAM 2412 · `sedExnerFoam` · Nielsen (1992) Bedload · 2-Phase Automated Workflow

Fully calibrated, production-ready pipeline scour case. 2-phase execution (spin-up → morphodynamics), morphological acceleration factor 2×, 20-core MPI. Contains 18+ documented solver fixes for stability and mesh motion.

`Calibrated CFD` · `Morphological Acceleration` · `Production HPC Run`

---

### [Bridge Pier Contraction Scour — SedFOAM](https://github.com/DKS-MANAGER/bridge_sedfoam)
> OpenFOAM 2412 · `sedFoam_rbgh` · Majid et al. (2026) · ASCE J. Hydraulic Engineering

Mobile-bed 2D CFD simulation of vertical bridge contraction scour. Implements Grade-III Ahmedabad sand (d₅₀ = 0.294 mm), Boyer μ(I) granular rheology, and 8-core parallel execution. References validated ASCE experimental benchmark.

`Bridge Scour` · `Contraction Scour` · `sedFoam` · `ASCE Validation`

---

### [Bridge Pier Scour — Live-Bed](https://github.com/DKS-MANAGER/bridge_sedfoam_livebed)
> OpenFOAM 2412 · `sedFoam_rbgh` · θ > θcr · Upstream Sediment Supply Active

Live-bed scour variant with active upstream sediment transport (θ∞ > θcr). Fixed-value inlet alpha.a boundary condition supplies continuous sediment feed for equilibrium scour under fully mobile-bed conditions.

`Live-Bed Scour` · `Sediment Supply` · `Equilibrium Scour`

---

### [Bridge Pier Scour — Downstream Wake](https://github.com/DKS-MANAGER/bridge_sedfoam_downstream)
> OpenFOAM 2412 · `sedFoam_rbgh` · Wake Morphology · Downstream Domain Extension

Downstream wake morphology variant. Extended domain captures sediment deposition ridge, wake vortex reattachment length, and TKE distribution downstream of bridge abutment.

`Wake Scour` · `Downstream Morphology` · `Deposition Ridge`
