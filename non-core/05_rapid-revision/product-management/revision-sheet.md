# Product Management: 60-Minute Last-Minute Revision

> High-density framework card for PM product design, strategy, and execution interviews.

---

## 1. CIRCLES Method for Product Design
1. **C**omprehend Situation (Goal, Constraints, Target Market)
2. **I**dentify Customer Persona (Demographics, Pain Points, JTBD)
3. **R**eport Customer Needs (User journey frictions)
4. **C**ut Through Prioritization (High frequency $\times$ High severity)
5. **L**ist Solutions (3 diverse concepts: 1 incremental, 1 moonshot, 1 platform)
6. **E**valuate Trade-offs (Effort vs. Impact matrix)
7. **S**ummarize & North Star Metric (Primary KPI, Guardrail metrics)

---

## 2. Prioritization & Metrics
* **RICE**: $\frac{\text{Reach} \times \text{Impact} \times \text{Confidence}}{\text{Effort}}$
* **Pirate Funnel (AARRR)**: Acquisition $\to$ Activation $\to$ Retention $\to$ Referral $\to$ Revenue.
