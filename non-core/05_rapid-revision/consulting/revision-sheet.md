## 1. Top Case Framework Formulas
* **Profitability**: $\Delta \text{Profit} = \Delta \text{Revenue} (P \times Q) - \Delta \text{Cost} (\text{Fixed} + \text{Var} \times Q)$
  * *Revenue Drivers*: Units sold, product mix, pricing tiers, discounts, channel commissions.
  * *Cost Drivers*: Value chain breakdown $\rightarrow$ Procurement $\rightarrow$ Manufacturing $\rightarrow$ Logistics $\rightarrow$ Sales/Marketing.
* **Market Entry**: Market Size & CAGR $\rightarrow$ Customer Segments $\rightarrow$ Competitive Moats $\rightarrow$ Entry Mode (Organic / JV / Acquisition / Licensing) $\rightarrow$ Financial Feasibility & Payback.
* **Pricing Framework**: Cost-Plus (Floor) $\leftrightarrow$ Competitor-Based (Reference) $\leftrightarrow$ Value-Based (Ceiling).

---

## 2. Universal Sanity Checks & Traps
- **MECE Check**: Does your structure cover both internal and external factors?
- **Top-Down Delivery**: State the conclusion first (*"We recommend entering via JV for three reasons..."*).
- **Numbers with Units**: Always compute percentages, absolute margins, and payback horizon.
