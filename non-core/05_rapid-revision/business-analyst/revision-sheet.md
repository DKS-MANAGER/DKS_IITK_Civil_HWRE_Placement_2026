# Business Analyst: 60-Minute Last-Minute Revision

> High-density cheat sheet for Business Analyst case studies and problem-solving interviews.

## 1. BA Problem Deconstruction
* **Metric Trees**: North Star $\rightarrow$ Input Metrics $\rightarrow$ Operational KPIs.
* **Root Cause Archetypes**: Segment by Geography, User Cohort, Platform (iOS/Android/Web), Traffic Source (Organic vs Paid).
* **Requirements Traceability**: Business Problem $\to$ User Stories $\to$ Functional Requirements $\to$ Data Telemetry $\to$ Success Criteria.

## 2. Quick SQL & Data Drills
* COALESCE(val, 0) for NULL handling.
* CASE WHEN condition THEN 1 ELSE 0 END for conditional aggregations.
* DATE_TRUNC('month', created_at) for monthly cohort grouping.
