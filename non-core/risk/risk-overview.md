## Key Concepts

### Expected Value
```
EV = Σ (Probability × Impact)
```

**Example:** 10% chance of losing ₹1 lakh, 90% chance of no loss.
EV = 0.10 × 1,00,000 + 0.90 × 0 = ₹10,000 expected loss

### Risk Matrix

| Probability \ Impact | Low | Medium | High |
|:---------------------|:---:|:------:|:----:|
| **High** | Medium | High | Critical |
| **Medium** | Low | Medium | High |
| **Low** | Low | Low | Medium |

### Risk Response Strategies

| Strategy | When to Use |
|:---------|:------------|
| **Avoid** | Eliminate the risk entirely |
| **Mitigate** | Reduce probability or impact |
| **Transfer** | Shift risk (insurance, contracts) |
| **Accept** | Tolerate if low impact/probability |

### Sensitivity Analysis
- Change one variable at a time
- Measure impact on outcome
- Identify which variables matter most

### Scenario Analysis
- Define best case, base case, worst case
- Assign probabilities
- Calculate expected outcome

## Quantitative Problems

### Problem 1: Expected Value
**Prompt:** "A project has 30% chance of ₹5 lakh profit, 50% chance of ₹2 lakh, 20% chance of ₹1 lakh loss. Calculate expected value."

**Solution:**
EV = 0.30 × 5,00,000 + 0.50 × 2,00,000 + 0.20 × (-1,00,000)
= 1,50,000 + 1,00,000 - 20,000 = **₹2,30,000**

### Problem 2: Risk Decision
**Prompt:** "Option A: guaranteed ₹3 lakh. Option B: 50% chance of ₹8 lakh, 50% chance of ₹0. Which do you choose?"

**Solution:**
- Option A EV = ₹3 lakh
- Option B EV = 0.5 × 8,00,000 + 0.5 × 0 = ₹4 lakh
- Option B has higher EV but higher risk. Decision depends on risk appetite.

### Problem 3: Sensitivity
**Prompt:** "A project's profit depends on volume (₹100/unit margin) and fixed costs (₹5 lakh). At 10,000 units, profit = 10,000 × 100 - 5,00,000 = ₹5 lakh. If volume drops 20%, what's the impact?"

**Solution:**
New volume = 8,000 units
New profit = 8,000 × 100 - 5,00,000 = ₹3 lakh
**Profit drops 40% from a 20% volume drop** (operating leverage).
