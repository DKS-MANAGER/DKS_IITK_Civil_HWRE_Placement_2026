## What a Business Analyst Does

A Business Analyst bridges business needs and data. They translate business questions into data analyses, and data insights into business decisions.

**Core responsibilities:**
- Understand business problems and translate them into analytical questions
- Write SQL queries to extract and analyze data
- Build dashboards and reports for stakeholders
- Identify trends, patterns, and root causes
- Communicate insights to non-technical stakeholders
- Support decision-making with data

### Day-to-Day Work

| Task | Example | % of Time |
|:-----|:--------|:---------:|
| **SQL queries** | "Pull monthly revenue by region" | 30% |
| **Data analysis** | "Why did churn increase in Q3?" | 25% |
| **Dashboard building** | "Create a KPI dashboard for sales" | 15% |
| **Stakeholder communication** | "Present findings to product team" | 15% |
| **Requirements gathering** | "What does the marketing team need?" | 10% |
| **Process improvement** | "Automate weekly report generation" | 5% |

| Round | Format | What's Tested |
|:------|:-------|:--------------|

## Case Questions (BA-Specific)

### Case 1: KPI Drop
**Prompt:** "Monthly active users dropped 15% last month. Investigate."

**Structure:**
1. Define the KPI (what counts as active?)
2. Segment the drop (by region, device, cohort, feature)
3. Check external factors (seasonality, competitor activity, outages)
4. Check internal factors (product changes, bugs, marketing changes)
5. Form hypothesis → test → validate

### Case 2: Revenue Analysis
**Prompt:** "Revenue grew 10% but profit fell 5%. Why?"

**Structure:**
1. Revenue side: volume vs. price vs. mix
2. Cost side: fixed vs. variable
3. Segment by product, region, customer
4. Identify the driver of the profit decline

### Case 3: Campaign Measurement
**Prompt:** "We ran a marketing campaign. How do we know if it worked?"

**Structure:**
1. Define success metric (conversion, revenue, retention)
2. Establish baseline (pre-campaign performance)
3. Control for external factors (seasonality, other campaigns)
4. Consider A/B testing or cohort analysis
5. Measure incremental impact
