# Placement Strategy: Shortlisting & Slotting Optimization

> Tactical playbook for maximizing shortlists, interview conversion, and slotting advantage at IIT Kanpur placement season.

---

## 1. The IITK Placement Funnel Mechanics

1. **Phase 1: Resume Verification & Master Portal Setup**: Clean, bulletproof, verified 1-page resume variants (Consulting, Analytics, Product/General Management).
2. **Phase 2: Aptitude & Online Assessment (OA) Blitz**: Clearing role-specific cutoffs (CAT-style quant, logical reasoning, data interpretation, domain tests).
3. **Phase 3: Pre-Interview Shortlists & GD Rounds**: Conversion of OA scores into day-wise interview shortlists.
4. **Phase 4: Day 1 to Day 4 Interview Slots**: Managing parallel interview panels, fatigue, and offer negotiations.

---

## 2. Multi-Track Resume Strategy
- **Track A (Consulting & General Management)**: Highlight leadership, PORs, case competitions, structured problem solving, and quantified cross-functional impact.
- **Track B (Analytics & Data Science)**: Highlight SQL, Python, machine learning, statistical modeling, data pipelines, and HWRE computational research.
- **Track C (Product & Operations)**: Highlight user-centric problem solving, agile workflows, operational bottlenecks resolved, and engineering optimizations.

---

## 3. Shortlist Maximization Principles
- **Apply Broadly in Primary & Secondary Clusters**: Do not bet everything on a single tier-1 firm. Maintain a balanced portfolio (Tier 1 aspirational, Tier 2 solid, Tier 3 safety).
- **Control Test Accuracy over Speed**: OAs reward high accuracy; penalize negative marks.
- **Prepare 3 Anchor Stories**: Master 3 versatile engineering/POR leadership stories adaptable to 90% of behavioral prompts.
