# Profit, Loss & Discount

## Concept Definitions

- **Cost Price (CP)**: Price at which an article is purchased.
- **Selling Price (SP)**: Price at which an article is sold.
- **Profit**: Occurs when $SP > CP$.
- **Loss**: Occurs when $CP > SP$.
- **Marked Price (MP / List Price)**: Price printed on the article.
- **Discount**: Reduction offered on the Marked Price.
- **Markup**: Increase above CP to set the MP.

---

## Key Formulas & Shortcuts

### Basic Calculations
| Formula | Equation |
|---------|----------|
| Profit | $SP - CP$ |
| Loss | $CP - SP$ |
| Profit % | $\frac{\text{Profit}}{CP} \times 100$ |
| Loss % | $\frac{\text{Loss}}{CP} \times 100$ |
| Discount | $MP - SP$ |
| Discount % | $\frac{\text{Discount}}{MP} \times 100$ |

### SP and CP Relations
- When profit: $SP = CP \times \frac{100 + \text{Profit}\%}{100}$
- When loss: $SP = CP \times \frac{100 - \text{Loss}\%}{100}$

### MP to CP Ratio Shortcut
$$\frac{MP}{CP} = \frac{100 + \text{Profit}\%}{100 - \text{Discount}\%}$$

### Successive Discounts
$$\text{Net Discount} = d_1 + d_2 - \frac{d_1 \times d_2}{100}$$

### Dishonest Dealer / Faulty Weights
$$\text{Gain \%} = \frac{\text{Error}}{\text{True Value} - \text{Error}} \times 100$$

### Shortcut: When CP of x = SP of y
$$\text{Profit/Loss \%} = \frac{x-y}{y} \times 100$$

### Shortcut: Selling at Same Price
If two items sold at same SP, one at $x\%$ profit, other at $x\%$ loss:
$$\text{Net Loss \%} = \frac{x^2}{100}$$

## Practice Problems (10 Problems)

### Problem 1
An article is sold at a profit of 20%. If it had been sold for Rs. 60 less, there would have been a loss of 10%. Find the CP.

### Problem 2
Find the single discount equivalent to successive discounts of 20%, 10%, and 5%.

### Problem 3
A shopkeeper sells sugar at cost price but uses a false weight of 900 g instead of 1 kg. What is his profit percentage?

### Problem 4
By selling an article for Rs. 720, a shopkeeper incurs a loss of 10%. At what price should he sell it to gain 20%?

### Problem 5
A retailer marks goods 40% above CP and then allows a discount of 25% on the MP. Find the net profit or loss percentage.

### Problem 6
A man sells two articles for Rs. 1000 each. On one he gains 20% and on the other he loses 20%. Find his overall profit or loss.

### Problem 7
A dealer marks his goods 30% above CP and allows a discount of 15%. If he sells the article for Rs. 1105, find the CP.

### Problem 8
By selling 12 pens for a rupee, a man loses 20%. How many pens for a rupee should he sell to gain 20%?

### Problem 9
A trader marks his goods at 50% above CP and allows a discount of 20%. He also uses a false weight of 900g for 1kg. Find his profit percentage.

### Problem 10
The CP of 20 articles is equal to the SP of 16 articles. Find the profit percentage.

## Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. Let CP be $C$.
2. SP at 20% profit $= 1.20C$. SP at 10% loss $= 0.90C$.
3. Difference is Rs. 60: $1.20C - 0.90C = 60 \implies 0.30C = 60 \implies C = 200$
4. **Answer**: **Rs. 200**.

### Solution 2
1. Combine 20% and 10%: $D_{12} = 20 + 10 - \frac{20 \times 10}{100} = 28\%$
2. Combine 28% and 5%: $D_{123} = 28 + 5 - \frac{28 \times 5}{100} = 31.6\%$
3. **Answer**: **31.6%**.

### Solution 3
1. True value $= 1000$ g, false value $= 900$ g. Error $= 100$ g.
2. Gain % $= \frac{100}{1000 - 100} \times 100 = \frac{100}{900} \times 100 = 11.11\%$
3. **Answer**: **$11\frac{1}{9}\%$** (approx. 11.11%).

### Solution 4
1. SP $= 720$, loss $= 10\%$. $CP = \frac{720 \times 100}{90} = 800$
2. New SP for 20% profit: $800 \times \frac{120}{100} = 960$
3. **Answer**: **Rs. 960**.

### Solution 5
1. Assume CP $= 100$. MP $= 140$.
2. SP after 25% discount: $140 \times 0.75 = 105$
3. Profit $= 5$. Profit % $= 5\%$.
4. **Answer**: **5% profit**.

### Solution 6
1. SP of each = 1000. Total SP = 2000.
2. CP of first = $1000/1.2 = 833.33$. CP of second = $1000/0.8 = 1250$.
3. Total CP = 2083.33. Loss = 83.33.
4. Loss % $= 83.33/2083.33 \times 100 = 4\%$
5. **Answer**: **4% loss** (using shortcut: $20^2/100 = 4\%$).

### Solution 7
1. SP = 1105. Discount = 15% → MP = $1105/0.85 = 1300$
2. MP = 1.3 × CP → CP = $1300/1.3 = 1000$
3. **Answer**: **Rs. 1000**.

### Solution 8
1. SP of 12 pens = 1 rupee. Loss = 20%.
2. CP of 12 pens = $1/0.8 = 1.25$ rupees.
3. CP of 1 pen = $1.25/12$ rupees.
4. For 20% profit, SP of 1 pen = $1.2 \times 1.25/12 = 1.5/12 = 1/8$ rupees.
5. Pens per rupee = 8.
6. **Answer**: **8 pens**.

### Solution 9
1. Let CP = 100. MP = 150.
2. SP after 20% discount = $150 \times 0.8 = 120$.
3. False weight: sells 900g as 1000g → effective SP for 1000g = $120 \times 1000/900 = 133.33$
4. Profit = $133.33 - 100 = 33.33\%$
5. **Answer**: **33.33% profit**.

### Solution 10
1. CP of 20 = SP of 16.
2. Let CP of 1 = 1. CP of 20 = 20. SP of 16 = 20.
3. SP of 1 = $20/16 = 1.25$.
4. Profit % $= (1.25-1)/1 \times 100 = 25\%$
5. **Answer**: **25% profit**.
