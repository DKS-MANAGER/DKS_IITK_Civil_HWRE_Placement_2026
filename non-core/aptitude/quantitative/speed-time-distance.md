# Speed, Time & Distance

## Concept Definitions

- **Speed**: Rate of covering distance. $\text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}}$
- **Relative Speed**: Speed of one object measured with respect to another.
- **Downstream**: Moving in the direction of the current/stream.
- **Upstream**: Moving against the direction of the current/stream.

---

## Key Formulas & Shortcuts

### Conversions
- **km/h to m/s**: Multiply by $\frac{5}{18}$.
- **m/s to km/h**: Multiply by $\frac{18}{5}$.

### Average Speed
- General: $\text{Average Speed} = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}}$
- Equal distances at speeds $x$ and $y$:
  $$\text{Average Speed} = \frac{2xy}{x + y}$$
- Equal times at speeds $x$ and $y$:
  $$\text{Average Speed} = \frac{x + y}{2}$$

### Relative Speed
- **Same direction**: $S_{\text{rel}} = S_1 - S_2$ (assuming $S_1 > S_2$).
- **Opposite direction**: $S_{\text{rel}} = S_1 + S_2$.

### Trains
- Cross a stationary pole/man:
  $$\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Length of Train}}{\text{Speed of Train}}$$
- Cross a platform/bridge/tunnel of length $L_P$:
  $$\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Length of Train} + L_P}{\text{Speed of Train}}$$
- Two trains crossing each other:
  $$\text{Time} = \frac{L_1 + L_2}{\text{Relative Speed}}$$

### Boats and Streams
Let speed of boat in still water $= u$, speed of stream $= v$.
- **Downstream speed**: $u + v$
- **Upstream speed**: $u - v$
- **Speed of boat in still water**: $\frac{d + u_p}{2}$
- **Speed of stream**: $\frac{d - u_p}{2}$

### Shortcut: Meeting Point
Two persons start from A and B towards each other. After meeting, they take $t_1$ and $t_2$ to reach destinations.
$$\frac{S_1}{S_2} = \sqrt{\frac{t_2}{t_1}}$$

## Practice Problems (10 Problems)

### Problem 1
A car traveling at 60 km/h covers a distance in 4 hours. By how much must its speed increase to cover the same distance in 3 hours?

### Problem 2
A train 150 m long crosses a bridge 250 m long in 20 seconds. Find the speed of the train in km/h.

### Problem 3
A boat's speed in still water is 12 km/h. Stream speed is 4 km/h. It takes 6 hours to go to a place and return. Find the distance.

### Problem 4
A man walks at 4 km/h and reaches office 5 minutes late. At 5 km/h, he reaches 10 minutes early. Find the distance.

### Problem 5
Two trains of lengths 120 m and 140 m run in opposite directions at 48 km/h and 30 km/h. Find the crossing time.

### Problem 6
A train passes a pole in 15 seconds and a platform 100 m long in 25 seconds. Find the length of the train.

### Problem 7
A man rows to a place 48 km away and back in 14 hours. He finds he can row 4 km with the stream in the same time as 3 km against the stream. Find the rate of the stream.

### Problem 8
Two cars start from A and B, 300 km apart, towards each other. Car A at 60 km/h, Car B at 40 km/h. After how long will they meet? How far from A?

### Problem 9
A train 200 m long running at 72 km/h crosses another train 300 m long running at 54 km/h in the same direction. Find the time taken.

### Problem 10
A man can row 6 km/h in still water. The river flows at 2 km/h. It takes him 3 hours more to row upstream than downstream for the same distance. Find the distance.

## Step-by-Step Solutions

### Solution 1
1. Distance $= 60 \times 4 = 240$ km.
2. New speed $= \frac{240}{3} = 80$ km/h.
3. Increase $= 80 - 60 = 20$ km/h.
4. **Answer**: **20 km/h**.

### Solution 2
1. Total distance $= 150 + 250 = 400$ m. Time $= 20$ s.
2. Speed in m/s $= \frac{400}{20} = 20$ m/s.
3. Convert to km/h: $20 \times \frac{18}{5} = 72$ km/h.
4. **Answer**: **72 km/h**.

### Solution 3
1. Downstream speed $= 12 + 4 = 16$ km/h. Upstream speed $= 12 - 4 = 8$ km/h.
2. Let distance $= D$.
   $$\frac{D}{16} + \frac{D}{8} = 6 \implies \frac{3D}{16} = 6 \implies D = 32$$
3. **Answer**: **32 km**.

### Solution 4
1. Time difference $= 5 \text{ min late} + 10 \text{ min early} = 15 \text{ min} = \frac{1}{4}$ hour.
2. Let distance $= D$.
   $$\frac{D}{4} - \frac{D}{5} = \frac{1}{4} \implies \frac{D}{20} = \frac{1}{4} \implies D = 5$$
3. **Answer**: **5 km**.

### Solution 5
1. Total length $= 120 + 140 = 260$ m.
2. Relative speed (opposite directions) $= 48 + 30 = 78$ km/h.
3. Convert to m/s: $78 \times \frac{5}{18} = \frac{65}{3}$ m/s.
4. Crossing time: $\frac{260}{65/3} = 260 \times \frac{3}{65} = 12$ seconds.
5. **Answer**: **12 seconds**.

### Solution 6
1. Let train length $= L$. Speed $= S$.
2. $L/S = 15$ and $(L+100)/S = 25$.
3. $L = 15S$, $L+100 = 25S \implies 15S + 100 = 25S \implies 10S = 100 \implies S = 10$ m/s.
4. $L = 15 \times 10 = 150$ m.
5. **Answer**: **150 m**.

### Solution 7
1. Let boat speed $= u$, stream speed $= v$.
2. Time for 4 km downstream = Time for 3 km upstream:
   $$\frac{4}{u+v} = \frac{3}{u-v} \implies 4u - 4v = 3u + 3v \implies u = 7v$$
3. Total time for 48 km each way:
   $$\frac{48}{u+v} + \frac{48}{u-v} = 14$$
   $$\frac{48}{8v} + \frac{48}{6v} = 14 \implies \frac{6}{v} + \frac{8}{v} = 14 \implies \frac{14}{v} = 14 \implies v = 1$$
4. **Answer**: **1 km/h**.

### Solution 8
1. Relative speed $= 60 + 40 = 100$ km/h.
2. Time to meet $= 300/100 = 3$ hours.
3. Distance from A $= 60 \times 3 = 180$ km.
4. **Answer**: **3 hours, 180 km from A**.

### Solution 9
1. Total length $= 200 + 300 = 500$ m.
2. Relative speed (same direction) $= 72 - 54 = 18$ km/h $= 5$ m/s.
3. Time $= 500/5 = 100$ seconds.
4. **Answer**: **100 seconds**.

### Solution 10
1. Upstream speed $= 6 - 2 = 4$ km/h. Downstream speed $= 6 + 2 = 8$ km/h.
2. Let distance $= D$.
   $$\frac{D}{4} - \frac{D}{8} = 3 \implies \frac{D}{8} = 3 \implies D = 24$$
3. **Answer**: **24 km**.
