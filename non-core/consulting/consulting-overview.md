# Consulting Preparation — Complete System

> What consulting firms do, what they test, how to prepare, and how your Civil/M.Tech profile positions you for consulting roles.

---

## What Consulting Actually Does

Consulting firms are hired by companies to solve problems they can't solve themselves:
- "Should we enter the Indian market?"
- "Why are our costs increasing?"
- "How do we grow revenue 20% in 2 years?"
- "Should we acquire this competitor?"

**The consultant's job:** Structure the problem → Analyze the data → Recommend a solution → Help implement it.

### Types of Consulting

| Type | Focus | Example Firms |
|:-----|:------|:-------------|
| **Strategy** | Business strategy, market entry, growth | McKinsey, BCG, Bain |
| **Management** | Operations, org design, transformation | Deloitte, EY, KPMG, PwC |
| **IT/Digital** | Technology implementation, digital transformation | Accenture, Capgemini |
| **Financial** | Valuation, restructuring, M&A | Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan |
| **Operations** | Process optimization, supply chain | Alvarez & Marsal |

### Day-to-Day Work

| Phase | What You Do | % of Time |
|:------|:------------|:---------:|
| **Problem Definition** | Understand client's question, scope the engagement | 10% |
| **Data Gathering** | Interviews, financial data, market research | 25% |
| **Analysis** | Frameworks, models, data analysis, synthesis | 40% |
| **Recommendations** | Build decks, present to client | 15% |
| **Implementation** | Help client execute (some firms) | 10% |

## What Consulting Firms Test

### Interview Process (Typical)

| Round | Format | Duration | What's Tested |
|:------|:-------|:--------:|:--------------|
| **Online Assessment** | Aptitude + logical reasoning | 60-90 min | Speed, accuracy, quantitative ability |
| **Case Interview #1** | Individual case | 30-45 min | Structuring, analysis, communication |
| **Case Interview #2** | Individual case | 30-45 min | Depth, creativity, business judgment |
| **Behavioral/Fit** | STAR questions | 20-30 min | Leadership, teamwork, motivation |
| **Partner Round** | Case + Fit | 45-60 min | Executive presence, judgment, fit |

### Core Skills Tested

| Skill | Weight | How to Prepare |
|:------|:------:|:---------------|
| **Structured Thinking** | 30% | Practice issue trees, MECE breakdowns |
| **Business Acumen** | 20% | Read business news, understand business models |
| **Quantitative Ability** | 20% | Mental math, data interpretation, guesstimates |
| **Communication** | 20% | Think aloud, synthesize, be concise |
| **Personal Fit** | 10% | Behavioral stories, motivation, self-awareness |

## Question Bank

### Basic (Warm-Up)

1. What is a consulting firm? How does it create value?
2. Explain the difference between strategy and management consulting.
3. What makes a good consultant?
4. How would you structure a problem you know nothing about?
5. What's the difference between a framework and a structure?

### Intermediate (Case Prep)

6. A restaurant's profits are declining. How would you analyze this?
7. Should a smartphone company enter the wearable market?
8. How would you estimate the number of coffee shops in Delhi?
9. A client's market share dropped from 40% to 25% in 3 years. Diagnose.
10. How would you recommend pricing for a new SaaS product?

### Advanced (Partner-Level)

11. Your client is losing money on every unit sold. What are your options?
12. Two business units compete for the same customer. How do you resolve this?
13. A client asks you to help with a decision that conflicts with your values. What do you do?
14. How would you advise a traditional Indian company going digital?
15. Your analysis contradicts the CEO's belief. How do you handle it?

## Resume Tips for Consulting

**Emphasize:**
- Structured problem solving (research, analysis)
- Leadership and teamwork
- Quantitative skills (any data-driven work)
- Communication (presentations, publications)

**Bullet formula:** "Led/Analyzed/Developed [X] resulting in [quantified outcome]"
