## What Program / Project Management Does

Program and Project Managers plan, execute, and deliver projects on time, within scope, and within budget. They coordinate teams, manage risks, and communicate with stakeholders.

**Core responsibilities:**
- Planning and scheduling
- Risk management
- Stakeholder management
- Resource allocation
- Progress tracking and reporting
- Escalation management
- Quality and delivery assurance

### Day-to-Day Work

| Task | Example | % of Time |
|:-----|:--------|:---------:|
| **Planning** | "Create project plan, timeline, milestones" | 20% |
| **Coordination** | "Align teams, vendors, stakeholders" | 25% |
| **Tracking** | "Monitor progress, identify risks" | 20% |
| **Communication** | "Status updates, stakeholder meetings" | 20% |
| **Problem solving** | "Resolve issues, unblock teams" | 15% |

**"Managed site activity" →**
> "Planned and coordinated daily site operations for [project], aligning [X] workers and [Y] vendors to deliver [milestone] on schedule."

**"Handled project delays" →**
> "Identified schedule risks from [cause], implemented [mitigation], and recovered [X] days of delay through [action]."

## Question Bank

### Basic
1. What's the difference between a project and a program?
2. What is the critical path?
3. What are the key phases of a project?
4. How do you manage stakeholders?
5. What is a risk register?

### Intermediate
6. A project is behind schedule. What do you do?
7. How do you prioritize tasks in a project?
8. How do you handle scope creep?
9. Describe your approach to risk management.
10. How do you communicate project status?

### Advanced
11. A key team member quit mid-project. What do you do?
12. A stakeholder keeps changing requirements. How do you handle it?
13. How do you measure project success?
14. A project is over budget. What are your options?
15. How do you manage a project with unclear requirements?
