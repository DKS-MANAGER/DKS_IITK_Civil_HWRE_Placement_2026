# 03. Finance: Financial Statement Linking & Valuation

> 3-Statement financial modeling, Discounted Cash Flow (DCF), Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC), and FinTech economics.

---

## 1. The 3 Financial Statements & Linking Mechanics
$$\text{Net Income (from Income Statement)} \to \text{Cash Flow from Operations (Cash Flow Statement)}$$
$$\text{Ending Cash (from Cash Flow Statement)} \to \text{Cash Asset (Balance Sheet)}$$
$$\text{Retained Earnings (Balance Sheet)} = \text{Beginning Retained Earnings} + \text{Net Income} - \text{Dividends Paid}$$

---

## 2. Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Valuation Model
$$\text{Enterprise Value (EV)} = \sum_{t=1}^n \frac{\text{FCFF}_t}{(1 + \text{WACC})^t} + \frac{\text{Terminal Value}_n}{(1 + \text{WACC})^n}$$

$$\text{Terminal Value (Gordon Growth)} = \frac{\text{FCFF}_{n+1}}{\text{WACC} - g}$$

$$\text{WACC} = \left( \frac{E}{E+D} \times K_e \right) + \left( \frac{D}{E+D} \times K_d \times (1 - T) \right)$$
