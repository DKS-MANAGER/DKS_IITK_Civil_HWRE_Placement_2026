# 05. Business Analyst (BA): Selection Process & Interview Strategy

> Stage-by-stage guide from Online Assessment (OA) cutoffs to Technical, Case, and Partner rounds.

---

## 1. Selection Process Stages

### Stage 1: Online Assessment (OA)
- **Format & Pattern**: Duration: 60-90 mins. Section 1: Quantitative Aptitude & Speed Math (20 Qs). Section 2: Data Interpretation Tables & Graphs (15 Qs). Section 3: Live SQL Querying (3-5 coding queries on Joins/Aggregations). Section 4: Logical Reasoning & Puzzles (15 Qs).
- **Winning Strategy**: Solve high-scoring technical/case questions first; maintain speed on speed math.

### Stage 2: Technical & Modeling Round
- **Format & Focus**: Live SQL coderpad (Aggregations, Window functions like RANK/LAG, CTEs), Data extraction logic, Explaining approach to dirty/missing data, Excel formula drills.
- **Key Tactic**: Think out loud, clarify edge cases before typing code or writing equations.

### Stage 3: Case / Business Problem Round
- **Format & Focus**: KPI Drop Diagnosis (e.g. 'Orders fell 15% last week - diagnose'), Funnel conversion drop, Product/Marketing campaign ROI measurement, Unit economics breakdown.
- **Key Tactic**: Clarify goal -> Build MECE structure -> State hypothesis -> Calculate -> Synthesize.

### Stage 4: HR & Behavioral Round
- **Format & Focus**: Why BA from Civil Engineering?, Conflict with engineering/marketing teams, Handling deadlines with incomplete data, 3 STAR stories.
- **Key Tactic**: Deliver structured STAR stories with quantified outcomes; emphasize why your Civil/HWRE background is an asset.
