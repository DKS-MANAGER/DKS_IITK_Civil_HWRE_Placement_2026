# 03. Business Analyst: Business Knowledge & Industry Models

> Business fundamentals, business model archetypes, unit economics, and KPI hierarchies across major hiring sectors.

---

## 1. Primary Business Models & Core Metrics

### 1. E-Commerce & Retail (e.g., Flipkart, Amazon, Myntra)
- **Top Metrics**: Gross Merchandise Value (GMV), Average Order Value (AOV), Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Return to Origin (RTO) Rate, Cart Abandonment Rate.
- **Key Problem Areas**: Checkout funnel drop-offs, return logistics cost reduction, discounting elasticity.

### 2. SaaS & Enterprise Software
- **Top Metrics**: Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR), Churn Rate (Logo vs. Revenue Churn), Net Revenue Retention (NRR), Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).
- **Key Problem Areas**: Trial-to-paid conversion rate, feature adoption velocity, expansion revenue.

### 3. Banking, Financial Services & FinTech (e.g., Amex, Capital One, PhonePe)
- **Top Metrics**: Net Interest Margin (NIM), Non-Performing Assets (NPA), Default Probability, Take Rate, Total Payment Volume (TPV), Approval Rate.
- **Key Problem Areas**: Fraud detection false-positive optimization, credit underwriting scorecards.

### 4. On-Demand & Ride-Hailing (e.g., Uber, Ola, Swiggy, Zepto)
- **Top Metrics**: Daily Active Users (DAU), Fulfillment Rate, Driver Utilization Rate, ETA Accuracy, Surge Multiplier.
- **Key Problem Areas**: Supply-demand matching in localized geo-clusters, delivery time optimization.

## 2. Unit Economics & Equation Cheat Sheet

$$\text{Revenue} = \text{Active Users} \times \text{Transactions per User} \times \text{Average Order Value (AOV)}$$

$$\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Revenue} - \text{COGS}$$

$$\text{Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)} = \frac{\text{ARPU} \times \text{Gross Margin \%}}{\text{Monthly Churn Rate}}$$

$$\text{LTV / CAC Ratio Benchmark} \ge 3.0$$
