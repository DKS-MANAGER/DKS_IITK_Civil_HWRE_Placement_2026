## 4. Day-to-Day Deliverables of a Business Analyst

1. **KPI Tracking & Metric Trees**: Defining, monitoring, and debugging executive North Star metrics (e.g. GMV, Contribution Margin, MAU, Churn).
2. **Root-Cause Investigation**: Writing complex SQL queries across billions of rows to explain why conversion rate dropped 120 bps week-over-week.
3. **Experimentation & A/B Testing**: Designing sample sizes, tracking variant conversion lift, and evaluating statistical significance.
4. **Cross-Functional Translation**: Converting executive business problems into technical analytics requirements and vice versa.
