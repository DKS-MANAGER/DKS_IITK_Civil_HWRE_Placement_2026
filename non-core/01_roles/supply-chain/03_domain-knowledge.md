## 1. The Bullwhip Effect: Causes & Mathematics
Variance of orders increases upstream in the supply chain:
$$\text{Var}(\text{Manufacturer Orders}) > \text{Var}(\text{Distributor Orders}) > \text{Var}(\text{Retailer Orders}) > \text{Var}(\text{Consumer Demand})$$
- **Primary Causes**: Demand forecast updating lag, order batching, price fluctuations/promotions, rationing and shortage gaming.
- **Mitigation**: Point-of-Sale (POS) data sharing, Vendor Managed Inventory (VMI), smaller frequent batch deliveries.
