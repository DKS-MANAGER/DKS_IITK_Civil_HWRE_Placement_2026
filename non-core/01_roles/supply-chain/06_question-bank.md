### Question 1: [P0][SCM] Explain the 4 primary drivers of the Bullwhip Effect and how to counteract them.

**Direct Answer / Solution Approach:**
```text
1. Demand Forecast Updating: Mitigated by sharing real-time Point-of-Sale (POS) data across all supply tiers. 2. Order Batching: Mitigated by reducing setup/ordering costs to enable smaller, more frequent shipments. 3. Price Fluctuations / Promotions: Mitigated by Every Day Low Pricing (EDLP) policies. 4. Shortage Gaming / Rationing: Mitigated by allocating orders based on past historical sales rather than inflated current demand requests.
```

**Interviewer Follow-Up & Deep Dive:**
> Interviewer follow-up: How does Vendor Managed Inventory (VMI) structurally eliminate order batching?
