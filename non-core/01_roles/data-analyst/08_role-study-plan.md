# 08. Data Analyst: 4-Week Milestone Study Plan

> Structured 4-week preparation roadmap for IIT Kanpur Civil Engineering & M.Tech students targeting Data Analyst.

---

## 📅 Roadmap Overview

`	ext
WEEK 1: Domain Foundations & Technical Literacy
  ↳ Master core domain equations, terminology, and tools.

WEEK 2: Quantitative Problem Solving & Modeling
  ↳ Build mathematical models, spreadsheets, and analytical workflows.

WEEK 3: Case Practice & Issue Tree Deconstruction
  ↳ Practice structured problem solving and live case scenarios.

WEEK 4: Mock Simulations & Behavioral Polish
  ↳ Complete timed mock tests, peer interviews, and STAR story rehearsal.
`
