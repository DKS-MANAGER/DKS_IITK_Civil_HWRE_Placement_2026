# Consulting Math & Business Formulas Cheat-Sheet

> **Document Purpose**: Essential business mathematics formulas, financial metrics, mental math shortcuts, and unit economics calculations.

---

## 1. Core Financial & Business Formulas

### 1. Profitability & Margins
$$\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Revenue} - \text{COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)}$$
$$\text{Gross Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Gross Profit}}{\text{Revenue}} \times 100$$
$$\text{Operating Profit (EBIT)} = \text{Gross Profit} - \text{Operating Expenses (OpEx)}$$
$$\text{Net Profit Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Revenue}} \times 100$$

---

### 2. Unit Economics & Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
$$\text{Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)} = \frac{\text{Total Sales \& Marketing Expenses}}{\text{Number of New Customers Acquired}}$$
$$\text{Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)} = \text{Average Order Value (AOV)} \times \text{Purchase Frequency} \times \text{Customer Lifespan}$$
$$\text{Healthy SaaS / Business Ratio} = \frac{\text{LTV}}{\text{CAC}} \ge 3.0$$

---

### 3. Break-Even Analysis
$$\text{Break-Even Volume (Units)} = \frac{\text{Fixed Costs}}{\text{Price per Unit} - \text{Variable Cost per Unit}}$$
$$\text{Contribution Margin} = \text{Price per Unit} - \text{Variable Cost per Unit}$$
$$\text{Contribution Margin Ratio} = \frac{\text{Contribution Margin}}{\text{Price per Unit}}$$

---

### 4. Growth Rates & Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR)
$$\text{Simple Growth Rate \%} = \frac{\text{Value}_{\text{New}} - \text{Value}_{\text{Old}}}{\text{Value}_{\text{Old}}} \times 100$$

$$\text{CAGR} = \left( \frac{\text{End Value}}{\text{Start Value}} \right)^{\frac{1}{n}} - 1$$

#### Fast CAGR Estimation (Rule of 72):
* **Years to Double** $\approx \frac{72}{\text{Annual Growth Rate \%}}$
* *Example*: A market growing at 12% per year will double in size in $\frac{72}{12} = \mathbf{6\text{ years}}$.

## 3. Practice Math Problems

### Problem 1: Break-Even Volume
* Fixed Rent & Salaries = ₹50 Lakhs/month.
* Selling price per unit = ₹2,500.
* Variable cost per unit = ₹1,500.
* **Question**: How many units must be sold per month to break even?
* **Solution**: $\text{Contribution Margin} = 2,500 - 1,500 = ₹1,000/\text{unit}$.  
  $$\text{Break-Even Volume} = \frac{₹50,000,000}{1,000} = \mathbf{5,000\text{ units/month}}$$

---

### Problem 2: Rule of 72 Market Estimation
* Current EV market size in India = $2 Billion.
* Expected growth rate = 24% CAGR.
* **Question**: What will be the EV market size in 6 years?
* **Solution**: Years to double = $72 / 24 = 3\text{ years}$. In 6 years, it doubles twice ($2 \times 2 = 4\text{x}$).  
  $$\text{Future Market Size} = \$2\text{B} \times 4 = \mathbf{\$8\text{ Billion}}$$
