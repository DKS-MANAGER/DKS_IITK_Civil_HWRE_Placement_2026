## Case 1: Indian Cement Manufacturer Profitability Turnaround

### Case Prompt
*"Our client is a top-5 cement manufacturer in India with 12,000 Cr INR annual revenue. Over the past 18 months, their EBITDA margin dropped from 22% to 14%, while the industry average remained stable at 20%. The CEO has hired us to diagnose the issue and recommend a 100-day turnaround plan."*

### Step 1: Clarification & Scoping
- **Candidate**: *"Are all regions affected equally, or is the margin contraction localized to specific geographic clusters (North, South, East, West)?"*
- **Interviewer**: *"Good question. The client operates across India, but 80% of the margin drop is concentrated in the South India regional cluster."*
- **Candidate**: *"Has the Southern market experienced a price collapse, or is the issue driven by production and supply chain cost escalation?"*
- **Interviewer**: *"Realized prices in the South are down 4%, but cost per bag has increased by 16%."*

### Step 2: Issue Tree & Quantitative Analysis
```text
Cost Per 50kg Bag (South India): 250 INR → 290 INR (+40 INR / +16%)
├── Raw Material / Clinker (80 INR → 82 INR, +2 INR)
├── Power & Fuel (Petcoke / Coal) (65 INR → 85 INR, +20 INR) ← Key Driver!
├── Logistics & Freight (60 INR → 76 INR, +16 INR) ← Key Driver!
└── Plant Depreciation & Fixed (45 INR → 47 INR, +2 INR)
```

**Data Exhibit Analysis**:
1. **Power & Fuel**: South plant shifted from domestic linkage coal to imported petcoke due to local environmental rail bottlenecks. Global petcoke prices spiked 35%.
2. **Logistics**: Average lead distance from plant to distributor expanded from 180 km to 310 km because the client opened aggressive dealer accounts outside their optimal freight radius.

### Step 3: Mathematical Impact Calculation
- Southern volume: 15 Million Tons (300 Million Bags/year).
- Excess Freight Cost: $300\text{M bags} \times 16\text{ INR} = 480\text{ Cr INR}$.
- Excess Fuel Cost: $300\text{M bags} \times 20\text{ INR} = 600\text{ Cr INR}$.
- Total Annual EBITDA Drag = **1,080 Cr INR**.

### Step 4: Executive Recommendation & Synthesis
*"We recommend a 3-pillar turnaround to recover ~800 Cr INR in EBITDA over 12 months:*
1. **Freight Optimization**: Rationalize unprofitable peripheral dealer routes (>250 km); establish rail-head split-grinding units to cut lead distance back to <200 km (recovers ~320 Cr INR).
2. **Alternative Fuel Mix**: Increase Alternate Fuel and Raw Materials (AFR) thermal substitution rate to 15% using industrial waste biomass (recovers ~300 Cr INR).
3. **Dynamic Regional Pricing**: Re-align dealer discount slabs in high-density urban clusters (recovers ~180 Cr INR).*

**Key Implementation Risks**: Dealer attrition during route rationalization; biomass supply seasonality."*
