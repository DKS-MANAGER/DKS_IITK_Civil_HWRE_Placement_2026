## 2. Archetype 1 — Profitability Cases

### Fundamental Profit Equation:
$$\text{Profit} = \text{Total Revenue} - \text{Total Costs}$$
$$\text{Profit} = (\text{Quantity} \times \text{Price}) - (\text{Fixed Costs} + \text{Variable Costs})$$

```text
                                [Profit Decline]
                                       │
            ┌──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────┐
            ▼                                                     ▼
    [Revenue Issues]                                       [Cost Issues]
            │                                                     │
   ┌────────┴────────┐                                   ┌────────┴────────┐
   ▼                 ▼                                   ▼                 ▼
[Price / Unit]   [Volume / Qty]                      [Fixed Costs]     [Variable Costs]
                 │                                   • Rent            • Raw Materials
                 ├─ Market Demand                    • Salaries        • Commission
                 └─ Distribution                     • Depreciation    • Packaging
```

### Solved Profitability Case: National Quick Service Restaurant (QSR) Chain
* **Prompt**: A fast-food chain with 200 stores in India has seen a **20% drop in net profit margins** over the last year.
* **Clarifying Questions**:
  * *Is the drop across all regions or specific stores?* → Nationwide across 80% of stores.
  * *Have competitor prices changed?* → Competitor prices remain unchanged.
* **Analysis**:
  * Revenue per store is UP 5% (due to delivery apps like Swiggy/Zomato).
  * Costs are UP 28%.
  * Cost Breakdown: Food ingredient costs (up 4%), Labor (stable), **Aggregator Commissions** (up significantly).
  * *Root Cause*: Online delivery volume increased from 15% to 50% of total sales. Aggregators charge 24% commission per order, eroding profit margins compared to dine-in.
* **Recommendation**:
  1. Introduce exclusive direct ordering via QSR Mobile App with loyalty reward points.
  2. Implement a slight markup on delivery menu items (+8%) to offset aggregator commissions.

## 3. Archetype 2 — Market Entry Cases

### Framework (4 Ps & 3 Cs):
1. **Market Attractiveness**: Market size, growth rate, profit margins, regulatory barrier.
2. **Competitive Landscape**: Major players, market share, barriers to entry.
3. **Company Capabilities**: Financial capital, supply chain, brand equity, technical fit.
4. **Entry Mode Strategy**: Organic build, Acquisition, Joint Venture (JV), or Licensing.

```text
                             [Market Entry Decision]
                                         │
    ┌───────────────────┬────────────────┴───────────────────┬───────────────────┐
    ▼                   ▼                                    ▼                   ▼
[Market Size]    [Competition]                       [Capabilities]        [Entry Mode]
• TAM / SAM      • Competitor Share                  • Capital Required    • Build (Organic)
• Growth Rate    • Barriers to Entry                 • Tech & Distribution • Buy (M&A)
• Margin %       • Regulatory Constraints            • Brand fit           • Partner (JV)
```

---

### Solved Market Entry Case: EV Charging Infrastructure Entry
* **Prompt**: An Indian oil marketing company wants to enter the Electric Vehicle (EV) fast-charging network market. Should they enter?
* **Analysis**:
  * **Market Size**: EV adoption growing at 35% CAGR; strong government subsidies (FAME policy).
  * **Competitive Advantage**: Land availability at 10,000+ existing petrol stations across major highways.
  * **Financial Feasibility**: High initial CapEx (₹15 Lakhs per 120kW fast charger); break-even point reached at 18% utilization.
* **Recommendation**: **Enter via Hybrid Partner Model**. Partner with EV charger OEM (e.g., Tata Power) to share CapEx while monetizing existing fuel station real estate.

## 4. Archetype 3 — Operational & Process Efficiency Cases

### Framework (Value Chain & Bottleneck Analysis):
$$\text{Throughput Capacity} = \text{Capacity of the Slowest Process Step (Bottleneck)}$$

```text
[Raw Material Ingestion] ──► [Step 1: Processing] ──► [Step 2: Assembly] ──► [Packaging & Delivery]
    (100 units/hr)             (80 units/hr)            (40 units/hr)         (90 units/hr)
                                                             ▲
                                                             │
                                                  [BOTTLENECK STEP: Max output = 40]
```

## 5. Archetype 4 — Digital Transformation (DX) Cases

### Framework (Legacy vs Digital Ecosystem):
1. **Core Problem**: Manual workflows, high error rate, poor customer experience.
2. **Tech Solution Architecture**: Cloud infrastructure, AI/ML models, IoT sensors, APIs.
3. **Change Management**: Employee training, agile rollouts, digital adoption KPIs.
