## 1. Profitability Decomposition Architecture

Profitability is the most frequently tested case archetype (40%+ of all consulting interview rounds). Never treat profit as a static formula; treat it as an interconnected operational value chain.

### The Mathematical Profit Tree
$$\text{Profit} = \text{Total Revenue} - \text{Total Cost}$$

$$\text{Revenue} = \sum_{i=1}^n (\text{Price}_i \times \text{Volume}_i) = \text{Units Sold} \times \text{Average Selling Price (ASP)}$$

$$\text{Total Cost} = \text{Fixed Overhead Costs} + (\text{Variable Unit Cost} \times \text{Volume})$$

```text
PROFIT
├── REVENUE (Price × Volume)
│   ├── Price Strategy
│   │   ├── List Price vs. Realized Price (Discounts, Rebates)
│   │   ├── Price Elasticity of Demand: E = (%ΔQ) / (%ΔP)
│   │   └── Product Mix (High-Margin vs. Low-Margin Tier SKU Contribution)
│   └── Volume Drivers
│       ├── Market Size & Growth (Macro Headwinds, Industry Cycles)
│       ├── Market Share (Competitive Wins/Losses, Brand Perception)
│       ├── Customer Acquisition: Leads × Conversion Rate
│       └── Customer Retention: Churn Rate, Repeat Purchase Frequency
└── COST (Fixed + Variable)
    ├── Cost of Goods Sold (COGS / Direct Variable)
    │   ├── Raw Material Procurement (Commodity Prices, Supplier Leverage)
    │   ├── Direct Labor (Wages, Shift Efficiency, Overtime Penalties)
    │   └── Manufacturing / Utilities (Machine Uptime, Yield Loss, Scrap Rate)
    ├── Operating Expenses (SG&A / Semi-Fixed)
    │   ├── Sales & Marketing (CAC, Agency Fees, Channel Commissions)
    │   ├── Distribution & Logistics (Freight Rates, Warehousing, Route Density)
    │   └── General & Administrative (Salaries, Legal, IT Infrastructure)
    └── Capital Overhead (Depreciation, Debt Servicing, Facility Leases)
```

## 2. Market Entry & Growth Strategy

### Strategic Decision Matrix for Market Entry
1. **Market Attractiveness**:
   - Total Addressable Market (TAM) >= 500 Cr INR, Serviceable Available Market (SAM), Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM).
   - Historical CAGR & 5-Year Forecasted Growth.
   - Profitability benchmarks in the target sector (Average EBITDA Margins).
2. **Competitive Landscape (Porter's 5 Forces)**:
   - Threat of New Entrants (Capital requirements, regulatory licenses, moats).
   - Bargaining Power of Buyers (Fragmented consumers vs. concentrated B2B buyers).
   - Bargaining Power of Suppliers (Proprietary raw materials, sole-source suppliers).
   - Threat of Substitutes (Direct competitors vs. cross-industry alternatives).
   - Competitive Rivalry (Price wars, advertising intensity).
3. **Capabilities & Synergies**:
   - Core Competencies: Brand equity, distribution reach, manufacturing capacity.
   - Gap Analysis: What technology or localized relationships are missing?
4. **Entry Mode Trade-Off Analysis**:
   - **Organic Greenfield**: High control, slow time-to-market, high Capex risk.
   - **M&A (Acquisition)**: Instant market share, cultural integration risk, premium over asset value.
   - **Joint Venture (JV)**: Shared capital risk, local regulatory ease, governance conflict risk.
   - **Licensing / Franchise**: Low Capex, rapid scale, lowest operational control.

## 3. Pricing Economics Framework

```text
PRICING CEILING: Maximum Perceived Customer Value (Willingness to Pay)
      ↑
      ├── Value-Based Pricing (Cost of Next-Best Alternative + Value of Differentiators)
      │
      ├── Competitor-Indexed Pricing (Premium / Parity / Penetration Pricing)
      ↓
PRICING FLOOR: Marginal Cost of Production + Minimum Hurdle Margin
```

- **Price Elasticity ($E_d$)**:
  - $|E_d| > 1$: Elastic (Price cuts increase total revenue; price hikes destroy volume).
  - $|E_d| < 1$: Inelastic (Strong pricing power; price increases flow straight to EBITDA).
