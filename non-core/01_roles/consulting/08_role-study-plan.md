# Consulting — Study Plan

> 4-week preparation roadmap for civil/M.Tech students targeting consulting roles.

---

## Role Overview

**What consultants do:** Solve business problems for clients — market entry, growth strategy, cost reduction, organizational change.

**Day-to-day:** Research industries, build frameworks, analyze data, synthesize findings, present recommendations.

**Civil/M.Tech advantage:** Quantitative rigor from research, systematic problem-solving from engineering, ability to translate technical complexity into business language.

**Target companies:** McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Deloitte, EY, KPMG, PwC, Accenture Strategy, Tata Consulting.

## Topic 1: Case Interview Frameworks

### Why This Matters
Case interviews are 80% of consulting interview weight. You must master structuring, analysis, and communication.

### What to Learn
- [ ] Profitability framework (Revenue − Cost analysis)
- [ ] Market entry framework (Market + Company + Entry mode)
- [ ] Market sizing / guesstimates (Top-down, Bottom-up)
- [ ] Issue tree construction (MECE principle)
- [ ] Hypothesis-driven thinking
- [ ] Growth strategy framework
- [ ] Pricing framework

### Worked Example
> **"A restaurant's profits are declining. How would you analyze this?"**
>
> Structure: Profit = Revenue − Cost → Revenue = Price × Volume → Cost = Fixed + Variable
> → Segment by menu item, location, time → Identify root cause → Recommend fix
>
> → Full walkthrough in [case-frameworks.md](case-frameworks.md)

### Practice
- [ ] Basic: 3 framework applications (profitability, market entry, pricing)
- [ ] Intermediate: 5 cases from [case-bank.md](case-bank.md) (Cases 1-5)
- [ ] Interview-level: 5 civil-specific cases (Cases 11-13 + 2 from guesstimates)

### Interview Questions
1. A client's profits dropped 20% in 2 years. Diagnose. — *Tests: profitability framework*
2. Should an Indian infrastructure company enter the Middle East market? — *Tests: market entry framework*
3. How many coffee shops are there in Delhi? — *Tests: market sizing*
4. A SaaS company's churn increased from 5% to 12%. What happened? — *Tests: structured analysis*
5. Your client is losing money on every unit. What are your options? — *Tests: creative problem solving*

### Completion Criterion
- [ ] Can structure any case in 2 minutes
- [ ] Can solve profitability cases in 15 minutes
- [ ] Can do market sizing within 5 minutes
- [ ] Can answer 5 case questions confidently

## Rapid Revision

### Must-Memorize Frameworks
1. **Profitability:** Revenue (Price × Volume) − Cost (Fixed + Variable)
2. **Market Entry:** Market Attractiveness + Company Fit + Entry Mode + Financial Viability
3. **MECE:** Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive — every element is separate and covers everything
4. **Issue Tree:** Break problem into components, each component into sub-components
5. **Porter's Five Forces:** Rivalry, Supplier Power, Buyer Power, Threat of Substitution, Threat of Entry

### Mental Math Quick Reference
| Calculation | Shortcut |
|:------------|:---------|
| 15% of 240 | 10% = 24, 5% = 12, total = 36 |
| Growth from 100 to 150 in 3 years | ~14.5% CAGR (rule of 72: 72/3 ≈ 24, 50% growth in 3 years) |
| Break-even | Fixed Costs ÷ (Price − Variable Cost per unit) |
| Market size | Population × Eligibility × Willingness to Pay |
