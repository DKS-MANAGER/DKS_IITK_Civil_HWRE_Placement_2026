# 06. Management Consulting: High-Yield Question Bank

> Categorized interview questions with model answers, technical solutions, and interviewer follow-up questions.

---

### Question 1: [P0][CASE] An Indian airline's profit margin dropped from 12% to 4% over the last 12 months. Walk me through your structure.

**Direct Answer / Solution Approach:**
```text
Step 1: Clarify business model (LCC vs Full-service, domestic vs international routes). Step 2: Mathematical Profit Tree: Profit = (Passenger Revenue + Ancillary) - (Fuel/ATF + Aircraft Lease + Crew + Airport Charges + Maintenance + Marketing). Step 3: Segment by route profitability (Metros vs Tier-2). Step 4: Isolate ATF fuel price spikes vs passenger yield compression. Step 5: Synthesize 3 prioritized recommendations with estimated impact.
```

**Interviewer Follow-Up & Deep Dive:**
> Interviewer follow-up: If fuel costs rose 30% for all airlines, why did our client's margin fall 800 bps while competitor margin only fell 300 bps? (Older fuel-inefficient fleet, unhedged fuel contracts, or unfavorable route network density).

### Question 2: [P0][GUESSTIMATE] Estimate the annual market size of electric 2-wheelers in Delhi NCR in 2026.

**Direct Answer / Solution Approach:**
```text
Top-down approach: Population of Delhi NCR = 3.2 Cr -> Households = ~70 Lakh. Middle/Upper income target households = 60% = 42 Lakh. Vehicle ownership: 1.5 2-wheelers/household = 63 Lakh active 2-wheelers. Replacement cycle = 7 years -> Annual sales = 9 Lakh units/year. EV penetration in Delhi NCR (subsidies + fuel parity) = 25% -> Annual EV 2-Wheeler Sales = 2.25 Lakh units. ASP = 1.2 Lakh INR -> Market Size = ~2,700 Cr INR/year.
```

**Interviewer Follow-Up & Deep Dive:**
> Interviewer follow-up: What are the 2 biggest supply-side bottlenecks to doubling this market size? (Charging station/swapping grid density and battery cell localization).

### Question 3: [P1][PRICING] How would you price a breakthrough commercial water-filtration membrane that cuts industrial energy consumption by 40%?

**Direct Answer / Solution Approach:**
```text
Value-Based Pricing framework: Floor = Cost of manufacturing + minimum markup (e.g. 1 Lakh INR). Ceiling = Annual customer energy savings discounted over membrane lifecycle (e.g. 10 Lakh INR savings over 3 years). Target Price = Value Sharing: Capture 30-50% of incremental economic value created (Price = ~4-5 Lakh INR), providing client a 2-3x ROI and compelling payback under 12 months.
```

**Interviewer Follow-Up & Deep Dive:**
> Interviewer follow-up: How would you structure payment terms to accelerate adoption among conservative enterprise buyers? (Shared-savings model / zero upfront Capex with performance-linked monthly fee).

### Question 4: [P0][FIT] Tell me about a time you led a team through significant conflict or pushback.

**Direct Answer / Solution Approach:**
```text
STAR structure: S = Leading a 4-person computational research sub-team facing divergent modeling approaches. T = Deliver verified simulation results before conference deadline. A = Conducted an objective trade-off evaluation matrix assessing accuracy vs compute time, assigned clear modular ownership based on member strengths. R = Delivered final results 4 days early with zero data errors.
```

**Interviewer Follow-Up & Deep Dive:**
> Interviewer follow-up: If you had to redo this project, what is one process change you would make on Day 1?
