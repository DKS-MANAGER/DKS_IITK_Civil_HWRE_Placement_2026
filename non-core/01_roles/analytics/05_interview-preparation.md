# 05. Analytics & Decision Science: Selection Process & Interview Strategy

> Stage-by-stage guide from Online Assessment (OA) cutoffs to Technical, Case, and Partner rounds.

---

## 1. Selection Process Stages

### Stage 1: Online Assessment (OA)
- **Format & Pattern**: Duration: 90 mins. Section 1: Advanced SQL coding (4 queries). Section 2: Probability, Statistics & Quant (25 Qs). Section 3: Business Analytics Case / Data Interpretation (15 Qs).
- **Winning Strategy**: Solve high-scoring technical/case questions first; maintain speed on speed math.

### Stage 2: Technical & Modeling Round
- **Format & Focus**: Live SQL & Python data wrangling (Pandas merges, groupby, lambda transforms), Probability distributions, A/B test setup, Feature engineering discussion.
- **Key Tactic**: Think out loud, clarify edge cases before typing code or writing equations.

### Stage 3: Case / Business Problem Round
- **Format & Focus**: Analytics Case Study: 'User retention dropped in Month 2 - build an analytical diagnostic model', Designing an attribution model for multi-channel marketing, Customer lifetime value segmentation.
- **Key Tactic**: Clarify goal -> Build MECE structure -> State hypothesis -> Calculate -> Synthesize.

### Stage 4: HR & Behavioral Round
- **Format & Focus**: Translating complex technical data to non-technical stakeholders, handling ambiguous problem statements, career trajectory in analytics.
- **Key Tactic**: Deliver structured STAR stories with quantified outcomes; emphasize why your Civil/HWRE background is an asset.
