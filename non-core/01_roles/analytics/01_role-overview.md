## 4. What an Analytics Professional Actually Does (Day-to-Day Deliverables)

1. **Experimentation Design & Evaluation**: Calculating sample sizes, configuring 50/50 randomized control trials (RCTs), monitoring Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM), and measuring true causal lift.
2. **Metric Tree Architecture**: Translating executive business goals into actionable Leading and Lagging indicators (e.g. AARRR funnel metrics).
3. **Data Wrangling & Pipeline Queries**: Writing high-performance SQL and Python scripts across multi-terabyte tables to extract customer behavior cohorts.
4. **Predictive Modeling & Segmentation**: Training tree-based classifiers (XGBoost/LightGBM) and customer clustering models to predict churn and lifetime value.
