## What Operations Does

Operations is about making processes efficient, reliable, and scalable. It's the engine that turns inputs into outputs — products, services, or outcomes.

**Core responsibilities:**
- Process optimization
- Bottleneck analysis
- Capacity planning
- Resource allocation
- Quality management
- Cost optimization
- Scheduling and forecasting

### Day-to-Day Work

| Task | Example | % of Time |
|:-----|:--------|:---------:|
| **Process improvement** | "Reduce turnaround time by 20%" | 30% |

## Civil-Relevant Operations Cases

These leverage your Civil background directly.

### Case 1: Construction Productivity
**Prompt:** "A construction project is 30% behind schedule. Diagnose and fix."

**Structure:**
1. Map the critical path
2. Identify bottlenecks (labor, materials, equipment)
3. Check resource allocation
4. Analyze delays (weather, approvals, rework)
5. Recommend: reschedule, add resources, improve logistics

### Case 2: Concrete Batching
**Prompt:** "A concrete batching plant has 40% equipment utilization. Improve it."

**Structure:**
1. Map the batching process
2. Identify idle time causes (waiting for trucks, mix changes)
3. Optimize scheduling
4. Improve material handling
5. Measure: utilization, throughput, cost per batch

### Case 3: Site Logistics
**Prompt:** "Material deliveries to a site are causing congestion and delays."

**Structure:**
1. Map delivery flow
2. Identify peak times and bottlenecks
3. Implement delivery scheduling
4. Optimize storage and handling
5. Measure: on-time delivery, congestion, idle time
