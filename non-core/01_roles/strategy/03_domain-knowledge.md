# 03. Corporate Strategy: Competitive Moats & 7 Powers

> Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers framework, Blue Ocean strategy, diversification matrix, and M&A due diligence.

---

## 1. Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers (Strategic Moats)
1. **Scale Economies**: Declining per-unit cost with increased volume (e.g., TSMC).
2. **Network Effects**: Value to user increases with user base size (e.g., Visa, Uber).
3. **Counter-Positioning**: New business model that incumbents cannot copy without harming their core business (e.g., Netflix vs. Blockbuster).
4. **Switching Costs**: Prohibitive friction/cost to migrate to a competitor (e.g., SAP, Salesforce).
5. **Branding**: Higher willingness-to-pay driven by historical trust and identity (e.g., Apple).
6. **Cornered Resource**: Preferential access to unique talent, patent, or regulatory asset (e.g., ASML).
7. **Process Power**: Complex organizational operational capability embedded over decades (e.g., Toyota Production System).
