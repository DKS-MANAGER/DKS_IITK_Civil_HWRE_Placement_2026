### Question 1: [P0][RISK] Explain the formula for Expected Loss and how banks use it under Basel regulations.

**Direct Answer / Solution Approach:**
```text
Expected Loss $EL = PD 	imes LGD 	imes EAD$. $PD$ = Probability of Default (% chance of defaulting within 1 year). $LGD$ = Loss Given Default (1 - Recovery Rate). $EAD$ = Exposure at Default (Total outstanding loan balance). Banks use EL to determine required loan loss provisioning reserves.
```

**Interviewer Follow-Up & Deep Dive:**
> Interviewer follow-up: What is the difference between Expected Loss and Unexpected Loss? (EL is covered by provisioning/pricing; UL is covered by equity economic capital buffers).
