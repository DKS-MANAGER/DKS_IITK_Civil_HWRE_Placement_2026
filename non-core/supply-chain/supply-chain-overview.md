## What Supply Chain Does

Supply chain manages the flow of goods, information, and finances from raw materials to end customers. It's about getting the right product, to the right place, at the right time, at the right cost.

**Core responsibilities:**
- Demand forecasting
- Inventory management
- Procurement and sourcing
- Warehousing and distribution
- Transportation and logistics
- Network design
- Service level management

## Key Formulas

### EOQ (Economic Order Quantity)
```
EOQ = √(2DS / H)

D = Annual demand
S = Ordering cost per order
H = Holding cost per unit per year
```

**Example:** D = 10,000, S = ₹500, H = ₹10
EOQ = √(2 × 10,000 × 500 / 10) = √1,000,000 = 1,000 units

### Reorder Point
```
ROP = (Daily Demand × Lead Time) + Safety Stock
```

### Safety Stock
```
Safety Stock = Z × σ × √(Lead Time)

Z = Service level factor (1.65 for 95%)
σ = Demand variability
```

### Inventory Turnover
```
Inventory Turnover = COGS / Average Inventory
```

### Fill Rate / Service Level
```
Service Level = Units Delivered on Time / Total Units Ordered × 100
```

## Quantitative Problems

### Problem 1: EOQ
**Prompt:** "Annual demand is 12,000 units. Ordering cost is ₹300/order. Holding cost is ₹5/unit/year. Calculate EOQ."

**Solution:**
EOQ = √(2 × 12,000 × 300 / 5) = √1,440,000 = 1,200 units

### Problem 2: Reorder Point
**Prompt:** "Daily demand is 50 units. Lead time is 5 days. Safety stock is 100 units. Calculate reorder point."

**Solution:**
ROP = (50 × 5) + 100 = 350 units

### Problem 3: Inventory Turnover
**Prompt:** "COGS is ₹50 lakh. Average inventory is ₹10 lakh. Calculate turnover."

**Solution:**
Turnover = 50,00,000 / 10,00,000 = 5 times/year

### Problem 4: Safety Stock
**Prompt:** "Demand std dev is 20 units/day. Lead time is 4 days. Service level 95% (Z=1.65). Calculate safety stock."

**Solution:**
Safety Stock = 1.65 × 20 × √4 = 1.65 × 20 × 2 = 66 units
