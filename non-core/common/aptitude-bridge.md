## Non-Core Specific Aptitude Additions

The existing content covers standard aptitude well. For non-core, add these business-specific applications:

### Mental Math for Case Interviews

| Operation | Shortcut |
|:----------|:---------|
| Multiply by 15 | ×10 + ×5 |
| Multiply by 11 | Split digits, sum in middle (23×11 = 253) |
| Square of numbers ending in 5 | Last digit always 25, prefix = n×(n+1) (35² = 1225) |
| Divide by 5 | Multiply by 2, move decimal left 1 |
| 15% of a number | 10% + 5% |
| 25% of a number | Divide by 4 |
| 33% of a number | Divide by 3 |

### Business Math

| Concept | Formula | Quick Method |
|:--------|:--------|:-------------|
| Growth rate | (New-Old)/Old × 100 | (New/Old - 1) × 100 |
| Market share | Company/Market × 100 | Think in fractions |
| CAGR | (End/Start)^(1/n) - 1 | For small n, approximate with simple growth/n |
| Profit margin | (SP-CP)/CP × 100 | Always use CP as base |
| Discount successive | d1+d2-(d1×d2)/100 | One formula for all |
| Simple interest | P×R×T/100 | For quick estimates |
| Compound interest | P(1+R/100)^T | Use approximation for estimation |

### Unit Conversion (for Guesstimates)

| Unit | Conversion |
|:-----|:-----------|
| 1 million | 10 lakh |
| 1 billion | 100 crore |
| 1 km | 0.62 miles |
| 1 acre | 4,047 sq m ≈ 4,000 sq m |
| 1 sq ft | 0.093 sq m |
| 1 litre | 0.26 gallons |
| 1 kg | 2.2 pounds |
| 1 year | 8,760 hours ≈ 8,800 hours |
| 1 month | ~30 days ≈ 720 hours |
