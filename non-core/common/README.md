## 1. Directory Overview & Universal Core Architecture

The `non-core/common/` directory serves as the universal foundation across all corporate non-core tracks. While role-specific modules (such as SQL for Data Analysts or Guesstimates for Consultants) provide specialized domain depth, the **Common Layer** equips candidates with the foundational competencies required across every stage of the placement selection pipeline:

## 3. Recommended Study & Practice Workflow

```
Step 1: Resume Formulation (resume.md)
   ↓ Ensure every bullet has a reproducible, measured metric and clear technical-to-business translation.
Step 2: Story Banking (behavioral.md + hr.md)
   ↓ Map 5–7 authentic personal experiences across the 10 competency archetypes.
Step 3: Business Literacy & Synthesis (business-fundamentals.md + communication.md)
   ↓ Master P&L unit economics and practice top-down ("Answer-First") communication.
Step 4: Framework Selection (interview-frameworks.md + aptitude-bridge.md)
   ↓ Internalize frameworks as cognitive aids rather than rigid scripts.
Step 5: Full Mock Simulation (interview-readiness.md)
   ↓ Conduct recorded peer mocks and grade against the 100-Point Scorecard (Target: $\ge 85.0$).
```
