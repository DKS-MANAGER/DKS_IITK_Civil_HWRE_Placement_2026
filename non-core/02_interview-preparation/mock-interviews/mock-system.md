# Non-Core Mock Interview System

> 10 mock interview formats with question banks, scoring rubrics, and feedback templates. Practice like it's real.

---

## How to Use This System

1. **Choose your mock format** based on target role
2. **Set up** — find a partner, time yourself, record
3. **Run the mock** — follow the structure, ask follow-ups
4. **Score** using the rubric
5. **Debrief** — identify strengths and gaps
6. **Repeat** — practice until you're comfortable

---

## Mock Format 1: Consulting

**Duration:** 45 minutes

| Section | Time | Content |
|:--------|:-----:|:--------|
| Resume walkthrough | 5 min | "Walk me through your resume" |
| Behavioral | 10 min | 2 STAR questions + follow-ups |
| Case interview | 25 min | Profitability or market entry case |
| Fit / Closing | 5 min | "Why consulting?", "Questions for us?" |

### Sample Questions
1. Walk me through your resume.
2. Tell me about a time you led a team.
3. Why consulting? Why not stay in Civil?
4. **Case:** "A restaurant chain's profits are declining. Analyze."
5. What's your biggest weakness?
6. Do you have any questions for us?

### Scoring Rubric
| Criterion | Weight | Score (1-5) |
|:----------|:------:|:-----------:|
| Structure | 25% | __ |
| Analysis | 25% | __ |
| Communication | 20% | __ |
| Business judgment | 20% | __ |
| Fit / motivation | 10% | __ |
