## 2. Objective Pre-Placement Verification Checklist

### A. Resume & Portfolio
- [ ] Resume strictly 1 page, zero formatting inconsistencies, zero unverified or fabricated metric claims.
- [ ] Every project bullet follows: `[Action Verb] + [Technical Tool] + [Quantified Operational Impact]`.
- [ ] Civil/HWRE research translated into transferable computational/analytics frameworks (`[11_projects.md]`).

### B. Technical & Quantitative
- [ ] Solves medium-hard SQL queries (CTEs, Window functions, Cohorts) in $<10$ minutes with zero syntax errors.
- [ ] Mental math fluency: Calculates percentages, CAGR approximations, and fractions ($rac{1}{7}, rac{1}{8}$) in $<30$ seconds.
- [ ] Applied probability & statistics: Bayes' theorem, Normal distributions, and A/B test sample sizing mastered.

### C. Case & Business Problem Solving
- [ ] Clarifies goals and constructs a customized 3-branch MECE issue tree in $<90$ seconds.
- [ ] Delivers executive synthesis in $<60$ seconds using Pyramid Principle (Answer $ightarrow$ 3 Reasons $ightarrow$ Risks).
- [ ] Conducted at least 10 live peer mock interviews with detailed feedback records.

### D. Behavioral & Executive Presence
- [ ] Delivered the *"Walk me through your resume"* intro in 90 seconds smoothly without notes.
- [ ] Rehearsed high-conviction answer for *"Why transition from Civil Engineering to Non-Core?"*.
- [ ] Prepared 5 distinct STAR stories (Leadership, Failure, Conflict, Impact, Ambiguity).
