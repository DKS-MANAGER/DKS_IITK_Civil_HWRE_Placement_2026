- **Equation**: $	ext{Population} 	imes 	ext{Target Consumer \%} 	imes 	ext{Annual Consumption (Liters)} 	imes 	ext{Average Price/Liter}$.
- **Calculation**:
  - Urban population (50 Cr). Focus on traveling / on-the-go consumers (30% = 15 Cr people).
  - Average consumption: 2 bottles/month = 24 liters/year $\implies 15	ext{ Cr} 	imes 24 = 3.6	ext{ Billion Liters}$.
  - Price = 20 INR/Liter $\implies 3.6	ext{B} 	imes 20 = \mathbf{7,200	ext{ Cr INR / year}}$.
