## What a Product Manager Does

A Product Manager (PM) owns the vision, strategy, and execution of a product. They decide **what** gets built, **why**, and **for whom** — then coordinate engineering, design, and business teams to make it happen.

**Core responsibilities:**
- Define product vision and strategy
- Understand user needs (research, feedback)
- Prioritize features and build roadmaps
- Coordinate cross-functional teams
- Track metrics and iterate
- Make trade-off decisions

### Day-to-Day Work

| Task | Example | % of Time |
|:-----|:--------|:---------:|
| **User research** | Interviews, surveys, feedback analysis | 20% |
| **Prioritization** | Decide what to build next | 20% |
| **Stakeholder alignment** | Sync with engineering, design, sales | 25% |
| **Metrics analysis** | Track KPIs, analyze data | 15% |
| **Writing specs** | PRDs, requirements, user stories | 15% |
| **Launch & iteration** | Ship features, measure, iterate | 5% |

## What Recruiters Test

### Interview Process (Typical)

| Round | Format | What's Tested |
|:------|:-------|:--------------|
| **Online Assessment** | Aptitude + logical reasoning | Quantitative ability |
| **Product Sense** | "Design X", "Improve Y" | User thinking, creativity |
| **Metrics Round** | "What metrics would you track?" | Data-driven thinking |
| **Strategy Round** | "Should we enter market Y?" | Business judgment |
| **Execution Round** | "A metric fell. Diagnose." | Analytical problem solving |
| **Behavioral / Fit** | STAR questions | Leadership, teamwork, motivation |

### Core Skills Tested

| Skill | Weight | How to Prepare |
|:------|:------:|:---------------|
| **Product sense** | 25% | Practice design/improve questions |
| **Metrics** | 20% | Understand product metrics deeply |
| **Strategy** | 20% | Market analysis, competitive thinking |
| **Prioritization** | 15% | Frameworks (RICE, MoSCoW, ICE) |
| **Behavioral** | 20% | Leadership, conflict, execution stories |

## Topic Checklist

### Must Know [P0]

- [ ] Product sense framework (User → Need → Solution)
- [ ] Metrics: DAU/MAU, retention, churn, conversion, engagement
- [ ] Prioritization frameworks (RICE, MoSCoW, ICE)
- [ ] User research basics (interviews, surveys, personas)
- [ ] A/B testing basics
- [ ] North Star metric concept
- [ ] 3-4 strong behavioral stories

### Should Know [P1]

- [ ] Product strategy (market entry, competitive analysis)
- [ ] User journeys and personas
- [ ] MVP definition and scope
- [ ] Product analytics (funnel, cohort)
- [ ] Pricing basics
- [ ] Growth frameworks (AARRR)

### Good to Know [P2]

- [ ] Product launch planning
- [ ] Experimentation frameworks
- [ ] Technical literacy (APIs, databases, SDLC)
- [ ] Agile/Scrum basics

## Question Bank

### Product Sense
1. Design a feature for [user group].
2. How would you improve [product]?
3. Design a product for [specific problem].
4. What would you add to [product]?

### Metrics
5. What metrics would you track for [product]?
6. What's your North Star metric for [product]?
7. DAU dropped 15%. Diagnose.
8. How would you measure the success of a new feature?

### Strategy
9. Should [company] enter [market]?
10. How would you compete with [competitor]?
11. What's the biggest risk to [product]?

### Prioritization
12. Which feature should we build first?
13. How do you decide between two features?
14. A stakeholder wants X, users want Y. What do you do?

### Execution
15. A product metric fell. Diagnose why.
16. A feature shipped but didn't move the metric. What now?
17. How do you handle a missed deadline?

### Behavioral
18. Tell me about a product you built.
19. Describe a time you influenced without authority.
20. How do you handle disagreement with engineering?

## Scenario Questions

| Scenario | What They Test |
|:---------|:---------------|
| "A stakeholder wants a feature you disagree with." | Prioritization, communication |
| "A metric fell after a release." | Execution, root-cause analysis |
| "Two teams want different things." | Stakeholder management |
| "A feature shipped but users don't use it." | Product sense, iteration |
| "You have 3 features and time for 1." | Prioritization, trade-offs |

## Resume Tips for PM

**Emphasize:**
- User/stakeholder understanding
- Cross-functional coordination
- Decision-making and trade-offs
- Outcomes and metrics

**Bullet example:**
- "Led cross-functional research project, defining requirements and coordinating 3 departments, delivering 2 weeks ahead of schedule"
- "Prioritized features based on user feedback and impact analysis, improving [metric] by X%"
