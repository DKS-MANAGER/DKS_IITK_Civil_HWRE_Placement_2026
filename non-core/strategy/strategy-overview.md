# Strategy — Complete Preparation System

> What strategy roles do, what companies test, and how your research/analytical background positions you.

## What Strategy Roles Do

Strategy professionals analyze markets, competitors, and business models to inform long-term corporate decisions. They help companies decide where to play and how to win.

**Core responsibilities:**
- Market and competitive analysis
- Business model analysis
- Strategic planning
- Financial modeling
- M&A support
- New business development

### Day-to-Day Work

| Task | Example | % of Time |
|:-----|:--------|:---------:|
| **Market research** | "Analyze market size, trends, competitors" | 25% |
| **Analysis** | "Evaluate business opportunities" | 25% |
| **Financial modeling** | "Build revenue/cost models" | 20% |
| **Strategic planning** | "Develop growth strategy" | 15% |
| **Presentation** | "Present recommendations to leadership" | 15% |

## Strategy Frameworks

### Market Analysis
```
1. Market size (TAM/SAM/SOM)
2. Market growth (CAGR, drivers)
3. Market trends
4. Customer segments
5. Competitive landscape
6. Regulatory environment
```

### Competitive Analysis (Porter's Five Forces)
```
1. Threat of new entrants
2. Bargaining power of suppliers
3. Bargaining power of buyers
4. Threat of substitutes
5. Competitive rivalry
```

### Business Model Analysis
```
1. Value proposition (what you offer)
2. Customer segments (who you serve)
3. Revenue model (how you make money)
4. Cost structure (what it costs)
5. Distribution channels (how you reach customers)
6. Key resources and activities
```

### Growth Strategy
```
1. Market penetration
2. Market development
3. Product development
4. Diversification
```
