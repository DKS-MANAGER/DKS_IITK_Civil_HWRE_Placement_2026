## What a Product Analyst Does

A Product Analyst sits at the intersection of product management and data analysis. They use data to inform product decisions — what to build, what to improve, and whether changes worked.

**Core responsibilities:**
- Analyze user behavior and product usage
- Build and track product metrics (funnels, retention, cohorts)
- Design and analyze A/B tests
- Identify opportunities for product improvement
- Communicate insights to PMs and stakeholders

### Day-to-Day Work

| Task | Example | % of Time |
|:-----|:--------|:---------:|
| **SQL analysis** | "Pull funnel conversion by cohort" | 30% |
| **A/B test analysis** | "Did the new onboarding improve activation?" | 20% |
| **Dashboard building** | "Create product health dashboard" | 15% |
| **Cohort/retention analysis** | "Why is retention dropping for new users?" | 15% |
| **Insight communication** | "Present findings to PM team" | 15% |
| **Experimentation** | "Design test for new feature" | 5% |

## Case Questions (Product Analyst-Specific)

### Case 1: DAU Drop
**Prompt:** "DAU dropped 15% last week. Investigate."

**Structure:**
1. Define DAU precisely
2. Segment: platform, region, new vs. returning
3. Check external: seasonality, competitor, outage
4. Check internal: releases, bugs, marketing
5. Form hypothesis → test → validate

### Case 2: Conversion Drop After Release
**Prompt:** "Checkout conversion fell 10% after a new release."

**Structure:**
1. Was the release the cause? (timing correlation)
2. Segment by platform, device, user type
3. Check specific step in funnel
4. Check for bugs, UI changes, performance issues
5. Rollback vs. fix forward decision

### Case 3: Retention Up, Revenue Down
**Prompt:** "Retention improved but revenue declined. Why?"

**Structure:**
1. Define both metrics precisely
2. Is it the same users? (cohort analysis)
3. Check pricing, monetization changes
4. Check engagement vs. monetization trade-off
5. Is this a real problem or a metric artifact?
