# 🎯 Non-Core Role Selector

> Find the right non-core role for your profile. No universal rankings — only trade-offs that matter for your specific situation.

---

## How to Use This

1. **Rate yourself** honestly on each skill dimension (1-5)
2. **Identify your interests** — what work energizes you?
3. **Map your resume evidence** — what can you actually prove?
4. **Check preparation time** — how long until your interview?
5. **Pick 1-2 target roles** — focus beats scatter

## Role Deep-Dives

### 🏢 Consulting

**What you do:** Solve business problems for clients — market entry, growth strategy, cost reduction, organizational change.

**Day-to-day:** Research industries, build frameworks, analyze data, synthesize findings, present recommendations.

**Best fit if you:**
- Enjoy structured problem solving
- Can think under ambiguity
- Communicate clearly and persuasively
- Are comfortable with travel and intensity

**Civil/HWRE advantage:** Quantitative rigor, research methodology, ability to handle complex technical-to-business translation.

**What recruiters test:** Case interviews (80%), behavioral (15%), resume (5%)

→ [Consulting Overview](consulting/consulting-overview.md)

---

### 📊 Strategy

**What you do:** Analyze markets, competitors, and business models to inform long-term corporate decisions.

**Day-to-day:** Market research, competitive analysis, financial modeling, strategic recommendations.

**Best fit if you:**
- Think analytically about business
- Enjoy research and synthesis
- Can work with incomplete information
- Like big-picture thinking

**Civil/HWRE advantage:** Research skills from thesis, quantitative analysis, systems thinking.

**What recruiters test:** Case/study interviews, business knowledge, analytical thinking

→ [Strategy Overview](strategy/strategy-overview.md)

---

### 📈 Business Analyst

**What you do:** Bridge business needs and data — translate questions into analyses, analyses into decisions.

**Day-to-day:** Write SQL queries, build dashboards, analyze KPIs, support stakeholders with data.

**Best fit if you:**
- Are comfortable with SQL and Excel
- Can explain data insights to non-technical people
- Enjoy finding patterns in data
- Like structured, detail-oriented work

**Civil/HWRE advantage:** Data analysis from research, Python/MATLAB skills, systematic problem solving.

**What recruiters test:** SQL rounds, case questions, analytical reasoning, communication

→ [BA Overview](business-analyst/ba-overview.md)

---

### 📊 Data Analyst

**What you do:** Clean, analyze, and visualize data to drive business decisions.

**Day-to-day:** Data wrangling, statistical analysis, A/B testing, dashboard creation, reporting.

**Best fit if you:**
- Are strong in statistics and probability
- Can code in Python/R
- Enjoy exploratory data analysis
- Can tell stories with data

**Civil/HWRE advantage:** Statistical analysis from research, Python/MATLAB programming, experimental methodology.

**What recruiters test:** SQL, statistics, Python/Pandas, case studies, communication

→ [DA Overview](data-analyst/da-overview.md)

### 📱 Product Manager

**What you do:** Define what gets built, why, and for whom. Own the product vision and roadmap.

**Day-to-day:** User research, prioritization, stakeholder alignment, metrics tracking, cross-functional coordination.

**Best fit if you:**
- Can think from the user's perspective
- Enjoy making trade-off decisions
- Can communicate across engineering, design, and business
- Are comfortable with ambiguity

**Civil/HWRE advantage:** Project management experience, user-centric thinking (lab/stakeholder work), technical understanding.

**What recruiters test:** Product sense, metrics, strategy, behavioral, execution

→ [PM Overview](product-management/pm-overview.md)

## 🔄 Decision Framework

```
Step 1: What energizes you?
├── Solving client problems → Consulting / Strategy
├── Working with data → BA / DA / Product Analyst
├── Building products → Product Manager
├── Optimizing processes → Operations / Supply Chain
├── Managing delivery → Program / Project Management
├── Working with numbers → Finance / Risk
└── Tech + business → Technology

Step 2: What are you good at?
├── Strong math + SQL → Data Analyst / BA / Supply Chain
├── Strong communication + thinking → Consulting / Strategy / PM
├── Strong organization + coordination → Program Mgmt / Biz Ops
└── Strong quantitative + modeling → Finance / Risk / Supply Chain

Step 3: What can you prove from your resume?
├── Research/thesis → Consulting / Strategy / Risk
├── Project management → Operations / Program Mgmt
├── Programming (Python/MATLAB) → DA / BA / Technology
├── Data analysis → DA / BA / Product Analyst
└── Team leadership → Consulting / PM / Program Mgmt

Step 4: How much time do you have?
├── 1 month+ → Any role with dedicated prep
├── 2 weeks → Focus on roles matching existing skills
└── 3 days → Roles closest to your current skill set
```

*Pick 1-2 roles. Go deep. Depth beats breadth in placement preparation.*
