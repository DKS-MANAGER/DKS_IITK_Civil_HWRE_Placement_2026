# Reference Sources & Preparation Registry

> Canonical bibliographic registry of industry reference sources, case books, and interview preparation materials used across 
on-core/.

---

## 1. Case Interview & Consulting
- **Case in Point** by Marc Cosentino — Classic consulting framework guide
- **Case Interview Secrets** by Victor Cheng — Hypothesis-driven problem solving and synthesis
- **IIT Bombay & IIT Delhi Casebooks** — Student-compiled Indian campus placement cases
- **IIM Ahmedabad / Bangalore Casebooks** — Post-graduate level case interviews and business strategy

## 2. Product Management & Analytics
- **Decode and Conquer** by Lewis C. Lin — CIRCLES method for product design
- **Cracking the PM Interview** by Gayle Laakmann McDowell & Jackie Bavaro
- **SQL for Data Analytics** — Hands-on querying and window functions
- **The Lean Product Playbook** by Dan Olsen — Product-market fit and metrics

## 3. General Business & Communication
- **The Pyramid Principle** by Barbara Minto — Structured executive writing and communication
- **The Personal MBA** by Josh Kaufman — Core business mental models
- **Principles of Corporate Finance** by Brealey, Myers, Allen — Valuation and capital structure
