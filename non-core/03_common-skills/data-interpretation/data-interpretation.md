# Business Data Interpretation & KPI Evaluation

> Guide to interpreting business tables, financial graphs, executive charts, and product metrics.

---

## 1. Core Framework for Interpreting Charts & Tables

1. **Title & Axis Orientation**: Understand units (INR Cr, USD M, %, ratios), time horizons (YoY, MoM, CAGR), and baseline.
2. **Key Trend Identification**: Identify upward/downward inflections, seasonal patterns, anomalies, and outliers.
3. **Cross-Variable Synthesis**: Correlate changes in independent variables (e.g., ad spend) with dependent outcomes (e.g., CAC, LTV, churn).
4. **Business Implications**: Translate mathematical findings into executive insights (*"Why did this happen and what should we do?"*).

---

## 2. Primary Executive Chart Types
- **Waterfall Chart**: Displays cumulative step-wise breakdown of revenue-to-EBITDA or cost drivers.
- **Cohort Heatmap**: Visualizes user retention or repeat purchase decay over weekly/monthly intervals.
- **Scatter Matrix / 2x2 Quadrant**: Maps items across Effort vs. Impact, Risk vs. Return, or Growth vs. Margin.
- **Dual-Axis Line & Bar**: Tracks operational volume (bar) against efficiency percentage (line).

---

## 3. Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- Confusing correlation with causation.
- Ignoring denominator effects (percentage growth on tiny bases vs large bases).
- Overlooking survivorship bias in customer cohort tracking.
