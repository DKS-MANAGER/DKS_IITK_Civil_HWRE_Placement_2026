# 🎤 Self-Introduction System — Master Your First 2 Minutes

> **The "Tell me about yourself" question is asked in 90%+ of interviews. Your answer sets the tone for the entire interview. This system gives you everything you need to nail it.**

---

## Why This Matters

| Statistic | Implication |
|-----------|-------------|
| Asked in 90%+ of interviews | You WILL face this question |
| First 2 minutes set the tone | Strong intro = confident rest of interview |
| Interviewers decide early | Your intro influences the rest of the conversation |
| Weak intro = uphill battle | You spend the rest of the interview recovering |

---

## The Present-Past-Future Framework

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                                  │
│  PRESENT (40%)         PAST (40%)           FUTURE (20%)        │
│  ──────────────        ──────────           ────────────        │
│  • Current role         • Education          • Why this role     │
│  • Key skills           • Key projects       • What you'll bring │
│  • Major achievement    • Internships        • Connection        │
│  • What you're doing    • Publications       • Enthusiasm        │
│                                                                  │
│  [30-45 sec]           [30-45 sec]          [15-30 sec]        │
│                                                                  │
│  Total Target: 90-120 seconds                                    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 5 Industry-Specific Introductions

>
> Before IITK, I completed B.Tech in Civil Engineering with strong fundamentals in hydraulics, hydrology, and structural design. I interned at [Company] working on [specific project], and I've published a paper on [topic] in [venue].
>
> I'm drawn to [Company] because of your work in [specific area — hydropower, flood management, water treatment]. My CFD expertise combined with field experience from internships would allow me to contribute immediately to your [department/project]."

**⏱️ Target: 90-100 seconds**

## Customization Checklist

Before EACH interview, update your intro:

- [ ] **Company name** mentioned explicitly
- [ ] **Specific project/area** of company referenced (research it!)
- [ ] **Skills matched** to job description keywords
- [ ] **Quantified achievement** included (%, numbers, scale)
- [ ] **Time check**: Practice with timer, aim for 90-120 seconds
- [ ] **Natural delivery**: Not memorized, conversational tone
- [ ] **Enthusiasm**: Genuine interest visible in voice

---

## Timing Drills

### Drill 1: The 60-Second Version
Write your intro in 60 seconds. This forces you to cut to essentials.
- Present: 20 sec
- Past: 25 sec
- Future: 15 sec

### Drill 2: The 90-Second Version (Target)
The gold standard. Detailed enough to be compelling, concise enough to respect time.

### Drill 3: The 120-Second Version
The maximum. Use only if the interviewer invites a longer response.

### Drill 4: The 30-Second Elevator Pitch
For networking events, casual encounters, or "give me the quick version."

### Practice Routine
1. Write your base intro (200-250 words)
2. Record yourself on phone
3. Listen and note: filler words, pace, energy
4. Time it — cut to 90-120 seconds
5. Practice 3x daily for 1 week before interviews
6. Get feedback from 2-3 peers/mentors
7. Customize for each company (5 min per company)

## Common Mistakes & Fixes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Reciting resume chronologically | Use Present-Past-Future, highlight relevance |
| Too long (> 3 minutes) | Time yourself, cut to 90-120 seconds |
| Generic "I'm hardworking" | Replace with specific achievement |
| No company research | Mention specific project/value of company |
| Monotone delivery | Practice with energy, vary pace |
| Ending weakly | End with clear "why this role" statement |
| Starting with "My name is..." | Start with what you're doing NOW |
| No quantified results | Add numbers, percentages, scale |
| Rushing through | Speak at natural pace, pause between sections |
