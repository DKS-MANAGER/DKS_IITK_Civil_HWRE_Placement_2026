## 1-Day Plan (Interview Tomorrow)

### Morning (2 hours)
| Time | Activity | Resource |
|------|----------|----------|
| 30 min | Read rapid revision cards | [`rapid-revision-cards.md`](rapid-revision-cards.md) |
| 30 min | Perfect your 90-sec intro | [`self_intro/self-introduction-system.md`](self_intro/self-introduction-system.md) |
| 30 min | Review your 3 core stories | [`story-bank/`](story-bank/) |
| 30 min | Research the company (10 min) + prepare 3 questions | [`company-fit-analysis.md`](company-fit-analysis.md) |

### Afternoon (2 hours)
| Time | Activity | Resource |
|------|----------|----------|
| 30 min | Practice 10 most likely questions | [`rapid-revision-cards.md`](rapid-revision-cards.md) Card 3 |
| 30 min | Practice answers aloud (record + review) | [`answer-quality-checker.md`](answer-quality-checker.md) |
| 30 min | Review frameworks (STAR, CARL, SOAR) | [`frameworks/answer-framework-library.md`](frameworks/answer-framework-library.md) |
| 30 min | Review resume defense (every line) | [`resume-defense-system.md`](resume-defense-system.md) |

### Evening (1 hour)
| Time | Activity | Resource |
|------|----------|----------|
| 30 min | Mock interview (solo, recorded) | [`mock-interviews/mock-interview-system.md`](mock-interviews/mock-interview-system.md) |
| 20 min | Review feedback + improve weak answers | [`answer-quality-checker.md`](answer-quality-checker.md) |
| 10 min | Prepare outfit, documents, route | — |

### Night Before
- [ ] Get a good night's sleep (7-8 hours)
- [ ] Lay out clothes and documents
- [ ] Set alarm with buffer time
- [ ] Review rapid revision cards one final time
