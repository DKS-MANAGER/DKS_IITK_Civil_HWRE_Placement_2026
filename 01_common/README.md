### Route 1: The 30-Day Comprehensive Placement Mastery Plan

*Designed for complete foundation building starting 4–6 weeks prior to Phase 1 campus recruitment.*

- **Days 1–5: Quantitative Foundations (Arithmetic)**
  - Study: [`aptitude/quantitative/number-system.md`](aptitude/quantitative/number-system.md), [`percentages.md`](aptitude/quantitative/percentages.md), [`ratio-proportion.md`](aptitude/quantitative/ratio-proportion.md), [`averages.md`](aptitude/quantitative/averages.md).
  - Solve: 25 foundational + intermediate problems per topic.
  - Review: Deviation principle, net percentage change formula ($a + b + \frac{ab}{100}$).
- **Days 6–10: Commercial Mathematics & Motion**
  - Study: [`aptitude/quantitative/profit-loss.md`](aptitude/quantitative/profit-loss.md), [`simple-compound-interest.md`](aptitude/quantitative/simple-compound-interest.md), [`time-work.md`](aptitude/quantitative/time-work.md), [`time-speed-distance.md`](aptitude/quantitative/time-speed-distance.md).
  - Practice: LCM work rate models, relative speed, circular track meeting times.
- **Days 11–15: Modern Mathematics & Probability**
  - Study: [`permutations-combinations.md`](aptitude/quantitative/permutations-combinations.md), [`probability.md`](aptitude/quantitative/probability.md), [`mixtures-alligation.md`](aptitude/quantitative/mixtures-alligation.md), [`mensuration.md`](aptitude/quantitative/mensuration.md).
  - Solve: Multiset letter arrangements, conditional probability, alligation cross-rule.
- **Days 16–20: Data Interpretation & Placement Math**
  - Study: [`placement-math/business-engineering-math.md`](placement-math/business-engineering-math.md), [`placement-math/data-interpretation/data-interpretation.md`](placement-math/data-interpretation/data-interpretation.md), [`placement-math/Excel.md`](placement-math/Excel.md).
  - Practice: Break-even analysis, CAGR, Fermi estimation for Indian demographics, pivot table formulas.
- **Days 21–25: Logical Reasoning & Verbal Ability**
  - Study: [`aptitude/logical-reasoning/seating-arrangement.md`](aptitude/logical-reasoning/seating-arrangement.md), [`syllogisms.md`](aptitude/logical-reasoning/syllogisms.md), [`coding-decoding.md`](aptitude/logical-reasoning/coding-decoding.md), [`aptitude/verbal/critical-reasoning.md`](aptitude/verbal/critical-reasoning.md), [`grammar-error-spotting.md`](aptitude/verbal/grammar-error-spotting.md).
  - Practice: 8-person circular/linear arrangement grids, Venn syllogism truth matrices.
- **Days 26–28: Full-Length Timed Mock Testing**
  - Attempt under 60-minute strict exam timer:
    - [`aptitude/mocks/full-placement-mock-01.md`](aptitude/mocks/full-placement-mock-01.md)
    - [`aptitude/mocks/full-placement-mock-02.md`](aptitude/mocks/full-placement-mock-02.md)
    - [`aptitude/mocks/full-placement-mock-03.md`](aptitude/mocks/full-placement-mock-03.md)
  - Post-Mortem: Calculate negative marks ($-0.25$ per incorrect answer); categorize errors into Concept, Calculation, or Time Management.
- **Days 29–30: Interview & Communication Architecture**
  - Finalize 1-page resume using [`resume/resume-template.md`](resume/resume-template.md).
  - Master 90-second self-introduction using [`interview-fundamentals/self-intro-template.md`](interview-fundamentals/self-intro-template.md).
  - Rehearse 5 core STAR stories (Leadership, Failure, Conflict, Technical Setback, Integrity) via [`behavioral/README.md`](behavioral/README.md).

### Route 4: The 24-Hour Pre-Assessment Checklist

1. Review the universal Reciprocal Fractions ($\frac{1}{6}$ to $\frac{1}{19}$) and mental squaring tricks ($x5^2 = x(x+1) \cdot 25$).
2. Review the Joukowsky equation ($c = \sqrt{K_w / [\rho(1 + KD/Et)]}$), Euler pump laws, and Darcy's porous-medium law ($q = -K \nabla h$).
3. Keep scrap paper structured in neat numbered grids during tests to eliminate transcription errors.
4. If an arrangement puzzle fails to untangle in 3 minutes during an interleaved test, mark for review and skip to direct arithmetic MCQs.
5. Rehearse your 90-second self-introduction aloud three times: Background $\to$ Academic Rigor at IIT Kanpur $\to$ Research & Projects $\to$ Target Alignment.
