# PLACEMENT COMMUNICATION

> **Professional communication for the placement process** — recruiter emails, interview etiquette, scheduling, and follow-up. Keep it practical and concise.

---

## ✉️ Professional Email Templates

### 1. Application / Cover Email

```
Subject: Application for [Role] — [Your Name] — [Batch]

Dear [Recruiter Name / Hiring Team],

I am [Your Name], a [Degree] student at IIT Kanpur ([Batch]).
I am writing to apply for the [Role] position at [Company].

I have attached my resume for your consideration. My background
in [key skill/domain] and my work on [key project] align with
the requirements of this role.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I can contribute
to [Company]. Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Phone] | [Email] | [LinkedIn]
```

### 2. Follow-Up After Interview

```
Subject: Thank You — [Role] Interview — [Your Name]

Dear [Interviewer Name],

Thank you for taking the time to interview me for the [Role]
position on [Date]. I enjoyed learning more about [Company]
and the [specific topic discussed].

I was particularly interested in [specific detail from interview].
I am confident my experience with [skill] would allow me to
contribute to [Company]'s [goal/project].

Please let me know if you need any additional information.
I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

### 3. Scheduling Request

```
Subject: Interview Scheduling — [Role] — [Your Name]

Dear [Recruiter Name],

Thank you for the opportunity to interview for the [Role]
position. I am available on the following days/times:
[Day 1, Time] · [Day 2, Time] · [Day 3, Time].

Please let me know which slot works best. I am flexible and
happy to accommodate your schedule.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

### 4. Status Inquiry (Polite)

```
Subject: Follow-Up on Application — [Role] — [Your Name]

Dear [Recruiter Name],

I hope this message finds you well. I applied for the [Role]
position on [Date] and wanted to check on the status of my
application.

I remain very interested in the opportunity and would be happy
to provide any additional information needed.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## 🎩 Interview Etiquette

### Before
- [ ] Confirm interview time + location (email or portal)
- [ ] Arrive 15 minutes early
- [ ] Dress formally, professionally
- [ ] Bring multiple resume copies + ID

### During
- [ ] Greet confidently: "Good morning, thank you for this opportunity"
- [ ] Maintain eye contact, sit upright
- [ ] Listen fully before answering
- [ ] If you don't know, say so honestly + show your thinking
- [ ] Ask 2–3 thoughtful questions at the end

### After
- [ ] Thank the interviewer
- [ ] Send a thank-you email within 24 hours
- [ ] Note down questions asked (for postmortem)

---

## 📅 Scheduling & Follow-Up

| Situation | Action |
|:----------|:-------|
| Multiple offers | Communicate decisions professionally, don't ghost |
| Need to reschedule | Inform ASAP, propose alternatives |
| No response after follow-up | Wait 5–7 days before a second follow-up |
| Offer received | Confirm acceptance in writing, ask about next steps |

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Informal language / slang in emails
- ❌ Typos and grammatical errors (proofread!)
- ❌ Ghosting recruiters
- ❌ Arguing with interviewers
- ❌ Over-sharing personal details
- ❌ Being late without notice
