# 🎭 Aptitude Role Matrix

> Which aptitude topics matter for which placement role.
> Labels: `Critical` · `Important` · `Useful`
> This lets students prioritize without duplicating aptitude material.

---

## Legend

- **Critical** — high frequency, must master
- **Important** — frequently tested, strong priority
- **Useful** — occasionally tested, good to know

## How to Use

1. Find your target role column.
2. Master all **Critical** topics first.
3. Then cover **Important** topics.
4. **Useful** topics only if time permits.

This avoids duplicating aptitude material — one canonical source, many users.
