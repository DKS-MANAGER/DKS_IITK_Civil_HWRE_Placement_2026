# 🚀 Aptitude Roadmap

> A structured progression from fundamentals to placement-ready, optimized for engineering placement assessments (speed + accuracy + arithmetic + logical reasoning).

---

## The Four Stages

```
BEGINNER → INTERMEDIATE → ADVANCED → PLACEMENT READY
```

---

## Stage 1: Beginner — Fundamentals

**Goal:** Build a solid conceptual foundation in core arithmetic and basic reasoning.

| Focus | Topics | Target |
|:------|:-------|:-------|
| Arithmetic | Percentages, Ratio & Proportion, Averages, Number System | Understand concepts, solve easy questions |
| Reasoning | Series, Coding-Decoding, Direction Sense, Order & Ranking | Recognize patterns, solve easy questions |
| Verbal | Vocabulary, basic grammar | Build word power |

**Practice:** 10–15 easy questions per topic. No time pressure yet.

**Exit criteria:** Can solve easy questions in all P0 topics without referring to formulas.

---

## Stage 2: Intermediate — Mixed Practice + Time Constraints

**Goal:** Combine topics, introduce time pressure, learn shortcuts.

| Focus | Topics | Target |
|:------|:-------|:-------|
| Arithmetic | Profit/Loss, Time & Work, TSD, SI/CI, Mixtures | Solve medium questions with shortcuts |
| Reasoning | Seating Arrangement, Puzzles, Syllogism, Blood Relations | Solve medium puzzles |
| DI | Tables, Bar/Line, Pie Charts | Read data fast, approximate |
| Verbal | RC, Sentence Completion, Para Jumbles | Medium passages |

**Practice:** Mixed sets of 20–30 questions. Start timing (90 sec/question).

**Exit criteria:** Can solve medium questions within time limits; knows when to skip.

---

## Stage 3: Advanced — Hard Questions + Full Mocks

**Goal:** Handle hard questions, build stamina, master question selection.

| Focus | Topics | Target |
|:------|:-------|:-------|
| Arithmetic | All P0 + P1 topics | Hard questions, multi-step problems |
| Advanced Quant | P&C and Probability | [Advanced P&C Problem Bank](quantitative/advanced-pnc-problem-bank.md), [Advanced Prob & Stats Bank](quantitative/advanced-probability-statistics-problem-bank.md) |
| Reasoning | Complex puzzles, advanced syllogism | Hard arrangements |
| DI | Caselets, mixed graphs, missing-data | Complex calculations |
| Verbal | Critical reasoning, inference, tone | Hard passages |

**Practice:** Full mock tests under real time pressure. Analyze every error.

**Exit criteria:** Consistent 70%+ accuracy on full mocks.

---

## Stage 4: Placement Ready — High Accuracy + Error Correction

**Goal:** Peak performance — high accuracy, speed, and question selection.

| Focus | Target |
|:------|:-------|
| Timed tests | 80%+ accuracy on timed mocks ([Full Mocks 01–05](mocks/), [Hard Mock 01](mocks/hard-placement-mock-01.md), [Expert Mock 01](mocks/expert-placement-mock-01.md)) |
| Error correction | Log every mistake, reattempt at 1/3/7 days in [ERROR_LOG.md](ERROR_LOG.md) |
| Question selection | Know which questions to skip in 10 seconds |
| Rapid revision | Use [RAPID_REVISION.md](rapid-revision/RAPID_REVISION.md) in final days |

**Exit criteria:** Can complete a full mock with time to spare and high accuracy.

## 🎯 The 4-Level Difficulty Framework (L1–L4)

All practice problems, topic modules, and mock exams are calibrated against four distinct cognitive levels:

- **L1 — Fundamentals**: Tests definitions, basic formulas, and direct single-concept applications (e.g., standard factorial calculations, independent coin tosses).
- **L2 — Standard Placement**: Tests typical campus aptitude questions requiring reliable execution of 1–2 sequential steps (e.g., circular seating, Bayes' rule on 2 urns, standard binomial PMF).
- **L3 — Advanced Placement**: Tests multi-step reasoning, hidden constraints, competing solution methods, non-obvious setups, and efficient calculation (e.g., gap method on cycles, derangements, Poisson rate scaling, order statistics, hypothesis test power).
- **L4 — Expert Quantitative Reasoning**: Tests unfamiliar combinations of concepts, edge cases, conditional information asymmetry, proof-like elimination, optimization, and stopping times (e.g., double-sided bounded stars and bars via PIE, Markov chain stationary distributions, Gambler's ruin with drift, Simpson's paradox, collider conditioning).

## 🛣️ 12-Stage Mastery Pathway (P&C, Probability & Quantitative Reasoning)

For high-selectivity quant, analytics, and strategy consulting assessments, follow this sequential 12-stage curriculum:

1. **Counting Foundations & Factorials**: Addition/multiplication principles, factorials, permutations of distinct and repeated elements.
2. **Permutations & Combinations Core**: Tie/string method, gap method, combinations, binomial coefficients.
3. **Restricted Arrangements & Circular Cases**: Adjacency/non-adjacency, alternating seating, directional vs flippable circular permutations.
4. **Complement Counting & Inclusion-Exclusion**: PIE for 2, 3, and $n$ sets, derangements ($D_n$).
5. **Distribution of Objects & Stars and Bars**: Identical objects into distinct bins, non-negative vs strictly positive solutions, bounded allocations via PIE.
6. **Basic & Conditional Probability**: Kolmogorov axioms, reduced sample spaces, conditional probability definitions.
7. **Bayes' Theorem & Independence**: Law of Total Probability, posterior odds, diagnostic test base-rate fallacy.
8. **Discrete Distributions & Expectation**: Binomial, Poisson, Geometric, Negative Binomial, Hypergeometric, Linearity of Expectation.
9. **Advanced Combinatorial Probability**: Indicator random variables, occupancy, coupon collector, birthday collision, matching games.
10. **Statistical Interpretation & Inference**: Confidence intervals, $p$-values, Type I/II errors, statistical power, effect sizes, Simpson's paradox, regression to the mean.
11. **Mixed Advanced Problem Sets**: Solve all 60 problems in [Advanced P&C Problem Bank](quantitative/advanced-pnc-problem-bank.md) and all 60 problems in [Advanced Probability & Statistics Problem Bank](quantitative/advanced-probability-statistics-problem-bank.md).
12. **Timed Placement Mocks & Diagnostic Review**: Complete [Hard Mock 01](mocks/hard-placement-mock-01.md) and [Expert Mock 01](mocks/expert-placement-mock-01.md) with disciplined timing and error logging.

## 📐 Observable Topic Readiness Criteria

- **L1 Readiness:** Can state definitions without hesitation, apply basic formulas, and solve single-step textbook examples within 45 seconds.
- **L2 Readiness:** Can solve standard multi-step placement screening questions independently, correctly identifying formulas without scratch hints.
- **L3 Readiness:** Can handle multi-step problems with non-obvious constraints, select the most efficient method (e.g., complement counting over complex casework), and finish within 90 seconds.
- **L4 Readiness:** Can deconstruct novel mixed-concept problems, justify the sample space or count rigorously, eliminate edge-case traps (e.g., overcounting, collider bias), and defend the mathematical derivation.

## Key Skills to Develop

1. **Question selection** — scan, identify easy wins, skip time-sinks
2. **Approximation** — estimate before calculating
3. **Elimination** — rule out wrong options fast
4. **Time allocation** — spend time where marks are easiest
5. **Accuracy vs speed** — never sacrifice accuracy for speed on easy questions
