## Comprehensive Worked Solutions

### Q1: Stokes' Settling Law Dimensional Derivation
- **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
- **Derivation:** In creeping laminar flow ($Re_p < 0.1$), drag force $F_D = 3\pi \mu d w_s$. Submerged gravitational buoyant weight $F_g = \frac{\pi}{6} d^3 (\rho_s - \rho) g = \frac{\pi}{6} d^3 \rho g'$.
- Equating $F_D = F_g$:
  $$3\pi \mu d w_s = \frac{\pi}{6} d^3 \rho g' \implies w_s = \frac{g' d^2 \rho}{18 \mu}.$$
- Therefore, $w_s \propto \frac{g' d^2 \rho}{\mu}$.

### Q2: Buckingham $\Pi$ Theorem
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** Total variables $n = 6$: $\{y_2, y_1, V_1, g, \rho, \mu\}$.
- Fundamental dimensions $m = 3$: $[M, L, T]$.
- Number of independent dimensionless $\Pi$-groups $= n - m = 6 - 3 = 3$ (typically Froude number $Fr_1$, Reynolds number $Re_1$, and depth ratio $y_2/y_1$).

### Q3: Volumetric Discharge Conversion
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:**
  $$1\text{ cumec} = 1\text{ m}^3/\text{s} = 1,000\text{ litres/second}.$$
  $$\text{Seconds per day} = 24 \times 3,600 = 86,400\text{ seconds}.$$
  $$1\text{ cumec} = 86,400 \times 1,000 = 86,400,000\text{ litres/day} = 86.4\text{ MLD}.$$
  $$2.50\text{ cumecs} = 2.50 \times 86.4 = 216.0\text{ MLD}.$$

### Q4: Hydrostatic Pressure to Head Equivalence
- **Correct Answer:** **(C)**
- **Derivation:**
  $$P = \rho g h \implies h = \frac{P}{\rho g} = \frac{500,000\text{ N/m}^2}{1000 \times 9.81} = \frac{500}{9.81} = 50.968\text{ m} \approx 50.97\text{ m}.$$

### Q5: Manning's Equation for Uniform Flow
- **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
- **Derivation:**
  - Wetted Area $A = B \times y = 4.0 \times 2.0 = 8.0\text{ m}^2$.
  - Wetted Perimeter $P = B + 2y = 4.0 + 2(2.0) = 8.0\text{ m}$.
  - Hydraulic Radius $R = \frac{A}{P} = \frac{8.0}{8.0} = 1.0\text{ m}$.
  - Slope term: $\sqrt{S_0} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{1600}} = \frac{1}{40} = 0.025$.
  - Discharge:
    $$Q = \frac{1}{n} A R^{2/3} S_0^{1/2} = \frac{1}{0.020} \times 8.0 \times (1.0)^{2/3} \times 0.025 = 50 \times 8.0 \times 0.025 = 10.0\text{ m}^3/\text{s}.$$

### Q6: Rational Method Peak Runoff
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:**
  $$Q_p = \frac{C \cdot I \cdot A}{3.6} = \frac{0.70 \times 60 \times 1.8}{3.6} = \frac{42 \times 1.8}{3.6} = \frac{75.6}{3.6} = 21.0\text{ m}^3/\text{s}.$$

### Q7: Hydraulic Jump Energy Head Loss
- **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
- **Derivation:**
  $$\Delta E = \frac{(y_2 - y_1)^3}{4 y_1 y_2} = \frac{(2.00 - 0.50)^3}{4 \times 0.50 \times 2.00} = \frac{(1.50)^3}{4.00} = \frac{3.375}{4.00} = 0.84375\text{ m} \approx 0.844\text{ m}.$$

### Q8: Critical Flow Minimum Specific Energy
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** In a rectangular channel, minimum specific energy is exactly $1.5$ times critical depth:
  $$E_{\text{min}} = \frac{3}{2} y_c = 1.5 \times 0.96 = 1.44\text{ m}.$$

### Q9: Prismoidal Earthwork Embankment Volume
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:**
  $$V = \frac{L}{6}(A_1 + 4A_m + A_2) = \frac{100}{6} [40.0 + 4(65.0) + 80.0] = \frac{100}{6} [40 + 260 + 80] = \frac{100}{6} [380] = \frac{38,000}{6} = 6,333.33\text{ m}^3.$$

### Q10: Soil Compaction Mass Conservation
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** Dry mass of soil remains constant:
  $$M_d = V_{\text{compacted}} \times \gamma_{d,\text{compacted}} = V_{\text{borrow}} \times \gamma_{d,\text{borrow}}$$
  $$50,000 \times 18.0 = V_{\text{borrow}} \times 15.0 \implies V_{\text{borrow}} = 50,000 \times \frac{18.0}{15.0} = 50,000 \times 1.20 = 60,000\text{ m}^3.$$

### Q11: Concrete Mix Water Volumetrics
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:**
  $$\text{Water Mass} = \text{Cement Mass} \times (w/c) = 380\text{ kg} \times 0.45 = 171.0\text{ kg}.$$
- Since water density is $1.0\text{ kg/litre}$, water volume $= 171\text{ litres}$.

### Q12: Flownet Seepage Equation
- **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
- **Derivation:**
  $$q = K \cdot H \cdot \left(\frac{N_f}{N_d}\right) = (2.0 \times 10^{-6}) \times 20.0 \times \left(\frac{4}{16}\right) = 4.0 \times 10^{-5} \times 0.25 = 1.0 \times 10^{-5}\text{ m}^3/\text{s/m}.$$

### Q13: Confined Aquifer Thiem Equation
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:**
  $$Q = \frac{2\pi K b (s_1 - s_2)}{\ln(r_2 / r_1)}$$
  $$1800 = \frac{2\pi \times K \times 15 \times (2.5 - 1.0)}{\ln(100/20)} = \frac{2 \times 3.1416 \times K \times 15 \times 1.5}{1.609} = \frac{141.37 K}{1.609} \approx 87.86 K$$
  $$K = \frac{1800}{87.86} = 20.487\text{ m/day} \approx 20.5\text{ m/day}.$$

### Q14: Contour End-Area Storage Capacity
- **Correct Answer:** **(C)**
- **Derivation:**
  $$V = \frac{h}{2}(A_1 + A_2) = \frac{10\text{ m}}{2}(1.0 + 1.6)\text{ km}^2 = 5 \times 2.6 = 13.0\text{ km}^2\cdot\text{m}.$$
- Since $1\text{ km}^2\cdot\text{m} = 10^6\text{ m}^2 \times 1\text{ m} = 10^6\text{ m}^3 = 1\text{ MCM}$:
  $$V = 13.0\text{ MCM}.$$

### Q15: Annual Exceedance Probability
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**

### Q16: Cumulative Hydrological Risk
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** The probability of non-exceedance in all $n = 25$ years is $(1 - p)^n = (0.98)^{25} \approx 0.6035$.
- Risk of at least one exceedance $= 1 - (1 - p)^n = 1 - 0.6035 = 0.3965 = 39.65\%$.

### Q17: Net Present Value Analysis
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:**
  $$\text{PV of Operating Inflows} = ₹3.0\text{ Cr} \times 3.1699 = ₹9.5097\text{ Cr}.$$
  $$\text{NPV} = \text{PV of Inflows} - \text{Initial Outlay} = 9.51 - 10.00 = -₹0.49\text{ Crores}.$$
- Since $\text{NPV} < 0$, the project is economically unviable at a $10\%$ discount rate.

### Q18: Benefit-Cost Ratio

- Since $\text{BCR} = 1.60 > 1.0$, the flood protection project is economically viable.

### Q19: Straight-Line Asset Depreciation

### Q23: Terzaghi Consolidation Drainage Path Scaling
- **Correct Answer:** **(D)**

  $$\frac{t_1}{d_1^2} = \frac{t_2}{d_2^2} \implies t_2 = t_1 \times \left(\frac{d_2}{d_1}\right)^2$$
- For Layer 1: double drainage, $d_1 = 4.0 / 2 = 2.0\text{ m}$; $t_1 = 36\text{ months}$.

  $$t_2 = 36 \times \left(\frac{8.0}{2.0}\right)^2 = 36 \times (4)^2 = 36 \times 16 = 576\text{ months}.$$
