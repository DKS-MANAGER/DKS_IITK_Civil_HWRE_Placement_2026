## Comprehensive Worked Solutions

### Q1: Product Constancy & Expenditure Invariance
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** Total expenditure $E = P \times Q$. If price rises by $25\% = \frac{1}{4}$, the new price multiplier is $1 + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{5}{4}$.
- To keep $E$ constant: $Q_{\text{new}} = \frac{E}{P \times 1.25} = \frac{4}{5} Q_{\text{initial}} = 0.80 Q_{\text{initial}}$.
- Required reduction $= 1 - 0.80 = 0.20 = 20.0\%$.
- **Distractor Post-Mortem:** (A) $25\%$ is the naive trap assuming percentage reductions mirror percentage increases linearly.

### Q2: Alternate Day Work LCM Pacing Cycle
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** Assume total work units $= \text{LCM}(18, 24) = 72\text{ units}$.
- Daily efficiencies: Rate of $A = \frac{72}{18} = 4\text{ units/day}$; Rate of $B = \frac{72}{24} = 3\text{ units/day}$.
- In a 2-day cycle ($A$ then $B$): Work completed $= 4 + 3 = 7\text{ units}$.
- In $10$ full 2-day cycles ($20\text{ days}$): Work completed $= 10 \times 7 = 70\text{ units}$.
- Remaining work $= 72 - 70 = 2\text{ units}$.
- On Day 21, Contractor $A$ takes over. $A$ completes $4\text{ units/day}$, so $A$ needs $\frac{2}{4} = 0.5\text{ days}$.
- Total time required $= 20 + 0.5 = 20.5\text{ days}$.
- The question asks on which day the casting will be completely finished: since the work completes halfway through Day 21, it is finished on the **$21\text{st day}$**.

### Q3: Harmonic Mean Average Speed
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** For equal distances $D$, average speed is given by the harmonic mean:
  $$V_{\text{avg}} = \frac{2 V_1 V_2}{V_1 + V_2} = \frac{2 \times 60 \times 40}{60 + 40} = \frac{4800}{100} = 48.0\text{ km/h}.$$
- **Distractor Post-Mortem:** (A) $50\text{ km/h}$ is the arithmetic mean, which fails because more time is spent driving at the lower speed.

### Q4: 3-Year Compound vs Simple Interest Difference
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** The difference between CI and SI for 3 years is:
  $$\Delta = P \left(\frac{r}{100}\right)^2 \left(3 + \frac{r}{100}\right)$$
  $$\Delta = 10,000 \times (0.10)^2 \times (3 + 0.10) = 10,000 \times 0.01 \times 3.10 = 100 \times 3.10 = \text{INR } 310.$$

### Q5: Repeated Admixture Dilution & Replacement
- **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
- **Derivation:** When volume $x$ is removed and replaced with water $n$ times from total volume $V$:
  $$V_{\text{rem}} = V \left(1 - \frac{x}{V}\right)^n = 80 \left(1 - \frac{8}{80}\right)^3 = 80 \times (0.9)^3 = 80 \times 0.729 = 58.32\text{ litres}.$$

### Q6: Fraudulent Weights & True Margin
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** The vendor supplies $900\text{ kg}$ of goods while charging for $1,000\text{ kg}$.
- Profit percentage $= \frac{\text{Stated Weight} - \text{True Weight}}{\text{True Weight}} \times 100\% = \frac{1000 - 900}{900} \times 100\% = \frac{100}{900} \times 100\% = 11.11\%$.

### Q7: Deviation Balance in Cohort Averages

- A drop of $1.0\text{ year}$ across $25$ individuals represents a total aggregate age drop of:

- Therefore, the incoming engineer must be $25\text{ years}$ younger than the departing director:
  $$\text{Age}_{\text{new}} = 52 - 25 = 27\text{ years}.$$

### Q8: Rates with Opposing Outlets
- **Correct Answer:** **(C)**

### Q9: Permutation with Restricted Block Derangement
- **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
- **Derivation:** HYDRAULICS has 10 distinct letters: H, Y, D, R, A, U, L, I, C, S (all 10 letters are unique).
- Total unrestricted arrangements $= 10! = 3,628,800$.
- Treat the 4 vowels [Y, A, U, I] as a single block. Together with the 6 consonants, we have $7$ entities.
- Arrangements with all 4 vowels together $= 7! \times 4! = 5,040 \times 24 = 120,960$.
- Ways where vowels are NOT all together $= 3,628,800 - 120,960 = 3,507,840$.

### Q10: Conditional Combinatorial Committee Selection
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** Total available: $7$ Hydraulic ($H$), $5$ Geotechnical ($G$). Committee size $= 5$.
- We require at least $3$ $H$:
  1. $3H$ and $2G$: $\binom{7}{3} \times \binom{5}{2} = 35 \times 10 = 350$.
  2. $4H$ and $1G$: $\binom{7}{4} \times \binom{5}{1} = 35 \times 5 = 175$.
  3. $5H$ and $0G$: $\binom{7}{5} \times \binom{5}{0} = 21 \times 1 = 21$.
- Total ways $= 350 + 175 + 21 = 546$.

### Q11: Bayes' Theorem with Low Prior Base Rate
- **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
- **Derivation:**
  - $P(\text{Crack}) = 0.02$, $P(\text{Sound}) = 0.98$.
  - $P(\text{Alarm} \mid \text{Crack}) = 0.95$.
  - $P(\text{Alarm} \mid \text{Sound}) = 0.05$.
  - Total $P(\text{Alarm}) = (0.02 \times 0.95) + (0.98 \times 0.05) = 0.0190 + 0.0490 = 0.0680$.
  - $P(\text{Crack} \mid \text{Alarm}) = \frac{0.0190}{0.0680} = \frac{19}{68} \approx 27.94\%$.

### Q12: Binomial Distribution with Tail Probabilities
- **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
- **Derivation:** Probability of station success $p = 1 - 0.20 = 0.80$. $q = 0.20$, $n = 5$.
  - $P(X \ge 4) = P(X = 4) + P(X = 5)$.
  - $P(X = 4) = \binom{5}{4} (0.8)^4 (0.2)^1 = 5 \times 0.4096 \times 0.2 = 0.4096$.
  - $P(X = 5) = \binom{5}{5} (0.8)^5 = 0.32768$.
  - Total $P(X \ge 4) = 0.4096 + 0.32768 = 0.73728$.

### Q13: Fermat's Little Theorem Modulo Primes
- **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
- **Derivation:** Fermat's Little Theorem states that if $p$ is prime and $\gcd(a, p) = 1$, then $a^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod p$.
- Since $101$ is prime and $\gcd(7, 101) = 1$, $7^{101-1} = 7^{100} \equiv 1 \pmod{101}$.

### Q14: Legendre's Formula for Prime Powers in Factorials
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** Highest power $E_3(100!) = \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \lfloor \frac{100}{3^k} \rfloor$:
  $$E_3(100!) = \left\lfloor\frac{100}{3}\right\rfloor + \left\lfloor\frac{100}{9}\right\rfloor + \left\lfloor\frac{100}{27}\right\rfloor + \left\lfloor\frac{100}{81}\right\rfloor = 33 + 11 + 3 + 1 = 48.$$

### Q15: Prime Factor Pairs for Trailing Zeros
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** Trailing zeros are governed by the number of factors of $10 = 2 \times 5$, so $N_0 = \min(n_2, n_5)$.
  - Factors of $5$: $125^{12} = (5^3)^{12} = 5^{36}$; $55^6 = (5 \times 11)^6 = 5^6 \implies n_5 = 36 + 6 = 42$.
  - Factors of $2$: $64^7 = (2^6)^7 = 2^{42} \implies n_2 = 42$.
  - Therefore, $N_0 = \min(42, 42) = 42$.

### Q16: Vieta's Formulas & Symmetric Polynomials
- **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
- **Derivation:** For $2x^2 - 7x + 4 = 0$, by Vieta's formulas: $\alpha + \beta = \frac{7}{2}$ and $\alpha \beta = \frac{4}{2} = 2$.
  $$\frac{\alpha}{\beta} + \frac{\beta}{\alpha} = \frac{\alpha^2 + \beta^2}{\alpha \beta} = \frac{(\alpha + \beta)^2 - 2\alpha \beta}{\alpha \beta} = \frac{(7/2)^2 - 2(2)}{2} = \frac{49/4 - 4}{2} = \frac{33/4}{2} = \frac{33}{8}.$$

### Q17: Matrix Consistency for Infinite Solutions
- **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
- **Derivation:** For a $2 \times 2$ system to have infinitely many solutions, the two equations must be linearly dependent:
  $$\frac{k}{2} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{7}{14} \implies \frac{k}{2} = \frac{1}{2} \implies k = 1.$$

### Q18: Arithmetic-Geometric Mean Inequality ($AM \ge GM$)
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** For positive terms $4x$ and $\frac{9}{x}$, by the $AM \ge GM$ inequality:
  $$\frac{4x + \frac{9}{x}}{2} \ge \sqrt{4x \times \frac{9}{x}} = \sqrt{36} = 6 \implies 4x + \frac{9}{x} \ge 12.$$
- Equality occurs at $4x = \frac{9}{x} \implies x^2 = \frac{9}{4} \implies x = 1.5$.

### Q19: Change of Base Logarithmic Simplification
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** Express all terms with base 2:
  $$\log_4(x) = \frac{\log_2(x)}{\log_2(4)} = \frac{1}{2}\log_2(x)$$
  $$\log_{16}(x) = \frac{\log_2(x)}{\log_2(16)} = \frac{1}{4}\log_2(x)$$
  $$\log_2(x) + \frac{1}{2}\log_2(x) + \frac{1}{4}\log_2(x) = \left(1 + \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4}\right)\log_2(x) = \frac{7}{4}\log_2(x)$$
- Equating to $\frac{21}{4}$:
  $$\frac{7}{4}\log_2(x) = \frac{21}{4} \implies \log_2(x) = 3 \implies x = 2^3 = 8.$$

### Q20: Pythagorean Chord-Center Distance
- **Correct Answer:** **(C)**
- **Derivation:** The perpendicular from the center of a circle to a chord bisects the chord.
- Half chord length $= \frac{16}{2} = 8\text{ cm}$.
- In the right triangle formed by radius $R = 10\text{ cm}$, half chord $8\text{ cm}$, and perpendicular distance $d$:
  $$d = \sqrt{R^2 - (\text{half chord})^2} = \sqrt{10^2 - 8^2} = \sqrt{100 - 64} = \sqrt{36} = 6\text{ cm}.$$

### Q21: Angle Bisector Proportionality Theorem
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** By the Angle Bisector Theorem:
  $$\frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{AB}{AC} = \frac{12}{18} = \frac{2}{3}$$
- Since $BD + DC = BC = 15\text{ cm}$:
  $$BD = \frac{2}{2 + 3} \times 15 = \frac{2}{5} \times 15 = 6.0\text{ cm}.$$

### Q22: Volume Conservation in Solid Casting
- **Correct Answer:** **(C)**

### Q28: Definite Integral Reflection Property
- **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
- **Derivation:** Let $I = \int_{0}^{\pi/2} \frac{\sin x}{\sin x + \cos x} dx$.
- Using King's property $\int_{0}^a f(x) dx = \int_{0}^a f(a - x) dx$:
  $$I = \int_{0}^{\pi/2} \frac{\sin(\pi/2 - x)}{\sin(\pi/2 - x) + \cos(\pi/2 - x)} dx = \int_{0}^{\pi/2} \frac{\cos x}{\cos x + \sin x} dx.$$
- Adding both integrals:
  $$2I = \int_{0}^{\pi/2} \frac{\sin x + \cos x}{\sin x + \cos x} dx = \int_{0}^{\pi/2} 1 \, dx = \frac{\pi}{2} \implies I = \frac{\pi}{4}.$$

### Q29: Vector Curl Definition & Irrotational Flow
- **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
- **Derivation:** For $\vec{V} = u\hat{i} + v\hat{j}$ with $u = 3x^2 y$ and $v = x^3 - 4y$:
  $$\nabla \times \vec{V} = \left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)\hat{k}$$
  $$\frac{\partial v}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}(x^3 - 4y) = 3x^2$$
  $$\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(3x^2 y) = 3x^2$$
  $$\nabla \times \vec{V} = (3x^2 - 3x^2)\hat{k} = 0\hat{k}.$$
- The fluid velocity field is identically irrotational everywhere.
