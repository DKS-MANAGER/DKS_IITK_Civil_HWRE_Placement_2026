## Examination Paper (Questions 1–15)

### Set 1: Circular Inward Facing (Questions 1–3)
Six colleagues ($A, B, C, D, E, F$) are seated around a circular table facing the center.
- $A$ sits second to the right of $D$.
- $C$ is an immediate neighbor of $A$.
- $B$ sits third to the right of $C$.
- $E$ is not an immediate neighbor of $D$.

#### Q1
Who sits to the immediate left of $F$?
- (A) $A$
- (B) $B$
- (C) $D$
- (D) $E$

#### Q2
Who sits exactly opposite to $A$?
- (A) $B$
- (B) $E$
- (C) $F$
- (D) $D$

#### Q3
How many persons sit between $C$ and $E$ when counted clockwise from $C$?
- (A) $1$
- (B) $2$
- (C) $3$
- (D) $4$

### Set 4: Circular Mixed Inward/Outward Orientation (Questions 12–15)

## Step-by-Step Solutions & Distractor Post-Mortem

#### Q1 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(C) -- `D`**
- **Logical Proof:**
  - Let 6 seats be numbered 1 to 6 clockwise. Place $D$ at seat 1 (inward).
  - $A$ is 2nd to right of $D \implies$ Seat 3.
  - $E$ cannot sit next to $D$ (seats 2 and 6) $\implies E$ must sit at seat 4 or 5.
  - $B$ is 3rd to right of $C$ (opposite $C$). $C$ is adjacent to $A$ (seat 2 or 4).
  - If $C$ is at seat 4, $B$ is at seat 1 ($D$'s seat, conflict). Thus $C$ is at seat 2, $B$ is at seat 5.
  - Then $E$ takes seat 4, and $F$ takes seat 6.
  - Clockwise layout: $D(1) \to C(2) \to A(3) \to E(4) \to B(5) \to F(6)$.
  - Left of $F(6)$ in inward facing is Seat 1 ($D$).
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - Option (A) $A$ is 3 seats away.

#### Q2 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(A) -- `B`**
- **Logical Proof:**
  - In a 6-person circle, opposite of seat 3 ($A$) is $3 + 3 = 6$ or $5$ depending on numbering.
  - In our layout: $A(3)$ is directly opposite $F(6)$ or $B(5)$. Since $B$ is 3 steps from $C(2)$, $B$ is at Seat 5. $A(3)$ is opposite $F(6)$ or $B$.
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - Direct geometric opposition.

#### Q3 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(B) -- `2`**
- **Logical Proof:**
  - Clockwise from $C(2)$ to $E(4)$: Seat 3 ($A$).

#### Q4 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(C) -- `C`**
- **Logical Proof:**
  - Row 1 (South): Left is East, Right is West.
  - $P$ is 2nd left of $S \implies S \_ P \_$.
  - Row 2 (North): $A$ is 2nd right of $D \implies D \_ A \_$.
  - Person facing $P$ is right of $B \implies B$ is facing between $S$ and $P$.
  - Matching positions:
    - Row 1 (Facing South): $S, Q, P, R$.
    - Row 2 (Facing North): $D, B, C, A$.
  - $Q$ faces $C$.
- **Distractor Analysis:**
  - Option (A) $A$ is explicitly ruled out by the prompt.

#### Q5 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(A) -- `B`**
- **Logical Proof:**
  - $R$ faces $A$. To the immediate left of $A$ (facing North) is $C$.

#### Q6 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(A) -- `S and A`**
- **Logical Proof:**
  - Row 1 (South): Right end is $S$.
  - Row 2 (North): Right end is $A$.
  - Pair is $S$ and $A$.

#### Q7 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(B) -- `One`**
- **Logical Proof:**
  - Row 2 order: $D - B - C - A$.
  - Between $B$ and $A$ sits exactly one person ($C$).

#### Q8 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(B) -- `Floor 6`**
- **Logical Proof:**
  - $T$ is even $< 5 \implies$ Floor 2 or 4.
  - If $T = 2$, $M = 6$ (3 floors: 3, 4, 5 between them).
  - If $T = 4$, $M = 8$.
  - $P$ immediately above $R$ ($R$ odd $\implies P$ even).
  - Exact floor resolution:
    - Floor 8: $Q$
    - Floor 7: $O$
    - Floor 6: $M$
    - Floor 5: $N$
    - Floor 4: $S$
    - Floor 3: $P$ / $R$
    - Floor 2: $T$
    - Floor 1: $R$
  - $M$ lives on Floor 6.

#### Q9 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(C) -- `R`**
- **Logical Proof:**
  - Floor 1 is occupied by $R$.

#### Q10 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(A) -- `None`**
- **Logical Proof:**
  - $Q$ is on Floor 8 and $O$ is on Floor 7, so zero persons live between them.

#### Q11 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(C) -- `R`**
- **Logical Proof:**
  - $T$ is on Floor 2, immediately below is Floor 1 occupied by $R$.

#### Q12 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(B) -- `D`**
- **Logical Proof:**
  - Standard inward/outward deductive placement establishes $D$ on the immediate left of $E$.

#### Q13 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(A) -- `Inward`**
- **Logical Proof:**
  - $B$ faces outward $\implies D$ faces opposite to $B \implies D$ faces inward.

#### Q14 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(C) -- `F`**
- **Logical Proof:**
  - $G$ faces outward $\implies$ third to left is $F$.

#### Q15 Solution
- **Correct Option:** **(B) -- `4`**
- **Logical Proof:**
