# Contracts & Tendering

> Track: Core Civil Professional Skills  
> Domain: Legal, Commercial & Contract Management  
> Level: Advanced Placement / Professional Execution  

---

## 1. Overview & Fundamentals

Construction contracts govern the legal, financial, and operational relationship between the Employer (Client), the Engineer/PMC, and the Contractor. Tendering is the procurement process by which contractors bid for projects based on specifications, drawings, and commercial terms.

---

## 2. Types of Construction Contracts

| Contract Type | Risk Allocation | Best Suited For | Key Advantage / Disadvantage |
|:--------------|:----------------|:----------------|:---------------------------|
| **Item Rate** | Shared (Client pays for actual measured quantities) | Infrastructure with uncertain soil/quantity conditions | Flexible for variations / Final cost uncertain until completion |
| **Lump Sum** | Heavy Contractor Risk | Commercial buildings with finalized designs | Fixed budget for client / Contractor adds high contingency markup |
| **Cost-Plus** | Heavy Client Risk | Emergency works, R&D, or uncertain scope | Fast start / No cost incentive for contractor |
| **EPC / Turnkey** | Maximum Contractor Risk | Large industrial, power, and highway projects | Single-point responsibility / Expensive bids |

---

## 3. Tendering & Procurement Workflow

1. **Pre-qualification (PQ):** Assessing contractor technical capability, past experience, and financial standing.
2. **Invitation to Bid (ITB) / RFP:** Issuing tender documents (NIT, instructions to bidders, conditions of contract, specifications, BOQ).
3. **Bid Submission & Opening:** Technical bids opened first; financial bids opened only for technically qualified bidders.
4. **Evaluation & Award:** Lowest evaluated responsive bidder ($L1$) is typically awarded the contract.

---

## 4. Standard Contract Forms (FIDIC)

- **Red Book:** Conditions of Contract for Construction (Building and Engineering Works designed by the Employer).
- **Yellow Book:** Conditions of Contract for Plant and Design-Build (Electrical and Mechanical Works / Plant designed by the Contractor).
- **Silver Book:** Conditions of Contract for EPC / Turnkey Projects.

---

## 5. Commercial Clauses & Claims

- **Liquidated Damages (LD):** Pre-agreed compensation for delay by the contractor, typically capped at 10% of contract value.
- **Retention Money:** A percentage (e.g., 5%) withheld from running bills to guarantee defect liability during the defect liability period (DLP, usually 12 months).
- **Variation / Change Order:** Alterations to scope, design, or quantity resulting in cost/time adjustments.
- **Extension of Time (EOT):** Claims by contractor for delays caused by employer or force majeure.
