# QA/QC & Site Safety

> Track: Core Civil Professional Skills  
> Domain: Quality Assurance, Quality Control & Construction Safety  
> Level: Advanced Placement / Professional Execution  

---

## 1. Overview & Fundamentals

Quality Assurance (QA) refers to process-oriented procedures ensuring that construction quality requirements are met, while Quality Control (QC) refers to product-oriented testing and inspection activities. Site safety ensures Zero Harm to workers, equipment, and public infrastructure.

---

## 2. Quality Assurance & Quality Control (QA/QC)

### Concrete Testing Standards (IS 456 / IS 1199):
- **Cube Testing:** Cast $150\text{ mm} \times 150\text{ mm} \times 150\text{ mm}$ cubes. Tested at 7 days (expects ~67% strength) and 28 days (expects 100% characteristic strength $f_{ck}$).
- **Acceptance Criteria (IS 456):** The mean strength of a group of 4 consecutive test results must be $\ge f_{ck} + 0.825 \times \text{Standard Deviation}$ (or $f_{ck} + 3\text{ N/mm}^2$, whichever is greater).
- **Slump Cone Test (IS 1199):** Measures workability.
  - Paved roads / Mass concrete: $25 - 50\text{ mm}$
  - Beams / Slabs / Columns: $75 - 100\text{ mm}$
  - Piles / Under-water concreting: $150 - 175\text{ mm}$

### Non-Destructive Testing (NDT):
- **Rebound Hammer Test:** Surface hardness method (qualitative assessment of concrete compressive strength).
- **Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity (UPV) Test:** Measures pulse velocity through concrete to detect internal honeycombing, cracks, and voids.
- **Core Cutter Test / Core Extraction:** Direct destructive testing of hardened concrete cores.

## 3. Health, Safety & Environment (HSE)

- **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Mandatory helmets (IS 2925), safety boots, high-visibility vests, full-body harnesses for working at height ($\ge 1.8\text{ m}$), eye and ear protection.
- **Excavation Safety:** Sloping, benching, or shoring/sheet piling required for excavations deeper than $1.5\text{ m}$. Spoil banks kept at least $1\text{ m}$ away from excavation edges.
- **Scaffolding Safety:** Base plates, sole boards, toe boards, guardrails, and periodic tagging by competent scaffold inspectors.
- **Electrical Safety:** ELCBs, double-insulated tools, proper cable routing overhead or buried safely.
