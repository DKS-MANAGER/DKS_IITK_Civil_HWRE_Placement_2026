## Case 1: Design an Urban Commute Solution for Wheelchair Users

### 1. Goal & Boundary Conditions
- **Objective**: Design an accessible multi-modal mobility assistant within Google Maps or standalone app.
- **Constraints**: Focus on Tier-1 Indian metropolitan public transit ecosystems (Metro + Bus + Auto).

### 2. User Persona & Friction Points
- **Persona**: Rahul, 24-year-old student using a motorized wheelchair.
- **Core Pain Points**: Broken pedestrian curb ramps, unannounced elevator maintenance at metro stations, lack of wheelchair-accessible autos/cabs.

### 3. Proposed Solutions
1. **Crowdsourced Accessibility Heatmap**: Community-verified elevator uptime and accessible ramp routing.
2. **Dedicated Accessible Fleet Booking**: Pre-scheduled ramp-equipped micro-transit fleet integration.
3. **AR Ramp Navigation**: Live smartphone camera overlay pointing to nearest functional elevator/ramp.

### 4. North Star & Guardrails
- **North Star Metric**: Weekly Completed Accessible Trips per User.
- **Guardrail Metric**: Navigation route safety incident rate (<0.01%).
