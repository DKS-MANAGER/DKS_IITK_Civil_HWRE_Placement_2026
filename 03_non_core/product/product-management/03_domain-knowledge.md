## 1. CIRCLES Framework for Product Design
1. **C**omprehend Situation (Goal, Constraints, Scope)
2. **I**dentify User Persona (Demographics, Psychographics, Core Pain Points)
3. **R**eport Customer Needs (User journey map, friction points)
4. **C**ut Through Prioritization (High value vs. High effort matrix)
5. **L**ist Solutions (Incremental, Moonshot, Platform)
6. **E**valuate Trade-Offs (Technical feasibility, UX friction, business viability)
7. **S**ummarize & Define North Star Metric (Primary metric + Guardrail metrics)

---

## 2. Prioritization Frameworks (RICE & MoSCoW)
$$\text{RICE Score} = \frac{\text{Reach} \times \text{Impact} \times \text{Confidence}}{\text{Effort}}$$
- **Reach**: Number of users impacted per quarter.
- **Impact**: 3 = Massive, 2 = High, 1 = Medium, 0.5 = Low, 0.25 = Minimal.
- **Confidence**: 100% = High data confidence, 80% = Medium, 50% = Low.
- **Effort**: Person-months of engineering/design time.
