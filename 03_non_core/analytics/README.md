# Analytics & Decision Science Track Hub

> **Scope**: Master navigation hub and curriculum guide for IIT Kanpur placement preparation across **Analytics & Decision Science**, **Business Analyst**, and **Data Analyst** career tracks. `[PREPARATION HEURISTIC]`

---

## 1. Track Architecture & Core Distinction

The `03_non_core/analytics/` directory provides preparation pathways for three interrelated but distinct roles:

```text
03_non_core/analytics/
├── README.md                      # Master directory guide, track comparison & shared standards
├── analytics/                     # Analytics & Decision Science (Statistics, ML, A/B Testing, Python)
├── business-analyst/              # Business Analyst (Problem Structuring, Requirements, Metrics, SQL)
└── data-analyst/                  # Data Analyst (Data Quality, Advanced SQL, EDA, BI Dashboards)
```

### Track Comparison Matrix

| Track | Primary Objective | Typical Preparation Emphasis | Key Software & Tools |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Analytics & Decision Science** (`analytics/`) | Solve quantitative business problems, design experiments, build predictive models, and guide strategic decision-making. | Advanced probability & statistics, A/B testing design, statistical inference, predictive modeling, Python (Pandas/Scikit-learn), advanced SQL, product analytics cases. | Python, SQL, Git, Statsmodels, Scikit-learn, Excel |
| **Business Analyst** (`business-analyst/`) | Translate ambiguous business objectives into structured requirements, analyze operations, optimize workflows, and interface with stakeholders. | Structured problem solving (MECE, issue trees), business metrics & unit economics, requirement elicitation, process mapping, data interpretation, business case defense. | Excel (Advanced/VBA), SQL, Power BI / Tableau, Jira / Confluence |
| **Data Analyst** (`data-analyst/`) | Extract, clean, validate, transform, summarize, visualize, and explain structured and semi-structured enterprise data. | Relational database theory, data hygiene & quality assurance, complex multi-table SQL queries, exploratory data analysis, dashboard architecture, executive visualization. | SQL (PostgreSQL/Snowflake), Power BI / Tableau, Python (Pandas/Seaborn), Excel |

> [!NOTE]
> **Role Overlap**: These distinctions represent conceptual focus areas. In campus placement drives, job descriptions frequently combine aspects across tracks (e.g., a Data Analyst OA often tests SQL and business metrics identical to an Analytics OA). Candidates are advised to master the shared core competencies before specializing.

## 2. Evidentiary Standards & Factual Policy

To maintain factual integrity throughout recruitment preparation, all placement statements adhere to standardized evidentiary labels:

* `[OFFICIAL PLACEMENT SOURCE]` — Directly verified from an official campus placement circular or proforma issued by the IIT Kanpur Students' Placement Office (SPO).
* `[OFFICIAL COMPANY SOURCE]` — Confirmed from an official employer careers portal, job description, or company regulatory filing.
* `[HISTORICAL SOURCE]` — Derived from documented previous placement cycles or verified alumni interview debriefs.
* `[PREPARATION HEURISTIC]` — Pedagogical study framework, practice benchmark, or simulated problem set.
* `[UNVERIFIED]` — Data point currently unconfirmed or pending publication by recruiters.
