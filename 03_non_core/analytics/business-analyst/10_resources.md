# 10. Business Analyst: Curated Resources & Tooling

> Handpicked books, online SQL practice sandboxes, dataset platforms, and repository links for Business Analyst preparation.

---

## 1. Essential Books & Reading
- **Storytelling with Data** by Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic — The gold standard for data visualization and executive presentations.
- **SQL for Data Analysis** by Cathy Tanimura (O'Reilly) — Advanced analytical querying, window functions, and time-series manipulation.
- **Case in Point** by Marc Cosentino — Core business case deconstruction techniques.
- **Lean Analytics** by Alistair Croll & Benjamin Yoskovitz — Metrics and unit economics for modern startups and enterprises.

---

## 2. Interactive Practice Sandboxes
- **LeetCode (Database Track)**: Practice Easy/Medium SQL problems focused on `JOIN`, `RANK`, and aggregation.
- **StrataScratch**: Real SQL interview questions from Amazon, Microsoft, Uber, and Airbnb.
- **Mode Analytics SQL Tutorial**: Excellent hands-on tutorial with interactive SQL query editor and live datasets.
