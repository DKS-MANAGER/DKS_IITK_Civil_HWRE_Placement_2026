## 1. Core Business Fundamentals & Unit Economics

Every Business Analyst must instinctively deconstruct business performance into mathematical driver trees.

```text
                               PROFITABILITY DRIVER TREE
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                       NET PROFIT                                        │
│                                            │                                            │
│                    ┌───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐                    │
│                    │                                               │                    │
│              TOTAL REVENUE                                    TOTAL COSTS               │
│                    │                                               │                    │
│         ┌──────────┴──────────┐                         ┌──────────┴──────────┐         │
│         │                     │                         │                     │         │
│     VOLUME (Q)            PRICE (P)               VARIABLE COSTS         FIXED COSTS    │
│   (Active Users ×       (Base Price -           (COGS, Payment       (Tech hosting,     │
│    Orders/User)          Discounts)              gateway, Shipping)   Salaries, R&D)    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Essential Business Metrics & Equations
| Metric | Mathematical Formula | `[PREPARATION HEURISTIC]` Strategic Meaning & Context |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)** | $\sum (\text{Price} \times \text{Gross Units Sold})$ | Total value of merchandise sold through a platform before returns and discounts. |
| **Net Revenue** | $\text{GMV} \times \text{Take Rate} - \text{Discounts/Returns}$ | Actual top-line revenue captured by the marketplace platform. |
| **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**| $\frac{\text{Total Sales \& Marketing Spend}}{\text{New Customers Acquired}}$ | Marketing cost required to acquire one incremental paying user. |
| **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)** | $\frac{\text{ARPU} \times \text{Gross Margin } \%}{\text{Churn Rate}}$ | Total net gross profit generated by a customer over their entire lifecycle. |
| **LTV / CAC Ratio** | $\frac{\text{LTV}}{\text{CAC}}$ | `[RULE OF THUMB]` $<1.0$ (Unsustainable), $3.0\times$ (Healthy benchmark), $>5.0\times$ (Under-investing in growth). |
| **Contribution Margin 1 (CM1)** | $\text{Revenue} - \text{Direct Variable Product Cost (COGS)}$ | Gross margin before variable fulfillment, payment gateway, and delivery fees. |
| **Contribution Margin 2 (CM2)** | $\text{CM1} - (\text{Payment Gateway} + \text{Packaging} + \text{Last-Mile Delivery})$ | True per-order operational profit contribution before fixed overheads. |
| **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)** | $\frac{\text{Starting ARR} + \text{Expansion} - \text{Contraction} - \text{Churn}}{\text{Starting ARR}} \times 100$ | SaaS metric measuring revenue growth from existing customer cohort ($>100\%$ indicates net expansion). |
| **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)** | $\frac{\text{Revenue Generated by Campaign}}{\text{Advertising Spend}}$ | Efficiency of digital performance marketing campaigns ($>4\times$ typical e-commerce target). |

## 2. Sector-by-Sector Business Mechanics & KPIs

### 1. E-Commerce & Quick Commerce (Flipkart, Amazon, Swiggy Instamart, Zepto)
- **Core Operating Model**: Direct-to-consumer 1P inventory vs. 3P marketplace commission + advertising + seller services.
- **Critical KPIs**: Gross Margin %, Average Order Value (AOV), Return-to-Origin (RTO) %, Dark Store Picking Time (seconds), Last-Mile Cost per Order, Repeat Purchase Rate (30/60/90-day).
- **Common BA Problem**: *"Cart abandonment increased from 68% to 74% on the Android app after the last release. Diagnose."*

### 2. SaaS & Enterprise Software (Salesforce, Postman, BrowserStack)
- **Core Operating Model**: Subscription recurring licenses (monthly/annual) or consumption/usage-based API billing.
- **Critical KPIs**: Monthly/Annual Recurring Revenue (MRR/ARR), Logo Churn vs. Net Revenue Churn, Customer Acquisition Cost Payback Period ($\frac{\text{CAC}}{\text{Monthly Gross Margin}}$), Trial-to-Paid Conversion %.
- **Common BA Problem**: *"ARR grew 35% YoY but gross revenue retention dropped from 92% to 84%. What is causing the cohort decay?"*

### 3. FinTech & Consumer Lending (American Express, Capital One, Cred, PhonePe)
- **Core Operating Model**: Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) on payments + Net Interest Margin (NIM) on loan book + late payment penalty fees.
- **Critical KPIs**: Total Payment Volume (TPV), Loss Provisioning Rate, Cost of Funds %, Default Probability (PD), Exposure at Default (EAD), Loss Given Default (LGD), 30/60/90-day DPD (Days Past Due).
- **Common BA Problem**: *"Credit card approval rate was relaxed by 5%, leading to 18% higher revenue but 40% higher 90-day default losses. Optimize the cutoff threshold."*

### 4. Food Delivery & Ride-Hailing (Uber, Ola, Zomato, Swiggy)
- **Core Operating Model**: Two-sided hyper-local marketplace extracting take-rate from restaurants/drivers and charging delivery/platform fees to consumers.
- **Critical KPIs**: Delivery Executive (DE) Utilization %, Surge Multiplier %, Fulfillment Rate (Orders Delivered / Orders Placed), Cost per Delivery Drop, ETA Variance.
- **Common BA Problem**: *"Fulfillment rate in Bengaluru dropped from 94% to 82% on Friday evenings between 8 PM and 10 PM. Is this a supply deficit, weather anomaly, or pricing friction?"*

### 5. Telecom & Digital Services (Jio, Airtel)
- **Core Operating Model**: High upfront fixed Capex (spectrum, fiber, towers) with near-zero marginal cost per GB, monetized via monthly prepaid/postpaid ARPU.
- **Critical KPIs**: ARPU, Data Traffic per User (GB/month), Churn Rate %, Tower Tenancy Ratio, Network Operating Expense per Site.
- **Common BA Problem**: *"Prepaid churn rose 150 bps following a 10% tariff hike. Evaluate whether total operating revenue increased or decreased."*
