### Day-by-Day Execution Schedule
- **Days 1–4**: Master Advanced SQL (CTEs, Window Functions, Funnels, Cohorts) in [04_tools-and-technical-stack.md](04_tools-and-technical-stack.md). Solve Q1–Q15 in [06_question-bank.md](06_question-bank.md).
- **Days 5–7**: Complete Pandas data wrangling and cohort computation drills in Python.
- **Days 8–11**: Master Applied Statistics, Probability distributions, and Hypothesis testing ([03_domain-knowledge.md](03_domain-knowledge.md)).
- **Days 12–14**: Master A/B testing math (Sample size calculation, MDE, SRM Chi-Square tests).
- **Days 15–18**: Study Machine Learning fundamentals (Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, XGBoost, PR-AUC vs ROC-AUC, SHAP).
- **Days 19–21**: Work through Cases 1–10 in [07_practice-and-cases.md](07_practice-and-cases.md).
- **Days 22–24**: Practice timed quantitative aptitude and Data Interpretation tests.
- **Days 25–27**: Complete 3 Full Mock Assessments in [12_mock-assessment.md](12_mock-assessment.md).
- **Days 28–30**: Final review of [09_rapid-revision.md](09_rapid-revision.md) and rehearse BridgeRisk / Civil-to-Analytics behavioral fit stories.
