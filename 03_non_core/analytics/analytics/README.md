# Analytics & Decision Science Placement Track

> Placement-focused preparation curriculum for IIT Kanpur students targeting **Analytics & Decision Science** roles at top recruiting firms.

---

## 📋 Role Snapshot
- **Target Companies**: Fractal Analytics, Tiger Analytics, EXL Analytics, Mu Sigma, Cartesian Consulting, LatentView, ZS Associates, American Express, Mastercard, Flipkart, Swiggy
- **Typical CTC Range**: 10 LPA - 24 LPA
- **Department Eligibility**: B.Tech / M.Tech across engineering branches. Strong quantitative & coding foundation.
- **Core Skills Tested**: SQL (Advanced Joins, CTEs, Window Aggregations), A/B Testing Design & Power Analysis, Exploratory Data Analysis, Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn basics), Business Metrics (AARRR, CAC, LTV, Churn)
