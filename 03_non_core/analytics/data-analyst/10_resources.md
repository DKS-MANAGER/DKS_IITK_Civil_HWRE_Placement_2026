## 1. Foundational Textbooks & Industry Guides

### 1.1 Data Modeling & Relational Systems
* ***The Data Warehouse Toolkit* by Ralph Kimball & Margy Ross**: The industry benchmark on dimensional modeling, Star Schemas, Fact vs Dimension tables, and slowly changing dimensions (SCD).
* ***SQL Antipatterns* by Bill Karwin**: Essential reading for avoiding common SQL design mistakes, join multiplication bugs, and indexing pitfalls.
* ***Designing Data-Intensive Applications* by Martin Kleppmann**: Chapters 1–3 provide unmatched clarity on storage engines, indexes (B-Trees vs LSM-Trees), and OLTP vs OLAP data processing.

### 1.2 Data Storytelling & Dashboard Design
* ***Storytelling with Data* by Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic**: Gold standard on cognitive load reduction, chart selection, color theory, and executive presentation design.
* ***The Visual Display of Quantitative Information* by Edward Tufte**: Foundational principles on graphical integrity, data-ink ratio, and avoiding chartjunk.

### 1.3 Business Metrics & Analytics Strategy
* ***Lean Analytics* by Alistair Croll & Benjamin Yoskovitz**: Comprehensive operational breakdowns of SaaS, E-Commerce, and Marketplace metric hierarchies (AARRR, CAC, LTV, Churn).
