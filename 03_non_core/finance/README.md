## 1. Curriculum Architecture & The 5 Career Tracks

The `03_non_core/finance/` curriculum is engineered into 5 specialized tracks built upon shared accounting, quantitative, and economic foundations:

```text
03_non_core/finance/
├── AUDIT_AND_COVERAGE.md             # Comprehensive curriculum audit & gaps remediation plan
├── SYLLABUS_AND_COVERAGE.md          # 13-stage beginner pathway & 25 module mastery syllabus
├── QUESTION_BANK_WITH_SOLUTIONS.md   # 250 verified interview practice questions with step-by-step math
├── MOCK_TESTS_AND_ASSESSMENTS.md     # 7 role-specific timed mock assessments (370 total marks)
├── README.md                         # Master curriculum guide and navigation hub (this file)
├── finance/                          # Core Modules for Tracks A, B, C (Corp Fin, IB/ER, FP&A)
└── risk/                             # Core Modules for Tracks D, E (Banking Analytics, Credit & Market Risk)
```

### The 5 Targeted Career Pathways

| Track | Target Roles | Core Focus Areas | Key Deliverables & Modules |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Track A** | **General Corporate Finance & Financial Analyst** | Financial statement analysis, 3-statement linking, working capital optimization, capital budgeting (NPV/IRR), capital structure & cost of capital (WACC). | [`finance/03_domain-knowledge.md`](finance/03_domain-knowledge.md), Modules M01–M04 |
| **Track B** | **Investment Banking (IB), M&A & Equity Research** | Business valuation (DCF, Trading & Transaction Comps), Football Field charts, M&A accretion/dilution models, Leveraged Buyouts (LBOs), MoIC & IRR returns. | [`finance/03_domain-knowledge.md`](finance/03_domain-knowledge.md), Modules M05, M06, M08, M09 |
| **Track C** | **Corporate FP&A & Commercial Finance** | Price-Volume-Mix (PVM) variance analysis, flexible budgeting, SaaS cohort metrics (ARR, NRR, CAC/LTV), rolling forecasts, margin bridges, and scenario planning. | [`finance/03_domain-knowledge.md`](finance/03_domain-knowledge.md), Modules M10, M11 |
| **Track D** | **Banking Analytics & Financial Data Modeling** | Net Interest Margin (NIM), CASA ratios, delinquency aging curves, roll-rate transition matrices, production SQL queries, cohort analysis. | [`risk/03_domain-knowledge.md`](risk/03_domain-knowledge.md), [`risk/04_tools-and-technical.md`](risk/04_tools-and-technical.md), Modules M07, M12, M13 |
| **Track E** | **Credit Risk & Market Risk Analytics** | Credit scorecards (WoE/IV, KS, Gini, PSI), Expected Loss (PD, LGD, EAD), IFRS 9 staging, Parametric/Historical/Monte Carlo VaR, Expected Shortfall, Greeks, Basel III capital governance. | [`risk/03_domain-knowledge.md`](risk/03_domain-knowledge.md), [`risk/04_tools-and-technical.md`](risk/04_tools-and-technical.md), Modules M14–M19 |

## 3. Recommended Study Paths by Time Horizon

### Path 1: 14-Day Intensive Crash Course (Screening Test Preparation)
- **Days 1–3**: Master Accounting & 3-Statement Linking ([`finance/03_domain-knowledge.md`](finance/03_domain-knowledge.md), Q001–Q025).
- **Days 4–6**: Master Financial Ratios, Working Capital & Capital Budgeting (Q026–Q075).
- **Days 7–8**: Master WACC & DCF Valuation (Q076–Q125).
- **Days 9–10**: Complete Track-Specific Modules (M&A/LBO for IB, or Scorecards/VaR for Risk).
- **Days 11–12**: Solve Mock 01 (Foundational) and your Target Role Mock (Mock 02 to 07).
- **Days 13–14**: Review Rapid Revision notes ([`finance/09_rapid-revision.md`](finance/09_rapid-revision.md) and [`risk/09_rapid-revision.md`](risk/09_rapid-revision.md)).

### Path 2: 30-Day Comprehensive Placement Pathway (Superday Readiness)
- **Week 1 (Stages 1–3)**: M01 Financial Statements, M02 Ratio Analysis, M03 Capital Budgeting. Practice Q001–Q075. Take Mock 01.
- **Week 2 (Stages 4–6)**: M04 Cost of Capital, M05 DCF Valuation, M06 Multiples, M07 Fixed Income & Banking. Practice Q076–Q150. Take Mock 02.
- **Week 3 (Stages 7–10)**: Track Specialization:
  - *IB/ER Focus*: M08 M&A Modeling, M09 LBOs. Practice Q151–Q175. Take Mock 03.
  - *FP&A Focus*: M10 FP&A Variance Analysis, M11 SaaS Metrics. Practice Q176–Q200. Take Mock 04.
  - *Analytics/Risk Focus*: M12 Banking SQL, M14 Credit Risk, M15 Scorecards, M17 Market Risk VaR. Practice Q201–Q250. Take Mocks 05, 06, and 07.
- **Week 4 (Stages 11–13)**: Python modeling, SQL query execution, full resume defense, and mock interview drills.

## 5. Evidentiary Standards & Factual Integrity

To maintain factual integrity throughout recruitment preparation, all placement statements adhere to standardized evidentiary labels:

* `[OFFICIAL PLACEMENT SOURCE]` — Directly verified from an official campus placement circular or proforma issued by the IIT Kanpur Students' Placement Office (SPO).
* `[OFFICIAL COMPANY SOURCE]` — Confirmed from an official employer careers portal, job description, or regulatory financial filing.
* `[REGULATORY SOURCE]` — Supported by official standards issued by financial regulators (e.g., RBI, Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, SEC).
* `[HISTORICAL SOURCE]` — Derived from documented previous placement cycles or verified alumni interview debriefs.
* `[PREPARATION HEURISTIC]` — Pedagogical study framework, practice benchmark, or simulated problem set.
* `[PRACTICE ASSUMPTION]` — Hypothetical financial figures or case assumptions formulated for practice drills.
* `[UNVERIFIED]` — Data point currently unconfirmed or pending publication by recruiters.
