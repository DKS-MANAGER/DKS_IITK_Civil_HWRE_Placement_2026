# Finance & Financial Risk Placement Curriculum

> **Scope**: Master navigation hub and curriculum guide for IIT Kanpur placement preparation across **General Corporate Finance** and **Financial Risk Analytics** career tracks. `[PREPARATION HEURISTIC]`

---

## 1. Track Architecture & Core Distinction

The `03_non_core/finance/` directory provides targeted preparation for two distinct career pathways:

```text
03_non_core/finance/
├── README.md                      # Master directory guide, track comparison & shared standards
├── finance/                       # General Finance (Accounting, Corporate Finance, Valuation, Markets)
└── risk/                          # Risk Analytics (Credit Risk, Market Risk, Scorecards, Loss Modeling)
```

### Track Comparison Matrix

| Track | Primary Objective | Typical Preparation Emphasis | Key Software & Tools |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **General Finance** (`finance/`) | Analyze financial statements, value businesses, evaluate capital investments, and understand corporate finance & market dynamics. | 3-Statement financial modeling, accounting mechanics & adjustments, Discounted Cash Flow (DCF), comparable multiples, capital budgeting (NPV/IRR/WACC), working capital cycles, financial markets & banking products. | Excel (Advanced Modeling), Python, Financial Statement Analysis, SQL |
| **Risk Analytics** (`risk/`) | Quantify and mitigate credit, market, and operational risk using statistical modeling, scorecards, and regulatory loss frameworks. | Probability of Default (PD), Loss Given Default (LGD), Exposure at Default (EAD), Expected Loss ($EL$), Weight of Evidence (WoE), Information Value (IV), KS statistic, Population Stability Index (PSI), Value at Risk (VaR), Expected Shortfall, SQL portfolio querying. | SQL, Python (Statsmodels, Scikit-learn), Excel, R, SAS |

> [!NOTE]
> **Track Synergies**: While General Finance emphasizes financial statement interpretation and corporate decision-making, Risk Analytics focuses on quantitative probability distributions and tail-risk loss modeling. Candidates targeting quantitative finance or banking analytics (e.g., Citi AIM, Amex Risk, Goldman Sachs Risk) should master both tracks.

## 2. Evidentiary Standards & Factual Integrity

To maintain factual integrity throughout recruitment preparation, all placement statements adhere to standardized evidentiary labels:

* `[OFFICIAL PLACEMENT SOURCE]` — Directly verified from an official campus placement circular or proforma issued by the IIT Kanpur Students' Placement Office (SPO).
* `[OFFICIAL COMPANY SOURCE]` — Confirmed from an official employer careers portal, job description, or regulatory financial filing.
* `[REGULATORY SOURCE]` — Supported by official standards issued by financial regulators (e.g., RBI, Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, SEC).
* `[HISTORICAL SOURCE]` — Derived from documented previous placement cycles or verified alumni interview debriefs.
* `[PREPARATION HEURISTIC]` — Pedagogical study framework, practice benchmark, or simulated problem set.
* `[PRACTICE ASSUMPTION]` — Hypothetical financial figures or case assumptions formulated for practice drills.
* `[UNVERIFIED]` — Data point currently unconfirmed or pending publication by recruiters.
