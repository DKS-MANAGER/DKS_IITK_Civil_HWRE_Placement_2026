# 11. Risk Analyst: Civil/HWRE Project-to-Resume Translation

> Strategic guide for translating IIT Kanpur Civil Engineering, Water Resources, and Computational Modeling projects into high-impact Quantitative Risk Analyst resume bullets.

---

## 1. The Core Translation Principle

Financial risk recruiters look for **applied probability, uncertainty quantification, failure prediction, and statistical robustness**.

```text
CIVIL / RESEARCH ANCHOR                   QUANTITATIVE RISK TRANSLATION
┌────────────────────────────────┐       ┌────────────────────────────────┐
│ Structural Scour / BridgeRisk  │ ───>  │ Probability of Default / ML    │
│ Rainfall-Runoff Extremes (GEV) │ ───>  │ Extreme Value Theory / Tail VaR│
│ Dam Breach Hydrodynamics       │ ───>  │ Stress Testing & Macro Scenarios│
│ Sensor Telemetry Calibration   │ ───>  │ Model Calibration & Validation │
└────────────────────────────────┘       └────────────────────────────────┘
```
