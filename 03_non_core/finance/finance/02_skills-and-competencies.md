## 2. 4-Tier Competency Mastery Ladder

To measure progress objectively, each competency is evaluated across four defined levels:

| Competency Area | Awareness | Developing | Interview-Ready | Strong (Top 5%) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **3-Statement Accounting** | Recalls definitions of assets, liabilities, revenue, and expenses. | Traces simple adjustments (e.g., ₹100 cash sale) through statements. | Walks complex transactions (e.g., asset sale with gain/loss, deferred revenue) with precise tax and cash reconciliations. | Instantly diagnoses accounting manipulation, capitalized interest distortions, and lease accounting changes (IFRS 16). |
| **Corporate Finance & TVM** | Knows present value and future value formulas. | Computes simple NPV and explains why NPV is superior to payback period. | Calculates WACC from raw inputs, re-levers betas using Hamada's equation, and solves crossover IRR problems. | Analyzes optimal capital structure trade-offs, financial distress costs, and debt service covenant headroom (DSCR). |
| **Financial Analysis & Ratios** | Computes basic current ratio and gross margin. | Interprets ROE and debt-to-equity ratios. | Executes 3-stage DuPont decomposition; analyzes Cash Conversion Cycle and diagnoses liquidity squeezes. | Identifies working capital leakage, distinguishes accounting profit from cash generation, and benchmarks across industry peers. |
| **Valuation (DCF & Comps)** | States that DCF discounts future cash flows. | Builds a simple 3-year DCF when given ready-made cash flows. | Builds full 5-year FCFF projection from raw financial statements, computes terminal value, bridges EV to Equity Value, and builds sensitivity tables. | Defends terminal growth assumptions against macro GDP bounds; normalizes peer multiples for non-recurring charges and cross-border accounting rules. |
| **Financial Modeling Tools** | Uses basic Excel formulas with mouse reliance. | Builds simple models with `SUM`, `AVERAGE`, and basic charts. | Navigates Excel via keyboard shortcuts; sets up 2D Data Tables, XLOOKUP, and writes clean Python ratio engines. | Builds circularity switches for debt sweeps; models dynamic scenarios with toggle switches; writes automated Python DCF pipelines. |
| **Case Defense & Fit** | Explains why they want finance in general terms. | Structures basic business answers with pros and cons. | Delivers concise top-down executive recommendations supported by numerical calculations; defends Civil/HWRE background credibly. | Confidently defends valuation assumptions under hostile interviewer cross-examination; frames risk mitigations proactively. |

## 3. General Finance Readiness Self-Assessment Scorecard

Evaluate your current readiness across the 10 core dimensions below. Score each item from 1 to 10 based on demonstrable ability:

| Item # | Preparation Dimension | Self-Score (1–10) | Observable Verification Criteria |
|:---:|:---|:---:|:---|
| 1 | **3-Statement Accounting** | `___ / 10` | Can walk a ₹100 depreciation change or ₹80 inventory write-down without pausing or making sign errors. |
| 2 | **Depreciation Tax Shield** | `___ / 10` | Can calculate tax shield value under different corporate tax rates and zero-tax jurisdictions. |
| 3 | **Capital Budgeting & NPV** | `___ / 10` | Can compute NPV, IRR, and payback period for uneven multi-year cash flows and explain reinvestment rate differences. |
| 4 | **Cost of Capital (WACC)** | `___ / 10` | Can calculate cost of equity via CAPM, after-tax cost of debt, and capital weights using market values. |
| 5 | **Beta Unlevering/Re-levering**| `___ / 10` | Can unlever peer betas and re-lever for a target capital structure using Hamada's formula. |
| 6 | **DuPont ROE Analysis** | `___ / 10` | Can decompose ROE into Net Margin, Asset Turnover, and Equity Multiplier, diagnosing low-quality leverage. |
| 7 | **Cash Conversion Cycle** | `___ / 10` | Can calculate DSO, DSI, DPO, and quantify cash released from compressing working capital cycles. |
| 8 | **DCF Valuation Mechanics** | `___ / 10` | Can calculate FCFF from EBIT, compute Gordon Growth TV, bridge EV to Equity Value, and derive per-share price. |
| 9 | **Trading Comps & Multiples**| `___ / 10` | Can calculate EV/EBITDA, P/E, and explain why EV multiples neutralize capital structure choices. |
| 10 | **Civil/HWRE Transition Story**| `___ / 10` | Can deliver a crisp, confident 2-minute answer connecting engineering math to commercial finance without sounding apologetic. |
| **Total**| **Composite Score** | `___ / 100` | Target: $\ge 75/100$ before Day 1 interviews. |

### Readiness Interpretation Rubric ([PREPARATION HEURISTIC])
- **85–100 Points (Top-Tier Ready)**: Excellent command. Focus on speed drills, mock interviews, and company-specific deal history.
- **70–84 Points (Interview-Ready)**: Ready for technical and case rounds. Address minor gaps in multi-step calculations and speed math.
- **50–69 Points (Developing)**: Core theory understood, but execution speed and edge-case handling need work. Focus on Chapters 03, 06, and 07.
- **< 50 Points (Foundational Stage)**: Revisit fundamental accounting and corporate finance principles before attempting full mock assessments.
