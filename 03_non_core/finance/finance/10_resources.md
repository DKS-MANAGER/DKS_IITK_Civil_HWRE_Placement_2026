## 1. Foundational Textbooks & Academic Treatises

### 1.1 Financial Accounting & Statement Analysis
1. **Financial Statement Analysis and Security Valuation** (Stephen H. Penman)
   - *Why it matters*: Best-in-class academic foundation connecting financial statements to economic value creation. Rigorously explains how accounting conservative bias affects ROE and P/B multiples.
   - *Best for*: Understanding why accounting earnings diverge from economic cash flows.
2. **Financial Accounting for Management** (N. Ramachandran & Ram Kumar Kakani)
   - *Why it matters*: Contextualizes corporate accounting under Indian Accounting Standards (Ind-AS) and Companies Act provisions.
   - *Best for*: Indian placement candidates preparing for domestic conglomerates, banks, and rating agencies.

### 1.2 Corporate Finance & Valuation
1. **Investment Valuation: Tools and Techniques for Determining the Value of Any Asset** (Aswath Damodaran, NYU Stern)
   - *Why it matters*: The definitive global reference for DCF modeling, beta estimation, equity risk premiums, and relative valuation.
   - *Official Online Resource*: [Aswath Damodaran's NYU Stern Homepage](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/) (Free datasets on industry betas, ERP, synthetic rating spreads, updated annually).
   - *Best for*: Learning DCF theory, terminal value mechanics, and multi-stage growth modeling.
2. **Principles of Corporate Finance** (Richard A. Brealey, Stewart C. Myers, Franklin Allen)
   - *Why it matters*: The gold standard for capital budgeting, NPV vs IRR theory, Modigliani-Miller theorems, and weighted average cost of capital.
   - *Best for*: Technical interview questions on capital structure, dividend policy, and real options.
3. **Valuation: Measuring and Managing the Value of Companies** (McKinsey & Company / Tim Koller, Marc Goedhart, David Wessels)
   - *Why it matters*: Practical framework for Return on Invested Capital (ROIC), economic profit, and stripping accounting noise from valuation models.
   - *Best for*: Corporate finance case rounds and M&A advisory preparation.

## 2. Official Regulatory Standards & Accounting Authorities

1. **Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) — Indian Accounting Standards (Ind-AS)**
   - *Official Portal*: [ICAI Ind-AS Implementation](https://www.icai.org/)
   - *Key Standards for Interviews*:
     - **Ind-AS 115**: Revenue from Contracts with Customers (5-step revenue recognition model).
     - **Ind-AS 116**: Leases (Capitalization of operating leases onto the balance sheet).
     - **Ind-AS 23**: Borrowing Costs (Capitalization of interest on qualifying assets).
2. **Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Notifications & Financial Stability Reports**
   - *Official Portal*: [RBI Bulletin & Publications](https://www.rbi.org.in/)
   - *Best for*: Keeping abreast of repo rates, banking sector non-performing assets (GNPA/NNPA ratios), and macro-prudential regulations.
3. **Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)**
   - *Official Portal*: [SEBI Regulatory Framework](https://www.sebi.gov.in/)
   - *Best for*: Takeover regulations, minimum public shareholding rules, and IPO pricing guidelines.

## 3. Financial Modeling Platforms & Practice Sandboxes

1. **BSE / NSE India Financial Statement Archives**
   - *Portals*: [NSE India](https://www.nseindia.com/) & [BSE India](https://www.bseindia.com/)
   - *How to use*: Download annual reports (10-K equivalents) of Nifty 50 companies (e.g., Tata Motors, Infosys, Reliance Industries) and reconstruct 3-statement models in Excel.
2. **Screener.in (Indian Corporate Financial Database)**
   - *Portal*: [Screener.in](https://www.screener.in/)
   - *How to use*: Free tool providing 10-year standardized P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and shareholding patterns for all listed Indian enterprises. Excellent for rapid ratio analysis and historical trend visualization.
3. **GitHub / Open Source Financial Modeling Libraries**
   - `yfinance` Python library: For pulling historical equity price data and estimating historical stock betas.
   - `numpy-financial`: Official Python package containing vectorized financial functions (`npf.npv`, `npf.irr`).
