## 3. What the Role Involves: Day-to-Day Deliverables

1. **Financial Modeling & Projection**:
   - Constructing dynamic 3-statement models from 10-K, 10-Q, and annual reports.
   - Performing DCF valuations, debt schedules, and sensitivity data tables.
2. **Comparable Company & Precedent Transaction Analysis**:
   - Standardizing trading multiples ($EV/EBITDA$, $P/E$, $EV/Sales$) across industry peer groups.
   - Adjusting reported EBITDA for non-recurring expenses, restructuring charges, and accounting standard differences (Ind-AS vs US GAAP).
3. **Transaction Structuring & Capital Allocation**:
   - Evaluating debt versus equity financing options for acquisitions.
   - Modeling EPS accretion/dilution and assessing impact on credit ratings and debt covenants.
4. **Executive Presentations & Deal Collateral**:
   - Creating investment committee memorandums, management presentations, and valuation summary charts (football-field valuation plots).

## 4. Academic Background Defense: Civil/HWRE to Finance

Engineering postgraduates from IIT Kanpur offer high analytical value to finance teams:
- **Mathematical Fluency**: Facility with calculus, differential equations, and linear algebra makes time-value-of-money, bond duration, and matrix operations straightforward.
- **Data & Computation**: Experience analyzing complex numerical datasets and writing Python scripts enables automation of repetitive financial analysis.
- **Physical Asset Intuition**: Civil engineering coursework in project management, lifecycle costing, and infrastructure economics directly translates to project finance, capital budgeting, and infrastructure debt underwriting.
