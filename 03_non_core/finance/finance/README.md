# General Finance & Investment Banking Placement Track

> Placement preparation curriculum for IIT Kanpur students targeting **General Finance, Corporate Finance, Investment Banking, and FinTech** roles in Phase 1 campus recruitment.
> 
> *Notice: Historical recruitment lists and compensation figures represent past placement benchmarks ([HISTORICAL BENCHMARK]). Confirm official 2026–27 criteria with the IITK Students' Placement Office (SPO) ([OFFICIAL PLACEMENT SOURCE]).*

---

## 1. Role Snapshot & Recruitment Landscape

| Dimension | Placement Details ([HISTORICAL BENCHMARK]) |
|:---|:---|
| **Target Recruiting Firms** | Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, J.P. Morgan Chase, Deutsche Bank, Barclays, Nomura, Edelweiss, Axis Bank, ICICI Bank, Piramal |
| **Typical Role Designations** | Financial Analyst, Investment Banking Analyst, Corporate Finance Associate, Equity Research Associate |
| **Historical Compensation Range** | ₹14 LPA – ₹32 LPA (Fixed: ₹12 LPA – ₹22 LPA; Performance Bonus: ₹2 LPA – ₹10 LPA) |
| **Department Eligibility** | B.Tech & M.Tech all departments eligible in most firms. Strong mathematical aptitude, accounting literacy, and modeling speed required. |
| **Core Capabilities Evaluated** | 3-Statement Financial Accounting, DCF Valuation (FCFF), Valuation Multiples (EV/EBITDA, P/E), Capital Budgeting (NPV/IRR/WACC), Working Capital & Ratios (DuPont, CCC), Excel Modeling |
