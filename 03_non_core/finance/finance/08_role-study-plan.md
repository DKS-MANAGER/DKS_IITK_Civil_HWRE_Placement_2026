## 1. 30-Day Comprehensive Mastery Roadmap

### Week 1: Financial Accounting Foundations & The 3 Statements
- **Days 1–2: Accounting Mechanics**:
  - Read [03_domain-knowledge.md](03_domain-knowledge.md#1-financial-accounting--the-3-statement-framework).
  - Master revenue recognition, accrual vs cash accounting, and the fundamental balance sheet equation.
- **Days 3–4: 3-Statement Dynamic Linking**:
  - Practice Q1 to Q6 in [06_question-bank.md](06_question-bank.md#category-1-accounting--3-statement-linking).
  - Walk the ₹100 depreciation change and ₹80 asset impairment out loud without reference material.
- **Days 5–7: Financial Statement Analysis & Ratios**:
  - Study DuPont decomposition and Cash Conversion Cycle in [03_domain-knowledge.md](03_domain-knowledge.md#3-financial-statement-analysis--ratio-diagnostics).
  - Run the Python ratio engine in [04_tools-and-technical.md](04_tools-and-technical.md#31-financial-statement-ratio--dupont-engine).
  - *Milestone Check*: Score $\ge 80\%$ on Section 1 of the practice question bank.

### Week 2: Corporate Finance, Capital Budgeting & Modeling
- **Days 8–10: Time Value of Money & Capital Budgeting**:
  - Study NPV, IRR, payback period, and crossover rates in [03_domain-knowledge.md](03_domain-knowledge.md#2-corporate-finance--time-value-of-money).
  - Solve Q7 to Q12 in [06_question-bank.md](06_question-bank.md#category-2-corporate-finance-capital-budgeting--tvm).
- **Days 11–12: Cost of Capital (WACC & CAPM)**:
  - Practice unlevering and re-levering betas via Hamada's equation.
  - Compute market-weighted WACC under various tax shields.
- **Days 13–14: Financial Modeling in Excel**:
  - Build a 3-statement dynamic model in Excel from scratch without a mouse.
  - Set up a 2D Data Table for sensitivity analysis in Excel.
  - *Milestone Check*: Complete the bond duration script and verify DV01 in [04_tools-and-technical.md](04_tools-and-technical.md#33-bond-pricing--macaulaymodified-duration).

### Week 3: Valuation Methodologies & Case Deconstruction
- **Days 15–17: Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Deep-Dive**:
  - Study discrete FCFF formulas, terminal value via Gordon Growth and Exit Multiples in [03_domain-knowledge.md](03_domain-knowledge.md#4-valuation-methodologies).
  - Solve [Case 1: DCF Valuation of B2B SaaS Enterprise](07_practice-and-cases.md#case-1-end-to-end-dcf-valuation-of-an-indian-b2b-saas-enterprise).
- **Days 18–20: Relative Valuation & Comps**:
  - Solve [Case 6: Trading Comps for EV Component Maker](07_practice-and-cases.md#case-6-comparable-company-analysis-trading-comps-for-ev-component-maker).
  - Understand differences between EV/EBITDA and P/E multiples.
- **Days 21–22: Strategic Cases & M&A**:
  - Work through [Case 3: Debt vs Equity Financing](07_practice-and-cases.md#case-3-capital-structure--ma-financing-debt-vs-equity-eps-dilution) and [Case 4: CCC Turnaround](07_practice-and-cases.md#case-4-cash-conversion-cycle-turnaround-of-an-fmcg-distributor).
  - *Milestone Check*: Complete 5 full cases independently with documented assumptions and sensitivity grids.

### Week 4: Timed Mocks, Behavioral Polish & Partner Defense
- **Days 23–24: Full Timed Simulation**:
  - Sit for the complete 90-minute [12_mock-assessment.md](12_mock-assessment.md) under strict timed conditions.
  - Grade your answers against the provided solutions; identify weak sections using the Remedial Matrix.
- **Days 25–26: Speed Drills & Question Bank Review**:
  - Review all 32 questions in [06_question-bank.md](06_question-bank.md).
  - Drill mental approximations (e.g., compounding shortcuts, margin calculations).
- **Days 27–28: Behavioral Polish & Fit Round**:
  - Structure and rehearse your "Why Finance from Civil/HWRE?" narrative following [05_interview-preparation.md](05_interview-preparation.md#stage-4-executive-fit--partner-round).
  - Prepare 2 recent Indian financial market developments (RBI monetary policy stance, major M&A deal).
- **Days 29–30: Rapid Revision**:
  - Review [09_rapid-revision.md](09_rapid-revision.md) in full.
  - Re-verify key formulas and enjoy adequate rest.

## 4. 3-Day Emergency Rapid-Revision (72 Hours to Interview)

- **Day -3 (T-72h: Core Math & Accounting)**:
  - Review 3-statement rules and the ₹100 depreciation example.
  - Drill WACC formula, CAPM equation, and Gordon Growth terminal value.
  - Re-read [09_rapid-revision.md](09_rapid-revision.md) Sections 1 & 2.
- **Day -2 (T-48h: Valuation & Multiples)**:
  - Practice bridging Enterprise Value to Equity Value: $\text{EV} - \text{Total Debt} + \text{Cash} = \text{Equity Value}$.
  - Review why EV/EBITDA is capital-structure neutral while P/E is not.
  - Work through [Case 1 summary numbers](07_practice-and-cases.md#case-1-end-to-end-dcf-valuation-of-an-indian-b2b-saas-enterprise).
- **Day -1 (T-24h: Behavioral Pitch & Mental Rest)**:
  - Rehearse your 2-minute "Why Finance from Civil Engineering at IITK?" story.
  - Verify current macro figures: Indian 10-Yr G-Sec yield (~7.0%), RBI repo rate, Nifty 50 P/E.
  - Stop studying at 8:00 PM; ensure 8 hours of sleep before interview day.
