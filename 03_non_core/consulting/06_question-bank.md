## Section 1: Profitability & Revenue Decomposition

### Q1 [P0][PROFITABILITY]: A leading private hospital chain in South India saw EBITDA decline 25% despite 10% higher patient footfall. Structure your diagnosis.
- **Model Approach**:
  $$	ext{EBITDA} = (	ext{Inpatient Rev} + 	ext{Outpatient Rev} + 	ext{Pharmacy/Diagnostics}) - (	ext{Doctor Fees} + 	ext{Consumables} + 	ext{Admin/Overheads})$$
  1. *Mix Shift Analysis*: Inpatient (high margin) vs. Outpatient (low margin). Footfall grew in low-margin general consultations while high-margin elective surgeries (cardiology, orthopedics) declined.
  2. *Cost Inflation*: Spike in surgical consumable prices or aggressive doctor recruitment payouts.
- **Interviewer Follow-Up**: *"If elective surgeries fell 15%, how would you determine if it is a brand reputation issue or a doctor capacity bottleneck?"*
  *(Check Average Length of Stay (ALOS), bed occupancy rates, and surgery queue cancellation data).*

### Q2 [P0][COST DRIVERS]: An FMCG company manufacturing biscuits has a 12% rise in COGS. What are the key drivers?
- **Model Approach**:
  $$	ext{COGS} = 	ext{Raw Materials (Flour, Sugar, Palm Oil)} + 	ext{Packaging (Laminate, Corrugated Box)} + 	ext{Direct Factory Labor} + 	ext{Energy/Baking Fuel}$$
  Isolate commodity price inflation vs. factory scrap/waste rate increase.

### Q3 [P1][PROFITABILITY]: A SaaS enterprise software provider has growing top-line (+30% YoY) but widening operating losses. Walk me through the diagnosis.
- **Model Approach**: Analyze Unit Economics: Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Payback Period, Net Revenue Retention (NRR), Churn Rate, and Customer Success/Hosting gross margins. High sales commission and enterprise onboarding overheads outpacing Year-1 ARR.

### Q4 [P0][PROFITABILITY]: An Indian retail fashion brand's same-store sales growth (SSSG) is negative 8%. How do you isolate the root cause?
- **Model Approach**: SSSG = Footfall $	imes$ Conversion Rate $	imes$ Average Order Value (AOV). AOV = Units Per Transaction (UPT) $	imes$ Average Unit Price (AUP). Break down traffic by store layout, inventory stockout frequency, and discounting depth.

### Q5 [P1][PROFITABILITY]: A telecom operator's Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) increased by 15%, but total operating profit fell. Why?
- **Model Approach**: Total Profit = Total Subscribers $	imes$ ARPU $	imes$ Margin $\%$ - Fixed Network Costs. Rapid subscriber churn in high-density circles offset ARPU gains, while 5G spectrum amortization and tower lease rentals escalated fixed costs.

### Q6 [P1][PROFITABILITY]: A luxury watchmaker faces falling margins. They want to cut marketing spend by 40%. Is this advisable?
- **Model Approach**: No. Luxury goods rely on brand equity and perceived prestige pricing. Cutting marketing destroys pricing power, lowering gross margins further. Focus instead on dealer commission restructuring and direct-to-consumer (D2C) boutique margins.

### Q7 [P0][REVENUE]: How would you help an OTT streaming platform monetize free users without hurting daily active users (DAU)?
- **Model Approach**: Implement a hybrid ad-supported tier (AVOD), freemium content gating (first 2 episodes free), micro-transactions (pay-per-view live sports), and telco bundle distribution partnerships.

### Q8 [P1][PROFITABILITY]: A commercial bank's Net Interest Margin (NIM) compressed from 4.2% to 3.1%. What happened?
- **Model Approach**: $	ext{NIM} = rac{	ext{Interest Income} - 	ext{Interest Expense}}{	ext{Average Earning Assets}}$. Rising policy repo rates increased cost of deposits (CASA ratio declined as depositors shifted to fixed deposits) faster than loan book yields could reprice.

### Q9 [P2][PROFITABILITY]: An Indian port terminal operator is operating at 95% capacity utilization but ROIC is below cost of capital. Diagnose.
- **Model Approach**: Low-margin bulk cargo (coal, iron ore) clogging berths instead of high-margin containerized freight; high terminal dwell times causing congestion penalties and demurrage costs.

### Q10 [P1][COST]: A commercial airline's aircraft turnaround time at gates is 45 minutes vs. benchmark 25 minutes. What is the financial and operational impact?
- **Model Approach**: 20 extra minutes per flight $	imes$ 5 legs/day = 100 minutes lost per plane = 1 fewer flight leg per day across a 50-plane fleet. Lost revenue = Millions in ticket sales; higher gate parking fees.

### Q11 [P0][REVENUE]: A coffee chain wants to increase average ticket size in morning hours. Give 4 actionable ideas.
- **Model Approach**: Breakfast combo bundling (Coffee + Croissant at 15% discount), digital upselling at checkout, loyalty app rewards for multi-item orders, and impulse purchase grab-and-go counter re-engineering.

### Q12 [P1][PROFITABILITY]: A commercial solar power producer has flat tariffs but declining equity IRR. Why?
- **Model Approach**: Module degradation rates higher than modeled (0.8%/yr vs 0.5%/yr), soiling losses due to dust, higher curtailment by state grid DISCOMs, and rising inverter maintenance costs.

## Section 2: Market Entry & Opportunity Sizing

### Q13 [P0][MARKET ENTRY]: A global pharmaceutical firm wants to launch an oncology biosimilar in India. Evaluate the decision.
- **Model Approach**: 
  1. *Market Attractiveness*: Patient incidence, affordability, out-of-pocket vs. insurance coverage, TAM.
  2. *Competitive Landscape*: Existing local players (Dr. Reddy's, Biocon), price erosion expectations.
  3. *Regulatory & Distribution*: CDSCO approvals, oncologist hospital network relationships.
  4. *Financial Feasibility*: Clinical trial costs, manufacturing Capex, payback period.

### Q14 [P0][MARKET ENTRY]: Should a leading Indian 2-wheeler OEM enter the commercial electric 3-wheeler (E-rickshaw / cargo) segment?
- **Model Approach**: Total cost of ownership (TCO) parity with CNG 3-wheelers, daily running km (>100 km makes EV highly economical), financing availability for drivers, existing dealer network leverage.

### Q15 [P1][MARKET ENTRY]: An international luxury fashion brand wants to enter India. Standalone stores, franchise, or joint venture?
- **Model Approach**: Compare FDI norms (100% single-brand retail vs local sourcing norms), brand control vs local market real estate expertise (Reliance Brands / Aditya Birla Fashion JV model).

### Q16 [P0][MARKET SIZING]: Estimate the annual market size of residential rooftop solar installations in India in 2026.
- **Model Approach**: 300 Million households $ightarrow$ Independent houses (top 15% income) = 15 Million eligible homes. Adoption rate under PM Surya Ghar subsidy (10%) = 1.5 Million installations. Average system size = 3 kW $	imes$ 50,000 INR/kW = 1.5 Lakh INR per home. Market Size = $1.5	ext{M} 	imes 1.5	ext{ Lakh} = 22,500	ext{ Cr INR}$.

### Q17 [P1][MARKET ENTRY]: A B2B logistics tech company wants to expand from India into Southeast Asia (Indonesia & Vietnam). How should they prioritize?
- **Model Approach**: Compare market logistics spend as % of GDP, island geography fragmentation (Indonesia maritime freight complexity = high SaaS value), ease of doing business, and local trucking fleet digitization levels.

### Q18 [P0][MARKET ENTRY]: Should an ed-tech platform launch offline tuition centers (hybrid model)?
- **Model Approach**: High digital customer acquisition cost (CAC) makes online-only models unsustainable; offline centers provide high trust, higher retention (80%+), and higher ticket size, balancing higher fixed Capex.

- **Model Approach**: Annual real estate + infrastructure construction area constructed $pprox$ 100 Million sq ft. Concrete intensity $pprox$ 0.04 cubic meters per sq ft $ightarrow 4	ext{ Million m}^3$ RMC/year. At 4,500 INR/$	ext{m}^3$, market size $pprox 1,800	ext{ Cr INR}$.

### Q20 [P2][MARKET ENTRY]: A defense manufacturing conglomerate wants to enter the commercial drone payload sector. What entry mode is optimal?
- **Model Approach**: Evaluate Make vs. Buy vs. Partner. High proprietary software IP favors strategic acquisition of a specialized drone startup with existing DGCA type certifications.

### Q21 [P1][MARKET ENTRY]: Should an Indian food delivery giant enter the 10-minute pharmacy delivery space?
- **Model Approach**: Synergies with existing dark store network and delivery fleet vs. regulatory licensing (Schedule H drug prescription verification, cold-chain storage) and low basket size.

### Q22 [P0][MARKET SIZING]: Estimate the number of commercial aircraft required in India over the next 10 years.

### Q23 [P1][MARKET ENTRY]: A multinational seed company wants to introduce genetically modified drought-resistant seeds in Rajasthan. What are the key risks?
- **Model Approach**: Regulatory biosafety approvals, farmer price sensitivity, counterfeiting/seed-saving practices, and monsoon dependency.

### Q24 [P2][MARKET ENTRY]: An Australian lithium mining company wants to build a processing refinery in Gujarat. What factors determine viability?
- **Model Approach**: Power tariffs (refining is energy-intensive), port proximity, chemical reagents (sulfuric acid/soda ash availability), environmental clearance timeline, and offtake agreements with Indian battery cell makers.

## Section 3: Pricing & Value Capture

### Q25 [P0][PRICING]: How should a developer price apartments in a new township project in Noida?
- **Model Approach**: 3-Method Triangulation:
  1. *Cost-Plus Floor*: Land acquisition + Construction + Approvals + Finance cost + 20% margin.
  2. *Competitive Benchmark*: Surrounding completed vs under-construction projects adjusted for amenities and metro connectivity.
  3. *Value-Based / Economic Value to Customer*: Commute time savings, integrated schools/retail premium.

### Q26 [P0][PRICING]: A biotech firm developed a rapid 5-minute water contamination testing kit for municipal boards. How do you price it?
- **Model Approach**: Value-based pricing: Current lab testing costs 2,000 INR per sample with 48-hour turnaround and risk of epidemic spread. Pricing the rapid test at 600 INR gives municipal boards 70% cost savings and instantaneous detection, capturing high value while enabling rapid adoption.

### Q27 [P1][PRICING]: Should an airline charge for seat selection and cabin baggage, or keep it all-inclusive?
- **Model Approach**: Unbundling strategy allows a lower headline fare to win price-comparison search engines (MakeMyTrip, Google Flights), driving higher search volume while extracting consumer surplus through ancillary fees.

### Q28 [P1][PRICING]: How would you price dynamic tolls on a newly constructed expressway?
- **Model Approach**: Price elasticity of demand based on value of time saved for passenger cars vs commercial trucks (fuel efficiency at steady speeds vs urban detour congestion costs). Peak vs. off-peak pricing to maximize throughput and revenue.

### Q29 [P0][PRICING]: A B2B SaaS startup charges per user seat. When should they transition to usage-based pricing?
- **Model Approach**: Transition when product value scales with volume (e.g., API calls, gigabytes of data processed, transactions processed) rather than employee count, preventing seat-sharing cannibalization.

### Q30 [P1][PRICING]: An FMCG company faces 10% raw material inflation. Should they raise product price from 10 INR to 11 INR, or reduce pack weight from 100g to 90g?
- **Model Approach**: Grammage reduction (shrinkflation) is superior in India because price points like 5 INR, 10 INR, and 20 INR are strong psychological thresholds. Breaking coin-barrier price points causes severe volume loss in general trade (Kirana stores).

### Q31 [P1][PRICING]: How to price a new toll tunnel that saves commuters 45 minutes in peak traffic?
- **Model Approach**: Calculate Average Hourly Wage of target commuter base (e.g. 600 INR/hr $ightarrow 45	ext{ mins} = 450	ext{ INR}$ time value) + Fuel saved (1.5 liters $	imes$ 100 INR = 150 INR). Total economic value = 600 INR. Price toll at 150-200 INR to capture ~30% of value.

### Q32 [P2][PRICING]: How should a cloud gaming platform structure its pricing (Monthly Subscription vs. Hourly Pay-as-you-go)?
- **Model Approach**: Two-part tariff: Low base platform fee covering user authentication/cloud storage + hourly GPU compute billing to align platform infrastructure costs with heavy-gamer usage.

### Q33 [P1][PRICING]: A luxury resort has 40% vacancy during weekdays. What pricing mechanisms fill rooms without brand dilution?
- **Model Approach**: Opaque discounting (corporate retreat packages, wellness bundles, closed-loop loyalty member pricing) rather than public price cuts on public OTAs.

### Q34 [P0][PRICING]: How would you price enterprise carbon credit consulting services for cement companies?
- **Model Approach**: Base retainer + Success fee tied to % of verified carbon credits (EACs) issued and traded on international registries.

## Section 4: Operations, Supply Chain & Capacity

### Q35 [P0][OPERATIONS]: A warehouse is experiencing high fulfillment errors (3.5% error rate). How do you systematically reduce it?
- **Model Approach**: Process mapping across Receiving $ightarrow$ Put-away $ightarrow$ Picking $ightarrow$ Packing $ightarrow$ Dispatch. 80% of errors occur during manual picking; introduce barcode scanning verification, pick-to-light systems, and SKU layout optimization based on order affinity.

### Q36 [P0][CAPACITY]: A manufacturing line has 3 machines in series with cycle times: Machine A = 3 mins, Machine B = 6 mins, Machine C = 4 mins. How do you double output?
- **Model Approach**: Machine B is the bottleneck (Capacity = 10 units/hr). To double line output to 20 units/hr, add a parallel Machine B2 (cutting stage cycle time to 3 mins) and optimize Machine C cycle time from 4 to 3 mins.

### Q37 [P1][SUPPLY CHAIN]: How can an e-commerce platform reduce Last-Mile delivery costs which represent 55% of total logistics spend?
- **Model Approach**: Increase delivery density per pin code, introduce parcel locker pickup points, batch multiple orders per delivery executive, and dynamically re-route using real-time traffic heuristics.

### Q38 [P1][OPERATIONS]: An automobile OEM has 45 days of inventory across dealerships while benchmark is 21 days. What are the consequences and solutions?
- **Model Approach**: High working capital interest costs, cash crunch, dealer margin erosion due to distress discounting. Solution: Transition from push-based production to pull-based build-to-order forecasting with shared visibility.

### Q39 [P0][OPERATIONS]: A metro rail transit network has severe platform crowding at 3 interchange stations during peak hours. Suggest operational fixes.
- **Model Approach**: Short-term: Shorten train headways from 4 mins to 2.5 mins, deploy platform marshals, skip-stop train scheduling during peak 30 minutes. Medium-term: Wider staircases, dynamic signage, platform screen doors.

### Q40 [P1][SUPPLY CHAIN]: Should a consumer electronics brand use central warehousing or regional fulfillment centers in India?
- **Model Approach**: Trade-off between inventory holding costs (centralized = lower safety stock) vs. logistics transit time and freight cost (regional = faster 1-day delivery to customers, lower last-mile freight).

### Q41 [P2][OPERATIONS]: How would you optimize turnaround times for bulk cargo vessels at a private port?
- **Model Approach**: Upgrade grab cranes to continuous ship unloaders, automate conveyor evacuation from jetty to stockyard, and optimize railway rake availability.

### Q42 [P1][SUPPLY CHAIN]: What is the Bullwhip Effect in retail supply chains and how is it mitigated?
- **Model Approach**: Small fluctuations in consumer demand amplify upstream into massive swings in wholesale and factory production. Mitigated via Point-of-Sale (POS) data sharing, Vendor-Managed Inventory (VMI), and smaller order batches.

### Q43 [P0][OPERATIONS]: A hospital emergency room (ER) has average patient wait times of 90 minutes. Walk me through a queueing theory solution.
- **Model Approach**: Implement rapid triage (categorize patients by severity Level 1-5), establish a "Fast Track" lane for minor injuries handled by nurse practitioners, freeing senior ER physicians for critical resuscitation.

### Q44 [P1][OPERATIONS]: How can a quick-commerce dark store optimize picking time from 180 seconds to 60 seconds?
- **Model Approach**: Re-layout dark store based on fast-moving SKU velocity (place top 100 SKUs near packing table), batch picking for multi-orders, and paperless handheld scanner routing.
