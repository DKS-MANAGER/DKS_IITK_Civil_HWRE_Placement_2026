> **Case Type**: Corporate Strategy / Valuation / M&A / Unit Economics

## 1. Case Overview & Candidate Instructions

### Candidate Problem Statement
> *"Our client is FlyAero, a major international full-service carrier generating $10 Billion in annual revenue. Over the past 3 years, FlyAero's enterprise valuation multiple has compressed to 6.0x EV/EBITDA due to cyclical fuel price volatility and heavy capital expenditure on aircraft leases.*  
> *The Chief Financial Officer has approached us to evaluate an aggressive corporate strategy proposal: carving out and spinning off FlyAero's frequent-flyer loyalty program ('SkyPoints') into a standalone, independently listed corporate entity.*  
> *The Board of Directors is sharply divided. Financial proponents argue that pure-play loyalty businesses trade at premium tech-like multiples (12.0x+), creating massive shareholder value. Operational traditionalists argue that separating the loyalty program will alienate premium flyers, destroy commercial alignment, and create catastrophic seat-inventory friction.*  
> *The Board has hired us to answer two decisive questions:*  
> 1. **Does spinning off SkyPoints genuinely create net enterprise value for FlyAero shareholders, or is it financial engineering that hollows out the core airline?**  
> 2. **What operational, commercial, and contractual governance rules must be established if the board proceeds with the transaction?”*

## 4. Section 3 — Step-by-Step Quantitative Solution & Analysis

### Step 1: Calculate Standalone SkyPoints Revenue

SkyPoints receives cash inflows from two revenue streams:

1. **Revenue from Bank Partners**:
   $$\text{Bank Revenue} = 60\text{ Billion points} \times \$0.0150 = \mathbf{\$900\text{ Million}}$$
2. **Revenue from Airline Passenger Issuance**:
   $$\text{Airline Flight Revenue} = 40\text{ Billion points} \times \$0.0120 = \mathbf{\$480\text{ Million}}$$
3. **Total Annual Gross Revenue**:
   $$\text{Total Gross Revenue} = \$900\text{M} + \$480\text{M} = \mathbf{\$1,380\text{ Million (\$1.38 Billion)}}$$

---

### Step 2: Calculate Redemption Costs & Standalone EBITDA

1. **Calculate Redeemed Points**:
   $$\text{Total Points Redeemed} = 100\text{ Billion points} \times (1 - 0.15\text{ Breakage}) = \mathbf{85\text{ Billion points}}$$
   *(Note: 15 Billion points represent pure profit via breakage).*

2. **Cost of Flight Redemptions (80% of 85B points = 68B points)**:
   $$\text{Flight Redemption Cost} = 68\text{ Billion points} \times \$0.0100 = \mathbf{\$680\text{ Million}}$$
   *(This $680M is transferred directly to FlyAero as seat ticket revenue).*

3. **Cost of Non-Air Redemptions (20% of 85B points = 17B points)**:
   $$\text{Non-Air Merchandise Cost} = 17\text{ Billion points} \times \$0.0080 = \mathbf{\$136\text{ Million}}$$

4. **Total Cost of Redemptions**:
   $$\text{Total Redemption Cost} = \$680\text{M} + \$136\text{M} = \mathbf{\$816\text{ Million}}$$

5. **Standalone OpEx & EBITDA**:
   $$\text{Total Expenses} = \$816\text{M (Redemptions)} + \$150\text{M (OpEx)} = \mathbf{\$966\text{ Million}}$$

   $$\text{SkyPoints Standalone EBITDA} = \$1,380\text{M} - \$966\text{M} = \mathbf{\$414\text{ Million}}$$

   $$\text{SkyPoints EBITDA Margin} = \frac{\$414\text{M}}{\$1,380\text{M}} \times 100 = \mathbf{30.0\%}$$

---

### Step 3: Sum-of-the-Parts (SOTP) Valuation Arbitrage Calculation

1. **Current Pre-Transaction FlyAero Enterprise Value**:
   $$\text{Current Consolidated EV} = \$1,200\text{ Million EBITDA} \times 6.0\text{x Multiple} = \mathbf{\$7,200\text{ Million (\$7.20 Billion)}}$$

2. **Standalone SkyPoints Enterprise Value (Post-Spin)**:
   $$\text{SkyPoints EV} = \$414\text{ Million EBITDA} \times 12.0\text{x Loyalty Multiple} = \mathbf{\$4,968\text{ Million (\$4.97 Billion)}}$$

3. **Core Airline Standalone EBITDA (Post-Spin)**:
   - Total consolidated EBITDA was $1,200M.
   - SkyPoints contributed $414M of that EBITDA.
   $$\text{Core Airline Standalone EBITDA} = \$1,200\text{M} - \$414\text{M} = \mathbf{\$786\text{ Million}}$$

4. **Core Airline Enterprise Value (Post-Spin at 5.0x Multiple)**:
   $$\text{Core Airline EV} = \$786\text{ Million EBITDA} \times 5.0\text{x Multiple} = \mathbf{\$3,930\text{ Million (\$3.93 Billion)}}$$

5. **Combined Sum-of-the-Parts (SOTP) Enterprise Value**:
   $$\text{Combined Post-Spin EV} = \$4,968\text{M (SkyPoints)} + \$3,930\text{M (Core Airline)} = \mathbf{\$8,898\text{ Million (\$8.90 Billion)}}$$

6. **Net Value Unlocked for Shareholders**:
   $$\Delta \text{Enterprise Value} = \$8,898\text{M} - \$7,200\text{M} = \mathbf{+\$1,698\text{ Million (+\$1.70 Billion)}}$$
   $$\text{Percentage Value Expansion} = \frac{\$1,698\text{M}}{\$7,200\text{M}} \times 100 = \mathbf{+23.6\%}$$

## 5. Section 4 — Strategic Risks & Contractual Governance Safeguards

While the numerical model proves a **+$1.70 Billion valuation unlock**, an exceptional candidate must advise the board on the operational tripwires:

1. **Peak-Season Seat Cannibalization (Exhibit 3)**:
   - *Risk*: If SkyPoints has an unconstrained right to book seats at $0.010/point ($0.10/seat-km) during peak summer travel, FlyAero loses full-fare cash passengers paying $0.24/seat-km.
   - *Contractual Remedy*: Implement **Dynamic Capacity Gating**: SkyPoints receives a guaranteed minimum allocation (e.g., 6% of seats), but redemptions during high-load factor flights (>85%) must be priced dynamically at market-linked point tariffs.
2. **The "Working Capital Float" Ownership**:
   - *Insight*: SkyPoints collects cash from banks upfront (today) for points that members may not redeem for 18–36 months. This creates an interest-free cash float of ~$1.5 Billion.
   - *Contractual Remedy*: FlyAero must retain custody of the existing accrued deferred revenue reserve rather than transferring all cash float to the newly listed spin-off.
3. **Counterparty Alignment in an Economic Downturn**:
   - *Risk*: If fuel spikes or an economic recession hits, an independent SkyPoints might partner with rival airlines or devalue point redemption tables, destroying customer trust in the FlyAero brand.
   - *Remedy*: Maintain a **20-year exclusive commercial agreement** and retain a **51%+ controlling equity stake** with super-majority board veto rights over redemption devaluation.
