# Role-to-Syllabus Competency Matrix

> **Document Type**: Cross-Functional Alignment Matrix
> **Objective**: Maps the 6 target consulting career pathways to the required syllabus modules, technical depth, and interview formats.

---

## 1. The 6 Consulting Career Pathways

Recruiting procedures and interview formats differ significantly across firm types. This matrix eliminates one-size-fits-all assumptions by defining the exact preparation requirements for each pathway:

1. **Path A: Management Consulting (MBB & Top Tier)**: McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Kearney, Strategy&, Oliver Wyman, Roland Berger.
2. **Path B: Strategy Consulting & Corporate Strategy**: In-house strategy teams (Tata Sons, Reliance Strategy, Aditya Birla Group), L.E.K., Parthenon.
3. **Path C: Big Four Business & Operations Consulting**: Deloitte USI / India, PwC (PwC Strategy& / Consulting), EY (Parthenon / Business Consulting), KPMG.
4. **Path D: Digital & Technology Consulting**: Accenture Strategy & Tech Consulting, IBM Consulting, Capgemini Invent.
5. **Path E: Operations, Supply Chain & Process Improvement**: Kearney Operations, Miebach, Alvarez & Marsal (A&M Performance Improvement), PwC Operations.
6. **Path F: Analytics-Led Consulting & Business Analysis**: ZS Associates, EXL Analytics, Mu Sigma, Fractal Analytics, Citi AIM.

## 3. Firm-Specific Interview Format Guide

| Pathway | Stage 1 Screening | Stage 2 Round | Stage 3 Round | Final Partner Round |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MBB (McKinsey, BCG, Bain)** | Digital Assessment (Solve / Casey), Resume Screen | 2x Live Case Interviews (Interviewer-led or Candidate-led) + PEI | 2x Live Case Interviews + Personal Fit | Partner Case Interview + Stress Test + Cultural Fit |
| **Corporate Strategy (Tata Sons, Aditya Birla)** | Aptitude / Psychometric Test + Resume Shortlist | CXO Presentation Case / Long-form Business Memo | Financial Modeling & Valuation Case | CXO / Managing Director Personal Interview |
| **Big Four (Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG)** | Online Aptitude (Quants, Verbal, Logical) + Tech MCQ | Group Discussion (GD) or Group Case Exercise | 1-2 Technical Case Interviews | Director / Partner Behavioral Interview |
| **Digital & Tech (Accenture, Capgemini)** | Cognitive & Coding / Tech Assessment (Cloud/Agile) | Tech Transformation Case Interview | Solution Architecture / Process Design Case | Leadership Fit & Behavioral Round |
| **Operations (Kearney, A&M, Miebach)** | Quantitative Test + Resume Shortlist | Supply Chain / Manufacturing Bottleneck Case | Plant Capacity & Network Optimization Case | Practice Leader Case & Experience Defense |
| **Analytics (ZS, EXL, Fractal)** | Quantitative Aptitude + SQL / Data Test | Guesstimate + Structured Analytics Case Study | Technical Modeling Walkthrough (SQL/Python) | Client-Partner Fit & Communication Interview |
