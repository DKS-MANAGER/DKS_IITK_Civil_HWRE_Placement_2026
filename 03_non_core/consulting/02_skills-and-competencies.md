# 02. Management Consulting: Skills & Competencies Framework

> A rigorous, placement-tested competency blueprint defining exact expectations for MBB (McKinsey, BCG, Bain), Tier-2 (Kearney, Oliver Wyman, Strategy&), and Big-4 / Analytics Strategy firms visiting IIT Kanpur.

## 1. Core Competency Framework

Consulting selection tests cognitive agility, structured structuring under ambiguity, mathematical precision, and executive presence.

```text
                        ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                        │       CONSULTING RECRUITMENT PILLARS         │
                        └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                               │
        ┌──────────────────────┬───────────────┴───────────────┬──────────────────────┐
        │                      │                               │                      │
 ┌──────▼──────┐        ┌──────▼──────┐                 ┌──────▼──────┐        ┌──────▼──────┐
 │  STRUCTURE  │        │ QUANT/MATH  │                 │  BUSINESS   │        │  SYNTHESIS  │
 │  (MECE /    │        │ (Mental Math│                 │  (Judgment  │        │  (Pyramid   │
 │ Hypothesis) │        │  & Modeling)│                 │  & Acumen)  │        │  & Presence)│
 └─────────────┘        └─────────────┘                 └─────────────┘        └─────────────┘
```

| Competency Area | Priority | Specific Capabilities Tested | Evaluation Round |
|:---|:---:|:---|:---|
| **MECE Structuring** | **P0** | Breaking ambiguous business problems into Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive branches; custom frameworks tailored to prompt. | Case Round 1 & 2 / Partner Rounds |
| **Hypothesis-Driven Synthesis** | **P0** | Formulating early verifiable hypotheses; testing via data exhibits; pivoting quickly when data invalidates premise. | Partner Case Interviews |
| **Speed Math & Mental Arithmetic** | **P0** | Percentages, CAGR, break-even, unit economics, fractions-to-decimals, weighted averages done mentally with zero paper errors. | Online Assessment & Live Case Math |
| **Top-Down Communication** | **P0** | Pyramid Principle: Lead with the recommendation/answer first, followed by supporting pillars and quantified risks. | Fit Round / Partner Round |
| **Business Acumen & Judgment** | **P1** | Understanding industry cost drivers (Aviation, FMCG, Banking, Infrastructure, Tech), 80/20 commercial instincts. | Case Discussion / Commercial Fit |
| **Excel & Financial Modeling** | **P1** | 3-statement basics, sensitivity tables, valuation metrics (NPV, IRR, Payback), scenario analysis. | Technical / Modeling / Take-Home Cases |
| **Executive Presence & Coachability**| **P0** | Responding calmly to partner pushback, taking interviewer hints immediately without being defensive. | All Final Rounds |

## 3. The 3-Level Competency Benchmark Ladder

```text
LEVEL 1: SCREENING & OA READY
├── Flawless 60-second speed math: 17x18, 14% of 350 Cr, CAGR 12% over 4 yrs.
├── Passing scores on McKinsey Solve Game / BCG Chatbot Case / SHL Numerical Reasoning.
└── Clean 1-page CV structured with Action Verb + Context + Quantified Metric.

LEVEL 2: CASE-READY (ASSOCIATE LEVEL)
├── Ability to clarify, structure, and state 3 prioritized hypotheses in <2 minutes.
├── Effortless navigation of data charts, profitability trees, and unit economics tables.
└── Synthesis delivered in <90 seconds using Pyramid Principle (Answer -> 3 Reasons -> Risks).

LEVEL 3: PARTNER & OFFER READY
├── 80/20 instinct: Quickly isolates the 1 critical driver moving 80% of client profit.
├── Coachable under pressure: Seamlessly incorporates interviewer challenges without flustering.
└── Genuine commercial enthusiasm: Connects technical engineering discipline to business P&L.
```
