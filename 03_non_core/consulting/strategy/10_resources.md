# 10. Corporate Strategy: Resources, Reading & References

> **Document Type**: Canonical Strategic Management Bibliography
> **Audience**: Strategy analysts and aspirants targeting corporate strategy leadership programs.

---

## 1. Foundational Strategic Management Texts
1. **Hamilton Helmer**: *7 Powers: The Foundations of Business Strategy* (Deep Five Powers & Moats framework).
2. **Michael Porter**: *Competitive Strategy: Techniques for Analyzing Industries and Competitors* & *Competitive Advantage*.
3. **Richard Rumelt**: *Good Strategy / Bad Strategy: The Difference and Why It Matters* (Crux, diagnosis, guiding policy).
4. **Clayton Christensen**: *The Innovator's Dilemma: When New Technologies Cause Great Firms to Fail*.
5. **Robert Grant**: *Contemporary Strategy Analysis* (Top-tier MBA textbook standard).

## 2. Public Strategy Resources & Annual Reports
- **Annual Reports to Deconstruct**: Reliance Industries Limited (Jio/Retail capital allocation), Tata Sons portfolio disclosures, Berkshire Hathaway Shareholder Letters (Warren Buffett on moats and capital allocation).
- **Consulting Strategy Publications**: McKinsey Quarterly, BCG Strategy Insights, Harvard Business Review (HBR).
