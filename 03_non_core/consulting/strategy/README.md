# Corporate Strategy Placement Track

> Placement-focused preparation curriculum for IIT Kanpur students targeting **Corporate Strategy, Conglomerate Leadership, and CXO Strategic Planning** roles at top recruiting firms.

---

## 📋 Role Snapshot
- **Target Companies & Programs**: Tata Administrative Services (TAS), Aditya Birla Group (LEAP), Mahindra Group Management Cadre (GMC), Reliance Strategic Initiatives, ITC Strategy & Operations, JSW Strategy.
- **Typical CTC Range**: 16 LPA – 32 LPA.
- **Department Eligibility**: B.Tech / M.Tech all branches. High emphasis on holistic leadership, structured strategic judgment, capital allocation, and executive communication.
- **Core Strategy Frameworks Tested**: Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers, Porter's 5 Forces, Economic Value Added (EVA = Capital $\times$ [ROIC - WACC]), SOTP Valuation Multiples, Organic Build vs. Inorganic M&A Strategy, Market Disruption Defense.

---

## 🎯 Corporate Strategy vs. Management Consulting

| Dimension | Management Consulting (Client Advisory) | Corporate Strategy (Principal Owner) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Perspective** | External strategic advisor across varied client engagements. | Internal corporate principal driving multi-year board decisions. |
| **Mandate Scope** | 3–6 month diagnostic and turnaround projects. | 5–10 year capital allocation, M&A due diligence, and conglomerate portfolio governance. |
| **Primary Financial Metric** | Client EBITDA margin recovery, cost per unit, conversion rate. | Economic Value Added (EVA), Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) vs. WACC, Sum-of-the-Parts (SOTP) multiple expansion. |
| **Primary Modules** | [`../02_problem-solving.md`](../02_problem-solving.md), [`../04_guesstimates.md`](../04_guesstimates.md), [`../06_case-practice.md`](../06_case-practice.md) | [`07_practice-and-cases.md`](07_practice-and-cases.md), [`12_mock-assessment.md`](12_mock-assessment.md), [`03_domain-knowledge.md`](03_domain-knowledge.md) |
