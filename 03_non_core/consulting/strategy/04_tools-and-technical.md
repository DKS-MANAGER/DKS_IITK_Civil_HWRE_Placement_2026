## 2. CXO Boardroom Presentation Storylining

In corporate strategy, deliverables take the form of **Executive Strategic Memoranda** and **Board of Directors Pitchbooks**.

### 2.1 The 4-Part Strategic Board Deck Template
1. **Executive Summary / Decision Mandate**: A 1-page synthesis stating the recommended capital allocation or strategic acquisition, expected ROI, and risks.
2. **Industry Macro & Competitive Imperative**: Why the status-quo trajectory is unviable (industry profit pools shifting, competitive moat erosion).
3. **Strategic Options & Trade-Off Matrix**: Comparison of 3 competing strategic paths (Organic Build, M&A Buy, Partnership) scored across NPV, Strategic Control, and Execution Complexity.
4. **Execution Roadmap & Risk Mitigation**: 100-day integration plan, capital deployment schedule, and regulatory approval triggers.
