### Detailed Week-by-Week Operational Breakdown

#### Week 1: Quantitative Foundations & Toolkit Mastery
- **Day 1-2**: Master [04_tools-and-technical.md](04_tools-and-technical.md). Practice 30 minutes of speed math daily (fractions, CAGR approximations, percentages).
- **Day 3-4**: Complete the 2 timed Excel modeling drills (Unit Economics & Break-Even).
- **Day 5-6**: Study [03_domain-knowledge.md](03_domain-knowledge.md). Memorize key business metrics (EBITDA, Gross Margin, CAC, LTV, Working Capital).
- **Day 7**: Draft 5 Civil-to-Consulting resume project bullets using [11_projects.md](11_projects.md).

#### Week 2: Core Frameworks & Online Assessment Preparation
- **Day 8-9**: Master Profitability, Cost Reduction, and Revenue Growth frameworks. Solve Cases 1–5 in [06_case-practice.md](06_case-practice.md).
- **Day 10-11**: Master Market Entry, M&A Synergy, and Pricing frameworks. Solve Cases 6–10.
- **Day 12-13**: Practice McKinsey Solve / Ecosystem simulation drills and BCG Casey chatbot case formats.
- **Day 14**: Solve 10 Guesstimates from [06_question-bank.md](06_question-bank.md) under 8-minute time limits.

#### Week 3: Live Case Drills & Executive Synthesis
- **Day 15-18**: Conduct 2 live case interviews daily with a prep partner (1 as candidate, 1 as interviewer). Focus on Pyramid Principle synthesis.
- **Day 19-20**: Practice exhibit analysis: Read 20 business charts/tables and state the *"So What?"* in <45 seconds.
- **Day 21**: Refine and rehearse the 4 PEI stories ([05_interview-preparation.md](05_interview-preparation.md)) out loud.

#### Week 4: Full Mock Simulations & Partner Polish
- **Day 22-25**: Complete 4 full-length mock interviews with senior mentors or alumni ([12_mock-assessment.md](12_mock-assessment.md)).
- **Day 26**: Perfect your *"Why Consulting from Civil Engineering?"* response.
- **Day 27-28**: Review [09_rapid-revision.md](09_rapid-revision.md) and conduct a zero-pressure mental math walkthrough.
