# 08. Management Consulting: Operational Study & Execution Plans

> **Document Purpose**: Structured, realistic preparation schedules (30-Day Foundational Track, 14-Day Accelerated Sprint, and 7-Day Final Countdown) tailored for IIT Kanpur engineering students balancing academic commitments and placement preparation. Every referenced case, exercise, and mock corresponds directly to verified files in this repository.

---

## 1. Study Plan Design Rules (Placement Reality)

1. **Realistic Time Allocation**: Most M.Tech and dual-degree candidates have 2.0 to 2.5 hours on weekdays and 4.0 hours on weekends for non-core preparation. Do not prescribe 40 live cases in 5 days.
2. **Quality of Case Debrief > Case Quantity**: Solving 15 cases deeply with thorough 30-minute debriefs outperforms rushing through 50 cases without reviewing mistakes.
3. **Paired Practice Requirement**: Case interviews are an oral performance. At least 40% of case practice must be conducted out loud with a preparation partner (alternating roles as candidate and interviewer).
4. **Concrete Measurable Deliverable**: Every single study day has a defined written or spoken artifact (e.g., 3 issue trees sketched, 1 written case debrief, 1 timed mock completed).

## 4. The 7-Day Final Countdown (Interview Week Plan)

*Daily Commitment: 2.0 Hours (Focus on composure, structure, and verbal articulation)*

- **Day 1 (Framework Refresh)**: Re-read [`02_problem-solving.md`](02_problem-solving.md) and [`03_business-cases.md`](03_business-cases.md). Focus on tailored issue trees over generic memorization.
- **Day 2 (Math & Guesstimates Drill)**: Practice 30 minutes of speed math in [`05_consulting-math.md`](05_consulting-math.md). Solve 2 guesstimates aloud under 8-minute timers.
- **Day 3 (High-Yield Case Review)**: Review Case 1 and Case 3 in [`07_practice-and-cases.md`](07_practice-and-cases.md). Re-read executive recommendations and synthesis language.
- **Day 4 (Partner Mock Simulation)**: Conduct a single timed 45-minute peer mock interview using [`12_mock-assessment.md`](12_mock-assessment.md) or [`06_case-practice.md`](06_case-practice.md). Focus on top-down communication.
- **Day 5 (HR, Fit & Story Rehearsal)**: Rehearse your personal narrative and 4 STAR behavioral stories from [`07_interview-preparation.md`](07_interview-preparation.md). Ensure smooth, natural delivery without sounding robotic.
- **Day 6 (Rapid Revision)**: Review all formulas, ratios, and exhibit rules in [`09_rapid-revision.md`](09_rapid-revision.md). Confirm interview logistics, attire, and tech setup.
- **Day 7 (Interview Day)**: No heavy problem solving. Conduct a 10-minute mental warm-up (calculating simple percentage conversions); maintain high energy and structured composure.
