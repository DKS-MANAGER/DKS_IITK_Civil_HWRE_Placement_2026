# Management & Strategy Consulting — Master Preparation Dashboard

> **Target Roles**: Management Consultant, Strategy Consultant, Business Analyst / Associate, Operations Consultant, Digital Consultant  
> **Target Audience**: IIT Kanpur students preparing for consulting placement and internship selection cycles  
> **Module Status**: Rigorous, Placement-Verified Preparation Track

---

## 1. Canonical Consulting Preparation Sequence

The consulting repository provides two complementary layers:
1. **Core Instructional Pillars (Blocks 1–4)**: Methodological depth in problem-solving, frameworks, guesstimates, and quantitative business mathematics.
2. **Standard 12-Module Placement Architecture**: Complete role lifecycle from skills benchmark and technical modeling to case banks, truthful resume positioning, and timed mock simulations.

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        CANONICAL CONSULTING LEARNING PATHWAY                           │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 01. Role & Industry Blueprint    ──► Industry landscape, firm tiers, Civil positioning │
│ 02. Structured Problem Solving   ──► 9-step workflow, 5 issue tree archetypes, MECE    │
│ 03. Business Case Frameworks     ──► Profitability, market entry, operations, platform │
│ 04. Guesstimates & Sizing        ──► Top-down & bottom-up models, baseline data bank   │
│ 05. Consulting Math & Economics  ──► EBITDA bridges, unit economics, CAGR, break-even │
│ 06. Master Case & Question Bank  ──► 65-question bank & tiered case practice library   │
│ 07. Case Practice Bank           ──► 4 multi-step worked cases (Cement, EV, Q-Com, Hosp)│
│ 08. Role Study Plans             ──► 30-Day, 14-Day, 7-Day grounded preparation plans  │
│ 09. Diagnostic Mock Assessment   ──► 10 math questions, 3 guesstimates, 2 mini-cases   │
│ 10. Truthful Resume Positioning  ──► Civil/HWRE project templates & evidence guidelines│
│ 11. Full-Length Partner Mock     ──► 60-min Airline Loyalty Spin-Off simulation & rubric│
│ 12. Corporate Strategy Track     ──► Dedicated nested track for CXO & conglomerate roles│
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
