## 3. Preparation Schedules (30-Day / 14-Day / 7-Day Plans)

### 30-Day Master Schedule (Comprehensive Track)
* **Days 01–05**: Study [`01_role-overview.md`](01_role-overview.md) & [`02_problem-solving.md`](02_problem-solving.md). Practice building 5 issue trees.
* **Days 06–12**: Master Business Cases in [`03_business-cases.md`](03_business-cases.md). Solve all profitability and market entry cases.
* **Days 13–18**: Study [`04_guesstimates.md`](04_guesstimates.md) & [`05_consulting-math.md`](05_consulting-math.md). Solve 10 guesstimates.
* **Days 19–25**: Solve Intermediate & Hard cases in [`06_case-practice.md`](06_case-practice.md). Conduct 5 peer mock interviews.
* **Days 26–28**: Complete Mock Tests in [`08_mock-assessment.md`](08_mock-assessment.md) and prepare HR stories in [`07_interview-preparation.md`](07_interview-preparation.md).
* **Days 29–30**: Final review with [`09_rapid-revision.md`](09_rapid-revision.md).
