# Consulting Curriculum Master Syllabus & Course Coverage

> **Document Type**: Comprehensive 6-Stage Master Syllabus & Learning Progression
> **Audience**: IIT Kanpur engineering students preparing for Management, Strategy, Big 4, Digital, Operations, and Analytics Consulting recruitment.
> **Course Architecture**: 6 Pedagogical Stages, 30 Dedicated Modules (C01–C30), explicit prerequisites, priority tiers (P1/P2/P3), and verifiable mastery criteria.

---

## 1. The 6-Stage Pedagogical Pathway

The consulting preparation curriculum is organized into a cumulative 6-stage learning sequence. Each stage builds directly upon the concepts mastered in the previous stage:

```text
STAGE 1: Foundations
  (Business Terminology, Speed Arithmetic, Consulting Math, Mental Shortcuts, Order of Magnitude)
        │
        ▼
STAGE 2: Structured Problem-Solving
  (Problem Clarification, Objective Definition, MECE Principle, Issue Trees, Hypothesis Testing)
        │
        ▼
STAGE 3: Business Economics & Domain Knowledge
  (Revenue Models, Cost Structures, Value Chains, Profit Pools, Industry Profiles, Competitive Moats)
        │
        ▼
STAGE 4: Case Solving & Core Archetypes
  (Profitability, Market Entry, Pricing, Growth, M&A, Supply Chain, Digital Transformation, AI Cases)
        │
        ▼
STAGE 5: Communication & Executive Synthesis
  (Pyramid Principle, Answer-First Synthesis, Slide Storylining, Exhibit Interpretation, Defending Assumptions)
        │
        ▼
STAGE 6: Interview Execution & Live Drills
  (Guesstimates, Online Assessment Games, PEI / Fit Stories, Stress Rounds, Full-Length Timed Mocks)
```

## 3. Priority Tiers Explained

- **Priority 1 (P1 - Core Non-Negotiable)**: Tested in 100% of consulting interviews across all firms (McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Kearney, Strategy&, Big 4). Must be mastered before appearing for any round.
- **Priority 2 (P2 - Functional & Advanced Specialization)**: High-frequency topics in operational, digital, or corporate strategy interviews. Essential for Big 4, Accenture, and internal strategy roles.
- **Priority 3 (P3 - Selective / Role-Dependent)**: Specialized tools (e.g., Python regression, deep M&A legal structuring) tested primarily when explicitly stated in the job description.
