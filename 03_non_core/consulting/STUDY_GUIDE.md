> **Document Type**: Role-Specific Execution Plans & Study Schedules  
> **Timelines Provided**: 30-Day Foundational Roadmap, 14-Day Intensive Plan, 7-Day Interview-Week Sprint, and 3-Day Emergency Protocol.  
> **Canonical Structure**: Fully integrated with the modular [`lessons/`](lessons/), [`worked_cases/`](worked_cases/), [`practice/`](practice/), [`assessments/`](assessments/), and [`tools_labs/`](tools_labs/) directories.

---

## 1. Study Plan Design Principles

1. **Active Solving over Passive Reading**: Simply reading case transcripts creates an illusion of competence. Every case must be solved by speaking out loud or writing issue trees closed-book before looking at solutions.
2. **First-Principles Arithmetic**: Calculate all mental math, percentages, and conversions without an electronic calculator.
3. **Structured Reflection**: Maintain a "Case Error Journal" logging every error made (e.g., failed to clarify objective, jumped into calculations without tree, arithmetic mistake).
