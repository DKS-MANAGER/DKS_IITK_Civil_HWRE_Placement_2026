## ⏱️ Mock Case: Airline Loyalty Program Spin-Off

### Case Scenario
*"A legacy full-service airline is considering carving out and spinning off its frequent flyer loyalty program into an independent business entity. The board wants to evaluate whether this move will unlock shareholder value or create long-term operational friction."*

### Candidate Evaluation Rubric

| Dimension | Weak (1-2) | Competent (3-4) | Exceptional / Hire (5) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Case Structuring (MECE)** | Unstructured brainstorming; misses revenue/cost drivers | Standard commercial tree (Revenue, Cost, Valuation) | Custom-tailored multi-stakeholder tree (Airline, Loyalty Co, Credit Card Partners, Consumers) |
| **Quantitative Agility** | Struggles with percentage conversions, makes calculation errors | Calculates partner redemption margins with guidance | Fast mental math, proactively computes break-even redemption volume and enterprise valuation multiples |
| **Business Acumen** | Views loyalty points as pure marketing expense | Understands that selling points to banks at 1.5 cents and redeeming for 0.8 cents generates massive float | Explains deferred revenue accounting, working capital float investment, and breakage rate dynamics |
| **Synthesis & Recommendation** | Rambling recap of calculations | Structured conclusion with revenue and risk factors | Top-down Pyramid Principle conclusion, bold recommendation, 3 supporting pillars, prioritized risk mitigation |
