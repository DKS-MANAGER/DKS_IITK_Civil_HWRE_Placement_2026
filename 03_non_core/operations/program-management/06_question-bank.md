---

### Question 1: [P0][PGM] How do you handle a critical cross-functional program when Engineering informs you 2 weeks before launch that a core feature is delayed by 1 month?

**Direct Answer / Solution Approach:**
```text
1. Impact Assessment: Determine if feature is P0 blocker or can be decoupled for Phase 2. 2. Trade-Off Options: (A) Descope feature and launch core product on time; (B) Reallocate surge engineering bandwidth from non-critical tracks; (C) Delay launch with stakeholder alignment. 3. Executive Communication: Present options with clear risk/benefit analysis to leadership.
```

**Interviewer Follow-Up & Deep Dive:**
> Interviewer follow-up: How do you prevent such surprise delays in future sprints? (Establish weekly milestone burn-down tracking and early dependency flags).
