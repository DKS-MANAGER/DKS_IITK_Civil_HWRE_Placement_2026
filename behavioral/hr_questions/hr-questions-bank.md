# HR Questions Bank

## How to Use This Bank

Categorize questions by theme and prepare 2–3 stories per theme. Each story should be answerable with the STAR or CAR framework and adaptable to multiple prompts.

## Core Values Questions

### Customer-Centricity / User Focus
- Describe a situation where you customized a solution for a client or user.
- How do you ensure client needs drive your decisions?
- Tell me about a time you handled a difficult customer. Were you successful?
- Describe an innovative solution you developed for a customer.
- How did you promote a customer-centric culture among colleagues or stakeholders?
- Describe a time a customer could not see your product's value. How did you act?
- Describe a time a customer situation escalated. How did you resolve it?

### Team First Mentality
- Tell me about a time you developed internal relationships within a team. What was your role and impact?
- Describe a time you had to align different teams or stakeholders. What were the challenges?
- Tell me about a situation where you were disappointed in a project team outcome. What did you learn?
- Describe a conflict of opinion with a colleague. How did you solve it?
- Tell me about a time a colleague failed to deliver and endangered a project. How did you address it?
- How have you actively strengthened relationships or knowledge sharing in a team?
- Tell me about a time you needed another team's expertise. What did you do?

### Ownership
- Tell me about a time you proactively took responsibility for a project or idea that impacted the business.
- How do you make sure you reach your commitments? Give an example.
- Describe a situation where you overcame an obstacle to complete work. Did you involve others?
- Tell me about a time you failed to deliver what you promised. How did you handle it?
- Describe a challenging project and how you stayed motivated to finish it.
- Tell me about a time when you were new to an organization. How did you gather business knowledge?
- How do you manage your workload? Give an example of adapting your plan.

### Open-Mindedness
- Describe a time you disagreed with a colleague about reaching a shared goal. How did you resolve it?
- Describe working in a multicultural environment. What challenges did you face?
- Give an example where you fostered an open feedback culture.
- Tell me about a time you expressed disagreement to a superior.
- Tell me about a time you communicated a difficult message.
- Tell me about a time you faced uncertainty and had to adapt. How did you cope?

## Behavior Questions

### Motivation & Drive
- What motivates you for this role?
- Describe a time you felt particularly motivated.
- Tell me about a situation where you felt demotivated. How did you recover?

### Planning & Organizing
- How do you manage your workload? Give an example of adapting your plan.
- Would you do anything differently if the same situation arose again?
- How do you prioritize tasks or projects?

### Prioritization & Big Picture
- Tell me about receiving multiple urgent requests. How did you prioritize?
- How do you keep the big picture in view while working on multiple projects?

### Self-Reflection
- Describe a mistake you made that affected a project or customer. What did you learn?
- Describe a decision you later realized was incorrect. How did you handle it?

### Resilience
- Tell me about a time you worked under extreme pressure. How did you cope?

### Problem-Solving & Creativity
- Tell me about a project you solved creatively. What was the challenge?
- How did you monitor and measure success?
- What would you do differently next time?

### Proactiveness
- Tell me about a time you proactively took responsibility for an idea with business impact.
- Describe a time you improved an internal process. What was the outcome?
- Tell me about a time you were new to an organization. How did you gather product knowledge?

### Reliability
- Tell me about a time you realized you could not achieve a KPI or goal. What did you do?
- Give an example where you could not keep a promise to a customer or stakeholder.

### Personal Development & Growth
- What do you do to stay up to date in your field?

### Facilitating Development & Growth
- Give an example where you facilitated growth in your team or a team member.
- How did you improve the learning culture in your company?

### Process
- Walk us through a specific project and all the steps you went through.

### Establishing Partnerships & Negotiations
- Tell me about a time you convinced a difficult customer or partner of your product's value.
- How did you prepare for the negotiation?

### Tech Behavior
- What steps do you take to ensure high code quality and performance?
- When does a developer's responsibility for maintaining code end?
- What was the last technology or framework you selected and why?
