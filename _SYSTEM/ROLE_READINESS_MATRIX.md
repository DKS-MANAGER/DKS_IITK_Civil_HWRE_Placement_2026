## Scoring Method

| Component | Max | What It Measures |

| Core Topics | 5 | 3-4 highest-value topic blocks identified |
| Study Material | 10 | Actual learnable content (not just headings) |
| Worked Examples | 10 | Real worked examples with method |
| Practice Questions | 10 | Basic + intermediate + interview-level |
| Mock/Test | 10 | Realistic timed test |
| Interview Questions | 10 | Fundamentals + applied + deep dive |
| Interview Strategy | 5 | Round-by-round prep guide |
| Behavioral/HR | 5 | STAR stories, HR questions |
| Resume/Project | 5 | Role-specific resume tips |
| Common Mistakes | 5 | What candidates do wrong |
| Rapid Revision | 5 | Last-minute cheat sheet |
| Cross-Links | 5 | Navigation to related files |
