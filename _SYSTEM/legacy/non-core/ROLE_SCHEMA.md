## 2. The 18 Essential Placement Questions Every Track Must Answer

1. **What job is this?** (Core industry function, value proposition, and role definition).
2. **What jobs/designations exist?** (Associate Analyst, Decision Scientist, Strategy Associate, etc.).
3. **What does the job actually involve?** (Day-to-day work, deliverables, cross-functional stakeholders).
4. **Who hires for it at IITK?** (Target companies visiting campus, historical recruiter profiles).
5. **What eligibility is typical?** (CPI cutoffs, department opening status tagged as `[HISTORICAL]`).
6. **What skills are required?** (P0 essential vs P1 preferred competency matrix).
7. **What tools & software are required?** (Exact production tools: SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI).
8. **What domain knowledge is tested?** (Mathematical foundations, unit economics, industry mechanics).
9. **What does the Online Assessment (OA) test?** (Sectional time limits, scoring patterns, question types).
10. **What does the technical interview test?** (Live Coderpad SQL, algorithmic wrangling, modeling).
11. **What cases/problems are asked?** (Metric drop diagnosis, profitability, turnaround cases).
12. **What behavioral questions are asked?** (Role-specific fit, leadership, conflict, "Why Non-Core?").
13. **What projects demonstrate readiness?** (BridgeRisk ML, Streamflow forecasting, CFD optimization).
14. **How should a Civil/HWRE candidate position their resume?** (Transferable quantitative bullets).
15. **What should be practiced?** (Curated question bank with $\ge 60$ questions with solutions).
16. **What is the operational study plan?** (Structured 30-day, 14-day, and 7-day roadmaps).
17. **What is the rapid-revision layer?** (High-yield formula and framework cheat sheet).
18. **How is placement readiness assessed?** (Timed mock assessment with objective scoring rubric).

## 4. Provenance & Factual Tagging Standard

To maintain academic and professional integrity across the repository, all information must adhere to strict provenance tags:
- `[FACT]`: Verified mathematical equations, SQL syntax, statistical definitions, or formal regulatory rules.
- `[HISTORICAL]`: Historical IITK recruitment data, CTC ranges, and previous season CPI cutoffs.
- `[PREPARATION HEURISTIC]`: Rules of thumb, benchmarks ($LTV/CAC \ge 3	imes$), and self-study targets.
- `[EXAMPLE]`: Illustrative resume bullet templates or case numbers (must be replaced with verified personal data).
- `[STORY TEMPLATE]`: Behavioral interview structural frames.
