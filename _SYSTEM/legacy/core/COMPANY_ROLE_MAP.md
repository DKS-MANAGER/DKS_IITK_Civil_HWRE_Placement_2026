## Universal Preparation Path (Covers 70%+ of Companies)

```
RCC Design (IS 456)  →  Structural Analysis  →  Steel Design (IS 800)
        ↓                        ↓                       ↓
   rcc/PRACTICE.md      structural-analysis/       steel/PRACTICE.md
   rcc/TEST.md          PRACTICE.md                steel/TEST.md
   rcc/INTERVIEW.md     TEST.md                    steel/INTERVIEW.md
        ↓                        ↓                       ↓
        └──────────────  Construction + PM  ──────────────┘
                        infrastructure/PRACTICE.md
                        infrastructure/TEST.md
                        infrastructure/INTERVIEW.md
```

**Key Insight (from cross-company strategy):** RCC Design + Construction Technology + Structural Analysis covers **70%+ of all HIGH/MEDIUM companies**. This is the maximum-ROI preparation path.
