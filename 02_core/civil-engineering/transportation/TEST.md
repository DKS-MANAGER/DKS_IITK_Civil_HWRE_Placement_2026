## Section A — Geometric Design (Q1–Q5)

**Q1.** The stopping sight distance formula is:
- (a) $SSD = 0.278 \cdot V \cdot t + \frac{V^2}{254(f \pm G)}$
- (b) $SSD = 0.278 \cdot V \cdot t + \frac{V^2}{127(f \pm G)}$
- (c) $SSD = V \cdot t + \frac{V^2}{254f}$
- (d) $SSD = 0.5 \cdot V \cdot t + \frac{V^2}{254f}$

**Q2.** The minimum radius of a horizontal curve is:
- (a) $R_{min} = \frac{V^2}{127(e + f)}$
- (b) $R_{min} = \frac{V^2}{254(e + f)}$
- (c) $R_{min} = \frac{V^2}{127e}$
- (d) $R_{min} = \frac{V}{127(e + f)}$

**Q3.** The IRC maximum superelevation for plain terrain is:
- (a) 0.05
- (b) 0.07
- (c) 0.10
- (d) 0.15

**Q4.** The reaction time used in SSD calculation (IRC) is:
- (a) 1.0 s
- (b) 1.5 s
- (c) 2.5 s
- (d) 3.5 s

**Q5.** Overtaking sight distance is:
- (a) $d_1 + d_2 + d_3$
- (b) $d_1 + d_2$
- (c) $2 \times SSD$
- (d) $d_2 + d_3$

## Section B — Traffic Engineering (Q6–Q10)

**Q6.** The fundamental traffic flow equation is:
- (a) $q = k \cdot v$
- (b) $q = k/v$
- (c) $q = v/k$
- (d) $q = k + v$

**Q7.** In the Greenshields model, maximum flow is:
- (a) $q_{max} = v_f \cdot k_j$
- (b) $q_{max} = \frac{v_f \cdot k_j}{2}$
- (c) $q_{max} = \frac{v_f \cdot k_j}{4}$
- (d) $q_{max} = \frac{v_f \cdot k_j}{8}$

**Q8.** The PCU factor for a bus/truck is:
- (a) 0.5
- (b) 1.0
- (c) 2.0
- (d) 3.0

**Q9.** Webster's optimal cycle length is:
- (a) $C_o = \frac{1.5L + 5}{1 - \sum Y_i}$
- (b) $C_o = \frac{1.5L + 5}{\sum Y_i}$
- (c) $C_o = \frac{L + 5}{1 - \sum Y_i}$
- (d) $C_o = \frac{1.5L}{\sum Y_i}$

**Q10.** The 85th percentile speed is used for:
- (a) Setting speed limits
- (b) Signal timing
- (c) Pavement design
- (d) Capacity analysis
