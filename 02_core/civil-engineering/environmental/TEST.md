## Section A — Water Quality & Treatment (Q1–Q5)

**Q1.** The standard BOD test incubates the sample for:
- (a) 3 days at 25°C
- (b) 5 days at 20°C
- (c) 7 days at 20°C
- (d) 5 days at 30°C

**Q2.** The BOD reaction rate constant $k_1$ is corrected for temperature using:
- (a) $k_T = k_{20} \cdot \theta^{(T-20)}$
- (b) $k_T = k_{20} \cdot \theta^{(20-T)}$
- (c) $k_T = k_{20} \cdot (T-20)$
- (d) $k_T = k_{20}/\theta^{(T-20)}$

**Q3.** The surface loading (overflow) rate of a sedimentation tank is:
- (a) $Q/A$
- (b) $A/Q$
- (c) $Q \times A$
- (d) $V/t$

**Q4.** The optimal pH range for alum coagulation is:
- (a) 4.0–5.0
- (b) 6.0–7.5
- (c) 8.5–11.0
- (d) 11.0–12.0

**Q5.** Breakpoint chlorination is the point where:
- (a) Combined residual is maximum
- (b) Free residual first appears
- (c) Chlorine demand is zero
- (d) All organics are oxidized

## Section B — Wastewater Treatment (Q6–Q10)

**Q6.** The F/M ratio in the activated sludge process is:
- (a) $Q \cdot S_0/(V \cdot X)$
- (b) $V \cdot X/(Q \cdot S_0)$
- (c) $Q \cdot X/(V \cdot S_0)$
- (d) $S_0 \cdot X/(Q \cdot V)$

**Q7.** The typical BOD removal efficiency of secondary treatment is:
- (a) 25–40%
- (b) 50–70%
- (c) 85–95%
- (d) 99.9%

**Q8.** Nitrification requires:
- (a) Anoxic conditions
- (b) Aerobic conditions
- (c) Anaerobic conditions
- (d) No oxygen

**Q9.** The oxygen requirement for nitrification of 1 mg/L NH₄-N is:
- (a) 1.0 mg/L
- (b) 2.87 mg/L
- (c) 4.57 mg/L
- (d) 0.5 mg/L

**Q10.** Biogas from anaerobic digestion is approximately:
- (a) 60–65% CH₄, 35–40% CO₂
- (b) 90% CH₄, 10% CO₂
- (c) 50% CH₄, 50% CO₂
- (d) 30% CH₄, 70% CO₂

## 📋 Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Explanation |
|:-:|:------:|-------------|
| 1 | (b) | BOD₅: 5 days at 20°C in dark |
| 2 | (a) | $k_T = k_{20}\theta^{(T-20)}$, $\theta = 1.047$ |
| 3 | (a) | Overflow rate $v_s = Q/A$ |
| 4 | (b) | Alum optimal pH 6.0–7.5 |
| 5 | (b) | Breakpoint: free residual first appears |
| 6 | (a) | $F/M = Q \cdot S_0/(V \cdot X)$ |
| 7 | (c) | Secondary: 85–95% BOD removal |
| 8 | (b) | Nitrification is aerobic (autotrophs) |
| 9 | (c) | O₂ = 4.57 × NH₄-N oxidized |
| 10 | (a) | Biogas: 60–65% CH₄, 35–40% CO₂ |
