# Wastewater Engineering

## Overview

Wastewater engineering involves the collection, treatment, and disposal of domestic, industrial, and stormwater. It protects public health and the environment by removing contaminants before discharge or reuse.

## Wastewater Characteristics

### Physical Characteristics
- Temperature, color, odor, turbidity, total solids

### Chemical Characteristics
- pH, BOD₅ (Biochemical Oxygen Demand), COD (Chemical Oxygen Demand)
- Nitrogen species: TKN, ammonia, nitrate, nitrite
- Phosphorus: Total phosphorus, orthophosphate
- Chlorides, sulfates, heavy metals

### Biological Characteristics
- Coliform bacteria, fecal coliform, E. coli
- Pathogens: Viruses, protozoa, helminth eggs

## Sewerage Systems

### Separate System
- Sanitary sewers carry domestic/industrial wastewater
- Storm drains carry rainfall runoff
- Advantages: No dilution of wastewater; treatment plant efficiency
- Disadvantages: Cross-connections, storm drain pollution

### Combined System
- Single sewer carries both wastewater and stormwater
- Advantages: Lower initial cost, single infrastructure
- Disadvantages: Dilution during storms, CSO events, oversized treatment plants

### Sewer Hydraulics
- Design discharge: Peak factor method (Harmon's formula: Qp = P × Qavg)
- Self-cleansing velocity: Minimum 0.6 m/s at peak dry weather flow
- Maximum velocity: 3–4 m/s to prevent abrasion
- Gradient and pipe sizing for gravity flow

## Wastewater Treatment Processes

### Preliminary Treatment
- Screening: Removal of large solids (coarse and fine screens)
- Grit removal: Removal of sand, gravel, and inorganic particles
- Equalization: Flow and load balancing

### Primary Treatment
- Sedimentation (primary clarifiers): Removal of settleable solids and organic matter
- Typical BOD removal: 25–35%
- Detention time: 1.5–2.5 hours

### Secondary Treatment (Biological)

#### Activated Sludge Process
- Aerobic microbial degradation of dissolved and colloidal organics
- Components: Aeration tank, secondary clarifier, sludge recycling
- Parameters: MLSS (mixed liquor suspended solids), SVI (sludge volume index), F/M ratio
- Modifications: Extended aeration, oxidation ditch, sequencing batch reactor (SBR)

#### Trickling Filter
- Fixed-film biological process on a packed bed
- Aerobic conditions as wastewater trickles over biofilm
- Advantages: Lower energy, robust to shock loads

### Secondary Clarification
- Solid-liquid separation of biological flocs
- Sludge thickening and return activated sludge (RAS) control
- Waste activated sludge (WAS) disposal

### Tertiary / Advanced Treatment
- Nutrient removal: Nitrification-denitrification for nitrogen; chemical or biological phosphorus removal
- Filtration: Sand filters, multimedia filters
- Disinfection: Chlorination, UV, ozonation
- Membrane processes: Ultrafiltration, reverse osmosis for reuse

### Sludge Management
- Thickening: Gravity thickeners, dissolved air flotation (DAF)
- Stabilization: Anaerobic digestion (biogas production), aerobic digestion, lime stabilization
- Dewatering: Belt filter press, centrifuge, drying beds
- Final disposal: Land application, incineration, landfilling

## Stormwater Management

### Urban Drainage
- Design storm selection and IDF curves
- Rational method: Q = CiA
- Time-area method for large catchments

### BMPs (Best Management Practices)
- Detention ponds, retention basins, constructed wetlands
- Permeable pavements, green roofs, rain gardens
- Low Impact Development (LID) and Sustainable Drainage Systems (SuDS)
