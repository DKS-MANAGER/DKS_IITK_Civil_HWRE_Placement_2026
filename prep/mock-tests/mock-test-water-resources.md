# Mock Test — Water Resources Engineer

> **Timed test:** 60 minutes · 3 sections · 50 marks. Simulates a PSU/core water-resources technical round (WAPCOS, NTPC, AECOM, Rodic).

---

## Section A — Technical MCQ (10 × 2 = 20 marks) · 15 min

1. The mass curve method is used to determine:
   - (a) Flood peak
   - (b) Reservoir storage capacity
   - (c) Aquifer transmissivity
   - (d) Canal seepage

2. In the Muskingum method, the weighting factor X ranges:
   - (a) 0 to 0.5
   - (b) 0 to 1
   - (c) -1 to 1
   - (d) 0.5 to 1

3. A confined aquifer is characterized by:
   - (a) Water table as upper boundary
   - (b) Impermeable layer above and below
   - (c) No storage coefficient
   - (d) Unconfined flow

4. Environmental flows refer to:
   - (a) Maximum flood discharge
   - (b) Minimum flow needed to sustain river ecology
   - (c) Design flood for dam spillway
   - (d) Groundwater recharge

5. The Theis equation assumes:
   - (a) Unsteady flow in a confined aquifer
   - (b) Steady flow in an unconfined aquifer
   - (c) Constant drawdown
   - (d) No well storage

6. Stage-discharge relationship is used to:
   - (a) Convert water level to discharge
   - (b) Design canal cross-section
   - (c) Estimate infiltration
   - (d) Route floods

7. The specific yield of an aquifer is:
   - (a) Volume of water drained by gravity / total volume
   - (b) Ratio of transmissivity to storage
   - (c) Hydraulic conductivity × thickness
   - (d) Porosity

8. A reservoir's live storage is the volume:
   - (a) Below dead storage
   - (b) Between dead storage and FRL
   - (c) Above FRL
   - (d) Of the dam body

9. The Rational method estimates:
   - (a) Peak runoff from a catchment
   - (b) Reservoir evaporation
   - (c) Groundwater recharge
   - (d) Sediment load

10. Hardy Cross method is used for:
    - (a) Pipe network analysis
    - (b) Flood routing
    - (c) Unit hydrograph derivation
    - (d) Aquifer testing

## Section B — Numerical (3 × 8 = 24 marks) · 30 min

**Q1.** A reservoir has a catchment of 200 km². Annual inflow volume is 150 Mm³ and annual demand is 120 Mm³. Using the mass curve approach, estimate the required storage if the maximum cumulative deficit is 40 Mm³. (Assume no losses.)

**Q2.** A confined aquifer has T = 0.002 m²/s and S = 0.0002. A well pumps at Q = 0.02 m³/s. Using the Theis equation, estimate drawdown at r = 100 m after 10 days. (W(u) for u = 0.005 is ≈ 4.72.)

**Q3.** A canal of trapezoidal section (b = 4 m, side slope 1:1) carries Q = 20 m³/s at a bed slope of 1 in 4000. Using Manning's n = 0.025, estimate the normal depth.

## Section C — Behavioral / HR (2 × 3 = 6 marks) · 15 min

1. "Describe a time you had to justify a technical decision under time pressure." (STAR)
2. "Why water resources engineering, and why this company?" (Motivation)

## Answer Key

**Section A:** 1-b, 2-a, 3-b, 4-b, 5-a, 6-a, 7-a, 8-b, 9-a, 10-a

**Section B:**
- Q1: Required storage = max cumulative deficit = 40 Mm³.
- Q2: u = r²S/4Tt = 100²×0.0002/(4×0.002×864000) = 0.000289. W(u) ≈ ln(1/u) − 0.5772 ≈ 7.57. s = (Q/4πT)W(u) = (0.02/(4π×0.002))×7.57 ≈ 6.02 m.
- Q3: A = by + zy² = 4y + y²; P = b + 2y√(1+z²) = 4 + 2.828y; R = A/P. Solve Q = (1/n)A R^(2/3) S^(1/2) → y ≈ 1.9 m.

**Section C:** Assess STAR structure, quantified results, role motivation, company research.
