# Mock Test — GIS / Survey Engineer

> **Timed test:** 60 minutes · 3 sections · 50 marks. Simulates a GIS/geospatial technical round (GIST, Vassarlabs, remote sensing consultancies).

---

## Section A — Technical MCQ (10 × 2 = 20 marks) · 15 min

1. GIS stands for:
   - (a) Geographic Information System
   - (b) Global Imaging System
   - (c) Geospatial Indexing Service
   - (d) General Information System

2. A raster data model represents:
   - (a) A grid of cells with values
   - (b) Points, lines, and polygons
   - (c) Only vector data
   - (d) Only attribute tables

3. GNSS stands for:
   - (a) Global Navigation Satellite System
   - (b) Geographic Navigation Survey System
   - (c) Global Network of Survey Stations
   - (d) General Navigation Satellite Service

4. Remote sensing detects:
   - (a) Reflected/emitted electromagnetic radiation
   - (b) Only visible light
   - (c) Only sound waves
   - (d) Only ground vibration

5. The NDVI index is used to assess:
   - (a) Vegetation health
   - (b) Soil moisture
   - (c) Urban heat
   - (d) Water quality

6. LiDAR measures:
   - (a) Distance using laser pulses
   - (b) Temperature
   - (c) Humidity
   - (d) Pressure

7. A digital elevation model (DEM) represents:
   - (a) Terrain elevation
   - (b) Land use
   - (c) Soil type
   - (d) Population density

8. The UTM coordinate system divides the world into:
   - (a) 60 zones
   - (b) 24 zones
   - (c) 180 zones
   - (d) 10 zones

9. Buffering in GIS creates:
   - (a) A zone of specified distance around a feature
   - (b) A new raster
   - (c) A 3D model
   - (d) A network

10. The spatial resolution of a satellite image refers to:
    - (a) The ground area represented by one pixel
    - (b) The number of bands
    - (c) The revisit time
    - (d) The file size

---

## Section B — Numerical (3 × 8 = 24 marks) · 30 min

**Q1.** A satellite image has a spatial resolution of 30 m. How many pixels cover an area of 1 km²?

**Q2.** A surveyor measures a distance of 500 m with a total station. If the instrument has an accuracy of ±5 mm + 5 ppm, find the expected error.

**Q3.** A catchment of 100 km² is delineated in GIS. If the average annual rainfall is 1200 mm and the runoff coefficient is 0.4, estimate the annual runoff volume.

---

## Section C — Behavioral / HR (2 × 3 = 6 marks) · 15 min

1. "Describe a GIS analysis you performed and its application." (STAR)
2. "Why geospatial engineering, and what GIS tools do you use?" (Motivation)

---

## Answer Key

**Section A:** 1-a, 2-a, 3-a, 4-a, 5-a, 6-a, 7-a, 8-a, 9-a, 10-a

**Section B:**
- Q1: 1 km² = 1,000,000 m². Pixel area = 30×30 = 900 m². Pixels = 1,000,000/900 = 1111.
- Q2: Error = 5 mm + 5 ppm × 500 m = 5 mm + 2.5 mm = 7.5 mm.
- Q3: Runoff = 0.4 × 1200 mm × 100 km² = 0.4 × 1.2 m × 100×10⁶ m² = 48×10⁶ m³ = 48 Mm³.

**Section C:** Assess STAR structure, analysis depth, tool proficiency, motivation.
