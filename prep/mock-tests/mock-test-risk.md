# Mock Test — Risk

> **Timed test:** 60 minutes · 3 sections · 50 marks. Simulates a risk/quant technical round (risk analytics, credit risk, market risk roles).

---

## Section A — Technical MCQ (10 × 2 = 20 marks) · 15 min

1. Value at Risk (VaR) measures:
   - (a) Maximum loss at a given confidence level
   - (b) Average loss
   - (c) Total risk
   - (d) Expected return

2. The 5% VaR means:
   - (a) 5% chance of losing more than the VaR amount
   - (b) 95% chance of losing more
   - (c) Loss is always 5%
   - (d) 5% return

3. Credit risk is the risk of:
   - (a) Borrower default
   - (b) Market price movement
   - (c) Interest rate change
   - (d) Currency fluctuation

4. Market risk includes:
   - (a) Interest rate, equity, and currency risk
   - (b) Only credit risk
   - (c) Only operational risk
   - (d) Only liquidity risk

5. The standard deviation measures:
   - (a) Volatility of returns
   - (b) Average return
   - (c) Median return
   - (d) Skewness

6. A credit score is used to:
   - (a) Assess borrower creditworthiness
   - (b) Measure market risk
   - (c) Calculate VaR
   - (d) Price derivatives

7. Operational risk arises from:
   - (a) Failed internal processes and systems
   - (b) Market movements
   - (c) Borrower default
   - (d) Currency changes

8. The Sharpe ratio measures:
   - (a) Risk-adjusted return
   - (b) Total return
   - (c) Volatility
   - (d) Beta

9. Stress testing involves:
   - (a) Simulating extreme adverse scenarios
   - (b) Normal market conditions
   - (c) Only historical data
   - (d) Only optimistic scenarios

10. Liquidity risk is the risk of:
    - (a) Inability to meet short-term obligations
    - (b) Market crash
    - (c) Borrower default
    - (d) Inflation

---

## Section B — Numerical (3 × 8 = 24 marks) · 30 min

**Q1.** A portfolio has an expected return of 12% and a standard deviation of 20%. If the risk-free rate is 5%, find the Sharpe ratio.

**Q2.** A bank's daily portfolio returns have a mean of 0 and standard deviation of ₹2 Cr. Estimate the 1-day 95% VaR (assume normal distribution, z = 1.645).

**Q3.** A loan portfolio has a 2% default rate and 40% loss given default. Find the expected loss as a percentage of exposure.

---

## Section C — Behavioral / HR (2 × 3 = 6 marks) · 15 min

1. "Describe a time you identified and mitigated a risk." (STAR)
2. "Why risk management, and how does your analytical background help?" (Motivation)

---

## Answer Key

**Section A:** 1-a, 2-a, 3-a, 4-a, 5-a, 6-a, 7-a, 8-a, 9-a, 10-a

**Section B:**
- Q1: Sharpe = (12% − 5%)/20% = 0.35.
- Q2: VaR = 1.645 × 2 = ₹3.29 Cr.
- Q3: Expected loss = PD × LGD = 2% × 40% = 0.8% of exposure.

**Section C:** Assess STAR structure, risk awareness, analytical rigor, motivation.
