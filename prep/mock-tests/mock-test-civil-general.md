# Mock Test — Civil Engineer (General)

> **Timed test:** 60 minutes · 3 sections · 50 marks. Simulates a broad civil technical round (PSU / generalist core roles).

---

## Section A — Technical MCQ (10 × 2 = 20 marks) · 15 min

1. The effective stress principle was proposed by:
   - (a) Terzaghi
   - (b) Coulomb
   - (c) Rankine
   - (d) Darcy

2. The hydraulic gradient in Darcy's law is:
   - (a) Head loss / flow length
   - (b) Flow length / head loss
   - (c) Discharge / area
   - (d) Velocity × area

3. The Froude number is the ratio of:
   - (a) Inertia to gravity forces
   - (b) Inertia to viscous forces
   - (c) Pressure to inertia forces
   - (d) Gravity to surface tension

4. In CPM, the critical path is:
   - (a) The longest path with zero float
   - (b) The shortest path
   - (c) Any path with float
   - (d) The path with most activities

5. The bearing capacity of soil depends on:
   - (a) Shear strength parameters
   - (b) Foundation depth
   - (c) Unit weight of soil
   - (d) All of the above

6. The slump test measures:
   - (a) Workability of concrete
   - (b) Compressive strength
   - (c) Water-cement ratio
   - (d) Aggregate size

7. The moment of inertia of a rectangular section (b × d) about its centroidal axis is:
   - (a) bd³/12
   - (b) bd³/3
   - (c) bd²/6
   - (d) b²d/12

8. The unit of dynamic viscosity is:
   - (a) N·s/m²
   - (b) N/m²
   - (c) m²/s
   - (d) kg/m³

9. The standard penetration test (SPT) measures:
   - (a) N-value of soil
   - (b) Water content
   - (c) Specific gravity
   - (d) Atterberg limits

10. The design life of a typical highway pavement (flexible) is:
    - (a) 10–15 years
    - (b) 30–40 years
    - (c) 5 years
    - (d) 100 years

## Section B — Numerical (3 × 8 = 24 marks) · 30 min

**Q1.** A soil sample has a void ratio of 0.6 and specific gravity of 2.65. If the degree of saturation is 80%, find the water content and bulk unit weight.

**Q2.** A rectangular channel (b = 3 m) carries Q = 12 m³/s at a depth of 1.5 m. Find the Froude number and classify the flow.

**Q3.** A simply supported beam of span 5 m carries a point load of 30 kN at mid-span. Draw the bending moment and shear force diagrams and find the maximum bending moment.

---

## Section C — Behavioral / HR (2 × 3 = 6 marks) · 15 min

1. "Describe a time you solved a problem on a construction site or in a project." (STAR)
2. "Why civil engineering, and what is your preferred domain?" (Motivation)

---

## Answer Key

**Section A:** 1-a, 2-a, 3-a, 4-a, 5-d, 6-a, 7-a, 8-a, 9-a, 10-a

**Section B:**
- Q1: e = 0.6, G = 2.65, S = 0.8. w = Se/G = 0.8×0.6/2.65 = 0.181 = 18.1%. γ = (G + Se)γw/(1+e) = (2.65 + 0.8×0.6)×9.81/1.6 = 19.2 kN/m³.
- Q2: V = Q/(by) = 12/(3×1.5) = 2.67 m/s. Fr = V/√(gy) = 2.67/√(9.81×1.5) = 0.70. Fr < 1 → subcritical.
- Q3: RA = RB = 15 kN. Max BM at mid-span = 15×2.5 = 37.5 kN·m. SF jumps from +15 to −15 at mid-span.

**Section C:** Assess STAR structure, site/problem awareness, domain clarity, quantified results.
