# Mock Test — Operations

> **Timed test:** 60 minutes · 3 sections · 50 marks. Simulates an operations technical round (operations analyst, business operations roles).

---

## Section A — Technical MCQ (10 × 2 = 20 marks) · 15 min

1. Operations management focuses on:
   - (a) Efficiently converting inputs to outputs
   - (b) Only marketing
   - (c) Only finance
   - (d) Only HR

2. Throughput is:
   - (a) The rate of production/output
   - (b) The inventory level
   - (c) The cost of production
   - (d) The lead time

3. The bottleneck is:
   - (a) The constraint limiting throughput
   - (b) The fastest process
   - (c) The cheapest process
   - (d) The most expensive process

4. Lean operations aim to:
   - (a) Eliminate waste
   - (b) Increase inventory
   - (c) Increase lead time
   - (d) Reduce quality

5. Six Sigma focuses on:
   - (a) Reducing process variation
   - (b) Increasing variation
   - (c) Reducing cost only
   - (d) Increasing speed only

6. The DMAIC framework stands for:
   - (a) Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control
   - (b) Design, Manage, Assess, Implement, Control
   - (c) Define, Monitor, Analyze, Improve, Check
   - (d) Develop, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control

7. Capacity utilization is:
   - (a) Actual output / maximum possible output
   - (b) Maximum output / actual output
   - (c) Inventory / output
   - (d) Cost / output

8. The theory of constraints states that:
   - (a) Every system has a bottleneck
   - (b) Every process is perfect
   - (c) Inventory is always good
   - (d) Speed is everything

9. A KPI is:
   - (a) A key performance indicator
   - (b) A key process input
   - (c) A cost metric
   - (d) A quality metric

10. The service level in operations is:
    - (a) The probability of meeting demand from stock
    - (b) The cost of service
    - (c) The speed of service
    - (d) The quality of service

---

## Section B — Numerical (3 × 8 = 24 marks) · 30 min

**Q1.** A factory produces 500 units/day with a capacity of 800 units/day. Find the capacity utilization.

**Q2.** A process has a cycle time of 2 minutes per unit. Find the throughput per hour.

**Q3.** A warehouse has an average inventory of 1000 units and annual sales of 12,000 units. Find the inventory turnover ratio.

---

## Section C — Behavioral / HR (2 × 3 = 6 marks) · 15 min

1. "Describe a time you improved an operational process." (STAR)
2. "Why operations, and how does your engineering background help?" (Motivation)

---

## Answer Key

**Section A:** 1-a, 2-a, 3-a, 4-a, 5-a, 6-a, 7-a, 8-a, 9-a, 10-a

**Section B:**
- Q1: Utilization = 500/800 = 62.5%.
- Q2: Throughput = 60/2 = 30 units/hour.
- Q3: Turnover = 12,000/1000 = 12 times/year.

**Section C:** Assess STAR structure, process improvement, analytical rigor, motivation.
