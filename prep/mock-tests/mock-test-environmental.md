# Mock Test — Environmental Engineer

> **Timed test:** 60 minutes · 3 sections · 50 marks. Simulates an environmental technical round (Rodic, BPCL, EIA consultancies).

---

## Section A — Technical MCQ (10 × 2 = 20 marks) · 15 min

1. BOD measures:
   - (a) Oxygen consumed by microorganisms to decompose organic matter
   - (b) Chemical oxygen demand
   - (c) Dissolved oxygen
   - (d) Total suspended solids

2. The BOD₅ test is incubated at:
   - (a) 20°C for 5 days
   - (b) 37°C for 24 hours
   - (c) 25°C for 3 days
   - (d) 30°C for 7 days

3. COD is always:
   - (a) Greater than or equal to BOD
   - (b) Less than BOD
   - (c) Equal to BOD
   - (d) Independent of BOD

4. The activated sludge process is a:
   - (a) Biological treatment method
   - (b) Physical treatment method
   - (c) Chemical treatment method
   - (d) Thermal treatment method

5. EIA stands for:
   - (a) Environmental Impact Assessment
   - (b) Environmental Inspection Agency
   - (c) Ecological Impact Analysis
   - (d) Energy Impact Assessment

6. The primary treatment of wastewater removes:
   - (a) Suspended solids
   - (b) Dissolved organic matter
   - (c) Nutrients
   - (d) Pathogens

7. The permissible limit of BOD in treated effluent discharged into inland surface water (India) is:
   - (a) 30 mg/L
   - (b) 100 mg/L
   - (c) 5 mg/L
   - (d) 500 mg/L

8. Sludge volume index (SVI) is used to assess:
   - (a) Sludge settling characteristics
   - (b) Sludge moisture content
   - (c) Sludge calorific value
   - (d) Sludge age

9. The greenhouse gas primarily responsible for global warming is:
   - (a) CO₂
   - (b) O₂
   - (c) N₂
   - (d) Ar

10. The trickling filter is a:
    - (a) Attached-growth biological treatment
    - (b) Suspended-growth treatment
    - (c) Physical filtration
    - (d) Chemical precipitation

---

## Section B — Numerical (3 × 8 = 24 marks) · 30 min

**Q1.** A wastewater has BOD₅ = 200 mg/L and a rate constant k = 0.23/day (base 10). Estimate the ultimate BOD (L₀).

**Q2.** A city of 100,000 people generates 150 L/capita/day of wastewater with a BOD of 300 mg/L. Find the total BOD load in kg/day.

**Q3.** An activated sludge plant treats 10,000 m³/day with an influent BOD of 250 mg/L. If the effluent BOD is 20 mg/L, find the BOD removal efficiency.

---

## Section C — Behavioral / HR (2 × 3 = 6 marks) · 15 min

1. "Describe an environmental project and its impact." (STAR)
2. "Why environmental engineering, and what regulations are you familiar with?" (Motivation)

---

## Answer Key

**Section A:** 1-a, 2-a, 3-a, 4-a, 5-a, 6-a, 7-a, 8-a, 9-a, 10-a

**Section B:**
- Q1: BOD₅ = L₀(1 − 10^(−kt)). 200 = L₀(1 − 10^(−0.23×5)) = L₀(1 − 10^(−1.15)) = L₀(1 − 0.0708) = 0.929L₀. L₀ = 200/0.929 = 215 mg/L.
- Q2: Flow = 100,000 × 150 = 15,000 m³/day. BOD load = 15,000 × 300 mg/L = 15,000 × 0.3 kg/m³ = 4,500 kg/day.
- Q3: Efficiency = (250 − 20)/250 × 100 = 92%.

**Section C:** Assess STAR structure, environmental impact, regulatory awareness, motivation.
