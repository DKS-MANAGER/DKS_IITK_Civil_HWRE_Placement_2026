# Mock Test — Consulting

> **Timed test:** 60 minutes · 3 sections · 50 marks. Simulates a consulting case + fit round (BCG, McKinsey, Bain, Accenture Strategy).

---

## Section A — Case Frameworks (2 × 10 = 20 marks) · 20 min

**Case 1 — Market Sizing (10 marks):**
Estimate the annual market size for ready-to-eat packaged food in India. State all assumptions clearly and show your calculation chain.

**Case 2 — Profitability (10 marks):**
A client's profit has declined 15% over two years despite stable revenue. Build a framework to diagnose the root cause. List the key levers you would investigate under revenue and cost.

---

## Section B — Guesstimate & Quantitative (2 × 8 = 16 marks) · 20 min

**Q1.** Estimate the number of petrol pumps in India. Show your logic.

**Q2.** A company's revenue is ₹500 Cr with 20% EBITDA margin. If costs rise 10% and revenue stays flat, what is the new margin?

---

## Section C — Fit / Behavioral (2 × 7 = 14 marks) · 20 min

1. "Walk me through your resume. Why consulting?" (7 marks)
2. "Tell me about a time you led a team under ambiguity." (STAR, 7 marks)

---

## Answer Key / Evaluation Guide

**Section A:**
- Case 1: Population 1.4B → urban ~35% → target income segment ~40% → frequency ~2 packs/month → price ~₹50. Market ≈ 1.4B×0.35×0.4×24×50 ≈ ₹235 Bn. Assess clarity of assumptions and arithmetic.
- Case 2: Revenue levers (price, volume, mix, channel) and cost levers (COGS, opex, fixed/variable). Assess structured MECE breakdown.

**Section B:**
- Q1: Population 1.4B → vehicles ~300M → ~1 pump per 1000 vehicles → ~300,000 pumps. Assess logic chain.
- Q2: Revenue 500, EBITDA 20% = 100. Costs = 400. New costs = 440. New EBITDA = 60. New margin = 60/500 = 12%.

**Section C:** Assess STAR structure, leadership, ambiguity handling, motivation, communication.
