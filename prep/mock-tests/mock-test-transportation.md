# Mock Test — Transportation Engineer

> **Timed test:** 60 minutes · 3 sections · 50 marks. Simulates a transportation technical round (ASC Infratech, highway/railway consultancies).

---

## Section A — Technical MCQ (10 × 2 = 20 marks) · 15 min

1. The design speed of a highway is determined by:
   - (a) Terrain and functional classification
   - (b) Only traffic volume
   - (c) Only vehicle type
   - (d) Weather

2. The CBR test is used to design:
   - (a) Flexible pavement thickness
   - (b) Rigid pavement joints
   - (c) Bridge superstructure
   - (d) Tunnel lining

3. The stopping sight distance depends on:
   - (a) Speed, reaction time, and friction
   - (b) Only speed
   - (c) Only gradient
   - (d) Only vehicle length

4. The Greenshields model relates:
   - (a) Speed and density
   - (b) Flow and headway
   - (c) Volume and capacity
   - (d) Delay and queue

5. The superelevation is provided to:
   - (a) Counteract centrifugal force on curves
   - (b) Drain the pavement
   - (c) Increase speed limit
   - (d) Reduce friction

6. The design life of a rigid (concrete) pavement is typically:
   - (a) 20–30 years
   - (b) 5–10 years
   - (c) 50 years
   - (d) 100 years

7. The level of service (LOS) of a highway ranges from:
   - (a) A to F
   - (b) 1 to 10
   - (c) I to IV
   - (d) Low to High

8. The camber of a road is provided for:
   - (a) Surface drainage
   - (b) Structural strength
   - (c) Skid resistance
   - (d) Aesthetic appeal

9. Traffic volume is measured in:
   - (a) Vehicles per hour
   - (b) Vehicles per km
   - (c) Km per hour
   - (d) Passengers per km

10. The transition curve is provided to:
    - (a) Gradually change curvature and superelevation
    - (b) Increase speed
    - (c) Reduce pavement width
    - (d) Improve drainage

---

## Section B — Numerical (3 × 8 = 24 marks) · 30 min

**Q1.** A vehicle travels at 80 km/h. If the reaction time is 2.5 s and the coefficient of friction is 0.35, find the stopping sight distance.

**Q2.** On a horizontal curve of radius 300 m, find the required superelevation for a design speed of 80 km/h (neglect lateral friction).

**Q3.** A highway has a design hourly volume of 2000 veh/h and a capacity of 2500 veh/h. Find the volume-to-capacity ratio and the level of service.

---

## Section C — Behavioral / HR (2 × 3 = 6 marks) · 15 min

1. "Describe a transportation project you worked on." (STAR)
2. "Why transportation engineering, and what software do you use?" (Motivation)

---

## Answer Key

**Section A:** 1-a, 2-a, 3-a, 4-a, 5-a, 6-a, 7-a, 8-a, 9-a, 10-a

**Section B:**
- Q1: SSD = vt + v²/2gf = (80/3.6)×2.5 + (80/3.6)²/(2×9.81×0.35) = 55.6 + 72.0 = 127.6 m.
- Q2: e = v²/gR = (80/3.6)²/(9.81×300) = 493.8/2943 = 0.168 = 16.8% (cap at 7% for design).
- Q3: v/c = 2000/2500 = 0.8. LOS ≈ D (0.8–0.9 range).

**Section C:** Assess STAR structure, project depth, software proficiency, motivation.
