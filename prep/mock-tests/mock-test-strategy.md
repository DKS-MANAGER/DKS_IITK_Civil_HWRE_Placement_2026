# Mock Test — Strategy

> **Timed test:** 60 minutes · 3 sections · 50 marks. Simulates a strategy consulting/analyst technical round (strategy roles, corporate strategy).

---

## Section A — Strategy Frameworks (2 × 10 = 20 marks) · 20 min

**Q1.** A company wants to enter a new market. Build a market entry strategy framework covering market attractiveness, competition, entry mode, and risks.

**Q2.** A client's revenue is declining despite a growing market. Build a diagnostic framework to identify the root cause.

---

## Section B — Quantitative (2 × 8 = 16 marks) · 20 min

**Q1.** A company has a 20% market share in a ₹1000 Cr market. If the market grows 10% and the company maintains share, what is the new revenue?

**Q2.** A product has a 30% gross margin. If costs increase 15% and price stays flat, what is the new margin?

---

## Section C — Fit / Behavioral (2 × 7 = 14 marks) · 20 min

1. "Walk me through your resume. Why strategy?" (7 marks)
2. "Tell me about a time you made a strategic decision with limited data." (STAR, 7 marks)

---

## Answer Key / Evaluation Guide

**Section A:**
- Q1: Assess market attractiveness (size, growth, margins), competition (Porter's Five Forces), entry mode (JV, acquisition, organic), risks (regulatory, cultural, execution).
- Q2: Assess market vs company growth gap, share analysis, customer churn, competitive pressure, internal execution.

**Section B:**
- Q1: Market = 1000 × 1.1 = ₹1100 Cr. Revenue = 20% × 1100 = ₹220 Cr.
- Q2: Cost = 70% of price. New cost = 70 × 1.15 = 80.5%. New margin = 100 − 80.5 = 19.5%.

**Section C:** Assess STAR structure, strategic thinking, data-driven decision making, communication.
