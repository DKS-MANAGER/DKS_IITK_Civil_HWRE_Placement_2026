# Mock Test — Finance

> **Timed test:** 60 minutes · 3 sections · 50 marks. Simulates a finance/quant technical round (Barclays, Auronova, finance roles).

---

## Section A — Technical MCQ (10 × 2 = 20 marks) · 15 min

1. The time value of money states that:
   - (a) A rupee today is worth more than a rupee tomorrow
   - (b) A rupee tomorrow is worth more
   - (c) Money has no time value
   - (d) Inflation is irrelevant

2. NPV (Net Present Value) is:
   - (a) Present value of cash inflows minus outflows
   - (b) Future value of cash flows
   - (c) Total profit
   - (d) Revenue minus cost

3. IRR is the discount rate at which:
   - (a) NPV = 0
   - (b) NPV is maximum
   - (c) NPV is minimum
   - (d) Cash flow is zero

4. The CAPM model relates:
   - (a) Expected return to systematic risk (beta)
   - (b) Return to inflation
   - (c) Risk to liquidity
   - (d) Price to earnings

5. A bond's price is inversely related to:
   - (a) Interest rates
   - (b) Face value
   - (c) Coupon rate
   - (d) Maturity

6. Working capital is:
   - (a) Current assets minus current liabilities
   - (b) Total assets minus total liabilities
   - (c) Fixed assets
   - (d) Equity

7. The P/E ratio measures:
   - (a) Price per share / earnings per share
   - (b) Earnings / price
   - (c) Dividend / price
   - (d) Book value / price

8. Diversification reduces:
   - (a) Unsystematic risk
   - (b) Systematic risk
   - (c) Market risk
   - (d) Inflation risk

9. The cost of equity is estimated using:
   - (a) CAPM
   - (b) NPV
   - (c) IRR
   - (d) Payback

10. A company with high leverage has:
    - (a) High debt relative to equity
    - (b) High equity relative to debt
    - (c) No debt
    - (d) High cash reserves

---

## Section B — Numerical (3 × 8 = 24 marks) · 30 min

**Q1.** A project requires an initial investment of ₹100 L and generates cash flows of ₹40 L/year for 4 years. Find the NPV at a 10% discount rate.

**Q2.** A stock has a beta of 1.2. If the risk-free rate is 6% and the market return is 12%, find the expected return using CAPM.

**Q3.** A company has current assets of ₹50 L and current liabilities of ₹30 L. Find the current ratio and interpret it.

---

## Section C — Behavioral / HR (2 × 3 = 6 marks) · 15 min

1. "Describe a time you analyzed financial data to make a decision." (STAR)
2. "Why finance, and how does your engineering background help?" (Motivation)

---

## Answer Key

**Section A:** 1-a, 2-a, 3-a, 4-a, 5-a, 6-a, 7-a, 8-a, 9-a, 10-a

**Section B:**
- Q1: NPV = −100 + 40/1.1 + 40/1.1² + 40/1.1³ + 40/1.1⁴ = −100 + 36.36 + 33.06 + 30.05 + 27.32 = 26.79 L.
- Q2: E(R) = Rf + β(Rm − Rf) = 6% + 1.2×(12% − 6%) = 6% + 7.2% = 13.2%.
- Q3: Current ratio = 50/30 = 1.67. Healthy (1.5–2.0 range indicates good liquidity).

**Section C:** Assess STAR structure, financial acumen, engineering-to-finance bridge, motivation.
