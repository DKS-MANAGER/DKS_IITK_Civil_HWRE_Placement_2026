# Mock Test — Construction Engineer

> **Timed test:** 60 minutes · 3 sections · 50 marks. Simulates a construction/site engineering technical round (L&T, Tata Projects, Godrej Properties).

---

## Section A — Technical MCQ (10 × 2 = 20 marks) · 15 min

1. The slump test measures:
   - (a) Workability of concrete
   - (b) Compressive strength
   - (c) Water-cement ratio
   - (d) Aggregate size

2. The water-cement ratio primarily controls:
   - (a) Concrete strength
   - (b) Concrete color
   - (c) Aggregate size
   - (d) Formwork type

3. The characteristic strength of concrete (fck) is:
   - (a) The strength below which 5% of tests fall
   - (b) The average strength
   - (c) The maximum strength
   - (d) The minimum strength

4. Curing of concrete is done to:
   - (a) Maintain moisture for hydration
   - (b) Increase slump
   - (c) Reduce setting time
   - (d) Improve color

5. The cube compressive strength of M25 concrete at 28 days should be at least:
   - (a) 25 MPa
   - (b) 20 MPa
   - (c) 30 MPa
   - (d) 15 MPa

6. Formwork is removed:
   - (a) After concrete gains sufficient strength
   - (b) Immediately after pouring
   - (c) Before curing
   - (d) Only after 28 days

7. The compaction factor test measures:
   - (a) Workability of concrete
   - (b) Strength of concrete
   - (c) Durability
   - (d) Water content

8. A bar bending schedule (BBS) provides:
   - (a) Cutting and bending details of reinforcement
   - (b) Concrete mix design
   - (c) Formwork details
   - (d) Soil test results

9. The safe bearing capacity of soil is used to:
   - (a) Design foundations
   - (b) Design superstructure
   - (c) Select concrete grade
   - (d) Plan curing

10. The standard curing period for ordinary Portland cement concrete is:
    - (a) 7 days
    - (b) 28 days
    - (c) 3 days
    - (d) 90 days

---

## Section B — Numerical (3 × 8 = 24 marks) · 30 min

**Q1.** A concrete mix has a water-cement ratio of 0.5 and cement content of 350 kg/m³. Find the water content per m³.

**Q2.** A slab of 10 m × 6 m × 0.15 m is to be cast. Find the volume of concrete and the number of cement bags (50 kg) needed at 350 kg/m³.

**Q3.** A footing requires 500 kg of steel. If the wastage is 5%, find the total steel to order.

---

## Section C — Behavioral / HR (2 × 3 = 6 marks) · 15 min

1. "Describe a construction site challenge you faced and solved." (STAR)
2. "Why construction engineering, and what site experience do you have?" (Motivation)

---

## Answer Key

**Section A:** 1-a, 2-a, 3-a, 4-a, 5-a, 6-a, 7-a, 8-a, 9-a, 10-a

**Section B:**
- Q1: Water = w/c × cement = 0.5 × 350 = 175 kg/m³ = 175 L/m³.
- Q2: Volume = 10×6×0.15 = 9 m³. Cement = 350×9 = 3150 kg = 63 bags.
- Q3: Total = 500 × 1.05 = 525 kg.

**Section C:** Assess STAR structure, site awareness, problem-solving, motivation.
