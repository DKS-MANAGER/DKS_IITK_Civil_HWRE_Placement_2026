# Mock Test — Product Manager

> **Timed test:** 60 minutes · 3 sections · 50 marks. Simulates a PM product-sense + metrics round.

---

## Section A — Product Sense (2 × 10 = 20 marks) · 20 min

**Q1.** Design a feature for a ride-hailing app to reduce driver cancellations. Define the target user, problem, solution, success metrics, and risks.

**Q2.** A food-delivery app sees a 20% drop in repeat orders. Build a framework to diagnose the cause and propose prioritized experiments.

---

## Section B — Metrics & Strategy (2 × 8 = 16 marks) · 20 min

**Q1.** Define the North Star metric for an e-commerce marketplace. List 3 supporting metrics and explain the trade-offs.

**Q2.** A product has 1M MAU, 10% weekly retention, and ₹50 ARPU. Estimate monthly revenue and identify the biggest lever for growth.

---

## Section C — Behavioral / Product Judgment (2 × 7 = 14 marks) · 20 min

1. "Tell me about a product you use daily and how you would improve it." (7 marks)
2. "Describe a time you influenced a decision without authority." (STAR, 7 marks)

---

## Answer Key / Evaluation Guide

**Section A:**
- Q1: Assess user segmentation (driver pain: low earnings, long waits, unclear destination), solution (surge, destination preview, guaranteed earnings), metrics (cancellation rate, driver ETA, earnings), risks (cost, driver gaming).
- Q2: Assess funnel analysis (discovery → order → delivery → repeat), cohort analysis, retention levers (quality, price, loyalty, notifications), prioritization.

**Section B:**
- Q1: North Star = GMV or orders. Supporting: conversion rate, repeat rate, CAC, AOV. Assess trade-off reasoning.
- Q2: Monthly revenue = 1M × 0.10 × ₹50 = ₹5 Cr (weekly retention proxy). Biggest lever = retention (10% → 20% doubles revenue).

**Section C:** Assess product thinking, user empathy, influence, STAR structure.
