# Mock Test — Business Operations (BizOps)

> **Timed test:** 60 minutes · 3 sections · 50 marks. Simulates a business operations/analyst technical round (BizOps, ops analyst roles).

---

## Section A — Business & Ops (5 × 4 = 20 marks) · 20 min

**Q1.** Define 3 KPIs for an e-commerce marketplace and explain why each matters.

**Q2.** A business's revenue is flat but costs are rising. List the levers you would investigate.

**Q3.** Explain the difference between a metric and a KPI.

**Q4.** A process has a 10% error rate. What is the probability of 3 errors in 10 units (assuming independence)?

**Q5.** Define unit economics for a subscription business.

---

## Section B — Quantitative (2 × 8 = 16 marks) · 20 min

**Q1.** A company has 1000 customers, each paying ₹500/month, with a 5% monthly churn. Estimate monthly revenue and the number of customers after 1 month.

**Q2.** A business has revenue ₹100 Cr, COGS ₹60 Cr, and opex ₹25 Cr. Find gross margin and operating margin.

---

## Section C — Case / Behavioral (2 × 7 = 14 marks) · 20 min

1. "A key operational metric dropped. How would you investigate it?" (7 marks)
2. "Tell me about a time you improved a business process." (STAR, 7 marks)

---

## Answer Key / Evaluation Guide

**Section A:**
- Q1: GMV (growth), conversion rate (efficiency), repeat rate (retention). Assess rationale.
- Q2: Revenue levers (price, volume, mix) and cost levers (COGS, opex, fixed/variable).
- Q3: A metric is any measure; a KPI is a metric tied to strategic objectives.
- Q4: P(3 errors in 10) = C(10,3)×0.1³×0.9⁷ = 120×0.001×0.478 = 0.0574 = 5.74%.
- Q5: LTV = ARPU × gross margin / churn; CAC; LTV:CAC ratio.

**Section B:**
- Q1: Revenue = 1000 × 500 = ₹5,00,000/month. After 1 month = 1000 × 0.95 = 950 customers.
- Q2: Gross margin = (100−60)/100 = 40%. Operating margin = (100−60−25)/100 = 15%.

**Section C:** Assess analytical framework, metric decomposition, process improvement, STAR structure.
