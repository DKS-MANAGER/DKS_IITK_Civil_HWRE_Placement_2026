# Mock Test — Supply Chain

> **Timed test:** 60 minutes · 3 sections · 50 marks. Simulates a supply chain/operations technical round.

---

## Section A — Technical MCQ (10 × 2 = 20 marks) · 15 min

1. The Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) minimizes:
   - (a) Total inventory cost
   - (b) Ordering cost only
   - (c) Holding cost only
   - (d) Purchase cost

2. The bullwhip effect refers to:
   - (a) Demand variability amplification upstream
   - (b) Supply variability
   - (c) Price fluctuation
   - (d) Quality variation

3. Lead time is:
   - (a) Time from order placement to delivery
   - (b) Time to manufacture
   - (c) Time to sell
   - (d) Time to pay

4. Safety stock is held to:
   - (a) Buffer against demand/supply uncertainty
   - (b) Increase inventory cost
   - (c) Reduce order frequency
   - (d) Increase lead time

5. The reorder point is:
   - (a) Inventory level triggering a new order
   - (b) Maximum inventory
   - (c) Safety stock
   - (d) EOQ

6. Just-in-Time (JIT) aims to:
   - (a) Minimize inventory by aligning supply with demand
   - (b) Maximize inventory
   - (c) Increase lead time
   - (d) Reduce quality

7. A supply chain network includes:
   - (a) Suppliers, manufacturers, distributors, retailers
   - (b) Only suppliers
   - (c) Only retailers
   - (d) Only customers

8. The ABC analysis classifies inventory by:
   - (a) Value and importance
   - (b) Weight
   - (c) Color
   - (d) Age

9. Vendor Managed Inventory (VMI) means:
   - (a) The supplier manages the buyer's inventory
   - (b) The buyer manages supplier inventory
   - (c) No inventory
   - (d) Third-party inventory

10. The fill rate measures:
    - (a) The percentage of demand met from stock
    - (b) The order cost
    - (c) The lead time
    - (d) The inventory value

---

## Section B — Numerical (3 × 8 = 24 marks) · 30 min

**Q1.** Annual demand is 10,000 units, ordering cost is ₹200/order, and holding cost is ₹5/unit/year. Find the EOQ.

**Q2.** Lead time is 5 days and daily demand is 100 units with a standard deviation of 20. Find the reorder point for a 95% service level (z = 1.645).

**Q3.** A warehouse has 500 units of inventory valued at ₹100 each. If the holding cost is 20% of value per year, find the annual holding cost.

---

## Section C — Behavioral / HR (2 × 3 = 6 marks) · 15 min

1. "Describe a time you optimized a process or reduced cost." (STAR)
2. "Why supply chain, and how does your engineering background help?" (Motivation)

---

## Answer Key

**Section A:** 1-a, 2-a, 3-a, 4-a, 5-a, 6-a, 7-a, 8-a, 9-a, 10-a

**Section B:**
- Q1: EOQ = √(2DS/H) = √(2×10000×200/5) = √800000 = 894 units.
- Q2: ROP = d×L + z×σ×√L = 100×5 + 1.645×20×√5 = 500 + 73.6 = 574 units.
- Q3: Holding cost = 500 × 100 × 0.20 = ₹10,000/year.

**Section C:** Assess STAR structure, optimization mindset, analytical rigor, motivation.
