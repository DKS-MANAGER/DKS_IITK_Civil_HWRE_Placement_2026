# Mock Test — Geotechnical Engineer

> **Timed test:** 60 minutes · 3 sections · 50 marks. Simulates a geotechnical technical round (Reliance New Energy, L&T, foundation consultancies).

---

## Section A — Technical MCQ (10 × 2 = 20 marks) · 15 min

1. The effective stress principle states that:
   - (a) Effective stress = total stress − pore water pressure
   - (b) Effective stress = total stress + pore water pressure
   - (c) Effective stress = pore water pressure
   - (d) Effective stress = total stress

2. Terzaghi's bearing capacity equation includes factors for:
   - (a) Cohesion, surcharge, and unit weight
   - (b) Only cohesion
   - (c) Only depth
   - (d) Only width

3. The coefficient of consolidation (Cv) is determined from:
   - (a) Oedometer test
   - (b) Triaxial test
   - (c) Direct shear test
   - (d) Proctor test

4. The Mohr-Coulomb failure criterion relates:
   - (a) Shear strength to normal stress
   - (b) Void ratio to pressure
   - (c) Permeability to gradient
   - (d) Settlement to time

5. The standard penetration test (SPT) N-value is used to estimate:
   - (a) Relative density and bearing capacity
   - (b) Water content
   - (c) Specific gravity
   - (d) Atterberg limits

6. Rankine's earth pressure theory assumes:
   - (a) Smooth vertical wall, no wall friction
   - (b) Rough wall with friction
   - (c) Cohesive backfill only
   - (d) Saturated backfill

7. The critical hydraulic gradient for quick sand condition is approximately:
   - (a) 1.0
   - (b) 0.5
   - (c) 2.0
   - (d) 0.1

8. Consolidation settlement is caused by:
   - (a) Expulsion of pore water
   - (b) Immediate elastic deformation
   - (c) Creep only
   - (d) Grain crushing

9. The Proctor compaction test determines:
   - (a) Optimum moisture content and maximum dry density
   - (b) Shear strength
   - (c) Permeability
   - (d) Consolidation coefficient

10. A pile foundation is preferred when:
    - (a) Shallow soil is weak
    - (b) Water table is deep
    - (c) Loads are light
    - (d) Rock is at surface

## Section B — Numerical (3 × 8 = 24 marks) · 30 min

**Q1.** A soil has c = 20 kPa, φ = 30°, and unit weight γ = 18 kN/m³. Using Terzaghi's bearing capacity factors (Nc = 37.2, Nq = 22.5, Nγ = 19.7), find the ultimate bearing capacity of a strip footing of width 1.5 m at depth 1 m.

**Q2.** A clay layer of thickness 4 m has Cv = 2×10⁻⁷ m²/s. Estimate the time for 50% consolidation (Tv = 0.197) for double drainage.

**Q3.** A retaining wall is 5 m high with a dry sand backfill (γ = 17 kN/m³, φ = 30°). Find the active earth pressure resultant using Rankine's theory.

---

## Section C — Behavioral / HR (2 × 3 = 6 marks) · 15 min

1. "Describe a geotechnical investigation you were part of." (STAR)
2. "Why geotechnical engineering, and what software do you use?" (Motivation)

## Answer Key

**Section A:** 1-a, 2-a, 3-a, 4-a, 5-a, 6-a, 7-a, 8-a, 9-a, 10-a

**Section B:**
- Q1: qu = cNc + qNq + 0.5γBNγ = 20×37.2 + (18×1)×22.5 + 0.5×18×1.5×19.7 = 744 + 405 + 266 = 1415 kPa.
- Q2: t = Tv·H²/Cv = 0.197×(2)²/(2×10⁻⁷) = 0.197×4/2e-7 = 3.94×10⁶ s ≈ 45.6 days (H = 2 m for double drainage).
- Q3: Ka = (1−sinφ)/(1+sinφ) = (1−0.5)/(1+0.5) = 0.333. Pa = 0.5×Ka×γ×H² = 0.5×0.333×17×25 = 70.8 kN/m at H/3 = 1.67 m from base.

**Section C:** Assess STAR structure, investigation depth, software proficiency, motivation.
