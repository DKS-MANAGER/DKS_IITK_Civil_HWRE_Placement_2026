## ✅ 5. Before-Interview Checklist

> Run through this checklist 2 hours before your interview.

### Night Before

- [ ] Company researched: products, recent news, role requirements → [`company-profiles/`](../prep/company-profiles/)
- [ ] Self-intro practiced 3 times (under 90 seconds) → [`self-intro-template.md`](../prep/templates/self-intro-template.md)
- [ ] Resume updated, printed (2 copies), error-free
- [ ] Portfolio/LinkedIn profile consistent with resume
- [ ] Interview location/video link confirmed
- [ ] Professional outfit ready (formal: shirt + trousers, polished shoes)
- [ ] Stationery: pen, notepad, portfolio folder
- [ ] Laptop charged (for virtual interviews), internet tested
- [ ] Printout of key formulas (1 page) for final glance

### Morning Of

- [ ] Light breakfast (avoid heavy food)
- [ ] Reach venue 15 min early (or join video call 5 min early)
- [ ] Review self-intro once
- [ ] Review top 5 formulas for your target role (see 1-Day plan above)
- [ ] Silence phone, switch off smartwatch notifications

### During Interview

- [ ] **First 30 seconds:** Smile, firm handshake (or "Good morning" on video), eye contact
- [ ] **Self-intro:** Confident, 60–90 seconds, end with "I'm particularly interested in [this role]"
- [ ] **Technical answers:** 30-second answer → pause → offer deeper explanation
- [ ] **If stuck:** "That's a great question — let me think about that for a moment" → buy 5 seconds
- [ ] **Math on paper:** If asked to solve, write clearly, narrate your thought process
- [ ] **Ask questions:** "What does a typical day look like?" / "What are the main projects I'd work on?"
- [ ] **Closing:** Thank them, express enthusiasm, ask about next steps

### After Interview

- [ ] Send thank-you email within 24 hours (specific reference to interview discussion)
- [ ] Note down all questions asked (for future preparation)
- [ ] If no response in 1 week → polite follow-up email
