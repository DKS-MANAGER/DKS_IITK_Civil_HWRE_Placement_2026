# Johnson Controls — Placement Strategy

> **Track:** Core Civil / Mechanical / HWRE. **Relevance:** HIGH for civil candidates interested in building services, HVAC, and smart infrastructure.

## 1. Company Snapshot

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Company** | Johnson Controls International |
| **Domain** | Building Technologies & Solutions — HVAC, fire safety, security, smart buildings |
| **Role** | Graduate Engineer Trainee / Project Engineer |
| **Location** | PAN India (major offices in Mumbai, Pune, Gurugram, Chennai) |
| **Eligibility** | All engineering disciplines; civil/mechanical preferred |
| **Relevance** | **HIGH** — civil engineers work on building systems, HVAC design, fire protection, and project execution |

**About:** Johnson Controls is a global leader in building technology and services. They design, install, and maintain HVAC systems, fire detection/suppression, security systems, and building management systems (BMS). Civil engineers are needed for building services design, site execution, and commissioning.

## 2. Role Reverse Engineering

| Job Responsibility | Required Knowledge | Preparation Action |
|---|---|---|
| HVAC system design & installation | Thermal engineering, duct design | Study HVAC fundamentals |
| Building services coordination | MEP coordination, clash detection | Learn BIM basics |
| Fire protection system design | Fire safety codes, sprinkler design | Study NBC & IS codes |
| Site execution & commissioning | Project management, quality control | CPM/PERT basics |
| BMS integration | IoT, sensors, control systems | Understand BMS architecture |

## 5. Key Interview Questions

1. What is the difference between a split AC and a central AC system?
2. Explain the refrigeration cycle with a diagram.
3. What is a VAV (Variable Air Volume) system?
4. How do you size an HVAC system for a building?
5. What are the main components of a fire suppression system?
6. Explain the difference between wet and dry sprinkler systems.
7. What is BMS and how does it work?
8. What are the energy efficiency standards for commercial buildings?
9. Why Johnson Controls? How does civil engineering fit in building technology?
10. What is the difference between PSV and PRV in plumbing systems?
