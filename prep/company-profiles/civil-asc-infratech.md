## 5. Interview Questions (Key)

1. What are the different types of bridges? Classify based on structural system.
2. Explain the IRC loading for a highway bridge. What is class AA loading?
3. What is the difference between flexible and rigid pavement?
4. How do you design a road alignment? What are the geometric design criteria?
5. What is a DPR? What sections does it contain?
6. What is camber and superelevation? How are they provided?
7. Explain the CBR method of pavement design.
8. What are the stages of a highway construction project?
