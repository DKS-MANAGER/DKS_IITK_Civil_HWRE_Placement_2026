## 6. Interview Question Strategy

### A. Core Technical
1. Explain the difference between a cast-in anchor and a post-installed anchor.
2. What are the failure modes of an anchor in concrete?
3. How do you calculate the breakout capacity of an anchor?
4. What is the difference between ductile and brittle anchor failure?
5. Explain the concept of anchor grouping and spacing effects.
6. What are the key differences between IS 456 and ACI 318 for anchorage?
7. What is a firestop system and how does it work?
8. Explain the importance of edge distance in anchor design.

### B. Role-Specific (CSV-DERIVED: "structural analyses reports, building construction codes, energy/civil/railway/metro")
1. `[CSV-DERIVED]` How would you select the right anchor for a concrete facade installation?
2. `[CSV-DERIVED]` A client asks for a cable tray support on a concrete wall. Walk me through your design approach.
3. `[CSV-DERIVED]` How do seismic forces affect anchor design in a metro station?
4. `[CSV-DERIVED]` What considerations apply when designing anchoring solutions for nuclear power plants?
5. `[CSV-DERIVED]` How would you prepare a structural analysis report for an anchoring solution?

### C. Company-Domain
1. What do you know about Hilti's product range for construction?
2. How does Hilti's value engineering approach differ from traditional design?
3. What industries does Hilti serve in India?
4. How do post-installed anchors compare to welded connections?

### D. Software / Numerical
1. How would you model anchor loads in SAP2000?
2. Calculate the concrete breakout capacity of a single anchor (M16, edge distance 100mm, f'c = 25 MPa).
3. A cable tray weighing 50 kg/m is supported at 2m intervals. Design the support bracket.

### E. HR / Behavioral
1. Why Hilti and not a design consultancy?
2. How do you see the construction technology industry evolving?
3. Are you comfortable with a role that combines engineering with product knowledge?
4. Tell me about a time you solved a technical problem creatively.
