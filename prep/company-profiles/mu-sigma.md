# MuSigma

## Interview Process
MuSigma's selection process consists of three main rounds:

1. **Virtual Test** — Online aptitude-based test with moderate difficulty
2. **Group Discussion (GD)** — Random topic given; evaluates communication skills, logical thinking, and teamwork
3. **Technical + HR Interview** — Combination of technical skills assessment and personality fit evaluation

## Key Notes
- No coding round expected
- Aptitude is part of the virtual test in the first round
- GD focuses on confidence, clarity, and unique presentation of ideas

## Preparation Tips
- Practice moderate-level aptitude questions
- Prepare for GD on general topics
- Review basic technical concepts relevant to your domain
