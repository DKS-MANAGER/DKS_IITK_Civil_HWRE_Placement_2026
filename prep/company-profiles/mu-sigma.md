# Mu Sigma — Placement Strategy

> **Track:** Data Analytics / Decision Sciences. **Relevance:** HIGH for candidates interested in analytics careers; no domain-specific civil knowledge tested.

## 1. Company Snapshot

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Company** | Mu Sigma Inc. |
| **Domain** | Analytics & Decision Sciences — consulting for Fortune 500 companies |
| **Role** | Decision Scientist / Business Analyst |
| **Eligibility** | All engineering disciplines; strong aptitude + communication required |
| **Relevance** | **HIGH (analytics track)** — one of India's largest analytics employers; no coding round |

**About:** Mu Sigma is one of the world's largest pure-play analytics and decision sciences firms. They help Fortune 500 companies with marketing analytics, supply chain analytics, and risk analytics. They hire freshers from all engineering disciplines and train them in analytics methods. The interview process focuses on aptitude, group discussion, and communication rather than coding.

## 4. Key Interview Questions

1. How many tennis balls can fit in a room? (Guesstimate)
2. A bag has 5 red and 3 blue balls. If you pick 2, what's the probability both are red?
3. Explain the difference between correlation and causation.
4. What is a p-value? What does p < 0.05 mean?
5. How would you analyze a drop in website traffic? (Case study)
6. Tell me about a time you solved a problem using data. (Behavioral)
7. What is the difference between mean, median, and mode? When would you use each?
8. Explain Type I and Type II errors with a real-world example.
9. How would you present analytical findings to a non-technical stakeholder?
10. What is A/B testing? Give an example.

| Day | Focus |

| 7 | Full mock interview + behavioral stories |
