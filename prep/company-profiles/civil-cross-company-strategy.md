## 4. Specialized Tracks

### Track A: Structural Design
**Target companies:** Thornton Tomasetti, SPECTRUM, Hilti, Smarttrak AI, Dimension Renewables
**Core preparation:**
- Advanced structural analysis (FEM, matrix methods)
- RCC + Steel design (IS 456 + IS 800) at expert level
- Seismic design (IS 1893)
- Software: ETABS, SAP2000, Revit
- Differentiator: Pushover analysis, performance-based design

### Track B: Geotechnical / Foundation
**Target companies:** Reliance New Energy
**Core preparation:**
- Soil mechanics (classification, shear strength, compaction)
- Foundation engineering (bearing capacity, settlement, pile design)
- Geotechnical investigation (IS 1892, SPT, CPT)
- Retaining wall design
- Software: PLAXIS, GeoStudio, Excel

### Track C: Water / GIS
**Target companies:** Vassarlabs, Rodic, GIST
**Core preparation:**
- Hydrology (unit hydrograph, flood frequency)
- Hydraulics (Manning's, open channel, pipe flow)
- WTP/STP design
- GIS (QGIS/ArcGIS, PostGIS)
- Software: HEC-RAS, HEC-HMS, EPANET, Python

### Track D: Construction / PMC
**Target companies:** L&T, Godrej, ITC, BPCL, HPCL, JSW, Petronet, Reliance Industries, ASC Infratech
**Core preparation:**
- Construction technology (concreting, formwork, curing, pre-stressing)
- Quality control (IS 456 acceptance, cube tests)
- Estimation & BOQ (IS 1200, rate analysis)
- Project management (CPM, Primavera)
- Software: Primavera, MS Project, AutoCAD, Excel

### Track E: EPC / Industrial Infrastructure
**Target companies:** BPCL, HPCL, Petronet LNG, Reliance Industries, ISGEC
**Core preparation:**
- Industrial construction processes
- RCC + Steel design for industrial structures
- NDT and quality inspection
- Project management (EPC context)
- Safety management

### Track F: BIM / Digital Construction
**Target companies:** Thornton Tomasetti, Godrej, Smarttrak AI
**Core preparation:**
- Revit Structure (BIM modeling)
- Clash detection (Navisworks)
- LOD levels, BIM workflow
- Digital twin concepts

## 5. Maximum-ROI Preparation Path

**Design one preparation strategy that keeps the student eligible for the maximum number of companies:**

### Weeks 1-2: Universal Foundation (covers 80% of companies)
| Day | Focus | Companies Covered |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | RCC Design (IS 456) — beam, slab, column, footing | 9+ companies |
| 3-4 | Structural Analysis — moment distribution, indeterminate structures | 7+ companies |
| 5-6 | Steel Design (IS 800) — connections, members | 6+ companies |
| 7-8 | Construction Technology — concreting, formwork, curing, quality | 9+ companies |
| 9-10 | Estimation & BOQ — IS 1200, rate analysis, quantities | 6+ companies |
| 11-12 | Project Management — CPM, Primavera basics | 7+ companies |
| 13-14 | AutoCAD + Excel — structural drawings, BOQ templates | Universal |

### Weeks 3-4: Specialization (choose 1 track)
- **Structural Track:** ETABS/SAP2000 + seismic design + Revit
- **Construction Track:** Primavera + quality control + site management
- **Water/GIS Track:** HEC-RAS/HEC-HMS + GIS + WTP/STP
- **Geotech Track:** Soil mechanics + foundation design + PLAXIS

### Week 5: Interview Preparation
- Mock interviews (4+ across different company types)
- Project presentation practice (2 projects)
- Company-specific research (3 priority companies)
- Behavioral questions (STAR method)

### Week 6: Final Revision
- Quick revision of all P0 topics
- Company-specific interview questions (top 5 companies)
- Code provisions (IS 456 Table 18, cl. 26.5.1.1, IS 800 Table 19)
- Resume refinement
