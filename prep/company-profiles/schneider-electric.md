## 4. Interview Process

| Round | Focus | Duration / Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Technical + Analytical Aptitude | 60 questions in 60 minutes | Technical (Electronics): 30; Analytical (Quant, DI, Logical Puzzles): 30 |

### Round-by-Round Preparation

**Round 1: Aptitude**
- **Technical:** Control Systems, OP-AMPS, Zener Diode, Transistors.
- **Analytical:** CAT-level quantitative problems, data interpretation, and logical puzzles.
- Time management is critical; practice 60-question timed mocks.

**Round 2: Group Discussion**
- Topic example: "Business ethics in the corporate world: A passing fad?"
- Practice structuring arguments (introduction, 2–3 points with examples, conclusion).
- Speak clearly, maintain eye contact, and listen before interjecting.

**Round 3: Technical Interview**
- **Flip Flops:** Draw J-K and S-R using NAND gates; truth tables; race around condition vs race condition.
- **Rectifiers:** Bridge wave rectifier circuit, waveforms, and filtered output.
- **K-Maps:** Draw and simplify Boolean expressions.
- **Semiconductor Memories:** Types; volatile vs non-volatile differences.
- **Digital vs Analog Signals:** Definitions and classification of DC signals.

**Round 4: HR Interview**
- Tell me about yourself.
- What do you know about Schneider Electric?
- What are the different ways in which you can manage energy?
- Energy management problem-solving scenario.
- Are you flexible to work anywhere in India?

## 5. Key Interview Questions

1. What do you know about Schneider Electric's energy management solutions?
2. Draw a J-K flip-flop using NAND gates and explain the race-around condition.
3. Explain the working of a bridge wave rectifier with filter capacitor.
4. What is the difference between volatile and non-volatile semiconductor memories?
5. Simplify the Boolean expression using K-Maps: F(A,B,C,D) = Σ(0,1,5,7,8,14).
6. What are the different ways to manage energy in a building?
7. Describe the difference between Thevenin's and Norton's theorems.
8. Why do you want to join Schneider Electric?
