## 6. Interview Question Strategy

### A. Core Technical
1. Explain the activated sludge process (ASP). What are its design parameters?
2. What is the difference between aerobic and anaerobic treatment? When do you use each?
3. How do you design a water distribution network? What are the design criteria?
4. Explain primary, secondary, and tertiary treatment in an STP.
5. What is BOD and COD? How are they related?
6. How do you design a sewage pumping station?
7. What are the different types of sewage treatment plants?
8. Explain the concept of environmental impact assessment.

### B. Role-Specific (CSV-DERIVED)
1. `[CSV-DERIVED]` How would you design a water treatment plant for a city of 100,000 population?
2. `[CSV-DERIVED]` Walk me through the design of an industrial ETP for a textile factory.
3. `[CSV-DERIVED]` What are the CPCB discharge standards for treated sewage?
4. `[CSV-DERIVED]` How do you design a gravity sewer network? What slope and velocity criteria do you use?
5. `[CSV-DERIVED]` What is the role of an environmental consultant in an EIA process?

### C. Numerical / Problem Solving
1. Design an activated sludge process for Q = 10 MLD, BOD_in = 250 mg/L, target BOD_out = 10 mg/L.
2. Calculate the pipe diameter for a sewer carrying 5 MLD at 0.5 m/s velocity with 1:500 slope.
3. Design a rapid sand filter for Q = 20 MLD (approach velocity, bed depth, backwash rate).
4. A factory discharges 2 MLD with BOD = 500 mg/L. Design an ETP to meet CPCB standards.

### D. HR / Behavioral
1. Why Rodic Consultants? What interests you about environmental consulting?
2. How do you see India's water infrastructure evolving?
3. Are you comfortable with field visits for project assessment?
4. What environmental issues are you most passionate about?

## 10. Project Strategy

### Project 1: STP Design for a Residential Township
- **Objective:** Design a 5 MLD STP using ASP technology
- **Software:** Excel + AutoCAD (process flow diagram)
- **Method:** Population estimate → BOD loading → aeration tank sizing → secondary clarifier → disinfection
- **Resume line:** "Designed 5 MLD ASP-based STP for a 2000-unit residential township per CPCB standards"
