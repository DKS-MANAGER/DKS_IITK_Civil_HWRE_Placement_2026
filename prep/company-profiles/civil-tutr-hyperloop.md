## 5. Key Interview Questions

1. `[CSV-DERIVED]` Explain the finite element method. What are shape functions?
2. What is the difference between implicit and explicit FEA?
3. How do you perform a dynamic analysis in ANSYS?
4. What is LS-DYNA used for? When do you use explicit dynamics?
5. Explain modal analysis. Why is it important for structural design?
6. What is a digital twin? How does it apply to transportation infrastructure?
7. How would you mesh a complex geometry for FEA?
