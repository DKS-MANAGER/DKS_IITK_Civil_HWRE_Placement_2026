## 6. Interview Question Strategy

### A. Core Technical
1. Explain the complete process of concreting at site — from ordering to curing.
2. What are the quality checks you perform before, during, and after concreting?
3. What is the difference between nominal mix and design mix concrete?
4. How do you calculate the quantity of cement, sand, and aggregate for a given volume?
5. Explain the concept of development length. Why is it important?
6. What are the common defects in concrete and how do you prevent them?

### B. Role-Specific (CSV-DERIVED from JD: "on-site project execution, PMC management, SAP, quality & safety")
1. `[CSV-DERIVED]` How do you manage multiple vendors and subcontractors at a construction site?
2. `[CSV-DERIVED]` Describe your experience with quality control processes at site.
3. `[CSV-DERIVED]` How do you prepare and verify a running bill of quantities?
4. `[CSV-DERIVED]` What is your approach to ensuring safety compliance on a construction site?
5. `[CSV-DERIVED]` How would you handle a situation where concrete cubes fail the 7-day strength test?

### C. Company-Domain
1. What do you know about Godrej Properties' recent projects?
2. How does real-estate construction management differ from infrastructure construction?
3. What challenges do you anticipate in high-rise residential construction?
4. How do you ensure timely delivery in a real-estate project?

### D. Software
1. How would you create a construction schedule in Primavera P6?
2. Have you used SAP for material management?
3. Can you read and interpret a structural drawing in AutoCAD?

### E. Project-Based
1. Tell me about a construction project you've been involved in.
2. What was the most challenging aspect of your project?
3. How did you manage quality in your project?

### F. Numerical / Problem Solving
1. Calculate the quantity of materials for M25 concrete for a slab of 10m × 5m × 0.15m.
2. A beam is 6m long with effective cover 40mm. Calculate cutting length for a bar with standard bends.
3. Prepare a rate analysis for concreting M25 for 1 cubic meter including material and labor costs.

### G. HR / Behavioral
1. Why Godrej Properties and not a construction company like L&T?
2. Are you comfortable working at project sites across India?
3. Tell me about a time you showed leadership.
4. How do you handle pressure and tight deadlines?
5. What is your understanding of the real-estate industry in India?

## 8. Candidate Preparation Strategy

### Phase 1 — Screening (Before Applying)
- [ ] Civil engineering degree from Tier-1 college (mandatory per JD)
- [ ] Resume highlights construction/site experience
- [ ] AutoCAD proficiency mentioned
- [ ] At least one construction project on resume

### Phase 2 — Technical Preparation
- [ ] Concreting process: batching → transport → placing → compaction → curing — master every step
- [ ] Quality control: cube tests, slump test, rebar testing, cover verification
- [ ] Estimation: BOQ preparation, rate analysis for concreting and plastering
- [ ] IS 456 key clauses: curing (cl. 12), tolerances (Table 13), acceptance criteria (cl. 15)

### Phase 3 — Software
- [ ] AutoCAD: Read structural drawings, identify beams/columns/slabs
- [ ] Primavera/MS Project: Create a building construction schedule
- [ ] Excel: Build BOQ and running-bill templates

### Phase 4 — Projects
- [ ] Prepare 1 construction-execution-focused project for discussion
- [ ] Emphasize quality control, scheduling, and cost management aspects
- [ ] Practice explaining challenges and solutions

### Phase 5 — Interview
- [ ] Practice 15 construction management questions
- [ ] Prepare "Why Godrej Properties?" with specific project references
- [ ] Research Godrej Properties recent launches (e.g., Godrej Platinum, The Trees)
- [ ] Understand real-estate project lifecycle (land → approval → construction → handover)

### Phase 6 — Final Revision
- [ ] Concrete technology: mix design, workability tests, curing periods
- [ ] Material specifications: cement (IS 269/8112/12269), aggregates (IS 383)
- [ ] BOQ measurement rules (IS 1200)
- [ ] Site safety basics
