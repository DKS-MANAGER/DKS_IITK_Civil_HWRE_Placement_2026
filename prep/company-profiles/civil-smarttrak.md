## 5. Key Interview Questions

1. `[CSV-DERIVED]` How do you calculate wind loads on a solar tracker structure per IS 875?
2. What seismic considerations apply to ground-mounted solar structures?
3. Explain fatigue analysis. When is it critical for structural design?
4. What is the difference between ANSYS and Abaqus for structural FEA?
5. How would you design a steel tracker rail for a solar farm?
6. What are the loading conditions for a solar panel support structure?
7. How do you account for dynamic wind loads on a cantilevered structure?
