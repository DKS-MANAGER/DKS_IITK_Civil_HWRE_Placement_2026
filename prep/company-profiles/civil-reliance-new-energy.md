## 6. Interview Question Strategy

### A. Core Technical (Geotech focus)
1. Explain Terzaghi's bearing capacity theory. What are the failure modes?
2. What is the difference between shallow and deep foundations? When do you choose each?
3. How do you interpret SPT N-values for bearing capacity?
4. Explain consolidation settlement. What is the difference between immediate and consolidation settlement?
5. What are the different types of pile foundations? How do you calculate pile capacity?
6. Explain Rankine's earth pressure theory. When would you use active vs passive pressure?
7. What is the difference between normally consolidated and overconsolidated soil?
8. How do you assess slope stability? What methods do you use?

### B. Role-Specific (CSV-DERIVED: geotech investigation, foundation design, BOQ, retaining structures)
1. `[CSV-DERIVED]` Describe the process of a geotechnical investigation from planning to report.
2. `[CSV-DERIVED]` How do you prepare a BOQ for foundation works?
3. `[CSV-DERIVED]` A client wants to build a giga-factory on soft clay. What foundation system would you recommend?
4. `[CSV-DERIVED]` How do you review a geotechnical investigation report submitted by a consultant?
5. `[CSV-DERIVED]` Walk me through the design of a retaining wall for a 5m deep excavation.
6. `[CSV-DERIVED]` What is a dynamic foundation? How does it differ from a static foundation design?

### C. Company-Domain
1. What do you know about Reliance New Energy's giga-factory project?
2. How does giga-factory construction differ from regular industrial construction?
3. What geotechnical challenges would you expect at a large industrial site?

### D. Numerical / Problem Solving
1. Calculate the ultimate bearing capacity of a strip footing (B=2m, c=20 kPa, φ=30°, γ=18 kN/m³).
2. A 10m deep borehole shows N-values of 15, 25, 35, 40 at depths 3, 6, 9, 12m. Determine foundation recommendation.
3. Design a cantilever retaining wall for 4m height in cohesionless soil (φ=30°, γ=18 kN/m³).
4. Calculate the consolidation settlement of a 3m clay layer (Cc=0.25, e0=0.8, σ'0=80 kPa, Δσ=60 kPa).

### E. HR / Behavioral
1. Why Reliance New Energy over a traditional consultancy?
2. How do you see the renewable energy sector evolving for civil engineers?
3. Are you comfortable working at the Jamnagar site?
4. Tell me about a challenging geotechnical problem you encountered in academics.
