## 5. Interview Questions (Key)

1. Design a singly reinforced beam for span 5m, UDL 20 kN/m (M20, Fe415).
2. What are the minimum reinforcement requirements for beams and slabs (IS 456)?
3. Explain the difference between one-way and two-way slabs. When is each used?
4. What is development length? How do you calculate it?
5. What are the types of bolted connections? Which is more efficient?
6. How do you detail reinforcement at a beam-column joint?
7. What is the difference between limit state of collapse and limit state of serviceability?
8. Explain the design of a square footing for a column load of 500 kN.
