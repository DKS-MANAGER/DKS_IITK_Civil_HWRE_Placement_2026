## 📊 Case Interview

### What is a Case Interview?

A business problem presented to you to solve aloud. Interviewers assess **structured thinking**, not the "right" answer.

### Case Frameworks

| Framework | Use For |
|:----------|:--------|
| **Profitability** | Why is profit declining? How to improve? |
| **Market Sizing** | How many X in Y? |
| **Guesstimates** | Estimate a quantity with limited data |
| **Market Entry** | Should company enter market X? |
| **Pricing** | How to price product X? |
| **Product Case** | Improve/launch a product |

### Market Sizing Example

**Question:** How many cars are sold in India per year?

**Framework:**
1. Population of India ≈ 1.4 billion
2. Urban population ≈ 35% ≈ 490 million
3. Households ≈ 490M / 4 (avg family size) ≈ 122 million
4. % households owning a car ≈ 10% ≈ 12 million
5. Car replacement cycle ≈ 10 years
6. Annual sales ≈ 12M / 10 ≈ 1.2 million cars/year

> **Key:** Show your assumptions clearly. The interviewer cares about your logic, not the exact number.

## 🗣️ Group Discussion (GD)

### What is a GD?

A group of candidates discusses a topic. Assessors evaluate **communication, structure, listening, and contribution quality** — not who talks the most.

### GD Structure

1. **Opening (30–60 sec):** State your position clearly
2. **Body:** Present 2–3 structured points with examples
3. **Listening:** Acknowledge others' points, build on them
4. **Closing:** Summarize the group's consensus

### GD Do's & Don'ts

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|:------|:--------|
| Speak early (first 2–3 min) | Dominate the discussion |
| Structure points (First, Second, Third) | Interrupt others |
| Build on others' ideas | Go off-topic |
| Use examples/data | Get emotional |
| Summarize at the end | Stay silent the whole time |
