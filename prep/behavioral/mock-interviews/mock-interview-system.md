# 🎭 Mock Interview System — Structured Practice with Scoring

> **Mock interviews are the single most effective way to prepare. This system gives you structured scenarios, scoring rubrics, and feedback templates.**

---

## Why Mock Interviews Matter

| Benefit | Why |
|---------|-----|
| Builds confidence | Practice reduces anxiety |
| Reveals weaknesses | You discover gaps you didn't know existed |
| Improves delivery | Feedback on pace, filler words, eye contact |
| Tests knowledge | You find what you actually know vs. think you know |
| Simulates pressure | You learn to think on your feet |

---

## Mock Interview Formats

### Format 1: Solo Practice (Self-Recorded)
**Best for:** First-time practice, timing drills
**How:**
1. Pick 5 questions from the question master database
2. Record yourself answering each (60-90 sec)
3. Review the recording — note filler words, pace, structure
4. Score yourself using the answer quality checker
5. Re-record the weak answers

### Format 2: Peer Mock (2 People)
**Best for:** Realistic practice with feedback
**How:**
1. Partner A interviews Partner B (15 min)
2. Partner A asks 5-8 questions (mix of behavioral + technical)
3. Partner B answers, Partner A takes notes
4. Switch roles
5. 10 min feedback session using the scoring rubric

### Format 3: Panel Mock (3+ People)
**Best for:** Simulating real interview pressure
**How:**
1. 2-3 interviewers, 1 candidate
2. 30-minute interview (intro + behavioral + technical + questions)
3. Panel scores using the rubric
4. 15-minute feedback session

### Format 4: Stress Mock (Pressure Simulation)
**Best for:** Pressure interview preparation
**How:**
1. Interviewer uses pressure tactics (interruption, challenge, rapid-fire)
2. 20-minute intense session
3. Focus on composure, not content
4. Debrief on how you handled pressure

## 5 Mock Interview Scenarios

### Scenario 1: PSU Interview (BPCL/EIL/NHPC)
**Duration:** 30 min
**Structure:**
- Intro (2 min)
- Why PSU? Why this company? (5 min)
- Technical: Hydraulics, hydrology, IS codes (10 min)
- Behavioral: Teamwork, leadership, adaptability (8 min)
- Questions for interviewer (5 min)

**Focus:** IS codes, water resources, site awareness, PSU motivation

### Scenario 2: Core Civil Interview (L&T/AECOM)
**Duration:** 30 min
**Structure:**
- Intro (2 min)
- Why civil? Why this company? (5 min)
- Technical: Design, construction, project management (10 min)
- Behavioral: Problem-solving, quality, collaboration (8 min)
- Questions for interviewer (5 min)

**Focus:** Design principles, project execution, quality assurance

### Scenario 3: Analytics Interview (Barclays/Accenture)
**Duration:** 30 min
**Structure:**
- Intro (2 min)
- Why transition from civil to analytics? (5 min)
- Technical: SQL, Python, statistics (10 min)
- Behavioral: Problem-solving, data-driven thinking (8 min)
- Questions for interviewer (5 min)

**Focus:** SQL/Python, analytical thinking, transition story

### Scenario 4: Consulting Interview (BCG/KPMG)
**Duration:** 30 min
**Structure:**
- Intro (2 min)
- Why consulting? (5 min)
- Case study (10 min)
- Behavioral: Leadership, communication, judgment (8 min)
- Questions for interviewer (5 min)

**Focus:** Case frameworks, structured thinking, communication

### Scenario 5: Research Interview (IIT/CSIR)
**Duration:** 30 min
**Structure:**
- Intro (2 min)
- Thesis defense (10 min)
- Technical: CFD, turbulence, sediment transport (10 min)
- Behavioral: Research motivation, collaboration (5 min)
- Questions for interviewer (3 min)

**Focus:** Thesis depth, technical rigor, research motivation

## Mock Interview Practice Plan

### Week 1: Foundation
- 2 solo practice sessions (recorded)
- 1 peer mock (Format 2)
- Focus: Structure and timing

### Week 2: Content
- 2 peer mocks (Format 2)
- 1 panel mock (Format 3)
- Focus: Specificity and quantification

### Week 3: Pressure
- 1 stress mock (Format 4)
- 1 panel mock (Format 3)
- Focus: Composure under pressure

### Week 4: Final Polish
- 2 full panel mocks (Format 3)
- 1 stress mock (Format 4)
- Focus: Everything together
