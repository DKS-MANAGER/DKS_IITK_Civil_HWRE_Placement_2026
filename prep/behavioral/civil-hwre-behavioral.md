**Framework:** Framework + Example

### Q7: What IS codes are you familiar with? 🟡 P1

- IS 2911 (Pile Foundations)
- IS 456:2000 — Key values: fck, fy, modular ratio, moment of resistance

## Category 3: HWRE-Specific (7 Questions)

### Q19: Why HWRE over other civil specializations? 🔴 P0
**Framework:** PPP
**Answer Guide:**
- Specific passion: "Water resources engineering sits at the intersection of fluid mechanics, environmental science, and computational modeling"
- Impact: "India's water challenges need engineering solutions — floods, droughts, water quality"
- Career alignment: "HWRE opens doors in both core engineering and computational/analytical roles"

### Q20: Tell me about sediment transport phenomena. 🟡 P1
**Framework:** Technical + Practical
**Answer Guide:**
- Theory: "Sediment transport is the movement of particles by flowing water — bed load, suspended load, wash load"
- Application: "Bridge scour, reservoir sedimentation, channel design all depend on sediment transport"
- Your work: "My thesis modeled this using an Eulerian two-phase CFD solver"

### Q21: How does CFD apply to water resources engineering? 🟡 P1
**Framework:** CRIT
**Answer Guide:**
- Applications: "CFD predicts flood flow patterns, sediment transport, dam break analysis, water treatment processes"
- Advantages: "CFD is cheaper than physical models, allows parametric studies, and provides detailed flow field data"
- Your work: "My CFD work on bridge pier scour provides rapid scour prediction for bridge design"

### Q22: What are the key challenges in water resources engineering today? 🟡 P1
**Framework:** Framework + Example
**Answer Guide:**
- Climate change: "Changing rainfall patterns affect flood and drought prediction"
- Urbanization: "Increasing impervious surfaces change urban hydrology"
- Water quality: "Pollution and treatment remain major challenges"
- Infrastructure: "Aging dams and bridges need assessment and retrofitting"

### Q23: How do you approach canal or river engineering problems? 🟢 P2
**Framework:** Technical + Process
**Answer Guide:**
- Manning's equation for flow
- Channel design principles
- Sediment transport considerations
- Environmental flow requirements
- Your experience: "My thesis on scour is directly relevant to river engineering"

### Q24: Tell me about groundwater management. 🟢 P2
**Framework:** Technical
**Answer Guide:**
- Darcy's law, aquifer properties
- Groundwater modeling (MODFLOW if familiar)
-可持续抽水, recharge techniques
- Connect to HWRE: "Groundwater is a critical component of water resources management"

**Answer Guide:**
