# ❓ Questions to Ask the Interviewer — 50+ Questions by Category

> **"Do you have any questions for us?" is NOT optional. It's your chance to show curiosity, preparation, and genuine interest. Always ask 2-3 questions.**

---

## Why This Matters

| Statistic | Implication |
|-----------|-------------|
| Asked in 95%+ of interviews | You WILL face this question |
| "No questions" = disinterest | Always have questions ready |
| Good questions = memorable | Interviewers remember curious candidates |
| Bad questions = red flag | Don't ask about salary in the first round |

---

## Category 1: Role & Responsibilities (10 Questions)

1. What does a typical day look like in this role?
2. What are the key responsibilities in the first 6 months?
3. What does success look like in this role?
4. What are the biggest challenges the team is currently facing?
5. How is performance measured and reviewed?
6. What tools and technologies does the team use daily?
7. How does this role interact with other teams?
8. What projects will I be working on initially?
9. How is work prioritized and assigned?
10. What is the team structure and reporting hierarchy?

## How to Choose Your Questions

### For PSU Interviews
- Focus on: Role responsibilities, team structure, project types, location details
- Avoid: Salary, bond details (ask HR separately)

### For Core Civil Interviews
- Focus on: Project types, design vs. site work, growth path, team culture
- Avoid: Generic questions — be specific to their projects

### For Analytics/Tech Interviews
- Focus on: Tools/technologies, data challenges, team structure, growth
- Avoid: Questions that show you don't understand the role

### For Consulting Interviews
- Focus on: Client work, case types, team structure, travel
- Avoid: Questions about work-life balance (ask subtly)

---

## The 3-Question Rule

**Always prepare 3 questions:**
1. One about the **role** (shows you care about the work)
2. One about the **team/culture** (shows you care about fit)
3. One about the **company/industry** (shows you did research)

**If they answer your questions during the interview, have backups ready.**

---

## Questions to AVOID

| Bad Question | Why It's Bad | Better Alternative |
|--------------|--------------|-------------------|
| "What does the company do?" | Shows you didn't research | "I noticed your recent work on [project] — how does this role contribute?" |
| "How much does this pay?" | Too early, seems money-focused | Ask HR after the interview |
| "How soon can I get promoted?" | Seems impatient | "What does career progression look like?" |
| "Do you have other questions?" | Not a question | Ask something specific |
| "No, I have no questions" | Shows disinterest | Always have 2-3 ready |
