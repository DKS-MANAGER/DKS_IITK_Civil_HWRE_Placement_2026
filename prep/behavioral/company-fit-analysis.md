## How to Research Each Company

### 10-Minute Pre-Interview Research
1. **Company website** (3 min) — About page, recent news, values
2. **LinkedIn** (2 min) — Company page, recent posts, employee profiles
3. **Glassdoor** (2 min) — Interview experiences, salary data
4. **News** (2 min) — Recent projects, awards, initiatives
5. **Your connection** (1 min) — How your skills/experience connect

### Key Questions to Answer
- What does this company do in ONE sentence?
- What are their 3 most recent projects?
- What are their core values?
- What tools/technologies do they use?
- What is their interview format?
- How does YOUR background connect to THEIR work?
