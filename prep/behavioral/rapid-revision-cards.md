# ⚡ Rapid Revision Cards — 30-Minute Crash Course Before Any Interview

> **Use this as your final review before an interview. It condenses the entire behavioral system into quick-reference cards.**

---

## Card 1: The 5 Core Frameworks

| Framework | Use For | Structure |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| **STAR** | "Tell me about a time..." | Situation → Task → Action → Result |
| **CARL** | Failure/learning questions | Context → Action → Result → Learning |
| **Present-Past-Future** | "Tell me about yourself" | Now → Background → Why this role |
| **SOAR** | "What would you do if..." | Situation → Options → Action → Result |
| **PREP** | Opinion/approach questions | Point → Reason → Evidence → Point |

---

## Card 2: The 3 Must-Have Stories

### Story 1: Leadership/Teamwork
> "I led a 4-member CFD thesis team at IIT Kanpur. I divided tasks by strengths, ran daily stand-ups, and when the simulation diverged (y+ = 300+), I diagnosed and fixed it with boundary layer refinement. We finished 2 weeks early with 95% convergence."

### Story 2: Problem-Solving
> "Our pipeline scour simulation diverged after 500 steps. I systematically checked BCs, time step, and mesh — found y+ was 300+ (should be <5). Refined the mesh, simulation converged in 3 days. Published the validated results."

### Story 3: Adaptability
> "Mid-thesis, our supervisor changed focus from pipeline to bridge pier scour. I created a revised plan in 1 day, reallocated tasks, and completed on time. The pivot led to a stronger conference paper."

## Card 3: The 10 Most Likely Questions

| # | Question | Framework | Key Point |
|---|----------|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Tell me about yourself | Present-Past-Future | 90 sec, end with why this company |
| 2 | Why this company? | PPP | Research + connect |

| 4 | What are your strengths? | STRENGTH | 2-3 with examples |

| 6 | Tell me about a time you led a team | STAR | Leadership story |

## Card 4: The 3-Level Follow-Up Defense

For any story, be ready for 3 levels:

**Level 1 (30 sec):** "I led a CFD thesis team."
**Level 2 (60 sec):** "I divided tasks by strengths, ran daily stand-ups, and fixed a divergence issue."
**Level 3 (90 sec):** "The divergence was caused by y+ = 300+ (should be <5). I refined the boundary layer with 15 inflation layers, 1.15 growth ratio. I used GCI for validation following ASME V&V 20."

## Card 5: Power Phrases

### Starting
- "In my experience at IIT Kanpur..."
- "Let me give you a specific example..."
- "During my thesis project..."

### Actions
- "I took the initiative to..."
- "I systematically..."
- "I collaborated with [specific people] to..."

### Results
- "This resulted in a [X]% improvement..."
- "We completed [task] [X weeks] ahead of schedule..."
- "The solution was adopted by [X people/teams]..."

### Learning
- "This taught me that..."
- "I learned the importance of..."
- "Since then, I've applied this principle to..."

## Card 6: The 5-Minute Pre-Interview Routine

### 5 Minutes Before
1. Deep breath (5 slow breaths)
2. Review your 3 core stories
3. Review your intro (90 sec version)
4. Review 3 questions to ask
5. Remind yourself: "I'm prepared. They want to see my best."

### During the Interview
- Pause before answering (2-3 sec)
- Use frameworks (STAR, CARL, etc.)
- Quantify results (%, weeks, people)
- Use "I" for your actions
- End with a confident summary

### After the Interview
- Note what went well
- Note what to improve
- Prepare for the next interview

---

## Card 7: Red Flags to Avoid

| Red Flag | Fix |
|----------|-----|
| "We worked together" | Use "I" for your actions |
| "It went well" | Quantify: "95% convergence, 2 weeks early" |
| "I'm a hard worker" | Show, don't tell — give an example |
| No questions for interviewer | Always have 2-3 ready |
| Rambling answers | Use frameworks, keep to 60-90 sec |
| Generic answers | Be specific with tools, numbers, names |

## Card 8: Company-Specific Quick Prep

### For PSU
- Why PSU? → Nation-building, stability, technical depth
- Remote locations? → "Absolutely, I see it as growth"
- IS codes? → IS 456, IS 800, IS 1893
- Recent projects? → Research 2-3 before interview

### For Core Civil
- Why civil? → Passion for building, impact
- Quality/safety? → IS codes, validation, peer review
- Proud project? → Thesis/CFD with quantified results

### For Analytics/Tech
- Why transition? → Analytical skills, Python/SQL
- Data-driven decision? → CFD data analysis story
- Automated task? → Python visualization script story
