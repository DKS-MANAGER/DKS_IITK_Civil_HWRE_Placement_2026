# Interview Day Survival Guide

> **The complete operational playbook for interview day — from 24 hours before to recovery after mistakes.** Read this entire file the night before your interview.

---

## ⏰ Timeline Overview

```
24 HOURS BEFORE → 3 HOURS BEFORE → 30 MIN BEFORE → WALK IN → INTERVIEW → OUT
```

---

## 🕐 24 Hours Before Interview

### Priority Checklist

| Time Block | Action | Resource |
|------------|--------|----------|
| **Morning** | Review formula sheet for target subject | [Formula Sheet](../../core/gate/formulas/gate-civil-formulas.md) |
| **Mid-morning** | Run through 20 rapid-fire questions | Subject-specific Q-bank sections |
| **Afternoon** | Prepare self-introduction (3 versions: 30s, 60s, 90s) | [Self Intro System](../behavioral/self_intro/self-introduction-system.md) |
| **Afternoon** | Prepare 5 STAR stories (leadership, teamwork, problem-solving, failure, initiative) | [Behavioral Guide](../behavioral/behavioral-interview-guide.md) |
| **Evening** | Research the company (recent projects, CTC, role details, interview process) | [Company Profiles](../company-profiles/) |
| **Evening** | Prepare 3 questions to ask the interviewer | [Questions to Ask](../behavioral/questions-to-ask-interviewer.md) |
| **Night** | Lay out clothes, documents, charger, notepad | — |
| **Night** | Sleep 7+ hours. No studying after 10 PM. | — |

### What NOT to Do

- ❌ Don't try to learn new topics
- ❌ Don't cram 50 formulas — review 12 key ones deeply
- ❌ Don't read company glassdoor reviews (they'll make you anxious)
- ❌ Don't compare yourself with peers who already interviewed

## 🕐 3 Hours Before Interview

### Quick Revision Protocol

| Minutes | Focus Area | What to Review |
|---------|------------|----------------|
| **0–15** | Self-Introduction | Read aloud 3 times. Vary emphasis based on role. |
| **15–30** | Formula Blitz | Open formula sheet. Cover the right side. Recall each formula from the name alone. |
| **30–45** | Technical Rapid-Fire | Answer 10 questions from your target subject aloud. Focus on WHY questions, not just definitions. |
| **45–60** | Project/Thesis | Rehearse 2-minute project explanation. Prepare for "What was innovative?" and "What were limitations?" |
| **60–75** | Company Prep | Re-read company profile. Know: recent project, role description, why you fit. |
| **75–90** | STAR Stories | Rehearse 2 key stories aloud. Keep each under 90 seconds. |
| **90+** | Transition | Shower, dress, eat light, leave early. |

```
Inhale (4 seconds) → Hold (4 seconds) → Exhale (6 seconds) × 5 cycles
```

This activates the parasympathetic nervous system and reduces cortisol.

## 🎯 During the Interview

### First 2 Minutes — Set the Tone

1. **Greet confidently:** "Good morning. Thank you for this opportunity."
2. **Sit upright.** Make eye contact. Smile.
3. **Wait for them to start.** Don't rush into your introduction unless asked.

### When You Get an Unknown Question

**Never say "I don't know" and stop.** Use the **Bridge Technique:**

```
Acknowledge → Partial Knowledge → Bridge → Offer
```

**Example:**
> "That's an interesting question. While I haven't studied [specific topic] in depth, my understanding from [related topic] is [partial answer]. I believe the core principle would be [bridge]. I'd be happy to explore this further if given the chance."

**This shows:**
- Honesty (you're not bluffing)
- Thinking ability (you can connect concepts)
- Learning attitude (you want to grow)

### When You Disagree with the Interviewer

```
Acknowledge → Respect → Present Your View → Invite Feedback
```

**Example:**
> "I understand your point about [their view]. However, based on my understanding of [source/reasoning], I would approach it as [your view]. Could you share your perspective on why [specific aspect]? I'd love to learn."

**Never:** Argue, get defensive, or say "you're wrong."

### When You Make a Technical Mistake

**Detect it yourself before they point it out:**

> "Actually, let me reconsider. I think I mixed up [X] with [Y]. The correct relationship is [corrected answer]. Thank you for giving me a moment to think."

**If they point it out:**

> "You're right. I misspoke. The correct answer is [corrected]. I appreciate the correction."

**Never:** Insist you're right when you're not. Never try to justify a wrong answer.

### When You Get Follow-Up Questions (Deep Dive)

Follow-up questions mean **they're interested.** This is good.

**Strategy:**
1. **Don't panic.** They want to see how deep you go.
2. **Use layered answers:** Concept → Assumption → Application → Limitation
3. **If you reach your limit:** "That's beyond my current understanding, but I'd approach it by [method]. This is something I'd love to learn more about."

### When They Ask About Your Project

**Use the 2-Minute Framework:**

```
Problem (15 sec) → Objective (10 sec) → Method (30 sec) → Result (15 sec) → Impact (10 sec)
```

**Prepare for these follow-ups:**

| Follow-Up | How to Answer |
|-----------|---------------|
| "What was innovative?" | Focus on methodology or tool combination |
| "What were the limitations?" | Be honest — shows maturity |
| "What would you do differently?" | Shows learning and self-awareness |
| "How does this relate to our work?" | Connect project to company's domain |
| "What was YOUR specific contribution?" | Use "I" not "we" — be specific |
| "What validation did you perform?" | Mention quantitative metrics (NSE, RMSE, accuracy) |

### When They Ask Behavioral Questions

Use the framework that fits the question:

| Question Type | Framework | Duration |
|---------------|-----------|----------|
| "Tell me about a time when…" | STAR | 90 sec |
| "What's your greatest weakness?" | Weakness + Growth | 60 sec |
| "Why this company?" | Research + Connect | 60 sec |
| "Why should we hire you?" | Skills + Evidence + Value | 60 sec |
| "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?" | Ambition + Realism | 45 sec |
| "Tell me about yourself" | Present-Past-Future | 90 sec |

## 🔄 After Technical Failure — Recovery Protocol

If you bomb a technical question or series of questions, **don't spiral.** Use this recovery protocol:

### Step 1: Reset (Internal)
Take a breath. One bad answer does not define the interview. Interviewers expect some gaps.

### Step 2: Pivot to Strength
If the next question is in your area of strength, **nail it.** Show them what you DO know.

### Step 3: Recover Explicitly
If you get a chance:
> "I want to circle back to your earlier question about [topic]. I've been thinking about it, and I believe [improved answer]."

### Step 4: End Strong
The last 5 minutes matter most. End with:
- A confident summary of your strengths
- A genuine expression of interest
- A thoughtful question for them

### Step 5: Post-Interview
- **Don't over-analyze** until you're outside the building
- **Write down questions asked** while they're fresh (useful for next interview)
- **Reflect on 1 thing you did well** and 1 thing to improve

## 📋 Quick Reference: Interview Etiquette

### Do's
- ✅ Arrive 10–15 minutes early
- ✅ Bring 3 printed copies of your resume
- ✅ Maintain eye contact (60–70% of the time)
- ✅ Listen fully before answering
- ✅ Use specific examples (numbers, dates, outcomes)
- ✅ Ask thoughtful questions at the end
- ✅ Thank them before leaving

### Don'ts
- ❌ Don't check your phone
- ❌ Don't badmouth previous employers/professors
- ❌ Don't lie about skills or experiences
- ❌ Don't give one-word answers
- ❌ Don't interrupt the interviewer
- ❌ Don't negotiate salary in the first round
- ❌ Don't say "I have no questions" when asked
