# RESUME DEFENSE

> **Prepare to defend every important line of your resume.** For each major CV section, map `Claim → Possible Question → Preparation`.

---

## The Method

For every important CV line, prepare:

```
CLAIM (what your resume says)
      ↓
POSSIBLE QUESTION (what they might ask)
      ↓
PREPARATION (your answer + evidence)
```

---

## 1. Projects

| Claim | Possible Question | Preparation |
|:------|:------------------|:------------|
| "Modeled flood inundation using HEC-RAS" | "Walk me through your methodology" | 30-sec + 2-min explanation → [PROJECT_DEFENCE](../PROJECT_DEFENCE.md) |
| "Reduced peak runoff by 22%" | "How did you calculate that?" | Show the calculation + assumptions |
| "Used Python for data analysis" | "What libraries? What was the challenge?" | Name libraries + a specific challenge solved |

**Prepare for:** Methodology · assumptions · tools · validation · results · limitations · future work

---

## 2. Internships

| Claim | Possible Question | Preparation |
|:------|:------------------|:------------|
| "Interned at [Company]" | "What did you actually do?" | 2-min summary of role + contribution |
| "Improved process efficiency" | "How did you measure improvement?" | Quantified before/after |
| "Worked with [team]" | "How did you handle a conflict?" | STAR story → [Story Bank](../behavioral/story-bank/) |

**Prepare for:** Role · contribution · skills used · what you learned · challenges

---

## 3. Research

| Claim | Possible Question | Preparation |
|:------|:------------------|:------------|
| "Research on [topic]" | "Explain your research in simple terms" | 30-sec + 2-min explanation |
| "Published in [venue]" | "What was your contribution?" | Be specific about your role |
| "Used [method]" | "Why did you choose that method?" | Justify your choice + alternatives |

**Prepare for:** Problem · methodology · results · significance · limitations

---

## 4. Technical Skills

| Claim | Possible Question | Preparation |
|:------|:------------------|:------------|
| "Proficient in Python" | "Write a function to…" | Practice coding basics → [Software Hub](../../software-and-tech/) |
| "Expert in AutoCAD" | "What's your workflow for X?" | Describe a real workflow |
| "Knowledge of HEC-RAS" | "Explain the key inputs" | Know the theory + practice |

**Prepare for:** Honesty about level · a concrete example of use · willingness to learn

> ⚠️ **Never overstate skill levels.** Interviewers probe. It's better to say "working knowledge" honestly than "expert" and fail.

---

## 5. Achievements

| Claim | Possible Question | Preparation |
|:------|:------------------|:------------|
| "Ranked top 5% in [exam]" | "What did that require?" | Effort + strategy story |
| "Led [team/event]" | "How did you lead? What was the outcome?" | Leadership STAR story |
| "Award for [X]" | "What made you win?" | Specific contribution + result |

---

## 6. Education

| Claim | Possible Question | Preparation |
|:------|:------------------|:------------|
| "CGPA X.XX" | "Why is your CGPA in [subject] low?" | Honest + improvement story |
| "Relevant coursework" | "Tell me about [course]" | 1-line takeaway per course |
| "Backlog in [subject]" | "What happened?" | Honest + how you recovered |

---

## 🎯 High-Probability Resume Questions

1. "Walk me through your resume"
2. "Tell me about your most impactful project"
3. "What's your biggest technical challenge?"
4. "Why did you choose [skill/tool]?"
5. "What would you improve about your project?"
6. "What did you learn from your internship?"
7. "Why is your CGPA in X low?"
8. "What's your proudest achievement?"

---

## 📋 Resume Defense Checklist

- [ ] Every project has a 30-sec + 2-min explanation
- [ ] Every skill level is honest
- [ ] Every metric can be explained
- [ ] Every achievement has a story
- [ ] Every weakness has an honest + recovery answer
- [ ] You can walk through your resume in 2 minutes
